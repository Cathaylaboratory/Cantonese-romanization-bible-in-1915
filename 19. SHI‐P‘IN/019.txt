1 SHEÛNG‐T‘IN cheung‐mīng Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐wīng‐kwong; k‘ūng‐ts‘ong hín‐ch‘ut k‘uĭ kung‐foo.
2 Ni‐yāt ch‘uēn‐kwòh kóh‐yāt, ni‐yê shî chì‐waî kwòh‐kóh‐yê.
3 Mō īn mō uĕ, yīk mō sheng‐yam hóh t‘eng.
4 K‘uĭ kw‘ai‐kuí wân‐hāng ts‘uēn‐teî, k‘uĭ hô‐lîng ch‘uēn‐tò teî‐kīk. Sheûng‐Taì lîng t‘in tsô yāt‐t‘aū‐chi‐cheùng‐mōk,
5 Yāt‐t‘aū hó‐ts‘z̆ san‐lōng, ch‘ut k‘uĭ‐chi‐fōng, yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ yŭng‐sẑ, foon‐heí tsaú‐tûng.
6 Ts‘ūng ni‐pin‐t‘in, hāng‐tò kóh‐pin‐t‘in: mō yat māt m̄‐shaû k‘uĭ‐chi‐wōh‐nuĕn.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ uēn‐ts‘uēn, nāng so‐síng yān līng‐wān: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐īn‐uĕ chan‐shāt, nāng lîng uē‐ch‘ún‐yān ts‘ung‐mīng.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐lūt‐laî chìng‐chīk, hóh‐ĭ foon‐uēt yān‐chi‐sam‐chì: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mîng‐lîng shūn‐suì, nāng lîng yān‐chi‐ngaăn‐mūk kwong‐mīng.
9 Kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐tô‐leĭ ts‘ing‐kitₒ, ts‘uēn‐chì‐wĭng‐uĕn: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐lūt‐laî chan‐shāt, ts‘uēn shî kung‐î.
10 Haî pó‐pooì‐kwòh‐kam, pó‐pooì‐kwòh‐hó‐toh tsing‐kam: t‘īm‐kwòh māt‐t‘ōng, t‘īm‐kwòh fung‐fōng tik‐ch‘ut‐chi‐māt‐t‘ōng.
11 Neĭ‐chi‐pūk‐yān ĭ ts‘ź waī kíng‐kaaì: shaú‐chuê ni‐yeûng, tsaû tak taaî‐sheúng.
12 Shuī‐nāng chi‐tò tsẑ‐keí mō‐sam‐chi‐shat? uên neĭ kitₒ‐tsîng ngŏh yán‐meī‐chi‐kwòh.
13 Māt peí pūk‐yān koò‐ì faân‐tsuî; yīk māt yâm‐tsuî tsô ngŏh‐chi‐chué: kóm ngŏh tsaû tuen‐chìng, mĭn faân‐taaî‐tsuî.
14 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî ngŏh‐chi‐p‘oōn‐shēk, haî ngŏh‐chi‐kaù‐shūk‐ché, uên ngŏh haú‐chung īn‐uĕ, sam‐chung ì‐nîm, tak neĭ foon‐heí tsipₒ‐naāp.
