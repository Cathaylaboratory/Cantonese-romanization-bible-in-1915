1 À‐LAĀN wōng‐kè tseung‐kwan Naaĭ‐maân, tsoî k‘uĭ chué mîn‐ts‘īn waī taaî‐yān k‘āp tsuen‐kwaì‐kè, yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng yûng k‘uĭ kaù À‐laān‐yān: k‘uĭ yīk‐haî ying‐yŭng‐kè yān, taân‐haî shan waân fung‐tsāt.
2 Ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn À‐laān‐yān shīng‐kw‘ān ch‘ut‐laī ts‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐teî, lŏ‐leūk yat‐kòh shiù‐nuĭ; kòh‐shiù‐nuĭ fūk‐sẑ Naaĭ‐maân‐kè ts‘ai.
3 Shiù‐nuĭ tuì chué‐mŏ wâ, Hóh‐sik lokₒ! uên ngŏh chué‐yān tak kìn Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐kè sin‐chi, k‘uĭ pit‐nāng i k‘uĭ‐kè fung‐tsāt.
4 Yaŭ yān yāp‐huì tuì chué wâ, Shūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐teî‐kè shiù‐nuĭ yaŭ kóm‐yeûng kóm‐yeûng wâ.
5 À‐laān wōng wâ, Neĭ chikₒ‐koón huì la, ngŏh sé‐sùn t‘ung‐chi Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng. Naaĭ‐maân tsaû huì, taaì ngān yat‐maân‐nğ‐ts‘in, wōng‐kam lūk‐ts‘in, i‐sheūng shāp‐tsāp.
6 Taaì sùn tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng, sùn‐noî wâ, Ngŏh tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐shān Naaĭ‐maân huì kìn neĭ, neĭ tsipₒ‐tò ni‐fung‐sùn, k‘aū i‐hó k‘uĭ‐kè fung‐tsāt.
7 Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng, t‘aí‐uēn sùn, tsaû maakₒ‐līt i‐fūk, wâ, Ngŏh heí haî Sheûng‐Taì, nāng lîng yān shang‐sź me? chì ni‐kòh yān tá‐faatₒ yān laī ngŏh‐ch‘uè, shaí ngŏh i‐hó k‘uĭ‐kè fung‐tsāt, k‘aū neĭ‐teî sai‐sz‐seúng‐hă, k‘uĭ seúng tsè‐tuen heí‐wôh kung‐kik ngŏh che.
8 Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān Ĭ‐leî‐sha t‘eng‐mān Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng maakₒ‐līt i‐fūk, tsaû tá‐faatₒ yān huì kìn wōng, wâ, Neĭ waî‐mat maakₒ‐līt neĭ‐kè i‐fūk ni? hóh‐shaí kóh‐yān laī ngŏh‐ch‘uè, táng k‘uĭ chi‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chung, chan yaŭ sin‐chi haí‐ch‘uè à.
9 Kóm‐tsaû Naaĭ‐maân taaì‐chuê ch‘e‐mă, laī‐tò Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐kè uk, k‘eĭ tsoî moōn‐ts‘īn.
10 Ĭ‐leî‐sha tá‐faatₒ yat‐kòh sz̀‐ché, tuì Naaĭ‐maân wâ, Iù huì tsoì Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh, saí‐shan ts‘at‐ts‘z̀, kóm neĭ shan‐t‘aí tsaû ts‘uēn‐uê, neĭ pit tak‐kitₒ‐tsîng.
11 Naaĭ‐maân tsaû nau‐nô ī‐huì, wâ, Ngŏh koó k‘uĭ pit‐tîng ch‘ut‐laī kìn ngŏh, k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘uĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè mēng, ī‐ch‘é tsoî waân‐sheûng‐ch‘uè iū‐hă‐shaú, tsaû i‐hó kòh‐fung‐tsāt‐kè yān.
12 Taaì‐mă‐shik‐kè hōh, À‐pa‐nā k‘āp Faatₒ‐faatₒ, peí‐kaaù Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐teî kokₒ‐shuí, heí‐m̄‐kàng‐shìng me? ngŏh tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè saí‐shan, heí‐pat‐nāng tak kitₒ‐tsîng me? Ue‐shî făn‐nô, faan‐chuèn‐t‘aū huì.
13 K‘uĭ chùng‐pūk laī tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ngŏh foô à, yeūk sin‐chi fan‐foò neĭ tsô yat‐kîn taaî‐sẑ, neĭ heí‐m̄‐iù‐tsô me? hōh‐fòng k‘uĭ pat‐kwòh kiù neĭ huì saí‐shan, tsaû tak kitₒ‐tsîng ni?
14 Ue‐shî Naaĭ‐maân chiù‐i Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān shóh‐kóng: lōk‐huì tsoî Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh saí‐shan ts‘at‐ts‘z̀, k‘uĭ shan‐kè yūk, p‘īng‐fūk uē‐kaû, hó‐ts‘z̆ so‐tsaí‐kè yūk yat‐yeûng, tsaû tak‐kitₒ‐tsîng.
15 Naaĭ‐maân t‘ūng kan‐ts‘uī‐k‘uĭ‐kè yān, faan‐tò Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān kóh‐ch‘uè, k‘eĭ‐tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn: wâ, T‘aí‐a, uē‐kam ngŏh chi‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt chi‐ngoî, t‘ung‐t‘in‐hâ mŏ Sheûng‐Taì: ts‘éng neĭ shaû neĭ pūk‐kè laĭ‐māt.
16 Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Ngŏh chí‐shang‐oōt‐kè Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘eĭ‐tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn wâ. Ngŏh pit m̄‐shaû. Naaĭ‐maân līk‐huèn k‘uĭ, taân k‘uĭ t‘ui‐ts‘z̄.
17 Naaĭ‐maân wâ, Keì‐īn neĭ m̄‐háng shaû, ts‘éng tseung naī‐t‘ó, tsoì‐ue leŭng‐chekₒ‐luī‐kè pooì‐sheûng, ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ pūk? ts‘ūng‐kam‐ĭ‐haû, ngŏh m̄‐hìn faān‐tsaì tsaì‐māt kwòh‐pīt‐shān, tūk‐haî hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā che.
18 Waī yaŭ yat‐kìn‐sẑ, uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shè‐mīn neĭ pūk, ngŏh chué‐yān yāp Lām‐moōn‐kè tîn k‘aù‐paaì, ngŏh iù foō‐chuê k‘uĭ: kóm ngŏh tsoî Lām‐moōn tîn yīk oò‐tai‐t‘aū, tsoî Lām‐moōn oò‐tai‐t‘aū chi‐shī, ni‐yat‐kin‐sẑ, uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shè‐mīn neĭ pūk.
19 Ĭ‐leî‐sha tsaû tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ p‘īng‐on huì la. Naaĭ‐maân tsaû leī‐hoi‐huì, hāng mŏ keí uĕn.
20 ¶ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐kè pūk K‘eī‐hap‐sai sam‐ì wâ, Ngŏh chué t‘aí‐koò ni‐kòh À‐laān‐yān Naaĭ‐maân, m̄‐háng shaû k‘uĭ shóh‐taaì‐laī‐kè laĭ‐māt: ngŏh chí shang‐oōt kè Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faatₒ‐shaî, ngŏh pit tsaú‐huì chui‐tò k‘uĭ, t‘ūng k‘uĭ lóh ti.
21 K‘eī‐hap‐sai tsaû kan‐chuê Naaĭ‐maân. Naaĭ‐maân kìn yat‐kòh‐yān chui‐laī, tsaû hă‐ch‘e yīng‐tsipₒ k‘uĭ wâ, P‘īng‐on a?
22 Wâ, P‘īng‐on. Ngŏh chué‐yān tá‐faatₒ ngŏh laī tuì neĭ wâ. Uē‐kam yaŭ leŭng‐kòh shiù‐nīn‐yān, haî sin‐chi moōn‐t‘ō, tsẑ Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐shaan‐teî laī kin ngŏh: ts‘éng neĭ ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ ngān yat‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ, k‘āp i‐sheūng î tsāp.
23 Naaĭ‐maân wâ, Ts‘éng neĭ shaû saam‐ts‘in. Tsoì k‘eŭng k‘uĭ shaû, tsaû ning ngān saam‐ts‘in, chong‐tsoì leŭng‐kòh toî‐chung, yaû ning i‐sheūng i tsāp, kaau kwòh leŭng‐kòh pūk‐yān; k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû tsoî K‘eī‐hap‐sai ts‘īn‐t‘aū t‘oī‐huì.
24 Tò shaan shī, K‘eī‐hap‐sai tsaû heùng k‘uĭ‐teî shaú lóh‐hiu, shau‐ts‘ōng ka‐chung: tá‐faatₒ î pūk faan‐huì.
25 K‘eī‐hap‐sai yāp‐huì k‘eĭ‐tsoî k‘uĭ chué‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn. Ĭ‐leî‐sha tuì k‘uĭ wâ, K‘eī‐hap‐sai, neĭ huì pin‐ch‘uè‐laī? Taapₒ‐wâ, Neĭ pūk mŏ‐huì pin‐ch‘uè.
26 Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Kóh‐yān hă‐ch‘e faan‐chuèn‐t‘aū yīng‐tsipₒ‐neĭ chi‐shī, ngŏh sam heí m̄‐t‘ūng neĭ huì me? Ts‘ź shī heí‐hóh shaû ngān, i‐fūk, kóm‐laám‐uēn, p‘ō‐t‘ō‐uēn, ngaū, yeūng, p‘eĭ, pūk me?
27 Yan‐ts‘ź Naaĭ‐maân‐kè fung‐tsāt, pit oo‐ĭm neĭ, k‘āp neĭ haû‐yuî, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn. K‘eī‐hap‐sai tsaû yaū Ĭ‐leî‐sha mîn‐ts‘īn t‘uì‐ch‘ut, shīng‐hiu fung‐tsāt, hó‐ts‘z̆ suetₒ kòm‐paāk.
