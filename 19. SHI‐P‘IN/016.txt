1 SHEÛNG‐TAÌ à, k‘aū neĭ pó‐oô ngŏh: yan ngŏh haî í‐laaî neĭ.
2 Ngŏh‐chi‐līng‐wān, yă‐ts‘āng tuì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Neĭ haî ngŏh Chué: ngŏh‐chi‐fuk‐lūk, mō‐fei neĭ ts‘z̀;
3 Shaì‐sheûng shìng‐īn‐chi‐yān, haî ngŏh shóh‐heí‐lōk.
4 Sùn‐ts‘ūng pīt‐yeûng‐shān‐ché, pit toh‐yau‐shaū: k‘uĭ shóh‐tîn huetₒ tsaì‐tsẑ‐ché, ngŏh m̄‐tsaì‐tsẑ, ngŏh haú m̄‐t‘aī k‘uĭ‐kè mēng.
5 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘aán‐īp, haî ngŏh‐chi‐tsaú‐pooi: neĭ pit lîng ngŏh fuk‐fân tsîm‐taaî.
6 Shóh‐leūng‐kwòh ngŏh chi‐t‘īn, to haî meĭ‐t‘īn; chan‐chìng ngŏh tak hó īp.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kaaù‐fàn ngŏh, ngŏh chan kóm‐tsê: ngŏh‐chi‐sam‐ch‘eūng, yê‐kaan yīk kíng‐kaaì ngŏh.
8 Ngŏh sheūng on‐chì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî ngŏh‐chi‐ts‘īn: yan k‘uĭ haî tsoî ngŏh yaû‐pîn, koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh m̄‐chàn‐tûng.
9 Shóh‐ĭ ngŏh sam foon‐heí, ngŏh līng‐wān faaì‐lōk: ngŏh shan kwai t‘ó, yīk tak on‐kui yaŭ mông.
10 Yan‐waî neĭ pit m̄‐tiu‐heì ngŏh līng‐wān tsoî yam‐kaan; neĭ pit m̄‐peí neĭ shìng‐kitₒ‐chi‐yān faì‐waaî.
11 Neĭ tseung shang‐mîng‐chi‐lô chí‐shî ngŏh: tsoî neĭ‐chi‐ts‘īn, yaŭ foon‐heí moŏn‐tsuk; tsoî neĭ‐chi‐yaû, yaŭ wĭng‐uĕn faaì‐lōk.
