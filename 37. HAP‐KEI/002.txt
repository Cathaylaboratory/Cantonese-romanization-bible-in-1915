1 TONG ts‘at‐uēt î‐shāp‐yat‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, t‘okₒ‐lōk sin‐chi Hap‐kei ch‘uēn‐tò, wâ,
2 Uē‐kam tuì Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè fong‐paakₒ Ch‘itₒ‐t‘itₒ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ, kûng tsaì‐sz‐cheúng Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐taapₒ kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à, uĕ‐k‘āp tsź‐mān uē‐shîng‐kè yān kóng‐wâ,
3 Tsoî neĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè, pin‐kòh ts‘āng‐kìn‐kwòh ni‐kaan‐tîn‐uk ts‘īn‐shī‐kè wīng‐kwong ni? tò uē‐kam neĭ‐teî t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ haî tím‐yeûng ni? heí‐m̄‐haî tsoî‐neĭ‐ngaăn‐chung suèn‐haî‐mō‐waî‐chi‐māt me?
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ à, neĭ uē‐kam sheûng‐ch‘é iù‐kong‐ngaî‐kè; Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsaì‐sz‐cheúng Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐taapₒ‐kè tsaí à, neĭ to‐iù‐kong‐ngaî‐kè; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Kíng‐noî‐kè chùng‐tsź‐mān, yīk‐iù‐kong‐ngaî, yaû iù tsô‐kung‐foo; maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh t‘ūng‐maaī‐neĭ à:
5 Tsun‐i ngŏh ts‘āng‐t‘ūng‐neĭ yeukₒ‐tîng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsik‐haî neĭ ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp, yaŭ ngŏh‐kè shān chuê‐tsoî neĭ chung‐kaan kòh‐shī, neĭ‐teî‐m̄‐hó king‐fong à.
6 Yan‐waî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Hitₒ‐mŏ‐noî, ngŏh pit‐tsoì‐yat‐ts‘z̀ chàn‐tûng‐t‘in‐k‘āp‐teî, hoí‐kûng‐ngôn‐sheûng;
7 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh yīk‐iù‐chàn‐tûng maân‐kwokₒ, yaū maân‐kwokₒ shóh‐ch‘ut‐lōk‐ì‐kè māt, to‐ooĭ‐laī‐tò: ngŏh tsaû k‘aaĭ wīng‐kwong ch‘ung‐moŏn ni‐kaan‐uk‐lokₒ.
8 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngān haî shūk‐ngŏh‐kè kam to‐haî shūk‐ngŏh‐kè.
9 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh pit‐tsoî ni‐taatₒ‐teî‐fong shi‐p‘īng‐on à: ni‐kaan‐uk meĭ‐haû‐kè wīng‐wā, pit‐shìng‐kwòh ts‘īn‐shī‐kè, maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kóm wâ.
10 ¶ Tong Taaî‐leî‐oo wōng taî‐î nīn, î‐shāp‐sz̀‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, t‘okₒ‐sin‐chi Hap‐kei ch‘uēn‐kóng wâ,
11 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Kwaan‐ue lūt‐faatₒ‐chi‐sẑ, chikₒ‐koón‐ts‘éng‐mân‐tsaì‐sz wâ,
12 T‘óng‐yeūk yat‐kòh‐yān tsoî‐shaam‐meĭ taaì‐shìng‐kitₒ‐kè yūk, yaû‐k‘aaĭ‐shaam‐meĭ moh‐kwòh‐péng, waāk t‘ong, waāk tsaú, waāk yaū, waāk pīt‐yeûng‐shīk‐māt, waān‐suèn‐haî shìng‐kitₒ m̄‐ni? Tsaì‐sz tsaû‐taapₒ‐yìng wâ, M̄‐haî‐lokₒ.
13 Ue‐shî Hap‐kei wâ, Uē‐kwóh yat‐kòh‐yān ts‘āng‐moh‐sź‐shi tsô pat‐kitₒ‐kè, tsaû moh‐hă ni‐ti chùng‐māt yat‐kîn, kòh‐kîn‐māt tsik‐haî m̄‐shìng‐kitₒ‐kè, m̄‐haî ni? Tsaì‐sz tsaû‐taapₒ‐wâ, Kòh‐kîn‐māt haî‐m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè lokₒ.
14 Ue‐shî Hap‐kei taapₒ‐wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Tsoî‐ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn ni‐ti‐tsź‐mān haî kóm‐yeûng, ni‐kòh‐kwokₒ to‐haî; k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shaú shóh‐tsô‐kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè kung‐foo to‐haî; ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐hìn‐sheŭng‐kè laĭ‐māt, to‐haî m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè.
15 Uē‐kam ngŏh ts‘éng‐k‘aū neĭ, tsẑ‐ni‐kòh‐yāt‐tsź‐ĭ‐sheûng sz‐seúng‐hă, tsẑ‐yat‐kaû shēk m̄‐ts‘āng tīp‐lōk yat‐kaû‐shēk‐sheûng, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐ch‘uè.
16 King‐līk kòm‐noî shī‐haû, yaŭ‐yān‐laī‐tò wōh‐tui kòh‐shī, mông‐tak î‐shāp shing, kán‐tak shāp shing che: yaŭ‐yān laī‐tò‐tsaú‐chà kòh‐shī, seúng‐tak nğ‐shāp t‘úng, kán‐tak î‐shāp t‘úng che.
17 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh k‘aaĭ sheung‐ts‘aān kûng foô‐laân k‘āp pōk, tsoî‐neĭ‐kokₒ‐yeûng‐kung‐foo kung‐tá neĭ; neĭ sheûng‐ch‘é m̄‐chuèn‐heùng‐ngŏh à.
18 Ngŏh ts‘éng‐k‘aū neĭ tsẑ‐ni‐kòh‐yāt‐tsź‐ĭ‐sheûng‐sz‐seúng‐hă, tsẑ kaú‐uēt î‐shāp‐sz̀‐yāt heí, tsik‐haî tsẑ‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè kei‐chí laāp‐tîng kòh‐yāt, iù‐ts‘ām‐kaù ts‘ź‐sẑ là.
19 Kuk‐chúng yīng‐īn tsoî‐ts‘ong‐lăm me? p‘ō‐t‘aī‐shuê kûng mō‐fa‐kwóh‐shuê, shēk‐laū‐shuê k‘āp kóm‐laám‐shuê, shāt‐haî m̄‐ts‘āng kitₒ‐kwóh là: tsẑ‐kam‐yāt‐ĭ‐haû, ngŏh pit‐chuk‐fuk neĭ à.
20 ¶ Tong‐kòh‐uēt î‐shāp‐sz̀‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, taî‐î‐ts‘z̀ kòng‐lām‐ue Hap‐kei wâ,
21 Iù‐tuì Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè fong‐paakₒ Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ kóng‐wâ, Ngŏh iù‐chàn‐tûng chùng‐t‘in uĕ‐k‘āp teî‐k‘aū;
22 Ngŏh iù‐k‘ing‐titₒ līt‐kwokₒ‐kè tsôh‐waî, yaû mīt‐tsuēt līt‐pong chùng‐kwokₒ‐kè nāng‐līk; yaû iù‐k‘ing‐titₒ chùng‐ch‘e uĕ‐k‘āp ts‘ŏh‐tsoî k‘eī‐sheûng‐kè; kòh‐ti‐mă uĕ‐k‘āp k‘ē‐sheŭng‐kè yān pit‐kòng‐lōk, kokₒ‐yān peî‐hing‐taî‐kè kìm lîng‐pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè à.
23 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh‐kè pūk Ch‘itₒ‐t‘itₒ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ, tong‐kòh‐yāt ngŏh pit‐ts‘uí‐neĭ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, yaû shaí‐neĭ‐tsô‐yàn‐chìng: yan‐waî ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng kaán‐suén neĭ à, maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kóm wâ lokₒ.
