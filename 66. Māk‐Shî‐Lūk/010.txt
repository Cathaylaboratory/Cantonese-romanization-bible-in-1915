1 NGŎH yaû t‘aí‐kìn yaŭ taaì‐līk‐kè t‘in‐sz̀, ts‘ūng t‘in lōk‐laī, cheukₒ‐chuê t‘in‐wān: t‘aū‐sheûng yaŭ t‘in‐hūng, mîn‐maaû hó‐ts‘z̆ īt‐t‘aū, tuì‐keukₒ hó‐ts‘z̆ fóh‐ch‘uĕ:
2 K‘uĭ shaú cha‐chuê siú‐shue, haî k‘itₒ‐hoi‐kè: k‘uĭ yaû‐keukₒ taāp‐chuê hoí, tsóh‐keukₒ taāp‐chuê teî,
3 Taaî‐sheng iu, hó‐ts‘z̆ sz‐tsź p‘aaū‐haau kóm: iu uēn, yaŭ ts‘at‐kòh luī faatₒ‐sheng.
4 Ts‘at‐kòh luī faatₒ‐sheng‐chi‐haû, ngŏh seúng sé‐lōk: taân t‘eng‐mān ts‘ūng t‘in yaŭ sheng tuì ngŏh wâ, Ts‘at‐kòh luī shóh‐kóng‐kè, iù fung‐chuê, maĭ‐sé‐lōk.
5 Ngŏh shóh‐t‘aí‐kìn k‘eĭ‐tsoî‐hoí‐kûng‐teî‐kè t‘in‐sz̀, tsaû kuí‐shaú heùng‐t‘in,
6 Chí‐chuê wĭng‐shang‐kè Chué, tsik‐haî ch‘òng‐tsô t‘in, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung‐chi‐māt, teî, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung‐chi‐māt, hoí, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung‐chi‐māt‐kè, laī faatₒ‐shaî wâ, Ĭ‐haû m̄‐tsoì yaŭ shī‐yāt lokₒ:
7 Taân‐haî tong taî‐ts‘at t‘in‐sz̀ tseung‐kân ch‘ui‐heúng‐kokₒ kòh‐shī, Sheûng‐Taì‐kè ò‐miû shīng‐ts‘uēn, hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘uĭ ĭ‐king chí‐shî‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ pūk, tsik‐haî chùng‐sin‐chi‐kè à.
8 Ngŏh shóh‐t‘eng‐mān ts‘ūng‐t‘in‐kè sheng, tsoì tuì ngŏh wâ, K‘eĭ‐tó‐hoí kûng‐teî‐chi‐t‘in‐sz̀‐kè shaú, shóh‐cha‐chuê ĭ‐king‐k‘itₒ‐hoi‐kè siú‐shue, neĭ huì‐tò tsipₒ‐shaû.
9 Ngŏh tsaû hāng‐maaī t‘in‐sz̀‐ch‘uè, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ts‘éng neĭ k‘aaĭ siú‐shue peí‐kwòh ngŏh. T‘in‐sz̀ taapₒ wâ, Neĭ nim‐laī shīk la; tsaû lîng neĭ t‘ŏ‐luĭ kìn‐foó, taân tsoî neĭ haú‐chung, tsaû hó‐ts‘z̆ māt‐t‘ōng kòm‐t‘īm.
10 Ngŏh yaū t‘in‐sz̀‐kè shaú‐chung, nim siú‐shue shīk‐hiu; kwóh‐īn tsoî haú hó‐ts‘z̆ māt‐t‘ōng kòm‐t‘īm: shīk‐chi‐haû, ngŏh t‘ŏ‐luĭ tsaû foó lokₒ.
11 T‘in‐sz̀ tuì ngŏh wâ, Neĭ pit‐tsoì ch‘uēn uê‐īn laī‐chí toh‐mān toh‐tsūk toh‐yam toh‐wōng‐kè à.
