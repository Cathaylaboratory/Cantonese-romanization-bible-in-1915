1 T‘AÍ‐A, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîng yat‐yāt laī‐tò, tseung‐neĭ‐kè leūk‐māt, tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung k‘aaĭ‐laī‐fan‐wān.
2 Yan‐waî ngŏh‐pit‐tsuî‐maaī‐maân‐mān, kung‐tá Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng; kòh‐shēng‐yap pit‐peî tá‐p‘òh, uk‐shè pit‐peî‐kipₒ‐leūk, foŏ‐yān‐pit‐peî‐k‘eūng‐kaan; kòh‐shēng peî‐leūk‐huì yat‐poòn, waī‐k‘eī‐uē‐kè tsź‐mān, meî‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt ue‐shēng‐chung.
3 Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ooĭ‐ch‘ut‐huì kûng‐kòh‐ti‐kwokₒ chìn‐chaang, hó‐ts‘z̆ lām‐chân kòh‐yāt chìn‐chaang yat‐yeûng.
4 Tong‐kòh‐yāt k‘uĭ‐keukₒ tsaû‐k‘eĭ‐tsoî Kóm‐laám‐shaan‐sheûng, tsik‐haî heùng tung‐pîn Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè ts‘īn‐mîn, Kóm‐laám‐shaan tsẑ‐tung‐chì‐sai, tsoî‐chung‐kaan ooĭ‐līt‐hoi, shīng‐waī yat‐kòh kīk‐taaî‐kè kuk; kòh‐shaan yat‐poòn heùng‐pak‐pin, yat‐poòn‐heùng naām‐pin poon‐huì.
5 Neĭ‐teî pit‐hāng‐kwòh‐ngŏh‐shaan‐kuk t‘ō‐huì; yan‐waî shaan‐chung‐kè kuk, ch‘eūng‐tò À‐saatₒ: neĭ‐teî chan‐chìng ooĭ‐t‘ō‐tsaú, hó‐ts‘z̆ Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Oo‐sai‐à shī, t‘ō‐peî‐teî‐chàn‐yat‐yeûng: ī‐ch‘é Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ngŏh‐kè Sheûng‐Taì to‐ooĭ‐laī, taaî‐chùng shìng‐ché to‐t‘ūng‐maaī‐t‘im.
6 Tong kòh‐yāt shīng‐tsaû haî kóm, kòh‐kwong faatₒ‐ch‘ut, yaû‐m̄‐tak‐mīng, yaû m̄‐tak‐òm:
7 Waī‐haî shīng‐waī yat‐yāt, haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐chi‐kè, m̄‐haî paāk‐yāt, yīk‐m̄‐haî hak‐yê: taân tseung‐pit‐haî‐kóm, tò‐aai‐maăn‐shī‐haû, tsaû yaŭ kwong lokₒ.
8 Tong kòh‐yāt shīng‐tsaû haî‐kóm, yaŭ‐oōt‐shuí yaū‐Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng faatₒ‐ch‘ut; yat‐poòn‐laū‐heùng‐tung‐hoí, yat‐poòn‐laū‐heùng‐sai‐hoí: tsoî‐hâ‐t‘in, yaû tsoî‐tung‐t‘in‐kòh‐shī, to‐haî‐kóm.
9 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yīk tsoî‐ts‘uēn‐teî‐chi‐sheûng tsô‐wōng: tong kòh‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit‐haî‐tūk‐yat, k‘uĭ‐kè mēng to‐haî‐tūk‐yat kà.
10 Ts‘uēn‐teî tsẑ‐Kei‐pa chì‐Lām‐moōn, haí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng naām‐pin, ooĭ‐pìn‐shīng À‐la‐paakₒ yat‐yeûng: pit‐peî‐ko‐kuí kui‐tsoî‐poón‐waî, tsẑ Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐chi‐moōn, chì‐taî‐yat‐moōn‐ch‘uè, yaû chì‐shēng‐kokₒ‐chi‐moōn, yaû tsẑ‐Hap‐nā‐īp‐kè shaú‐laū, tò‐wōng‐kè tsaú‐chà.
11 Yīk‐yaŭ‐yān kui‐chuê tsoî‐noî, m̄‐tsoì‐yaŭ‐chaù‐chòh; Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng yīk‐pit‐on‐īn‐kui‐chuê lokₒ.
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐shaí‐kè tsoi‐wôh, laī‐kung‐kik kòh‐ti‐kung‐kik Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè chùng‐mān, haî‐kóm‐yeûng; K‘uĭ‐k‘eĭ‐chīk‐keukₒ‐kòh‐shī, k‘uĭ‐yūk‐ooĭ‐shui‐paaî, k‘uĭ‐ngaăn‐tsoî‐hong‐ch‘uè, yīk‐ooĭ‐shui‐paaî, k‘uĭ t‘iū‐leî yīk‐tsoî‐haú‐chung shui‐paaî.
13 Tong‐kòh‐yāt shīng‐tsaû haî kóm, yaŭ taaî‐iŭ‐luên, yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lām‐tò‐k‘uĭ‐teî chung‐kaan; k‘uĭ‐teî‐kokₒ‐yān ooĭ‐cha‐chuê‐lūn‐shè‐kè shaú, k‘uĭ‐shaú yaû‐hing‐heí kung‐kik lūn‐shè‐kè shaú.
14 Yaū‐t‘aaì to‐ooĭ kûng‐Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng chaang‐chìn; ī‐ch‘é sz̀‐waī chùng‐kwokₒ‐kè ts‘oī‐paāk, tsuî‐maaī‐yat‐ch‘uè, tsik‐haî taaî‐toh‐i‐sheūng kûng kam‐ngān.
15 Kim‐ch‘é yaŭ‐tsoi‐wôh lām‐tò‐lōh, luī, mă, kûng lokₒ‐t‘ōh, uĕ‐k‘āp tsoî‐yīng‐p‘oōn shóh‐yaŭ ch‘uk‐shang‐chi‐sheûng, haî‐chiù‐faan ni‐ti‐tsoi‐wôh yat‐yeûng.
16 Tseung‐pit‐haî‐kóm, sheŭng‐laī kung‐tá Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè chùng‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung, shóh‐laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè kokₒ‐yān, iù nīn‐nīn sheŭng‐huì kìng‐paaì kòh‐Wōng, tsik‐haî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yaû‐shaú‐kitₒ‐maaū‐tsitₒ.
17 Yīk‐pit‐haî‐kóm, tsoî‐teî‐sheûng maân‐ka‐chi‐chung, taân‐faān m̄‐sheŭng‐huì Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng kìng‐paaì kòh‐Wōng, tsik‐maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, pit‐mŏ‐uĕ‐lōk k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè à.
18 Uē‐kwóh Oi‐k‘āp kòh‐ka m̄‐sheŭng‐huì, yaû m̄‐laī‐tò, tsaû mŏ‐uĕ‐lōk k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè; ī‐ch‘é pit‐yaŭ‐tsoi‐wôh, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yûng‐laī kung‐tá kòh‐ti m̄‐sheŭng‐huì shaú‐kitₒ‐maaū‐tsitₒ‐kè līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐tsoi‐wôh.
19 Ts‘ź‐haî Oi‐k‘āp‐chi‐yīng‐fāt, yaû‐haî kòh‐ti‐m̄‐sheŭng‐huì shaú‐kitₒ‐maaū‐tsitₒ‐kè chùng‐kwokₒ‐chi‐yīng‐fāt.
20 Tong‐kòh‐yāt, mă‐kè līng‐sheûng pit‐sé‐lōk, wâ, SHÌNG‐KITₒ KWAI‐UE YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐t‘ōng‐chi‐chung‐kè p‘oōn, to‐shīng‐waī t‘aān‐ts‘īn‐kè chung kóm;
21 Ī‐ch‘é tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè p‘oōn, chekₒ‐chekₒ shīng‐waī shìng‐kitₒ, kwai‐ue maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; taaî‐chùng hìn‐tsaì‐kè yān, ooĭ‐tseung‐k‘uĭ k‘aaĭ‐laī chué‐yūk: yaû tong‐yāt pit‐m̄‐tsoì yaŭ Ka‐naām‐yān, tsoî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐shat‐chi‐chung lokₒ.
