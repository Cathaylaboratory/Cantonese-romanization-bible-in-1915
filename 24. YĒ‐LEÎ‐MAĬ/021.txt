1 YAŪ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐kè īn‐uĕ, tsik‐haî tong Sai‐taí‐ka wōng tá‐faatₒ Mă‐k‘eī‐à‐kè tsaí Pa‐shūt, kûng tsaì‐sz Mă‐sai‐ngă‐kè tsaí Sai‐faan‐ngă tò k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè kòh‐chân‐shī, wâ,
2 Ts‘éng neĭ toî‐ngŏh‐teî‐k‘aū‐mân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; yan Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ tá‐cheùng kung‐kik‐ngŏh‐teî; waāk‐ché Yē‐Wōh‐Wā háng chiù‐i k‘uĭ k‘eī‐nāng‐kè tsokₒ‐waī, lîng Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng leī‐hoi ngŏh‐teî sheŭng‐huì la.
3 ¶ Ue‐shî Yē‐leî‐maĭ tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù‐tuì Sai‐taí‐ka kóm‐kóng:
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh tseung lîng‐neĭ‐shaú shóh‐cha‐chuê‐kè kwan‐heì faán‐chuèn t‘uì‐huì, tsik‐haî neĭ‐teî yûng‐laī kung‐kik Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng, k‘āp kung‐kik tsoî shēng‐moōn‐ngoî waī‐kw‘àn neĭ‐ti Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè, ngŏh tsaû tsuî‐maaī k‘uĭ‐teî yāp‐ts‘ź‐shēng‐chi‐chung.
5 Ngŏh tsẑ‐keí ooĭ‐tseung shan‐hoi‐kè‐shaú, kûng nāng‐līk‐kè shaú‐peì; ī‐ch‘é faatₒ‐nô, yaû făn‐hân, k‘āp taaî‐līt‐nô, heùng‐neĭ‐teî tá‐cheùng.
6 Ngŏh to‐iù kung‐tá ts‘ź‐shēng‐kè kui‐mān, līn‐yān kûng ch‘uk‐shang: k‘uĭ‐teî tseung‐waân taaî‐wan‐yīk, tsaû sź‐mōng lokₒ.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Tsoî‐haû ngŏh pit‐tseung Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka, kûng k‘uĭ pūk‐yān, uĕ‐k‘āp tsź‐mān, tsik‐haî tsoî ts‘ź‐shēng‐chung yaū wan‐yīk, yaū to‐kìm, yaū kei‐fong; shóh‐uē‐shîng‐kè, kaau‐lōk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ‐kè shaú, ī‐ch‘é kaau‐k‘uĭ‐teî lōk ch‘aū‐tīk‐kè shaú, uĕ‐k‘āp chaaú‐ts‘ām seúng‐hoî‐k‘uĭ‐shang‐mîng‐chi‐yān‐kè shaú: k‘uĭ pit‐k‘aaĭ‐kìm‐kè leî‐yân laī‐kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐teî; tsúng‐m̄‐oì‐sik, m̄‐hóh‐līn, m̄‐king‐sut k‘uĭ‐teî.
8 Ī‐ch‘é neĭ iù‐heùng ni‐ti‐tsź‐mān wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh tseung shang‐mîng‐kè lô, kûng sź‐mōng‐kè lô, on‐chì tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn.
9 Faān‐haî chuê‐tsoî ts‘ź‐shēng‐chi‐chung‐kè, tsaû peî‐to‐kìm, kûng wan‐yīk, k‘āp kei‐fong, laī‐sź‐mōng: waī‐haî ch‘ut‐huì kwai‐shūk waī‐kw‘àn‐neĭ‐ti Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè, kòh‐yān pit‐tak‐shang, k‘uĭ shang‐mîng heùng k‘uĭ shīng‐waī leūk‐māt.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Yan ngŏh tîng‐shāt‐mîn heùng‐ts‘ź‐shēng, m̄‐haî waî leî‐yik, haî waî tsoi‐wôh: tsaû pit‐tseung laī‐kaau‐kwòh Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè shaú, k‘uĭ pit‐yûng‐fóh shiu‐waaî ts‘ź‐shēng lokₒ.
11 ¶ Lûn‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng‐chi ka, iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè‐īn‐uĕ;
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Taaî‐waî‐chi‐ka à; tong chiu‐tsó iù‐hāng shám‐p‘oòn, yaû kaù peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐yān ch‘ut haak‐pōk‐yān‐kè shaú, mĭn‐chì ngŏh‐kè făn‐hân yan neĭ hāng‐waī‐kè okₒ‐ts‘īng, hó‐ts‘z̆‐fóh kóm‐faatₒ‐ch‘ut, yaû shiu‐heí chì‐tò mŏ‐yān nāng sik‐mīt à.
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ; Kuk‐chung‐kè kui‐mān, kûng p‘īng‐uēn p‘oōn‐shēk‐kè kui‐mān, tsaû wâ, Mat‐shuī nāng‐lōk‐laī kung‐kik ngŏh‐teî ni? waāk mat‐shuī yāp‐tak ngŏh‐teî‐kè chuê‐shóh ni? T‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik neĭ.
14 Ngŏh pit‐chiù‐i‐neĭ‐hāng‐waī shóh‐kitₒ‐shāt‐kè, laī‐yīng‐fāt neĭ: yaû t‘aù‐fóh tsoî‐ts‘ź‐teî‐kè shuê‐lām, ts‘ź‐fóh ooĭ‐t‘an‐mīt‐saaì k‘uĭ sz̀‐waī shóh‐yaŭ‐chi‐māt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ à.
