1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tseung peî‐lŏ‐chi‐Sun‐mān, shik‐fòng faan‐kwai, kòh‐shī ngŏh‐teî hó‐ts‘z̆ faatₒ‐mûng yat‐yeûng.
2 Tong ts‘ź chi‐shī, moŏn‐haú foon‐siù, moŏn tsuí koh‐ch‘eùng: līt‐pong chung‐yaŭ yān wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waî k‘uĭ paakₒ‐sìng hāng‐ch‘ut taaî‐sẑ.
3 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā keì waî ngŏh‐teî hāng‐ch‘ut taaî‐sẑ; ngŏh‐teî tsaû kìn‐foon‐heí.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh‐teî peî‐lŏ‐chi‐yān, k‘aū neĭ fòng‐saaì faan‐laī, hó‐ts‘z̆ naām‐fong‐hōh‐shuí fūk‐laū.
5 Laū‐luî laī‐saatₒ‐chúng‐ché, pit‐tîng faaì‐lōk laī‐shau‐kotₒ.
6 Faān ch‘ut‐huì yat‐lô‐huk, nim‐chuê kuk‐chúng, pit faan‐laī yat‐sam foon‐heí, taam‐chuê wōh‐pá.
