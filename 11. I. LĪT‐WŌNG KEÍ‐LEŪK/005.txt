1 T‘UI‐LŌH wōng Hei‐laān, tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ pūk huì kìn Shóh‐lōh‐moōn; yan‐waî t‘eng‐mān chùng‐yān ĭ‐king kò k‘uĭ kaì foô‐ts‘an tsô wōng, yan Hei‐laān p‘īng‐sò oì Taaî‐waî.
2 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tsaû tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché huì kìn Hei‐laān, wâ,
3 Neĭ chi ngŏh foô‐ts‘an Taaî‐waî pat‐nāng waî k‘uĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mēng, kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk, yan‐waî sz̀‐waī‐kè sz̀‐fong to yaŭ chaang‐chìn, chì‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaí chùng‐ch‘aū‐tīk chai‐tsoî k‘uĭ keukₒ‐hâ.
4 Taân uē‐kam ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ĭ‐king ts‘z̀ ngŏh sz̀‐fong p‘īng‐on, chì mō ch‘aū‐tīk, kûng mō tsoi‐wôh.
5 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh yaŭ laāp‐ì kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk, waî ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mēng ch‘itₒ‐laāp, chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐kwòh ngŏh foô‐ts‘an Taaî‐waî, wâ, Neĭ tsaí, tsik‐haî ngŏh tseung lîng kaì‐chuê neĭ ts‘ŏh‐waî‐kè pit waî ngŏh mēng kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk.
6 Shóh‐ĭ neĭ uē‐kam iù fan‐foò yān tsoî Leî‐pa‐nuên, waî ngŏh chaám paakₒ‐heung‐shuê; ngŏh pūk to pit t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ pūk: ngŏh yīk chiù‐i neĭ faān‐shóh‐tîng‐kè, tseung neĭ pūk‐kè kung‐kà kaau‐kwòh neĭ: yan‐waî neĭ chi‐tò ngŏh chi‐chung, mŏ yān ts‘z̆ Sai‐tûn‐yān kòm haaú‐shaú chaám mūk à.
7 Hei‐laān t‘eng‐mān Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐chi‐īn, tsaû shâm‐foon‐heí, yaû wâ, Kam‐yāt iù chuk‐tsê tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yan k‘uĭ ts‘āng ts‘z̀ yat‐kòh‐yaŭ‐chì‐waî‐kè tsaí kwòh‐Taaî‐waî, shaí k‘uĭ koón‐leĭ uē‐ts‘ź kòm‐toh tsź‐mān à.
8 Hei‐laān tá‐faatₒ yān huì kìn Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, wâ, Neĭ tá‐faatₒ yān tuì ngŏh kóng‐kè‐sẑ, ngŏh ĭ‐king t‘eng lokₒ: lûn paakₒ‐heung‐mūk kûng ts‘ūng‐mūk, ngŏh pit chiù‐i neĭ faān shóh‐uên‐kè laī‐tsô.
9 Ngŏh pūk‐yān tseung ts‘ź‐mūk, yaū Leî‐pa‐nuên poon‐wân tò‐hoí‐ch‘uè: ī‐ch‘é tseung‐laī chaatₒ‐shīng p‘aaī, faū‐tsoî hoí‐mîn, chì neĭ shóh‐tîng‐kwòh ngŏh kóh‐ch‘uè, ngŏh tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè tsaû ch‘aakₒ‐hoi peí neĭ shau la: neĭ ts‘z̀ shīk‐māt kwòh‐ngŏh ka, kóm‐tsaû shīng ngŏh shóh‐uên lokₒ.
10 Kóm‐tsaû Hei‐laān k‘aaĭ paakₒ‐heung‐mūk, k‘āp ts‘ūng‐mūk, ts‘z̀‐kwòh Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, chiù‐i k‘uĭ faān‐shóh‐uên‐kè.
11 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn ts‘z̀‐kwòh Hei‐laān māk î‐maân koh‐ĭ: yaû ts‘z̀ ts‘ing‐yaū î‐shāp koh‐ĭ tsô k‘uĭ ka‐kè shīk‐māt: Shóh‐lōh‐moōn mooĭ nīn, yīk haî kóm ts‘z̀‐kwòh Hei‐laān.
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung chì‐waî ts‘z̀‐kwòh Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, chiù‐i k‘uĭ shóh‐ying‐shīng‐kè: yaû Hei‐laān kûng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn yaŭ t‘aaì‐p‘īng; leŭng‐yān taaî‐ka laāp‐yeukₒ.
13 ¶ Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng ts‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐chung, chiu‐mô kung‐tseûng‐kè yān; kung‐tseûng‐yān‐kè shò yaŭ saam‐maân.
14 Mooĭ uēt lūn‐laū tá‐faatₒ yat‐maân yān huì Leî‐pa‐nuên: k‘uĭ‐teî yat‐uēt tsoî Leî‐pa‐nuên, leŭng‐uēt tsoî ka: À‐toh‐neī‐laān tsô tuk‐leĭ kung‐tseûng‐kè yān.
15 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn yaŭ taam‐t‘oī‐kè yān ts‘at‐maân, yaû‐yaŭ tsoî shaan chaám‐mūk‐kè paatₒ‐maân‐yān;
16 Ts‘ź‐ngoî Shóh‐lōh‐moōn yaŭ tsúng‐leĭ kung‐tseûng‐kè saam‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ‐yān, tsik‐haî tuk‐leĭ tsô‐kung‐tseûng‐kè tsź‐mān.
17 Wōng ch‘ut mîng‐lîng, tsaû chùng‐yān poon‐wân taaì‐shēk, kûng kwaì‐chûng‐kè shēk, tsōk shīng‐kè shēk, tseung‐laī kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk‐kè ts‘eūng‐kei.
18 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn kìn‐chuk‐kè yān, kûng Hei‐laān kìn‐chuk‐kè yān, k‘āp Kat‐leî‐yān tsōk‐hó kóh‐ti shēk: tsaû uê‐peî mūk kûng shēk, kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk à.
