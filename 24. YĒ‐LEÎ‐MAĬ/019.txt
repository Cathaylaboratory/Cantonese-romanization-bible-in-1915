1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ kóm wâ, Iù huì‐maaĭ t‘ō‐yān‐kè ngă‐tsun yat‐kòh, yaû taaì‐maaī tsź‐mān‐kè lŏ‐taaî, uĕ‐k‘āp tsaì‐sz‐kè lŏ‐taaî;
2 Yaû ch‘ut‐huì tò Pîn‐yan‐nuên‐chi‐kuk‐ch‘uè, tsik‐haî kân‐yāp Hâ‐sik‐moōn kòh‐ch‘uè, tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè suen‐ch‘uēn ngŏh shóh‐kóng‐kwòh‐neĭ‐kè īn‐uĕ,
3 Yaû wâ, Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè līt‐wōng, uĕ‐k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān à, iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ; maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh tseung lîng tsoi‐wôh kòng‐lōk‐ni‐ch‘uè, faān‐shóh‐t‘eng‐mān‐kè, k‘uĭ‐ĭ pit‐kokₒ‐tak‐heúng.
4 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ waī‐heì‐ngŏh, yaû lîng ts‘ź‐teî leī‐shoh ngŏh, yaû heùng‐pīt‐î‐kòh‐shān shiu‐heung, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ kûng k‘uĭ‐līt‐tsó k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè līt‐wōng meî‐shik‐tak kè, yaû tseung mō‐koo‐chi‐yān‐kè huetₒ ch‘ung‐moŏn ts‘ź‐teî;
5 Yaû waî Pa‐līk kìn‐heí shūng‐yau, yaû seúng‐k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ chùng‐tsaí tsoî fóh shiu‐waaî tsô‐faān‐tsaì, laī‐hìn‐kwòh Pa‐līk, ts‘ź‐sẑ haî ngŏh meî‐fan‐foò, yaû meî‐kóng‐heí, yīk meî‐tsoî sam‐chung faatₒ‐ch‘ut ì‐sz̀‐kè:
6 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, yāt‐tsź laī‐kân, ni‐ch‘uè m̄‐tsoì ch‘ing T‘ōh‐faatₒ, waāk Pîn‐yan‐nuên‐chi‐kuk.
7 Ngŏh tseung tsoî ni‐ch‘uè lîng Yaū‐t‘aaì k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng shóh‐maū‐tîng‐kè sẑ shīng‐waī hung‐hui; yaû lîng k‘uĭ tsoî ch‘aū‐tīk mîn‐ts‘īn peî‐kìm kung‐kik, pūk‐tó seúng‐ts‘aān‐hoî k‘uĭ‐kè yān‐chi‐shaú‐ch‘uè: ngŏh to tseung k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shi‐t‘aí peí‐kwòh t‘in‐hung‐kè tseukₒ‐niŭ, kûng teî‐sheûng‐kè tsaú‐shaù tsô‐shīk‐māt.
8 Ngŏh tseung lîng ts‘ź‐shēng pìn‐waī king‐hoī‐chi‐shēng, peî‐yān‐faatₒ‐ch‘i‐sheng; faān‐haî king‐kwòh‐kè pit‐king‐k‘eī, yan k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè tsoi‐wôh laī‐faatₒ‐ch‘i‐sheng.
9 Ngŏh tseung lîng k‘uĭ‐teî shīk‐tsẑ‐keí‐tsaí‐kè yūk, uĕ‐k‘āp tsẑ‐keí‐nuĭ‐kè yūk; ī‐ch‘é tong shaû waī‐kw‘àn pik‐naân‐kòh‐shī, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ ch‘aū‐tīk, uĕ‐k‘āp chaaú‐ts‘ām seúng‐hoî k‘uĭ shang‐mîng‐kè, pik‐naân k‘uĭ kòh‐shī, kokₒ‐yān ooĭ‐shīk p‘āng‐yaŭ‐kè yūk.
10 Ue‐shî neĭ iù‐tsoî t‘ūng‐maaī‐neĭ‐huì‐kè yān ngaăn‐ts‘īn‐chíng‐laân‐kòh‐tsun,
11 Yaû tuì‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh to‐seúng chíng‐waaî ni‐ti‐tsź‐mān kûng ni‐kòh‐shēng, hó‐ts‘z̆ yat‐kòh‐yān chíng‐waaî t‘ō‐yān‐kè ngă‐heì, chì‐tò pat‐nāng tsoì‐pó‐faan hó‐kè; ī‐ch‘é‐k‘uĭ‐teî tseung‐tsoî T‘ōh‐faatₒ maaī‐tsòng, chì‐tò mŏ‐teî‐fong maaī‐tsòng kè.
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh pit‐heùng‐ts‘ź‐teî uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung kui‐mān chiù‐kóm‐yeûng, tsaû lîng ts‘ź‐shēng hó‐ts‘z̆ T‘ōh‐faatₒ‐kóm:
13 Ī‐ch‘é Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè uk‐uĕ, uĕ‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì līt‐wōng‐kè uk‐uĕ, ĭ‐king peî‐tìm‐oo‐kè, pit‐shīng‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ T‘ōh‐faatₒ‐kè teî, tsik‐haî tsoî ngă‐mîn heùng‐chùng‐t‘in‐kwan shiu‐heung, yaû tîn‐tsaú heùng‐pīt‐î‐kòh‐shān‐kè chùng‐uk.
14 ¶ Ue‐shî Yē‐leî‐maĭ ts‘ūng T‘ōh‐faatₒ tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tá‐faatₒ‐k‘uĭ‐huì kóng‐uê‐īn kóh‐ch‘uè faan‐laī; k‘eĭ‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè uên‐chung; tuì‐chùng‐tsź‐mān kóng, wâ,
15 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh tseung‐pit‐lîng‐ngŏh heùng‐k‘uĭ shóh‐kóng‐ch‘ut‐kè tsoi‐wôh kòng‐lām‐ue ts‘ź‐shēng, uĕ‐k‘āp shūk‐k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐shēng‐yap‐chi‐sheûng, yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî chíng‐ngaâng‐k‘uĭ‐kéng, táng‐k‘uĭ‐m̄‐t‘eng‐kìn ngŏh‐īn‐uĕ à.
