1 TSOKₒ‐OKₒ‐CHI‐YĀN, neĭ m̄‐shaí făn‐nô k‘uĭ, pat‐î‐chi‐yān, neĭ m̄‐shaí tò‐keî k‘uĭ.
2 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ó, mŏ noî iù chaám‐kotₒ, yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘oì, pat‐kaú iù kon‐foo.
3 Tong í‐laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā laī hāng shîn: tsaû hóh‐chuê ts‘ź‐teî, leūng‐shīk‐tak‐tsuk.
4 Tong k‘aaĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā laī‐foon‐heí; k‘uĭ pit chiù neĭ sam shóh‐k‘aū‐ché ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ.
5 Tseung neĭ‐chi‐lô kaau‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; ī‐ch‘é í‐laaî k‘uĭ; neĭ‐chi‐sẑ shî‐pit shīng‐tsaû.
6 K‘uĭ pit hín‐în neĭ‐chi‐shîn‐î, hó‐ts‘z̆ kwong yat‐yeûng, pit piú‐mīng neĭ‐chi‐òn‐ts‘īng, hó‐ts‘z̆ aàn‐chaù‐chi‐īt‐t‘aū.
7 Neĭ tong paì‐haú í‐k‘aaù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yán‐noî laī‐táng‐haû: neĭ m̄‐hó yan okₒ‐yān shûn‐leî, okₒ‐maū shīng‐tsaû, laī‐heí făn‐nô.
8 Neĭ m̄‐shaí nau, m̄‐shaí nô: m̄‐hó‐sam‐waaī pat‐p‘īng, húng pìn‐waī okₒ.
9 Yan‐waî tsokₒ‐okₒ‐ché, pit‐peî‐tsiú‐mīt: í‐laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ché, pit‐tak t‘ó‐teî.
10 Hitₒ mŏ‐keí‐noî, okₒ‐yān pit kwai oo‐yaŭ: neĭ huì k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè, yīk wán‐m̄‐kìn.
11 Waī‐haî him‐sùn‐chi‐yān, pit tak t‘ó‐teî; toh heúng p‘īng‐on, kīk k‘eī faaì‐lōk.
12 Okₒ‐yān ch‘itₒ‐kaì, seúng hoî shîn‐yān, heùng‐chuê shîn‐yān, ngaaŭ‐ngā‐ts‘it‐ch‘í.
13 Chué pit siù k‘uĭ: yan kìn k‘uĭ shaû pò‐chi yāt‐tsź laī‐kán.
14 Okₒ‐yān laai‐kung hoi‐kìm, seúng‐shê tó p‘ān‐foó‐chi‐yān, seúng shaatₒ‐lūk chìng‐chīk‐chi‐yān.
15 Shuī‐chi k‘uĭ kìm faán‐kat tsẑ‐keí sam, k‘uĭ kung pit‐tîng peî‐chitₒ‐t‘uĕn.
16 Shîn‐yān shóh‐yaŭ sui siú, chûng‐shìng‐kwòh chùng‐okₒ‐yān‐chi foò‐shîng.
17 Yan‐waî okₒ‐yān shaú‐peì, shî‐pit chitₒ‐t‘uĕn, waī‐haî shîn‐yān, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit foō‐ch‘ī.
18 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chi‐tò shîn‐î‐yān‐chi‐suì‐uēt: k‘uĭ ch‘aán‐īp pit laū‐ts‘uēn tò wĭng‐uĕn.
19 K‘uĭ tsoî waân‐naân‐chi‐shī, m̄‐shaí sau‐kw‘aì: tsoî kei‐fong‐chi‐yāt, pit‐tîng tak paaú.
20 Okₒ‐yān pit‐tîng paaî‐waaî, Chué‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, hó‐ts‘z̆ yeūng‐chi‐ko‐yaū: yūng‐î shiu‐fà; hó‐ts‘z̆ faū‐in.
21 Okₒ‐yān yaŭ‐tsè mŏ‐waān: shîn‐yān ts‘z̄‐pei, foon‐heí shi‐shé.
22 Yan‐waî mūng Sheûng‐Taì chuk‐fuk‐ché, pit tak t‘ó‐teî; shaû Sheûng‐Taì chaù‐chòh‐ché, pit peî mīt‐tsuēt.
23 Î‐yān‐chi‐keukₒ‐pô, haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐tîng: koò‐ts‘ź foon‐heí hāng k‘uĭ‐chi‐lô.
24 Sui shat‐keukₒ pat‐chì pūk‐tó: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yûng shaú foō‐chuê.
25 Ngŏh tsẑ‐yaù chì‐lŏ; meî‐kìn‐kwòh î‐yān peî‐tiu‐heì, k‘uĭ tsź‐suen hat‐shīk ché.
26 Shîn‐yān shīng‐yāt līn‐sut yān, yaû tsè‐t‘aaì kwòh yān; k‘uĭ tsź‐suen pit‐tîng tak‐fuk.
27 Neĭ tong koí‐okₒ ts‘ūng‐shîn; tsaû tak wĭng‐uĕn on‐kui.
28 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hò shîn‐î, m̄‐tiu‐heì kìng‐k‘īn‐chi‐yān; kìng‐k‘īn‐chi‐yān, wĭng‐uĕn mūng k‘uĭ peì‐yaû: waī‐haî okₒ‐yān tsź‐suen, pit‐peî‐mīt‐tsuēt.
29 Shîn‐yān pit‐tak t‘ó‐teî, wĭng‐uĕn on‐kui.
30 Shîn‐yān haú‐shuetₒ chì‐waî, shīt kóng kung‐î.
31 Sheûng‐Taì lūt‐faatₒ, tsoî k‘uĭ sam‐chung: k‘uĭ‐chi‐keukₒ‐pô, mō‐pat‐kin‐wán.
32 Okₒ‐yān chong‐chuê shîn‐yān, seúng shaatₒ‐lūk k‘uĭ.
33 Waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, pit‐m̄‐peí k‘uĭ lōk‐tsoî okₒ‐yān‐chi‐shaú, tò shám‐p‘oòn shī, m̄‐chap‐chaakₒ k‘uĭ.
34 Neĭ tong yeŭng‐mông Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsun‐shaú k‘uĭ lô‐t‘ō, k‘uĭ tsaû tseung neĭ shing‐ko, lîng tak t‘ó‐teî: yaû ts‘an‐ngaăn t‘aí‐kìn okₒ‐yān‐chi‐shaû‐mīt.
35 Ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng kìn okₒ‐yān k‘eūng‐pô, k‘uĭ hing‐wông, hó‐ts‘z̆ shuê tsoî uēn‐naī‐chi‐maû‐shîng.
36 Shuī‐chi k‘uĭ kwòh‐huì, kwai‐ue oo‐yaŭ: ngŏh huì wán k‘uĭ, to wán‐m̄‐kìn.
37 Neĭ shì‐ch‘ā‐hă uēn‐ts‘uēn‐chi‐yān, t‘aí‐hă chìng‐chīk‐chi‐yān: wōh‐p‘īng‐chi‐yān, k‘eī fuk tsoî‐haû.
38 Waī‐haî pooì‐yīk‐ché, pit tsûn‐teî paaî‐mōng: hāng‐okₒ‐ché mīt‐tsuēt mō‐haû.
39 Shîn‐yān pit laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘íng‐kaù: tong kaan‐naān shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô k‘uĭ‐chi‐līk.
40 K‘uĭ í‐laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā foō‐chôh k‘uĭ, ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ: lîng k‘uĭ t‘uetₒ‐leī okₒ‐yān.
