1 KÒH‐SHĪ Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè kokₒ‐tsūk‐cheúng, laī tò tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ kûng Nuên‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à, pìng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chi‐p‘aaì‐kè kokₒ‐tsūk‐cheúng;
2 Tsoî Ka‐naām‐teî Shî‐lōh kóh‐ch‘uè, tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ Moh‐sai shaú, fan‐foò k‘aaĭ keí‐tsôh‐shēng, ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh‐teî kui‐chuê, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, fòng yeŭng ch‘uk‐shang.
3 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsun Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mîng, ts‘ūng tsẑ‐keí ch‘aán‐īp‐chung shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè shēng, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, līt mīng tsoî‐haû.
4 ¶ Waî Koh‐hàt kokₒ‐tsūk chap‐ch‘aū: tsaì‐sz À‐lūn‐kè tsź‐suen, shūk Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè, ts‘ūng Yaū‐taaî, Sai‐mîn, Pîn‐ngă‐măn, saam chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung, kûng‐chap tak shāp‐saam shēng.
5 ¶ Koh‐hāt k‘eī‐uē‐kè haû‐toî, ts‘ūng Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè kokₒ‐tsūk, ts‘ūng Taân‐kè chi‐p‘aaì, Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì teî‐īp‐chung, kûng‐chap‐tak shāp shēng.
6 ¶ Kaakₒ‐shûn‐kè haû‐toî, ts‘ūng Ĭ‐saatₒ‐ka chi‐p‘aaì‐kè kokₒ‐tsūk, À‐ch‘itₒ‐kè chi‐p‘aaì, Naāp‐taaî‐leî‐kè chi‐p‘aaì chuê Pa‐shaan‐kè Mă‐nā‐sai poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì teî‐īp‐chung, kûng‐chap‐tak shāp‐saam shēng.
7 ¶ Maĭ‐la‐leĭ‐kè haû‐toî, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî kokₒ‐tsūk, ts‘ūng Laū‐pîn, Ka‐tak, Sai‐pò‐lūn, saam chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung kûng‐chap‐tak shāp‐î shēng.
8 ¶ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ Moh‐sai shaú shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè, tsaû k‘aaĭ ni‐ti shēng, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ laī chap‐ch‘aū, fan ts‘z̀‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān.
9 Yaû ts‘ūng Yaū‐taaî, Sai‐mîn î‐chi‐p‘aaì teî‐īp‐chung, shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè shēng, līt mīng tsoî‐haû,
10 Tsik‐haî Leî‐meî chi‐p‘aaì‐chung, Koh‐hāt tsūk, À‐lūn‐kè tsź‐suen shóh‐tak‐kè: yan‐waî chap cheūk taî‐yat‐fân ch‘aū kwai k‘uĭ à.
11 Tsaû k‘aaĭ À‐naāp tsūk‐kè tsó À‐pa shēng, k‘āp shēng sz̀‐waī‐kè kaau‐yĕ, peí‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî, ts‘ź shēng tsik‐haî Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn, haí Yaū‐taaî‐shaan‐ch‘uè‐kè.
12 Taân‐haî shūk shēng‐kè t‘īn‐maŭ kûng ts‘uen‐heung, to ts‘z̀‐peí Yē‐foo‐neī‐kè tsaí Ka‐lāk tsô ch‘aán‐īp.
13 ¶ Yaû k‘aaĭ Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, ts‘z̀‐kwòh tsaì‐sz À‐lūn‐kè tsź‐suen, tsô m̄‐koò‐ì shaatₒ‐yān‐kè t‘ō‐peî shēng; yaû ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ‐teî Laāp‐nā līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ.
14 Ngă‐tik līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Ĭ‐shāt‐t‘aī‐moh līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ,
15 Hōh‐lūn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Taí‐pit līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ,
16 À‐yan līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Yeūk‐taaî līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Paakₒ‐shî‐māk līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ kûng kaú‐kòh shēng; to‐haî ts‘ūng ni‐leŭng‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp fan‐ch‘ut laī kè.
17 Yaû ts‘ūng Pîn‐ngă‐măn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung, ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ‐teî Kei‐p‘ìn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Kei‐pa līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ,
18 À‐nā‐tāt līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, À‐lāk‐moōn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng sz̀‐kòh shēng.
19 À‐lūn tsź‐suen tsô tsaì‐sz shóh‐tak‐kè shēng, kûng shāp‐saam‐kòh, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ.
20 ¶ Leî‐meî‐kè chi‐p‘aaì‐chung Koh‐hāt k‘eī‐uē‐kè tsź‐suen, chap‐ch‘aū shóh‐fan‐tak‐kè shēng, yaŭ ts‘ūng Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn chi‐p‘aaì‐chung fan‐ch‘ut‐laī.
21 Yīk haî tsô m̄‐koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè t‘ō‐shēng, Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐shaan‐ch‘uè‐kè Shî‐kìm, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; Kei‐shik līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ.
22 K‘āp‐sin līn‐maaī kaau‐yè, Paakₒ‐wōh‐lūn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng sz̀‐kòh shēng.
23 Yaû‐yaŭ ts‘ūng Taân‐kè chi‐p‘aaì teî‐īp‐chung fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî Leî‐t‘aī‐kei līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, K‘eī‐peì‐tûn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ,
24 À‐ngă‐lūn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Ka‐tāk‐lām‐moōn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng sz̀‐kòh shēng.
25 Yaû‐yaŭ ts‘ūng Mă‐nā‐sai poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung, fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî Taaî‐naāp līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Ka‐tāk‐lām‐moōn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng leŭng‐kòh shēng.
26 Ts‘z̀‐kwòh Koh‐hāt k‘eī‐uē‐kè tsź‐suen‐kè; kûng shāp‐kòh shēng, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ.
27 ¶ Yaû ts‘z̀ Leî‐meî chi‐p‘aaì‐chung‐kè Kaakₒ‐shûn tsź‐suen, ts‘ūng Mă‐nā‐sai poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung, fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî tsô m̄‐koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè, t‘ō‐peî‐shēng, Pa‐shaan‐kè Hóh‐laān, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; Pīt‐t‘aī‐la līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng leŭng‐kòh shēng.
28 Yaû‐yaŭ ts‘ūng Ĭ‐saatₒ‐ka chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî Kei‐shûn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Taaî‐pa‐laāt līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ,
29 Yē‐moōt līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Yán‐nīng‐kon līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng sz̀‐kòh shēng.
30 Yaû‐yaŭ ts‘ūng À‐ch‘itₒ chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî Maĭ‐sha‐lāk līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Aapₒ‐tûn līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ.
31 Haak‐kaapₒ līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Leĭ‐hōp līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng sz̀‐kòh shēng.
32 Yaû‐yaŭ ts‘ūng Naāp‐taaî‐leî chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî tsô m̄‐koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè t‘ō‐shēng, Ka‐leî‐leî‐kè Kei‐tāk, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; Hap‐moōt‐toh‐ĭ līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Ka‐taân līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng saam‐kòh shēng.
33 Kaakₒ‐shûn‐kè tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî kokₒ‐tsūk kûng tak shēng shāp‐saam‐kòh, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ.
34 ¶ K‘eī‐uē‐kè Leî‐meî chi‐p‘aaì Maĭ‐la‐leĭ tsūk‐kè yān, shóh‐tak‐kè shēng, yaŭ ts‘ūng Sai‐pò‐lūn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî Yeukₒ‐nîm līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Ka‐taaî līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ,
35 Ting‐nā līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Nā‐hap‐laāt līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng sz̀‐kòh shēng.
36 Yaû yaŭ ts‘ūng Laū‐pîn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî Peì‐shik līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Ngă‐hap‐saatₒ līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ,
37 Kei‐taí‐mōk līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Maĭ‐faatₒ‐aapₒ līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng sz̀‐kòh shēng.
38 Yaû‐yaŭ ts‘ūng Ka‐tak chi‐p‘aaì‐kè teî‐īp‐chung fan‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè, tsik‐haî tsô m̄‐koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān kè t‘ō‐shēng, Kei‐līt‐kè La‐moōt līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; Mă‐hap‐nîm līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ,
39 Hei‐shāt‐poón līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ, Ngā‐ch‘itₒ līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ; kûng sz̀‐kòh shēng.
40 K‘eī‐uē‐kè Leî‐meî chi‐p‘aaì‐yān, tsik‐haî Maĭ‐la‐leĭ tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî kokₒ‐tsūk chap‐ch‘aū, shóh‐tak‐kè shēng, kûng shāp‐î‐kòh.
41 ¶ Faān Leî‐meî‐yān shóh‐tak Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐ch‘aán‐īp‐chung‐kè shēng, kûng sz̀‐shāp‐paatₒ‐kòh, līn‐maaī kaau‐yĕ.
42 Ni‐ti chùng‐shēng sz̀‐waī yaŭ kaau‐yĕ: kokₒ‐shēng to‐haî kóm‐yeûng.
43 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘aaĭ ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn faatₒ‐shaî shóh‐ying‐shīng ts‘z̀‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chi‐līt‐tsó‐kè; yat‐k‘oì to ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî, shīng‐shaû laī kui‐chuê.
44 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tò‐ch‘uè to tak p‘īng‐on, chiù‐i faān heùng k‘uĭ‐teî līt‐tsó faatₒ‐shaî shóh‐ying‐shīng‐kè: shaí k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐yaŭ‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk, mŏ yat‐kòh kóm k‘eĭ‐tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî mîn‐ts‘īn; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk, tsûn‐teî kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî shaú.
45 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐ying‐shīng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè hó‐ch‘uè, mŏ‐yat‐kîn m̄‐tak‐tó; yat‐k‘oì to yìng‐îm à.
