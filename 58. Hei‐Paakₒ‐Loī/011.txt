1 SÙN‐TAK haî shóh‐mông‐chi‐sẑ‐kè shāt‐kei, meî‐kìn‐chi‐sẑ‐kè p‘āng‐kuì.
2 Koó‐yān yaū‐ue‐sùn, tsaû tak‐tó hó mīng‐shing.
3 Ngŏh‐teî yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, tsaû chi‐tò shaì‐kaaì haî k‘aaĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐īn laī‐tsô‐shīng, chì‐tò shóh‐kìn‐chi‐māt, m̄‐haî ts‘ūng yaŭ yīng‐chi‐māt tsô‐ch‘ut‐laī.
4 À‐paakₒ yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, hìn‐tsaì kwòh‐Sheûng‐Taì, peí‐kaaù Koi‐yán kàng‐hó, Sheûng‐Taì waî k‘uĭ‐kè laĭ‐māt tsô‐chìng, yan‐ts‘ź k‘uĭ tak‐ch‘ing‐waī‐î: shóh‐ĭ À‐paakₒ sui‐īn sź‐hiu to‐chûng‐kóng.
5 Ĭ‐nōk yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, tsaû peî‐tsipₒ‐sheŭng‐t‘in m̄‐shaí‐sź, yān m̄‐tsoì kìn‐tak‐k‘uĭ; yan Sheûng‐Taì tsipₒ k‘uĭ sheŭng‐t‘in: k‘uĭ meî‐tsipₒ‐shing‐chi‐sin, tak‐tó chìng‐kuì, haî Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐foon‐heí‐kè.
6 Taân mŏ‐sùn‐tak, tsaû pat‐nāng tak Sheûng‐Taì foon‐heí: yan‐waî ts‘an‐tsaû Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐yān, pit‐iù‐sùn k‘uĭ haî yaŭ‐kè, yaû iù‐sùn k‘uĭ pit‐sheúng‐ts‘z̀ faān ts‘itₒ‐k‘aū‐k‘uĭ‐kè.
7 Nōh‐à yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, tsaû mūng Sheûng‐Taì māk‐shî meî‐în‐kè sẑ, tsaû kìng‐waì uê‐peî fong‐chau, laī‐kaù‐k‘uĭ ts‘uēn‐ka; yaū ts‘ź shóh‐ĭ tîng‐shaì‐yān‐chi‐tsuî, ī‐ch‘é tak‐tó yaū‐ue‐sùn‐tak‐kè î.
8 À‐paakₒ‐la‐hón yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, shaû‐mîng kiù k‘uĭ ch‘ut‐tò tseung‐loī‐tak‐tsô‐ka‐īp kòh‐ch‘uè, k‘uĭ tsaû tsun‐i; ch‘ut‐huì‐chi‐shī, to m̄‐chi huì‐tò pin‐ch‘uè.
9 K‘uĭ yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, uê‐kui tsoî shóh‐ying‐huí‐chi‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆ tsoî î‐teî, chuê‐tsoî cheùng‐mōk, kûng‐maaī t‘ūng‐tak‐ts‘ź‐ying‐huí‐chi‐Ĭ‐saatₒ Ngă‐kokₒ yat‐yeûng:
10 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ mông yaŭ‐kei‐chí‐kè shēng, tsik‐haî Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐king‐yīng shóh‐kìn‐tsô‐kè.
11 Yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, tsik‐sź Saatₒ‐la sui‐kwòh‐hiu shang‐yūk‐chi‐k‘eī, yaŭ‐nāng waaī‐yân shang‐tsaí, yan‐waî k‘uĭ ĭ‐waī‐kóh Waî ying‐huí‐kè, haî shīng‐shāt‐kè.
12 Koò‐ts‘ź yaŭ yat‐kòh hó‐ts‘ź sź‐hiu‐kè yān, shang‐ch‘ut tsź‐suen, uē t‘in‐sing kòm‐toh, uē hoí‐pin pat‐hóh‐shing‐shó‐kè sha.
13 ¶ Ni‐ti k‘ui‐haî cha‐shāt sùn‐sam‐ī‐sź‐kè, meî‐ts‘āng tak‐tó shóh‐ying‐huí‐kè, waī yă‐ts‘āng uĕn‐uĕn kìn‐kwòh, tsaû on‐īn tsipₒ‐shaû, tsẑ‐keí wâ, tsoî shaì‐sheûng tsô yān‐haakₒ, haî‐keì‐kui‐kè.
14 Faān‐haî kóm‐yeûng‐kóng‐kè, haî piú‐mīng k‘uĭ chûng‐wán ka‐heung à.
15 K‘uĭ‐teî yeūk‐haî keì‐nîm shóh‐ch‘ut‐kè ka‐heung, to yaŭ kei‐ooî hóh‐ĭ faan‐tak‐huì.
16 Taân k‘uĭ‐teî yeŭng‐mô kàng‐hó‐kè ka‐heung, tsik‐haî t‘in‐sheûng‐kè: koò‐ts‘ź, Sheûng‐Taì peî‐ch‘ing‐waī k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, yīk m̄‐kokₒ sau‐ch‘í: yan k‘uĭ yă‐ts‘āng waî k‘uĭ‐teî uê‐peî‐kòh‐shēng lokₒ.
17 ¶ À‐paakₒ‐la‐hón yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, tong‐kìn‐shì‐chi‐shī, hìn Ĭ‐saatₒ: kóh‐kòh shaû‐hiu ying‐huí‐kè, hìn k‘uĭ tūk‐shang‐kè tsaí,
18 Lûn‐k‘āp ni‐kòh‐tsaí, ts‘āng‐yaŭ‐wâ, Neĭ‐kè haû‐yuî, pit‐yaū Ĭ‐saatₒ ī‐ch‘ing:
19 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ ĭ‐waī Sheûng‐Taì nāng‐lîng‐k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí, yaū‐sź‐fūk‐shang; kóm‐yeûng, k‘uĭ hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ūng‐sź‐luĭ tsipₒ‐faan k‘uĭ yat‐yeûng.
20 Ĭ‐saatₒ yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, waî tseung‐loī‐kè sẑ, chuk‐fuk Ngă‐kokₒ k‘āp Ĭ‐sò.
21 Ngă‐kokₒ yaū‐ue‐sùn‐tak, lām‐sź‐chi‐shī, chuk‐fuk Yeukₒ‐shat leŭng‐kòh tsaí; foō‐chuê k‘uĭ‐kè kwaaí‐cheûng laī‐shūng‐paaì.
22 Yeukₒ‐shat yaū‐ue‐sùn‐tak, lām‐sź‐chi‐shī, lûn‐k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐tsūk ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp; yaû waî tsẑ‐keí‐kè hoī‐kwat fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐teî.
23 Yaū‐ue‐sùn‐tak, Moh‐sai ch‘ut‐shaì‐chi‐shī, peî‐foô‐mŏ shau‐maaī k‘uĭ saam‐kòh‐uēt, yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî kìn‐kòh‐tsaí shang‐tak hó‐yeûng; yaû m̄‐p‘à wōng‐sheûng‐kè mîng‐lîng.
24 Moh‐sai yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, cheúng‐taaî‐chi‐shī, m̄‐háng peî‐ch‘ing‐waī Faatₒ‐lŏ nuĭ‐kè tsaí;
25 Ts‘īng‐uên kûng Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐mān t‘ūng‐shaû foó‐naân, m̄‐háng tsaâm‐shī heúng tsuî‐okₒ‐kè faaì‐lōk;
26 K‘uĭ ĭ‐waī yan Kei‐Tuk shaû līng‐yūk, chûng‐pó‐pooì‐kwòh‐Oi‐k‘āp‐kè fòh‐ts‘oī: yan‐waî k‘uĭ mông tseung‐loī‐kè sheúng‐ts‘z̀ à.
27 K‘uĭ yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, leī‐hoi Oi‐k‘āp, m̄‐p‘à wōng‐kè nau‐nô: yan k‘uĭ yaŭ hāng‐sam yán‐noî, hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘aí‐kìn pat‐hóh‐kìn‐kè Sheûng‐Taì.
28 K‘uĭ yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, tsaû shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ, uĕ‐k‘āp shá‐huetₒ‐kè laĭ, táng tsiú‐mīt cheúng‐tsź‐kè m̄‐lām‐tò paakₒ‐sìng.
29 Paakₒ‐sìng yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, tsaû hāng‐kwòh Hūng‐hoí, hó‐ts‘z̆ hāng lūk‐teî yat‐yeûng: Oi‐k‘āp‐yān shì‐hă‐hāng‐kwòh, tsaû châm‐sź‐saaì.
30 Paakₒ‐sìng yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, ts‘ūn‐hāng Yē‐leî‐koh‐shēng, līk‐ts‘at‐yāt, kòh shēng‐kei tsaû titₒ‐hiu.
31 Keî‐foŏ La‐hōp yaū‐ue sùn‐tak, m̄‐kûng pat‐sùn‐ts‘ūng‐kè t‘ūng‐sź, yan‐k‘uĭ k‘aaĭ‐p‘īng‐wōh laī‐tsipₒ‐toî kòh‐ti‐t‘aàm‐tsź à.
32 ¶ Ngŏh chûng‐iù kóng mat‐yĕ ni? yeūk kóng‐tò K‘eī‐t‘īn, Pa‐lāk, Ts‘aam‐suen, Yē‐fat‐taaî; Taaî‐waî, Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ, k‘āp chùng‐sin‐chi, tsaû shī‐haû m̄‐kaù lokₒ:
33 K‘uĭ‐teî yaū‐ue‐sùn‐tak, shóh‐ĭ nāng fūk‐tīk‐kwokₒ, hāng‐kung‐î, tak‐ying‐huí, k‘īm sz‐tsź‐kè haú,
34 Mīt fóh‐îm, peî‐to‐kìm, uĕn‐yeūk pìn‐waī kong‐k‘eūng, tá‐cheùng‐shī yaŭ yŭng‐līk, kung‐p‘òh î‐pong‐kè chân.
35 Foŏ‐yān tak‐tó k‘uĭ‐kè sź‐ché‐fūk‐shang: yaû‐yaŭ‐yān yán‐shaû hūk‐yīng, m̄‐háng kaú‐mĭn; waī seúng tak‐kàng‐hó‐kè fūk‐shang:
36 Yīk‐yaŭ‐yān shaû heì‐lûng, pin‐tá, kw‘án‐póng, kaam‐kàm‐kè mōh‐lîn:
37 Waāk peî‐shēk‐tèng‐sź, shaû kuì‐kaaì‐sź, uê‐shì‐lîn, peî‐to‐chaám: shan‐cheukₒ mīn‐yeūng, shaan‐yeūng‐kè‐p‘eī; tò‐ch‘uè pan‐tsaú, shaû p‘ān‐fāt, waân‐naân kaan‐san;
38 Ī‐ch‘é chau‐laū tsoî‐kw‘òng‐yĕ, shaan‐lĭng, shaan‐ngaām, teî‐tûng‐chi‐kaan, kóm‐yeûng‐kè yān: (ni‐kòh‐shaì‐kaaì pat‐hom kui‐chuê k‘uĭ.)
39 Ni‐ti kòm‐toh‐yān yaŭ‐ue sùn‐tak, tsaû tak‐tó hó‐chìng‐kuì, taân chûng‐meî‐tak Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐ying‐huí‐kè:
40 Yan Sheûng‐Taì waī ngŏh‐teî uê‐peî kàng‐hó‐kè, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî t‘óng‐yeūk m̄‐līn‐maaī ngŏh‐teî, tsaû pat‐nāng‐uēn‐ts‘uēn.
