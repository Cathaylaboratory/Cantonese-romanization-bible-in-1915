1 NGŎH kw‘àn‐foó p‘ān‐k‘ūng: k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chak‐ĭ‐t‘eng ngŏh.
2 K‘aū neĭ pó‐oô ngŏh līng‐wān; yan ngŏh haî neĭ‐chi‐oì‐ché: k‘aū ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì ch‘íng‐kaù neĭ‐chi‐pūk, yan ngŏh tūk‐haî í‐laaî neĭ.
3 Ngŏh shīng‐yāt foo‐kiù neĭ: k‘aū Chué līn‐măn ngŏh.
4 Ngŏh‐sam yeŭng‐mông Sheûng‐Chué: k‘aū Chué lîng neĭ pūk sam‐chung foon‐lōk.
5 Sheûng‐Chué haî chì‐leūng‐shîn, chì‐foon‐shuè; taân‐faān foo‐kiù‐neĭ‐chi‐yān, neĭ pit taaî‐shi yan‐tín kwòh‐k‘uĭ.
6 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chak‐ĭ‐t‘eng ngŏh‐chi‐k‘eī‐t‘ó; t‘eng ngŏh hán‐k‘aū‐chi‐sheng‐yam.
7 Uê‐naân‐chi‐yāt, ngŏh pit foo‐kiù neĭ: yan‐waî neĭ pit‐tîng ying‐shīng ngŏh.
8 Chué à, kokₒ‐yeûng‐shān‐chi‐chung, mŏ‐yat‐kòh ts‘z̆‐tak neĭ mŏ‐yat‐kòh ts‘z̆‐tak neĭ‐chi‐tsokₒ‐waī.
9 Chué shóh‐tsô‐chi‐maân kwokₒ, pit laī paaì‐kwaî neĭ‐chi‐ts‘īn; pit‐tîng tsuen‐wīng neĭ‐chi‐mēng.
10 Yan‐waî neĭ haî chì‐taaî, ī‐ch‘é hāng‐ch‘ut î‐tsik: waī‐tūk neĭ chì haî Sheûng‐Taì.
11 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tseung neĭ chi‐lô chí‐yăn ngŏh; ngŏh tsaú pit tsun‐hāng neĭ‐chi‐chan‐leĭ: táng ngŏh yat‐sam kìng‐waì neĭ‐chi mēng.
12 Ngŏh Chué Sheûng‐Taì, ngŏh iù ts‘uēn‐sam tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ: ngŏh wĭng‐uĕn tsuen‐wīng neĭ‐chi‐mēng.
13 Yan neĭ peí taaî‐yan‐tín laī toî ngŏh: ī‐ch‘é kaù ngŏh līng‐wān, mĭn‐yāp kīk‐sham‐chi‐yam‐foó.
14 Sheûng‐Taì à, kiu‐ngô‐chi‐yān, ĭ‐king heí‐laī kung‐kik ngŏh, k‘eūng waāng‐chi‐tóng, iù ts‘ām‐hoî ngŏh‐chi‐līng‐wān; ī‐ch‘é m̄‐tseung neĭ chai‐tsoî ngaăn‐ts‘īn.
15 Chué à, neĭ haî līn‐măn ts‘z̄‐pei‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, shī‐sheūng yán nô, yan‐tín chan‐shāt, to mŏ haân‐leûng‐ché.
16 K‘aū neĭ t‘aí‐sut ngŏh, tseung yan‐tín ts‘z̀‐kwòh‐ngŏh; tseung neĭ līk‐leûng ts‘z̀‐kwòh‐neĭ‐pūk, ch‘íng‐kaù neĭ‐p‘eĭ‐chi‐tsź.
17 K‘aū neĭ tseung yan‐tín‐chi‐hó chiû‐t‘aū chí‐shî ngŏh; táng tsang‐oò‐ngŏh‐chi‐yān, t‘aí‐kìn tsaû sau‐kw‘aì heí‐laī: yan‐waî chi‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pong‐chôh ngŏh, ī‐ch‘é on‐waì ngŏh.
