1 YAŪ‐TAAÎ chi‐p‘aaì, chiù kokₒ‐tsūk chap‐ch‘aū shóh‐tak‐kè teî, līt mīng tsoî haû; naām‐pîn‐kè pin‐kaaì, haî heùng naām‐kè Sùn‐yĕ, chì‐tò Ĭ‐tung kíng‐kaaì.
2 Naām‐pîn kaaì, tsik‐haî ts‘ūng Īm hoí pin‐uē, yaû ts‘ūng heùng naām‐pîn‐kè hoí‐kóng:
3 Yaû ch‘ut tò naām‐pîn, chì‐tò À‐hak‐la‐pan p‘oh, chīk huì‐tò Sùn, yaû sheŭng‐tò Ka‐t‘itₒ‐pa‐neī‐à naām‐pîn, king‐kwòh Hei‐sz‐lūn, sheŭng‐tò À‐t‘aatₒ, chuèn‐tò Kaapₒ‐ka:
4 Yaû ts‘ūng kóh‐ch‘uè heùng‐chuê Aapₒ‐moōn, ch‘ut‐tò Oi‐k‘āp‐hōh, t‘ung ch‘ut‐tò hoí; ni‐ti kaau‐kaaì: tsô neĭ‐kè naām‐pîn kaaì.
5 Ts‘ūng Īm‐hoí chì‐tò Yeukₒ‐taân hōh‐haú, haî tung‐pîn‐kè kaau‐kaaì. Ts‘ūng hoí‐kóng chì‐tò Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐haú, haî pak‐pîn‐kè kaau‐kaaì:
6 Kóh‐ti kaaì sheŭng‐tò Paakₒ‐hotₒ‐la, king‐kwòh Paakₒ‐à‐la‐pa‐kè pak‐pîn; kóh‐ti kaaì yīk sheŭng‐tò Laū‐pîn‐kè tsaí Poh‐hón‐kè p‘oōn‐shēk:
7 Kóh‐ti kaaì yaû ts‘ūng À‐kotₒ‐kuk, sheŭng‐tò Taí‐pit, heùng pak‐pîn sheŭng‐huì, heùng À‐to‐mīng p‘oh tuì‐mîn‐kè Kat‐kaapₒ, tsoî hōh‐kè naām‐pîn ngôn: kóh‐ti kaaì chì‐tò Ìn‐shî‐māk‐kè shuí, ni‐ti kaaì t‘ung ch‘ut tò Ìn‐lōh‐kitₒ:
8 Kóh‐ti kaaì sheŭng‐tò Pîn‐yan‐nuên‐kè kuk, chì‐tò Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐kè naām‐pîn; Yē‐pò‐sẑ tsik‐haî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng: kóh‐ti kaaì yaû sheŭng‐tò Pîn‐yan‐nuên‐kè kuk‐ts‘īn‐pîn, heùng sai‐kè shaan‐téng, tsik‐haî tsoî Leĭ‐fāt‐yam‐shaan‐kuk‐pak pin‐kè kīk pin:
9 Kóh‐ti kaaì yaû ts‘ūng shaan‐téng chì‐tò Neī‐fat‐toh‐à‐kè shuí‐uēn, ch‘ut tò Ĭ‐fat‐lūn‐shaan‐kè kokₒ‐shēng; yaû chì‐tò Pá‐la, Pá‐la tsik‐haî Kei‐līt‐yē‐lām:
10 Kóh‐ti kaaì yaû ts‘ūng Pá‐la heùng sai, chuèn‐tò Sai‐ĭ‐shaan, huì‐tò Yē‐lām‐shaan pak‐pin Yē‐lām, tsik‐haî Kei‐saatₒ‐lūn. Yaû lōk‐tò Paakₒ‐shî‐māk, huì‐tò T‘īng‐nā:
11 Kóh‐ti kaaì yaû ch‘ut‐tò Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn pak‐pin: yaû tò Shāt‐lūn, heùng‐ts‘īn‐tò Pá‐la‐shaan, ch‘ut‐tò Aapₒ‐īp; ts‘ź kaaì t‘ung ch‘ut‐tò‐hoí.
12 Sai‐pîn‐kè kaau‐kaaì, haî chì‐tò taaî hoí, yaû kân‐chuê hoí‐pin. Ni‐ti haî Yaū‐taaî chi‐p‘aaì, chiù kokₒ‐tsūk shóh‐tak‐kè kíng‐kaaì.
13 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsun Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐kè, tsoî Yaū‐taaî‐yān chi‐chung, tseung yat fân ts‘z̀‐kwòh Yē‐foo‐neī‐kè tsaí Ka‐lāk, tsik‐haî Kei‐līt‐à‐pa yaû mēng Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn, À‐pa haî À‐naāp‐kè foô‐ts‘an.
14 Ka‐lāk tsaû yaū Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn kón‐chūk À‐naāp‐kè saam‐kòh‐tsaí, Shî‐shai, À‐hei‐maân, Taāt‐maaĭ, haî À‐naāp‐kè tsaí.
15 Tsaû ts‘ūng Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn sheŭng tò Taí‐pit‐kè paakₒ‐sìng‐ch‘uè: ni‐ch‘uè Taí‐pit, ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn mēng‐kiù Kei‐līt‐sai‐fat.
16 Ka‐lāk wâ, Yaŭ yān nāng kung‐tá Kei‐līt‐sai‐fat, tseung shēng tuēt‐hiu‐kè, ngŏh pit tseung ngŏh‐kè nuĭ Aapₒ‐saatₒ kà k‘uĭ.
17 Ka‐lāk‐kè saì‐ló Kei‐naāp‐kè tsaí Oh‐tak‐īp tuēt‐hiu kóh‐shēng: Ka‐lāk tsaû tseung kóh‐nuĭ Aapₒ‐saatₒ kà k‘uĭ.
18 Aapₒ‐saatₒ kwòh‐moōn kòh‐shī, huèn cheûng‐foo heùng k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an k‘aū yat‐faaì t‘īn: Aapₒ‐saatₒ ts‘ūng luī‐sheûng lōk‐laī; Ka‐lāk tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ seúng tím ni?
19 Taapₒ wâ, K‘aū neĭ ts‘z̀ fuk kwòh‐ngŏh; yan‐waî neĭ ĭ‐king tseung naām‐pîn‐kè teî ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh; k‘aū neĭ yīk tseung shuí‐ts‘uēn ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh. K‘uĭ tsaû tseung sheûng‐ts‘uēn hâ‐ts‘uēn ts‘z̀‐kwòh kòh‐nuĭ.
20 ¶ Ni‐ti haî Yaū‐taaî‐yān chi‐p‘aaì‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, chiù kokₒ‐tsūk shóh‐tak‐kè.
21 ¶ Yaū‐taaî‐yān chi‐p‘aaì kīk‐pin‐kè chùng‐shēng, tsoî naām‐pîn heùng Ĭ‐tung kaau‐kaaì‐kè, tsik‐haî Kaapₒ‐sitₒ, Ĭ‐tak, Ngă‐koo‐ĭ,
22 Kei‐nā, Taí‐moh‐nā, Sai‐taaî‐t‘aatₒ,
23 Kei‐tāk, Hâ‐shokₒ, Yīk‐naân,
24 Sai‐fat, T‘aī‐līm, Peì‐à‐lūk,
25 Hâ‐shokₒ, Hap‐t‘aatₒ‐taaî, Ka‐leūk, Hei‐sz‐lūn, tsik‐haî Hâ‐shokₒ,
26 À‐maān, Shî‐mă, Moh‐la‐taaî,
27 Hap‐saatₒ‐ka‐taaî, Haak‐shāt‐moōn, Paakₒ‐p‘à‐līt,
28 Hap‐saatₒ‐shue‐à, Pīt‐shî‐pa, Pit‐yeūk‐ngă,
29 Pá‐la, Ĭ‐yan, À‐sham,
30 Ĭ‐toh‐laāp, Kei‐shat, Hotₒ‐mă,
31 Sik‐laāp, Māk‐mă‐nā, Saatₒ‐saatₒ‐nā,
32 Leî‐pa‐māt, Shāt‐yan, À‐yan, Lām‐moōn: kûng î‐shāp‐kaú shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
33 ¶ Tsoî shaan‐kuk‐kè yaŭ Ĭ‐shāt‐tô, Sóh‐līt, À‐shāt‐nā,
34 Saatₒ‐nōh‐à, Yán‐kon‐nīng, Taaî‐pò‐à, Ĭ‐naām,
35 Yē‐moōt, À‐t‘ó‐laān, Sóh‐koh, À‐sai‐ka,
36 Sha‐la‐yam, À‐taí‐taaî‐yam, K‘eī‐taí‐la, K‘eī‐taí, Lŏ‐t‘īn; kûng shāp‐sz̀ shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
37 ¶ Saí‐naām, Hap‐taaî‐sha, Māk‐taaî‐kaapₒ,
38 Taí‐līn, Māt‐sẑ‐pa, Yeukₒ‐t‘ipₒ,
39 La‐kat, Pat‐kaapₒ, Aak‐lūn,
40 Kaapₒ‐poón, La‐maân, Kat‐lāk,
41 K‘eī‐tak‐lōk, Paakₒ‐taaî‐kung, Nā‐mă, Mă‐kei‐taaî; kûng shāp‐lūk shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
42 ¶ Laāp‐nā, Ĭ‐t‘ipₒ, À‐shaan,
43 Yik‐fat‐taaî, À‐shāt‐nā, Neī‐sik,
44 Chi‐la, À‐kaakₒ‐shik, Mă‐leĭ‐sha; kûng kaú shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
45 ¶ Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
46 Yaû ts‘ūng Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn chì‐tò hoí, faān kân À‐shāt‐tāt‐kè, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
47 ¶ Yaû À‐shāt‐tāt k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung, Ka‐saatₒ k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung, chì‐tò Oi‐k‘āp‐hōh, yīk chì‐tò hoí‐pin:
48 ¶ Tsoî shaan‐teî‐kè yaŭ Sha‐māt, Ngă‐tik, Sóh‐koh,
49 Taaî‐nā, Kei‐līt‐saatₒ‐nā, tsik‐haî Taí‐pit,
50 À‐nā‐paakₒ, Ĭ‐shāt‐t‘aī‐mōk, À‐nîm,
51 Hóh‐shaan, Hōh‐lūn, K‘eī‐lūk; kûng shāp‐yat shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
52 ¶ À‐la, T‘ó‐mă, Ĭ‐shaan,
53 Ngă‐nūng, Paakₒ‐taaî‐pò‐à, À‐fei‐ka,
54 Fàn‐taaî, Kei‐līt‐à‐pa, tsik‐haî Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn, Sai‐oh‐ĭ; kûng kaú‐kòh shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
55 ¶ Mă‐wān, Ka‐māt, Sai‐fat, Yeūk‐taaî,
56 Yē‐sz‐līt, Yeukₒ‐t‘īn, Saatₒ‐nōh‐à,
57 Koi‐yán, K‘eī‐peì‐à, T‘īng‐nā; kûng shāp shēng, k‘āp kòh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
58 ¶ Hap‐fat, Paakₒ‐suk, K‘eī‐tāt,
59 Mă‐laāp, Paakₒ‐à‐nōk. Leî t‘aī‐kwan; kûng lūk shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
60 ¶ Kei‐līt‐pa‐līk, tsik‐haî Kei‐līt‐yē‐lām, La‐pa; kûng leŭng‐kòh shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung:
61 ¶ Tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ‐ch‘uè, yaŭ Paakₒ‐à‐la‐pa, Māk‐ting, Sai‐ka‐ka,
62 Nīk‐shaan, Īm‐shēng, Yán‐k‘eī‐taí; kûng lūk shēng, k‘āp kóh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung.
63 ¶ Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐yān chuê‐tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè, Yaū‐taaî‐yān mŏ nāng kón‐chūk k‘uĭ‐teî: koò‐ts‘ź Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐yān kûng Yaū‐taaî tsź‐suen, t‘ūng‐chuê tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
