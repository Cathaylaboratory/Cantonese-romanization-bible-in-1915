1 Ĭ‐LEÎ‐SHA ts‘āng tuì shóh‐kaù‐shang k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí kòh‐foŏ‐yān wâ, Neĭ t‘ūng neĭ‐kè ka‐kuèn iù heí‐laī, poon‐huì neĭ hóh‐chuê‐tak‐kè teî‐fong laī kui‐chuê: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîng kei‐fong kòng‐lām; pit tsoî ni‐taatₒ‐teî‐fong ts‘at nīn.
2 Foŏ‐yān tsaû tsun‐i Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān shóh‐kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ: heí‐laī taaì ka‐kuèn huì Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè teî‐fong, kui‐chuê ts‘at nīn.
3 Ts‘at nīn chi‐haû, foŏ‐yān ts‘ūng Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè teî faan‐ch‘ut‐laī: waî tsẑ‐keí fōng‐uk t‘īn‐maŭ huì oi‐kò wōng.
4 Kong‐ngaam wōng kûng Sheûng‐Taì‐yān‐kè pūk K‘eī‐hap‐sai kóng wâ, Ts‘éng neĭ tseung Ĭ‐leî‐sha faān shóh‐hāng‐kè taaî‐sẑ, kóng‐kwòh ngŏh chi.
5 K‘eī‐hap‐sai tseung Ĭ‐leî‐sha ts‘āng shaí sź‐yān fūk‐shang‐kè sẑ, tuì wōng kóng‐hoi, ngaam‐ngaam Ĭ‐leî‐sha shóh‐kaù‐shang k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí kóh‐foŏ‐yān, waî tsẑ‐keí‐kè fōng‐uk t‘īn‐maŭ, laī oi‐kò wōng. K‘eī‐hap‐sai wâ, Ngŏh chué, ngŏh‐wōng, ni‐kòh tsaû haî kòh‐foŏ‐yān, ni‐kòh tsaû haî k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí, tsik‐haî Ĭ‐leî‐sha shóh‐fūk‐shang‐kè.
6 Wōng mân kòh‐foŏ‐yān, foŏ‐yān tsaû tseung kóh‐ti‐sẑ kóng‐kwòh k‘uĭ chi. Kóm‐tsaû wōng waî k‘uĭ p‘aaì yat‐kòh waân‐koon, wâ, Faān shūk ni‐kòh‐foŏ‐yān‐kè, to peí‐faan k‘uĭ, tsẑ k‘uĭ leī‐hoi poón‐t‘ó chì‐tò kam‐yāt, k‘uĭ t‘īn‐maŭ shóh‐ch‘aán‐kè, yīk iù peí‐faan k‘uĭ.
7 ¶ Ĭ‐leî‐sha laī‐tò Taaî‐mă‐shik; À‐laān wōng Pîn‐hap‐taāt waân pêng; yaŭ yān tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān laī‐tò ni‐ch‘uè a.
8 Wōng tuì Hap‐sitₒ wâ, Neĭ iù k‘aaĭ shaú ning‐chuê laĭ‐māt huì kìn Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān, ts‘éng k‘uĭ waî ngŏh mân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā. Ngŏh‐kè pêng hóh‐ĭ tak‐hó m̄‐tak‐hó ni?
9 Hap‐sitₒ ning‐hiu Taaî‐mă‐shik shóh‐ch‘aán kokₒ‐yeûng meĭ‐pán, yûng sz̀‐shāp‐chekₒ lokₒ‐t‘ōh tsoì wân, tseung‐laī tsô laĭ‐māt, huì kìn Ĭ‐leî‐sha, k‘eĭ‐tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn, wâ, Neĭ tsź À‐laān wōng Pîn‐hap‐taāt, tá‐faatₒ ngŏh laī kìn neĭ, wâ, Ngŏh ni‐ti pêng tak‐hó m̄‐tak‐hó?
10 Ĭ‐leî‐sha tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ chikₒ‐koón huì ooī‐fuk k‘uĭ, wâ, Ts‘ź‐pêng pit tak‐hó: taân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng māk‐shî ngŏh, k‘uĭ pit iù sź.
11 Ĭ‐leî‐sha tîng ngaăn t‘aí‐chuê Hap‐sitₒ, chì k‘uĭ sau‐kw‘aì: Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān tsaû huk.
12 Hap‐sitₒ wâ, Ngŏh chué waî‐mat oi‐huk ni? Taapₒ wâ, Yan‐waî ngŏh chi‐tò neĭ tseung‐loī pit ka‐hoî tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān: shiu‐waí k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè pó‐cheùng, k‘aaĭ to shaatₒ‐sź k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè naām‐ting, wing sź k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè so‐tsaí, t‘ong‐hoi k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè yân‐foŏ.
13 Hap‐sitₒ wâ. Neĭ‐kè pūk yat‐chekₒ kaú mō‐î, tím‐nāng hāng ts‘ź taaî‐sẑ ni? Ĭ‐leî‐sha taapₒ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng māk‐shî ngŏh, neĭ pit tsô À‐laān‐kè wōng.
14 Hap‐sitₒ tsaû leī‐pīt Ĭ‐leî‐sha huì kìn k‘uĭ‐kè chué; k‘uĭ chué mân wâ, Ĭ‐leî‐sha tuì neĭ kóng mat‐yĕ ni? Taapₒ wâ, K‘uĭ tuì ngŏh wâ, Neĭ pit‐tak‐hó.
15 Ts‘z̀ yāt Hap‐sitₒ tseung ch‘ōng‐p‘eĭ tsàm‐tsoî shuí‐chung, k‘aaĭ‐laī k‘ám‐chuê wōng‐kè mîn, chì k‘uĭ sź: yaû Hap‐sitₒ kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
16 ¶ Tong Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng À‐hap‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐laān wōng taî‐nğ nīn, Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐laān tang‐waî tsô wōng kòh‐chân, Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ sheûng tsô Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng.
17 Yeukₒ‐laān tang‐waî chi‐shī, nīn‐keí saam‐shāp‐î suì; haí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng paatₒ nīn.
18 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ: hōk‐faan Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐wōng, t‘ūng À‐hap ka shóh‐hāng‐kè yat‐yeûng: yan‐waî ts‘uì À‐hap‐kè nuĭ tsô ts‘ai.
19 Taân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yan Taaî‐waî‐kè uēn‐koò, yīng‐īn m̄‐háng tsiú‐mīt Yaū‐taaî, chiù‐i shóh‐ying‐shīng Taaî‐waî‐kè, wĭng‐uĕn ts‘z̀ tang‐kwong kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐kè tsź‐suen.
20 Tong Yeukₒ‐laān chi‐shī, Ĭ‐tung‐yān poôn‐yīk Yaū‐taaî, waî tsẑ‐keí laāp wōng ch‘ī‐leĭ.
21 Yeukₒ‐laān taaì‐lĭng shóh‐yaŭ chìn‐ch‘e huì Saatₒ‐yik: laû‐yê heí‐laī kung‐kik waī‐kw‘àn k‘uĭ‐kè Ĭ‐tung‐yān, k‘āp taaì‐lĭng ch‘e‐kè chùng‐tseùng: paakₒ‐sìng tsaû t‘ō‐tsaú kwai tsẑ‐keí cheùng‐mōk.
22 Kóm‐tsaû Ĭ‐tung‐yān poôn‐yīk Yaū‐taaî, chì‐tò kam‐yāt. Tong‐shī Laāp‐nā‐yān to poôn‐yīk.
23 Yeukₒ‐laān k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì lōk Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
24 Yeukₒ‐laān kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám, tsòng‐tsoî k‘uĭ tsó Taaî‐waî‐kè shēng: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí À‐hap‐tsê kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
25 ¶ Tong Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng À‐hap‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐laān taî‐shāp‐î nīn, Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐laān‐kè tsaí À‐hap‐tsê, tang‐waî tsô wōng.
26 À‐hap‐tsê haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Òm‐leî‐kè nuĭ, À‐taaî‐leî shóh‐shang‐kè, tang‐waî chi‐shī, nīn‐keí î‐shāp‐î suì; tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng yat nīn.
27 À‐hap‐tsê hōk‐faan À‐hap ka haí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ, t‘ūng À‐hap ka shóh‐hāng‐kè yat‐yeûng; yan‐waî k‘uĭ haî À‐hap ka‐kè nuĭ‐saì à.
28 À‐hap‐tsê kûng À‐hap‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐laān, huì Kei‐līt‐kè La‐moōt, t‘ūng À‐laān wōng Hap‐sitₒ chaang‐chìn: À‐laān‐yān tá‐sheung Yeukₒ‐laān.
29 Yeukₒ‐laān wōng tsaû faan‐tò Yē‐sz‐līt seúng i‐leĭ shóh‐shaû‐kè sheung, tsik‐haî chìn À‐laān wōng Hap‐sitₒ kóh‐shī, À‐laān‐yān haí La‐moōt shóh‐kik‐sheung kè. Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐laān‐kè tsaí À‐hap‐tsê, huì‐tò Yē‐sz‐līt, mân‐haû À‐hap‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐laān, yan k‘uĭ yaŭ pêng.
