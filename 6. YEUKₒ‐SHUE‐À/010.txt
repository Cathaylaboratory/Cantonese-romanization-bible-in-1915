1 YĒ‐LÔ‐SAATₒ‐LAĂNG wōng À‐toh‐neī‐sai‐tāk, t‘eng‐mān Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tuēt Aai‐shēng, mīt‐saaì k‘uĭ‐teî; toî Aai‐shēng k‘āp Aai‐shēng‐kè wōng, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn toî Yē‐leî‐koh, k‘āp kóh‐ch‘uè‐kè wōng kóm; yaû t‘eng‐mān Kei‐p‘ìn‐yān kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān laāp wōh‐yeukₒ, chuê‐tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî chung‐kaan;
2 Tsaû hó‐king‐fong, yan‐waî Kei‐p‘ìn haî taaî‐shēng, kûng wōng‐shēng yat‐yeûng, taaî‐kwòh Aai‐shēng, kóh‐ti‐yān to haî yŭng‐sẑ.
3 Ue‐shî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng wōng À‐toh‐neī‐sai‐tāk tá‐faatₒ yān huì Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn wōng Wōh‐hām, Yē‐moōt wōng Peì‐laān, La‐kat wōng Yē‐fei‐à, Ak‐lūn wōng Taí‐pit‐ch‘uè, tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ,
4 Ts‘éng neĭ‐teî sheŭng‐laī ngŏh‐ch‘uè, pong‐chôh ngŏh, táng ngŏh‐teî kung‐tá Kei‐p‘ìn yān: yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî ĭ‐king kûng Yeukₒ‐shue‐à k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān laāp wōh‐yeukₒ à.
5 Kóm‐tsaû À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān‐kè nğ wōng, tsik‐haî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng wōng, Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn wōng, Yē‐moōt wōng, La‐kat wōng, Ak‐lūn wōng, tsuî‐tsaāp, kûng k‘uĭ‐teî yat‐ts‘aì‐kè kwan‐ping to sheŭng‐huì, tsoî Kei‐p‘ìn ts‘īn‐pîn on‐yīng, kung‐tá Kei‐p‘ìn shēng.
6 Kei‐p‘ìn‐yān tá‐faatₒ yān huì‐tò Kat‐kaapₒ‐yīng, kìn Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Neĭ m̄‐hó heùng neĭ pūk‐yān shuk‐shaú; iù kap‐ti laī‐tò ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh‐teî, pong‐chôh ngŏh‐teî: yan‐waî chuê‐tsoî shaan‐sheûng‐kè À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān‐kè chùng‐wōng, to tsuî‐tsaāp laī kung‐tá ngŏh‐teî à.
7 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsaû t‘ūng‐maaī kwan‐ping, k‘āp taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ, yaū Kat‐kaapₒ sheŭng‐huì.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Neĭ m̄‐shaí‐p‘à k‘uĭ‐teî: yan‐waî ngŏh ĭ‐king tseung kóh‐ti‐yān, kaau‐kwòh neĭ shaú; mŏ yat yān nāng‐tīk‐chuê neĭ‐teî.
9 Koò‐ts‘ź Yeukₒ‐shue‐à fat‐īn‐kaan lām‐tò, kòh‐ti‐yān shīng‐maăn hāng, yaū Kat‐kaapₒ sheŭng‐huì.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaí k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn paaî tsaú, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à taaî‐shaatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî, tsoî Kei‐p‘ìn chui shaatₒ, yaû ts‘ūng huì Paakₒ‐wōh‐lūn‐kè lô‐sheûng chui‐kón k‘uĭ‐teî, yat‐chīk tá‐tò À‐sai‐ka k‘āp Mă‐kei‐taaî teî‐fong.
11 Chùng‐yān tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn t‘ō‐tsaú, lōk‐tò Paakₒ‐wōh‐lūn kòh‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaí shēk‐t‘aū ts‘ūng t‘in‐sheûng kòng‐lōk chùng‐yān‐ch‘uè, yat‐lô tò À‐sai‐ka: lîng k‘uĭ‐teî sź‐mōng, peî shēk‐pōk tá‐sź‐kè yān, peí‐kaaù Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān yûng to shaatₒ‐sź‐kè yān, uēt‐faatₒ toh à.
12 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘aaĭ À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān kaau‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kóh‐yāt, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à t‘ó‐kò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tong Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn wâ, Īt‐t‘aū à, uên neĭ t‘īng‐chí tsoî Kei‐p‘ìn sheûng‐t‘aū; Uēt à, uên neĭ t‘īng‐chí tsoî À‐yē‐lūn shaan‐kuk sheûng‐t‘aū.
13 Ue‐shî yāt uēt to t‘īng‐chí, táng‐tò paakₒ‐sìng heùng tīk‐yān pò‐ch‘aū. Ni‐ti‐sẑ heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Yē‐shūt‐shue me? Kòh‐īt‐t‘aū t‘īng‐chí tsoî t‘in‐chung‐kaan, taaî‐yeukₒ yaŭ yat‐yāt m̄‐lōk.
14 Ts‘ź‐yāt chi‐ts‘īn, ts‘ź‐yāt chi‐haû, meî‐ts‘āng yaŭ kóm‐kè yāt‐tsź, tak Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐yeûng foó‐t‘eng yān sheng‐yam: yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tá‐cheùng.
15 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à t‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān faan‐huì Kat‐kaapₒ yīng‐p‘oōn.
16 ¶ Ni‐nğ‐kòh wōng t‘ō‐tsaú tsoî Mă‐kei‐taaî‐kè shaan‐ngaām ni‐maaī.
17 Yaŭ yān tuì Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Wán‐tó kóh‐nğ‐kòh wōng, tsoî Mă‐kei‐taaî‐kè shaan‐ngaām ni‐maaī.
18 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Tseung taaî‐shēk luk‐maaī shaan‐ngaām‐haú, p‘aaì yān tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè hon‐shaú:
19 Waī neĭ‐teî m̄‐hó hitₒ‐keukₒ, iù chui‐kón ch‘aū‐tīk, kung‐tá kòh‐ti tsoî meĭ‐haû‐kè; m̄‐peí k‘uĭ‐teî yāp tsẑ‐keí‐kè shēng: yan‐waî neĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ĭ‐king tseung k‘uĭ‐teî kaau‐kwòh neĭ‐teî shaú‐sheûng.
20 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān taaî‐taaî shaatₒ‐lūk, chì‐tò mīt‐saaì k‘uĭ‐teî waī chí, k‘eī‐chung yaŭ shîng‐hâ‐kè, tsaú‐yāp kin‐koò‐kè shēng.
21 Kóm‐tsaû paakₒ‐sìng on‐īn faan‐huì yīng‐p‘oōn, tò Mă‐kei‐taaî Yeukₒ‐shue‐à‐ch‘uè: mŏ yat‐kòh yān kóm hoi‐haú kung‐kik Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
22 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Neĭ‐teî tá‐hoi shaan‐ngaām‐haú, tséung kóh‐nğ‐kòh wōng taaì‐laī ngŏh‐ch‘uè.
23 Kóh‐ti yān tsaû kóm‐yeûng tsô, tseung Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng wōng, Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn wōng, Yē‐moōt wōng, La‐kat wōng, Aak‐lūn wōng, to taaì‐ch‘ut shaan‐ngaām.
24 Ĭ‐king taaì ni‐nğ‐kòh wōng tò Yeukₒ‐shue‐à‐ch‘uè, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à chiû Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān laī‐tò, tuì k‘uĭ shóh‐taaì‐huì tá‐cheùng‐kè ping‐t‘aū wâ, Neĭ‐teî hāng‐ts‘īn‐laī, tseung keukₒ taāp‐tsoî nğ‐kòh‐wōng‐kè kéng‐sheûng. K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû hāng‐ts‘īn, tseung keukₒ taāp‐chuê nğ‐kòh wōng‐kè kéng‐sheûng.
25 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à yaû tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî m̄‐hó king‐fong, m̄‐hó kuî‐p‘à, iù kin‐k‘eūng kong‐ngaî: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, pit chiù‐kóm‐yeûng toî neĭ‐teî shóh‐kung‐tá‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk.
26 Haû‐loī Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tseung nğ‐kòh wōng shaatₒ‐sź, kwà‐tsoînğ‐p‘oh‐shuê‐sheûng: yat‐chīk kwà‐tò aai‐maăn.
27 Īt‐t‘aū‐lōk kòh‐shī, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à fan‐foò‐ti yān, ts‘ūng shuê‐sheûng ning k‘uĭ‐teî shi‐hoī lōk‐laī, wing‐yāp k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐ni‐maaī‐kè shaan‐ngaām, tseung taaî‐shēk chai‐lōk ngaām‐haú, laū‐tò kam‐yāt.
28 ¶ Kóh‐yāt Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kung ts‘uí Mă‐kei‐taaî‐shēng, yûng to shaatₒ‐mīt kòh‐wōng, k‘āp faān chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung‐kè yān; mō yat‐kòh laū‐ts‘uēn: k‘uĭ toî Mă‐kei‐taaî wōng, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn toî Yē‐leî‐koh wōng kóm.
29 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, ts‘ūng Mă‐kei‐taaî huì‐tò Laāp‐nā, heùng Laāp‐nā kung‐tá:
30 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung Laāp‐nā kûng kòh‐wōng, kaau‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè shaú; Yeukₒ‐shue‐à yûng to laī‐kung‐shaatₒ kòh‐shēng, k‘āp faān chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung‐kè yān; yat‐kòh to mŏ laū‐ts‘uēn, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn toî Yē‐leî‐koh wōng kóm.
31 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à t‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, ts‘ūng Laāp‐nā huì‐tò La‐kat tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè on‐yīng, kung‐tá kòh‐shēng.
32 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung La‐kat kaau‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shaú‐sheûng, taî‐î yāt, tsaû tak‐hiu kòh‐shēng, yaû yûng to shaatₒ‐lūk, faān‐chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung‐kè yān, hó‐ts‘z̆ tsoî Laāp‐nā shóh‐hāng‐kè, yat‐yeûng.
33 ¶ Kóh‐shī Kei‐shik wōng Wōh‐laān laī pong‐chôh La‐kat; Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kung‐tá k‘uĭ k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, chì‐tò mŏ‐yat‐kòh laū‐lōk.
34 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à t‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān ts‘ūng La‐kat huì‐tò Aak‐lūn; tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè on‐yīng, kung‐tá kòh‐shēng:
35 Kóh‐yāt tuēt‐hiu shēng, yûng to laī shaatₒ, yaû tong kóh‐yāt mīt‐saaì faān chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung‐kè yān, hó‐ts‘z̆ tsoî La‐kat shóh‐hāng kè yat‐yeûng.
36 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à t‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, ts‘ūng Aak‐lūn huì‐tò Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn, kung‐tá kòh‐shēng.
37 Tuēt‐hiu shēng yûng to laī‐shaatₒ kòh‐wōng, kung‐tá shóh‐shūk‐kè shēng shaatₒ‐saaì, faān‐chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung‐kè yān; mŏ yat‐kòh laū‐ts‘uēn, hó‐ts‘z̆ tsoî Aak‐lūn shóh‐hāng kwòh‐kè; tsik‐haî mīt‐saaì shēng kûng chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung‐kè yān.
38 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à t‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, faan‐huì Taí‐pit; kung‐tá kòh‐shēng:
39 Tuēt‐hiu shēng chukₒ kòh‐wōng; yaû tuēt‐hiu faān‐shóh‐shūk‐kè shēng, yûng to laī shaatₒ, mīt‐saaì faān‐chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung‐kè yān; mŏ‐yat‐kòh laū‐lōk, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn tsoî Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn shóh‐hāng‐kè: uē‐kam toî Taí‐pit k‘āp kòh‐wōng; yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn toî Laāp‐nā k‘āp kòh‐wōng kóm.
40 ¶ Kóm‐tsaû Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsûn‐teî kung‐tá shaan‐teî, naām fong, p‘īng‐uēn, shaan‐p‘oh, kûng kóh‐ch‘uè‐kè wōng: mŏ yat‐kòh laū‐lōk, faān yaŭ‐heì‐kè to mīt‐saaì, haî tsun‐ts‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè.
41 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à shóh‐kung‐tá‐kè, tsik‐haî ts‘ūng Ka‐t‘itₒ‐pa‐neī‐à chì‐tò Ka‐saatₒ yaû ts‘ūng Hóh‐shaan ts‘uēn‐teî chì‐tò Kei‐p‘ìn.
42 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à yat‐ts‘aī tá‐paaî ni‐ti chùng‐wōng, tuēt‐hiu k‘uĭ‐kè teî, yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tá‐cheùng à.
43 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à t‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān faan‐huì Kat‐kaapₒ tò yīng‐p‘oōn.
