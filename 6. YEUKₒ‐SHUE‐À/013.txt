1 YEUKₒ‐SHUE‐À nīn‐keí lŏ‐maaî; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ nīn‐keí ĭ‐king lŏ‐maaî lokₒ, shóh‐shîng‐hâ meî‐ts‘āng‐tak‐tó‐kè teî, chûng yaŭ hó‐toh.
2 Shóh‐shîng‐hâ‐kè teî; tsik‐haî Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè kíng‐kaaì, k‘āp Kei‐shūt ts‘uēn‐teî,
3 Ts‘ūng Oi‐k‘āp ts‘īn‐pin‐kè Sai‐hotₒ, chì‐tò Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn heùng‐pak‐kè kíng‐kaaì, ni‐ti to shūk Ka‐naām‐yān‐kè teî: tsik‐haî Fei‐leî‐sẑ nğ‐kòh mūk‐cheúng‐kè teî; yaû haî Ka‐saatₒ‐yān, À‐shāt‐tāt‐yān, Ĭ‐shāt‐ka‐lūn‐yān, Ka‐tāk‐yān, Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn‐yān; k‘āp À‐ying‐yān‐kè teî:
4 Yaû‐yaŭ naām‐pîn À‐waî‐yān kè‐teî, Ka‐naām‐yān‐kè ts‘uēn‐teî, k‘āp shūk Sai‐tûn‐kè Maĭ‐à‐la, chì‐tò À‐fat, yaû chì‐tò À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān‐kè kíng‐kaaì:
5 Yaû yaŭ Kei‐pa‐lāk‐yān‐kè teî, heùng īt‐t‘aū‐ch‘ut‐ch‘uè‐kè ts‘uēn Leî‐pa‐nuên ts‘ūng Haak‐moōn‐shaan‐keukₒ‐kè Pa‐līk‐ka‐tak, chì‐tò yāp Hap‐moōt kóh‐ch‘uè.
6 Chuê‐tsoî shaan‐teî‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, ts‘ūng Leî‐pa‐nuên chì‐tò Māt‐leî‐fat‐mă‐yam, tsik‐haî Sai‐tûn chùng‐yān, ngŏh pit tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn kón‐saaì k‘uĭ‐teî: neĭ iù tsun‐ts‘ūng ngŏh shóh‐fan‐foò‐neĭ‐kè, chap‐ch‘aū fan‐hoi kóh‐ti teî, peí‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsô ch‘aán‐īp.
7 Uē‐kam neĭ iù tseung ts‘ź‐teî fan‐kwòh kaú chi‐p‘aaì, k‘āp Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì tsô ch‘aán‐īp,
8 Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì, ĭ‐king kûng Laū‐pîn Ka‐tak leŭng‐chi‐p‘aaì, t‘ūng shaû Moh‐sai shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, tsik‐haî tsoî Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh tung‐pîn, chiù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pūk‐yān Moh‐sai shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐kè;
9 Ts‘ūng À‐nuên‐kuk‐pin‐kè À‐lōh‐ĭ, k‘āp kuk‐noî‐kè shēng, kûng P‘ìn‐maĭ‐taí‐pa‐kè p‘īng‐uēn, chì‐tò Taí‐poón;
10 À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān wōng Sai‐wāng‐kè chùng‐shēng, tsik‐haî koón‐leĭ Hei‐shāt‐poón chì‐tò À‐moōn‐yān‐kè kíng‐kaaì;
11 Yaû‐yaŭ Kei‐līt kûng Kei‐shūt‐yān, k‘āp Mă‐ka‐yān‐kè kíng‐kaaì, yaū Haak‐moōn ts‘uēn‐shaan, Pa‐shaan ts‘uēn‐teî, chì‐tò Saatₒ‐ka;
12 Yaû‐yaŭ Pa‐shaan wōng Ngōk‐kè t‘ung kwokₒ, k‘uĭ haî koón‐leĭ À‐taaî‐lūk kûng Ĭ‐tak‐loī, tsik‐haî Leĭ‐fāt‐yam tsūk shóh‐laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè: yan ni‐ti to haî Moh‐sai shóh‐kung‐tá kón‐chūk‐kè.
13 Taân‐haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān m̄‐kón‐chūk Kei‐shūt‐yān, Mă‐ka‐yān: chì‐tò kam‐yāt, Kei‐shūt‐yān, Mă‐ka‐yān, yīng‐īn chuê‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chi‐chung.
14 Tūk‐haî m̄‐tseung ch‘aán‐īp ts‘z̀‐kwòh Leî‐meî chi‐p‘aaì; yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fóh‐tsaì‐kè tsaì‐māt, tsô k‘uĭ‐kè ch‘aán‐īp à, haî tsun‐ts‘ūng Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ‐kè.
15 ¶ Moh‐sai tseung ch‘aán‐īp ts‘z̀‐kwòh Laū‐pîn chi‐p‘aaì, haî chiù‐i k‘uĭ tsung‐tsūk.
16 K‘uĭ‐teî‐kè teî‐kaaì, ts‘ūng À‐nuên‐kuk‐pin‐kè À‐lōh‐ĭ, kûng kuk‐chung‐kè shēng, k‘āp kân‐chuê Maĭ‐taí‐pa ts‘uēn p‘īng‐uēn;
17 Hei‐shāt‐poón k‘āp kóh‐ch‘uè p‘īng‐uēn‐kè chùng‐shēng; Taí‐poón, Pa‐moōt‐pa‐līk, Paakₒ‐pa‐līk‐mĭn,
18 Yē‐hâ‐saatₒ, Kei‐taí‐mōk, Maĭ‐faatₒ‐aapₒ,
19 Kei‐līt‐t‘īng, Shāt‐mă, tsoî kuk‐chung shaan‐teî, Saatₒ‐līt‐shaatₒ‐hāt,
20 Paakₒ‐peí‐ĭ, Peí‐sẑ‐ka‐shaan‐p‘oh, Paakₒ‐yē‐sai‐moōt,
21 Yaû p‘īng‐uēn chùng‐shēng, k‘āp chuê‐tsoî Hei‐shāt‐poón, À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān‐kè wōng Sai‐wāng‐kè t‘ung‐kwokₒ, Moh‐sai yă‐ts‘āng kung‐tá Sai‐wāng, kûng Maĭ‐t‘īn‐yān‐kè chùng‐mūk‐cheúng, tsik‐haî chuê‐tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, Ĭ‐meî‐leĭ‐kam, So‐ĭ, Oô‐ĭ, Leĭ‐pa, k‘ui‐haî Sai‐wāng‐kè chue‐haū.
22 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shaatₒ kóh‐ti yān chi‐shī, yīk shaatₒ Peì‐ĭ‐kè tsaí hāng‐ts‘ē‐shūt‐kè Pa‐laān.
23 Laū‐pîn‐yān‐kè kíng‐kaaì, tsoî Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh k‘āp hōh‐pin. Ni‐ti haî Laū‐pîn‐yān‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî tsung‐tsūk shóh‐tak‐kè, kokₒ‐shēng kokₒ‐heung.
24 ¶ Moh‐sai tseung ch‘aán‐īp ts‘z̀‐kwòh Ka‐tak chi‐p‘aaì, tsik‐haî peí‐kwòh Ka‐tak‐yān‐kè kokₒ‐tsūk.
25 K‘uĭ‐kè teî‐kaaì, haî Ngă‐ch‘itₒ kûng Kei‐līt chùng‐shēng, k‘āp À‐moōn‐yān yat‐poòn‐kè teî, chì‐tò La‐pa ts‘īn‐pîn‐kè À‐lōh‐ĭ;
26 Yaû ts‘ūng Hei‐shāt‐poón chì‐tò La‐moōt‐māt‐sẑ‐pa kûng Peì‐toh‐nīng; yaû ts‘ūng Mă‐hap‐nîm chì‐tò Taí‐pit‐kè kíng‐kaaì;
27 Yaû tsoî shaan‐kuk‐chung‐kè Paakₒ‐à‐laān, Paakₒ‐nīng‐la, Shò‐kotₒ, Saatₒ‐fan, tsik‐haî Hei‐shāt‐poón wōng Sai‐wāng kwokₒ k‘eī‐uē‐kè teî, Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh waī kaaì, chì‐tò Kei‐neī‐līt hoí‐pin, tsoî Yeukₒ‐taân tung‐pîn.
28 Ni‐ti Ka‐tak‐yān‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, waāk shēng waāk heung, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî chi‐p‘aaì shóh‐tak‐kè.
29 ¶ Moh‐sai tseung ch‘aán‐īp ts‘z̀ kwòh Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì: Mă‐nā‐sai‐yān poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî kokₒ‐tsūk līt mīng.
30 K‘uĭ‐teî‐kè kíng‐kaaì, haî ts‘ūng Mă‐hap‐nîm, Pa‐shaan ts‘uēn‐teî, tsik‐haî Pa‐shaan wōng Ngōk‐kè t‘ung kwokₒ, k‘āp tsoî Pa‐shaan‐ngaaī‐ĭ‐kè chùng‐shēng, kûng yaŭ lūk‐shāp‐kòh‐shēng:
31 Kei‐līt‐kè yat‐poòn, kûng À‐taaî‐lūk, Ĭ‐tak‐loī leŭng‐kòh shēng, tsik‐haî Ngōk wōng‐kè Pa‐shaan kwokₒ‐kè shēng, chiù kokₒ‐tsūk ts‘z̀‐kwòh Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè tsaí Mă‐kat‐kè tsź‐suen, tsik‐haî yat‐poòn Mă‐kat‐yān.
32 ¶ Ĭ‐sheûng kokₒ‐ch‘uè teî‐fong haî Moh‐sai fan‐kwòh paakₒ‐sìng tsô ch‘aán‐īp, tsoî Moh‐aapₒ p‘īng‐uēn, Yeukₒ‐taân‐ngoî tung‐pîn, heùng‐chuê Yē‐leî‐koh‐kè.
33 Moh‐sai m̄‐tseung ch‘aán‐īp ts‘z̀‐kwòh Leî‐meî chi‐p‘aaì: yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, chiù Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐tuì‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐wâ‐kè.
