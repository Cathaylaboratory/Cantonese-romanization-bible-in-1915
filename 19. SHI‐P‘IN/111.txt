1 NEĬ‐TEÎ tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā. Ngŏh iù tsoî‐chìng‐chīk‐yān‐chi‐chung, k‘āp ooî‐chùng‐chi‐noî, yat‐sam chūk‐tsê Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐kung‐foo kīk‐taaî, yān yeūk‐háng ch‘ā‐ch‘aatₒ, tsaû pit‐tîng foon‐heí.
3 K‘uĭ‐chi‐tsokₒ‐waī, tsuen‐wīng, ī‐ch‘é īm‐suk: k‘uĭ‐chi‐kung‐î, wĭng‐uĕn laū‐ts‘uēn.
4 K‘uĭ‐chi î‐tsik, lîng yān keì‐nîm: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shi‐yan‐tín, faatₒ‐ts‘z̄‐pei.
5 K‘uĭ ts‘z̀ leūng‐shīk kwòh‐kìng‐waì‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐yān: yaû pit wĭng‐uĕn keì‐nîm tsẑ‐keí shóh‐laāp‐chi‐yeukₒ.
6 K‘uĭ‐yă‐ts‘āng tseung tsẑ‐keí shóh‐tsokₒ‐chi‐taaî‐nāng, hín‐în‐kwòh‐suén‐mān kìn, tseung î‐pong‐chi‐ch‘aán‐īp ts‘z̀‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ.
7 K‘uĭ shaú shóh‐tsokₒ‐ché, chan‐shāt kung‐î; k‘uĭ laāp‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, tsúng‐m̄‐kang‐koí.
8 Wĭng‐uĕn kin‐koò, chiù‐chan‐shāt chìng‐chīk ī ch‘itₒ‐laāp.
9 K‘uĭ yă‐ts‘āng kaù‐shūk k‘uĭ‐chi‐tsź‐mān: laāp‐wĭng‐uĕn‐chi‐yeukₒ: k‘uĭ‐chi‐mēng shìng‐kitₒ hóh‐waì.
10 Kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, naaĭ chì‐waî‐chi‐ch‘í: i‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐chi‐mîng‐ché, chì‐suèn ts‘ung‐mīng: Chué‐chi‐tsuen‐wīng, wĭng‐uĕn laū‐ts‘uēn.
