1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Neĭ iù kung‐tá Maĭ‐t‘īn‐yān, waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān pò‐ch‘aū: haû‐loī neĭ pit kwai neĭ līt‐tsó.
3 Moh‐sai tsaû tuì chùng‐paakₒ‐sìng wâ, Ts‘ūng neĭ‐teî chi‐chung iù kaán‐suén kw‘ān‐chùng, shaí k‘uĭ‐teî cha ping‐heì lām‐chân, huì kung‐tá Maĭ‐t‘īn‐yān, waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng Maĭ‐t‘īn‐yān pò‐ch‘aū.
4 Ĭ‐shik‐līt kokₒ‐chi‐p‘aaì, mooĭ chi‐p‘aaì iù tá‐faatₒ yat‐ts‘in‐yān huì tá‐cheùng.
5 Ue‐shî ts‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chung kaán‐ch‘ut yat‐maân‐î‐ts‘in yān, mooĭ chi‐p‘aaì yat‐ts‘in yān, to cha ping‐heì huì tá‐cheùng.
6 Moh‐sai tsaû tá‐faatₒ mooĭ chi‐p‘aaì‐kè yat‐ts‘in yān huì tá‐cheùng, yaû tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî kûng tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ‐kè tsaí Fei‐neī‐hap t‘ūng huì, Fei‐neī‐hap kw‘aī‐taaì shìng heì k‘āp hô‐t‘ūng.
7 Chùng‐yān tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè; tsaû kûng Maĭ‐t‘īn‐yān tá‐cheùng, shaatₒ‐saaì k‘uĭ‐teî naām‐ting.
8 Shóh‐shaatₒ chi‐noî, yīk shaatₒ Maĭ‐t‘īn‐yān chùng‐wōng; tsik‐haî Ĭ‐meî, Leĭ‐kam, So‐ĭ, Oô‐ĭ, Leĭ‐pa nğ‐kòh wōng: yaû yûng to shaatₒ Peì‐ĭ‐kè tsaí Pa‐laān.
9 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān lŏ‐leūk Maĭ‐t‘īn‐kè foŏ‐yān, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐teî tsaí‐nuĭ, yaû ts‘eúng‐tuēt k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐yaŭ‐kè ch‘uk‐shang yeūng‐kw‘ān, k‘āp yat‐ts‘aì ts‘oī‐māt.
10 Yûng fóh fān‐shiu k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐chuê‐kè shēng, k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ‐kè yīng‐p‘oōn.
11 Yaû tseung shóh‐chukₒ‐wōk‐kè yān‐haú, shóh‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, uĕ‐k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ‐kè yat‐ts‘aì ts‘oī‐māt.
12 To taaì‐tò Moh‐aapₒ kw‘òng‐yĕ, Yē‐leî‐koh tuì‐mîn, kân‐chuê Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐pin‐kè yīng‐p‘oōn, tò Moh‐sai kûng tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt ooî‐chùng‐ch‘uè.
13 ¶ Moh‐sai kûng tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ k‘āp ooî‐chùng mūk‐cheúng, t‘ūng‐ch‘ut yīng‐ngoî yīng‐tsipₒ k‘uĭ‐teî.
14 Moh‐sai nau‐nô ping‐t‘aū, tsik‐haî ts‘in‐tsúng, paakₒ‐pá‐tsúng, tá‐cheùng chi‐haû faan‐laī‐kè.
15 Moh‐sai tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ laū‐ts‘uēn chùng‐foŏ‐nuĭ me?
16 T‘eng‐ts‘ūng Pa‐laān‐kè kaì‐maū, yaŭ‐waāk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān paaì Peí‐ĭ, tak‐tsuî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, chì‐lîng ooî‐chung yaŭ wan‐yīk.
17 Tsaû‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî lokₒ, shóh‐ĭ iù shaatₒ‐saaì k‘eī‐chung‐kè naām‐tsaí, uĕ‐k‘āp shaatₒ‐saaì yă‐ts‘āng kûng naām‐yān t‘ūng‐fōng‐kè foŏ‐nuĭ.
18 Waī‐haî meî kûng naām‐yān t‘ūng‐fōng‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ, hóh‐ĭ laū‐ts‘uēn kwai‐ue neĭ‐teî.
19 Yaû iù chuê‐tsoî yīng‐ngoî, ts‘at yāt kòm noî: faān haî shaatₒ‐kwòh yān‐kè, k‘āp moh‐kwòh‐sź‐shi‐kè, taî‐saam yāt, taî‐ts‘at yāt, iù kitₒ‐tsîng shan‐t‘aí, yīk iù peí lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān kitₒ‐tsîng shan‐t‘aí.
20 Shóh‐yaŭ‐kè i‐fūk, waāk yeūng‐p‘eī, waāk shaan‐yeūng‐mō, waāk mūk‐chíng‐kè māt, to iù kitₒ‐tsîng k‘uĭ.
21 Tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ tuì tá‐cheùng faan‐laī‐kè ping‐wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè lūt‐laî haî kóm;
22 Waī kòh‐ti kam, ngān, t‘ūng, t‘itₒ, sekₒ, uēn,
23 Fóh pat‐nāng shiu‐waaî‐kè māt, pit iù king‐kwòh fóh, īn‐haû suèn waī kitₒ‐tsîng: yīk iù yûng pīt‐hoi‐kè shuí laī saí‐tsîng: faān fóh nāng shiu‐waaî‐kè māt, pit iù yûng shuí saí‐kwòh.
24 Tò taî‐ts‘at yāt, iù saí i‐fūk, tsẑ‐keí kitₒ‐tsîng, īn‐haû hóh‐ĭ yāp yīng.
25 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
26 Neĭ kûng tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ k‘āp ooî‐chùng tsūk‐cheúng, tseung shóh‐yaŭ ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè, waāk yān‐haú, waāk ch‘uk‐shang, to iù shó‐kwòh‐saaì:
27 Yaû k‘aaĭ‐laī fan‐hoi leŭng‐fân; yat fân peí‐kwòh‐lām chân tá‐cheùng‐kè ping, yat fân peí‐kwòh ooî‐chùng:
28 Kiù tá‐cheùng‐kè kwan‐ping, tseung shóh‐tak‐kè yān‐haú, k‘āp ngaū, luī, yeūng: ning mooĭ nğ‐paakₒ‐fân‐chi‐yat naāp‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā:
29 Tsik‐haî ts‘ūng k‘uĭ kóh yat‐poòn chi‐chung ning ch‘ut, kaau‐kwòh tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô kuí‐tsaì.
30 Yaû tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shóh‐tak kóh yat‐poòn chi‐chung, waāk yān‐haú, waāk ngaū, luī, yeūng, tsik‐haî chùng ch‘uk‐shang, ning mooĭ nğ‐shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat, peí‐kwòh hon‐shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐shìng‐shóh‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān.
31 Moh‐sai kûng tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, tsaû tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè laī hāng.
32 Tsoî kwan‐ping shóh‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè ts‘oī‐māt chi‐ngoî, shóh‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè, yaŭ yeūng lūk‐shāp‐ts‘at‐maân‐nğ‐ts‘in chekₒ,
33 Ngaū ts‘at‐maân‐î‐ts‘in chekₒ,
34 Luī lūk‐maân‐yat‐ts‘in p‘at,
35 T‘ūng‐nuĭ saam‐maân‐î‐ts‘in, meî‐ts‘āng kûng naām‐yān t‘ūng‐fōng‐kè.
36 Tá‐cheùng‐kè kwan‐ping shóh‐tak‐kè yat‐poòn, kûng‐kaì yeūng saam‐shāp‐saam‐maân‐ts‘at‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ chekₒ:
37 Ts‘ūng k‘eī‐chung naāp‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeūng, lūk‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐nğ chekₒ.
38 Ngaū saam‐maân‐lūk‐ts‘in chekₒ; ts‘ūng k‘eī‐chung naāp‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè, ts‘at‐shāp‐î chekₒ.
39 Luī saam‐maân‐līng‐nğ‐paakₒ chekₒ; ts‘ūng k‘eī‐chung naāp‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè, lūk‐shāp‐yat chekₒ.
40 Yān‐haú yat‐maân‐lūk‐ts‘in; ts‘ūng k‘eī‐chung naāp‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè, saam‐shāp‐î yān.
41 Moh‐sai tseung shóh‐yaŭ naāp‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô kuí‐tsaì‐kè, kaau‐kwòh tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò.
42 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shóh‐tak‐kè yat‐poòn, haî Moh‐sai ts‘ūng kwan‐ping shóh‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè, k‘aaĭ‐laī fan‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî,
43 (Tsik‐haî peí‐kwòh ooî‐chùng kóh‐yat‐poòn, kûng‐kaì yeūng saam‐shāp‐saam‐maân‐ts‘at‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ chekₒ,
44 Ngaū saam‐maân‐lūk‐ts‘in chekₒ,
45 Luī saam‐maân‐līng‐nğ‐paakₒ chekₒ,
46 Yān‐haú yat‐maân‐lūk‐ts‘in;)
47 Moh‐sai ts‘ūng ni‐ti Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shóh‐tak kóh‐yat‐poòn, m̄‐lûn yān‐haú waāk ch‘uk‐shang, to ning nğ‐shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat, peí‐kwòh hon‐shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐shìng‐shóh‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān; haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
48 Koón‐hāt kokₒ‐kwan‐kè ping‐t‘aū, tsik‐haî ts‘in‐tsúng, paakₒ‐pá‐tsúng, to‐laī tò Moh‐sai‐ch‘uè.
49 Tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Faān shūk‐ngŏh‐teî k‘uēn‐hâ‐kè ping, pūk‐yān ĭ‐king shó‐kwòh, k‘eī‐chung to mŏ shiú yat‐kòh‐yān.
50 Ngŏh‐teî to ning kokₒ‐yān shóh‐tak‐kè kam‐heì, kam‐līn, shaú‐aakₒ, kaaì‐chí, ĭ‐waān, k‘āp kam‐chue‐ch‘uèn, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô laĭ‐māt, tsoî Sheûng‐Taì mîn‐ts‘īn waî tsẑ‐keí shūk‐tsuî.
51 Moh‐sai kûng tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ tsaû shau shóh‐ning‐kè kam, tsik‐haî kokₒ‐yeûng tsô‐shīng‐kè kam‐heì.
52 Ts‘in‐tsúng, paakₒ‐pá‐tsúng, hìn‐kwòh‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè kam, kûng‐kaì yat‐maân‐lūk‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp shī‐haakₒ.
53 (Yan‐waî kwan‐ping shóh‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè ts‘oī‐māt, kòh‐kòh to k‘aaĭ‐laī kwai‐ue tsẑ‐keí.)
54 Ue‐shî Moh‐sai kûng tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ shaû‐hiu ts‘in‐tsúng, paakₒ‐pá‐tsúng‐kè kam, taaì‐tò ooî‐mōk, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsô keì‐hô.
