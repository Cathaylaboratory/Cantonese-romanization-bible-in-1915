1 WŌNG tá‐faatₒ yān chiu‐tsaāp Yaū‐taaî kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè chùng‐cheúng‐lŏ.
2 Wōng tsaû sheŭng‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, Yaū‐taaî chùng‐yān pìng chuê‐tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè chùng‐yān, k‘āp tsaì‐sz, sin‐chi kûng yat‐ts‘aì paakₒ‐sìng, m̄‐lûn taaî‐siú to t‘ūng‐maaī sheŭng‐huì: wōng tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐noî shóh‐wán‐tó yeukₒ‐shue‐chung‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tūk‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî t‘eng.
3 Wōng k‘eĭ tsoî t‘oī‐sheûng, haí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn laāp‐yeukₒ, pit tsûn‐sam, tsûn‐sìng, shûn‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsun‐shaú k‘uĭ kaaì‐mîng, faatₒ‐tô, laĭ‐ī, shīng‐tsaû ts‘ź‐shue‐chung shóh‐sé‐kè yeukₒ‐īn. Paakₒ‐sìng to ying‐shīng ts‘ūng ts‘ź‐yeukₒ.
4 Wōng fan‐foò tsaì‐sz‐cheúng Hei‐lāk‐ka, kûng taî‐î paan tsaì‐sz, k‘āp hon‐shaú tîn‐uk‐moōn‐kè yān, tseung waî Pa‐līk, waî mūk‐ch‘uĕ, waî t‘in‐sheûng maân‐kwan shóh‐chaì‐tsô‐kè heì‐mĭng, ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk poon‐ch‘ut‐laī: tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐ngoî, haí K‘ap‐lūn‐kè t‘īn fān‐shiu, ning kòh‐ti fooi tò Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî.
5 Kóh‐shī yaŭ‐kòh‐fûng‐sẑ ngaŭ‐tseûng‐kè tsaì‐sz, sik‐yāt Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐laāp laī fān‐heung, tsoî Yaū‐taaî kokₒ‐shēng‐kè yau‐t‘aān, k‘āp tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng sz̀‐waī teî‐fong; ch‘uē‐huì k‘uĭ‐teî, kim‐maaī heùng Pa‐līk, heùng yāt‐uēt chùng‐hāng‐sing, līn t‘in‐sheûng maân‐kwan fān‐heung‐kè yān.
6 Yaû tseung mūk‐ch‘uĕ‐kè tseûng, ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk poon‐ch‘ut‐huì Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐ngoî, haí K‘ap‐lūn‐k‘ai fān‐shiu, chung‐suì‐shīng‐fán, k‘aaĭ kóh‐ti fán saatₒ‐tsoî mān‐kè fān‐mô.
7 Yaû waí‐ch‘aakₒ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk chak‐pin waān‐t‘ūng shóh‐chuê‐kè uk, tsik‐haî foŏ‐nuĭ waî mūk‐ch‘uĕ chik līm‐mōk‐kè uk.
8 Yaû ts‘ūng Yaū‐taaî kokₒ‐shēng taaì chùng‐tsaì‐sz ch‘ut‐huì oo‐mooĭ tsaì‐sz fān‐heung‐kè yau‐t‘aān, ts‘ūng Kei‐pa chì‐tò Pīt‐shî‐pa, ī‐ch‘é waí‐ch‘aakₒ shēng‐moōn chak‐pin‐kè yau‐t‘aān, ts‘ź yau‐t‘aān tsoî shēng‐tsoí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à‐kè moōn, shēng‐moōn‐kè tsóh‐pîn.
9 Taân yau‐t‘aān‐kè tsaì‐sz, m̄‐sheŭng‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè t‘aān, waī‐haî tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî hing‐taî‐chung, shīk mō‐kaaù‐péng.
10 Yaû oo‐mooĭ tsoî Pîn‐yan‐nuên‐kuk‐kè T‘ōh‐faatₒ, táng mŏ yān shaí‐tak k‘uĭ‐teî tsaí‐nuĭ, waî Moh‐lokₒ king‐kwòh fóh‐chung.
11 Yaû ch‘uē Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng paaì‐yāt‐kè mă, tsoî yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn kóh‐ch‘uè, t‘aaì‐kaàm Nā‐taan‐maĭ‐lāk‐kè uk‐pin, yaû yûng fóh fān‐shiu paaì‐yāt‐kè chùng‐ch‘e.
12 Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng tsoî À‐hap‐sẑ laū‐téng‐sheûng, shóh‐chuk‐kè tsaì‐t‘aān, k‘āp Mă‐nā‐sai tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè î‐uên‐chung, shóh‐chuk‐kè tsaì‐t‘aān, wōng to waí‐ch‘aakₒ‐saaì, ts‘ūng kóh‐ch‘uè k‘ing‐waí‐hiu, tsaû tseung‐ti‐ch‘ān tó‐tsoî K‘ap‐lūn‐k‘ai‐ch‘uè.
13 Yaû Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn sik‐yāt tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng ts‘īn ts‘ē‐p‘ik‐shaan yaû‐pîn, shóh‐chuk yau‐t‘aān, fûng‐sẑ Sai‐tûn‐yān‐kè hóh‐tsang‐ché À‐taaî‐lūk, Moh‐aapₒ‐yān‐kè hóh‐tsang‐ché Kei‐mootₒ. À‐moōn‐yān‐kè hóh‐tsang‐ché Māt‐kwan, wōng to oo‐mooĭ‐saaì.
14 Yaû waí‐waaî chùng‐tseûng, hóm‐t‘uĕn kokₒ‐mūk‐ch‘uĕ, k‘aaí yān‐kè kwat ch‘ung‐moŏn kóh‐ch‘uè.
15 Kim‐chi Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt haâm‐lōk‐tsuî, sik‐yāt tsoî Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî kìn‐chuk yau‐t‘aān, to waí‐ch‘aakₒ, ī‐ch‘é fān‐shiu, chung‐suì‐shīng‐fán, yaû fān‐shiu mūk‐ch‘uĕ.
16 Yeukₒ‐sai‐à chuèn‐shan t‘aí‐kìn shaan‐chak‐pin‐kè fān‐mô, tsaû tá‐faatₒ yān huì ts‘uí fān‐mô‐luĭ‐kè kwat, fān‐tsoî yau‐sheûng laī oo‐mooĭ‐t‘aān, chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsik‐haî Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān, uê‐sin lûn‐k‘āp ni‐ti‐sẑ shóh‐kóng‐kè.
17 Yeukₒ‐sai‐à wâ, Ngŏh shóh‐kìn‐kè, haî mat‐yĕ pei‐shēk ni? Kòh‐shēng‐luĭ‐kè yān tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Sik‐yāt yaŭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān ts‘ūng Yaū‐taaî laī, uê‐sin kóng neĭ kam‐yāt shóh‐hāng‐kè sẑ, kung Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî‐kè t‘aān, ni‐ti tsaû haî k‘uĭ‐kè fān‐mô à.
18 Yeukₒ‐sai‐à wâ, Yaū‐tak‐k‘uĭ la; m̄‐hó peí yān ts‘in‐ī k‘uĭ‐kè hoī‐kwat. Chùng‐yān tsaû yaū‐tak k‘uĭ‐kè hoī‐kwat, to yaū‐tak ts‘ūng Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐laī‐kè sin‐chi‐kè hoī‐kwat haí‐ch‘uè kà.
19 Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à kokₒ‐shēng‐chung yau‐t‘aān‐kè tîn, haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè līt‐wōng shóh‐kìn, kon‐faân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè nau‐nô, Yeukₒ‐sai‐à to ch‘uē‐huì, chiù‐i k‘uĭ heùng Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî shóh‐hāng‐kè laī‐hāng.
20 Yaû tseung tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè yau‐t‘aān‐kè tsaì‐sz, to shaatₒ‐tsoî t‘aān‐sheûng, ī‐ch‘é fān‐shiu yān‐kwat tsoî t‘aān‐sheûng, faan‐huì Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng.
21 ¶ Wōng fan‐foò chùng‐paakₒ‐sìng wâ, Iù waî neĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ, chiù‐i shóh‐sé‐tsoî yeukₒ‐shue‐sheûng‐kè.
22 Tsẑ sẑ‐sz shám‐p‘oòn Ĭ‐shik‐līt chi‐shī, k‘āp haí Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng chi‐shī, ts‘ūng meî‐yaŭ‐shaú‐kwòh ni‐yeûng‐kè uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ;
23 Taân‐haî Yeukₒ‐sai‐à wōng taî‐shāp‐paatₒ nīn, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaú ts‘ź uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ.
24 Faān‐tsoî Yaū‐taaî‐teî kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng wán‐tak k‘ui‐kwaí‐kè, t‘iù‐shān‐kè, ka‐t‘ōng‐kè tseûng, k‘āp ngaŭ‐tseûng, kûng faān hóh‐tsang‐chi‐māt, Yeukₒ‐sai‐à to ch‘uē‐huì, chì‐shīng‐tsaû lūt‐faatₒ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, sé‐tsoî tsaì‐sz Hei‐lāk‐ka, shóh‐ngaŭ kìn‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk yeukₒ‐shue‐sheûng kè.
25 Tsoî Yeukₒ‐sai‐à ĭ‐ts‘īn, mŏ wōng ts‘z̆‐tak k‘uĭ tsûn‐sam, tsûn‐sìng, tsûn‐līk, chuen‐heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsun‐hāng Moh‐sai‐kè yat‐ts‘aì lūt‐faatₒ; ĭ‐haû yīk mŏ wōng ts‘z̆ k‘uĭ‐kè.
26 Taân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā m̄‐chí‐sik k‘uĭ‐kè chàn‐nô, tsik‐haî kung Yaū‐taaî‐kè nau‐nô, yan Mă‐nā‐sai shóh‐kon‐faân k‘uĭ nô‐kè kokₒ‐sẑ à.
27 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh tseung ts‘in‐ī Yaū‐taaî, leī‐hoi ngŏh ngaăn‐ts‘īn, hó‐ts‘z̆ ngŏh ts‘āng ts‘in‐ī Ĭ‐shik‐līt yat‐yeûng, ī‐ch‘é tiu‐heì ts‘ź Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè shēng, ngŏh shóh‐kaán‐suén‐kè, yīk tiu‐heì ts‘ź‐tîn, tsik‐haî ngŏh ts‘āng kóng‐k‘āp‐kè wâ, Ngŏh mēng pit‐tîng tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè.
28 Yeukₒ‐sai‐à k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, k‘āp k‘uĭ faān shóh‐hāng‐kè, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
29 Tong Yeukₒ‐sai‐à chi‐shī, Oi‐k‘āp wōng Faatₒ‐lŏ‐neī‐koh sheŭng‐tò Paakₒ‐laāt‐hōh, seúng‐kung‐tá À‐shūt wōng: Yeukₒ‐sai‐à wōng huì tuì‐tīk k‘uĭ; Oi‐k‘āp wōng uê‐kìn Yeukₒ‐sai‐à, tsoî Maĭ‐kat‐toh shaatₒ k‘uĭ.
30 K‘uĭ‐kè shān‐pūk tsoì k‘uĭ‐kè shi tsoî ch‘e‐sheûng, ts‘ūng Maĭ‐kat‐toh sùng‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, tsòng‐tsoî k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí‐kè fān‐mô. Ts‘ź‐teî‐kè mān tseung Yeukₒ‐sai‐à‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz kò k‘uĭ, kaì foô‐ts‘an tsô wōng.
31 ¶ Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz tang‐waî chi‐shī, nīn‐keí î‐shāp‐saam suì; tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng saam‐kòh uēt. K‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an mēng‐kiù Hap‐mŏ‐taāt, haî Laāp‐nā‐yān Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐kè nuĭ.
32 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ, hōk‐faan k‘uĭ līt‐tsó faān‐shóh‐hāng‐kè.
33 Faatₒ‐lŏ‐neī‐koh ts‘aū‐kàm k‘uĭ tsoî Hap‐moōt‐teî‐kè Laāp‐la, m̄‐peí k‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng; ī‐ch‘é fāt Yaū‐taaî‐kwokₒ ngān shāp‐nğ‐maân, kam yat‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ.
34 Faatₒ‐lŏ‐neī‐koh laāp Yeukₒ‐sai‐à‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐à‐kam tsô wōng, kaì k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an Yeukₒ‐sai‐à koí k‘uĭ mēng kiù Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam: taaì Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz tò Oi‐k‘āp, k‘uĭ tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè sź.
35 Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam k‘aaĭ ngān kûng kam peí‐kwòh Faatₒ‐lŏ; tsun‐i Faatₒ‐lŏ‐kè mîng laī‐heùng kwokₒ‐mān ts‘uí‐heúng, táng tak kam‐ngān peí‐kwòh Faatₒ‐lŏ: hoh‐haak ts‘uí kwokₒ‐mān‐kè kam‐ngān, kokₒ‐yān òn‐chuê k‘uĭ shóh‐ching‐shau‐kè tîng‐shò, k‘aaĭ‐laī peí‐kwòh Faatₒ‐lŏ‐neī‐koh.
36 ¶ Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam tang‐waî chi‐shī, nīn‐keí î‐shāp‐nğ suì; tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng shāp‐yat nīn. K‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an mēng‐kiù Sai‐pò‐taaî, haî Lŏ‐mă‐yān Peí‐taaî‐à‐kè nuĭ.
37 Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ, hōk‐faan k‘uĭ līt‐tsó faān‐shóh‐hāng‐kè.
