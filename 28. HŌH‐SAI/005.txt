1 NEĬ‐TEÎ tsaì‐sz‐kè, iù‐t‘eng‐ni‐ti la; Ĭ‐shik‐lit‐kè ka, iù‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng la; wōng‐kè ka iù‐chak‐ĭ la; shám‐p‘oòn haî‐kwaan‐ue neĭ à, yan‐waî tsoî Māt‐sẑ‐pa tsô‐kwòh kei‐laâm, yaû tsoî Taaî‐poh tsô‐kwòh p‘aaī‐hoi‐kè lōh‐mŏng à.
2 Ī‐ch‘é tsokₒ‐faán‐kè shaatₒ‐lūk kwòh‐toh, ngŏh pit‐tuk‐chaakₒ‐k‘uĭ‐teî à.
3 Ngŏh‐shik‐tak Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn, Ĭ‐shik‐līt heùng‐ngŏh‐to‐m̄‐tak yán‐maaī: Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn à, neĭ shāt‐hāng‐kaan‐yām lokₒ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt to‐peî tìm‐oo‐kè.
4 Shóh‐tsokₒ‐kè sẑ‐ts‘īng, chóh‐chuê k‘uĭ‐pat‐nāng kwai‐heùng Sheûng‐Taì: yan yām‐sam tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐chung, k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐shik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā là.
5 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè kiu‐ngô, tong‐mîn tuì‐k‘uĭ‐tsô‐chìng, shóh‐ĭ Ĭ‐shik‐līt kûng Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn hāng‐kán‐tsuî‐hin ooĭ‐shat‐keukₒ; Yaū‐t‘aaì yīk‐t‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ shat‐keukₒ‐kè.
6 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐taaì‐maaī ngaū‐kw‘ān, yeūng‐kw‘ān, sheŭng‐laī ts‘ām‐k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; waī‐haî m̄‐wán‐tak‐tó; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘uì‐leī k‘uĭ‐teî à.
7 K‘uĭ‐teî ts‘āng hāng kwaí‐mă‐kè sẑ, kung‐kik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: yaû‐shang‐î‐tsź: uē‐kam‐uēt‐shokₒ ooĭ‐t‘an‐mīt‐k‘uĭ‐teî, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè t‘īn.
8 ¶ Iù‐tsoî‐K‘eī‐peì‐à ch‘ui‐heúng‐kokₒ, yaû tsoî La‐mă ch‘ui‐hô‐t‘ūng: yaû tsoî Paakₒ‐à‐mān fòng‐kíng‐síng‐kè sheng, Pîn‐ngă‐măn à, ts‘ź‐sẑ tsoî‐neĭ‐haû‐pîn lokₒ.
9 Tong tuk‐chaakₒ‐kè yāt, Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn pit‐shīng‐waī fong‐faì: tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt chi‐p‘aaì‐chi‐chung, ngŏh ts‘āng‐hín‐mīng tseung‐loī‐pit‐yaŭ‐kè sẑ lokₒ.
10 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè kwan‐cheúng, hó‐ts‘z̆ ī‐t‘īn‐kaaì‐kè yān kóm: ngŏh pit‐k‘aaĭ‐nau‐nô, hó‐ts‘z̆ shuí‐cham‐lōk‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐ch‘uè.
11 Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn shaû‐naân, to peî‐shám‐p‘oòn‐aatₒ‐waaî, yan‐waî k‘uĭ kom‐sam i‐ts‘ūng‐mîng‐lîng‐laī‐hāng‐kè.
12 Shóh‐ĭ ngŏh heùng Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn hó‐ts‘z̆ chuè‐ch‘ūng, heùng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐chi‐ka, hó‐ts‘z̆ waí‐laân‐kóm.
13 Tong Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn kokₒ‐tak yaŭ pêng, yaû Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐tak shaû‐sheung‐chi‐shī, Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn tsaû‐huì À‐shūt‐ch‘uè, yaû tá‐faatₒ‐yān‐huì Ngă‐līt wōng‐ch‘uè: waī‐haî k‘uĭ‐pat‐nāng‐i‐hó neĭ, yaû m̄‐i‐tak‐neĭ‐kè sheung‐haú.
14 Yan‐waî ngŏh‐pit‐heùng Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn tsô‐taaî‐sz, yaû heùng‐Yaū‐t‘aaì tsô‐siú‐sz: ngŏh tsik‐haî ngŏh ooĭ‐maakₒ‐līt, yaû huì‐hiu; ngŏh pit‐lŏ‐leūk, to‐mŏ‐yān ooĭ‐kaù‐faan.
15 Ngŏh ooĭ‐huì yaû‐faan‐kwai poón‐ch‘uè, táng‐tò k‘uĭ‐teî yîng tsẑ‐keí‐kè tsuî, yaû ts‘ām‐k‘aū kìn‐ngŏh‐mîn: shaû‐foó‐naân‐chi‐shī, k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐iù‐hán‐ts‘itₒ ts‘ām‐k‘aū ngŏh à.
