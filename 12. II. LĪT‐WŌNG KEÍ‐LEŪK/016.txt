1 TONG Leĭ‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka taî‐shāp‐ts‘at nīn, Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐taam‐kè tsaí À‐hap‐sẑ tang‐waî.
2 À‐hap‐sẑ tang‐waî chi‐shī, nīn‐keí î‐shāp suì, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng shāp‐lūk nīn, m̄‐ts‘z̆ k‘uĭ tsó‐foô Taaî‐waî hāng k‘uĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn waī‐shîn‐kè.
3 K‘uĭ hōk‐faan Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng shóh‐tsô, lîng k‘uĭ tsaí king‐kwòh fóh‐chung, chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn shóh‐kón‐chūk‐chi‐î‐pong‐yān‐kè hāng‐waī.
4 Yaû hìn‐tsaì fān‐heung tsoî yau‐t‘aān, tsoî ko‐shaan‐chi‐sheûng, k‘āp kokₒ‐ts‘eng‐shuê‐chi‐hâ.
5 Kóh‐shī À‐laān wōng Leĭ‐sùn, k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Leĭ‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka, sheŭng‐laī kung‐tá Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng: waī‐kw‘àn À‐hap‐sẑ, taân pat‐nāng shìng‐tak k‘uĭ.
6 Tong‐shī À‐laān wōng Leĭ‐sùn, tak‐faan Ĭ‐laāt, lîng kwai À‐laān, kón‐chūk Yaū‐taaî‐yān ch‘ut Ĭ‐laāt: À‐laān‐yān laī‐tò Ĭ‐laāt, haí kòh‐ch‘uè kui‐chuê, chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
7 Kóm‐tsaû À‐hap‐sẑ tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché huì kìn À‐shūt wōng Tik‐laāp‐peí‐leî‐sz, wâ, Ngŏh haî neĭ‐kè pūk, haî neĭ‐kè tsaí: uē‐kam À‐laān wōng k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng kung‐tá ngŏh, ts‘éng neĭ laī kaù ngŏh, t‘uetₒ‐leī k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shaú.
8 À‐hap‐sẑ tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn, k‘āp wōng‐kung foó‐foò‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè kam‐ngān, sùng‐kwòh À‐shūt tsô laĭ‐māt.
9 À‐shūt wōng wăn‐chún k‘uĭ shóh‐k‘aū: huì‐kung‐tá Taaî‐mă‐shik tuēt‐hiu, shaatₒ Leĭ‐sùn, tseung shēng‐chung‐kè paakₒ‐sìng lŏ‐leūk tò Kat‐ch‘uè.
10 À‐hap‐sẑ wōng huì‐tò Taaî‐mă‐shik, yīng‐tsipₒ À‐shūt wōng Tik‐laāp‐peí‐leî‐sz, t‘aí‐kìn tsoî Taaî‐mă‐shik‐kè yat‐kòh tsaì‐t‘aān: À‐hap‐sẑ wōng tseung t‘aān‐kè yeûng‐tsź waāk‐ch‘ut, shóh‐yaŭ kung‐tsokₒ‐kè yīng‐t‘ō, keì‐huì tsaì‐sz Oo‐leĭ‐à‐ch‘uè.
11 Tsaì‐sz Oo‐leĭ‐à tsaû kìn‐chuk yat‐kòh‐t‘aān, chiù‐i À‐hap‐sẑ ts‘ūng Taaî‐mă‐shik keì‐laī‐kè yīng‐t‘ō: kóm‐yeûng kìn‐chuk, táng‐haû wōng ts‘ūng Taaî‐mă‐shik faan‐laī.
12 Wōng tsẑ Taaî‐mă‐shik faan‐laī, kìn‐kóh‐t‘aān tsaû ts‘an‐kân: haí t‘aān‐sheûng hìn‐tsaì.
13 Shiu faān‐tsaì, sò‐tsaì, yaû tîn‐tsaú, k‘aaĭ ch‘aū‐yan‐tsaì shang‐kè huetₒ shá‐lōk t‘aān‐sheûng.
14 Yaû tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn‐kè t‘ūng‐t‘aān, ts‘ūng tin‐ts‘īn, tsik‐haî san‐t‘aān, kûng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐kè chung‐kaan, poon tò san‐t‘aān‐kè pak‐pin.
15 À‐hap‐sẑ wōng fan‐foò tsaì‐sz Oo‐leĭ‐à wâ, Chiu‐tsó‐kè faān‐tsaì, aai‐maăn‐kè sò‐tsaì, k‘āp kwokₒ‐chung chùng‐mān‐kè faān‐tsaì, sò‐tsaì, tîn‐tsaú, k‘ui iù shiu‐tsoî taaî‐t‘aān‐sheûng, faān‐tsaì‐shang k‘āp ch‘aū‐yan‐tsaì‐shang‐kè huetₒ, yīk iù shá‐lōk ts‘ź‐t‘aān‐sheûng: taân t‘ūng‐t‘aān iù ngŏh yûng‐laī ts‘éng‐mân Sheûng‐Taì à.
16 Tsaì‐sz Oo‐leĭ‐à tsun‐ts‘ūng wōng shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè laī‐tsô.
17 À‐hap‐sẑ wōng tá‐t‘uēn yūk‐p‘oōn kokₒ‐tsôh‐kè pin‐laān, poon‐hoi p‘oōn leī‐hiu tsôh; yaû tseung t‘ūng‐hoí ts‘ūng shīng‐hoí‐kè t‘ūng‐ngaū‐sheûng poon‐lōk‐laī, chai‐tsoî p‘o‐fa‐shēk‐ch‘uè.
18 Ī‐ch‘é Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐luĭ shóh‐tsô che‐k‘oì‐kè lô, waî on‐sik‐yāt shóh‐yûng‐kè, kûng wōng tsẑ‐ngoî yāp tîn shóh‐hāng chi‐lōng, À‐hap‐sẑ waî À‐shūt wōng tseung‐laī ī‐hoi Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn.
19 À‐hap‐sẑ k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
20 À‐hap‐sẑ kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám, tsòng‐tsoî Taaî‐waî shēng, haí k‘uĭ līt‐tsó‐kè fān‐teî: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Hei‐sai‐ka kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
