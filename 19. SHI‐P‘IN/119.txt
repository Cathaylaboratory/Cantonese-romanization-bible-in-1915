1 HĀNG‐WAĪ chìng‐chīk, i‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ‐ché, kòh‐ti‐yān yaŭ‐fuk.
2 Shaú k‘uĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn, chuen‐sam‐k‘aū‐Sheûng‐Taì‐ché, kòh‐ti‐yān yaŭ‐fuk.
3 K‘uĭ m̄‐tsô okₒ‐sẑ: tūk‐haî iù hāng Chué lô.
4 Neĭ yă‐ts‘āng fan‐foò yān, kán‐shaú neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng.
5 Waī uên ngŏh‐chi‐tô uēn‐ts‘uēn, shaú‐tak neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî!
6 Táng ngŏh ts‘ūng neĭ shóh‐yaŭ‐mîng‐lîng, tsaû pat‐chì sau‐yūk.
7 Ngŏh keì hōk neĭ kung‐î‐chi‐laî, tsaû pit k‘aaĭ chìng‐chīk‐chi‐sam laī‐chuk‐tsê neĭ.
8 Ngŏh laāp‐ì shaú neĭ lūt‐faatₒ: k‘aū neĭ m̄‐hó tiu‐heì‐saaì ngŏh.
9 ¶ Nīn‐shiù‐chi‐yān, tím chì kitₒ‐tsîng‐tak tsẑ‐keí hāng‐waī, tsúng iù i‐ts‘ūng neĭ shuetₒ‐wâ, laī‐kán‐shân shaú‐chuê.
10 Ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng yat‐sam k‘aū neĭ: mông neĭ maĭ peí ngŏh leī‐hoi neĭ kaaì‐mîng.
11 Ngŏh keì tseung neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, ts‘ōng‐tsoî sam‐chung, mĭn‐chì tak‐tsuî neĭ.
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, waī uên neĭ shaû tsûng‐chuk: k‘aū neĭ tseung neĭ lūt‐laî kaaù ngŏh.
13 Neĭ haú shóh‐kóng‐chi‐lūt‐laî, ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng yûng tsuí laī‐ch‘uēn‐shuetₒ.
14 Ngŏh foon‐heí neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn, yaū‐uē foon‐heí tak‐hó‐toh fòh‐ts‘oī.
15 Ngŏh tsîng‐chung sz‐seúng neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, laū‐sam t‘aí‐shāt neĭ‐chi‐lô‐t‘ō.
16 Ngŏh shî‐pit foon‐heí neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî: ngŏh pit m̄‐mōng‐keì neĭ shuetₒ‐wâ.
17 ¶ K‘aū neĭ pó‐yaû neĭ pūk, táng ngŏh tsoî‐shang laī‐tsun‐shaú neĭ īn‐uĕ.
18 Hoi ngŏh ngaăn‐mūk, táng t‘aí‐kìn neĭ lūt‐faatₒ‐chung‐kè ò‐miû.
19 Ngŏh tsoî shaì‐sheûng, hó‐ts‘z̆ yān‐haakₒ: m̄‐hó ts‘ōng‐maaī neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, m̄‐peí ngŏh‐kìn.
20 Ngŏh shī‐shī sz‐mô neĭ lūt‐laî, sam‐ch‘eūng‐to‐līt.
21 Neĭ chaakₒ‐fāt kiu‐ngô‐chi‐yān, tsik‐haî pooì‐yīk neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, shaû‐neĭ‐chaù‐chòh‐ché.
22 K‘aū neĭ lîng ngŏh pat‐shaû waí‐p‘òng sau‐yūk; yan ngŏh tsun‐shaú neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn.
23 Wōng‐haū ts‘ŏh‐ch‘uè laī‐waí‐p‘òng ngŏh: taân neĭ pūk yīng‐īn tsîng‐seúng neĭ lūt‐laî.
24 Neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn, haî ngŏh shóh‐foon‐heí, tòng waī ngŏh‐chi‐maū‐sẑ.
25 ¶ Ngŏh mîng kei‐oō iù kwai naī‐t‘ó: k‘aū neĭ i‐faan ts‘īn‐shī shuetₒ‐wâ, lîng ngŏh faan‐shang.
26 Ngŏh kóng‐ch‘ut ngŏh shóh‐hāng‐chi‐lô, neĭ ĭ‐king t‘eng ngŏh: k‘aū neĭ k‘aaĭ neĭ lūt‐laî kaaù ngŏh.
27 Táng ngŏh mīng‐paāk neĭ‐kaaì‐mîng‐chi‐tô: ngŏh tsaû sz‐seúng neĭ‐chi‐k‘eī‐sẑ.
28 Ngŏh sam yau‐shaū, laū‐luî uē‐t‘ūng yūng‐fà: k‘aū neĭ i‐faan ts‘īn īn, laī foō‐ch‘ī ngŏh.
29 Lîng ngŏh uĕn‐leī kaan‐chà‐chi‐lô: uên neĭ shi yan‐ts‘z̀ neĭ lūt‐faatₒ kwòh ngŏh.
30 Ngŏh ĭ‐king kaán‐chaāk chan‐shāt‐chi‐lô: chai neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî, tsoî ngŏh ngaăn‐ts‘īn.
31 Ngŏh hó‐oì‐mô neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn: k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā maĭ lîng ngŏh shaû yūk.
32 Ngŏh iù chīk‐hāng neĭ shóh‐kaaì‐mîng‐chi‐lô, yan neĭ hoi‐footₒ ngŏh‐chi‐sam.
33 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū neĭ k‘aaĭ neĭ lūt‐laî‐chi‐lô yăn ngŏh; táng ngŏh chung‐shan shaú‐chuê.
34 Ts‘z̀ ts‘ung‐mīng kwòh‐ngŏh, táng ngŏh ts‘ūng neĭ lūt‐faatₒ; yat sam shaú‐chuê.
35 Yaû lîng ngŏh hāng neĭ kaaì‐mîng‐chi‐lô; yan‐waî ni‐t‘iū‐lô, haî ngŏh shóh‐foon‐heí‐ché.
36 Lîng ngŏh sam heùng neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn, m̄‐heùng fòh‐ts‘oī.
37 Táng ngŏh ngaăn‐mūk m̄‐t‘aí hui‐ká; táng ngŏh tsoî neĭ lô‐chung tak‐shang‐oōt.
38 Neĭ tuì kìng‐waì neĭ‐chi‐pūk yaŭ īn, k‘aū neĭ i‐faan ts‘īn‐īn.
39 K‘aū ch‘uē‐lat ngŏh shóh‐p‘à‐chi‐waí‐p‘òng: yan neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî haî tsûn‐shîn.
40 Ngŏh hó‐sz‐mô neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng: uên neĭ cha‐chuê kung‐î, lîng ngŏh tak‐shang.
41 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū neĭ i‐faan ts‘īn īn, táng neĭ‐chi‐līn‐sut lām‐tò ngŏh, ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh.
42 Ngŏh tsaû yaŭ shuetₒ‐wâ ooī‐taapₒ waí‐p‘òng‐ngŏh‐chi‐yān: yan ngŏh í‐laaî neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ.
43 K‘aū neĭ māt tseung chan‐shāt‐chi‐wâ, t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh haú; yan ngŏh táng‐haû neĭ‐chi‐p‘oòn‐tuèn.
44 Ngŏh tsaû pit shaú neĭ‐chi‐faatₒ‐lūt, wĭng‐uĕn‐mō‐tsûn.
45 Ngŏh sò‐yāt ts‘ām‐k‘aū neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng: koò‐ts‘ź tak‐hāng foon‐footₒ‐chi‐teî.
46 Ngŏh tsoî līt‐wōng‐chi‐ts‘īn, kóng‐lûn neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn, yīk m̄‐sau‐kw‘aì.
47 Ngŏh oì‐mô neĭ‐chi mîng‐lîng, shóh‐ĭ sam shâm‐foon‐heí.
48 Ngŏh oì‐mô neĭ‐chi‐mîng‐lîng, ngŏh pit kuí‐shaú laī kung‐kìng; ngŏh pit tsîng‐chung keì‐nîm neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî.
49 ¶ Neĭ shóh‐ying‐shīng‐neĭ‐pūk‐chi‐shuetₒ‐wâ, ĭ‐king lîng ngŏh p‘aàn‐mông, k‘aū neĭ keì‐chuê.
50 Ngŏh tong waân‐naân‐chi‐shī, k‘aaĭ ni‐ti laī on‐waì: yan neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, lîng ngŏh fūk‐shang.
51 Kiu‐ngô‐chi‐yān, hó‐ch‘í‐siù‐ngŏh: taân ngŏh tsúng‐m̄‐leī‐hoi neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ.
52 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘ūng‐koó ĭ‐loī, shóh‐laāp‐chi‐lūt‐laî; ngŏh seúng‐heí tsaû tsẑ‐keí on‐waì.
53 Ngŏh kìn okₒ‐yān, tiu‐heì neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ, ngŏh tsaû hó‐nau.
54 Ngŏh tsoî shaì‐sheûng keì‐kui, sheūng‐shī tseung neĭ lūt‐faatₒ laī‐tsô‐koh‐ch‘eùng.
55 Ngŏh tsoî yê‐kaan, keì‐nîm Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mēng, ī‐ch‘é tsun‐shaú neĭ lūt‐faatₒ.
56 Ngŏh yan kán‐shaú neĭ kaaì‐mîng, shóh‐ĭ tak ni‐ti on‐waì.
57 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh wâ kán‐shaú neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ: ni‐ti haî ngŏh poón‐fân.
58 Ngŏh yat‐sam heùng neĭ k‘eī‐k‘aū: mông neĭ chiù‐i ts‘īn‐īn, laī shi‐yan kwòh‐ngŏh.
59 Tsẑ‐keí seúng‐heí ts‘īn‐shī‐chi‐hāng‐waī, ngŏh tsaû ooī‐pô‐heùng neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn.
60 Ngŏh tsun‐shaú neĭ‐chi‐mîng‐lîng, kón‐kán ts‘īn huì, m̄‐kóm īn‐ch‘ī.
61 Okₒ‐tóng waī‐chuê ngŏh: ngŏh yīng‐īn m̄‐mōng‐keì neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ.
62 Ngŏh poòn‐yê heí‐shan, laī‐chuk‐tsê neĭ, yan neĭ‐chi‐p‘oòn‐tuèn haî kung‐î.
63 Taân‐faān kìng‐waì neĭ, shaú neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng‐ché, ngŏh chì‐kûng k‘uĭ tsô p‘āng‐yaŭ.
64 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ‐chi‐yan‐tín, ch‘ung‐moŏn teî‐sheûng: k‘aū neĭ tseung lūt‐laî kaaù ngŏh.
65 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ ĭ‐king i‐faan ts‘īn‐īn hó‐hon‐toî neĭ pūk.
66 K‘aū neĭ ts‘z̀ ngŏh‐chì‐waî kìn‐shik: yan ngŏh sham‐sùn neĭ‐chi mîng‐lîng.
67 Ngŏh meî‐shaû‐foó‐chi‐sin, hāng‐ts‘òh‐hiu‐lô: uē‐kam tsaû tsun‐shaú neĭ īn‐uĕ.
68 Neĭ haî chì‐shîn; ī‐ch‘é hāng shîn; k‘aū neĭ tseung neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî kaaù ngŏh.
69 Kiu‐ngô‐chi‐yān, tsô‐ch‘ut iū‐īn, laī‐waí‐p‘òng ngŏh: taân ngŏh chuen‐sam shaú neĭ kaaì‐mîng.
70 K‘uĭ‐teî sam‐chung mūng‐mooî, hó‐ts‘z̆ peî‐ko‐sak‐chuê; waī‐haî ngŏh tsaû foon‐heí neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ.
71 Ngŏh sik‐yāt shaû‐foó, faán waī yaŭ yik; haî lîng ngŏh hōk‐tsaāp neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî.
72 Neĭ haú‐chung‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, ngŏh ĭ‐waī chûng‐pó‐pooì kwòh‐ts‘in‐maân kam‐ngān.
73 ¶ Ngŏh peî‐neĭ‐shaú chaì‐tsô shīng yān, k‘aū tseung chì‐waî ts‘z̀‐kwòh‐ngŏh: táng ngŏh hōk‐tsaāp neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng.
74 Kìng‐waì‐chi‐yān kìn ngŏh, shî‐pit foon‐heí; yan ngŏh p‘aàn‐mông neĭ shóh‐ying‐huí.
75 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh chi neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî haî kung‐î, neĭ lîng ngŏh shaû foó, haî k‘aaĭ chan‐sam toî ngŏh.
76 K‘aū neĭ tseung ts‘z̄‐pei on‐waì ngŏh, i‐faan neĭ shóh‐ying‐huí neĭ pūk ché.
77 Uên neĭ‐chi‐ts‘z̄‐pei‐lām‐tò‐ngŏh shan, lîng ngŏh shang‐oōt yan neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ, haî ngŏh shóh‐foon‐heí.
78 Kiu‐ngô‐chi‐yān; mō‐tuen mŏ‐lûng ngŏh, uên k‘uĭ shaû yūk: waī‐haî ngŏh tsaû tsîng‐nîm neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng.
79 Taân‐faān kìng‐waì neĭ, uĕ‐k‘āp shik‐neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn‐ché, uên k‘uĭ kwai‐heùng ngŏh.
80 Táng ngŏh ts‘uēn‐sam chìng‐chīk, tsun‐shaú neĭ lūt‐laî; tsaû pat‐chì yaŭ sau‐kw‘aì.
81 ¶ Ngŏh sam ts‘itₒ‐mông neĭ‐chi‐ch‘íng‐kaù: ngŏh waī táng‐haû neĭ‐chi‐ying‐huí.
82 Ngŏh mông neĭ ying‐huí‐chi‐īn, ngaăn to mông‐kooî, ngŏh tsaû wâ, Neĭ keí‐shī chì on‐waì ngŏh ni?
83 Yan ngŏh hó‐ts‘z̆ p‘eī‐toî, kwà‐tsoî in‐chung; ngŏh tsúng m̄‐mōng‐keì neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ.
84 Neĭ pūk tsoî shaì, yāt‐tsź mō‐toh? neĭ keí‐shī chì shám‐p‘oòn pik‐hoî‐ngŏh‐chi‐yān?
85 Kiu‐ngô‐chi‐yān, m̄‐tsun neĭ faatₒ, kwāt‐hoi haâm‐haang laī‐hoî ngŏh.
86 Neĭ‐chi‐mîng‐lîng, tsûn haî chan‐shāt: yān‐teî mō tuen pik‐hoî ngŏh; mông neĭ foō‐ch‘ī ngŏh.
87 Ngŏh tsoî shaì‐sheûng, kei‐oō peî k‘uĭ mīt‐tsuēt; taân ngŏh to m̄‐tiu‐heì neĭ faatₒ‐tô.
88 Chiù neĭ yan‐tín laī‐lîng ngŏh shang‐oōt; ngŏh tsaû tsun‐shaú neĭ haú‐ch‘ut‐chi‐chìng‐īn.
89 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ‐chi‐īn‐ts‘z̄, wĭng‐uĕn laāp‐shāt tsoî t‘in.
90 Neĭ‐chi‐chan‐shāt, toî‐toî sheūng‐ts‘uēn; taaî‐teî yīk‐haî neĭ shóh‐laāp, wán‐chân pat‐tûng.
91 T‘in‐teî t‘eng‐ts‘ūng neĭ mîng, chì‐tò kam‐yāt chûng‐ts‘uēn: yan‐waî maân‐māt to haî neĭ pūk‐yīk.
92 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh m̄‐foon‐heí neĭ‐chi‐faatₒ‐tô, ngŏh tsaû tsoî foó‐naân‐chung, taaî‐tsó sź‐huì.
93 Ngŏh wĭng‐uĕn m̄‐mōng‐keì neĭ kaaì‐mîng: yan neĭ yă‐ts‘āng yûng ni‐ti laī‐shang‐oōt ngŏh.
94 Ngŏh haî shūk neĭ, mông neĭ kaù ngŏh; yan ngŏh sò‐yāt ts‘ām‐k‘aū neĭ‐chi kaaì‐mîng.
95 Okₒ‐yān kw‘ai‐t‘aàm ngŏh, seúng mīt‐tsuēt ngŏh: ngŏh waī‐haî hiú‐tak neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn.
96 Ngŏh kìn shóh‐yaŭ chì uēn‐ts‘uēn meĭ‐hó‐ché, to yaŭ tsûn‐shī: waī‐tūk neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, kwóng‐taaî mō‐k‘ūng.
97 ¶ Ngŏh kīk‐oì‐mô neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ! ngŏh shīng‐yāt pat‐hitₒ sz‐nîm.
98 Neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, sheūng‐ts‘uēn ngŏh sam, koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh‐chi‐chì‐waî, shìng‐kwòh ch‘aū‐tīk.
99 Ngŏh yan sz‐nîm neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn: koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh‐chi‐ts‘ung‐mīng, shìng kwòh ngŏh‐chi‐lŏ‐sz.
100 Ngŏh yan shaú neĭ kaaì‐mîng, koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh‐chi‐mīng‐paāk, yīk‐shìng‐kwòh lŏ‐yān.
101 Shóh‐yaŭ‐okₒ‐lô, ngŏh kàm‐chí keukₒ‐pô m̄‐hāng, yan haî seúng tsun‐shaú neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ.
102 Ngŏh m̄‐waī‐pooì neĭ lūt‐laî: yan kaaù‐fàn‐ngŏh‐ché haî Chué neĭ.
103 Neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, tsoî ngŏh sheûng‐ngōk, kokₒ‐tak shâm‐t‘īm! tsoî ngŏh haú‐chung, t‘īm‐kwòh māt‐t‘ōng!
104 Yaū neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, ka‐toh ngŏh chì‐waî: koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh tsang‐oò shóh‐yaŭ ts‘ē‐lô.
105 ¶ Neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, haî ngŏh keukₒ‐ts‘īn‐chi‐tang, haî ngŏh lô‐sheûng‐chi‐kwong.
106 Neĭ kung‐î‐chi‐lūt, ngŏh shaî‐uên tsun‐shaú, ngŏh pit‐tîng i shaî laī‐tsô.
107 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh shaû‐foó‐tò‐kīk: k‘aū neĭ i‐faan ts‘īn‐īn, lîng ngŏh shang‐oōt.
108 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsipₒ‐naāp ngŏh haú shóh‐huí‐chi‐uên; ī‐ch‘é tseung neĭ lūt‐faatₒ kaaù ngŏh.
109 Ngŏh‐chi‐sìng‐mîng, sheūng‐shī tsoî ngŏh shaú‐sheûng: ngŏh to m̄‐mōng‐keì neĭ lūt‐faatₒ.
110 Okₒ‐yān ch‘itₒ‐lōh‐mŏng laī‐hoî ngŏh: ngŏh to m̄‐waī‐pooì neĭ kaaì‐mîng.
111 Ngŏh sheūng‐shī tseung neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn laī‐tsô‐ch‘aán‐īp: yan‐waī ni‐ti haî ngŏh sam shóh‐foon‐heí‐ché.
112 Ngŏh chuen‐sam heùng neĭ lūt‐faatₒ, wĭng‐uĕn shaú‐chuê, tsik‐haî shaú‐tò‐meĭ.
113 ¶ Ngŏh shóh‐tsang‐ché, haî sz‐ī pat‐k‘uetₒ‐chi‐yān: ngŏh shóh‐hò‐ché, haî neĭ‐chi‐faatₒ‐tsak.
114 Neĭ haî ngŏh ts‘ōng‐shan‐chi‐shóh, haî ngŏh oô‐shan‐t‘āng‐p‘aaī: ngŏh waī yeŭng‐mông neĭ‐chi‐ying‐huí.
115 Tsokₒ‐okₒ‐chi‐yān, neĭ‐teî ying‐koi leī‐hoi ngŏh: yan ngŏh pit shaú ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì mîng‐lîng.
116 Sheûng‐Taì à, k‘aū neĭ i‐faan neĭ shóh‐ying‐huí laī‐foō‐chôh ngŏh, táng ngŏh shang‐oōt: m̄‐hó lîng ngŏh shat‐mông sau‐ch‘í.
117 Tseung ngŏh foō‐ch‘ī, lîng ngŏh tak‐kaù: ngŏh tsaû sheūng‐heùng‐neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ.
118 Waī‐pooì‐neĭ‐lūt‐faatₒ‐ché, neĭ pit miŭ‐shî k‘uĭ: yan k‘uĭ‐chi‐kaan‐kaì haî hui‐faū.
119 Shaì‐sheûng okₒ‐yān, neĭ tòng tsokₒ cha‐tsź kóm sò‐saaì k‘uĭ: koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh foon‐heí neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn.
120 Ngŏh kìng‐waì neĭ, sam king yūk t‘iù; neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî, lîng ngŏh kuî‐p‘à.
121 ¶ Ngŏh shóh‐hāng haî kung‐p‘īng chìng‐chīk: k‘aū neĭ maĭ peí tīk‐yān pik‐hoî ngŏh.
122 K‘aū neĭ pó‐yaû neĭ pūk, lîng tak‐hó‐ch‘uè: maĭ peí kiu‐ngô‐yān mŏ‐lûng ngŏh.
123 Ngŏh mông neĭ‐chi‐ch‘íng‐kaù, k‘āp neĭ kung‐î‐chi‐īn, ngaăn to mông‐kooî.
124 K‘aū i neĭ ts‘z̄‐pei laī toî neĭ pūk, tseung neĭ lūt‐faatₒ kaaù‐fàn ngŏh.
125 Ngŏh haî neĭ pūk; k‘aū neĭ ts‘z̀ ngŏh ts‘ung‐mīng, táng ngŏh mīng‐paāk neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn.
126 Shaì yān tiu‐heì neĭ lūt‐faatₒ: uē‐kam Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hing‐heí, chìng‐haî hōp‐shī.
127 Koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh oì neĭ‐chi‐mîng‐lîng, hó‐kwòh oì kam; hó‐kwòh chì‐tsing‐chi‐kam.
128 Neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, t‘iū‐t‘iū ngŏh to suèn‐chìng; kaan‐chà‐chi‐lô, t‘iū‐t‘iū ngŏh to tsang‐oò.
129 ¶ Neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn, kīk‐haî k‘eī‐miû: shóh‐ĭ ngŏh yat sam shaú‐chuê.
130 Neĭ hoi‐īn tsaû faatₒ‐ch‘ut kwong‐fai; nāng lîng uē‐chuetₒ‐chi‐yān, pìn‐tsokₒ‐ts‘ung‐mīng.
131 Neĭ‐chi‐mîng‐lîng, ngŏh sz‐mô tò ts‘itₒ: ngŏh maakₒ‐taaî‐haú heì‐ch‘uén.
132 K‘aū neĭ chiù‐i hon‐toî kìng‐oì‐neĭ‐mēng‐chi‐yān, laī kuèn‐koò ngŏh, līn‐măn ngŏh.
133 K‘aū neĭ tseung neĭ‐chi‐shuetₒ‐wâ, lîng ngŏh hāng‐pô kin‐wán: mōk peí tsuî‐okₒ chaì‐fūk ngŏh.
134 Kaù ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐leī yān‐chi‐pik‐hoî: ngŏh tsaû tsun‐shaú neĭ kaaì‐mîng.
135 K‘aū tseung neĭ mîn‐sheûng‐chi‐kwong, chiù‐chuê neĭ pūk; k‘aaĭ neĭ lūt‐laî kaaù‐fàn ngŏh.
136 Shaì‐yān m̄‐shaú neĭ lūt‐faatₒ, shóh‐ĭ ngŏh ngaăn‐luî hó‐ts‘z̆‐hōh‐laū.
137 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ haî kung‐î, neĭ lūt‐laî haî chìng‐chīk.
138 Neĭ shóh‐fan‐foò‐chi‐chìng‐īn, ts‘uēn‐haî kung‐î, kīk‐haî shīng‐shāt.
139 Ngŏh yan ch‘aū‐tīk mōng‐keì neĭ īn, shóh‐ĭ ngŏh sam hó‐ts‘z̆ fóh‐shiu.
140 Neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, kīk‐k‘eī tsing‐kitₒ: shóh‐ĭ neĭ pūk foon‐heí.
141 Ngŏh sui pei‐meī, peî‐yān‐miŭ‐shî: to m̄‐mōng‐keì neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng.
142 Neĭ‐chi‐kung‐î, wĭng‐uĕn laū‐ts‘uēn, neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ, k‘ui‐shî chan‐shāt.
143 Sui yaŭ foó‐naân yau‐shaū, lām‐tò ngŏh shan: ngŏh yīng‐īn foon‐heí neĭ‐chi‐mîng‐lîng.
144 Neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn, wĭng‐uĕn kung‐î: k‘aū neĭ ts‘z̀ ngŏh ts‘ung‐mīng, táng ngŏh shang‐oōt.
145 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh yat‐sam foo‐kiù neĭ; k‘aū neĭ t‘eng ngŏh: ngŏh tsaû tsun‐shaú neĭ lūt‐laî.
146 Ngŏh foo‐kiù neĭ, k‘aū neĭ kaù ngŏh, ngŏh tsaû tsun‐shaú neĭ chìng‐īn.
147 Ngŏh tong ts‘ing‐tsó chi‐shī, tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn foo‐kiù: ngŏh yeŭng‐mông neĭ ying‐huí‐chi‐īn.
148 Meî‐ts‘āng yê‐sham, ngŏh sin maakₒ‐hoi ngaăn, laī‐sz‐seúng neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ.
149 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū chiù‐neĭ‐chi‐ts‘z̄‐pei, t‘eng ngŏh sheng‐yam: chiù‐neĭ‐chi‐tîng‐laî, lîng ngŏh shang‐oōt.
150 Hāng‐okₒ‐chi yān, laī‐kân ngŏh‐ch‘uè: k‘uĭ haî uĕn‐leī neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ‐ché.
151 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ tsoî ngŏh tsóh‐yaû; neĭ‐chi‐mîng‐lîng, k‘ui haî chan‐shāt.
152 Ngŏh sò‐yāt chi‐tò neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn, haî wĭng‐uĕn laāp‐shāt.
153 ¶ K‘aū neĭ t‘aí‐koò ngŏh‐chi‐foó‐ch‘óh, laī‐ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh: yan ngŏh m̄‐mōng‐keì neĭ lūt‐faatₒ.
154 K‘aū neĭ t‘aì ngŏh shan‐uen, tseung ngŏh kaù‐shūk: chiù‐neĭ ts‘īn‐īn, lîng ngŏh shang‐oōt.
155 Okₒ‐yān m̄‐chui‐ts‘ām neĭ lūt‐faatₒ: neĭ‐chi‐kaù‐yan uĕn‐leī k‘uĭ.
156 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ‐chi‐ts‘z̄‐pei kīk‐taaî: k‘aū neĭ chiù‐neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî, laī shang‐oōt ngŏh.
157 Pik‐hoî ngŏh, kw‘àn‐chūk‐ngŏh‐ché, hó‐toh‐yān; ngŏh to m̄‐leī‐hoi neĭ chìng‐īn.
158 Ngŏh kìn pooì‐yīk‐chi‐yān, tsaû hó‐ìm‐heì; yan k‘uĭ m̄‐tsun‐i neĭ īn‐uĕ.
159 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ‐chi‐ts‘z̄‐pei kīk‐taaî: k‘aū chiù‐neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî, laī‐shang‐oōt ngŏh.
160 Neĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, uēn‐poón chan‐shāt: neĭ kung‐î lūt‐laî, mō‐pat‐wĭng‐uĕn laū‐ts‘uēn.
161 ¶ Wōng‐haū sui mō‐tuen pik‐hoî ngŏh: ngŏh sam yīng‐īn kìng‐waì neĭ īn‐uĕ.
162 Ngŏh foon‐heí neĭ īn‐uĕ, yaū‐uē tak‐hó‐taaî ts‘oī‐pó.
163 Taaî‐wâ ngŏh tsuì tsang‐oò ìm‐heì: waī‐haî neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ, ngŏh kīk‐foon‐heí.
164 Ngŏh yan neĭ kung‐î‐chi‐lūt‐laî, yat‐yāt ts‘at‐ts‘z̀ tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ.
165 Oì‐neĭ‐lūt‐faatₒ‐chi‐yān, taaî‐tak‐p‘īng‐on: tsúng mŏ chóh‐ngoî.
166 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh yeŭng‐mông neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù, ngŏh tsun hāng neĭ mîng‐lîng.
167 Ngŏh kīk‐oì neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn; ngŏh yat sam tsun‐shaú.
168 Ngŏh shaú neĭ‐chi‐kaaì‐mîng, k‘āp neĭ‐chi‐chìng‐īn: ngŏh‐chi‐hāng‐waī, neĭ mō‐pat‐kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ.
169 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū chún ngŏh‐chi‐foo‐kiù, taāt‐tò neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn: chiù neĭ‐chi‐īn laī‐ts‘z̀‐ngŏh chì‐waî.
170 Chún ngŏh‐chi‐t‘ó‐kò, taāt‐tò neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn: chiù‐neĭ‐chi‐īn laī‐ts‘z̀‐ngŏh tak‐kaù.
171 Neĭ tseung lūt‐faatₒ kaaù ngŏh, ngŏh haú pit tsaàn‐tsûng neĭ.
172 Neĭ‐chi kaaì‐mîng, k‘ui haî kûng‐î: ngŏh pit‐yûng‐leî ch‘uēn‐shūt neĭ īn‐uē.
173 K‘aū neĭ yûng‐shaú foō‐ch‘ī ngŏh; yan ngŏh ts‘uí neĭ‐chi‐faatₒ‐tsak.
174 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh ts‘itₒ‐mông neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù; hó‐foon‐heí neĭ lūt‐faatₒ.
175 K‘aū pó‐ts‘uēn ngŏh mîng, laī‐tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ; k‘aū neĭ‐chi‐lūt‐laî pong‐chôh ngŏh.
176 Ngŏh sam maī‐mŭng, hó‐ts‘z̆ shat‐lô‐chi‐yeūng; k‘aū neĭ wán‐faan neĭ pūk; yan ngŏh m̄‐mōng‐keì neĭ kaaì‐mîng.
