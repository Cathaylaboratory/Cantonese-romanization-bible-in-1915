1 KWÒH‐HIU ts‘at nīn, Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî tsẑ‐k‘eūng, tseung paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng Yē‐lōh‐hón‐kè tsaí À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐shāt‐mă‐leî, Oh‐paakₒ‐kè tsaí À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, À‐taaî‐ngă‐kè tsaí Mă‐sai‐ngă, Shik‐leĭ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐faatₒ, t‘ūng tsẑ‐keí laāp‐yeukₒ.
2 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû ts‘ūn‐hāng Yaū‐t‘aaì, chiû‐tsaāp kui tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐shēng‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān, k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐foô‐ka‐cheúng, kaai tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng.
3 Ooî‐tsaāp tsoî Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk kûng wōng laāp‐yeukₒ. Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî tuì chùng‐yān wâ, T‘aí‐a, wōng‐kè tsaí pit tsô wōng, tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐k‘āp Taaî‐waî tsź‐suen‐kè.
4 Neĭ iù kóm‐yeûng laī‐hāng; neĭ‐teî chùng‐tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān, on‐sik‐yāt yāp‐kè, saam‐fân‐chi‐yat, iù hon‐shaú moōn;
5 Saam‐fân‐chi‐yat iù tsoî wōng‐kung‐shat; saam‐fân‐chi‐yat iù tsoî kei‐chí moōn: chùng‐tsź‐mān iù tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐chung‐kè uên.
6 Waī‐haî tsaì‐sz kûng fûng‐sẑ‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān‐chi‐ngoî, m̄‐hó peí‐pīt‐yān yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk; ts‘ź‐táng‐yān hóh‐yāp, yan k‘uĭ‐teî haî shìng‐kitₒ‐kè à: waī‐haî chùng‐tsź‐mān iù waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hon‐shaú.
7 Leî‐meî‐yān iù kokₒ‐cha‐kwan‐heì oô‐waî wōng, faān haî pīt‐yān yāp kung‐tîn; tsaû iù shaatₒ‐sź k‘uĭ: taân wōng ch‘ut‐yāp‐chi‐shī, neĭ‐teî iù t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ.
8 Kóm‐tsaû Leî‐meî‐yān kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè chùng‐yān, tsun‐i tsaì‐sz Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî faān shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè laī‐hāng, kokₒ‐yān yăn‐taaì on‐sik‐yāt tong yāp‐chi‐yān, uĕ‐k‘āp on‐sik‐yāt tong‐ch‘ut‐chi‐yān: yan‐waî Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî m̄‐fan‐līt‐kokₒ‐paan.
9 Tsaì‐sz Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî tseung ts‘ōng‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐chung, sik‐yāt shūk Taaî‐waî‐wōng‐kè ts‘eung, kûng t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī, k‘āp tóng‐p‘aaī, kaau‐kwòh paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng.
10 Ī‐ch‘é ch‘itₒ‐laāp chùng‐tsź‐mān sz̀‐waī oô‐waî wōng, kokₒ‐yān shaú‐cha‐kwan‐heì, tsẑ tîn‐yaû sheûng‐hâ waān‐iŭ tsaì‐t‘aān, kûng shìng‐shóh, chì‐tò tîn‐tsóh.
11 Ue‐shî chùng‐yān tsaû taaì wōng‐kè tsaí ch‘ut‐laī, taaì wōng koon‐mĭn tsoî k‘uĭ t‘aū‐sheûng, yaû k‘aaĭ lūt‐faatₒ‐kè shue kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ, tsaû laāp‐k‘uĭ tsô wōng. Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî kûng k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí, tsaû kò k‘uĭ, foo‐kiù wâ, Uên wōng maân‐suì à.
12 À‐taaî‐leî keì t‘eng‐mān paakₒ‐sìng pan‐tsaú tsûng‐tsaàn wōng‐kè sheng, tsaû yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk, t‘ūng‐maaī tsź‐mān kòh‐taatₒ:
13 T‘aí‐kìn wōng k‘eĭ‐tsoî yāp‐ch‘uè‐kè ch‘uĕ chak‐pin, kân wōng yaŭ chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, kûng ch‘ui‐kokₒ‐kè, kwokₒ‐chung‐kè tsź‐mān kaai foon‐heí ch‘ui‐heúng‐kokₒ, yaû yaŭ au‐koh‐kè haau‐heí kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè ngōk‐heì, k‘āp kaaù‐yān‐tsaàn‐meĭ‐kè. Ue‐shî À‐taaî‐leî maakₒ‐laân k‘uĭ i‐fūk, foo‐kiù wâ, Tsokₒ‐faán à, Tsokₒ‐faán à.
14 Kòh‐shī, tsaì‐sz Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî taaì koón‐hāt kwan‐kè paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng ch‘ut‐laī, tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Tsoî paaí‐līt tuî‐nğ chung‐kaan kón k‘uĭ ch‘ut: taân‐faān kan‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐kè, tsaû iù k‘aaí to‐kìm laī‐shaatₒ k‘uĭ. Yan tsaì‐sz wâ, Maĭ shaatₒ ni‐kòh foŏ‐yān tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk‐noî.
15 Kóm‐tsaû chùng‐yān waî k‘uĭ hoi‐lô; k‘uĭ tsaû huì‐tò yāp‐Mă‐moōn‐chi‐shóh, kân wōng‐kung‐shat, chùng‐yān tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè shaatₒ k‘uĭ.
16 ¶ Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî kûng tsź‐mān t‘ūng‐maaī wōng laāp‐yeukₒ, tîng‐ì pit iù tsô Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tsź‐mān.
17 Chùng‐tsź‐mān huì‐tò Pa‐līk miû laī waí‐ch‘aakₒ, yaû waí‐mīt k‘uĭ‐kè tsaì‐t‘aān, k‘āp ngaŭ‐tseûng, yaû tsoî t‘aān‐ts‘īn shaatₒ Pa‐līk‐kè tsaì‐sz Mă‐taam.
18 Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî yaû chiù‐i Taaî‐waî sik‐yāt shóh‐tîng‐kè, ch‘itₒ‐laāp tsaì‐sz Leî‐meî‐yān, waî shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè chik‐yīk, sik‐yāt Taaî‐waî tseung ni‐ti paan‐līt p‘aaì‐waí tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, chì‐foon‐heí au‐koh, hìn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè faān‐tsaì, tsun‐i shóh‐tsoì‐lōk Moh‐sai lūt‐faatₒ‐kè.
19 Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî yīk ch‘itₒ‐laāp shaú‐moōn‐kè yān, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè kokₒ‐moōn, chì faān yaŭ m̄‐shìng‐kitₒ‐kè, pat‐tak‐yāp à.
20 Yaû tsẑ tuk‐lĭng paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng, kûng kokₒ‐tsuen‐kwaì‐kè kûng tsź‐mān‐chung‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, uĕ‐k‘āp ts‘ź‐teî‐kè tsź‐mān, chùng taaì wōng yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk hă‐laī: yaū sheûng moōn yāp wōng‐kung‐shat, laāp wōng ts‘ŏh‐tsoî kwokₒ‐waî.
21 Kóm‐tsaû kwokₒ‐chung chùng‐tsź‐mān hó‐foon‐heí: shēng‐to on‐tsîng, yaû k‘uĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ to‐kìm shaatₒ À‐taaî‐leî à.
