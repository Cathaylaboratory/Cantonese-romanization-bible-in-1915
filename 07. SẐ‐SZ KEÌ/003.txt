1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ shóh‐shîng‐hâ‐kè līt‐kwokₒ, seúng k‘aaĭ‐laī shì‐hă Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, tsik‐haî shì taân‐faān meî‐ts‘āng chi‐tò Ka‐naām shóh‐yaŭ‐chìn‐sẑ‐kè;
2 Iù shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè līk‐toî, chi‐tò kaaù k‘uĭ‐teî lîn‐tsaāp chìn‐sẑ chì‐kán‐iù kaaù meî‐ts‘āng chi‐tò‐kè à;
3 Shóh‐shîng‐hâ‐kè, tsik‐haî Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐kè nğ‐paakₒ, k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ Ka‐naām‐yān, Sai‐tûn‐yān, chuê Leî‐pa‐nuên‐shaan‐kè Hei‐meî‐yān, ts‘ūng Pa‐līk‐haak‐moōn‐kè shaan, yat‐chīk‐tò Hap‐moōt kóh‐ch‘uè.
4 Tsaû k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ‐teî laī shì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, seúng chi‐tò k‘uĭ‐teî háng t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, t‘okₒ Moh‐sai shaú shóh‐fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐teî līt‐tsó‐kè mîng‐lîng m̄‐háng.
5 Kóm‐tsaû Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, chuê‐tsoî Ka‐naām‐yān, Haak‐yān, À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān, Peí‐leĭ‐saí‐yān, Hei‐meî‐yān, Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐yān chung‐kaan:
6 Ts‘uì k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè nuĭ tsô ts‘ai, tseung tsẑ‐keí‐kè nuĭ, peí k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsaí tsô ts‘ai, ī‐ch‘é fūk‐sẑ k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shān.
7 ¶ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, haí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ, mōng‐keì k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaû fūk‐sẑ Pa‐līk, kûng À‐taaî‐lūk.
8 Koò‐ts‘ź Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān faatₒ‐nô, kaau k‘uĭ‐teî kwòh Maĭ‐shóh‐poh‐taaî‐maĭ wōng Koó‐shaan‐leĭ‐saatₒ‐t‘īn: Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān fūk‐sẑ Koó‐shaan‐leĭ‐saatₒ‐t‘īn paatₒ nīn.
9 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, foo‐yeūk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chí‐shî yat‐kaù‐ché hing‐heí, laī‐kaù k‘uĭ‐teî, tsik‐haî Ka‐lāk‐kè saì‐ló, Kei‐naāp‐kè tsaí Oh‐tak‐īp.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè Shān kóm‐tûng Oh‐tak‐īp, k‘uĭ tsaû tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè sẑ‐sz, yaû ch‘ut‐huì chaang‐chìn: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung Maĭ‐shóh‐poh‐taaî‐maĭ wōng Koó‐shaan‐leĭ‐saatₒ‐t‘īn, kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ shaú; k‘uĭ tsaû shìng‐hiu Koó‐shaan‐leĭ‐saatₒ‐t‘īn.
11 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐teî on‐heúng t‘aaì‐p‘īng sz̀‐shāp nīn. Kei‐naāp‐kè tsaí Oh‐tak‐īp tsaû sź.
12 ¶ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, yaû haí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaí Moh‐aapₒ wōng Aakₒ‐lūn k‘eūng‐shîng, laī kung‐tá Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ à.
13 Aakₒ‐lūn tsaû chiu‐tsaāp À‐moōn‐yān, kûng À‐mă‐līk‐yān, huì‐kung‐tá Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, ts‘uí‐hiu tsó‐shuê‐kè shēng.
14 Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsaû fūk‐sẑ Moh‐aapₒ wōng Aakₒ‐lūn shāp‐paatₒ nīn.
15 Waī‐haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān foo‐yeūk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû chí‐shî yat kaù‐ché hing‐heí, laī kaù k‘uĭ‐teî, tsik‐haî shūk Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐kè chi‐p‘aaì, K‘eī‐la‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐fat, k‘uĭ haî shaí tsóh‐shaú pîn‐tsīt‐kè: Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, t‘okₒ k‘uĭ sùng laĭ‐māt huì Moh‐aapₒ wōng Aakₒ‐lūn.
16 Ĭ‐fat tsô‐hiu yat‐pá leŭng‐yân‐kè kìm, yat ch‘ekₒ kóm ch‘eūng; póng tsoî i‐fūk‐luĭ yaû‐pîn taaî‐peí‐sheûng.
17 K‘uĭ tsaû sùng laĭ‐māt kwòh Moh‐aapₒ wōng Aakₒ‐lūn: Aakₒ‐lūn tsaû haî kīk‐feī‐taaî‐kè yān.
18 Ĭ‐fat sùng uēn laĭ‐māt chi‐haû, tsaû tá‐faatₒ t‘oī‐laĭ‐māt‐kè yān faan‐huì.
19 Ĭ‐fat ts‘ūng foô‐kân Kat‐kaapₒ‐kè ngaŭ‐tseûng kòh‐taatₒ faan‐laī, tuì wōng wâ, Ngŏh yaŭ kîn kei‐māt‐kè sẑ, wâ‐kwòh wōng chi: wōng wâ, Ooī peî la. Kóm‐tsaû tsóh‐yaû shî‐laāp‐kè yān to t‘uì‐ch‘ut.
20 Ĭ‐fat tsaû hāng‐maaī k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè; tūk‐haî kòh‐wōng tsẑ‐keí ts‘ŏh‐tsoî leūng‐t‘eng‐ch‘uè. Ĭ‐fat wâ, Ngŏh fûng Sheûng‐Taì‐kè mîng, yaŭ yat‐kînsẑ‐laī wâ‐neĭ‐chi. Wōng tsaû ts‘ūng tsôh‐waî heí‐shan.
21 Ĭ‐fat tsaû yûng tsóh‐shaú haí yaû‐pîn taaî‐peí pāt‐ch‘ut pá‐kìm, kat‐yāp wōng t‘ŏ‐noî:
22 Līn kìm‐pìng to yāp‐maaī‐huì; kìm peî chi‐ko shóh kaapₒ, m̄‐màng‐tak‐ch‘ut, tsaû yaū pooì‐haû ī‐ch‘ut.
23 Ĭ‐fat tsaû ts‘ūng yaū‐lōng faan ch‘ut, kwaan‐sóh leūng‐t‘īng kokₒ‐moōn.
24 Ĭ‐fat keì ch‘ut, wōng‐kè pūk tò‐hiu; kìn laū‐moōn kwaan‐sóh, tsaû wâ, K‘uĭ pit‐tîng haí leūng‐t‘eng‐ch‘uè k‘ám‐chuê sheung‐keukₒ.
25 Pūk‐táng tsaû kìn m̄‐hó‐ì‐sz̀; k‘uĭ yīng‐īn m̄‐hoi laū‐moōn; k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû ning sóh‐shī hoi moōn: t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ chué titₒ‐lōk teî ĭ‐king sź‐hiu.
26 Pūk‐yān ch‘ī‐ī yat‐chân‐shī, Ĭ‐fat ĭ‐king t‘ō‐tsaú, king‐kwòh ngaŭ‐tseûng kòh‐taatₒ, tsaú‐tò Sai‐laāp.
27 Tò‐hiu, tsaû haí Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐shaan ch‘ui‐heí heúng‐kokₒ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ lōk shaan, Ĭ‐fat tsoî ts‘īn‐t‘aū taaì‐lô.
28 Tuì chùng‐yān wâ, Kan‐ts‘uī ngŏh laī la: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung neĭ‐teî‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk Moh‐aapₒ‐yān, kaau‐kwòh neĭ‐teî shaú. Chùng‐yān tsaû kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ lōk‐huì, kuì‐shaú‐chuê Moh‐aapₒ tuì‐mîn‐kè Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh tô‐t‘aū, m̄‐peí yat‐kòh yān kwòh‐huì.
29 Tsaû haí kóh‐chân‐shī shaatₒ‐hiu Moh‐aapₒ‐yān, yeukₒ yaŭ yat‐maân, to haî ko‐taaî ī‐ch‘é yaŭ‐līk‐kè yān; mŏ yat‐kòh yān t‘ō‐tsaú‐tak‐huì.
30 Kóm‐tsaû kóh‐yāt Moh‐aapₒ kwai‐fūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān. Kóh‐ti teî‐fong tsaû on‐heúng t‘aaì‐p‘īng paatₒ‐shāp nīn kòm‐noî.
31 ¶ Ĭ‐fat chi‐haû, yaŭ À‐nā‐kè tsaí Shaan‐kaapₒ, Shaan‐kaapₒ tsaû yûng kón‐ngaū‐kè kwàn, tá‐sź lūk‐paakₒ Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān: k‘uĭ yaû haî kaù Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kè.
