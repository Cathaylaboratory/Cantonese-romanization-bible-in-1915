1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Moh‐sai wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh shaí neĭ heùng Faatₒ‐lŏ t‘aì‐toî Sheûng‐Taì: neĭ taaî‐ló À‐lūn tsaû tsô neĭ‐kè sin‐chi.
2 Faān ngŏh fan‐foò neĭ‐kè, neĭ pit iù kóng‐ch‘ut‐laī: neĭ taaî‐ló À‐lūn pit iù tuì Faatₒ‐lŏ kóng, táng k‘uĭ shik‐fòng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ch‘ut k‘uĭ‐kè kíng‐kaaì.
3 Ngŏh pit kong‐pik Faatₒ‐lŏ‐kè sam, yīk pit haí Oi‐k‘āp teî hāng hó‐toh k‘eī‐tsik.
4 Taân Faatₒ‐lŏ pit m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng neĭ, chì‐tò ngŏh haí Oi‐k‘āp tûng shaú, hāng taaî yīng‐fāt laī taaì ngŏh kwan‐luĭ, tsik‐haî ngŏh‐kè paakₒ‐sìng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp teî.
5 Ngŏh kuí shaú kung‐kik Oi‐k‘āp‐yān, taaì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî chi‐chung ch‘ut‐laī chi‐shī, Oi‐k‘āp‐yān tsaû pit chi ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
6 Moh‐sai À‐lūn tsaû chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò laī hāng.
7 Tuì Faatₒ‐lŏ kóng kòh‐shī, Moh‐sai ĭ‐king paatₒ‐shāp‐suì, À‐lūn paatₒ‐shāp‐saam suì.
8 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai À‐lūn wâ,
9 Faatₒ‐lŏ yeūk‐haî tuì neĭ wâ, Neĭ hāng k‘eī‐tsik ngŏh t‘aí: neĭ iù tuì À‐lūn wâ, K‘aaĭ neĭ kwaaí‐cheûng wing‐lōk Faatₒ‐lŏ mîn‐ts‘īn, táng kwaaí‐cheûng pìn‐waī shē.
10 Moh‐sai À‐lūn yāp‐huì kìn Faatₒ‐lŏ, tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò laī tsô: À‐lūn haí Faatₒ‐lŏ k‘āp k‘uĭ chùng pūk mîn‐ts‘īn, wing kwaaí‐cheûng lōk teî, tsaû pìn‐waī shē.
11 Faatₒ‐lŏ yīk chiû Oi‐k‘āp kè ts‘ung‐mīng yān, mō‐ché: tsik‐haî Oi‐k‘āp kè pokₒ‐sẑ, k‘uĭ‐teî yûng ts‘ē‐shūt laī tsô ni‐ti sẑ.
12 Kokₒ‐yān wing k‘uĭ‐kè kwaaí‐cheûng, k‘ui pìn‐waī shē: waī‐haî À‐lūn‐kè kwaaí‐cheûng, t‘an‐hiu k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè kwaaí‐cheûng.
13 Taân Faatₒ‐lŏ kè sam hó‐ngaâng, m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ; hó‐ts‘z̆ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng.
14 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Faatₒ‐lŏ waān‐káng, m̄‐háng fòng paakₒ‐sìng huì.
15 Mīng‐chiu, neĭ huì Faatₒ‐lŏ ch‘uè; k‘uĭ pit ch‘ut‐tò hōh pin; neĭ k‘eĭ tsoî hōh‐pin; shaú cha ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn pìn‐shē‐kè kwaaí‐cheûng laī táng k‘uĭ.
16 Neĭ iù tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Hei‐paakₒ‐loī‐yān‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tá‐faatₒ ngŏh laī kìn neĭ, wâ, Fòng ngŏh paakₒ‐sìng huì, táng k‘uĭ‐teî haí kw‘òng‐yĕ fūk‐sẑ ngŏh: heùng‐loī neĭ m̄‐háng t‘eng‐ts‘ūng.
17 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Yan ts‘ź sẑ, neĭ pit chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: t‘aí‐a, ngŏh tsaû k‘aaĭ shaú‐cha‐kè kwaaí‐cheûng laī tá hōh shuí, shuí pit pìn‐waī huetₒ.
18 Hōh‐chung‐kè uē pit sź, kòh‐hōh sing‐ch‘aù; Oi‐k‘āp‐yān tsang‐ìm yám hōh‐chung‐kè shuí.
19 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Neĭ iù tuì À‐lūn wâ, Ning neĭ kwaaí‐cheûng, shan‐hoi shaú chí‐chuê Oi‐k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ‐kè shuí, tsik‐haî kong, hōh, ch‘ī, k‘āp kokₒ‐shuí‐t‘ōng, lîng pìn‐waī huetₒ; táng Oi‐k‘āp t‘ung kwokₒ mūk‐shēk‐heì‐mĭng‐luĭ to yaŭ huetₒ.
20 Moh‐sai À‐lūn chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò laī tsô; tong Faatₒ‐lŏ k‘āp chùng shān mîn‐ts‘īn kuí‐heí kwaaí‐cheûng tá hōh shuí; hōh shuí tsaû pìn‐saaì huetₒ.
21 Hōh‐chung‐kè uē sź‐hiu; hōh tsaû seng‐ch‘aù, Oi‐k‘āp‐yān pat‐nāng yám hōh‐luĭ‐kè shuí; Oi‐k‘āp t‘ung kwokₒ to yaŭ huetₒ.
22 Oi‐k‘āp kè pokₒ‐sẑ, yûng ts‘ē‐shūt yīk chiū‐yeûng laī tsò: Faatₒ‐lŏ kè sam, yīng‐īn kong‐ngaâng, m̄‐háng t‘eng‐ts‘ūng; hó‐ts‘z̆ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh kóng.
23 Faatₒ‐lŏ faan‐yāp kung, yīk m̄ kwà‐nîm ni‐kîn‐sẑ.
24 Oi‐k‘āp‐yān yan‐hōh shuí pat‐nāng yám; koò‐ts‘ź kan‐chuê hōh pin, kwāt teî wán shuí.
25 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tá hōh chi‐haû, moŏn‐hiu ts‘at yāt.
