1 SHĀP‐Î‐UĒT, mēng À‐taāt‐uēt‐kè shāp‐saam yāt, wōng‐kè mîng kûng sheûng‐uê tseung‐kân kuí‐hāng, tsik‐haî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk, mông‐tak koón‐hāt k‘uĭ‐teî kòh‐yāt, (taân sẑ‐ts‘īng yaŭ koí‐pìn, faán lîng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān koón‐hāt faān uèn‐hân k‘uĭ‐kè yān;)
2 Kòh‐yāt Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān haí tsẑ‐keí kokₒ‐shēng, tsoî À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng kokₒ‐shaáng chi‐chung tsuî‐tsaāp: kuí‐shaú kung‐kik faān‐seúng‐hoî k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè yān: mŏ‐yān ooĭ taí‐tóng k‘uĭ‐teî; yan‐waî shóh‐yaŭ līt‐mān to p‘à k‘uĭ‐teî.
3 Kokₒ shaáng kwan‐cheúng kûng‐taaî‐shān fong‐paakₒ, pìng paân‐leĭ wōng‐sẑ‐kè yān, to yan kuî‐p‘à Mūk‐taí‐koí; tsaû pong‐chôh Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān.
4 Yan Mūk‐taí‐koí haí wōng‐kung shīng‐waī‐taaî, k‘uĭ‐kè mīng‐shing, ch‘uēn‐wān kokₒ‐shaáng: yan ts‘ź‐yān Mūk‐taí‐koí tsîm‐tsĭm uēt‐faatₒ shîng‐taaî.
5 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān tsaû yûng to‐kìm kung‐kik shóh‐yaŭ‐kè tīk‐yān, shaatₒ‐lūk mīt‐tsuēt k‘uĭ‐teî, yâm‐ì heùng faān‐uèn‐hân k‘uĭ‐kè laī‐tsô.
6 Haí Shue‐shaan‐kung, Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān shaatₒ‐mīt nğ‐paakₒ yān.
7 Yaû shaatₒ Pa‐shaan‐taaî‐t‘a, Taapₒ‐poón, Aapₒ‐pa‐t‘a,
8 P‘òh‐la‐t‘a, À‐taaî‐leî, À‐leĭ‐taaî‐t‘a,
9 Pa‐moōt‐taaî, À‐leĭ‐ts‘oì, À‐leĭ‐t‘aaì, Ngă‐yē‐saatₒ‐taaî, faān shāp yān,
10 Tsik‐haî Hap‐maĭ‐taāt‐taaî‐kè suen, Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk, Hap‐maân‐kè tsaí; Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān pìng mŏ‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt ts‘oī‐māt.
11 Tong kòh‐yāt tseung tsoî Shue‐shaan peî‐shaatₒ‐sź‐kè yān‐shò, ch‘īng sheŭng wōng‐ts‘īn.
12 Wōng tuì wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ wâ, Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān haí Shue‐shaan‐kung shaatₒ‐mīt nğ‐paakₒ yān, yaû shaatₒ‐hiu Hap‐maân‐kè shāp‐kòh tsaí; tsoî wōng k‘eī‐uē‐kè shaáng, m̄‐chi tsô‐kwòh mat‐yĕ? uē‐kam neĭ yaŭ pán‐mat‐yĕ‐ni? tsaû pit ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ: chûng k‘aū mat‐yĕ ni? yaû pit shīng‐tsaû.
13 Ue‐shî Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ wâ, Wōng yeūk ĭ‐waī shîn, uên chún‐tsoî Shue‐shaan‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān mīng‐yāt i‐faan kam‐yāt‐kè uê‐chí laī‐tsô, pìng tseung Hap‐maân‐kè shāp‐kòh‐tsaí, tiù tsoî mūk‐kà‐sheûng.
14 Wōng yaû fan‐foò kóm‐yeûng tsô: ch‘uēn chí tsoî Shue‐shaan; yaû tseung Hap‐maân shāp‐kòh tsaí tiù kéng.
15 À‐taāt‐uēt, shāp‐sz̀ yāt, tsoî Shue‐shaan‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, yaû tsuî‐tsaāp haí Shue‐shaan, shaatₒ‐hiu saam‐paakₒ yān; pìng mŏ ts‘eúng‐tuēt ts‘oī‐māt.
16 Tsoî wōng kokₒ‐shaáng, k‘eī‐uē‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān yīk tsuî‐tsaāp, tsẑ laāp pó‐oô shang‐mîng, shaatₒ‐hiu uèn‐hân k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè yān, ts‘at‐maân‐nğ‐ts‘in, t‘uetₒ‐leī ch‘aū‐tīk tak‐p‘īng‐on, taân mŏ‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt ts‘oī‐māt,
17 À‐taāt‐uēt, shāp‐saam yāt, shīng‐tsaû ts‘ź‐sẑ; shāp‐sz̀ yāt, chùng‐yān on‐sik, ĭ kóh‐yāt waī ch‘itₒ‐ìn foon‐lōk‐kè yāt‐tsź.
18 Waī tsoî Shue‐shaan‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, shāp‐saam yāt shāp‐sz̀ tsuî‐tsaāp; shāp‐nğ yāt on‐sik, ĭ kóh‐yāt waī‐ch‘itₒ‐ìn foon‐lōk‐kè yāt‐tsź.
19 Shóh‐ĭ chuê‐tsoî kokₒ‐faû ts‘uen‐heung‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, to ĭ À‐taāt‐uēt, shāp‐sz̀ yāt waī foon‐heí ch‘itₒ‐ìn‐kè yāt‐tsź, ĭ‐kóh‐yāt waī hó‐yāt‐tsź, peí‐ts‘ź seung‐sùng laĭ‐māt.
20 ¶ Mūk‐taí‐koí sé‐lōk ni‐ti‐sẑ, yaû keì‐shue‐sùn t‘ung‐chi faān chuê tsoî À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng kokₒ‐shaáng, waāk uĕn waāk kân‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān,
21 Laāp yat‐tîng‐laî tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî chi‐chung, tsik‐haî mooĭ‐nīn shaú À‐taāt uēt‐kè shāp‐sz̀ yāt, shāp‐nğ yāt,
22 Yan‐waî kòh‐uēt kòh‐yāt haî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, yau‐chuèn‐waī‐heí, hung‐chuèn‐waī‐kat, t‘uetₒ‐leī ch‘aū‐tīk tak‐p‘īng‐on: lîng chùng‐yān shaú ts‘ź‐leŭng‐yāt waī‐ch‘itₒ‐ìn foon‐lōk, peí‐ts‘ź seung‐sùng laĭ‐māt, chau‐tsaì k‘ūng‐yān‐kè yāt‐tsź.
23 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān i‐chuê Mūk‐taí‐koí shóh‐keì‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè sùn, tsaû shaú ts‘ź‐laî, hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘uĭ ch‘í‐ch‘oh shaú‐kè;
24 Yan‐waî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān chùng‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk, À‐kaapₒ tsūk, Hap‐maĭ‐taāt‐taaî‐kè tsaí Hap‐maân, maū‐hoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, seúng‐shaatₒ‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî, tsaû chaì Pò‐ĭ, tsik‐haî chap‐ch‘aū, seúng‐tsuēt k‘uĭ‐teî, shaatₒ‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî;
25 Taân ts‘ź‐sẑ tsaù sheŭng‐wōng‐chi, wōng tsaû ch‘uēn chí, lîng Hap‐maân maū‐hoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè okₒ‐sẑ kwai‐faan k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí t‘aū‐sheûng, yaû mîng tseung k‘uĭ kûng k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź, tiù‐tsoî mūk‐kà‐sheûng.
26 Yan‐waî chaì Pò‐ĭ, shóh‐ĭ kòh‐leŭng‐yāt kiù‐tsô Pò‐ĭ‐yāt. Kóm‐tsaû yan ts‘ź‐shue‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘an‐kìn ni‐ti‐sẑ, pìng shóh‐uê‐cheūk‐kè sẑ,
27 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān k‘uetₒ‐ì laāp tîng sheūng‐laî, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî, kûng k‘uĭ‐teî haû‐yuî, pìng kwai‐foô k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè yān, mooĭ‐nīn i‐k‘eī pit‐shaú‐kòh leŭng‐yāt tsô tsitₒ‐k‘eī, táng pat‐hóh faì‐hiu ts‘ź‐laî;
28 Yaû kokₒ‐shaáng kokₒ‐shēng, kokₒ‐ka līk‐toî pat‐hitₒ iù kán‐shaú keì‐nîm kòh‐leŭng‐yāt; chì‐ts‘ź Pò‐ĭ‐yāt, tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān chung, pat‐hóh faì‐hiu, waāk keì‐nîm ts‘ź‐yāt‐chi‐tsitₒ, tsoî k‘uĭ haû‐yuî chi‐chung chí‐sik.
29 Ue‐shî À‐peì‐hoī‐kè nuĭ, wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ, kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān Mūk‐taí‐koí, yûng ts‘uēn‐k‘uēn sé‐ts‘ź taî‐î shue‐sùn, t‘ung‐chi Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān shaú ts‘ź Pò‐ĭ yāt.
30 Yûng wōh‐p‘īng chan‐shāt chi‐īn, sé‐shue‐sùn t‘ung‐chi À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng kwokₒ, yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐ts‘at shaáng‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān,
31 Huèn k‘uĭ‐teî i shī‐haû, shaú ts‘ź Pò‐ĭ yāt, i‐chuê Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān Mūk‐taí‐koí kûng wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè, yīk i‐chuê chùng‐Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, waî tsẑ‐keí kûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè haû‐yuî shóh‐tîng‐hiu‐kè laî, kàm‐shīk oi‐huk‐chi‐sẑ.
32 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ fan‐foò shāt tîng shaú Pò‐ĭ yāt waī‐laî; kòh‐kîn‐sẑ yīk sé‐lōk shue‐sheûng.
