1 FÒNG‐CH‘É Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, neĭ heí‐ooĭ shám‐p‘oòn me? heí‐ooĭ shám‐p‘oòn laū‐shuí‐kè shēng me? kóm‐tsaû iù‐chi‐tò‐saaì k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐hóh‐oò‐chi‐sẑ.
3 Neĭ to iù wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Haî laū‐huetₒ tsoî k‘eī‐chung‐kè, táng‐tò k‘uĭ‐yāt‐k‘eī hóh‐ĭ‐laī‐tò, yaû chíng ngaŭ‐tseûng oo‐mooĭ kung‐kik‐tsẑ‐keí kòh‐shēng‐yap à.
4 Neĭ yan shóh‐laū‐kè huetₒ shīng‐waī yaŭ‐tsuî‐kè; yaû yan shóh‐chíng‐chi‐ngaŭ‐tseûng peî‐oo‐mooĭ; yaû lîng neĭ‐yāt‐tsź laī‐kân, yīk moŏn‐neĭ‐nīn‐shò‐chi‐k‘eī: shóh‐ĭ ngŏh ts‘āng lîng neĭ heùng‐līt‐pong tsô‐yūk‐mâ‐kè, heùng‐līt‐kwokₒ tsô‐kei‐siù‐kè māt.
5 Neĭ ch‘aù‐mēng‐kè kûng ch‘ung‐moŏn‐ts‘ō‐luên‐kè à, faān‐haî kân‐teî‐kè, kûng tsoî‐uĕn‐fong‐kè, to ooĭ‐kei‐siù‐neĭ.
6 T‘aí‐a, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè kwan‐cheúng, kokₒ i‐chuê‐k‘uĭ‐k‘uēn‐nāng tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung laū‐huetₒ.
7 Tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung to hing‐fat foô‐mŏ: tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung to hak‐pōk luĭ‐haakₒ: tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung kw‘ai‐foô koo‐tsź kûng kwá‐foŏ.
8 Neĭ ts‘āng miŭ‐shî ngŏh shìng‐māt, yaû tso‐t‘aapₒ ngŏh on‐sik‐yāt.
9 Tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung yaŭ p‘òng‐tūk‐kè yān laū‐huetₒ: tsoî neĭ‐chi‐chung to haí shaan‐sheûng shīk‐kwòh: tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung hāng‐oo‐waì‐kè sẑ.
10 Tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung yaû hín‐lô foô‐ts‘an‐kè ch‘ekₒ‐shan: tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung k‘eūng‐oo tsẑ‐pīt‐kè foŏ.
11 Yat‐kòh kûng lūn‐shè‐chi‐ts‘ai tsô hóh‐oò‐kè sẑ; yaû yat‐kòh yām‐luên tìm‐oo tsẑ‐keí‐sik‐foŏ; yaû yat‐kòh k‘eūng‐oo‐k‘uĭ tsź‐mooî, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐foô‐chi‐nuĭ.
12 Tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung yaŭ‐yān shaû‐fooí‐laū‐huetₒ; neĭ ts‘āng shau‐leî‐sik kûng ka‐tsang‐chi‐māt, neĭ t‘aam‐sam waî‐leî, hak‐pōk lūn‐shè, yaû mōng‐keì‐ngŏh, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
13 Shóh‐ĭ t‘aí‐a, ngŏh k‘aaĭ‐shaú kung‐tá‐neĭ, haî yan neĭ shóh‐chaân m̄‐kung‐tô‐kè leî, kûng neĭ‐chi‐chung shóh‐laū‐kè huetₒ.
14 Tong ngŏh‐ch‘ué‐chì‐neĭ‐kè yāt‐tsź, neĭ‐sam heí‐nāng‐yán‐shaû, neĭ‐shaú hóh‐tak‐kin‐k‘eūng me? ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng‐kóng‐kwòh, yīk pit‐shīng‐tsaû ts‘ź‐sẑ à.
15 Ngŏh to ooĭ‐fan‐saàn‐neĭ tsoî‐līt‐pong‐chi‐chung, p‘aaì‐hoi‐neĭ tsoî‐līt‐kwokₒ‐ch‘uè, ngŏh pit‐haí‐neĭ‐ch‘uè, tsiú‐mīt neĭ‐kè oo‐waì.
16 Neĭ pit‐tsoî‐tsẑ‐keí haí līt‐pong ngaăn‐ts‘īn peî‐tso‐t‘aapₒ, yīk pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
17 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
18 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, Ĭ‐shik‐līt heùng ngŏh shīng‐waī tsaāp‐liû: t‘ung‐to‐shīng‐waī fóh‐lō‐chung‐kè t‘ūng k‘āp sekₒ, kûng t‘itₒ, kûng uēn; yaû shīng‐waī ngān‐kè tsaāp‐liû.
19 Shóh‐ĭ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Yan neĭ‐teî t‘ung‐to‐shīng‐waī tsaāp‐liû, koò‐ts‘ź t‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐haí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐chi‐chung tsuî‐maaī neĭ.
20 Hó‐ts‘z̆ yān‐ka tsoî‐fóh‐lō‐chi‐chung, tsuî‐maaī‐ti‐ngān, ti‐t‘ūng, ti‐t‘itₒ, ti‐uēn, ti‐sekₒ, ch‘ui‐fóh tsoî‐k‘eī‐sheûng laī‐yūng‐fà hiu‐k‘uĭ; ngŏh faatₒ‐nau‐nô făn‐hân kòh‐shī, pit‐chiù‐kóm‐yeûng tsuî‐maaī‐neĭ, yaû fòng‐neĭ tsoî‐kòh‐ch‘uè laī‐yūng‐fà‐neĭ lokₒ.
21 Ngŏh shāt‐tsoî ooĭ‐tsuî‐maaī‐neĭ, yaû k‘aaĭ‐ngŏh‐līt‐nô‐kè fóh ch‘ui‐k‘āp‐neĭ‐sheûng, neĭ‐teî pit‐tsoî k‘eī‐chung peî‐yūng‐fà.
22 Hó‐ts‘z̆ ngān tsoî fóh‐lō‐chung peî‐yūng‐fà‐kè, neĭ‐teî pit‐tsoî‐k‘eī‐chung chiù‐kóm‐yeûng peî‐yūng‐fà; neĭ yīk‐pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘aaĭ‐făn‐hân tó‐lōk neĭ‐sheûng‐t‘aū lokₒ.
23 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
24 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ haí meî‐saí‐kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè teî, tsoî taaî‐nô‐chi‐yāt, meî‐yaŭ‐uĕ kòng‐lōk‐kè.
25 Tsoî‐k‘eī‐chung yaŭ k‘uĭ sin‐chi maū‐faán‐kè sẑ, hó‐ts‘z̆ p‘aaū‐haau‐kè sz, maakₒ‐līt shóh‐fokₒ‐nā‐chi‐māt; k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘āng t‘an‐mīt yān‐kè līng‐wān; yaû ts‘uí‐hiu ts‘oī‐paāk kûng pó‐māt; yaû tsoî‐k‘eī‐chung lîng‐k‘uĭ‐kwá‐foŏ ka‐toh.
26 K‘uĭ‐kè tsaì‐sz, faân‐hiu‐ngŏh‐lūt‐faatₒ, yaû tso‐t‘aapₒ‐ngŏh‐shìng‐māt: k‘uĭ‐teî mŏ‐fan‐pīt shìng‐māt kûng tsūk‐māt, yaû m̄‐ts‘āng lîng‐yān‐fan‐hoi pat‐kitₒ kûng kitₒ‐tsîng‐chi‐māt, yaû heùng‐ngŏh‐on‐sik‐yāt che‐ím k‘uĭ‐ngaăn, ngŏh yīk tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung peî‐sitₒ‐tūk à.
27 Tsoî k‘eī‐chung k‘uĭ kwan‐cheúng hó‐ts‘z̆ ch‘aaī‐lōng maakₒ‐līt shóh‐fokₒ‐kè māt, seúng‐laū‐huetₒ yaû mīt‐mōng‐yān‐kè līng‐wān, táng‐k‘uĭ hóh‐ĭ‐chaân‐tó m̄‐kung‐tô‐kè leî.
28 K‘uĭ‐kè sin‐chi yīk waî k‘uĭ k‘aaĭ‐shang‐fooi laī‐tông‐ts‘eūng, tsik‐haî heùng‐k‘uĭ‐teî kìn hui‐ká‐î‐tseûng, yaû chim‐puk‐taaî‐wâ, kóng‐wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, k‘eī‐shāt Yē‐Wōh‐Wā m̄‐ts‘āng‐kóng‐kè.
29 Kíng‐noî‐kè tsź‐mān hak‐pōk‐toî‐yān, yaû tsô‐kwaàn‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè sz̀, k‘uĭ‐teî shāt‐tsoî naān‐waī kw‘àn‐k‘ūng kûng p‘ān‐fāt‐kè yān: yaû m̄‐hōp‐leĭ‐hak‐pōk‐toî luĭ‐haakₒ.
30 Ī‐ch‘é ngŏh tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung, seúng‐wán yat‐kòh‐yān, laī‐chuk‐heí‐waī‐leī, yaû tsoî‐ngŏh‐mîn‐ts‘īn haí‐līt‐laân‐ch‘uè k‘eĭ‐laāp, táng‐tò m̄‐shaí‐mīt‐tsuēt‐kòh‐teî: waī‐haî tsúng‐m̄‐wán‐tak‐tó.
31 Shóh‐ĭ ngŏh tseung‐taaî‐nô tó‐lōk‐k‘uĭ‐sheûng; yaû k‘aaĭ‐ngŏh‐līt‐nô‐kè fóh tsiú‐mīt‐k‘uĭ‐teî: lîng k‘uĭ‐shóh‐hāng‐kè lô, waān‐faan tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐t‘aū‐sheûng, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
