1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ à, kw‘àn‐ngŏh‐chi‐yān kīk‐toh! kung‐ngŏh‐chi‐yān kīk‐chùng.
2 Yaŭ hó‐toh‐yān ĭ‐lûn ngŏh‐chi‐līng‐wān wâ, K‘uĭ shî‐pit pat‐tak Sheûng‐Taì ch‘íng‐kaù.
3 Taân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî ngŏh t‘āng‐p‘aaī, haî ngŏh wīng‐kwong; lîng ngŏh taam‐tak‐ko‐t‘aū.
4 Ngŏh faatₒ‐sheng k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî shìng‐shaan t‘eng ngŏh.
5 Ngŏh yat‐fàn yat‐síng; to laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā foō‐ch‘ī ngŏh.
6 Maân‐mān waī‐chuê kung‐kik ngŏh, ngŏh yīk m̄‐p‘à.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū neĭ heí‐shan; ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, k‘aū neĭ kaù ngŏh: yan neĭ yă‐ts‘āng kwaakₒ ngŏh ch‘aū‐tīk‐chi‐mîn; chitₒ okₒ‐yān‐chi‐ngā.
8 Ch‘íng‐kaù‐chi‐k‘uēn, shūk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: uên neĭ ts‘z̀‐fuk kwòh‐neĭ‐paakₒ‐sìng.
