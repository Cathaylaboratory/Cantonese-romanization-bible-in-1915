1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT‐YĀN ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp‐teî chi‐haû, taî‐î nīn, î‐uēt ch‘oh‐yat yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haí Sai‐naaĭ kw‘òng‐yĕ, tsoî ooî‐mōk tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Neĭ iù tseung Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè ooî‐chùng, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, yān mēng, chùng naām‐yān ch‘ā‐shó;
3 Faān haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè: neĭ kûng À‐lūn iù chiù‐i tuî‐nğ shó‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî.
4 Yaû iù tsoî mooĭ chi‐p‘aaì, kaán yat‐kòh yān t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ; tsô k‘uĭ poón‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè tsūk‐cheúng.
5 Iù t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ k‘eĭ‐laāp‐kè yān, līt mēng tsoî‐haû: shūk Laū‐pîn‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî Shî‐tiu‐ĭ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐suk.
6 Shūk Sai‐mîn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî So‐leĭ‐sha‐t‘aaì‐kè tsaí Shî‐lô‐mīt.
7 Shūk Yaū‐taaî chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî À‐maĭ‐nā‐taāt‐kè tsaí Nā‐shûn.
8 Shūk Ĭ‐saatₒ‐ka chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî So‐aapₒ‐kè tsaí Nā‐taân‐īp.
9 Shūk Sai‐pò‐lūn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî Hei‐lūn‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐aapₒ.
10 Yeukₒ‐shat‐kè tsź‐suen: yat‐kòh shūk Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî À‐maĭ‐fat‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă: yat‐kòh shūk Mă‐nā‐sai chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî Peí‐taaî‐suk‐kè tsaí Ka‐mă‐līt.
11 Shūk Pîn‐ngă‐măn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî K‘eī‐t‘īn‐neī‐kè tsaí À‐peì‐taân.
12 Shūk Taân chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî À‐maĭ‐sha‐t‘aaì‐kè tsaí À‐hīp‐sz.
13 Shūk À‐ch‘itₒ chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî Okₒ‐laān‐kè tsaí P‘à‐kitₒ.
14 Shūk Ka‐tak chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî Tiu‐ĭ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ.
15 Shūk Naāp‐taaî‐leî chi‐p‘aaì‐kè; haî Ĭ‐naām‐kè tsaí À‐hei‐la.
16 Ni‐ti haî ts‘ūng ooî‐chung suén‐chiû‐kè yān, kokₒ tsô poón‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè mūk‐cheúng, tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè ts‘in‐tsúng.
17 Ni‐ti‐yān peî mēng suén‐chiû‐kè yān, Moh‐sai, À‐lūn to taaì‐lĭng k‘uĭ‐teî:
18 Î‐uēt ch‘oh‐yat yāt chiu tsaāp ooî‐chùng, chiù nīn‐suì, faān tsẑ î‐shāp suì ĭ‐sheûng‐kè, to kóng mīng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, laī ch‘ā‐shó tím mēng.
19 Ue‐shî Moh‐sai tsoî Sai‐naaĭ kw‘òng‐yĕ, tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè laī ch‘ā‐shó.
20 ¶ Kaì k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt cheúng‐tsź Laū‐pîn‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, naām‐ting, laī ch‘ā‐shó, faān tsẑ î‐shāp suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
21 Ni‐ti shūk Laū‐pîn chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng yaŭ sz̀‐maân‐lūk‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ.
22 ¶ Sai‐mîn‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, naām‐ting, laī shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
23 Ni‐ti shūk Sai‐mîn chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng yaŭ nğ‐maân‐kaú‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ.
24 ¶ Ka‐tak‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
25 Ni‐ti shūk Ka‐tak chi‐p‘aaì, peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng yaŭ sz̀‐maân‐nğ‐ts‘in‐lūk‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp.
26 ¶ Yaū‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
27 Ni‐ti shūk Yaū‐taaî chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng yaŭ ts‘at‐maân‐sz̀‐ts‘in‐lūk‐paakₒ.
28 ¶ Ĭ‐saatₒ‐ka‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
29 Ni‐ti shūk Ĭ‐saatₒ‐ka chi‐p‘aaì peî ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng yaŭ nğ‐maân‐sz̀‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ.
30 ¶ Sai‐pò‐lūn‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī‐shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
31 Ni‐ti shūk Sai‐pò‐lūn chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng yaŭ nğ‐maân‐ts‘at‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ.
32 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shat‐kè haû‐toî Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
33 Ni‐ti shūk Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng yaŭ sz̀‐maân‐līng‐nğ‐paakₒ.
34 ¶ Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī‐shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
35 Ni‐ti shūk Mă‐nā‐sai chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng‐yaŭ saam‐maân‐î‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ.
36 ¶ Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī‐shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
37 Ni‐ti shūk Pîn‐ngă‐măn chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng‐yaŭ saam‐maân‐nğ‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ.
38 ¶ Taân‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī‐shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
39 Ni‐ti shūk Taân chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng‐yaŭ lūk‐maân‐î‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ.
40 ¶ À‐ch‘itₒ‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī‐shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
41 Ni‐ti shūk À‐ch‘itₒ chi‐p‘aaì peî‐ch‘ā‐shó‐kè yān, kûng yaŭ sz̀‐maân‐yat‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ.
42 ¶ Naāp‐taaî‐leî‐kè tsź‐suen, haî chiù k‘uĭ‐kè shaì‐toî, chi‐p‘aaì, tsung‐tsūk, mēng‐tsẑ, laī‐shó‐kwòh, faān tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
43 Ni‐ti shūk Naāp‐taaî‐leî chi‐p‘aaì‐kè yān, shó‐ch‘ut kûng‐yaŭ nğ‐maân‐saam‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ.
44 ¶ Ni‐ti chùng‐yān haî Moh‐sai À‐lūn kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt shāp‐î mūk‐cheúng shóh‐shó‐kwòh‐kè: kokₒ‐mūk‐cheúng ch‘ā‐shó tsẑ‐keí tsung‐tsūk.
45 Kóm‐tsaû Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān shóh‐shó‐kwòh‐kè, haî chiù‐k‘uĭ‐kè tsung‐tsūk laī‐kaì, tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè;
46 Tsik‐haî faān shóh‐shó‐ch‘ut‐kè, tsúng‐kûng yaŭ lūk‐shāp‐maân‐līng‐saam‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp.
47 ¶ Tūk‐haî Leî‐meî‐yān poón‐tsūk‐kè chi‐p‘aaì m̄‐yāp‐shò.
48 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yă‐ts‘āng fan‐foò Moh‐sai wâ,
49 Tūk‐haî Leî‐meî‐kè chi‐p‘aaì, pat‐hóh ch‘ā‐shó, pat‐hóh tseung k‘uĭ‐teî kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān t‘ūng kaì:
50 Iù fan‐p‘aaì Leî‐meî‐yān koón‐yeukₒ‐kwaî‐kè waī‐mōk, k‘āp kokₒ‐yeûng heì‐mĭng, kûng shóh‐yaŭ‐kè māt‐kîn: k‘uĭ‐teî iù t‘oī waī‐mōk, k‘āp kokₒ‐yeûng heì‐mĭng, tsoî waī‐mōk tsẑ‐haû; ī‐ch‘é òn‐yīng waī‐chuê waī‐mōk.
51 Waī‐mōk poon‐ts‘īn kòh‐shī, Leî‐meî‐yān pit iù ch‘aakₒ‐lat: laāp‐tîng waī‐mōk kòh‐shī, Leî‐meî‐yān iù taapₒ‐heí: yeūk yaŭ pīt‐yān kân‐ts‘īn‐kè, tsaû iù shaû sź.
52 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān òn‐yīng iù kokₒ‐yān kwai tsẑ‐keí k‘eī‐hâ, yaû kan‐chuê tuî‐nğ laī taapₒ cheùng‐mōk.
53 ¶ Taân Leî‐meî‐yān iù tsoî waī‐mōk‐kè sz̀‐waī paaí‐līt yīng‐p‘oōn: ī‐ch‘é kán‐shaú yeukₒ‐kwaî waī‐mōk‐kè chik‐yīk, mĭn‐chì nau‐nô lām‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt ooî‐chùng.
54 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû kóm‐yeûng hāng, tsik‐haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai laī‐hāng‐kè.
