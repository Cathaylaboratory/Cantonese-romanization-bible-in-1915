1 TONG‐SHĪ tsaì‐sz Yam‐māk‐kè tsaí Pa‐shūt, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk tsô‐chì‐taaî‐kè koon, t‘eng‐man Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐kóng‐kwòh ni‐ti‐uê‐īn.
2 Ue‐shî Pa‐shūt kung‐tá sin‐chi Yē‐leî‐maĭ, yaû k‘aaĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐noî kân Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐moōn‐chi‐mūk‐kà chaakₒ‐chuê k‘uĭ.
3 Mīng‐yāt shīng‐tsaû haî‐kóm, Pa‐shūt tsaû‐tseung Yē‐leî‐maĭ ch‘ut‐kòh‐mūk‐kà‐ch‘uè. Ue‐shî Yē‐leî‐maĭ tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā m̄‐ts‘āng ch‘ing neĭ mēng waī Pa‐shūt, waī‐haî ch‘ing neĭ waī Mă‐kokₒ‐maĭ‐saatₒ‐pat.
4 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Ngŏh tseung lîng neĭ heùng tsẑ‐keí, yaû heùng taaî‐chùng p‘āng‐yaŭ, shīng‐waī king‐fong‐kè: k‘uĭ‐teî tseung peî‐ch‘aū‐tīk‐kè kìm pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè, neĭ‐kè ngaăn to‐ooĭ‐t‘aí‐kìn: ngŏh iù‐tseung Yaū‐t‘aaì ts‘uēn‐teî kaau‐kwòh Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng, k‘uĭ tsaû lŏ‐leūk‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐huì‐tò Pa‐peí‐lūn, yaû k‘aaĭ‐kìm‐laī‐shaatₒ à.
5 Fòng‐ch‘é ngŏh iù‐k‘aaĭ‐saaì ts‘ź‐shēng‐kè ts‘oī‐paāk, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung shóh‐tsik‐maaī‐kè, kûng k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè pó‐māt, uĕ‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì shóh‐ts‘ōng‐kè fòh‐ts‘oī, t‘ung‐to‐kaau‐lōk k‘uĭ‐ch‘aū‐tīk‐chi‐shaú, tsik‐haî lŏ‐leūk‐k‘uĭ huì poon‐tò Pa‐peí‐lūn‐ch‘uè‐kè yān.
6 Pa‐shūt à, neĭ kûng faān‐chuê‐tsoî neĭ‐ka‐chung‐kè, t‘ung‐to iù‐peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐huì: neĭ yīk pit‐tò Pa‐peí‐lūn, tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè sź‐mōng, yaû tsoî‐kòh‐ch‘uè‐maaī‐tsòng, neĭ t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ‐kè chùng‐p‘āng‐yaŭ, tsik‐haî neĭ ts‘āng tuì‐k‘uĭ kóng ká‐ngaî uê‐īn‐kè, to‐haî kóm.
7 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ‐ts‘āng yăn‐yaŭ ngŏh, ngŏh tsaû peî‐yăn‐yaŭ lokₒ: neĭ līk‐taaî‐kwòh‐ngŏh‐kè, tsaû tak‐shìng lokₒ: ngŏh shīng‐yāt to‐waī yān shóh‐heì‐siù, kokₒ‐yān to‐heì‐lûng ngŏh.
8 Yan ngŏh mooĭ‐fūng kóng‐lûn‐chi‐shī, tsaû haàm‐sheng wâ, K‘eūng‐okₒ kŭng ts‘eúng‐tuēt à; haî yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, shīng‐yāt heùng‐ngŏh shīng‐waī‐līng‐yūk k‘āp heì‐siù‐kè sẑ.
9 Uē‐kwóh ngŏh wâ, Ngŏh m̄‐kóng‐heí‐k‘uĭ, yaû m̄‐t‘okₒ k‘uĭ mēng laī‐ch‘uēn‐suen. Tsoî ngŏh‐sam‐chung, hó‐ts‘z̆ shiu‐īt‐kè fóh, wàn‐chuê tsoî kwat‐luĭ, ngŏh yīk yán‐noî‐tò‐kaù‐kooî, yaû m̄‐taí‐tak‐chuê à.
10 Yan ngŏh ĭ‐king t‘eng‐tó‐yān sitₒ‐tūk‐kè, chau‐waī to‐yaŭ‐king‐fong sẑ‐ts‘īng. Shóh‐seung‐shik‐kè p‘āng‐yaŭ, tsik‐haî t‘aí‐chuê ngŏh chak‐keukₒ kóm‐hāng‐kè, to wâ iù‐kò‐ngŏh. Ngŏh‐teî to‐kò‐k‘uĭ, waāk‐ché k‘uĭ hóh‐ĭ peî‐yăn‐yaŭ, ngŏh‐teî tsaû shìng‐hiu‐k‘uĭ, yaû heùng k‘uĭ pò‐ch‘aū à.
11 Waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh tsô‐taaî‐nāng‐līk, yaû tsô‐hóh‐kuî‐p‘à‐kè: shóh‐ĭ kw‘àn‐pik‐ngŏh‐kè yān, ooĭ‐kwaàn‐titₒ m̄‐tak‐shìng: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐kìn‐sau‐kw‘aì‐hó‐kaau‐kwaan; tsik‐haî shaû‐wĭng‐uĕn pat‐hóh‐mōng‐keì‐kè līng‐yūk: yan k‘uĭ‐teî mŏ‐yûng chì‐waî hāng‐sẑ à.
12 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, shì‐lîn kung‐î‐kè yān, yaû kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ noî‐ch‘ung kûng sam‐līng‐kè sẑ à, k‘aū neĭ chún‐ngŏh t‘aí‐kìn neĭ tsoî kòh‐ti‐yān pò‐ch‘aū‐kè sẑ‐ts‘īng: yan ngŏh heùng‐neĭ hín‐mīng‐ngŏh kò‐tsûng à.
13 Iù‐heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘eùng‐koh, iù‐tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: yan k‘uĭ ts‘āng‐kaù kw‘àn‐fāt‐yān‐kè līng‐wān, t‘uetₒ‐leī hāng‐okₒ‐yān‐kè shaú à.
14 ¶ Ngŏh ch‘ut‐shaì‐kòh‐yāt, iù‐peî‐chaù‐chòh: mŏ‐ts‘an shang‐ngŏh‐kòh‐yāt, pat‐hóh‐suèn‐haî‐yaŭ‐fuk‐kè.
15 Ch‘uēn ts‘ź‐siu‐sik kwòh‐ngŏh‐foô‐ts‘an, wâ, Neĭ shang‐kè naām‐tsaí, lîng k‘uĭ kóm‐yeûng faaì‐lōk; kòh‐yān iù‐shaû‐chaù‐chòh.
16 Iù kóh‐kòh‐yān shīng‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐k‘ing‐paaî to‐mŏ‐tsẑ‐fooì‐kè shēng‐yap: iù‐peí‐k‘uĭ‐tsoî chiu‐tsó t‘eng‐kìn haàm‐foó‐kè sheng, tsoî aàn‐chaù t‘eng‐mān ts‘ō‐luên‐kè sheng;
17 Yan k‘uĭ haí mŏ‐t‘oi m̄‐ts‘āng shaatₒ ngŏh; táng ngŏh mŏ‐ts‘an kóm‐yeûng tsô‐fān‐mô, yaû wĭng‐uĕn aatₒ‐ngŏh tsoî‐t‘oi‐chung.
18 Ngŏh yan‐hōh ch‘ut‐mŏ‐t‘oi laī‐kìn‐lō‐foó kûng yau‐shaū, táng ngŏh tsoî shang‐ts‘īn‐kè yāt‐tsź shaû‐sau‐yūk tsiú‐mīt ni?
