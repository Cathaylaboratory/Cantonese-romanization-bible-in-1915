1 NŌH‐À kè tsaí Shím, Hōm, Ngă‐fat: tsoî hūng‐shuí haû, shóh shang chi tsź‐suen, tsūk‐p‘ó līt mīng tsoî tsóh.
2 ¶ Ngă‐fat kè tsaí Hóh‐mīt, Mă‐lokₒ, Maĭ‐t‘aaì, Ngă‐faan, T‘ó‐paatₒ, Maĭ‐ch‘itₒ, T‘aī‐laāp.
3 Hóh‐mīt kè tsaí À‐shāt, Kei‐nā, Leĭ‐faatₒ, T‘ōh‐ka‐mă.
4 Ngă‐faan kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐sha, Taaî‐shat, Kei‐taí, Toh‐taan.
5 Ni‐ti yān, saàn‐hoi chuê tsoî kokₒ ch‘uè hoí‐tó; kokₒ yān i‐faan tsẑ‐keí t‘ó‐t‘aām tsung‐tsūk pong‐kwokₒ laī fan‐pīt.
6 ¶ Hōm kè tsaí Koó‐shāt, Māk‐sai, Fat, Ka‐naām.
7 Koó‐shāt kè tsaí Sai‐pa, Hap‐feī‐la, Saapₒ‐taaî, La‐mă, Saapₒ‐t‘aī‐ka: La‐mă kè tsaí Shî‐pa, Taí‐taân.
8 Koó‐shāt yaû shang Nīng‐lūk: haî shaì‐sheûng kè ying‐hūng.
9 Tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘īn, haî yat‐kòh ying‐yŭng kè līp‐yān: shóh‐ĭ tsūk‐uĕ wâ, Hó‐ts‘z̆ Nīng‐lūk tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chi ts‘īn, tsaû haî tsô ying‐yŭng kè līp‐yān lokₒ.
10 K‘uĭ kè kwokₒ, ch‘í‐ch‘oh tsoî Shî‐nā kè Pa‐pīt, Ĭ‐līk, À‐kaapₒ, Kaapₒ‐neî teî‐fong.
11 À‐shāt ts‘ūng kòh‐ch‘uè ch‘ut, kìn‐laāp Neī‐neī‐meī, Leĭ‐hōh‐paakₒ, Ka‐la shēng,
12 Uĕ‐k‘āp Neī‐neī‐meī Ka‐la chi‐chung taaî shēng: mēng kiù Leĭ‐sin kè.
13 Māk‐sai shang Lô‐ting, On‐naām, Leî‐hap, Naāp‐t‘ó,
14 Paatₒ‐lŏ, Kaapₒ‐lô, Kopₒ‐t‘okₒ, (Kaapₒ‐lô shang Fei‐leî‐sẑ.)
15 ¶ Ka‐naām shang taaî tsaí, Sai‐tûn kûng Haak,
16 Yē‐pò‐sẑ, À‐moh‐leĭ, Kaakₒ‐ka‐saatₒ,
17 Hei‐meî, À‐kei‐sai‐neī,
18 À‐ngă‐taí, Saí‐mă‐leĭ, Hap‐mootₒ: tsẑ ts‘ź chi haû, Ka‐naām kè tsūk saàn‐hoi lokₒ.
19 Ka‐naām kè kíng‐kaaì, ts‘ūng Sai‐tûn king‐kwòh K‘eī‐laāp, tò Ka‐saatₒ; yaû tò Shóh‐toh‐mă, Ngōh‐moh‐la, Aapₒ‐mă, Sai‐p‘in, La‐sha.
20 Ni‐ti haî Hōm kè tsź‐suen, kokₒ yān i‐faan k‘uĭ tsung‐tsūk kè t‘ó‐t‘aām pong‐kwokₒ, laī kui‐chuê.
21 ¶ Ngă‐fat kè taaî‐ló Shím, yīk shang tsaí, Shím haî Hei‐paakₒ kokₒ tsūk kè tsó.
22 Shím kè tsaí haî Ĭ‐laān, À‐shūt, À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ, Lô‐tak, À‐laān.
23 À‐laān kè tsaí Oo‐sẑ, Oô‐lāk, K‘eī‐t‘ipₒ, Mă‐shāt.
24 À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ shang Saatₒ‐la; Saatₒ‐la shang Hei‐paakₒ.
25 Hei‐paakₒ shang leŭng‐kòh tsaí: yat‐kòh mēng kiù Faatₒ‐lāk; yan‐waî kòh‐shī pong‐kwokₒ fan‐līt; koò‐ts‘ź mēng waī Faatₒ‐lāk, k‘uĭ saì‐ló mēng kiù Yeukₒ‐taan.
26 Yeukₒ‐taan shang À‐moh‐t‘aatₒ, Sha‐nap, Hap‐saatₒ‐mă‐fei, Yē‐la,
27 Hap‐toh‐laān, Oo‐saatₒ, Tāk‐la,
28 Oh‐paatₒ, À‐peì‐mă‐leî, Shî‐pa,
29 Oh‐fei, Hap‐feī‐la, Yeukₒ‐paatₒ: ni‐ti to haî Yeukₒ‐taan kè tsaí.
30 K‘uĭ‐teî shóh chuê kè teî‐fong, ts‘ūng Maĭ‐sha chì‐tò tung‐pîn Saí‐faatₒ shaan.
31 Ni‐ti tsaû haî Shím kè haû‐yuî, kokₒ yān i‐faan tsẑ‐keí tsung‐tsūk, t‘ó‐t‘aām, teî‐fong, pong‐kwokₒ, laī kui‐chuê.
32 ¶ Ĭ‐sheûng to haî Nōh‐à saam‐kòh tsaí kè haû‐yuî, kokₒ yān i‐faan tsẑ‐keí shaì‐haî: pong‐kwokₒ, king hūng‐shuí chi haû, saàn‐hoi kokₒ kwokₒ teî‐fong kui‐chuê.
