1 À‐HAP‐TSÊ‐KÈ mŏ‐ts‘an À‐taaî‐leî, kìn k‘uĭ tsaí sź‐hiu, tsaû heí‐laī tsiú‐mīt chùng‐wōng‐tsūk.
2 Yeukₒ‐laān wōng‐kè nuĭ, À‐hap‐tsê‐kè mooî Yeukₒ‐shî‐pa, tseung À‐hap‐tsê‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hāt, ts‘ūng peî‐shaatₒ‐kè chùng‐wōng‐tsź‐chung t‘au‐hiu ch‘ut‐laī; tseung k‘uĭ k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè uĕ‐mŏ ts‘ōng tsoî ngôh‐fōng, laī peî À‐taaî‐leî, mĭn‐chì k‘uĭ peî‐shaatₒ.
3 Yeukₒ‐shî‐pa tseung k‘uĭ ni‐maaī tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn, līk‐chì lūk nīn. À‐taaî‐leî ch‘ī‐leĭ kwokₒ‐chung.
4 ¶ Taî‐ts‘at nīn, Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî tá‐faatₒ yān kiù chùng‐paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng, tsik‐haî koón oô‐waî‐ping, k‘āp pan‐tsaú‐ping‐kè laī, taaì k‘uĭ‐teî yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, tsaû t‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî laāp‐yeukₒ, shaí k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk faatₒ‐shaî, yaû tseung wōng tsź chí‐shî k‘uĭ‐teî.
5 Tsaû fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî shóh‐tong‐hāng‐kè haî ni‐yeûng; faān on‐sik‐yāt, neĭ‐teî chiù paan‐līt yāp‐laī, saam‐fân‐chi‐yat, iù hon‐shaú wōng‐kung;
6 Saam‐fân‐chi‐yat, iù haí So‐ĭ‐moōn; saam‐fân‐chi‐yat, iù haí pan‐tsaú‐ping‐kè haû‐moōn: kóm‐tsaû hóh‐shaú‐chuê wōng‐kung, chóh‐kaakₒ chùng‐yān.
7 Neĭ‐teî on‐sik‐yāt shóh‐ch‘ut‐huì‐kè kóh‐leŭng‐fân, iù hon‐shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk oô‐waî wōng.
8 Iù kokₒ‐ning kwan‐heì, sz̀‐wai oô‐waî wōng: faān shîn yāp neĭ‐teî paan‐līt‐kè, pit iù shaatₒ k‘uĭ: wōng ch‘ut‐yāp‐chi‐shī, neĭ‐teî pit iù t‘ūng k‘uĭ.
9 Chùng‐paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng, tsaû i‐ts‘ūng tsaì‐sz Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî faān shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè laī‐tsô: kokₒ‐taaì shóh‐koón‐leĭ‐kè waāk on‐sik‐yāt ch‘ut, waāk on‐sik‐yāt yāp‐kè yān, laī kìn tsaì‐sz Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî.
10 Tsaì‐sz tsaû tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐chung shóh‐ts‘ōng Taaî‐waî‐kè ts‘eung kûng t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī, kaau‐kwòh chùng‐paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng.
11 Pan‐tsaú‐ping kokₒ‐ning‐kwan‐heì tsoî shaú, k‘eĭ‐laāp wōng‐kè sz̀‐waī, tsẑ‐tîn yaû chì‐tò tîn‐tsóh, waī‐chuê tsaì‐t‘aān kûng shìng‐shóh.
12 Tsaì‐sz taaì wōng‐tsź ch‘ut‐laī, taaì mĭn‐laū tsoî k‘uĭ t‘aū‐sheûng, tseung lūt‐faatₒ‐shue kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ; yaû kò k‘uĭ laāp k‘uĭ tsô wōng; chùng‐yān p‘aakₒ‐cheúng wâ, Uên wōng maân‐suì.
13 À‐taaî‐leî t‘eng‐mān pan‐tsaú‐ping k‘āp mān‐kè taaî‐sheng, tsaû laī‐tò paakₒ‐sìng tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk.
14 T‘aí‐hă tsaû kìn wōng chiù‐laî k‘eĭ‐tsoî t‘oī‐sheûng, yaû kìn kw‘ān‐shān k‘āp ch‘ui‐kokₒ‐kè yān shî‐laāp wōng‐kè chak‐pin, kwokₒ‐chung‐kè paakₒ‐sìng foon‐heí ch‘ui‐kokₒ: À‐taaî‐leî tsaû maakₒ‐līt i‐fūk, foo‐kiù wâ, Tsokₒ‐faán lòh, Tsokₒ‐faán lòh.
15 Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî fan‐foò koón‐kwan‐kè paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng wâ, Iù tseung k‘uĭ kón‐ch‘ut paan‐līt chi‐ngoî: taân‐faān kan‐ts‘uī‐k‘uĭ‐kè, pit iù yûng to shaatₒ‐sź. Yan tsaì‐sz wâ, M̄‐hó tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shìng‐tîn‐uk shaatₒ k‘uĭ.
16 Chùng‐yān hoi‐lô; táng À‐taaî‐leî ts‘ūng mă‐lô heùng wōng‐kung huì; tsaû tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè peî‐shaatₒ.
17 ¶ Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî laāp‐yeukₒ, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kûng wōng k‘āp paakₒ‐sìng‐kè yeukₒ, shaí paakₒ‐sìng tsô Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tsź‐mān; yaû tsoî wōng kûng paakₒ‐sìng laāp‐yeukₒ.
18 Kwokₒ‐chung‐kè chùng‐mān huì Pa‐līk miû waí‐ch‘aakₒ miû, yaû tá‐suì shóh‐yaŭ‐kè tsaì‐t‘aān ngaŭ‐tseûng; yaû tsoî t‘aān‐ts‘īn, tseung Pa‐līk‐kè tsaì‐sz Mă‐taam shaatₒ‐hiu. Tsaì‐sz Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî ch‘itₒ‐koon shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk.
19 K‘āp taaì‐lĭng chùng‐paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng, k‘āp oô‐waî‐ping, pan‐tsaú‐ping, pìng kwokₒ‐chung‐kè chùng‐mān; tsaû taaì wōng ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐tîn lōk‐laī, yaū pan‐tsaú‐ping‐kè moōn, yāp‐tò wōng‐kung. Wōng tsaû ts‘ŏh k‘uĭ‐kè waî.
20 Kwokₒ‐chung chùng‐mān hó‐foon‐heí, kûng heúng t‘aaì‐p‘īng: chùng‐yān tsaû haí wōng‐kung‐ch‘uè, tseung kìm laī‐shaatₒ‐hiu À‐taaî‐leî.
21 Yeukₒ‐hāt tang‐waî chi‐shī, nīn‐keí ts‘at suì.
