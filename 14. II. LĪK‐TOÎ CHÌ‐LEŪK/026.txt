1 KÒH‐SHĪ Oo‐sai‐à nīn‐keí shāp‐lūk suì, Yaū‐t‘aaì chùng‐tsź‐mān laāp k‘uĭ tsô wōng, kaì k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an À‐mă‐tsê‐kè waî.
2 Wōng kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám‐chi‐haû, k‘uĭ kìn‐chuk Ĭ‐lūk, shaí kwai‐faan Yaū‐t‘aaì koón‐hāt.
3 Oo‐sai‐à tang‐waî tsô wōng‐chi‐shī, nīn‐keí shāp‐lūk suì, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng nğ‐shāp‐î nīn. K‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an mēng‐kiù Yē‐hóh‐leî, haî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐yān à.
4 Oo‐sai‐à hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn‐waī‐shîn‐kè, tsun‐i k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an À‐mă‐tsê faān shóh‐hāng‐kè.
5 Tong shik Sheûng‐Taì hín‐shî‐kè Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à tsoî‐shang‐kè yāt‐tsź, Oo‐sai‐à laāp‐tîng‐ì chuen‐sam k‘aū Sheûng‐Taì, ts‘ām‐k‘aū kòm‐noî‐chi‐shī, Sheûng‐Taì shaí k‘uĭ leî‐taāt.
6 K‘uĭ ch‘ut‐huì chaang‐chìn kung‐tá Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān, tsaû k‘ing‐mīt Ka‐tāk‐kè shēng, Aapₒ‐neī‐kè shēng, À‐shāt‐tāt‐kè shēng, tsoî Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐chi‐kaan, tsoî À‐shāt‐tāt kíng‐noî kìn‐chuk kokₒ‐shēng.
7 Sheûng‐Taì pong‐chôh k‘uĭ kung‐kik Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān, kûng kui‐chuê Koo‐ĭ‐pa‐līk‐kè À‐la‐paakₒ‐yān, k‘āp Maĭ‐oô‐nīng‐yān.
8 À‐mnōn‐yān naāp‐kùng kwòh‐Oo‐sai‐à: k‘uĭ‐kè mīng‐shing ch‘uēn‐yeūng chì‐tò yāp Oi‐k‘āp‐kè kíng‐noî; yan k‘uĭ shīng‐waī kīk‐chi‐k‘eūng‐shîng à.
9 Kim‐chi Oo‐sai‐à tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, kìn‐chuk shuè‐laū tsoî shēng‐kokₒ‐kè moōn, yaû tsoî Kuk‐moōn, k‘āp shēng‐kè chuèn‐waan kòh‐ch‘uè, ī‐ch‘é kin‐koò k‘uĭ‐teî à.
10 Yaû kìn‐chuk shuè‐laū tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ, kim‐ch‘é kwāt hó‐toh‐tséng tsoî tai‐t‘īn, k‘āp tsoî p‘īng‐uēn: yan‐waî yaŭ hó‐toh‐ch‘uk‐shang à, yaû tsoî kokₒ‐shaan k‘āp tsoì leūng‐t‘īn: k‘uĭ yaŭ nūng‐foo kûng sau‐leĭ p‘ō‐t‘aī‐kè yān: yan k‘uĭ foon‐heí kaang‐chùng à.
11 Kim‐chi Oo‐sai‐à yaŭ yat‐kwan chìn‐sẑ, tsaû chiù‐i shîn‐sé‐ché Yē‐īp, kûng mūk‐paakₒ Mă‐sai‐ngă shóh‐tím‐shó, to tsoî wōng chùng‐tseùng‐chung‐chi‐yat, Hap‐nā‐meĭ‐kè shaú‐hâ, fan‐līt tuî‐nğ ch‘ut chìn.
12 Kòh‐ti taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ chùng‐foô‐ka‐chi‐cheúng‐kè shò, kûng kaì î‐ts‘in‐lūk‐paakₒ yān.
13 Tsoî k‘uĭ shaú‐hâ, yaŭ lîn‐tsaāp‐kè kwan‐ping, kûng‐kaì saam‐shāp‐maân‐ts‘at‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ yān, kaai‐haî taaî‐nāng‐chaang‐chìn, pong‐chôh wōng kung‐tá ch‘aū‐tīk‐kè à.
14 Oo‐sai‐à waî k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘uēn kwan uê‐peî tóng‐p‘aaī, ts‘eung, t‘aū‐kw‘ai, chìn‐kaapₒ, kung‐tsìn, k‘āp wing‐shēk‐kè shīng.
15 Yaû tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsokₒ kei‐haaî, lîng haaú‐shaú‐kè yān shóh‐ch‘òng‐tsô, on‐chì tsoî shēng‐laū kûng shīng‐yan‐chi‐sheûng, tseung‐laī shê‐tsìn kûng faatₒ‐taaî‐shēk. Oo‐sai‐à‐kè mīng‐shing ch‘uēn‐yeūng hó‐uĕn; yan‐waî k‘uĭ tak‐pong‐chôh hó‐kaau‐kwaan, chì k‘uĭ k‘eūng‐shîng à.
16 ¶ Keì‐īn tak‐k‘eūng‐shîng‐chi‐shī, k‘uĭ sam tsaû faatₒ‐kiu‐ngô, chì tsẑ‐keí kìn‐paaî‐mōng, tak‐tsuî k‘uĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: yan k‘uĭ yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shìng‐tîn, seúng fān‐heung tsoî heung‐t‘aān‐sheûng.
17 Tsaì‐sz À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, tuk‐lĭng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tsaì‐sz, yaŭ yŭng‐kè paatₒ‐shāp yān kan‐chuê k‘uĭ yāp:
18 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû tīk‐chóh Oo‐sai‐à wōng, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Oo‐sai‐à wōng à, neĭ m̄‐ying‐koi fān‐heung kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, haî iù tsaì‐sz À‐lūn haû‐yuî, tsik‐haî ch‘itₒ‐laāp‐fān‐heung‐kè; neĭ iù faan‐ch‘ut shìng‐shóh‐la; yan‐waî neĭ ĭ‐king faân‐tsuî; ni‐ti‐sẑ pit m̄‐shaí neĭ yaū Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tak wīng‐iû à.
19 Ue‐shî Oo‐sai‐à hó‐nau, yaŭ heung‐lō tsoî‐shaú seúng‐fān‐heung: yaû t‘ūng tsaì‐sz faatₒ‐nô chi‐shī, fung‐tsāt tsaû heí‐tsoî ngaāk‐t‘aū, tsoî‐heung‐t‘aān chak‐pîn, tong tsaì‐sz mîn‐ts‘īn, haí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐chung.
20 Tsaì‐sz‐cheúng À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, kûng chùng‐tsaì‐sz t‘aí‐hă, kìn k‘uĭ ngaāk‐sheûng faatₒ‐fung, tsaû k‘ui‐chūk k‘uĭ ch‘ut tîn‐uk; wōng tsẑ‐keí yīk kap‐ts‘uk ch‘ut, yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kung‐kik k‘uĭ à.
21 Oo‐sai‐à wōng faatₒ‐fung chì‐tò k‘uĭ sź‐chi‐yāt, yan‐waî faatₒ‐fung tūk‐tsẑ‐keí chuê hoi pīt‐ch‘uè wōng‐kung‐shat; k‘uĭ kìn‐tsuēt pat‐nāng yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐taam koón‐leĭ wōng‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐taam koón‐leĕ wōng‐kè ka, shám‐p‘oòn kíng‐noî‐kè tsź‐mān à.
22 Oo‐sai‐à shóh‐hāng k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, tsẑ ch‘í‐ch‘oh chì‐meĭ, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk sin‐chi À‐moh‐sẑ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐ts‘oì‐à‐kè shue me?
23 Oo‐sai‐à kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám, tseung k‘uĭ tsòng‐tsoî shūk‐līt‐wōng‐kè t‘īn‐kaan, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ līt‐tsó fān‐pin; chùng‐yān kóm wâ, K‘uĭ haî fung‐tsāt à: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐taam, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
