1 NGŎH chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, yan‐hōh tiu‐heì ngŏh? uĕn‐uĕn leī‐hoi, m̄‐ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh, m̄‐t‘eng ngŏh t‘aàn‐sik īn‐uĕ.
2 Ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, ngŏh yāt‐kaan foo‐kiù, neĭ m̄‐ying‐shīng; yê‐kaan k‘eī‐k‘aū, tsúng m̄‐tak on‐waì.
3 Waī‐haî neĭ haî chì‐shìng, ts‘ŏh‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaàn‐tsûng‐chi‐waî.
4 Ngŏh‐teî līt‐tsó í‐laaî neĭ: í‐laaî neĭ tsaû tak‐kaù.
5 Foo‐kiù neĭ tak‐t‘uetₒ waân‐naân: í‐k‘aaù neĭ pat‐chi sau‐kw‘aì.
6 Ngŏh hó‐ts‘z̆ ch‘ūng‐ngaĭ, m̄‐yāp yān‐luî; peî yān‐teî līng‐yūk, peî paakₒ‐sìng miŭ‐shî.
7 Taân‐faān kìn‐ngŏh‐ché, to heì‐lûng ngŏh: nō‐tsuí iū‐t‘aū wâ,
8 K‘uĭ keì k‘aaù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaû pit laī ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ: yan k‘uĭ haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐foon‐heí‐ché.
9 Waī‐haî neĭ lîng ngŏh shang‐ch‘ut mŏ‐t‘oi: ngŏh tsoî mŏ‐ts‘an waaī‐p‘ŏ‐chi‐shī, neĭ shaí ngŏh í‐laaî neĭ.
10 Ngŏh tsẑ ch‘ut‐t‘oi, tsaû í‐k‘aaù neĭ: tsẑ ch‘ut‐mŏ‐t‘oi‐shī, neĭ haî ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì.
11 K‘aū neĭ m̄‐hó leī‐hoi ngŏh; yan‐waî waân‐naân lām‐shan; mŏ yān pong‐chôh ngŏh.
12 Hó‐toh ngaū‐kung waī‐chuê ngŏh: Pa‐shaan taaî‐līk‐chi‐ngaū‐kung kw‘àn‐chuê ngŏh.
13 K‘uĭ hoi‐haú heùng ngŏh, hó‐ts‘z̆ sz‐tsź wá‐māt, taaî‐sheng kiù‐haàm.
14 Ngŏh shan uĕn‐yeūk, hó‐ts‘z̆ shuí‐laū: ngŏh‐chi‐kwat‐t‘aū, to leī‐hoi tsitₒ; ngŏh sam ts‘z̆ laāp, tsoî t‘ŏ‐chung yūng‐fà.
15 Ngŏh‐chi‐tsing‐līk kon‐foo, hó‐ts‘z̆ ngă‐p‘ìn; ngŏh‐chi‐leî‐t‘aū, tíng‐sheŭng sheûng‐ngōk; neĭ pit chai ngŏh tsoî sź‐teî‐chi‐naī‐chung.
16 Okₒ‐yān tóng‐uĕ: hó‐ts‘z̆ okₒ‐kaú waī‐chuê ngŏh laī kung‐kik: kat‐sheung ngŏh shaú‐tsuk.
17 Ngŏh kwat lô‐ch‘ut, hóh‐ĭ shó‐kwòh: k‘uĭ‐teî tîng‐ngaăn laī‐t‘aí ngŏh.
18 Taaî‐chùng fan‐hoi ngŏh ngoî‐i, chap‐ch‘aū laī‐ts‘uí ngŏh luĭ‐i.
19 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, maĭ leī‐hoi ngŏh: ngŏh í‐ch‘ĭ neĭ tsô līk, k‘aū faaì‐laī pong‐chôh ngŏh.
20 Kaù ngŏh shang‐mîng, t‘uetₒ‐leī to‐kìm; kaù ngŏh koo‐shan, m̄‐peí okₒ‐kaú shóh‐sheung.
21 Kaù ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐ch‘ut sz‐tsź‐chi‐haú: yĕ‐ngaū peí‐kokₒ sheung ngŏh, k‘aū neĭ t‘eng‐kìn.
22 Ngŏh tsoî hing‐taî‐chung, shî‐pit ch‘uēn‐yeūng neĭ mēng: tsoî kaaù‐ooî‐chung, pit tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ.
23 Faān neĭ‐teî kìng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ché, tong tsaàn‐meĭ k‘uĭ; neĭ‐teî Ngă‐kokₒ‐chi‐tsź‐suen, tong kwai‐wīng k‘uĭ; neĭ‐teî Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsź‐suen, tong kìng‐waì k‘uĭ.
24 Chué m̄‐miŭ‐shî, m̄‐ìm‐heì kw‘àn‐foó‐chung yān‐chi‐foó‐naân; yīk m̄‐ím‐chuê‐mîn tuì k‘uĭ; k‘uĭ shóh‐k‘eī‐k‘aū, Chué pit shuī‐t‘èng.
25 Tsoî taaî‐ooî‐chung, ngŏh tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ: tsoî kìng‐waì‐neĭ‐ché‐chi‐ts‘īn, pit waān ngŏh uên.
26 Him‐pei‐chi‐yān, pit tak‐shīk‐paaú: k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ché, pit tsaàn‐meĭ k‘uĭ: sam‐chung wĭng uĕn faaì‐oōt.
27 Teî‐kīk‐chi‐yān, sam to keì‐nîm kwai Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: maân‐kwokₒ‐chi‐ka, to foó‐fūk paaì neĭ.
28 Yan‐waî kwokₒ‐k‘uēn haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsúng‐koón: k‘uĭ haî maân‐mān‐chi‐Chué‐Tsoí.
29 Shaì‐sheûng foò‐haû‐chi‐yān, shīk‐paaú ī paaì: faān kwai‐t‘ó‐chi‐yān, kwaî‐tsoî Chué‐ts‘īn: k‘ūng‐foó iù‐sź‐ché yīk‐haî.
30 Haû‐yuî pit fūk‐sẑ k‘uĭ; ī‐ch‘é iûn‐k‘āp Chué chi‐sẑ, pit ch‘uēn‐kwòh haû‐toî.
31 Haû‐yuî loī pit k‘aaĭ Chué‐chi‐kung‐î, ch‘uēn‐kwòh tseung ch‘ut‐shaì‐chi‐mān t‘eng, tsik‐haî Chué tsô‐shīng‐chi‐sẑ.
