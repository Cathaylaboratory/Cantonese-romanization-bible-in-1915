1 À‐HAP‐TS‘UĪ‐LŎ wōng ch‘ut mîng‐lîng, iù chuê hŏn‐teî, kûng hoí‐tó‐kè tsź‐mān naāp‐kùng.
2 K‘uĭ shóh‐yaŭ wai‐shaì k‘uēn‐nāng, tsokₒ‐waī kûng wōng ko‐shing Mūk‐taí‐koí, shaí k‘uĭ tak tsuen‐wīng‐kè sẑ, to heí‐m̄‐haî sé‐lōk Pa‐sai kûng Maĭ‐t‘aaì līt‐wōng‐kè keí‐leūk me?
3 Yan Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān Mūk‐taí‐koí, haí À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng‐ts‘īn tsô tsoí‐seùng, tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān chi‐chung shīng‐waī‐taaî, t‘ūng‐tsūk‐kè chùng‐hing‐taî to foon‐heí, k‘uĭ yaû sheūng k‘aū tsź‐mān‐kè yik, yaû on‐waì k‘uĭ tsung‐tsūk‐kè yān.
