1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ à, ngŏh í‐laaî neĭ; uên wĭng‐uĕn pat‐chì sau‐kw‘aì: uên i neĭ‐chi‐kung‐î, laī ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh.
2 Chak ĭ t‘eng ngŏh; kap‐ts‘uk‐kaù ngŏh: tsô pó‐oô‐ngŏh‐chi‐p‘oōn‐shēk, tsô peì‐yaû‐ngŏh‐chi‐pó‐cheùng.
3 Neĭ uēn‐poón haî ngŏh‐chi‐p‘oōn‐shēk, ngŏh‐chi‐pó‐cheùng; k‘aū yan‐neĭ‐chi‐mēng, tseung ngŏh yăn‐tô chí‐tím.
4 Neĭ haî ngŏh shóh‐í‐k‘aaù‐chi‐līk: pit kaù ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐ch‘ut ch‘aū‐tīk shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐chi‐mŏng.
5 Chan‐shāt‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, neĭ yă‐ts‘āng shūk ngŏh: ngŏh tseung līng‐wān t‘okₒ‐tsoî neĭ shaú.
6 Sùn‐fûng hui‐ká‐chi‐māt‐ché, ngŏh shóh‐tsang‐oò: waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ngŏh shóh‐í‐laaî.
7 Yan neĭ yă‐ts‘āng kaaí‐lat ngŏh waân‐naân: yīk sham‐chi ngŏh sam‐chung foó‐ch‘óh; ngŏh kìn neĭ kòm līn‐măn, shóh‐ĭ foon‐heí faaì‐lōk;
8 Lîng ngŏh m̄‐shaû tīk‐yān‐chi‐shaú shóh‐kw‘àn: lîng ngŏh keukₒ k‘eĭ‐tsoî kwóng‐footₒ teî‐fong.
9 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung ngŏh līn‐sut, yan ngŏh uê waân‐naân: shâm‐yau‐shaū, ngaăn‐mūk to nap, sam‐ch‘eūng yīk m̄‐on‐lōk.
10 Ngŏh‐chi‐shang‐mîng, yan yau‐shaū shóh‐ĭ shui‐yeūk: ngŏh‐chi nīn‐suì, yīk yan sheung‐t‘aàn shóh‐ĭ shui‐ts‘aān, yan ngŏh tsuî‐okₒ, heì‐līk tsîm‐yeūk, ngŏh‐chi‐kan‐kwat yaû shui‐waaî.
11 Ngŏh yan ch‘aū‐tīk, tsaû shaû līng‐yūk, ngŏh‐chi‐lūn‐shè, kàng‐haî kóm toî ngŏh: shik‐ngŏh‐ché kìn ngŏh tsaû p‘à, tsoî kaai‐sheûng uê‐cheūk ngŏh, leī ngŏh uĕn huì.
12 Yān‐chi‐sam‐chung, m̄‐keì‐nîm ngŏh, hó‐ts‘z̆ tòng ngŏh haî keì‐sź‐chi‐yān: yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ keì‐laân‐chi‐heì‐mĭng yat‐yeûng.
13 Yan‐waî mān‐tak hó‐toh‐yān waí‐p‘òng ngŏh: ngŏh‐chi‐sz̀‐waī, to yaŭ king‐fong: k‘uĭ‐teî maū‐tōk, seúng‐hoî‐ngŏh shang‐mîng.
14 Waī‐haî ngŏh í‐laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ: ngŏh wâ, Neĭ haî ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì.
15 Ngŏh mîng‐chi‐ch‘eūng‐tuén, tsoî neĭ cheúng‐chung: uên neĭ tseung ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐ch‘ut ch‘aū‐tīk‐chi‐shaú, uĕ‐k‘āp kw‘àn‐chūk‐ngŏh‐ché.
16 Táng neĭ‐chi‐mîn, kwong‐chiù neĭ pūk: yan neĭ‐chi‐līn‐măn, laī ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh.
17 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, m̄‐hó lîng ngŏh yaŭ sau‐ch‘aú; yan ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng foo‐kiù neĭ: uên neĭ lîng okₒ‐yān yaŭ sau‐ch‘aú, tsoî yam‐foó‐ch‘uè mŏ sheng ch‘ut.
18 Kiu‐ngô kw‘ōng‐mŏng‐chi‐yān, hò‐yûng taaî‐wâ laī‐waí‐p‘òng miŭ‐shî shîn‐yān; k‘aū neĭ kàm‐chaì k‘uĭ haú.
19 Kìng‐waì‐neĭ‐ché, neĭ waî k‘uĭ uê‐peî taaî‐yan; yīk shi‐yan ue faān tsoî‐shaì‐yān‐chi‐ts‘īn, í‐laaî‐neĭ‐ché!
20 Neĭ pit ts‘ōng‐maaī k‘uĭ tsoî neĭ‐chi‐māt‐shat, mĭn yān‐teî kaì‐suèn k‘uĭ: yaû ts‘ōng‐maaī k‘uĭ tsoî neĭ‐chi‐cheùng‐mōk, mĭn chùng‐haú kei‐t‘aān k‘uĭ.
21 Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaû yān tsûng‐chuk: yan neĭ yă‐ts‘āng shi‐fei‐sheūng‐chi‐yan kwòh‐ngŏh, lîng ngŏh hó‐ts‘z̆ tsoî kin‐koò‐shēng‐chung.
22 Ngŏh shaû king‐fong‐chi shī, ĭ‐waī neĭ ngaăn m̄‐t‘aí ngŏh: taân ngŏh foo‐kiù neĭ, neĭ to t‘eng ngŏh k‘eī‐t‘ó‐chi‐sheng.
23 Kìng‐k‘īn‐ché à, neĭ‐teî tong iù kìng‐oì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pó‐oô shīng‐sùn‐chi‐yān, īm pò kiu‐ngô‐chi‐yān.
24 Taân‐faān yeŭng‐mông Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ché, tong iù fòng‐hoi taám‐leûng, k‘uĭ tsaû kin‐koò neĭ sam.
