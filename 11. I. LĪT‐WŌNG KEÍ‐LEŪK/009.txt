1 SHÓH‐LŌH‐MOŌN ĭ‐king kìn‐chuk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, kûng wōng‐kè kung‐shat, k‘āp Shóh‐lōh‐moōn faān‐shóh‐seúng‐tsô‐kè to tsô‐shīng,
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoì hín‐în‐kwòh Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, hó‐ts‘z̆ sik‐yāt tsoî Kei‐p‘ìn hín‐în kóm‐yeûng.
3 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn shóh‐k‘eī‐k‘aū t‘ó‐kò‐kè, ngŏh ĭ‐king t‘eng‐mān: ngŏh ĭ‐king shìng‐pīt neĭ shóh‐kìn‐chuk ni‐kaan tîn‐uk, pit wĭng‐uĕn on‐chì ngŏh mēng tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè; ī‐ch‘é ngŏh ngaăn, ngŏh sam, pit sheūng‐shī tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè à.
4 Chì‐ue neĭ yeūk‐haî shīng‐sam, ī‐ch‘é chìng‐chīk hāng‐tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn, hó‐ts‘z̆ neĭ foô Taaî‐waî shóh‐hāng‐kè yat‐yeûng, tsun‐hāng ngŏh faān‐shóh‐fan‐foò neĭ‐kè, kán‐shaú ngŏh‐kè lūt‐faatₒ, kûng ngŏh‐kè kw‘ai‐t‘iū:
5 Ngŏh tsaû tseung kin‐koò laāp neĭ‐kè kwokₒ‐waî, tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn, i‐chuê ngŏh shóh‐ying‐shīng neĭ foô Taaî‐waî wâ, Neĭ pit‐m̄‐shiú‐k‘uetₒ‐yān ts‘ŏh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè kwokₒ‐waî à.
6 Taân‐haî neĭ‐teî, waāk neĭ tsź‐suen, t‘óng‐yeūk chuèn‐huì m̄‐i‐ts‘ūng ngŏh, m̄‐kán‐shaú ngŏh shóh chì‐tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn‐kè kaaì‐mîng, kûng lūt‐faatₒ, yaû huì fūk‐sẑ shūng‐paaì pīt‐kòh shān:
7 Ngŏh tsaû pit tsuēt Ĭ‐shik‐līt, chūk‐ch‘ut ngŏh shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐kè teî; ī‐ch‘é ngŏh waî ngŏh mēng shóh‐shìng‐pīt‐kè ni‐kaan tîn‐uk, tsoî ngŏh ngaăn‐ts‘īn pit tiu‐heì; shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsoî chùng‐mān chung, tseung‐loī tsô tsūk‐uĕ wâ‐pìng:
8 Ts‘ź‐tîn‐uk sui‐īn haî kòm‐ko, tseung‐loī shóh‐king‐kwòh‐kè yān, king‐k‘eī faatₒ‐ch‘i‐sheng; yaû wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waî‐mat heùng ts‘ź‐teî, ts‘ź‐tîn‐uk hāng kóm‐yeûng ni?
9 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû pit taapₒ wâ, Yan‐waî ni‐ti tsź‐mān tiu‐heì k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsik‐haî taaì k‘uĭ‐teî līt‐tsó ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp‐teî kóh‐waî, tsaû huì ts‘an‐kân pīt‐shān, shūng‐paaì fūk‐sẑ k‘uĭ: koò‐ts‘ź Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung ni‐ti kokₒ‐yeûng tsoi‐wôh, kòng‐lām ue k‘uĭ‐teî à.
10 ¶ Shīng‐tsaû haî kóm, moŏn‐hiu î‐shāp nīn, tsik‐haî Shóh‐lōh‐moōn kìn‐chuk leŭng‐kaan tîn‐uk, haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, k‘āp wōng‐kè kung‐shat chi‐shī,
11 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng tsaû tsoî Ka‐leî‐leî‐teî, tseung î‐shāp‐kòh‐shēng, ts‘z̀‐kwòh Hei‐laān, (yan‐waî T‘ui‐lōh wōng Hei‐laān, yă‐ts‘āng chiù‐i Shóh‐lōh‐moōn sam‐shóh‐uên‐kè, sùng‐sheŭng paakₒ‐heung‐shuê k‘āp ts‘ūng‐shuê kûng kam à.)
12 Hei‐laān ts‘ūng T‘ui‐lōh ch‘ut‐laī t‘aí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐kè kokₒ‐shēng; tsaû m̄‐foon‐heí kóh‐ti‐shēng.
13 Yaû tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ngŏh hing‐taî à, neĭ shóh‐ts‘z̀ ngŏh‐kè shēng, haî mat‐shēng ni? K‘uĭ tsaû koí kóh‐ti‐shēng mēng‐kiù Ka‐pat‐teî, chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
14 Hei‐laān k‘aaĭ kam kaú‐maân, tá‐faatₒ yān tseung‐laī sùng‐sheŭng‐huì wōng.
15 ¶ Shóh‐lōh‐moōn chiû kung‐yīk yān‐kè uēn‐koò; haî waî kìn‐chuk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, k‘āp tsẑ‐keí‐kè kung‐shat, kûng Maĭ‐lōh, k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè shēng‐oōn, kûng Hâ‐shokₒ, Maĭ‐kat‐toh, k‘āp Kei‐shik.
16 Yan‐waî Oi‐k‘āp wōng Faatₒ‐lŏ, sik‐yāt sheŭng‐huì ts‘uí Kei‐shik, k‘aaĭ fóh laī‐fān‐shiu, shaatₒ kui shēng‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān, tseung shēng ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ nuĭ Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè ts‘ai, tsô chong‐līm.
17 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn kìn‐chuk Kei‐shik, k‘āp hâ‐Paakₒ‐wōh‐lūn,
18 Pa‐la, yaû tsoî ts‘ź‐teî kw‘òng‐yĕ‐kè Taāt‐mōk,
19 K‘āp Shóh‐lōh‐moōn shóh‐yaŭ foó‐foò‐kè shēng, kûng chué‐tûn ch‘e‐kè shēng, k‘āp mă‐ping‐kè shēng, kûng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn waî tsẑ‐keí faaì‐lōk shóh‐seúng kìn‐chuk‐kè, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, tsoî Leî‐pa‐nuên, k‘āp tsoî k‘uĭ shóh‐ch‘ī‐leĭ‐kè ts‘uēn‐teî.
20 Chì‐ue shóh‐yaŭ uē‐shîng‐kè À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān, Haak‐yān, Peí‐leĭ‐saí‐yān, Hei‐meî‐yān, Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐yān, uēn‐poón m̄‐shūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān,
21 Tsik‐haî tsoî ts‘ź‐teî laū‐ts‘uēn k‘uĭ‐kè haû‐yuî, yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān pat‐nāng tsûn‐mīt‐kè, Shóh‐lōh‐moōn shaí k‘uĭ‐teî tsô nō‐pūk, chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
22 Waī‐haî shūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, Shóh‐lōh‐moōn m̄‐shaí k‘uĭ‐teî tsô nō‐pūk: waī‐haî shaí k‘uĭ‐teî tsô Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè chìn‐sẑ, shān‐pūk, kwan‐cheúng, chùng‐tseùng, tuk‐leĭ ch‘e kûng mă‐ping.
23 Ts‘ź haî tuk‐leí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn kung‐tseúng‐kè t‘aū‐mūk, kúng‐kaì ng‐paakₒ‐ng‐shāp yān, tsik‐haî koón‐leí kung‐tsokₒ‐kè tsź‐mān.
24 Waī‐haî Faatₒ‐lŏ‐kè nuĭ ts‘ūng Taaî‐waí shēng sheŭng‐tò k‘uĭ‐kè kung‐shat, tsik‐haí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn waí k‘uí kin‐chuk‐kè: ue‐shí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn yaú kin‐chuk Maĭ‐lōh.
25 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn mooĭ‐nīn saam ts‘z̀, tsoí k‘uĭ waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn‐chuk‐kè t‘aān‐sheúng, hìn faān‐tsaì, ch‘aū‐yan‐tsaì, yaú fān‐heung tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mín‐ts‘īn‐kè t‘aān‐sheúng. Kóm‐tsaú k‘uĭ tsô shīng ni‐kaan tín‐uk.
26 ¶ Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tsoî Ĭ‐tung‐teî, Hūng‐hoí‐kè ngôn, kân Ĭ‐lūk‐kè Ĭ‐ts‘ūn‐ka‐pīt, chong yat‐pong‐shuēn.
27 Hei‐laān tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ pūk, shūk‐shik hoí‐kè chau‐tsź, t‘ūng‐maaī Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè pūk tsoî chùng‐shuēn,
28 k‘uĭ‐teî huì‐tò Oh‐fei, yaū Oh‐fei taaì kam lūk‐shāp‐saam‐maân leŭng, taaì‐faan‐laī Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng à.
