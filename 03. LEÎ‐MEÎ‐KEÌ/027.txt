1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Neĭ iù tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kóng wâ, Yeūk yaŭ yûng yān laī huí‐uên‐kè, tsaû iù koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè shan‐kà, tseung kà‐ngān kwai‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
3 Koó‐kà chi‐faatₒ, ts‘ūng î‐shāp‐suì chì lūk‐shāp suì, yeūk haî naām, tsaû iù koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè kà‐ngān nğ‐shāp shī‐haakₒ, haî chiù shìng‐shóh‐kè shī‐haakₒ.
4 Yeūk‐haî nuĭ, tsaû iù koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè kà‐ngān saam‐shāp shī‐haakₒ.
5 Ts‘ūng nğ‐suì chì î‐shāp‐suì, yeūk‐haî naām, tsaû koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè kà‐ngān î‐shāp shī‐haakₒ, yeūk‐haî nuĭ, tsaû koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè kà‐ngān shāp shī‐haakₒ.
6 Ts‘ūng moŏn‐uēt chì nğ‐suì, yeūk‐haî naām, tsaû koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè kà‐ngān nğ shī‐haakₒ, yeūk‐haî nuĭ, tsaû koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè kà‐ngān saam shī‐haakₒ.
7 Lūk‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng; yeūk‐haî naām, tsaû koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè kà‐ngān shāp‐nğ shī‐haakₒ, yeūk‐haî nuĭ, tsaû koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè kà‐ngān shāp shī‐haakₒ.
8 Yeūk‐haî kòh‐yān p‘ān‐k‘ūng, pat‐nāng i shóh‐koó‐tîng‐kè kà‐ngān tsaû iù taaì‐tò tsaì‐sz mîn‐ts‘īn; tsaì‐sz chiù huí‐uên yān‐kè līk‐leûng, koó‐tîng k‘uĭ‐kè shan‐kà.
9 ¶ Faān shóh‐huí‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè, yeūk‐haî yûng hóh‐hìn‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, tsaû iù pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng.
10 Pat‐hóh tseung k‘uĭ kang‐oôn, yīk pat‐hóh tseung hó‐kè oôn ch‘aú‐kè, ch‘aú‐kè oôn hó‐kè: yeūk‐haî ch‘uk‐shang oôn ch‘uk‐shang, tsaû shóh‐huí‐kè kûng shóh‐oôn‐kè, to iù pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng.
11 Yeūk shóh‐huí, yûng m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, tsik‐haî yān m̄‐k‘aaĭ‐laī hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè, tsaû iù laai ch‘uk‐shang tò tsaì‐sz mîn‐ts‘īn:
12 Tsaì‐sz iù t‘aí‐kwòh, waāk hó waāk waaî: tsaû koó k‘uĭ‐kè kà, tsaû chiù tsaì‐sz shóh‐koó‐kè waī‐tîng.
13 Yeūk‐haî seúng shūk‐ch‘ut, tsaû tong chiù neĭ shóh‐koó‐tîng‐kè kà chi‐ngoî, ka‐toh nğ‐fân‐yat.
14 ¶ Yeūk yaŭ yān tseung tsẑ‐keí‐kè uk, pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaì‐sz iù t‘aí‐kwòh, waāk‐hó waāk‐ch‘aú: tsaû koó‐tîng uk‐kà, tsaû chiù tsaì‐sz shóh‐koó‐kè kà waī‐tîng.
15 Yeūk hìn‐uk‐kè yān, seúng tseung uk shūk‐ch‘ut, iù chiù‐neĭ‐shóh‐koó‐tîng‐kè kà chi‐ngoî, ka‐toh nğ‐fân‐yat, kóm‐tsaû kòh‐kaan‐uk, yīng‐īn kwai tsẑ‐keí.
16 ¶ Yeūk yaŭ yān tseung tsẑ‐keí‐kè t‘īn, pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaû iù chiù‐i shóh‐saatₒ‐kè chúng toh‐shiú, laī koó teî‐kà; yeūk saatₒ‐tak taaî‐māk‐chúng yat‐hōh‐maū‐kè, tsaû koó‐tîng kà‐ngān nğ‐shāp shī‐haakₒ.
17 K‘uĭ yeūk ts‘ūng hei‐nīn heí‐shaú, tseung t‘īn pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng, tsaû iù chiù neĭ shóh‐koó‐kè kà waī‐tîng.
18 Yeūk tsoî hei‐nīn‐chi‐haû, tseung t‘īn pīt‐hoi waī shìng, tsaì‐sz tsaû iù chiù‐uē‐shîng‐kè nīn‐shò, suèn‐tò hei‐nīn, īn‐haû kaì ngān toh‐shiú, hóh‐ĭ chiù nīn‐shò kaám‐shiú neĭ shóh‐koó‐kè kà.
19 Yeūk pīt‐hoi t‘īn waī‐shìng‐kè, seúng tseung t‘īn shūk‐ch‘ut, iù chiù‐neĭ‐shóh‐koó‐tîng‐kè kà chi‐ngoî, ka‐toh‐nğ‐fân‐yat, kóm‐tsaû t‘īn‐teî yīng‐īn kwai k‘uĭ.
20 Yeūk m̄‐tseung t‘īn shūk‐ch‘ut, waāk tsaì‐sz maaî‐kwòh pīt‐yān, haû‐loī tsaû pat‐nāng shūk.
21 Tò hei‐nīn chi‐shī, t‘īn‐teî ĭ‐king kaau‐ch‘ut, tsaû suèn haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shìng‐t‘īn, hó‐ts‘z̆ wĭng‐uĕn pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng‐kè; yaû pit kwai‐ue tsaì‐sz tsô ch‘aán‐īp.
22 Yeūk hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè t‘īn, m̄‐haî tsẑ‐keí‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, haî yûng ngān maaĭ‐kè;
23 Tsaì‐sz iù toî k‘uĭ suèn‐tò hei‐nīn: kaì t‘īn‐kà‐kè shò‐mūk, kòh‐yāt, k‘uĭ pit tseung neĭ shóh‐koó‐kè kà kaau‐ch‘ut, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô shìng‐māt.
24 Táng‐tò hei‐nīn, t‘īn‐teî yīng‐īn kwai‐maaî‐chué, tsik‐haî ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn‐kè īp‐chué.
25 Faān neĭ shóh‐koó‐kè kà, to iù chiù‐i shìng‐shóh‐kè shī‐haakₒ: tsik‐haî î‐shāp kwaì‐la waī yat shī‐haakₒ.
26 ¶ Waī‐haî faān ch‘oh‐t‘oi‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, waāk ngaū, waāk yeūng; poón‐loī haî shūk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè: koò‐ts‘ź mŏ yān hóh‐ĭ tseung kòh‐ti k‘aaĭ‐laī pīt‐hoi waī shìng.
27 Yeūk‐haî m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, k‘uĭ iù chiù neĭ shóh‐koó‐kè kà ka‐toh nğ‐fân‐yat laī shūk‐ch‘ut: t‘óng waāk m̄‐shūk, tsaû iù chiù neĭ shóh‐koó‐kè kà laī maaî‐hiu.
28 Wĭng‐uĕn pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng‐kè māt, yān shóh‐hìn‐kwòh‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè, waāk yān, waāk ch‘uk‐shang, waāk ch‘aán‐īp, to pat‐hóh maaî, yīk pat‐hóh shūk‐faan: faān wĭng‐uĕn pīt‐hoi‐kè māt, haî‐kwai‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô chì‐shìng‐kè.
29 Faān yān wĭng‐uĕn pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng‐kè, to pat‐hóh shūk‐faan; pit iù tseung k‘uĭ laī shaatₒ‐sź.
30 ¶ Faān teî‐sheûng shang‐māt, waāk t‘īn‐kaan‐kè leūng‐shīk, waāk shuê‐sheûng‐kè kwóh‐tsź, k‘ui iù tseung shāp‐fân‐yat kwai‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: haî hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waī‐shìng‐māt.
31 Yān yeūk seúng shūk shóh‐naāp‐kè shāp‐fân‐yat, iù lîng‐ngoî ka‐toh nğ‐fân‐yat.
32 Kóng‐k‘āp ngaū‐kw‘ān, yeūng‐kw‘ān‐kè shāp‐fân‐yat, faān king‐kwòh kwaaí‐cheûng chi‐hâ‐kè, iù tseung shāp‐fân‐yat hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô shìng‐māt.
33 Pat‐hóh shám‐tōk hó waaî, yīk pat‐hóh kang‐oôn: yeūk‐haî kang‐oôn, kòh‐leŭng‐chekₒ to suèn‐waī shìng‐māt; to m̄‐shūk‐tak.
34 ¶ Ni‐ti haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî Sai‐naaĭ‐shaan, fan‐foò Moh‐sai kóng‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐chi‐kè kaaì‐mîng.
