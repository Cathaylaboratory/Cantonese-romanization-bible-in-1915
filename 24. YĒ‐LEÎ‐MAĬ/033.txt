1 FÒNG‐CH‘É Yē‐leî‐maĭ yīng‐īn kw‘àn‐chuê tsoî‐oô‐waî‐ping‐kè uên‐noî, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, taî‐î‐ts‘z̀ kòng‐lām‐ue k‘uĭ, wâ,
2 Shīng‐tsaû ts‘ź‐sẑ kòh‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; tsokₒ‐ch‘ut yaû shīng‐laāp ts‘ź‐sẑ kòh‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘uĭ‐mēng ch‘ing‐waī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè; kóm wâ,
3 Iù‐heùng‐ngŏh pán‐k‘aū, ngŏh tsaû taapₒ‐yìng neĭ, yaû k‘aaĭ taaî‐sẑ, yīk naān‐tsô‐kè sẑ, tsik‐haî neĭ m̄‐ts‘āng chi‐tò‐kè sẑ, hín‐mīng kwòh‐neĭ.
4 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, lûn‐k‘āp ts‘ź‐shēng‐kè uk‐shè, yaû lûn‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì līt‐wōng‐kè uk‐shè, tsik‐haî waí‐ch‘aakₒ‐laân‐hiu‐kè, tseung‐laī‐che‐tóng tīk‐yān‐kè chìn‐t‘oī kûng to‐kìm‐kè, kóm wâ;
5 K‘uĭ‐teî laī‐seúng kûng Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān chaang‐chìn, k‘eī‐shāt laī‐k‘aaĭ sź‐yān‐kè shi‐hoī ch‘ung‐moŏn uk‐shè, tsik‐haî ngŏh faatₒ‐nau‐nô kûng făn‐hân kòh‐shī shóh‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, yan k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐hāng kòm‐toh‐okₒ‐sẑ, ngŏh to heùng‐ni‐kòh shēng che‐paì‐mîn.
6 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng k‘uĭ chòng‐kîn, yaû ts‘uēn‐uê, ngŏh pit‐ooĭ‐i‐hó k‘uĭ‐teî, yaû tseung p‘īng‐on kûng chan‐shāt‐kè foò‐haû, hín‐shî kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî.
7 Ngŏh to ooĭ‐lîng Yaū‐t‘aaì peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān, kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān faan‐laī, yaû kìn‐laāp k‘uĭ‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆ ch‘oh‐shī yat‐yeûng.
8 Ngŏh to‐lîng k‘uĭ‐teî ch‘uē‐ts‘ing‐saaì kokₒ‐yeûng tsuî‐hin, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ shóh‐tsô laī‐tak‐tsuî‐ngŏh‐kè; yaû shè‐mĭn‐saaì k‘uĭ kokₒ‐yeûng tsuî‐hin, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ shóh‐tsô‐laī tak‐tsuî‐ngŏh‐kè, t‘ūng‐maaī shóh‐tsô heùng‐ngŏh‐faân‐tsuî‐kè.
9 Ni‐kòh‐shēng pit‐heùng‐ngŏh shīng‐waī yaŭ‐faaì‐lōk‐kè mēng, yaû shīng‐waī tsaàn‐meĭ, kûng wīng‐kwong, tsoî teî‐sheûng maân‐kwokₒ mîn‐ts‘īn, tsik‐haî t‘eng‐mān ngŏh heùng‐k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐hāng shîn‐yik‐kè sẑ: yaû yan ngŏh lîng k‘uĭ‐teî tak‐tó shîn‐yik, kûng p‘īng‐on, tsaû king‐fong chìn‐lūt.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Tsoî ni‐ch‘uè‐teî‐fong, tsik‐haî neĭ kóng‐k‘āp wâ, haî fong‐faì‐kè, līn yān kûng ch‘uk‐shang to‐mŏ, yaû tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì chùng‐yap, k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kaai‐hông, tsik‐haî shīng‐waī fong‐faì, līn yān k‘āp kui‐mān kûng ch‘uk‐shang to‐mŏ‐kè,
11 Pit‐tsoì yaŭ‐foon‐heí faai‐lōk‐kè sheng, san‐lōng‐kè sheng, san‐foŏ‐kè sheng, yaû wâ, Iù‐kóm‐tsê maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî Sheûng‐Taì; yan k‘uĭ king‐sut ts‘uēn‐tò‐wĭng‐uĕn‐kè: kòh‐táng‐yān‐kè sheng, yīk yaŭ taaì‐tsê‐yan‐kè tsaì‐māt, yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, kòh‐táng‐yān‐kè sheng. Yan‐waî ngŏh pit‐lîng kíng‐teî, peí‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān faan‐laī, hó‐ts‘z̆ ch‘oh‐shī kóm, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
12 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Tsoî ts‘ź‐teî tsik‐haî pìn‐waī fong‐faì, līn‐yān kûng ch‘uk‐shang to‐mŏ‐kè, yaû tsoî k‘eī‐chung, shóh‐yaŭ‐kè shēng‐yap, tseung‐loī pit‐tsoì‐yaŭ mūk‐yān chuê‐shāt, lîng k‘uĭ yeūng‐kw‘ān fàn‐tó‐ch‘uè.
13 Tsoî shaan‐teî‐kè shēng‐yap, tsoî p‘īng‐uēn‐kè shēng‐yap, yaû tsoî naām‐fong‐kè shēng‐yap, kûng Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐chi‐teî, yīk‐tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsóh‐yaû‐kè teî‐fong, k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè shēng‐yap, pit‐tsoì‐yaŭ‐yeūng‐kw‘ān, king‐kwòh tím‐shó k‘uĭ‐kè yān‐chi‐shaú‐hâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ à.
14 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Yāt‐tsź tseung‐tò, ngŏh pit‐shīng‐tsaû ngŏh shóh‐lûn‐k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt ka, yaû lûn‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì ka, shóh‐kóng shîn‐yik‐kè īn‐uĕ.
15 Tsoî kòh‐ti‐yāt‐tsź, yaû tong‐kòh‐shī, ngŏh tsaû lîng kung‐î‐kè Miū‐Yuî, shūk Taaî‐waî shang‐heí‐laī; k‘uĭ pit‐tsoî‐kíng‐noî, hāng‐shám‐p‘oòn k‘āp kung‐î.
16 Tong kòh‐ti yāt‐tsź, Yaū‐t‘aaì pit‐tak‐kaù, Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng to on‐īn kui‐chuê; ī‐ch‘é shóh‐koí‐kè mēng laī‐ch‘ing‐foo k‘uĭ‐kè, haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, haî ngŏh‐teî‐kè kung‐î à.
17 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Taaî‐waî pit‐m̄‐shiú‐k‘uetₒ yat‐kòh yān ts‘ŏh Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng waî‐sheûng;
18 Ī‐ch‘é tsaì‐sz‐ka, tsik‐haî Leî‐meî‐yān, m̄‐hìm‐k‘uetₒ yat‐kòh‐yān tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn hìn‐faān‐tsaì, yaû shiu‐sò‐tsaì, yaû sheūng‐shī hìn‐kokₒ‐yeûng tsaì‐māt.
19 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ, wâ,
20 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Neĭ uē‐kwóh yaŭ‐nāng faì‐waí ngŏh‐kûng‐yāt shóh‐laāp‐kè yeukₒ, k‘āp ngŏh‐kûng‐yê shóh‐laāp‐kè yeukₒ, táng‐tò mŏ‐yāt‐yê i‐chuê hōp‐shī‐haû lūn‐laū;
21 Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh kûng Taaî‐waî shóh‐laāp‐kè yeukₒ, to‐ooĭ‐faì‐waaî, chì‐tò k‘uĭ m̄‐tak‐yaŭ‐tsaí ts‘ŏh kòh‐waî‐sheûng tsô wōng; chì‐ue tsaì‐sz, tsik Leî‐meî‐yān, waî ngŏh fūk‐yīk‐ché, shóh‐laāp‐kè to haî.
22 Hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘in‐sheûng‐kè kwan, pat‐nāng ts‘ing‐shó, taaî‐hoí‐kè sha, to pat‐hóh‐leūng‐tōk: ngŏh tseung‐loī pit‐chiù‐kóm‐yeûng ka‐toh ngŏh pūk Taaî‐waî‐kè haû‐yuî, uĕ‐k‘āp fûng‐sẑ ngŏh‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān.
23 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ, wâ,
24 Ni‐ti‐tsź‐mān shóh‐kóng‐kè, wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kaán‐suén kóh‐leŭng‐ka yān, tsaû ĭ‐king waī‐heì k‘uĭ‐teî, neĭ heí‐m̄‐ts‘āng seúng‐tò ts‘ź‐īn me? k‘uĭ‐teî kóm‐yeûng miŭ‐shî ngŏh tsź‐mān, táng k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐tsoì shīng‐waī kwokₒ tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn.
25 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Uē‐kwóh ngŏh kûng yāt‐yê shóh‐laāp‐kè yeukₒ, m̄‐tak‐shīng‐laāp, uē‐kwóh ngŏh m̄‐ts‘āng ch‘itₒ‐laāp t‘in‐kûng‐teî‐kè tîng‐laî;
26 Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐waī‐heì Ngă‐kokₒ‐kè haû‐yuî, uĕ‐k‘āp ngŏh pūk Taaî‐waî‐kè haû‐yuî, chì‐tò ngŏh m̄‐tseung k‘uĭ haû‐yuî tsô‐koon‐cheúng, tsoî À‐paakₒ‐la‐hón, Ĭ‐saatₒ, Ngă‐kokₒ, haû‐yuî‐chi‐sheûng là: yan‐waî ngŏh tseung‐loī pit‐lîng k‘uĭ peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān faan‐laī, yaû shi‐king‐sut‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî à.
