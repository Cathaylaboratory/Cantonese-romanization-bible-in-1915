1 TONG Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam chi‐shī, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, sheŭng‐laī Yaū‐taaî, Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam fūk‐sẑ k‘uĭ saam nīn: haû‐loī faán‐chuèn pooì‐yīk k‘uĭ.
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaí Ka‐lāk‐taí yān‐kè kwan, À‐laān‐yān‐kè kwan, Moh‐aapₒ‐yān‐kè kwan, À‐moōn‐yān‐kè kwan, laī kung‐tá Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam, tsik‐haî shaí k‘uĭ‐teî tsiú‐mīt Yaū‐taaî, chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ k‘uĭ pūk sin‐chi shóh‐kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ.
3 Ni‐ti‐sẑ shāt‐haî yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mîng, lām‐tò Yaū‐taaî, chì‐ts‘in‐ī k‘uĭ‐teî leī‐hoi k‘uĭ ngaăn‐ts‘īn, haî yan Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè tsuî‐okₒ, chiù‐i k‘uĭ faān‐shóh‐hāng‐kè;
4 Yaû yan k‘uĭ laū mō‐koo‐yān‐kè huetₒ; tseung mō‐koo‐kè huetₒ yīng‐moŏn Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng; ni‐ti tsuî‐okₒ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuèn‐m̄‐shè‐mĭn‐kè à.
5 Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, kûng k‘uĭ faān‐shóh‐hāng‐kè, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
6 Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
7 Oi‐k‘āp wōng m̄‐tsoì ts‘ūng k‘uĭ poón‐kwokₒ ch‘ut‐laī: yan‐waî Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng, yă‐ts‘āng kung ts‘uí faān shūk Oi‐k‘āp wōng‐kè teî, ts‘ūng Oi‐k‘āp‐hōh chì‐tò Paakₒ‐laāt‐hōh.
8 ¶ Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan tang‐waî‐chi‐shī, nīn‐keí shāp‐paatₒ suì, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng saam‐kòh uēt. K‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an mēng‐kiù Neī‐fat‐taaî, haî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐yān, Leî‐nā‐taan‐kè nuĭ.
9 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ, hōk‐faan k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an faān‐shóh‐hāng‐kè.
10 Tong‐shī Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ‐kè kwan‐ping, sheŭng‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng waī‐kw‘àn shēng.
11 K‘uĭ pūk waī‐kw‘àn‐shēng‐chi‐shī, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ laī‐tò.
12 Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan ch‘ut‐shēng tò Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐ch‘uè, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ‐kè lŏ‐mŏ, k‘āp shān‐pūk, mūk‐paakₒ, waân‐koon: Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng tang‐waî taî‐paatₒ nīn, kóh‐shī chukₒ‐chuê k‘uĭ.
13 Tsaû tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk foó‐foò‐kè pó‐māt, k‘āp wōng‐kung‐foó‐foò‐kè pó‐māt, to taaì‐huì, yaû tseung Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn shóh‐tsô Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐chung‐kè kam‐heì‐mĭng, to waí‐līt, chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ.
14 Yaû tseung Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng chùng‐mān, kûng chùng‐mūk‐paakₒ, k‘āp kokₒ‐taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ, pìng yat‐ts‘aì mūk‐tseûng, t‘itₒ‐tseûng, kûng‐kaì yat‐maân‐yān, to lŏ‐leūk‐huì: ch‘uē‐hiu chì‐p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐yān chi‐ngoî mŏ‐yān shîng‐ts‘uēn.
15 Tseung Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan wōng to taaì‐huì, t‘ūng wōng‐mŏ, wōng‐haû, waân‐koon, k‘āp kwokₒ‐chung‐kè yŭng‐sẑ, to ts‘ūng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng lŏ‐leūk tò Pa‐peí‐lūn.
16 Kwan‐ping kûng‐kaì ts‘at‐ts‘in yān, mūk‐tseûng, t‘itₒ‐tseûng, kûng‐kaì yat‐ts‘in yān, k‘āp faān‐nāng‐chìn‐kè yŭng‐sẑ, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng to lŏ‐leūk‐huì Pa‐peí‐lūn.
17 Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng, tsaû laāp Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan‐kè shuk Mă‐taaî‐neī, t‘aì k‘uĭ tsô wōng, koí k‘uĭ mēng‐kiù Sai‐taí‐ka.
18 ¶ Sai‐taí‐ka tang‐waî‐chi‐shī, nīn‐keí î‐shāp‐yat suì, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng shāp‐yat nīn. K‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an mēng‐kiù Hap‐mŏ‐taāt, haî Laāp‐nā‐yān Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐kè nuĭ.
19 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ, hōk‐faan Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan faān‐shóh‐hāng‐kè.
20 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng k‘āp Yaū‐taaî faatₒ‐nô, koò‐ts‘ź kón‐chūk k‘uĭ‐teî leī‐hoi k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn, Sai‐taí‐ka to poôn‐yīk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng à.
