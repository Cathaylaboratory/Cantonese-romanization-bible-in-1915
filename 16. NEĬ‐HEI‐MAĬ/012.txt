1 T‘ŪNG‐MAAĪ Saatₒ‐la‐t‘itₒ‐kè tsaí, Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ, kûng Yē‐shue‐à sheŭng‐huì‐kè tsaì‐sz, k‘āp Leî‐meî‐yān, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû: Sai‐loî‐à, Yē‐leî‐maĭ, Ĭ‐sẑ‐la,
2 À‐mă‐leĭ, Mă‐lūk, Hap‐tāt,
3 Shî‐ka‐neī, Leĭ‐wāng, Maĭ‐leĭ‐moōt,
4 Yīk‐toh, Kam‐neī‐toh, À‐peì‐ngă,
5 Maĭ‐à‐mān, Mă‐taí‐à, Pit‐ka,
6 Shî‐mâ‐ngă, Yeukₒ‐ngă‐laāp, Yē‐t‘aaì‐à.
7 Saatₒ‐lô, À‐mūk, Hei‐lāk‐ka, Yē‐t‘aaì‐à. Ts‘ź kaai tsoî Yē‐shue‐à‐kè yāt‐tsź, tsô tsaì‐sz kûng k‘uĭ‐teî hing‐taî‐kè cheúng.
8 Ī‐ch‘é shūk Leî‐meî‐yān yaŭ Yē‐shue‐à, Pán‐noî, Kaapₒ‐mīt, Shî‐leĭ‐peí, Yaū‐taaî, Mă‐taaî‐neī, Mă‐taaî‐neī k‘āp k‘uĭ hing‐taî, koón‐leĭ kóm‐tsê‐kè sẑ.
9 K‘uĭ‐teî hing‐taî Paatₒ‐pò‐ka, kûng Oo‐neī, kûng k‘uĭ‐teî paan‐līt seung‐tuì.
10 Yē‐shue‐à, shang Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam, Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam shang Ĭ‐leî‐à‐shāt, Ĭ‐leî‐à‐shāt, shang Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî,
11 Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî shang Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan, Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan shang Aapₒ‐t‘ó‐à.
12 Tong Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam‐kè yāt‐tsź, yaŭ tsaì‐sz tsô līt‐tsó‐foô‐kè ka‐cheúng: Sai‐loî‐à tsūk Maĭ‐la‐ngă; Yē‐leî‐maĭ tsūk Hap‐nā‐neī‐à;
13 Ĭ‐sẑ‐la tsūk Maĭ‐shue‐laān; À‐mă‐leĭ tsūk Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān;
14 Maĭ‐leî‐koó tsūk Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan; Shî‐pa‐neī tsūk Yeukₒ‐shat;
15 Hap‐lām tsūk Aapₒ‐nā; Maĭ‐la‐yeukₒ tsūk Hei‐lāk‐koí;
16 Yīk‐toh tsūk Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à; K‘eī‐neī‐tûn tsūk Maĭ‐shue‐laān;
17 À‐peì‐ngă tsūk Shik‐leĭ; Maĭ‐nā‐mān tsūk, Moh‐à‐taí tsūk Pit‐t‘aaì;
18 Pit‐ka tsūk Shiù‐mŏ‐à; Shî‐mâ‐ngă tsūk Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan;
19 Yeukₒ‐ngă‐laāp tsūk Mă‐teî‐naaĭ; Yē‐taaî‐à tsūk Oo‐sai;
20 Saatₒ‐loī tsūk Ka‐loī; À‐mūk tsūk Hei‐paakₒ;
21 Hei‐lāk‐ka tsūk Hap‐sha‐peì; Yē‐taaî‐à tsūk Nā‐taân‐īp;
22 Tong Ĭ‐leî‐à‐shāt, Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî, Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, Aapₒ‐t‘ó‐à‐kè yāt‐tsź, Leî‐meî‐yān tsô līt‐tsó‐foô‐kè ka‐cheúng, tsoì‐lōk ch‘aakₒ‐noî: tsoî Pa‐sai wōng Taaî‐leî‐oo ts‘ŏh‐waî‐chi‐shī, tsaì‐sz yīk tsoì‐lōk ch‘aakₒ‐noî.
23 Leî‐meî‐kè tsź‐suen, tsô līt‐tsó‐kè ka‐cheúng, tsoì‐lōk līk‐toî‐chì‐leūk‐chi‐shue, chì‐tò Ĭ‐leî‐à‐shāt‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān‐kè yāt‐tsź.
24 Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè tsūk‐cheúng Hap‐sha‐peì, Shî‐leĭ‐peí, Kaapₒ‐mīt‐kè tsaí Yē‐shue‐à, kûng k‘uĭ‐teî hing‐taî seung‐tuì, tsik‐haî līt‐paan kûng līt‐paan seung‐tuì, tsaàn‐meĭ chuk‐tsê, tsun‐i Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐yān Taaî‐waî shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè.
25 Mă‐taaî‐neī, Paatₒ‐pò‐ka, Oh‐pa‐taí, Maĭ‐shue‐laān, Taāt‐moōn, À‐kuk, tsô‐hon‐moōn‐kè, tsik‐haî shaú‐moōn‐ts‘īn‐kè foó‐foò.
26 Ts‘ź‐kaai tsoî Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐taāt‐kè suen Yē‐shue‐à‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam, kûng fong‐paakₒ Neī‐hei‐maĭ, k‘āp tsaì‐sz sẑ‐tsź Ĭ‐sẑ‐la‐kè yāt‐tsź.
27 ¶ Tong tāk‐pīt Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng shēng‐oōn chi‐shī, chùng‐yān ts‘ām Leî‐meî‐yān yaū k‘uĭ‐teî faān shóh‐tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè, seúng taaì k‘uĭ‐teî huì‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, chì‐foon‐heí kóm‐tsê, au‐koh, mīng‐poōt, koó‐shat, laī shaú tāk‐pīt‐kè laĭ.
28 Au‐koh‐kè yān, yaū Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng sz̀‐waī‐kè p‘īng‐uēn, k‘āp yaū Neī‐t‘ōh‐faatₒ‐kè ts‘uen‐heung tsuî‐tsaāp;
29 Yaû‐yaū Paakₒ‐kat‐kaapₒ ts‘ūng Kei‐pa, k‘āp Aapₒ‐mă‐fat‐kè t‘īn: yan‐waî au‐koh‐kè yān, haí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè sz̀‐waī, ĭ‐king waî tsẑ‐keí kìn‐laāp ts‘uen‐heung.
30 Tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān, tsaû kitₒ‐tsîng tsẑ‐keí, yīk kitₒ‐tsîng tsź‐mān, kûng shēng‐moōn k‘āp shēng‐oōn.
31 Ue‐shî ngŏh taaì Yaū‐t‘aaì chùng‐kwan‐cheúng sheŭng‐shēng‐oōn, laāp leŭng‐tuî kóm‐tsê‐kè yān, ts‘z̀‐tsuî hāng, taî‐yat tuî tsoî shēng‐oōn‐sheûng yaû‐pîn huì, tsik‐haî heùng Fàn‐moōn hāng:
32 Kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ‐teî laī‐hāng‐kè yaŭ Hōh‐sha‐ngă, kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì kwan‐cheúng‐kè yat‐poòn,
33 Yaû‐yaŭ À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, Ĭ‐sẑ‐la, Maĭ‐shue‐laān,
34 Yaū‐taaî, Pîn‐ngă‐măn, Shî‐mâ‐ngă, Yē‐leî‐maĭ,
35 Yaû‐yaŭ tsaì‐sz‐kè tsaí keí‐kòh‐yān cha‐heúng‐kokₒ; Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan‐kè tsaí Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à, Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan haî Shî‐mâ‐ngă‐kè‐tsaí, Shî‐mâ‐ngă haî Mă‐taaî‐neī‐kè tsaí, Mă‐taaî‐neī haî Maĭ‐koi‐ngă‐kè tsaí, Mă‐koi‐ngă haî Saatₒ‐haak‐kè tsaí, Saatₒ‐haak haî À‐saatₒ‐kè tsaí:
36 K‘āp k‘uĭ hing‐taî Shî‐mâ‐ngă, À‐saatₒ‐leî, Maĭ‐la‐loī, K‘eī‐la‐loī, Mă‐oi, Nā‐taân‐īp, Yaū‐taaî, Hap‐nā‐neī, ning‐chuê Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐yān Taaî‐waî‐kè ngōk‐heì, sẑ‐tsź Ĭ‐sẑ‐la tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘īn‐t‘aū.
37 Ts‘ź chùng tsoî Ts‘uēn‐moōn seung‐tuì, yaū Taaî‐waî shēng‐kè kaai‐k‘ap sheŭng, tsik‐haî sheŭng‐shēng‐oōn‐kòh‐ch‘uè, haí Taaî‐waî kung‐shat‐kè sheûng‐mîn, heùng tung‐pîn huì, chì‐tò Shuí‐moōn.
38 Taî‐î tuî kóm‐tsê‐kè yān, kûng kòh‐yat‐tuî seung‐tuì laī‐hāng, ngŏh t‘ūng tsź‐mān‐kè yat‐poòn haí shēng‐oōn‐sheûng, kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè, tsẑ lō‐kè mông‐laū, chì‐tò shēng‐oōn kwóng‐footₒ‐chi‐ch‘uè;
39 Yaû tsẑ Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐moōn‐chi‐sheûng Kaû‐moōn, Uē‐moōn, Hap‐nā‐īp‐kè mông‐laū, Hap‐maĭ‐à‐kè mông‐laū, tò Yeūng‐moōn tsaû chí‐chuê: k‘eĭ‐tsoî Yūk‐moōn.
40 Kóm‐tsaû kóm‐tsê leŭng‐tuî‐kè yān, k‘eĭ tsoî Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐chung, ngŏh kûng kwan‐cheúng yat‐poòn t‘ūng‐maaī k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè:
41 Tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐kam, Mă‐sai‐ngă, Maĭ‐nā‐mān, Maĭ‐koi‐ngă, Ĭ‐leî‐oh‐naaĭ, Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à, Hap‐nā‐neī‐à, kaai cha heúng‐kokₒ k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè;
42 Yaû‐yaŭ Mă‐sai‐ngă, Shî‐mâ‐à, Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, Oo‐sai, Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, Mă‐k‘eī‐à, Ĭ‐laān, Ĭ‐ch‘aatₒ. Yīk ch‘itₒ‐laāp au‐koh‐kè, kûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐cheúng p‐la‐hei‐ngă, tsaû taaî‐sheng au‐koh.
43 Tong kòh‐yāt, chùng‐yān hìn taaî‐tsaì, ī‐ch‘é heí‐lōk; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lîng k‘uĭ‐teî taaî‐foon‐heí faaì‐lōk: k‘āp k‘uĭ‐teî foŏ‐yān saì‐man‐tsaí yīk heí‐lōk: chì Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè foon‐heí‐chi‐sheng, t‘eng‐mān tò‐hó‐uĕn à.
44 ¶ Tong kòh‐yāt p‘aaì‐waí‐yān koón‐leĭ foò‐fōng, waî chué fòh‐ts‘oī kuí‐heí‐kè laĭ‐māt, ch‘oh‐shīng‐shūk‐kè māt, uĕ‐k‘āp shóh‐ts‘uí shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat‐kè, chì yaū kokₒ‐shēng‐kè t‘īn‐maŭ, chiù‐i lūt‐faatₒ shóh‐tîng shau‐laī‐kè kung‐k‘ap, tsaì‐sz k‘āp Leî‐meî‐yān‐chi‐fân, kaai chué‐tsoî ts‘ź‐foó‐foò: yan‐waî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān yan kûng fûng‐sẑ‐kè tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān tsaû foon‐lōk à.
45 K‘uĭ‐teî tsun‐shaú fûng‐sẑ Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐tong‐shaú‐kè chik, uĕ‐k‘āp shaú k‘uĭ‐teî kitₒ‐tsîng kokₒ‐ch‘uè‐kè chik, au‐koh‐kè, kûng hon‐moōn‐kè, to haî kóm‐tsô, kaai chiù‐i Taaî‐waî k‘āp k‘uĭ tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè.
46 Sik‐yāt tsoî Taaî‐waî kûng À‐saatₒ‐kè yāt‐tsź, yaŭ cheúng‐ché koón‐leĭ au‐koh, yûng shi tsaàn‐meĭ chuk‐tsê Sheûng‐Taì.
47 Tong Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ kûng Neī‐hei‐maĭ‐kè yāt‐tsź, Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, mooĭ‐yāt tseung k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐tong‐tak‐kè fân, kung‐k‘ap au‐koh‐kè, k‘āp hon‐moōn‐kè: yaû pīt‐hoi shìng‐māt, peí Leî‐meî‐yān; Leî‐meî‐yān yaû pīt‐hoi shìng‐māt peí À‐lūn‐kè tsź‐suen.
