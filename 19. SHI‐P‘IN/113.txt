1 NEĬ‐TEÎ tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā. Chué‐chi‐pūk‐yān, tong ch‘ing‐tsûng k‘uĭ, tsaàn‐tsûng k‘uĭ‐chi‐mēng.
2 Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mēng, shaû‐yān‐ch‘ing‐tsûng, tsẑ‐kam‐yāt chì‐tò‐wĭng‐uĕn.
3 Ts‘ūng yāt‐ch‘ut chì‐tò yāt‐yāp‐chi‐ch‘uè, to tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mēng.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ko‐ch‘ut maân‐kwokₒ, k‘uĭ‐chi‐wīng‐kwong ko‐kwòh‐t‘in.
5 Ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, waî‐kui chì‐ko, mat‐shuī ts‘z̆‐tak k‘uĭ ni?
6 T‘in‐sheûng teî‐hâ, k‘uĭ to kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ.
7 Ts‘ūng naī‐ch‘ān‐chi‐chung, kuí‐heí meī‐yeūk‐chi‐yān, yaū fàn‐t‘ó chi‐chung, foō‐ch‘ī p‘ān‐fāt‐chi‐yān;
8 Lîng k‘uĭ kûng mūk‐paakₒ, tsik‐haî suén‐mān‐chi‐mūk‐paakₒ, yat‐laî t‘ūng‐ts‘ŏh.
9 Yaû lîng pat‐nāng shang‐ch‘aán‐chi‐foŏ‐yān, yaŭ ka hóh‐chuê, yan shang toh tsaí, heí‐lōk pat‐shing. Neĭ‐teî tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
