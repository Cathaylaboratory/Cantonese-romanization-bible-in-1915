1 SHÎ‐PA nuĭ‐wōng, t‘eng‐mān Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè mīng‐shing, waí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mēng shóh‐tak‐kè, tsaú laī‐tò seúng yûng yán‐uē shì k‘uí.
2 K‘uí laī‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ‐kè yān hó‐toh, yaû‐yaŭ lokₒ‐t‘ōh pooì‐chuê heung‐liû kúng kam shâm‐toh, uĕ‐k‘āp pó‐shēk: k‘uĭ laī‐tò Shóh‐lōh‐moōn chi‐shī, tsaû tseung faān tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐sam‐luĭ‐kè tuì k‘uĭ wâ.
3 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tseung k‘uĭ‐kè yán‐uĕ, tsún‐teî taapₒ k‘uĭ: meî yaŭ yán‐uĕ chì wōng m̄‐ooĭ‐taapₒ‐tak k‘uĭ kà.
4 Shî‐pa nuĭ‐wōng, keì kìn Shóh‐lōh‐moōn shóh‐yaŭ‐kè chì‐waî, k‘āp k‘uĭ shóh‐kìn‐chuk‐kè tîn‐uk,
5 Paaí‐ch‘itₒ tsīk‐sheûng‐kè ngaaū‐chaân, k‘āp k‘uĭ pūk‐yān fan līt ts‘ŏh waî, k‘uĭ‐ti‐shān‐tsź shî‐laāp, kûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè i‐fūk, k‘āp hìn‐tsaú‐kè yān; ī‐ch‘é sheŭng‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè kaai‐k‘ap, tsaû king‐k‘eī sòng‐heì.
6 K‘uĭ tsaû tuì wōng wâ, Ngŏh tsoî poón‐kwokₒ t‘eng‐mān, wâ, neí shóh‐tsô‐kè sẑ, kûng neĭ chì‐waî, kaai‐haî chan‐shāt à.
7 Taân‐haî ngŏh m̄‐sùn k‘uĭ‐teî wá, k‘āp ngŏh laī‐tò, ts‘an‐ngaăn kin lokₒ: t‘aí‐a, shóh‐kóng‐kwòh‐ngŏh chi‐kè, to meî‐k‘āp‐yaŭ yat‐poòn: neĭ‐kè chì‐waî kûng leî‐taāt, toh‐kwòh ngŏh shóh‐t‘eng‐mān‐kè fung‐sheng à.
8 Fuk‐tsoi, neĭ‐kè‐yān à, fuk‐tsoi, neĭ‐kè pūk, sheūng‐shī shî‐fûng k‘eĭ‐tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn, t‘eng‐mān neĭ‐kè chì‐waî à.
9 Chuk‐tsê neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsik‐haî foon‐heí neĭ‐kè, ch‘itₒ‐laāp neĭ ts‘ŏh‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè kwokₒ‐waî: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wĭng‐uĕn oì Ĭ‐shik‐līt, koò‐ts‘ź laāp neĭ tsô wōng, shaí neĭ shám‐p‘oòn hāng kung‐î.
10 Ue‐shî nuĭ wōng k‘aaĭ kam shāp‐paatₒ‐maân‐leŭng, k‘āp heung‐liû shâm‐toh, kûng pó‐shēk, tseung‐laī sùng‐kwòh‐wōng: Shî‐pa nuĭ‐wōng sùng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng‐kè heung‐liû, m̄‐tsoì‐yaŭ kóm‐yeûng kòm‐toh sùng‐laī lokₒ.
11 Hei‐laān‐kè chùng‐shuēn, tsik‐haî yaū Oh‐fei‐taaì‐kam‐laī‐kè, to ts‘ūng Oh‐fei taaì t‘aān‐mūk, kûng pó‐shēk shâm‐toh faan‐laī à.
12 Wōng tsaû tseung t‘aān‐mūk waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk, k‘āp waî wōng‐kè kung‐shat tsô ch‘uĕ; ī‐ch‘é waî ch‘eùng‐shi‐kè tsô k‘ām, kûng shat: ts‘ūng‐loī meî‐yaŭ kóm‐yeûng‐kè t‘aān‐mūk laī à, chì‐tò kam‐yāt, to meî‐yaŭ kìn‐kwòh.
13 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng i‐chuê wōng‐kè kw‘ai‐laî, tseung laĭ‐māt sùng sheŭng Shî‐pa nuĭ‐wōng, ts‘ź‐ngoî yaû tseung nuĭ‐wōng faān‐shóh‐seúng‐kè, faān‐shóh‐k‘aū‐kè laī‐sùng‐kwòh k‘uĭ. Kóm‐tsaû nuĭ‐wōng kûng k‘uĭ pūk, chuèn‐faan‐huì poón‐kwokₒ à.
14 ¶ Shóh‐lōh‐moōn mooĭ‐nīn shóh‐tak‐kè kam, kûng‐yaŭ kaú‐shāp‐kaú‐maân‐kaú‐ts‘in leŭng,
15 Ts‘ź‐ngoî yaû tak kam, tsik‐haî ts‘ŏh‐haakₒ k‘āp hāng‐haakₒ shang‐ì‐kè, kûng seung‐kaau tsź‐mān‐kè chùng‐wōng, k‘āp ts‘ź teî‐kè kokₒ‐tsúng‐tuk shóh‐taaì‐laī‐kè.
16 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng k‘aaĭ tá‐kwòh‐kè kam, tsô taaî‐tóng‐p‘aaī î‐paakₒ: mooĭ tóng‐p‘aaī yûng kam lūk‐paakₒ shī‐haakₒ,
17 Yaû k‘aaĭ ts‘z̀ kam tsô t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī saam‐paakₒ; mooĭ t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī yûng kam saam meī‐nōh: wōng tsaû chai‐tsoî Leî‐pa‐nuên shuê‐lām‐kè kung‐shat.
18 Kim‐chi wōng k‘aaĭ tseûng‐ngā tsô yat taaî‐tsôh‐waî, yûng tsing‐kam laī p‘o‐chuê.
19 Kóh‐tsôh‐yaŭ lūk k‘ap, tsôh‐sheûng haû‐pîn haî uēn‐kè: leŭng‐p‘ōng yaŭ foō‐shaú, kân foō‐shaú yaŭ leŭng‐chekₒ sz k‘eĭ‐tó‐ch‘uè.
20 Yaû‐yaŭ shāp‐î‐chekₒ sz, k‘eĭ‐haí lūk k‘ap‐sheûng ni‐pîn kóh‐pîn: pīt‐kwokₒ kaai meî‐yaŭ kóm‐yeûng tsô‐kè.
21 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng shóh‐yûng‐kè heì‐mĭng, k‘ui k‘aaĭ kam laī‐tsô‐kè, Leî‐pa‐nuên shuê‐lām kung‐shat luĭ‐pîn kokₒ‐yeûng heì‐mĭng, kaai‐haî tsing‐kam; mŏ‐ti ngān‐tsô‐kè: tong Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè yāt‐tsź, yān pat‐ĭ ngān haî kwaì‐chûng à.
22 Yan‐waî wōng yaŭ Taaî‐shat shuēn yat pong, kûng Hei‐laān shuēn yat pong, t‘ūng‐maaī haí hoí hāng: ni‐ti Taaî‐shat shuēn, tsoì kam‐ngān, tseûng‐ngā, uēn‐haū, húng‐tseukₒ, mooĭ saam nīn yat‐ts‘z̀ faan‐laī.
23 Kóm‐tsaû Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè fòh‐ts‘oī kûng chì‐waî, shìng kwòh t‘in‐hâ‐kè līt‐wōng.
24 P‘ó‐t‘in‐hâ‐kè mān, k‘aū kìn Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè mîn, seúng t‘eng Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐sam‐kè chì‐waî.
25 Kokₒ‐yān yaŭ laĭ‐māt taaì‐laī, tsik‐haì kam‐heì, ngān‐heì, i‐fūk, kwan‐heì, heung‐liû, mă, lōh, mooĭ‐nīn yaŭ tîng‐laî fan‐faatₒ laī à.
26 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tsaû tsuî‐tsaāp chùng‐ch‘e kûng mă‐ping, yaŭ ch‘e yat‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ shîng: mă‐ping yat‐maân‐î‐ts‘in, shaí k‘uĭ‐teî chuê‐haí chai‐ch‘e‐kè chùng‐shēng, k‘āp tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, t‘ūng‐maaī wōng kóh‐ch‘uè.
27 Wōng tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, lîng ngān hó‐ts‘z̆ shēk kòm‐toh, paakₒ‐heung‐shuê hó‐ts‘z̆ shaan‐kuk song‐shuê kòm‐toh.
28 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn shóh‐yaŭ‐kè mă, haî yaū Oi‐k‘āp taaì‐laī‐kè: shūk wōng tsô‐shang‐ì‐kè yān, kw‘ān‐kw‘ān maaĭ shaû, mooĭ‐kw‘ān yaŭ tîng‐kè kà.
29 Yaû ts‘ūng Oi‐k‘āp sheŭng ch‘ut‐laī‐kè ch‘e, mooĭ‐ch‘e kà‐ngān lūk‐paakₒ shī‐haakₒ: mooĭ‐mă kà‐ngān yat‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp shī‐haakₒ. Haak‐yān‐kè līt‐wōng, k‘āp À‐laān‐kè līt‐wōng, kaai t‘okₒ k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shaú, i‐chuê ni‐ti kà maaĭ‐ch‘ut taaì‐laī à.
