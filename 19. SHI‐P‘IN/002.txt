1 Î‐PONG huen‐wa ch‘aaú‐naaû, chùng‐mān t‘ō‐īn maū‐tōk, haî yan‐hōh uēn‐koò ni?
2 Shaì‐sheûng kwan‐wōng hing‐heí, paakₒ‐koon ooî‐ĭ, iù kung‐kik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ī‐ch‘é kung‐kik k‘uĭ shóh‐fung‐laāp‐ché.
3 Wâ, Pat‐uē chíng‐t‘uĕn k‘uĭ‐kè shīng, naú‐hoi k‘uĭ‐kè sokₒ.
4 Ts‘ŏh‐tsoî t‘in‐kè, shî‐pit siù k‘uĭ: Chué pit‐tîng miŭ‐shî k‘uĭ.
5 Chué hó‐īm‐suk laī‐chaakₒ‐shīng k‘uĭ, hó‐nau‐nô laī‐húng‐haakₒ k‘uĭ.
6 Wâ, Ngŏh fung‐laāp ngŏh chi wōng tsoî Sun‐shaan, tsik‐haî ngŏh‐chi‐shìng‐shaan.
7 Shaû‐fung‐ché wâ, Ngŏh pit kóng‐mīng‐k‘uĭ‐chi mîng‐lîng: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yă‐ts‘āng fan‐foò ngŏh wâ, Neĭ haî ngŏh Tsź; ngŏh kam‐yāt shang neĭ.
8 Neĭ k‘aū ngŏh, ngŏh tsaû tseung î‐pong ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ tsô ka‐ch‘aán tseung teî‐chi‐sz̀‐kīk, sheúng‐kwòh‐neĭ tsô māt‐īp.
9 Neĭ sui tseung t‘itₒ‐kwàn tá k‘uĭ; tá‐suì k‘uĭ hó‐ts‘z̆ kong‐ngă yat‐yeûng.
10 Neĭ‐teî līt‐wōng à, ying‐tong mīng‐paāk: shaì‐sheûng sẑ‐sz à, neĭ ying‐tong shaû‐kaaù.
11 Tseung kìng‐waì‐chi‐sam, fūk‐sẑ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ī‐ch‘é chìn‐lūt laī‐foon‐heí.
12 Kûng k‘uĭ Tsź ts‘an‐tsuí, p‘à k‘uĭ fat‐īn nau‐nô, neĭ‐teî tsaû sź‐tsoî lô‐chung, yan‐waî k‘uĭ chi fán‐nô, hó‐kap‐ts‘uk maăng‐līt. Taân‐faāní‐k‘aaù‐k‘uĭ‐kè, shî‐pit tak‐fuk.
