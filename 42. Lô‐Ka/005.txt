1 TAAÎ‐CHÙNG tsai‐yúng seúng t‘eng Sheûng‐Taì‐kè tô‐leĭ. Yē‐So k‘eĭ‐tó Kaakₒ‐neī‐saatₒ‐lāk oō‐pin;
2 T‘aí‐kìn leŭng‐chekₒ shuēn haí oō‐pin: kòh‐ti lóh‐uē‐yān, leī‐hiu shuēn huì saí‐mŏng.
3 Yat‐chekₒ shuēn haî Sai‐moōn‐kè, Yē‐So lōk‐huì, ts‘éng‐k‘uĭ ch‘aang‐hoi, leī ngôn ti‐kòm‐uĕn. Tsaû ts‘ŏh‐tó‐ch‘uè, haí shuēn‐sheûng kaaù‐fàn chùng‐yān.
4 Kóng‐uēn, tsaû‐tuì Sai‐moōn wâ, Ch‘aang‐tò sham‐shuí kòh‐ch‘uè, saatₒ mŏng lóh uē la.
5 Sai‐moōn taapₒ k‘uĭ wâ, Foo‐tsź, ngŏh‐teî shīng‐yê san‐foó, to m̄‐lóh‐tak‐tó: uē‐kam t‘eng neĭ wâ, ngŏh tsaû huì saatₒ‐mŏng.
6 K‘uĭ mŏng keì‐saatₒ, tsaû waī‐tak hó‐toh uē: kòh‐cheung mŏng sheûng‐hă‐līt.
7 Tsaû‐ngāp taî‐î‐chekₒ shuēn‐kè fóh‐keì laī‐pong k‘uĭ laai. K‘uĭ‐teî laī‐tò, chong‐moŏn leŭng‐chekₒ‐shuēn, tseung‐kân‐ch‘ām lokₒ.
8 Sai‐moōn Peí‐tak t‘aí‐kìn, tsaû pūk‐tó Yē‐So keukₒ‐hâ‐wâ, Chué, ts‘éng neĭ leī‐hoi ngŏh; ngŏh haî tsuî‐yān lokₒ.
9 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ uĕ‐k‘āp t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ‐kè yān, lóh‐tó kòm‐toh‐uē, kòh‐kòh to‐hó‐fong:
10 Sai‐moōn‐kè fóh‐keì, Sai‐peí‐t‘aaì‐kè tsaí Ngă‐kokₒ Yeukₒ‐hôn, to haî kóm. Yē‐So tuì Sai‐moōn wâ, M̄‐shaí p‘à; ts‘ūng‐kam‐ĭ‐haû, neĭ‐pit‐tak‐yān lokₒ.
11 K‘uĭ‐teî laai shuēn sheŭng‐ngôn, tsaû shé‐huì kokₒ‐yeûng māt‐kîn, kan‐ts‘ūng Yē‐So.
12 ¶ Yē‐So‐haí yat‐kòh‐shēng, yaŭ‐kòh‐yān ts‘uēn‐shan mā‐fung, t‘aí‐kìn Yē‐So, tsaû pūk‐tó‐teî k‘aū k‘uĭ wâ, Chué, neĭ yeūk‐haî‐háng, tsaû i‐tak ngŏh kitₒ‐tsîng lokₒ.
13 Yē‐So shan‐shaú moh‐hă k‘uĭ wâ, Ngŏh háng, neĭ tak‐kitₒ‐tsîng là, kòh‐ti fung tsik‐shī tsaû ch‘uē‐hiu.
14 Yē‐So kàm‐chí k‘uĭ, m̄‐hó wâ‐kwòh‐yān‐chi: taân huì peí‐kwòh tsaì‐sz t‘aí‐hă, ī‐ch‘é yan‐waî neĭ tak‐kitₒ‐tsîng, hìn Moh‐sai shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè laĭ‐māt, peí‐kwòh chùng‐yān tsô chìng‐kuì à.
15 Yē‐So‐kè mīng‐shing, uēt‐faatₒ ch‘uēn‐yeūng: kw‘ān‐chùng ts‘aī tsaāp, laī t‘eng tô‐leĭ, yaû seúng k‘uĭ i‐hó tsẑ‐keí‐kè pêng.
16 Taân Yē‐So t‘uì‐huì kw‘òng‐yĕ k‘eī‐t‘ó.
17 ¶ Tong yat yāt, Yē‐So kaaù‐fàn kòh‐shī, yaŭ Faatₒ‐leî‐ts‘oì‐yān, t‘ūng kaaù‐faatₒ‐sz, haí Ka‐leî‐leî kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐shaáng kokₒ‐ts‘uen‐heung, uĕ‐k‘āp haí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, laī‐ts‘ŏh‐tó‐ch‘uè. Chué hín‐ch‘ut k‘uĭ‐kè k‘uēn‐nāng, i‐hó‐pêng‐yān.
18 Yaŭ‐yān k‘aaĭ‐ch‘ōng t‘oī kòh‐fung‐t‘aán‐kè: seúng t‘oī‐yāp‐huì, chai‐tó Yē‐So mîn‐ts‘īn.
19 Yan‐waî yān‐toh, m̄‐t‘oī‐tak yāp‐huì, tsaû sheŭng uk‐pooì, yaū ngă‐chung līn ch‘ōng tiù‐lōk‐huì, chai‐tó Yē‐So mîn‐ts‘īn.
20 Yē‐So kìn k‘uĭ kòm‐sùn tsaû wâ, P‘āng‐yaŭ, neĭ‐kè tsuî tak‐shè lokₒ.
21 Kòh‐ti tūk‐shue‐yān k‘āp Faatₒ‐leî‐ts‘oì‐yān, sz‐hâ ĭ‐lûn wâ, Ni‐kòh‐kòm sitₒ‐tūk‐kè, haî mat‐shuī ni? Sheûng‐Taì chi‐ngoî, pin‐kòh nāng‐shè tsuî ni?
22 Yē‐So chi‐tò k‘uĭ‐kè ì‐sz̀, tsaû taapₒ‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Neĭ‐teî sam‐noî ĭ‐lûn mat‐yĕ ni?
23 Wâ, Neĭ‐kè tsuî tak‐shè; yik‐waāk wâ, Neĭ heí‐shan hāng, pin‐kîn kàng‐î ni?
24 Taân‐haî lîng‐neĭ‐teî chi‐tò, Yān‐Tsź haí teî‐sheûng, yaŭ shè‐tsuî‐kè k‘uēn‐pìng, (tsaû tuì kòh‐fung‐t‘aán‐yān wâ,) Ngŏh fan‐foò neĭ heí‐shan, ning kòh‐cheung‐ch‘ōng huì‐kwai.
25 Kòh‐yān haí chùng‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn, tsik‐shī k‘eĭ‐heí‐shan, ning k‘uĭ‐kè shuî‐p‘o faan‐huì uk‐k‘eí, kwai‐wīng Sheûng‐Taì.
26 Chùng‐yān king‐k‘eī, to kwai‐wīng Sheûng‐Taì, ī‐ch‘é shāp‐fan king‐fong wâ, Ngŏh‐teî kam yāt t‘aí‐kìn k‘eī‐sẑ lokₒ.
27 ¶ Haû‐loī Yē‐So ch‘ut‐huì, t‘aí‐kìn yat‐kòh‐shau‐shuì‐kè yān, mēng kiù Leî‐meî, ts‘ŏh‐tó shuì‐kwaan: tsaû tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Kan‐ts‘ūng ngŏh la.
28 K‘uĭ tsaû shé‐huì kokₒ‐yeûng māt‐kîn, heí‐shan laī‐kan Yē‐So.
29 Leî‐meî haí uk‐k‘eí, taaî‐p‘aaī īn‐tsīk ts‘éng Yē‐So: yaŭ hó‐toh‐shau‐shuì‐kè, kûng pīt‐î‐ti‐yān, t‘ūng‐maaī ts‘ŏh‐tsīk.
30 Faatₒ‐leî‐ts‘oì‐yān k‘āp kòh‐ch‘uè‐kè tūk‐shue‐yān, t‘aān Yē‐So‐kè moōn‐shang wâ, Yan‐hōh kûng shau‐shuì‐kè, tsuî‐okₒ‐kè, t‘ūng‐maaī yám‐shīk ni?
31 Yē‐So taapₒ k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Chòng‐kîn‐kè yān, m̄‐shaí‐iù i‐shang; taân‐haî yaŭ‐pêng‐kè yān chì‐iù che.
32 Ngŏh laī m̄‐haî kiù î‐yān tooì‐koí, haî kiù tsuî‐yān fooì‐koí che.
33 ¶ Chùng‐yān tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Yeukₒ‐hôn‐kè moōn‐shang, luĭ‐ts‘z̀ kàm‐shīk k‘eī‐t‘ó, Faatₒ‐leî‐ts‘oì‐yān‐kè moōn‐shang, to‐haî kóm; taân neĭ‐kè moōn‐shang, yaû‐yám yaû‐shīk.
34 Yē‐So tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, San‐lōng chûng‐haí‐ch‘uè, tím kiù‐tak hôh‐heí‐kè yān kàm‐shīk ni?
35 Taân tseung‐loī san‐lōng pit‐tîng leī‐hoi k‘uĭ‐teî huì‐hiu, kòh‐shī tsaû kàm‐shīk lokₒ.
36 Tsaû tseung p‘eì‐uê tuì k‘uĭ kóng‐wâ, Mŏ‐yān tsín san‐shaam‐kè pò, laī pó kaû‐shaam kà; yeūk‐haî kóm, tsaû p‘à chíng‐laân san‐kè, ī‐ch‘é shóh‐yûng kòh‐ti san‐kè pó‐lōk‐huì, kûng kaû‐kè yīk m̄‐hōp à.
37 Mŏ‐yān shaí kaû‐p‘eī‐toî laī tsoì san‐tsaú; yeūk‐haî kóm, tsaû p‘à san tsaú pik‐līt‐kòh‐toî, tsaú laû‐ch‘ut‐laī, toî yaû laân‐hiu.
38 Waī‐haî san‐tsaú pit‐tsoì‐lōk san‐p‘eī‐toî.
39 Mŏ‐yān yám‐hoi kaû‐tsaú, yaû seúng yám san‐kè: yan‐waî k‘uĭ wâ, Kaû‐kè uēt‐faatₒ‐hó à.
