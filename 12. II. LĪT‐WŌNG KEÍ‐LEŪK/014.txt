1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT wōng, Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hāt taî‐î nīn, Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè tsaí À‐mă‐tsê tang‐waî.
2 K‘uĭ tang‐waî chi‐shī, nīn‐keí î‐shāp‐nğ suì, k‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng, î‐shāp‐kaú nīn: k‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an haî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng yān, mēng‐kiù Yeukₒ‐yē‐taân.
3 À‐mă‐tsê hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn waī‐shîn‐kè, taân‐haî m̄‐ts‘z̆ k‘uĭ tsó‐foô Taaî‐waî: haî chiù‐i k‘uĭ foô Yeukₒ‐hāt faān shóh‐tsô‐kè laī‐hāng.
4 Taân yau‐t‘aān‐meî‐ch‘uē: chùng‐yān yīng‐īn tsoî yau‐t‘aān‐sheûng hìn‐tsaì fān‐heung.
5 Kwokₒ ĭ‐king kin‐koò tsoî k‘uĭ shaú, tsaû tseung sik‐yāt shaatₒ k‘uĭ foô wōng‐kè shān‐pūk laī‐shaatₒ‐hiu.
6 Taân shaatₒ‐lūk yān‐kè tsaí mŏ shaatₒ, tsun‐i Moh‐sai lūt‐faatₒ‐shue shóh‐sé‐lōk‐kè: tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò, wâ, Pat‐hóh yan tsź shaatₒ foô, yīk pat‐hóh yan foô shaatₒ tsź; yān faân‐tsuî tūk tseung poón‐yān chî‐sź.
7 À‐mă‐tsê tsoî Īm‐kuk, shaatₒ Ĭ‐tung‐yān yat‐maân, yaû tá Sai‐la tuēt‐hiu, koí‐mēng kiù Yeukₒ‐t‘ipₒ, chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
8 ¶ À‐mă‐tsê tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché huì kìn Yē‐oô‐kè suen Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt wâ, Ts‘éng laī, táng ngŏh t‘ūng neĭ taaî‐ka‐tīk‐mîn seung‐kìn à.
9 Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché huì kìn Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐mă‐tsê wâ, Leî‐pa‐nuên‐kè king‐kik, tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché tuì Leî‐pa‐nuên‐kè paakₒ‐heung‐mūk wâ, Tseung neĭ nuĭ peí‐kwòh ngŏh tsaí tsô ts‘ai: Leî‐pa‐nuên yaŭ yat yĕ‐shaù king‐kwòh, ch‘aaí‐taāp king‐kik.
10 Neĭ ĭ‐king tá‐paaî Ĭ‐tung‐yān, tsaû sam‐chung kiu‐ngô: neĭ ĭ ts‘ź waī wīng‐iû, tsoî ka on‐kui tsaû pâ le: waî‐mat neĭ iù yĕ wôh, shaí tsẑ‐keí kûng Yaū‐taaî yat‐kwokₒ t‘ūng mīt‐mōng ni?
11 À‐mă‐tsê m̄‐háng t‘eng‐ts‘ūng. Koò‐ts‘ź Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt sheŭng‐huì; haí shūk Yaū‐taaî‐kè Paakₒ‐shî‐māk, t‘ūng Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐mă‐tsê tīk‐mîn taaî‐ka seung‐kìn.
12 Yaū‐taaî‐yān peî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tá‐paaî; kokₒ‐tsẑ‐t‘ō‐kwai cheùng‐mōk.
13 Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt haí Paakₒ‐shî‐māk, chukₒ‐chuê Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐hap‐sz‐kè suen Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè tsaí À‐mă‐tsê, tsaû huì‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, waí‐ch‘aakₒ Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè shēng‐ts‘eūng, tsẑ Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐moōn chì‐shēng‐kokₒ‐moōn, kûng‐kaì sz̀‐shāp cheûng.
14 Yaû tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn, k‘āp wōng foò‐chung‐kè kam‐ngān, pìng shóh‐yaŭ heì‐mĭng yat‐k‘oì ning‐huì, ī‐ch‘é taaì yān huì tsô òn‐tòng, tsaû faan‐huì Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à.
15 Yeukₒ‐hāt k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, k‘āp k‘uĭ yŭng‐līk, pìng‐t‘ūng Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐mă‐tsê seung‐chìn, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
16 Yeukₒ‐hāt kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám, tsòng‐tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à, haí Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐kè fān‐teî; k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
17 ¶ Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng À‐hap‐sz‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hāt sź‐haû, Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè tsaí À‐mă‐tsê, sheûng‐tsoî‐shaì shāp‐nğ nīn.
18 À‐mă‐tsê k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
19 Tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng yaŭ‐yān maū‐faán À‐mă‐tsê: k‘uĭ t‘ō‐tsaú‐tò La‐kat; taân poôn‐tóng tá‐faatₒ yān huì‐tò La‐kat, haí kóh‐ch‘uè shaatₒ k‘uĭ.
20 Tsaû tseung k‘uĭ chai‐tsoî mă‐pooì: faan‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ līt‐tsó, tsòng‐tsoî Taaî‐waî‐kè shēng.
21 Kòh‐shī Oo‐sai‐à nīn‐keí shāp‐lūk suì, Yaū‐taaî chùng‐mān laāp k‘uĭ kaì foô‐ts‘an À‐mă‐tsê tsô wōng.
22 À‐mă‐tsê keì kûng līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám, Oo‐sai‐à kìn‐heí Ĭ‐laāt, tsaû kwai‐faan Yaū‐taaî‐kwokₒ koón‐hāt.
23 ¶ Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè tsaí À‐mă‐tsê taî‐shāp‐nğ nīn, Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm, haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à tang‐waî, tsô wōng sz̀‐shāp‐yat nīn.
24 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng‐okₒ: pat‐leī Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè tsuî, shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haâm‐lōk tsuî.
25 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān peî‐tuēt‐hiu teî‐t‘ó, Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm to tak‐faan, tsẑ Hap‐moōt kíng chì‐p‘īng‐uēn‐kè hoí, chiù‐i Ĭ‐shik‐līt Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, t‘okₒ k‘uĭ‐kè pūk Kaapₒ‐hei‐fat‐yān À‐maĭ‐t‘aaì‐kè tsaí, sin‐chi Yeukₒ‐nā shóh‐kóng‐kè.
26 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘aí‐kìn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān uê waân‐naân kw‘àn‐foó tò kīk: k‘eī‐chung waāk tsẑ‐chué, waāk nō‐pūk to mŏ, yīk mŏ yān pong‐chôh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
27 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yīng m̄‐ts‘āng wâ iù ts‘ūng t‘in‐hâ mīt‐tsuēt Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān: shóh‐ĭ shaí Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ‐teî.
28 Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, k‘āp k‘uĭ yŭng‐līk, k‘uĭ tím‐yeûng chaang‐chìn, tím‐yeûng tak‐faan Taaî‐mă‐shik, pìng ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn shūk Yaū‐taaî‐kè Hap‐moōt, lîng kwai Ĭ‐shik‐līt, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
29 Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng t‘ūng‐ts‘ám; k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
