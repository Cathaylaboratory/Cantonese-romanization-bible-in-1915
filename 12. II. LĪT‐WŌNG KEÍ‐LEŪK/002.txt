1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ seúng yûng suên‐fung tsipₒ Ĭ‐leî‐à shing‐t‘in‐chi‐shī, Ĭ‐leî‐à t‘ūng Ĭ‐leî‐sha ts‘ūng Kat‐kaapₒ ī‐huì.
2 Ĭ‐leî‐à tuì Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, K‘aū neĭ haí ni‐ch‘uè táng‐haû; yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tá‐faatₒ ngŏh huì Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî. Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Ngŏh chí‐shang‐oōt‐kè Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘āp chí neĭ‐kè oōt‐līng faatₒ‐shaî, ngŏh m̄‐laī‐hoi neĭ. Kóm‐tsaû k‘uĭ‐teî lōk‐tò Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî.
3 Tsoî Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî‐kè sin‐chi moōn‐t‘ō, ch‘ut‐laī kìn Ĭ‐leî‐sha, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kam‐yāt tsẑ neĭ ts‘īn‐t‘aū iù tsipₒ neĭ lŏ‐sz huì, neĭ chi‐tò m̄‐ni? K‘uĭ wâ, Haî‐lokₒ, chi‐tò; neĭ m̄‐shaí ch‘ut‐sheng.
4 Ĭ‐leî‐à tuì Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, K‘aū neĭ haí ni‐ch‘uè táng‐haû; yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tá‐faatₒ ngŏh huì Yē‐leî‐koh. Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Ngŏh chí‐shang‐oōt‐kè Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘āp chí neĭ‐kè oōt‐līng faatₒ‐shaî, ngŏh m̄‐leī‐hoi neĭ. Kóm‐tsaû k‘uĭ‐teî huì‐tò Yē‐leî‐koh.
5 Tsoî Yē‐leî‐koh‐kè sin‐chi moōn‐t‘ō, laī kìn Ĭ‐leî‐sha, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kam‐yāt tsoî neĭ ts‘īn‐t‘aū iù tsipₒ neĭ lŏ‐sz huì, neĭ chi‐tò m̄‐ni? K‘uĭ wâ, Haî lokₒ, chi‐tò; neĭ m̄‐shaí ch‘ut‐sheng.
6 Ĭ‐leî‐à tuì Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, K‘aū neĭ haí ni‐ch‘uè táng‐haû; yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tá‐faatₒ ngŏh huì Yeukₒ‐taân. Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Ngŏh chí‐shang‐oōt‐kè Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘āp chí neĭ‐kè oōt‐līng faatₒ‐shaî, ngŏh m̄‐leī‐hoi neĭ. Kóm‐tsaû leŭng‐yān t‘ūng‐hāng.
7 Yaŭ sin‐chi moōn‐t‘ō nğ‐shāp yān, uĕn‐uĕn k‘eĭ‐laāp tuì‐mîn; kóh leŭng‐yān haí Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐pin k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè.
8 Ĭ‐leî‐à kuén‐heí tsẑ‐keí‐kè ngoî‐i, k‘aaĭ‐laī kik‐shuí, shuí tsaû fan‐hoi tsóh‐yaû, leŭng‐yān tsoî hŏn‐teî kwòh‐huì.
9 Kwòh‐huì chi‐haû, Ĭ‐leî‐à tuì Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Uē‐kam ngŏh meî‐ts‘āng peî‐tsipₒ‐huì, neĭ seúng ngŏh waî neĭ tsô mat‐yĕ, tsaû chikₒ‐koón k‘aū ngŏh. Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, K‘aū neĭ tseung kóm‐tûng neĭ‐kè shān, ka‐p‘ooĭ ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh.
10 Ĭ‐leî‐à wâ, Neĭ shóh‐k‘aū‐kè naān tak lokₒ: sui haî kóm, ngŏh peî‐tsipₒ‐huì‐chi‐shī, neĭ yeūk t‘aí‐kìn ngŏh, tsaû pit tak‐tó; yeūk m̄‐t‘aí‐kìn ngŏh, tsaû pit‐m̄‐tak‐tó lokₒ.
11 K‘uĭ‐teî yīng‐īn t‘ūng‐hāng kóng‐wâ chi‐shī, fat‐īn yaŭ yat‐leŭng fóh‐ch‘e k‘āp fóh‐mă, tseung k‘uĭ‐teî kaakₒ‐hoi; Ĭ‐leî‐à tsaû shîng suēn‐fung shing t‘in.
12 Ĭ‐leî‐sha t‘aí‐kìn, tsaû iu‐wâ, Ngŏh foô à, ngŏh foô à, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè ch‘e, kûng mă‐ping à. Haû‐loī tsaû m̄‐kìn k‘uĭ, ue‐shî tseung tsẑ‐keí‐kè shaam, maakₒ‐hoi leŭng‐p‘ìn.
13 Chap‐heí Ĭ‐leî‐à shóh‐titₒ‐lōk‐kè ngoî‐i, faan tò Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐pin k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè;
14 Tseung Ĭ‐leî‐à titₒ‐lōk kóh‐kîn ngoî‐i kik‐shuí wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐leî‐à‐kè Sheûng‐Taì haí pin‐ch‘uè ni? kik‐shuí chi‐haû, shuí tsaû fan‐hoi tsóh‐yaû: Ĭ‐leî‐sha kwòh‐huì.
15 Tsoî Yē‐leî‐koh‐kè sin‐chi moōn‐t‘ō, kìn k‘uĭ tsaŭ wâ, Kóm‐tûng Ĭ‐leî‐à‐kè shān, yīk kóm‐tûng Ĭ‐leî‐sha. K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû laī‐yīng‐tsipₒ k‘uĭ, tong k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn pūk‐tó teî‐sheûng.
16 Yaû tuì k‘uĭ wâ, T‘aí‐a, uē‐kam t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ‐kè chùng‐pūk, yaŭ nğ‐shāp‐kòh yŭng‐sẑ; k‘aū neĭ yūng k‘uĭ‐teì huì‐chaaú‐ts‘ām neĭ lŏ‐sz: húng‐p‘à Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè Shān tseung k‘uĭ taaì‐tò maŭ‐shaan maŭ‐kuk. Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Neĭ m̄‐hó tá‐faatₒ‐yān‐huì.
17 K‘uĭ‐teî ts‘ui k‘uĭ, chì k‘uĭ kìn sau‐kw‘aì, tsaû wâ. Chikₒ‐koón tá‐faatₒ huì la. K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû tá‐faatₒ nğ‐shāp yān huì; wán saam‐yāt to m̄‐kìn.
18 (Ĭ‐leî‐sha haí Yē‐leî‐koh táng‐haû chi‐shī,) chùng‐yān faan‐laī k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè. Ĭ‐leî‐sha tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Ngŏh heí‐m̄‐haì kiù neĭ‐teî maĭ‐huì me?
19 ¶ Shēng‐chung‐kè yān tuì Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Ts‘éng neĭ t‘aí‐hă ts‘ź shēng shóh‐kìn teî‐fong haî hó, ngŏh chué ngaăn kìn: taân‐haî shuí m̄‐hó‐kè, teî‐t‘ó pat‐ch‘aán.
20 Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Iù k‘aaĭ san‐p‘īng chong‐īm, ning‐laī peí‐ngŏh. K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû ning‐laī peí k‘uĭ.
21 K‘uĭ ch‘ut‐tò shuí‐uēn, tseung īm tó‐lōk shuí‐chung, wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Ngŏh chî‐hó ni‐ti shuí; tsẑ‐kam‐ĭ‐haû, m̄‐ooĭ tsoì lîng yān sź, yaû m̄‐lîng teî‐t‘ó pat‐ch‘aán.
22 Kóm‐tsaû kòh‐ti shuí pìn‐hiu hó‐kè, chì‐tò kam‐yāt, yìng‐îm Ĭ‐leî‐sha shóh‐kóng‐kè à.
23 ¶ Ĭ‐leî‐sha ts‘ūng kóh‐ch‘uè sheŭng huì Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî: hāng‐lô‐chi‐shī, yaŭ kw‘ān t‘ūng‐tsź tsẑ shēng‐chung ch‘ut‐laī heì‐siù k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ lŏ‐t‘uk sheŭng‐huì la; neĭ lŏ‐t‘uk sheŭng‐huì la.
24 K‘uĭ nîng‐chuèn‐t‘aū t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ‐teî, tsaû t‘okₒ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mēng chaù‐chòh k‘uĭ‐teî. Ue‐shî yaŭ leŭng‐chekₒ mŏ‐hūng, tsẑ lām‐chung ch‘ut‐laī, chaaú‐sheung kw‘ān t‘ūng chung sz̀‐shāp‐î kòh.
25 Ĭ‐leî‐sha tsẑ kóh‐ch‘uè sheŭng‐huì Ka‐māt‐shaan, yaû tsẑ Ka‐māt‐shaan faan‐huì Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à.
