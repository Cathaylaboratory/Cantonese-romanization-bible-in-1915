1 TONG À‐taāt‐sitₒ‐sai wōng taî‐î‐shāp nīn, Neī‐saàn uēt chi‐shī, yaŭ tsaú tsoî wōng‐mîn‐ts‘īn: ngŏh tsaû ning tsaú laī‐fûng wōng. Ngŏh tsoî wōng‐ts‘īn, p‘īng‐sò mŏ‐yau‐shaū‐kè yūng‐maaû.
2 Wōng tuì ngŏh wâ, Neĭ mŏ pêng waî‐mat yūng‐maaû yau‐shaū ni? ni‐ti mŏ‐pīt‐yeûng, shāt haî sam‐kè yau‐moôn che. Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh shâm‐kuî‐p‘à.
3 Tsaù wōng‐chi‐wâ, Uên wōng maân suì: ngŏh līt‐tsó‐chi‐fān‐mô kòh‐ch‘uè‐kè shēng fong‐faì, shēng‐moōn to peî‐fóh‐shiu, ngŏh heí‐m̄‐ying‐koi yaŭ yau‐shaū‐kè yūng‐maaû me?
4 Wōng mân ngŏh wâ, Uē‐kam neĭ seúng k‘aū mat‐yĕ ni? Ngŏh tsaû k‘eī‐t‘ó t‘in‐sheûng‐kè Sheûng‐Taì.
5 Ts‘uī‐haû tuì wōng wâ, Wōng yeūk foon‐heí, neĭ pūk waāk tsoî wōng‐ts‘īn tak yan‐ch‘úng, tsaû k‘aū tá‐faatₒ‐ngŏh‐huì Yaū‐t‘aaì, tò ngŏh līt‐tsó‐fān‐mô‐kè shēng, táng ngŏh tsoì kìn‐chuk.
6 (Ts‘ź‐shī, wōng‐haû kûng wōng t‘ūng‐ts‘ŏh.) Wōng tuì ngŏh wâ, Neĭ tseung‐hāng‐kè lô‐ch‘īng, haî keí‐noî ni? yaû keí‐shī faan‐laī ni? Kóm‐tsaû wōng foon‐heí tá‐faatₒ ngŏh huì; ngŏh tsaû tîng yāt‐k‘eī.
7 Kim‐chi ngŏh tsaù wōng wâ, Wōng yeūk foon‐heí, tsaû ts‘éng k‘aaĭ chiù‐shue ts‘z̀ ngŏh, t‘ung‐chi hōh‐ngoî‐kè chùng‐fong‐paakₒ, táng k‘uĭ chún ngŏh chì‐tò Yaū‐t‘aaì;
8 Yaû ts‘z̀‐shue yat‐fung, t‘ung‐chi koón‐leĭ‐wōng‐uēn‐lām‐shuê‐mūk‐kè À‐saatₒ, táng k‘uĭ peí ngŏh sui‐yûng‐kè mūk‐liû, tsô tîn‐chi‐kung‐moōn‐kè leūng, kûng kìn‐chuk shēng‐ts‘eūng, k‘āp ngŏh shóh‐tseung‐chuê‐kè uk. Wōng tsaû chún ngŏh, yan ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shaú shîn‐chôh ngŏh à.
9 Ue‐shî ngŏh huì‐tò hōh‐ngoî‐kè chùng‐fong‐paakₒ‐ch‘uè, tseung wōng‐kè chiù‐shue kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî. Wōng yă‐ts‘āng p‘aaì keí‐kòh kwan‐cheúng kûng mă‐ping t‘ūng‐maaī‐huì.
10 Wōh‐lūn‐yān Saatₒ‐paatₒ‐la, uĕ‐k‘āp tsô‐nō‐pūk‐kè À‐moōn‐yān Toh‐peí, mān‐tak yaŭ‐yān‐laī, seúng hing‐heí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, tsaû hó‐yau‐shaū.
11 Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng haí kóh‐ch‘uè chuê‐hiu saam‐yāt.
12 Yaû tsoî yê‐kaan heí‐shan, kûng keí‐kòh‐yān t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh; waī‐haî ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐chai‐tsoî‐ngŏh‐sam, heùng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng seúng‐tsô‐kè, ngŏh mŏ‐k‘aaĭ‐laī wâ‐kwòh‐yān‐chi: ngŏh shóh‐k‘ē‐kè shang‐haú chi‐ngoî, mŏ taî‐î‐chekₒ shang‐haú t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh.
13 Ngŏh laû‐yê ch‘ut Kuk‐moōn, tò Lūng‐tséng, yaû tò Fàn‐moōn, t‘aí‐kwòh Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè shēng‐ts‘eūng, ĭ‐king titₒ‐laân shēng‐moōn to peî‐fóh‐shiu.
14 Yaû hāng‐tò Ts‘uēn‐moōn k‘āp wōng‐kè ch‘ī: taân haí kóh‐taatₒ mŏ lô peí‐ngŏh‐shóh‐k‘ē‐kè shang‐haú hóh‐t‘ung‐hāng‐kè.
15 Ue‐shî laû‐yê yaū k‘ai‐pin chīk‐sheŭng, t‘aí‐kwòh shēng‐ts‘eūng, chuèn‐heùng Kuk‐moōn, kóm‐tsaû faan‐laī.
16 Chùng‐fong‐paakₒ m̄‐chi ngŏh huì‐pin‐ch‘uè, yīk m̄‐chi ngŏh tsô‐mat‐yĕ: ngŏh yīk meî‐ts‘āng tseung ni‐kîn‐sẑ t‘ung‐chi. Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, waāk tsaì‐sz, tseukₒ‐waî, waāk fong‐paakₒ, waāk k‘eī‐uē‐kè, tsik‐haî tsô‐kung‐kè.
17 Ngŏh tsaû tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî kìn ngŏh‐teî shóh‐uê‐cheūk‐kè tsoi‐naân, Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng ĭ‐king fong‐faì, shēng‐moōn to peî‐fóh‐shiu: ngŏh‐teî iù heí‐laī kìn faan Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè shēng‐ts‘eūng, mĭn ngŏh‐teî yīng‐īn shaû līng‐yūk à.
18 Ngŏh tsaû tseung ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shaú shîn chôh ngŏh; k‘āp wōng shóh‐fan‐foò ngŏh‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, kóng‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî chi. Chùng‐yān tsaû wâ, Iù heí‐laī kìn‐chuk à. Kóm‐tsaû kin‐koò k‘uĭ‐teî shaú tsô ni‐kîn shîn‐kung.
19 Wōh‐lūn‐yān Saatₒ‐paatₒ‐la, uĕ‐k‘āp tsô‐nō‐pūk‐kè À‐moōn‐yān Toh‐peí, kûng À‐la‐paakₒ‐yān Kei‐shîn, t‘eng‐mān tsaû kei‐siù ngŏh, miŭ‐shî ngŏh wâ, Neĭ‐teî shóh‐tsô haî mat‐yĕ ni? seúng heùng wōng tsokₒ‐faán me?
20 Ngŏh taapₒ k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, T‘in‐sheûng‐kè Sheûng‐Taì pit hing‐heí ngŏh; shóh‐ĭ ngŏh‐teî tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐kè pūk‐yān, pit heí‐laī kìn‐chuk: taân neĭ‐teî haí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng mō fân, mō īp, mō kwaan‐shipₒ à.
