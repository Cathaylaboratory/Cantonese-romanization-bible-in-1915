1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tsoî Sai‐naaĭ‐shaan tuì Moh‐sai kóng kóh‐yāt, Moh‐sai À‐lūn‐kè tsūk‐p‘o līt mīng tsoî haû.
2 À‐lūn chùng‐tsź‐kè mēng haî kóm; K‘uĭ cheúng‐tsź mēng‐kiù Nā‐taapₒ, yaû‐yaŭ À‐peì‐oô, Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, Ĭ‐taaî‐mă.
3 Ni‐ti haî À‐lūn chùng‐tsź‐kè mēng, tsik‐haî shaû‐ko tsô tsaì‐sz, yă‐ts‘āng kûng k‘uĭ hāng kuk‐yīng‐laĭ, táng k‘uĭ tong tsaì‐sz chik‐fân‐kè chik.
4 Nā‐taapₒ, À‐peì‐oô, k‘aaĭ m̄‐shìng‐kitₒ‐kè fóh hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, haí Sai‐naaĭ kw‘òng‐yĕ, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn tsaû sź, k‘uĭ‐teî mŏ tsaí: Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, Ĭ‐taaî‐mă kûng foô‐ts‘an À‐lūn t‘ūng tsô tsaì‐sz‐kè chik.
5 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
6 Neĭ iù taaì Leî‐meî chi‐p‘aaì laī, k‘eĭ‐tsoî tsaì‐sz À‐lūn mîn‐ts‘īn, táng fūk‐sẑ k‘uĭ.
7 T‘ūng k‘uĭ shaú kóh‐ti chik‐fân, k‘āp‐ooî‐chùng‐kè chik‐fân, tsoî ooî‐mōk mîn‐ts‘īn, tong mōk‐noî‐kè fūk‐yīk.
8 Yaû iù hon‐shaú mōk‐noî‐kè heì‐mĭng, toî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shaú kóh‐ti chik‐fân, tong mōk‐noî‐kè fūk‐yīk.
9 Neĭ iù tseung Leî‐meî yān kaau‐kwòh À‐lūn kûng k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź: yan k‘uĭ‐teî haî yaū Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chi‐chung, kaán‐ch‘ut laī ts‘z̀‐kwòh À‐lūn‐kè.
10 Neĭ iù p‘aaì À‐lūn k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź, tsô tsaì‐sz‐kè chik‐fân: yeūk‐yaŭ pīt‐yān kân shìng‐shóh tsaû iù shaû sź.
11 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
12 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chi‐chung, kaán‐ch‘ut Leî‐meî‐yān, laī t‘aì‐toî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ch‘oh‐t‘oi cheúng‐tsź: koò‐ts‘ź Leî‐meî‐yān to kwai‐ue ngŏh;
13 Faān cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè, poón‐loī kwai‐ue ngŏh; ngŏh tsoî Oi‐k‘āp shaatₒ‐saaì cheúng‐tsź chi‐shī, tsaû k‘aaĭ Ĭ‐shik‐līt chung shóh‐yaŭ cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè, m̄‐lûn yān waāk ch‘uk‐shang: to pīt‐hoi waī shìng kwai‐ue ngŏh: ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à.
14 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî Sai‐naaì kw‘òng‐yĕ tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
15 Neĭ iù shó Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè tsź‐suen, chiù‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐tsung‐tsūk chi‐p‘aaì: faān haî yat‐uēt‐ĭ‐sheûng‐kè naām‐ting, iù shó‐kwòh.
16 Ue‐shî Moh‐sai tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ laī shó‐kwòh.
17 Leî‐meî chùng‐tsź‐kè mēng, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû; yat‐kòh haî Kaakₒ‐shûn, yat‐kòh haî Koh‐hāt, yat‐kòh haî Maĭ‐la‐leĭ.
18 Kaakₒ‐shûn chùng‐tsź‐kè mēng, chiù‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐p‘aaì līt‐mīng; yat‐kòh haî Laāp‐neī, yat‐kòh haî Shî‐mooĭ.
19 Koh‐hāt‐kè tsaí, chiù‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐p‘aaì; tsaû haî Òm‐laān, Ĭ‐sz‐hap, Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn, Oo‐sitₒ.
20 Maĭ‐la‐leĭ‐kè tsaí, chiù‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐p‘aaì; tsaû‐haî Mootₒ‐leî, Mŏ‐shî. Ni‐ti haî Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè chi‐p‘aaì, chiù‐i kokₒ‐tsung‐tsūk‐kè.
21 ¶ Shūk Kaakₒ‐shûn‐kè, yaŭ Laāp‐neī tsūk, Shî‐mooĭ tsūk: ni‐ti haî Kaakₒ‐shûn‐yān‐kè î tsūk.
22 Kòh‐ti peî‐shó‐kè, tsẑ yat uēt ĭ‐sheûng‐kè chùng naām‐ting, faān peî‐shó‐kè yān, kûng‐kaì ts‘at‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ.
23 Kaakₒ‐shûn‐kè î‐tsūk, iù tsoî ooî‐mōk‐haû, sai‐pîn on yīng.
24 La‐leî‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, iù tsô Kaakₒ‐shûn‐yān poón tsūk‐kè mūk‐cheúng.
25 Kaakₒ‐shûn‐kè tsź‐suen tsoî ooî‐mōk‐chung shóh‐hon‐shaú‐kè, tsaû‐haî ooî‐mōk k‘āp mōk‐koì, mōk‐moōn‐kè līm,
26 Tsokₒ ts‘eūng‐kè pò‐waī, ts‘eūng moōn‐kè līm, tsik‐haî ooî‐mōk k‘āp t‘aān sz̀‐waī‐kè pò‐ts‘eūng, kûng yat‐ts‘aì shaí‐yûng‐kè shīng‐sokₒ.
27 ¶ Shūk Koh‐hāt‐kè, yaŭ Òm‐laān tsūk, Ĭ‐sz‐hap tsūk, Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn tsūk: Ni‐ti haî Koh‐hāt‐kè chùng tsūk.
28 Tsẑ yat uēt ĭ‐sheûng‐kè naām‐ting, tsô hon‐shaú shìng‐shóh‐kè chik‐yīk, kóh‐ti‐yān‐shò, kûng‐kaì paatₒ‐ts‘in‐lūk‐paakₒ.
29 Koh‐hāt‐tsaí‐kè chùng‐tsūk, iù tsoî ooî‐mōk‐kè naām‐pîn on‐yīng.
30 Oo‐sitₒ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐saatₒ‐faán, tong tsô Koh‐hāt‐yān poón tsūk‐kè mūk‐cheúng.
31 K‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐hon‐shaú‐kè, haî yeukₒ‐kwaî, òn‐t‘oī, tang‐t‘oī, kûng kokₒ‐t‘aān, k‘āp shìng‐shóh‐chung shaí‐yûng‐kĕ heì‐mĭng, moōn‐līm, k‘āp līm‐sheûng‐kè yat‐ts‘aì māt‐kîn.
32 Tsaì‐sz À‐lūn‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, iù tsô Leî‐meî‐yān chùng‐mūk‐cheúng‐kè tsúng‐t‘aū, t‘úng‐hāt hon‐shaú‐shìng‐shóh‐kè yān.
33 ¶ Shūk Maĭ‐la‐leĭ‐kè, yaŭ Mootₒ‐leî tsūk, Mŏ‐shî tsūk: ni‐ti haî Maĭ‐la‐leĭ‐kè î‐tsūk.
34 Tsẑ yat uēt ĭ‐sheûng‐kè naām‐ting, faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì yaŭ lūk‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ.
35 À‐peì‐hoī‐kè tsaí So‐līt, haî tsô Maĭ‐la‐leĭ poón‐tsūk‐kè mūk‐cheúng: k‘uĭ‐teî iù tsoî ooî‐mōk‐kè pak‐pîn on‐yīng.
36 Maĭ‐la‐leĭ‐kè tsź‐suen shóh‐hon‐shaú‐kè, haî ooî‐mōk‐kè paán, shaan, ch‘uĕ, tsôh, kûng yat‐ts‘aì heì‐mĭng, k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ shaí‐yûng‐kè,
37 Pò‐ts‘eūng‐kè ch‘uĕ, tsôh, teng, shīng.
38 Waī tsoî kui‐shóh‐ts‘īn heùng tung‐pîn, tsik‐haî ooî‐mōk‐ts‘īn heùng tung‐pîn, yāt‐ch‘ut‐kòh‐ch‘uè on‐yīng‐kè, iù Moh‐sai À‐lūn kûng k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź, tsô hon‐shaú shìng‐shóh‐kè chik‐yīk, tsik‐haî t‘aì‐toî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kè; yeūk yaŭ pīt‐yān kân‐ts‘īn tsaû iù shaû sź.
39 Faān peî‐shó‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān, tsik‐haî Moh‐sai À‐lūn i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî chi‐p‘aaì laī shó‐kwòh‐kè, tsẑ yat uēt ĭ‐sheûng‐kè naām‐ting, kûng yaŭ î‐maân î‐ts‘in.
40 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Neĭ iù tseung Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè naām‐ting, tsẑ yat uēt ĭ‐sheûng‐kè, kaì‐shò tím mēng.
41 (Ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā,) neĭ iù tseung Leî‐meî‐yān t‘aì‐toî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shóh‐yaŭ cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè tsaí; yīk tseung Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, t‘aì‐toî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ch‘oh‐t‘oi‐kè ch‘uk‐shang.
42 Moh‐sai tsaû i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîng‐lîng, kaì‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shóh‐yaŭ cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè.
43 Faān yat uēt ĭ‐sheûng, cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè naām‐ting, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî yān mēng laī kaì‐kwòh, kûng‐kaì î‐maân‐î‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐saam yān.
44 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
45 Neĭ iù tseung Leî‐meî‐yān t‘aì‐toî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chung cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè tsaí, yaû tseung Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, t‘aì‐toî k‘uĭ‐kè ch‘uk‐shang; kóm‐tsaû Leî‐meî‐yān pit kwai‐shūk ngŏh: ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à.
46 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chung cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè, chûng toh‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān î‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐saam mēng, ni‐ti haî tong iù shūk‐ch‘ut‐kè;
47 Neĭ iù waî mooĭ‐yat‐ting kaau‐shūk ngān nğ‐shī‐haakₒ, chiù shìng‐shóh‐kè shī‐haakₒ: (mooĭ shī‐haakₒ haî î‐shāp kwaì‐la:)
48 Neĭ iù tseung shóh‐shūk toh‐uē chi‐yān‐kè shūk‐ngān, kaau‐kwòh À‐lūn, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź.
49 Ue‐shî Moh‐sai heùng Leî‐meî‐yān shūk shóh‐uē‐shîng‐kè yān, ning kóh‐ti shūk ngān:
50 Tsik‐haî yaū Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chung cheúng‐t‘aū‐shang‐kè yān‐ch‘uè ning‐laī‐kè; kûng‐kaì yat‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ‐lūk‐shāp‐nğ shī‐haakₒ, chiù shìng‐shóh‐kè shī‐haakₒ:
51 Moh‐sai k‘aaĭ peî‐shūk‐kè ngān kaau‐kwòh À‐lūn, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź, tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò‐Moh‐sai‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ.
