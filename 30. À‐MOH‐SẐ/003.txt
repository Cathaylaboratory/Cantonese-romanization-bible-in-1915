1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT‐KÈ tsź‐suen à, iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐kung‐kik‐neĭ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, yaû kung‐kik yaū Oi‐k‘āp taaì‐laī‐ts‘uēn‐ka‐kè wâ,
2 Teî‐sheûng kokₒ‐ka‐chi‐chung, ngŏh‐tūk‐haî yîng‐shik‐neĭ: shóh‐ĭ iù‐k‘aaĭ‐neĭ‐shóh‐faân‐kè tsuî, waān‐faan tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐sheûng.
3 Uē‐kwóh‐leŭng‐yān m̄‐tak‐hōp‐sam, heí‐ooĭ‐t‘ūng‐hāng me?
4 Uē‐kwóh taaî‐sz m̄‐chukₒ‐tak‐shīk‐māt, heí‐ooĭ‐tsoî‐shuê‐lām‐chung p‘aaū‐haau me? sz‐tsź mŏ‐māt‐tak‐tó, heí‐ooĭ‐tsoî‐taû faatₒ‐sheng‐me?
5 Uē‐kwóh‐mŏ‐mŏng ch‘itₒ‐tîng‐tsoî‐teî, tseukₒ‐tsaí heí‐ooĭ‐titₒ‐lōk mŏng‐chung me? uē‐kwóh‐mŏ‐māt‐tsuî‐maaī, mŏng‐lōh heí‐ooĭ‐faatₒ‐heí me?
6 Hô‐t‘ūng‐tsoî‐shēng‐chung ch‘ui‐sheng, tsź‐mān heí‐m̄‐king‐fong me? uē‐kwóh‐tsoi‐naân kòng‐lām‐yat‐shēng, heí‐m̄‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐tsô me?
7 T‘óng‐yeūk Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā m̄‐ts‘āng k‘aaĭ‐k‘uĭ‐kè māt‐chí, hín‐mīng‐kwòh‐sin‐chi tsik‐k‘uĭ‐pūk, yat‐kîn‐sẑ shî‐pit‐m̄‐tsô‐shīng lokₒ.
8 Taaî‐sz ts‘āng‐p‘aaū‐haau, mat‐shuī m̄‐king‐fong‐ni? Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng‐kóng, mat‐shuī kóm‐m̄‐kóng‐uê‐īn ni?
9 ¶ Iù‐suen‐ch‘uēn tsoî À‐shāt‐tāt‐kè kung‐shat‐ch‘uè, yaû tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐kè kung‐shat‐ch‘uè wâ, Iù‐taaî‐ka tsuî‐maaī tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐kè shaan‐sheûng, yaû t‘aí‐hă shóh‐yaŭ‐taaî‐kaaú‐luên‐chi‐sẑ, k‘āp k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kw‘àn‐chūk‐chi‐sẑ.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Kòh‐ti‐haî‐k‘eūng‐okₒ, ts‘eúng‐tuēt, shau‐ts‘ōng‐yāp‐k‘uĭ‐kung‐shat‐kè yān, tsaû‐m̄‐chi‐tò tím‐yeûng‐hāng‐chìng‐chīk‐kè lokₒ.
11 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐wâ; Pit‐yaŭ‐yat‐kòh‐ch‘aū‐tīk haí‐neĭ‐kíng‐teî‐sz̀‐waī‐ch‘uè; k‘uĭ‐pit‐shaí‐neĭ‐nāng‐līk shīng‐waī‐pei‐meī, neĭ‐kè kung to‐peî‐ts‘eúng‐kipₒ‐kè.
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Hó‐ts‘z̆ mūk‐yān yaū taaî‐sz‐haú‐chung, kaù‐tuēt‐leŭng‐chekₒ‐keukₒ, waāk yat‐faaì‐ĭ; Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè tsź‐suen, haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐ch‘uè, ts‘ŏh‐shuî‐keí‐kokₒ‐t‘aū, waāk ch‘ōng‐sheûng sz‐tsô‐kè‐yūk‐tsź, pit‐chiù‐kóm‐yeûng tak‐kaù‐t‘uetₒ‐kè.
13 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsik‐haî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì wâ, Neĭ‐teî‐iù‐t‘eng, yaû‐tuì‐chuê‐Ngă‐kokₒ‐kè ka tsô‐kin‐chìng la;
14 Yan‐waî ngŏh‐tuk‐chaakₒ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè tsuî‐okₒ kòh‐yāt, to‐iù‐tuk‐chaakₒ Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî‐kè t‘aān: ī‐ch‘é t‘aān‐kokₒ‐to‐peî‐chaám‐lat, yaû‐titₒ‐lōk‐teî‐hâ.
15 Ngŏh to‐iù‐kung‐tá tung‐t‘in‐chi‐uk, t‘ūng‐maaī hâ‐t‘in‐chi‐uk; tseûng‐ngā‐kè uk iù‐kìn‐paaî‐waaî, ī‐ch‘é taaî‐uk to‐ooĭ‐līng‐lōk, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî‐kóm‐wâ lokₒ.
