1 IÙ tsoî Sun‐shēng ch‘ui‐hô‐t‘ūng, yaû tsoî‐ngŏh‐kè shìng‐shaan fòng‐chàn‐king‐kè sheng: iù‐kíng‐teî‐kè chùng‐kui‐mān chàn‐tûng: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yāt‐tsź‐laī‐kân, yaû tseung‐kân‐tò lokₒ;
2 Haî‐hak‐yê kûng yau‐òm‐chi‐yāt, haî‐māt‐wān‐kûng‐sham‐hak‐kè yāt, hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘in‐mung‐kwong faatₒ‐tsoî‐shaan‐sheûng: yaŭ taaî‐uĕ k‘āp k‘eūng‐chòng‐kè mān; līk‐loī meî‐ts‘āng‐yaŭ‐yān ts‘z̆‐k‘uĭ‐kè, haû‐loī yīk‐mŏ‐kóm‐yeûng, chì‐tò taaî‐toh shaì‐toî‐kè nīn lokₒ.
3 Tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐ts‘īn‐mîn yaŭ‐fóh t‘an‐mīt; yaû tsoî‐haû‐mîn yaŭ‐fóh‐shiu‐waaî: tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐ts‘īn‐mîn kíng‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆ Oi‐t‘īn‐uēn kóm, yaû tsoî‐haû‐mîn hó‐ts‘z̆‐fong‐faì‐kè kw‘òng‐yĕ: ī‐ch‘é mŏ‐yān‐tóh‐peî‐k‘uĭ‐teî.
4 K‘uĭ‐teî‐yīng‐yeûng hó‐ts‘z̆‐mă‐kè yīng‐yeûng; yaû‐hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘ē‐mă‐kè yān, kóm‐yeûng‐pan‐tsaú.
5 K‘uĭ‐teî t‘iù‐heí‐kè sheng, hó‐ts‘z̆ mă‐ch‘e‐tsoî‐shaan‐lĭng‐chi‐sheûng‐kè sheng, hó‐ts‘z̆‐fóh‐îm‐t‘an‐mīt‐ts‘ó‐kan‐kè sheng, yaû‐hó‐ts‘z̆‐k‘eūng‐līk‐kè tsź‐mān lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè sheng.
6 Tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn līt‐mān‐chìn‐lūt: chùng‐mîn pìn‐hiu‐ts‘eng‐shik.
7 K‘uĭ‐teî hó‐ts‘z̆‐yŭng‐sẑ kóm‐yeûng pan‐tsaú; yaû‐hó‐ts‘z̆ mŏ‐sẑ p‘ā‐sheŭng‐shēng‐oōn; kokₒ‐hāng‐keí‐lô, kokₒ‐heùng‐tuî‐nğ, yaû‐mŏ‐tsaú‐luên:
8 Yaû mŏ‐peí‐ts‘ź seung‐úng; kokₒ‐heùng‐keí‐lô‐laī‐hāng: p‘ùng‐cheūk‐kwan‐heì, to‐mŏ‐chuèn‐hāng‐pīt‐lô.
9 K‘uĭ‐teî t‘iù‐pan‐shēng‐sheûng; tsaú‐sheŭng‐shēng‐oōn, p‘ā‐sheŭng‐uk‐shè; haí‐ch‘eung‐haú‐yāp‐huì, hó‐ts‘z̆‐ts‘aāk kóm.
10 Tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐ts‘īn‐t‘ō teî‐chàn; t‘in‐to‐chàn‐tûng: yāt‐uēt‐pìn‐hak, sing‐suk mŏ‐kwong:
11 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haí k‘uĭ kwan‐ping ts‘īn‐t‘ō, faatₒ‐ch‘ut‐k‘uĭ‐sheng: yan k‘uĭ yīng‐p‘oōn hó‐taaî: haî‐yaŭ‐nāng‐līk, shīng‐tsaû k‘uĭ‐shóh‐ch‘ut‐kè mîng‐lîng: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yāt‐tsź hó‐taaî, ī‐ch‘é kīk‐chi‐hóh‐p‘à‐kè; mat‐shuī hóh‐nāng‐tong‐tak ni?
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Neĭ‐teî sheûng‐ch‘é uē‐kam‐iù‐tsûn‐sam‐kàm‐shīk, laū‐luî, oi‐huk chuèn‐heùng‐ngŏh:
13 Iù‐chíng‐līt neĭ‐sam, m̄‐chíng‐līt neĭ‐i‐sheūng, chuèn‐heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì: yan‐k‘uĭ haî‐shi‐yan‐tín, yaû ch‘ung‐moŏn‐ts‘z̄‐oì‐kè, shâm‐maân‐faatₒ‐nô, taaî‐shi‐king‐sut, yaû‐waî‐tsoi‐naân‐tsẑ‐fooì.
14 Shuī‐chi k‘uĭ‐háng‐chuèn‐ì tsẑ‐fooì, yaû‐waī‐fuk‐fân tsoî‐haû; tsik‐haî k‘aaĭ‐laĭ‐māt‐kûng‐tîn‐tsaú, hìn‐sheŭng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì ni?
15 ¶ Iù‐tsoî‐Sun‐shēng ch‘ui‐hô‐t‘ūng, iù‐shìng‐pīt uê‐peî‐kàm‐shīk, iù‐chiû‐tsaāp‐shìng‐ooî là:
16 Iù tsuî‐maaī chùng‐mān, shìng‐pīt‐taaî‐ooî, chiû‐tsaāp‐cheúng‐lŏ, tsuî‐maaī‐nuên‐tsaí k‘āp‐waì‐naaĭ‐kè: iù‐san‐lōng yaū‐k‘uĭ‐shat, san‐foŏ‐yaū‐k‘uĭ‐fōng‐ch‘ut‐laī.
17 Iù‐tsaì‐sz tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè fūk‐yīk, tsoî‐lōng‐t‘aū kûng‐tsaì‐t‘aān‐chung‐kaan huk‐yap: yaû‐kóm‐wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hóh‐līn‐neĭ‐kè tsź‐mān, m̄‐hó peí‐neĭ ka‐īp shaû‐līng‐yūk, peí‐līt‐kwokₒ koón‐hāt‐k‘uĭ‐teî: waî‐mat k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî‐līt‐mān‐chi‐chung kóm wâ, K‘uĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì haí‐pin‐ch‘uè ni?
18 ¶ Kòh‐chân‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waî‐k‘uĭ‐kíng‐teî kwà‐sam, yaû‐hóh‐līn‐k‘uĭ‐kè tsź‐mān à.
19 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû‐taapₒ‐yìng‐tuì‐k‘uĭ‐tsź‐mān wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐shaí‐neĭ tak‐kuk kûng‐tsaú k‘āp‐yaū, lîng neĭ hóh‐ĭ paaú‐tsuk: ngŏh yīk‐m̄‐tsoì‐lîng neĭ‐haí‐līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung shaû‐līng‐yūk‐kè lokₒ:
20 Waī‐haî iù‐shaí pak‐fong‐kè kwan leī‐hoi‐neĭ‐hó‐uĕn, yaû kón‐chūk‐k‘uĭ yāp‐yat‐shóh‐hŏn‐kon k‘āp‐fong‐faì‐chi‐teî, k‘uĭ ts‘īn‐t‘ō‐yāp‐tung‐hoí, k‘uĭ meĭ‐haû‐yāp‐sai‐hoí, k‘uĭ‐kè ch‘aù‐heì kûng‐okₒ‐meî shing‐sheŭng, yan‐waî k‘uĭ tsô‐kwòh‐taaî‐sẑ à.
21 Kíng‐teî à, m̄‐hó‐fong; iù‐foon‐heí‐faaì‐lōk: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô‐kwòh‐taaî‐sẑ à.
22 T‘īn‐kaan‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, m̄‐hó‐fong: yan‐waî‐kw‘òng‐yĕ‐kè ts‘eng‐ts‘ó faatₒ‐shang, kwóh‐mūk yaŭ‐kitₒ‐shāt‐kè, mō‐fa‐kwóh‐shuê tak‐līk kitₒ‐kwóh là.
23 Sun‐shēng‐kè tsaí‐nuĭ à, iù‐yan‐ts‘ź‐foon‐heí, yaû‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì faaì‐lōk: yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐i‐chìng leûng ts‘z̀‐neĭ‐tak‐tsó‐uĕ, yaû shaí‐hó‐uĕ kòng‐lōk‐neĭ‐ch‘uè, tsik‐haî sin‐uĕ k‘āp‐haû‐uĕ tsoî ching‐uēt‐chi‐shī à.
24 Ch‘eūng‐teî peí‐māk‐ch‘ung‐moŏn, tsaú‐chà kûng‐yaū‐chà, moŏn‐tò‐laū‐ch‘ut kóm.
25 Wōng‐ch‘ūng, ngāt‐kan‐ch‘ūng, kaú‐mō‐ch‘ūng, ch‘ekₒ‐tô‐ch‘ūng, tsik‐haî‐ngŏh‐tá‐faatₒ yāp‐neĭ‐chi‐chung kòh‐taaî‐tuî‐kwan‐ping, shò‐nīn‐chi‐kaan shóh‐shīk‐kè, ngŏh‐to‐p‘ooī‐faan‐kwòh‐neĭ à.
26 Neĭ‐pit‐yaŭ‐toh‐shīk, yaû kìn‐tsuk‐ì, yaû tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì, tsik‐haî waî‐neĭ hāng‐k‘eī‐nāng‐kè sẑ, kòh‐waî: ngŏh‐kè tsź‐mān wĭng‐uĕn‐m̄‐kìn sau‐ch‘í lokₒ.
27 Neĭ‐teî yīk‐pit‐tak‐chi ngŏh‐haí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chi‐chung, yaû chi‐tò ngŏh‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, lîng‐ngoî mŏ‐pīt‐kòh: ngŏh‐kè tsź‐mān, wĭng‐uĕn m̄‐kìn‐sau‐ch‘í lokₒ.
28 ¶ Ts‘ź‐haû pit‐haî‐kóm, ngŏh tsaû k‘aaĭ‐ngŏh‐kè shìng‐shān shi‐peí‐maân‐yān; neĭ‐kè tsaí‐nuĭ ooĭ‐kóng‐uê‐īn, nīn‐lŏ ooĭ‐tak‐mûng‐pò; shiù‐nīn ooĭ‐kìn‐î‐tseûng:
29 Tong kòh‐ti‐yāt‐tsź, ngŏh‐iù k‘aaĭ‐ngŏh Shìng‐Shān shi‐peí‐pūk‐p‘eĭ.
30 Ngŏh yaû‐tseung‐k‘eī‐sẑ hín‐în‐tsoî‐t‘in‐sheûng, î‐tsik tsoî‐teî‐hâ, yaŭ‐huetₒ, yaŭ‐fóh, kûng‐wān‐in‐kè ch‘uĕ.
31 Tong Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kīk‐taaî‐hóh‐p‘à‐chi‐yāt, meî‐tò‐chi‐sin, yāt‐t‘aū ooĭ‐pìn‐hak‐òm, uēt‐kwong ooĭ‐shīng‐huetₒ.
32 Tseung‐pit‐haî‐kóm, taân‐faān‐pán‐k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mēng, shî‐pit tak‐kaù‐t‘uetₒ: yan‐waî chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐wâ, tsoî Sun‐shaan yaû‐tsoî‐Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, pit‐yaŭ‐t‘ō‐tsaú‐kè yān, tsoî‐uē‐shîng‐kè yān‐chi‐chung, yīk‐yaŭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā suén‐chiû‐kè à.
