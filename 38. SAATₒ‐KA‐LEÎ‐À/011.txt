1 LEÎ‐PA‐NUÊN à, iù‐hoi‐neĭ‐kè moōn, táng fóh hóh‐ĭ t‘an‐mīt neĭ‐kè paakₒ‐heung‐shuê.
2 Ts‘ūng‐shuê à, iù‐kiù‐foó lokₒ; yan‐waî paakₒ‐heung‐shuê k‘ing‐titₒ; wā‐meĭ‐kè to‐kìn paaî‐waaî: Pa‐shaan chùng‐tseûng‐shuê à, iù‐hō‐foó; yan‐waî kin‐koò‐kè shuê‐lām, ĭ‐king titₒ‐lōk‐lokₒ.
3 Yaŭ‐mūk‐yān hō‐foó‐kè sheng; yan‐k‘uĭ‐wīng‐kwong kìn‐paaî‐waaî: yaû‐siú‐sz‐p‘aaū‐haau‐kè sheng; yīk yan Yeukₒ‐taân‐kè wā‐meĭ kìn‐paaî‐waaî à.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ngŏh‐kè Sheûng‐Taì haî‐kóm‐wâ; Iù‐yeŭng tseung‐shaatₒ‐kè yeūng‐kw‘ān lokₒ;
5 Maaĭ‐chué shaatₒ‐k‘uĭ‐teî, to‐ĭ‐waī tsẑ‐keí mŏ‐tsuî: maaî‐chué tsaû wâ, Tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; yan ngŏh tak‐ts‘oī‐paāk‐kè: tsẑ‐keí‐kè mūk‐yān, sheûng‐ch‘é m̄‐hóh‐līn k‘uĭ‐teî.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh m̄‐tsoì king‐sut kíng‐noî‐kè kui‐mān: t‘aí‐a, ngŏh iù‐k‘aaĭ chùng‐yān kokₒ‐kaan‐kwòh lūn‐shè‐kè shaú, yaû kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐wōng‐kè shaú: k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû kung‐tá kíng‐teî, ngŏh to‐m̄‐kaù tsź‐mān t‘uetₒ‐leī k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shaú à.
7 Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh‐yeŭng tseung‐shaatₒ‐kè yeūng‐kw‘ān, chan‐chìng haî‐kw‘ān‐chung chì‐pei‐meī‐kè. Ngŏh tsaû cha‐chuê leŭng‐t‘iū kwaaí‐cheûng; yat‐t‘iū ngŏh‐ch‘ing‐waī Meĭ‐yan, yat‐t‘iū ngŏh‐ch‘ing‐waī Shīng‐sokₒ; ngŏh tsaû yeŭng kòh‐kw‘ān‐yeūng.
8 Tsoî yat‐uēt‐chi‐kaan, ngŏh tsaû‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt kóh‐saam‐kòh mūk‐yān; yan‐waî ngŏh sam‐shān ìm‐heì‐k‘uĭ‐teî; ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè sam‐shān tsang‐ìm‐ngŏh lokₒ.
9 Ue‐shî ngŏh wâ, Ngŏh m̄‐yeŭng neĭ pòh: kòh‐ti‐ooĭ‐sź‐kè, yaū‐tak‐k‘uĭ sź‐lokₒ; kòh‐ti‐peî‐tsuēt‐kè, yaū‐tak‐k‘uĭ‐tsuēt‐lokₒ; kòh‐ti‐laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè, iù‐k‘uĭ peí‐ts‘ź‐seung‐shīk t‘ūng‐poôn‐kè yūk.
10 Ngŏh ts‘uí‐ngŏh‐chi‐kwaaí‐cheûng ch‘ing‐waī Meĭ‐yan‐kè, tsaû chaám‐t‘uĕn‐hiu, táng ngŏh hóh‐ĭ chiù‐yeûng chíng‐waaî‐ngŏh kûng‐chùng‐mān shóh‐laāp‐kè māng‐yeukₒ.
11 Yaû‐haî tong‐kòh‐yāt chíng‐waaî kè: kóm‐tsaû kw‘ān‐chung‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng ngŏh chì‐pei‐meī‐kè, chi‐tò shóh‐kóng‐kè, haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ.
12 Ngŏh tsaû tuì‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐wâ, Uē‐kwóh neĭ‐ĭ‐waī‐hó, tsaû peí‐ngŏh kung‐kà; uē‐kwóh m̄‐haî, tsaû m̄‐shaí‐peí. Kóm‐tsaû k‘uĭ‐teî waî‐ngŏh kung‐kà, p‘īng‐tuî‐ngān‐saam‐shāp‐faaì.
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì‐ngŏh‐wâ, Iù‐k‘aaĭ‐laī wing‐lōk peí‐t‘ō‐yān: tsik‐haî chùng‐yān shóh‐koó‐ngŏh‐kè hó‐kà‐chīk. Ngŏh tsaû ning‐kòh‐saam‐shāp‐faaì‐ngān, haí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐t‘ōng wing‐lōk peí‐kwòh‐t‘ō‐yān à.
14 Ue‐shî ngŏh ning taî‐î‐t‘iū kwaaí‐cheûng, tsik‐haî ch‘ing‐waī Shīng‐sokₒ‐kè chaám‐t‘uĕn‐hiu, táng‐hóh‐ĭ chiù‐yeûng chíng‐waaî Yaū‐t‘aaì kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt shóh‐laāp hing‐taî‐kè wōh‐yeukₒ.
15 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû tuì‐ngŏh‐wâ, Iù neĭ tsoì‐yat‐cheùng ning‐uē‐ch‘ún‐mūk‐yān‐kè heì‐kuî.
16 Yan ngŏh pit‐tsoî‐kíng‐noî hing‐heí yat‐kòh‐mūk‐yān, k‘uĭ‐pit‐m̄‐t‘aàm‐ch‘aatₒ kòh‐ti‐peî‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐kè, m̄‐chaaú‐ts‘ām kòh‐ti saàn‐hoi‐kè, m̄‐i‐hó kòh‐ti tá‐suén‐kè, yaû m̄‐yeŭng‐yūk kòh‐ti‐chòng‐kîn‐kè: waī‐haî iù‐shīk feī‐meĭ‐ché‐chi‐yūk, yaû maakₒ‐līt k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè t‘aī.
17 Waī‐heì k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè kw‘ān, mŏ‐yûng‐kè mūk‐yān yaŭ‐wôh‐lokₒ! pá‐kìm pit‐kòng‐lōk k‘uĭ‐shaú‐peì, k‘āp k‘uĭ yaû‐ngaăn‐chi‐sheûng: k‘uĭ shaú‐peì ooĭ‐tsûn kon‐foo, k‘uĭ‐yaû‐ngaăn to ooĭ‐maāng‐saaì lokₒ.
