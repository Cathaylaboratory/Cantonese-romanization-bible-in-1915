1 Ĭ‐LEÎ‐SHA wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Mīng‐yāt ni‐chân shī‐haû, tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à shēng‐moōn‐haú, saì‐mîn yat saì‐à chīk ngān yat shī‐haakₒ, taaî‐māk î‐saì‐à, chīk ngān yat shī‐haakₒ,
2 Foō‐chuê wōng‐kè tseung‐kwan, taapₒ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān wâ, T‘aí‐a, uē‐kwóh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaí t‘in hoi‐ch‘eung, heí‐yaŭ ni‐ti‐sẑ me? Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Neĭ ts‘an‐ngaăn pit kìn, taân m̄‐tak‐shīk.
3 ¶ Yāp shēng‐moōn‐chi‐ch‘uè, yaŭ sz̀‐kòh fung‐tsāt‐kè yān: peí‐ts‘ź seung‐kóng‐wâ, Ngŏh‐teî tsô‐mat ts‘ŏh‐tó ni‐ch‘uè táng‐sź ni?
4 Yeūk ngŏh‐teî wâ, Iù yāp shēng chì‐cheūk, shēng‐luĭ yaŭ kei‐fong, ngŏh‐teî pit tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè sź: yeūk‐īn ts‘ŏh ni‐ch‘uè, tsaû haî sź‐che. Pat‐uē huì t‘aū‐hōng À‐laān‐chi‐kwan: k‘uĭ‐teî yeūk háng shang‐ts‘uēn ngŏh‐teî, kóm‐tsaû tak‐shang; yeūk‐shaatₒ ngŏh‐teî, tsaû sź haî le.
5 Yāp haak shī‐haû, k‘uĭ‐teî heí‐ch‘īng‐huì À‐laān‐yān‐kè yīng: tò‐hiu À‐laān‐yīng‐kè ngoî‐kíng, t‘aí‐a, mŏ yat‐kòh‐yān haí‐ch‘uè à.
6 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng shaí À‐laān‐yān‐kè kwan‐luĭ, t‘eng‐mān ch‘e‐mă‐sheng, hó‐ts‘z̆ taaî‐kwan‐kè sheng kóm: À‐laān‐yān tsaû peí‐ts‘ź wâ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng pit fooí‐maaĭ Haak‐yān‐kè līt‐wōng, kûng Oi‐k‘āp‐kè līt‐wōng, k‘ui laī kung‐tá ngŏh‐teî pòh.
7 Chikₒ‐koón yāp haak‐shī, chùng‐yān tsaû heí‐laī t‘ō‐tsaú, tiu‐heì cheùng‐mōk, mă, lōh, ts‘uēn‐yīng‐sheûng‐tsoî, chikₒ‐koò t‘ō‐tsaú pó‐mîng.
8 Fung‐tsāt‐kè tò‐hiu yīng‐kè ngoî‐kíng, tsaû yāp yat‐tsôh cheùng‐mōk, yaû‐yám yaû‐shīk, ī‐ch‘é ts‘ūng kóh‐ch‘uè ning‐ch‘ut kam, ngān, i‐fūk, laī‐huì shau‐ts‘ōng; faan‐laī, yaû yāp yat‐tsôh cheùng‐mōk, yaû tak ts‘oī‐māt ning‐huì shau‐ts‘ōng.
9 Ue‐shî seung‐kóng‐wâ, Ngŏh‐teî shóh‐tsô‐kè m̄‐cheūk: kam‐yāt haî yaŭ hó‐yam‐kè yāt‐tsź, ngŏh‐teî m̄‐ch‘ut‐sheng: yeūk táng‐tò t‘in‐kwong, pit shaû yīng‐fāt: shóh‐ĭ iù tsik‐shī huì pò‐chi wōng‐kè ka.
10 Kóm‐tsaû huì kiù shaú‐shēng‐kè koon: tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ngŏh‐teî ĭ‐king yāp À‐laān‐yān‐kè yīng, mŏ‐yān haí‐ch‘uè, yīk mŏ‐yān sheng, tūk‐yaŭ mă kûng lōh póng‐chuê, yīng‐mōk k‘ui chiù‐kaû.
11 Shaú‐shēng‐kè koon tsaû kiù chùng‐shaú‐moōn‐kè yān laī; k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû tsoî shēng‐luĭ pò‐chi wōng‐ka.
12 Wōng laû‐yê heí‐shan tuì shān‐pūk wâ, À‐laān‐yān heùng ngŏh‐teî shóh‐tsô‐kè, ngŏh chí‐shî neĭ‐teî chi. Yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî chi‐tò ngŏh‐teî kei‐ngôh; koò‐ts‘ź leī‐yīng, tsoî t‘īn‐yē maaī‐fūk, sam‐ì wâ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt ch‘ut‐shēng chi‐shī, ngŏh‐teî hóh‐ĭ shang‐k‘ām k‘uĭ‐teî, ī‐ch‘é tak yāp shēng à.
13 Yaŭ yat‐kòh shān‐pūk taapₒ wâ, Yaŭ sheûng‐ts‘uēn‐kè mă, shēng‐luĭ shîng‐hâ‐kè, iù tseung nğ‐p‘at mă tá‐faatₒ yān huì kw‘ai‐t‘aàm, tsaû chi‐tò chan‐ts‘īng, (t‘aí‐a, mă hó‐ts‘z̆ shēng shóh‐shîng‐hâ‐kè Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yat‐yeûng: t‘aí‐a, mă hó‐ts‘z̆ Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐mīt‐tsuēt yat‐yeûng:)
14 Koò‐ts‘ź ts‘uí‐hiu leŭng‐leŭng‐ch‘e kûng mă laī; wōng tá‐faatₒ yān huì chui‐ts‘ām À‐laān‐yān‐kè kwan wâ, Iù huì kw‘ai‐t‘aàm la.
15 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû chui‐ts‘ām tò Yeukₒ‐taân: t‘aí‐kìn moŏn‐lô to‐yaŭ À‐laān‐yān kap‐tsaú‐shī shóh‐heì‐kè i‐fūk kwan‐heì. Sz̀‐ché faan‐laī pò‐chi wōng.
16 Paakₒ‐sìng tsaû ch‘ut‐huì lŏ‐leūk À‐laān‐yān‐kè yīng‐p‘oōn. Kóm‐tsaû saì‐mîn yat saì‐à, chīk ngān yat shī‐haakₒ, taaî‐māk î‐saì‐à chīk ngān yat shī‐haakₒ, chiù‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐kè.
17 Wōng tsaû p‘aaì foō‐wōng‐kè tseung‐kwan hon‐shaú shēng‐moōn: chùng‐yān tsoî shēng‐moōn ch‘aaí‐sź k‘uĭ, chiù‐i Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān shóh‐wâ.
18 Tsik‐tsoî wōng lōk‐laī t‘ūng k‘uĭ shóh‐kóng‐chi‐shī, ts‘ź‐sẑ shīng‐tsaû, haî chiù‐i Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān ts‘āng tuì wōng wâ, Mīng‐yāt ni‐chân‐shī, tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐kè shēng‐moōn‐haú, taaî‐māk î saì‐à, chīk ngān yat shī‐haakₒ, saì‐mîn yat saì‐à, chīk ngān yat shī‐haakₒ:
19 Tsaû kóh‐tseung‐kwan tuì Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān wâ, Uē‐kwóh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaí t‘in hoi‐ch‘eung, heí‐yaŭ ni‐ti‐sẑ me? Sheûng‐Taì‐kè yān wâ, T‘aí‐a, neĭ pit ts‘an‐ngaăn t‘aí‐kìn, taân m̄‐tak‐shīk.
20 Ni‐ti shuetₒ‐wâ, uē‐kam kwóh‐īn yìng‐îm: yan‐waî paakₒ‐sìng tsoî shēng‐moōn‐haú, tseung k‘uĭ ch‘aaí‐sź à.
