1 YAŪ‐TAAÎ wōng À‐hap‐tsê‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hāt taî‐î‐shāp‐î nīn, Yē‐oô‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz, haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à tang‐waî tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng, tsoî‐waî shāp‐ts‘at nīn.
2 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ, hōk‐faan Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè tsuî, shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haâm‐lōk tsuî; tsúng mŏ leī‐hoi ts‘ź‐tsuî.
3 Koò‐ts‘ź Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān faatₒ‐nô, shī‐sheūng kaau‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐kwòh À‐laān wōng Hap‐sitₒ‐kè shaú, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Pîn‐hap‐taāt‐kè shaú.
4 Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz tsaû k‘eī‐k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shuī‐t‘eng k‘uĭ: yan‐waî kìn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shóh‐shaû‐kè foó‐pik, tsik‐haî À‐laān wōng foó‐pik k‘uĭ‐teî.
5 (Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû ts‘z̀ yat‐kòh kaù‐ché kwòh‐Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, shaí k‘uĭ‐teî t‘uetₒ‐lat À‐laān‐yān‐kè shaú: kóm‐tsaû Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haí cheùng‐mōk kui‐chuê, hó‐ts‘z̆ kaû‐shī yat‐yeûng.
6 Taân k‘uĭ‐teî mŏ leī‐hoi Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm ka‐kè tsuî, tsik‐haî haâm Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān lōk‐tsuî, yīng‐īn hāng‐ts‘ź tsuî: pìng‐tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à sheûng‐yaŭ mūk‐ch‘uĕ tseûng laū‐ts‘uēn.)
7 À‐laān wōng tsiú‐mīt Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz‐kè mān, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî hó‐ts‘z̆ ch‘aaí‐wōh‐kè sha‐ch‘ān; tūk‐haî laū‐hâ mă‐ping nğ‐shāp, ch‘e shāp leŭng, pô‐ping yat‐maân kwòh Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz.
8 Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, k‘āp k‘uĭ yŭng‐līk, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
9 Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám; tsòng‐tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hāt, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
10 ¶ Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt taî‐saam‐shāp‐ts‘at nīn, Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hāt, haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à tang‐waî tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng, tsoî‐waî shāp‐lūk nīn.
11 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ; hōk‐faan Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè tsuî, shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haâm‐lōk tsuî: mŏ‐leī‐hoi ts‘ź‐tsuî.
12 Yeukₒ‐hāt k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, k‘āp k‘uĭ yŭng‐līk, kung‐tá Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐mă‐tsê chaang‐chìn‐kè sẑ, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
13 Yeukₒ‐hāt kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám; Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm tsaû ts‘ŏh k‘uĭ‐kè waî: Yeukₒ‐hāt kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng t‘ūng tsòng‐tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à.
14 ¶ Ĭ‐leî‐sha waân pêng, tsik‐haî tseung‐sź‐kè pêng. Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt lōk‐huì kìn k‘uĭ, tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn huk‐yap, wâ, Ngŏh foô à, ngŏh foô à; Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè leŭng‐ch‘e kûng mă‐ping à.
15 Ĭ‐leî‐sha tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ ning‐kung‐tsìn‐laī. Wōng tsaû ning‐kung‐tsìn‐laī.
16 Yaû tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng wâ, Iù yûng shaú cha‐kung. K‘uĭ tsaû yûng shaú cha‐kung: Ĭ‐leî‐sha tseung shaú òn‐tsoî wōng‐kè shaú‐sheûng.
17 Wâ, Neĭ hoi tung‐pîn ch‘eung. K‘uĭ tsaû hoi‐hiu. Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Shê‐tsìn la. K‘uĭ tsaû shê‐tsìn. Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Ni‐ti haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tá‐shìng‐cheùng‐kè tsìn, tsik‐haî tá‐shìng À‐laān‐yān: yan neĭ pit haí À‐fat kung‐tá À‐laān‐yān, chì‐tò tsiú‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî.
18 Ĭ‐leî‐sha yaû wâ, Tsoì ning‐tsìn‐laī. Tsaû ning‐laī. Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Iù kik‐teî. K‘uĭ kik‐teî saam‐ts‘z̀ tsaû hitₒ‐chuê.
19 Sheûng‐Taì‐kè‐yān heùng k‘uĭ faatₒ‐nô wâ, Iù ying‐koi kik‐tò nğ‐lūk‐ts‘z̀; tsaû nāng‐kik À‐laān‐yān, chì‐mīt‐tsuēt; taân uē‐kam kán‐nāng‐kik À‐laān‐yān saam‐ts‘z̀ che.
20 ¶ Ĭ‐leî‐sha tsaû sź, chùng‐yān tseung k‘uĭ maaī‐tsòng. Kwòh‐nīn‐chi‐shī, Moh‐aapₒ‐kè ping yāp kíng.
21 Mān‐kaan yaŭ sź‐yān, tseung tsòng chi‐shī, fat kìn kw‘ān‐tīk laī‐tò; tsaû k‘aaĭ kòh‐shi wing‐lōk Ĭ‐leî‐sha mô‐chung: tìm‐cheūk Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐kè hoī‐kwat, sź‐yān tsaû fūk‐shang, heí‐shan k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè.
22 ¶ Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz wōng nīn‐kaan, À‐laān wōng Hap‐sitₒ, shī‐sheūng foó‐pik Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
23 Taân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yan k‘uĭ kûng À‐paakₒ‐la‐hón, Ĭ‐saatₒ, Ngă‐kokₒ, shóh‐laāp‐kè yeukₒ, yīng‐īn shi‐yan kwòh‐Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, līn‐sut k‘uĭ‐teî, kuèn‐koò k‘uĭ‐teî, pat‐yán mīt k‘uĭ‐teî, to chûng m̄‐kón‐chūk k‘uĭ‐teî leī‐hoi tsẑ‐keí mîn‐ts‘īn.
24 À‐laān wōng Hap‐sitₒ sź‐hiu; k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Pîn‐hap‐taāt, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
25 Sik yāt Hap‐sitₒ t‘ūng Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè foô Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz chaang‐chìn, ts‘āng tuēt‐hiu shēng‐yap. Uē‐kam Yeukₒ‐hap‐sz‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hāt, saam‐ts‘z̀ kung‐kik Hap‐sitₒ‐kè tsaí Pîn‐hap‐taāt, tak‐faan Ĭ‐shik‐līt kokₒ‐shēng.
