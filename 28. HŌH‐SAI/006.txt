1 LAĪ‐LA, ngŏh‐teî iù‐faan‐ooī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ch‘uè: k‘uĭ ts‘āng maakₒ‐laân, yaû ooĭ‐i‐hó‐ngŏh‐teî; k‘uĭ ts‘āng tá‐sheung, yaû ooĭ‐chaatₒ‐hó‐ngŏh‐teî‐kè.
2 Kwòh‐leŭng‐yāt k‘uĭ pit‐fūk‐so‐ngŏh‐teî: tò‐saam‐yāt k‘uĭ ooĭ‐hing‐heí ngŏh‐teî, táng‐tò‐ngŏh‐teî tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn ooĭ‐shang lokₒ.
3 Ngŏh iù‐shik‐tak Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yaû iù‐chui‐k‘aū‐shik‐k‘uĭ: k‘uĭ‐ch‘ut‐yaū‐chi‐k‘eī, haî‐chiu‐tsó‐shī‐haû kòm‐wán‐chân; k‘uĭ pit‐hó‐ts‘z̆ uĕ‐kòng‐lām ngŏh‐ch‘uè, hó‐ts‘z̆ meĭ‐haû‐kè uĕ, tsz‐yûn‐teî‐t‘ó kóm‐yeûng.
4 ¶ Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn à, ngŏh iù‐heùng‐neĭ‐tím‐tsô ni? Yaū‐t‘aaì à, ngŏh iù‐heùng‐neĭ‐tím‐tsô ni? yan‐waî neĭ‐kè yān‐ts‘z̄, hó‐ts‘z̆‐chiu‐tsó‐kè wān, yaû hó‐ts‘z̆‐lô‐shuí, shān‐tsó‐tsaû‐saàn.
5 Shóh‐ĭ ngŏh k‘aaĭ sin‐chi seukₒ‐waaî‐k‘uĭ‐teî; k‘aaĭ‐ngŏh‐haú‐kè īn‐uĕ shaatₒ‐lūk‐k‘uĭ‐teî: neĭ shi‐shám‐p‘oòn, hó‐ts‘z̆‐kwong‐iû faatₒ‐ch‘ut‐kóm.
6 Yan‐waî ngŏh seúng‐king‐sut, m̄‐seúng tsaì‐tsẑ; yaû seúng‐shik Sheûng‐Taì‐kè chì‐shik, shìng‐kwòh‐faān‐tsaì.
7 Waī‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî‐hó‐ts‘z̆ À‐tong kóm‐yeûng faân‐yeukₒ: yaû heùng‐ngŏh yûng‐kwaí‐mă‐hāng‐sẑ à.
8 Kei‐līt haî hāng okₒ‐yān‐kè shēng‐yap, peî‐huetₒ‐ĭm‐oo‐kè.
9 Tô‐ts‘aāk‐shīng‐kw‘ān, maū‐fūk hoî‐yān, shīng‐tuî‐tsaì‐sz to‐kóm‐yeûng: haí Shî‐kìm‐kè lô‐sheûng shaatₒ‐yān, yaû hāng‐kaan‐yām.
10 Tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè‐ka, ngŏh kìn‐kwòh‐hóh‐oò‐king‐hoī‐kè sẑ: tsik‐haî tsoî Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn yaŭ‐hāng‐yām‐kè sẑ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt to‐peî‐tìm‐oo‐kè.
11 Yaū‐t‘aaì à, tò‐kòh‐chân ngŏh‐lîng‐ngŏh‐tsź‐mān peî‐lŏ‐leūk, faan‐ooī‐chi‐shī, tsaû‐yaŭ‐shau‐shīng, waî neĭ‐tîng‐tuēt‐kè lokₒ.
