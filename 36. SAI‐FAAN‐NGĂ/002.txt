1 MŎ‐SAU‐CH‘Í‐KÈ kwokₒ à, neĭ‐iù tsuî‐maaī tsẑ‐keí, shāt‐tsoî iù‐tsuî‐maaī là;
2 Mîng‐lîng meî‐faatₒ‐ch‘ut‐chi‐sin, kòh‐yāt hó‐ts‘z̆‐hong kòm‐faaì‐kwòh, tong‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā līt‐nô‐meî‐kòng, nau‐nô‐meî‐lām‐chi‐ts‘īn, tsaû‐iù‐tsuî‐maaī là.
3 Teî‐sheûng wan‐yaū‐kè yān à, tsik‐haî tsô‐ch‘ut k‘uĭ shám‐p‘oòn‐chi‐sẑ, iù‐taaî‐chùng ts‘ām‐k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; iù‐k‘aū‐kung‐î, iù‐k‘aū‐wan‐yaū; waāk‐ché tong Yē‐Wōh‐Wā nau‐nô‐kè yāt, neĭ hóh‐ĭ yán‐ts‘ōng‐maaī.
4 Yan‐waî Ka‐saatₒ pit‐kìn waī‐heì, À‐shāt‐kei‐lūn, pìn‐waī fong‐faì; k‘uĭ‐teî aàn‐chaù‐kòh‐shī, iù‐kón‐ch‘ut À‐shāt‐tāt, Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn, līn‐kan‐k‘eŭng to‐iù‐pāt‐heí‐kè.
5 Chuê‐hoí‐pin‐kè kui‐mān, tsik‐haî K‘eī‐leĭ‐yān‐kè kwokₒ, iù‐shaû‐wôh‐lokₒ! Ka‐naām à, tsik‐haî Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè kíng‐teî, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kung‐kik neĭ à; ngŏh pit‐tsiú‐mít‐neĭ, táng‐mŏ‐yat‐kòh kui‐chuê‐tsoî‐noî.
6 Hoí‐pin‐kè‐teî pit‐shīng‐waī ts‘ó‐t‘īn, waī‐mūk‐ché‐kè maaū‐uk, waī yeūng‐kw‘ān‐kè laān.
7 Kíng‐kaaì‐chi‐teî, pit‐waī Yaū‐t‘aaì‐ka shóh‐uē‐shîng‐chi‐yān laī‐yûng; k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî‐ts‘ź waì‐k‘uĭ‐yeūng‐kw‘ān; tò‐aai‐maăn pit‐haí À‐shāt‐kei‐lūn‐kè uk‐uĕ fàn‐tó‐ch‘uè; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, iù‐t‘aàm‐ch‘aatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî, yaû lîng peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè faan‐ooī lokₒ.
8 Ngŏh ĭ‐king t‘eng‐mān Moh‐aapₒ‐kè yūk‐mâ, uĕ‐k‘āp À‐moōn‐yān‐kè heì‐siù, tsik‐haî shóh‐kóng‐laī‐yūk‐mâ ngŏh‐kè tsź‐mān, yaû tsẑ‐kw‘a‐tsẑ‐taaî, kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐kè kíng‐kaaì à.
9 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì wâ, Chiù‐ngŏh‐shang‐oōt‐kè, koò‐ts‘ź Moh‐aapₒ shīng‐waī Shóh‐toh‐mă, À‐moōn hó‐ts‘z̆ Ngōh‐moh‐la yat‐yeûng, pit‐kaai‐pìn‐waī lāk‐ts‘ó‐kè teî, yaû tsô īm‐haang‐chi‐ch‘eūng, tsaû wĭng‐uĕn fong‐faì lokₒ: yaû ngŏh tsź‐mān sheûng‐shîng‐kè, ooĭ‐lŏ‐leūk k‘uĭ‐teî, ī‐ch‘é ngŏh kwokₒ‐chung uē‐shîng‐kè, pit‐shīng‐shaû k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè teî‐īp.
10 Yan kiu‐ngô‐kè tsaû‐pit‐kóm‐yeûng‐pòh, k‘uĭ ts‘āng yūk‐mâ, yaû tsẑ‐kw‘a‐tsẑ‐taaî, kung‐kik maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tsź‐mān à.
11 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit‐heùng‐k‘uĭ‐teî tsô‐hó‐kaau‐kwaan‐kè; yan‐waî k‘uĭ lîng teî‐sheûng‐chùng‐shān‐ngôh‐waaî; ī‐ch‘é yān‐teî shūng‐paaì k‘uĭ, kokₒ‐tsoî‐tsẑ‐keí‐poón‐ch‘uè, tsik‐haî līt‐mān‐chùng‐hoí‐tó à.
12 Koó‐shāt‐kè yān à, neĭ tseung‐pit‐peî ngŏh pá‐kìm‐shaatₒ‐lūk.
13 Sheûng‐Taì ooĭ‐shan‐hoi k‘uĭ‐shaú, kung‐kik pak‐fong, yaû tsiú‐mīt À‐shūt, lîng Neī‐neī‐meī tsô‐fong‐faì‐kè, hó‐ts‘z̆ kw‘òng‐yĕ kòm‐kon.
14 Ch‘uk‐shang shīng‐kw‘ān, pit‐tsoî k‘eī‐chung fàn‐tó‐ch‘uè, līt‐kwokₒ‐kè tsaú‐shaù to‐haî; t‘ōng‐ngōh uĕ‐k‘āp tsìn‐chue, tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐ch‘uĕ‐téng hitₒ‐suk; yaû tsoî‐ch‘eung‐haú faatₒ‐sheng‐ch‘eùng‐koh; tò‐moōn‐haú to‐pit‐yaŭ‐fong‐faì; yan k‘uĭ paakₒ‐heung‐mūk paán‐kè kung‐tsokₒ, to‐ĭ‐lô‐ch‘ut.
15 Ts‘ź‐haî‐faaì‐oōt‐kè shēng‐yap, tsúng‐mŏ‐kán‐shân kui‐chuê‐kè, yaû tsoî sam‐chung wâ ngŏh‐haî‐che, ch‘uē‐ngŏh‐chi‐ngoî mŏ‐pīt‐kòh la: k‘uĭ‐chan‐chìng shīng‐waī‐fong‐faì‐chi‐teî, tsô‐tsaú‐shaù‐fàn‐tó‐chi‐ch‘uè pòh! taân‐faān king‐kwòh‐kè, tsaû‐heùng‐k‘uĭ faatₒ‐ch‘i‐sheng, yaû iū‐hă‐shaú‐lokₒ.
