1 NGŎH hoi‐sheng foo‐kiù Sheûng‐Taì; k‘uĭ shî‐pit t‘eng ngŏh.
2 Ngŏh uê‐naân‐chi‐shī k‘aū Chué: yê‐kaan pat‐hitₒ kuí‐shaú: sam‐chung pat‐kóm on‐waì.
3 Ngŏh keì‐heí Sheûng‐Taì, tsaû oi‐t‘ùng heí‐laī: sz‐nîm‐chi‐kaan, sam tsaû yau‐sheung.
4 Neĭ lîng ngŏh m̄‐fàn‐tak‐cheūk‐ngaăn: shaû‐foó m̄‐kóng‐tak‐ch‘ut‐sheng.
5 Ngŏh sz‐nîm ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn‐chi‐yāt, tsik‐haî keì‐wŏng‐chi‐nīn.
6 Ngŏh keì‐yik yê‐kaan shóh‐ch‘eùng‐chi‐shi‐koh: ngŏh tsaû sam chung òm‐seúng: ngŏh līng‐wān tsź‐saì ch‘ā‐ch‘aatₒ.
7 Chué chan wĭng‐uĕn tiu‐heì ngŏh me? heí‐yaŭ m̄‐tsoì shi yan‐tín?
8 K‘uĭ‐chi‐līn‐măn, wĭng‐uĕn chí‐sik me? ying‐huí‐chi‐shuetₒ‐wâ, heí‐yaŭ līk‐toî m̄‐kaì‐k‘āp?
9 Ts‘uēn‐Nāng‐Chi‐Chué, m̄‐keì‐tak shi‐yan me? heí‐yaŭ tseung nô‐heì laī chóh‐sak ts‘z̄‐pei?
10 Ngŏh chuèn‐nîm wâ, Ni‐kòh seúng‐t‘aū, haî ngŏh‐chi‐mō‐pêng: ying‐koi keì‐yik Chì‐Sheûng‐Chi‐Chué, shi‐chín yaû‐shaú‐chi‐nīn.
11 Ngŏh iù nîm Chué‐chi‐tsokₒ‐waī: seúng‐tò neĭ koó‐shī‐chi‐î‐tsik.
12 Ngŏh pit sam‐seúng neĭ‐chi‐shóh‐tsokₒ, sz‐nîm neĭ‐chi‐shóh‐waī.
13 Sheûng‐Taì à, neĭ hāng‐sẑ chì‐shìng: yaŭ pin‐kòh hōk‐tak‐ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì waī Ts‘uēn‐Nāng‐Ché?
14 Neĭ haî hāng‐î‐tsik‐chi‐Ts‘uēn‐Nāng‐Ché: neĭ yă‐ts‘āng tseung nāng‐līk hín‐shî līt‐pong.
15 Yûng shaú kaù‐shūk neĭ‐chi‐tsź‐mān, tsik‐haî Ngă‐kokₒ kûng Yeukₒ‐shat‐chi‐haû‐toî.
16 Sheûng‐Taì à, hoí‐shuí kìn neĭ; k‘uĭ tsaû king‐fong: sham‐uen yīk chàn‐tûng‐heí‐laī.
17 Wān hāng uĕ lōk: t‘in‐sheûng faatₒ‐sheng: neĭ tsìn‐uĕn‐shê.
18 Neĭ luī faatₒ‐sheng, sz̀‐waī kwaang‐kwaang: tîn‐shím shaì‐kaan: taaî‐teî‐iū‐tûng.
19 Neĭ chi tô‐t‘ō, tsoî taaî‐hoí‐luĭ, neĭ‐chi‐lô‐kìng, tsoî sham‐shuí‐chung, neĭ‐chi‐tsung‐tsik, pat‐hóh ch‘ak‐tōk.
20 Sik‐yāt neĭ t‘okₒ Moh‐sai uĕ‐k‘āp À‐lūn‐chi‐shaú, taaì‐chuê neĭ‐chi‐suén‐mān, hó‐ts‘z̆ yeūng‐kw‘ān yat‐yeûng.
