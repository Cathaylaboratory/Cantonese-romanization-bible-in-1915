1 UĒ‐KAM Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tong wâ, T‘óng‐yeūk m̄‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pó‐yaû ngŏh‐teî;
2 Tīk‐yān heí‐laī kung‐kik ngŏh‐teî chi‐shī, t‘óng‐yeūk m̄‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pó‐yaû ngŏh:
3 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû hó‐nau, tseung ngŏh‐teî laī‐shang‐t‘an:
4 Shuí pit‐k‘ám‐chuê ngŏh, hōh pit‐châm‐ngŏh‐chi‐līng‐wān:
5 Lông pit taaî‐heí, laī‐châm‐ngŏh‐chi‐līng‐wān.
6 Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaû yān tsûng‐chuk, yan k‘uĭ m̄‐lîng ngŏh shaû‐yān‐chi‐ngā shóh‐ngaaŭ.
7 Ngŏh‐teî t‘uetₒ‐tak‐lat shan, hó‐ts‘z̆ tseukₒ‐niŭ tsoî līp‐yān mŏng‐chung t‘uetₒ‐lat: mŏng‐lōh līt‐laân, shóh‐ĭ ngŏh‐teî tak‐t‘uetₒ.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘òng‐tsô t‘in‐teî, ngŏh‐teî í‐k‘aaù k‘uĭ mēng, pit tak‐pó‐yaû.
