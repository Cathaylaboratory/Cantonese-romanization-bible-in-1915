1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ kóng‐k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè īn‐uĕ. ¶ Ch‘aang‐hoi chùng‐t‘in, laāp‐tîng‐teî‐k‘aū‐kè kei‐chí, yaû tsokₒ‐shīng yān‐chung‐kè līng‐wān kòh‐waî, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
2 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐shaí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng heùng‐sz̀‐waī‐kè chùng mān, shīng‐waī lîng‐yān hāng m̄‐tak‐wán‐kè pooi, yaû‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng shaû‐waī‐kw‘àn kung‐kik kòh‐shī, Yaū‐t‘aaì to uê‐cheūk kóm‐yeûng.
3 Tong‐kòh‐yāt shīng‐tsaû haî kóm, ngŏh pit‐shaí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng waî‐chùng‐mān tsô‐ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè shēk: taân‐faān lóh‐laī taam‐foô‐kè, pit‐shaû‐sheung hó kaau‐kwaan, teî‐sheûng maân kwokₒ, yīk‐pit‐tsuî‐maaī yat‐ts‘aī kung‐kik k‘uĭ à.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Tong‐kòh yāt ngŏh‐pit kung‐kik kokₒ‐mă, shaí‐k‘uĭ king‐luên, yaû shaí k‘ē‐mă‐kè yān faatₒ‐tin: ngŏh yīk‐heùng‐Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè ka hoi‐ngŏh‐ngaăn, yaû kung‐kik chùng‐mān‐kè mă, shaí chekₒ‐chekₒ maāng‐saaì.
5 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè t‘aū‐mūk tsoî‐sam‐chung ooĭ‐wâ, Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān, í‐laaî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, tsaû haî ngŏh‐chi‐nāng‐līk.
6 Tong‐kòh‐yāt ngŏh‐ooĭ‐shaí Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè t‘aū‐mūk, shīng‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ōng‐fóh‐kè kwoh tsoî‐ch‘aaī‐chung; yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ fóh‐pá tsoî wōh‐pá‐chi‐chung; k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî‐yaû‐shaú yaû tsoî‐tsóh‐shaú, pit‐ooĭ‐t‘an‐mīt sz̀‐waī‐kè chùng‐mān; ī‐ch‘é Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng haí k‘uĭ poón‐t‘ó‐ch‘uè tsoì‐kui‐chuê, tsik‐haî tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐ch‘uè à.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit‐sin ch‘íng‐kaù Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè cheùng‐mōk, táng‐tò Taaî‐waî‐ka‐kè wīng‐kwong, uĕ‐k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng kui‐mān‐kè wīng‐kwong, shīng‐waī haî‐taaî, taân‐m̄‐tak‐shìng‐kwòh Yaū‐t‘aaì kè.
8 Tong‐kòh‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ooĭ‐pó‐oô Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng kui‐mān; tò‐kòh‐yāt haí‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung, faān‐suèn‐haî uĕn‐yeūk‐kè, to‐shīng‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ Taaî‐waî kóm; ī‐ch‘é Taaî‐waî‐kè ka to‐shīng‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ Sheûng‐Taì, yaû‐hó‐ts‘z̆ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sz̀‐ché tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐teî mîn‐ts‘īn kóm.
9 Tong‐kòh‐yāt shīng‐tsaû haî‐kóm, ngŏh‐pit‐mĭn‐līk tsiú‐mīt‐saaì kòh‐ti‐laī kung‐kik Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kwokₒ.
10 Ngŏh‐pit‐k‘aaĭ yan‐ch‘úng kûng pán‐k‘aū‐kè shān kòng‐lōk, kóm‐tûng Taaî‐waî‐kè ka, uĕ‐k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān; k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐mông‐heùng ngŏh, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐kat‐ché, yaû waî‐k‘uĭ‐oi‐huk, hó‐ts‘z̆‐waî‐tūk‐tsaí‐oi‐huk yat‐yeûng, yaû‐waî‐k‘uĭ‐foó‐sam yau‐shaū, hó‐ts‘z̆ waî‐ch‘oh‐shang‐tsaí yau‐shaū yat‐yeûng.
11 Tong‐kòh‐yāt tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng pit‐yaŭ taaî‐oi‐huk‐kè sẑ, hó‐ts‘z̆ Hap‐t‘aatₒ‐lām‐moōn tsoî Maĭ‐kat‐toh‐kuk‐chung‐kè oi‐huk yat‐yeûng.
12 Kíng‐noî to‐ooĭ‐oi‐huk, kokₒ‐ka‐tūk‐fan‐pīt; shūk Taaî‐waî‐ka‐kè chùng‐yān yīk tsẑ‐fan‐pīt, k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘ai‐tsź tsẑ‐fan‐pīt; shūk Nā‐taan‐ka‐kè chùng‐yān tsẑ‐fan‐pīt, k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘ai‐tsź to‐haî kóm‐yeûng;
13 Shūk Leî‐meî‐yān tsẑ‐pīt, k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘ai‐tsź to‐haî; shūk Shî‐mooĭ‐ka‐kè yān tsẑ‐pīt, k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘ai‐tsź to haî;
14 K‘eī‐uē shóh‐yaŭ‐kè ka tsẑ‐pīt, k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘ai‐tsź to‐haî yat‐yeûng lokₒ.
