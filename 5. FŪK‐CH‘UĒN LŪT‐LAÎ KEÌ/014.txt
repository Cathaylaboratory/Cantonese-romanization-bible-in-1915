1 NEĬ‐TEÎ haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè tsaí‐nuĭ: pat‐hóh waî sź‐yān kotₒ tsẑ‐keí shan‐t‘aí, pat‐hóh t‘aì‐huì ngaăn‐meī chung‐kaan‐kè mō.
2 Yan‐waî neĭ haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shìng‐mān, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘ūng shaì‐sheûng maân‐kwokₒ chi‐chung, kaán‐suén neĭ tsô k‘uĭ‐kè tsź‐mān.
3 ¶ Faān hóh‐tsang‐oò‐kè māt, neĭ to pat‐hóh shīk.
4 Ch‘uk‐shang‐chi‐chung, neĭ hóh‐shīk‐tak‐kè: haî ngaū, mīn‐yeūng, shaan‐yeūng,
5 Lūk, cheung, taaî‐lūk, taaî‐yeūng, meī‐lūk, yĕ‐ngaū, līng‐yeūng.
6 Ch‘uk‐shang‐chi‐chung, faān haî t‘aī fan‐leŭng‐poòn, faan‐tseūk‐kè, neĭ to hóh‐ĭ shīk.
7 Waī faan‐tseūk‐kè kóh‐yat‐luî‐chung, t‘aī fan‐leŭng‐poòn‐kè kóh‐yat luî‐chung, yaŭ pat‐hóh shīk‐kè; tsik‐haî lokₒ‐t‘ōh, paāk‐t‘ò, sha‐faan: yan kóh‐ti haî faan‐tseūk m̄‐fan‐t‘aī‐kè; neĭ‐teî iù ĭ‐waī m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng.
8 Chue sui‐īn fan‐t‘aī, taân m̄‐faan‐tseūk, neĭ‐teî yīk iù ĭ‐waī m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng: pat‐hóh shīk k‘uĭ‐kè yūk, m̄‐hó moh k‘uĭ‐kè shi.
9 ¶ Tsoî shuí‐chung‐kè māt: faān haî yaŭ‐ch‘ì yaŭ‐lūn‐kè, neĭ to hóh‐ĭ shīk:
10 Waī mŏ‐ch‘ì mŏ‐lūn‐kè, neĭ‐teî pat‐hóh‐shīk; neĭ iù ĭ‐waī m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng.
11 ¶ Faān kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè tseukₒ‐niŭ, neĭ‐teî to hóh‐ĭ shīk.
12 Shóh yaŭ pat‐hóh‐shīk‐kè: haî ying, ngōk, tiu,
13 Uēn, chin, saì‐ying, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè t‘ūng luî,
14 Oo‐a kûng k‘uĭ‐kè t‘ūng luî,
15 T‘ōh‐niŭ kung, t‘ōh‐niŭ mŏ, uē‐ying, tseukₒ‐ying, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè t‘ūng luî,
16 Lō‐ts‘z̄, lô‐sz, shām,
17 T‘aī‐oō, oōn‐kui, uē‐kaú,
18 Paāk‐hōk, ying‐mŏ, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè t‘ūng‐luî, uē, fuk‐shué.
19 Faān pūk‐ch‘uè‐hāng‐kè fei‐ch‘ūng, neĭ‐teî iù ĭ‐waī m̄ kitₒ‐tsîng: pat‐hóh‐shīk.
20 Faān kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè fei māt, neĭ‐teî to hóh‐ĭ‐shīk‐kè.
21 ¶ Faān tsẑ‐sź‐kè māt, neĭ‐teî pat‐hóh shīk: hóh‐ĭ peí‐kwòh neĭ shēng‐noî‐kè luĭ‐haakₒ shīk; waāk maaî kwòh î‐pong‐yān: yan‐waî neĭ haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shìng‐mān. Neĭ pat‐hóh yûng yeūng‐mŏ‐kè naaĭ laī chué yeūng‐tsaí.
22 ¶ Mooĭ nīn tsoî teî saatₒ‐chúng shóh‐ch‘aán‐kè, neĭ iù shāp‐fân‐ts‘uí‐yat‐fân.
23 Nğ‐kuk, tsaú, yaū‐kè shāp‐fân‐yat, k‘āp taî‐yat‐t‘oi‐kè ngaū yeūng, neĭ iù tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn ning‐laī shīk, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ shóh‐kaán‐chaāk on‐chì k‘uĭ‐mēng‐kè teî‐fong; táng neĭ hóh‐ĭ hōk‐haaû sheūng‐shī kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì.
24 Tong Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì chuk‐fuk‐neĭ chi‐shī; yeūk kìn lô‐uĕn, pat‐nāng tseung ts‘ź māt ning‐huì kóh‐ch‘uè, yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐on‐chì‐k‘uĭ‐mēng‐kè teî‐fong, leī neĭ kòm‐uĕn;
25 Neĭ tsaû iù tseung ts‘ź‐māt oôn ngān, tseung ngān paau‐heí, yûng shaú ning‐huì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐kaán‐chaāk‐kè teî‐fong:
26 Tseung kóh‐ti ngān, chiù‐sam‐shóh‐seúng‐kè, waāk maaĭ ngaū, waāk maaĭ yeūng, waāk maaĭ ts‘ing‐tsaú, waāk maaĭ nūng‐tsaú, waāk maaĭ neĭ sam‐shóh‐seúng‐kè: neĭ k‘āp neĭ‐kè ka‐kuèn, to iù tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì mîn‐ts‘īn yám shīk, t‘ūng‐maaī foon‐lōk à,
27 Leî‐meî‐yān tsoî neĭ‐teî shēng‐noî chuê‐kè, yīk haî kóm; neĭ m̄‐hó tiu‐heì k‘uĭ; yan k‘uĭ tsoî neĭ‐teî chi‐chung, mŏ fân‐tsź, mŏ ch‘aán‐īp.
28 ¶ Mooĭ‐fūng tò saam nīn meĭ, iù tseung kóh‐nīn t‘ó‐ch‘aán‐kè shāp‐fân‐yat, ts‘ōng lōk neĭ‐kè shēng‐chung:
29 (Tsoî neĭ‐teî chi‐chung, mŏ fân‐tsź, mŏ ch‘aán‐īp,) Leî‐meî‐yān, kûng chuê‐tsoî‐neĭ‐shēng‐chung‐kè luĭ‐haakₒ, koo‐ī, kwá‐foŏ, to hóh‐ĭ laī‐tò shīk‐paaú; táng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì tsoî neĭ shaú faān shóh‐tsô‐kè sẑ laī‐chuk‐fuk neĭ.
