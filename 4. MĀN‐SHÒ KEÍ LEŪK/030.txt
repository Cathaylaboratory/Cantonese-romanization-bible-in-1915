1 MOH‐SAI tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kokₒ‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè tsūk‐cheúng wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè haî kóm.
2 Yān yeūk tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn huí‐uên, waāk faatₒ‐shaî, waāk laāp‐yeukₒ tsẑ‐keí kàm‐kaaì; tsaû pat‐hóh shat‐sùn, iù chiù k‘uĭ haú‐chung shóh‐kóng‐kè laī hāng.
3 Yeūk yaŭ t‘ūng‐nuĭ, shiù‐shī tsoî foô‐ts‘an ka‐chung, tong Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn huí‐uên, laāp‐yeukₒ tsẑ‐keí kàm‐kaaì;
4 K‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an t‘eng‐mān k‘uĭ shóh‐huí‐kè uên, k‘āp k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí kàm‐kaaì‐kè yeukₒ, yeūk mŏ‐ch‘ut‐sheng kûng k‘uĭ kóng: kóm‐tsaû k‘uĭ iù waān shóh‐huí‐kè uên, shaú shóh‐laāp tsẑ‐keí‐kàm‐kaaì‐kè yeukₒ.
5 Yeūk k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an t‘eng‐mān kóh‐yāt, kàm‐chí k‘uĭ; tsaû k‘uĭ shóh‐huí‐kè uên, k‘āp tsẑ‐keí kàm‐kaaì‐kè yeukₒ, to hóh‐ĭ m̄‐waān m̄‐shaú: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shè‐mĭn k‘uĭ, yan k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an kàm‐chí k‘uĭ à.
6 Yeūk k‘uĭ ĭ‐king yaŭ cheûng‐foo, waāk huí‐uên, waāk yan‐mat‐sẑ, ch‘ut īn tsẑ‐keí kàm‐kaaì;
7 K‘uĭ cheûng‐foo t‘eng‐mān kóh‐yāt, mŏ kàm‐chí k‘uĭ: kóm‐tsaû k‘uĭ iù waān shóh‐huí‐kè uên, shaú shóh‐laāp tsẑ‐keí‐kàm‐kaaì‐kè yeukₒ.
8 Yeūk k‘uĭ cheûng‐foo t‘eng‐mān kóh‐yāt, kàm‐chí k‘uĭ; tsaû k‘uĭ shóh‐huí‐kè uên, k‘āp ch‘ut‐īn tsẑ‐keí kàm‐kaaì‐kè, to hóh‐ĭ faì‐hiu: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shè‐mĭn k‘uĭ.
9 Waī‐haî faān kwá‐foŏ, k‘āp peî‐ch‘ut‐kè foŏ‐yān, k‘uĭ shóh‐huí‐kè uên, k‘āp tsẑ‐keí‐kàm‐kaaì‐kè, to pit iù waān, iù shaú.
10 Foŏ‐yān yeūk tsoî cheûng‐foo ka‐chung huí‐uên, waāk faatₒ‐shaî tsẑ‐keí kàm‐kaaì;
11 K‘uĭ cheûng‐foo t‘eng‐mān, mŏ ch‘ut sheng, m̄‐kàm‐chí k‘uĭ: kóm‐tsaû k‘uĭ iù waān shóh‐huí‐kè uên, shaú shóh‐laāp tsẑ‐keí‐kàm‐kaaì‐kè yeukₒ.
12 Yeūk k‘uĭ cheûng‐foo t‘eng‐mān kóh‐yāt, faì‐hiu k‘uĭ shóh‐huí‐kè uên, shóh‐laāp tsẑ‐keí kàm‐kaaì‐kè yeukₒ; tsaû hóh‐ĭ m̄‐waān m̄‐shaú: yan‐waî peî‐cheûng‐foo faì‐hiu; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shè‐mĭn k‘uĭ.
13 Faān foŏ‐yān waāk huí‐uên, waāk faatₒ‐shaî, sam‐chung foó‐ts‘itₒ‐kè, k‘uĭ cheûng‐foo hóh‐ĭ laāp‐tîng, yīk hóh‐ĭ faì‐hiu.
14 Yeūk k‘uĭ cheûng‐foo līn yāt to mŏ ch‘ut‐sheng; tsaû suèn haî laāp‐tîng k‘uĭ shóh‐huí‐kè uên, shaú shóh‐laāp‐kè yeukₒ; yan k‘uĭ cheûng‐foo ĭ‐king t‘eng‐mān kóh‐yāt, mŏ‐ch‘ut‐sheng; tsaû suèn haî laāp‐tîng k‘uĭ shóh‐huí‐kè uên, shóh‐laāp‐kè yeukₒ.
15 Yeūk cheûng‐foo t‘eng‐mān chi‐haû, faì‐hiu k‘uĭ shóh‐huí‐kè uên, shóh‐laāp‐kè yeukₒ; tsaû foŏ‐yān‐kè tsuî kwai‐ue cheûng‐foo shan‐sheûng.
16 Ni‐ti haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò Moh‐sai, kóng k‘āp cheûng‐foo koón foŏ‐yān, kûng foô‐ts‘an koón tsoî‐ka‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ, kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè tîng‐laî.
