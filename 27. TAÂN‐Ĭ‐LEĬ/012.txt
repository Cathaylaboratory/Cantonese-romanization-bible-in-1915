1 TSOÎ‐KÒH‐CHÂN‐SHĪ, waî‐pó‐yaû‐neĭ‐tsź‐mān‐ti‐tsẑ‐tsūk, laī‐ch‘itₒ‐laāp‐kè, tsik‐haî taaî‐kwan Maĭ‐ka‐lāk pit‐ooĭ‐k‘eĭ‐laāp: tsaû‐pit‐yaŭ‐kw‘àn‐foó‐kè shī‐haû, tsẑ‐ts‘ūng yaŭ‐kwokₒ‐ĭ‐loī, chì‐tò ni‐chân‐shī, meî‐yaŭ‐kóm‐yeûng‐kè foó à: kòh‐chân‐shī, neĭ‐tsź‐mān pit‐tîng‐tak‐kaù, tsik‐haî shóh‐yaŭ ĭ‐king‐chuè‐ch‘aakₒ‐kè.
2 Tsoî‐teî‐kè ch‘ān‐chung fàn‐hiu‐kè, hó‐toh ooĭ‐fūk‐síng, yaŭ‐ti‐tak‐tó wĭng‐shang, yaŭ‐ti‐tak‐tó sau‐kw‘aì, laī‐shaû‐wĭng‐uĕn‐kè līng‐yūk.
3 Taân ts‘ung‐mīng‐kè pit‐yaŭ‐wīng‐iû, hó‐ts‘z̆‐t‘in‐kè kwong‐mīng; kaaù‐toh‐yān kwai‐ue‐kung‐î‐kè, hó‐ts‘z̆ chùng‐sing‐kóm, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn.
4 Waī‐haî Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ à, neĭ iù‐ts‘ōng‐maaī‐ni‐ti shuetₒ‐wâ, fung‐chuê‐ni‐kuén‐shue, laī‐táng‐toî‐moōt‐chung‐kòh‐shī: tseung‐pit‐yaŭ toh‐yān‐loī‐wŏng; ī‐ch‘é chì‐shik‐ooĭ‐ka‐tsang‐t‘im.
5 Ngŏh Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ tsaû‐mông‐hă, t‘aí‐a, yaû‐yaŭ leŭng‐kòh‐yān, yat‐kòh haí‐hōh‐kè ni‐pin, yat‐kòh haí‐hōh‐kè kóh‐pin.
6 Yaŭ‐yān mân k‘eĭ‐haí‐hōh‐mîn kòh‐cheukₒ‐pò‐i‐fūk‐kè, wâ, Ni‐ti k‘eī‐tsik‐kè sẑ, tò‐keí‐shī chì‐tak‐tsô‐uēn ni?
7 Ngŏh t‘eng‐mān k‘eĭ‐haí‐hōh‐mîn kòh‐cheukₒ‐pò‐i‐fūk‐kè, kuí‐heí tsóh‐shaú, kûng‐k‘uĭ yaû‐shaú, heùng‐t‘in‐chí‐wĭng‐shang‐kè Chué laī‐faatₒ‐shaî, wâ, Iù‐tò‐yat‐shī, yaû‐leŭng‐shī, yaû‐poòn‐shī à; táng‐tò ĭ‐king‐fan‐saàn mān‐kè k‘uēn, tsaû ni‐ti‐chùng‐sẑ pit‐tîng‐tsô‐uēn‐kè lokₒ.
8 Ngŏh t‘eng‐mān taân‐m̄‐mīng‐paāk: ngŏh tsaû‐wâ, Ngŏh Chué à, ni‐keí‐kîn ti‐chùng‐sẑ, tò‐taí haî‐tím‐yeûng‐kitₒ‐kūk ni?
9 K‘uĭ wâ, Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ à, chikₒ‐koón‐huì la: yan‐waî ni‐ti‐shuetₒ‐wâ, ĭ‐king‐ts‘ōng‐maaī, ī‐ch‘é fung‐māt, táng‐tò‐moōt‐k‘eī‐shī‐haû à.
10 Hó‐toh‐yān ooĭ‐tsô‐k‘īn‐kitₒ‐kè, chíng‐paāk‐tsîng tsẑ‐keí‐kè, ī‐ch‘é peî‐shì‐lîn‐kè; taân‐okₒ‐yān ooĭ‐hāng‐okₒ, hāng‐okₒ‐kè to‐m̄‐mīng‐paāk; waī‐haî ts‘ung‐mīng‐kè pit‐ooĭ‐mīng‐paāk lokₒ.
11 Tsẑ‐ch‘uē‐yāt‐yāt‐shóh‐hìn‐kè tsaì‐pán, k‘āp‐chì‐laāp‐shi fong‐mō‐hóh‐tsang‐oò‐kè, kòh‐shī, pit‐yaŭ yat‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐kaú‐shāp yāt,
12 Faān táng‐toî yaû‐tak‐tó kòh‐yat‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐nğ‐yāt‐kè, kòh‐yān tsaû‐yaŭ‐fuk lokₒ.
13 Waī‐haî neĭ chikₒ‐koón‐huì, tò‐moōt‐chung‐chi‐shī: yan‐waî neĭ‐pit‐hóh‐ĭ on‐sik, ī‐ch‘é tò‐hiu‐yāt‐kè shī‐haû, neĭ tsaû hóh‐k‘eĭ‐tsoî‐neĭ tsẑ‐keí‐kè teî‐waî lokₒ.
