1 À‐HAP‐SẐ tang‐waî‐chi‐shī, nīn‐keí î‐shāp suì, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng shāp‐lūk nīn: k‘uĭ m̄‐ts‘z̆ k‘uĭ tsó Taaî‐waî hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn‐waī‐shîn‐kè:
2 Waī‐haî hāng Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐kè tô, chuè Pa‐līk‐kè ngaŭ‐tseûng.
3 Kim‐chi tsoî Pîn‐yan‐nuên‐kè kuk fān‐heung, yīk fān‐hiu‐k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí tsoî fóh‐ch‘uè, chiù‐i î‐pong shóh‐hāng hóh‐tsang‐oò‐kè sẑ, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kón‐chūk tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn‐kè î‐pong‐yān.
4 Yaû hìn‐tsaì fān‐heung tsoî yau‐t‘aān, tsoî shaan‐sheûng, yaû tsoî kokₒ‐ts‘eng‐shuê‐chi‐hâ.
5 Koò‐ts‘ź Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kaau‐k‘uĭ‐kwòh À‐laān wōng‐kè shaú; À‐laān‐yān kung‐kik k‘uĭ, tsaû lŏ‐leūk tsź‐mān shâm‐toh, taaì‐tò Taaî‐mă‐shik, À‐hap‐sẑ yīk peî‐kaau‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng‐kè shaú. Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng hāng taaî‐shaatₒ‐lūk laī‐shaatₒ k‘uĭ à.
6 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ tiu‐heì k‘uĭ līt‐tsó‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; koò‐ts‘ź Leĭ‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka, yat‐yāt‐chi‐kaan, tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì shaatₒ‐hiu shāp‐î‐maân yān, kaai‐haî yŭng‐sẑ à.
7 Yaŭ yat‐kòh Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn taaî‐nāng‐kè yān, mēng‐kiù Shik‐leĭ, tsaû shaatₒ wōng‐kè tsaí Mă‐sai‐ngă, k‘āp wōng‐kung‐shat‐kè ka‐tsoí Aapₒ‐leî‐kom, kûng wōng‐kè tsoí‐seùng Ĭ‐leî‐ka‐nā.
8 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû lŏ‐leūk k‘uĭ hing‐taî, kûng foŏ‐yān, k‘āp tsaí‐nuĭ î‐shāp‐maân yān, yaû ts‘eúng‐tuēt hó‐toh‐ts‘oī‐māt, taaì shóh‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè māt tò Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à.
9 Taân‐haî tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè yaŭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sin‐chi mēng‐kiù Oh‐tāk: ch‘ut‐huì yīng‐tsipₒ tò Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐kè kwan‐ping, tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, T‘aí‐a, yan‐waî neĭ līt‐tsó‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā nau‐nô Yaū‐t‘aaì, koò‐ts‘ź kaau‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐kwòh neĭ shaú, yaû neĭ nô‐heì ch‘ung‐t‘in tsaû shaatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî.
10 Uē‐kam neĭ yaŭ ì k‘eŭng‐pik Yaū‐t‘aaì, k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè yān, tsô neĭ pūk‐p‘eĭ: taân neĭ tsẑ‐keí heí‐m̄‐haî yaŭ faân‐tsuî neĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sẑ me?
11 Koò‐ts‘ź neĭ iù t‘eng ngŏh wâ, neĭ tsoî neĭ hing‐taî shóh‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān, iù shik‐fòng faan‐huì: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng neĭ‐teî hó‐nau‐nô à.
12 Ue‐shî Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐yān keí‐kòh mūk‐paakₒ, tsik‐haî Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān‐kè tsaí À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, Maĭ‐shāt‐leî‐moōt‐kè tsaí Peí‐leĭ‐ka, Sha‐lūng‐kè tsaí Yē‐hak‐ka, Hap‐loī‐kè tsaí À‐mă‐saatₒ, taaî‐chùng heí‐laī tīk‐chóh yaū chaang‐chìn faan‐laī‐kè yān,
13 Tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî pat‐hóh taaì shóh‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān yāp‐laī ni‐ch‘uè: yan‐waî neĭ‐kè chué‐ì ooĭ lîng ngŏh‐teî tak‐tsuî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaû ka‐tsang ngŏh‐teî‐kè hin‐yaū, ngŏh‐teî‐kè tsuî‐kwòh: yan‐waî ngŏh‐teî‐kè tsuî‐kwòh taaî, ĭ‐king yaŭ līt‐nô lām‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān à.
14 Kóm‐tsaû cha‐ping‐heì‐kè yān, tseung shóh‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān, kûng ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè ts‘oī‐māt, kaai laū‐hâ tsoî chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, k‘āp ooî‐chùng‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn.
15 Tong‐shī, ts‘īn shóh‐sé‐lōk mēng‐kè yān heí‐laī, tseung shóh‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān, yaû k‘aaĭ shóh‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè māt, cheukₒ chuèn k‘eī‐chung ch‘ekₒ‐shan‐kè, chong‐shik k‘uĭ‐teî, ī‐ch‘é peí haaī kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî cheukₒ, kung‐fûng yám‐shīk, yaû k‘aaĭ ko ch‘ā k‘uĭ‐teî, k‘eī‐chung waāk yaŭ uĕn‐yeūk‐kè yān, tsaû lîng k‘ē‐luī, taaì k‘uĭ‐teî huì‐tò tsó‐shuê‐kè shēng Yē‐leî‐koh, tò k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè hing‐taî‐ch‘uè, ts‘uī‐haû k‘uĭ‐teî faan‐huì Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à.
16 ¶ Tong‐shī À‐hap‐sẑ tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché huì kin À‐shūt‐kè chùng‐wōng, k‘aū k‘uĭ‐teî pong‐chôh.
17 Yan‐waî Ĭ‐tung‐yān tsoì ch‘ut‐laī kung‐kik Yaū‐taaî, tsaû lŏ‐leūk tsź‐mān.
18 Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān yaû ts‘am‐chìm tai‐hâ p‘īng‐uēn‐teî, k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì naām‐pin‐kè kokₒ‐shēng, ts‘uí‐hiu Paakₒ‐shî‐māk, À‐yē‐lūn, K‘eī‐tak‐lōk, Shuetₒ‐koh, kûng kòh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung, T‘īng‐naāp kûng kòh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung, Kam‐sóh kûng kòh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung: tsaû chuê‐tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè lokₒ.
19 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yan Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng À‐hap‐sẑ‐kè uēn‐koò, shaí Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān pei‐meī; yan‐waî À‐hap‐sẑ tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān fòng‐sz̀ waāng‐hāng, tsaû taaî‐tak‐tsuî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
20 À‐shūt wōng Tik‐laāp‐peí‐leî‐sz tò laī kaaú‐iŭ, m̄‐haî kin‐koò k‘uĭ‐teî.
21 Yan‐waî À‐hap‐sẑ yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk kûng wōng‐kung‐shat, k‘āp chùng‐kwan‐cheúng‐kè ka, ts‘uí‐ti ts‘oī‐pó sùng‐kwòh À‐shūt wōng: taân‐haî kóm to m̄‐pong‐chôh.
22 Ts‘ź wōng À‐hap‐sẑ tong kap‐naân‐chi‐shī: yaû uēt‐faatₒ tak‐tsuî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
23 K‘uĭ hìn‐tsaì kwòh‐kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐kè Taaî‐mă‐shik‐chi‐shān wâ, Yan‐waî À‐laān līt‐wōng‐kè shān pong‐chôh k‘uĭ‐teî, koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh pit hìn‐tsaì‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ, táng k‘uĭ pong‐chôh ngŏh. Taân‐haî ni‐ti shān shaí À‐hap‐sẑ kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān paaî‐mōng à.
24 À‐hap‐sẑ ts‘uí‐maaī Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐kè kokₒ‐yeûng heì‐mĭng, yaû waí‐waaî Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè heì‐mĭng, ī‐ch‘é shaan‐maaī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè moōn, yaû tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng mooĭ‐kokₒ‐t‘aū‐ch‘uè kìn‐chuk tsaì‐t‘aān.
25 Yaû tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè kokₒ‐shēng kìn‐chuk yau‐t‘aān, fān‐heung kwòh‐pīt‐shān, yaû kik‐tûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó chi Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè nau‐nô.
26 À‐hap‐sẑ shóh‐hāng k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, kûng k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè lô, tsẑ ch‘í‐ch‘oh chì shau‐meĭ, t‘aí‐a, kaai‐tsoì‐lōk Yaū‐t‘aaì k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue.
27 À‐hap‐sẑ kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám, chùng‐yān tsòng k‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng shēng: waī‐haî m̄‐taaì k‘uĭ yāp Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐kè fān‐mô: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Hei‐sai‐ka, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
