1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Neĭ chiû À‐lūn k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź laī, tseung i‐fūk, ko‐yaū, k‘āp tsô shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì‐kè ngaū‐kung yat‐chekₒ, yeūng‐kung leŭng‐chekₒ, mō‐kaaù‐péng yat‐lōh, to ning‐laī;
3 Yaû tsuî‐tsaāp ooî‐chùng, tò ooî‐mōk moōn‐ts‘īn.
4 Moh‐sai tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò laī hāng; ooî‐chùng tsaû tsuî‐tsaāp tsoî ooî‐mōk moōn‐ts‘īn.
5 Moh‐sai tuì ooî‐chùng wâ, Ni‐ti haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò laī tsô.
6 Moh‐sai taaì À‐lūn k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź laī, yaû yûng shuí saí k‘uĭ‐teî.
7 Tseung noî‐p‘ō kûng À‐lūn cheukₒ‐heí, tseung taaî‐taaì kûng k‘uĭ ch‘uk iu, yaû kûng k‘uĭ cheukₒ‐heí ngoî‐p‘ō, ka‐sheŭng ĭ‐fat, yaû tseung ĭ‐fat‐kè chik‐fa‐taaì kûng k‘uĭ ch‘uk‐chuê, póng ĭ‐fat tsoî k‘uĭ shan‐sheûng.
8 Cheukₒ‐chuê p‘ó‐tsź: yaû tseung Oo‐līng k‘āp T‘ó‐mīng fòng‐tsoî p‘ó‐tsź noî.
9 Yaû tseung koon taaì tsoî k‘uĭ t‘aū‐sheûng, tsoî koon‐kè ts‘īn‐mîn, taaì‐sheŭng‐kam‐p‘aaī, tsik‐haî shìng mĭn; to haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
10 Moh‐sai yûng ko‐yaū ch‘ā waī‐mōk, k‘āp k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè māt‐kîn, fan‐pīt waī shìng.
11 Yaû yûng yaū shá‐lōk t‘aān‐sheûng ts‘at ts‘z̀, ch‘ā t‘aān k‘āp shūk‐t‘aān‐kè heì‐mĭng, kûng saí‐yūk‐kè p‘oōn, k‘āp p‘oōn‐tsôh, pīt‐hoi‐saaì waī shìng.
12 Tseung ko‐yaū cham‐lōk À‐lūn t‘aū‐sheûng laī ch‘ā k‘uĭ, pīt‐hoi waī‐shìng.
13 Moh‐sai tsaû taaì À‐lūn‐kè chùng‐tsź laī, tseung noî‐p‘ō kûng k‘uĭ cheukₒ‐heí, tseung taaî‐taaì kûng k‘uĭ ch‘uk iu, tseung koon kûng k‘uĭ taaì; haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè.
14 Yaû laai shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì kè ngaū‐koó‐tsaí laī: À‐lūn k‘āp k‘uĭ chùng‐tsź òn shaú tsoî shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì‐kè ngaū‐tsaí t‘aū‐sheûng.
15 T‘ong‐hiu ngaū; Moh‐sai tsaû yûng shaú‐chí yăm huetₒ, ch‘ā‐lōk t‘aān‐kè sz̀‐kokₒ, lîng kòh‐t‘aān kitₒ‐tsîng, k‘eī‐uē‐kè huetₒ tó‐lōk t‘aān‐keukₒ, pīt‐hoi waī shìng, táng hóh‐ĭ tsoî t‘aān‐sheûng shūk‐tsuî.
16 Moh‐sai ning‐saaì shóh‐yaŭ k‘oì‐tsông‐kè mŏng‐yaū, kûng kon‐p‘ìn, leŭng‐kòh noî‐shăn, kûng iu‐kaan‐kè yaū, shiu tsoî t‘aān‐sheûng.
17 Tseung ngaū‐tsaí‐kè p‘eī‐yūk k‘āp fàn, to yûng fóh shiu tsoî yīng‐p‘oōn‐ngoî; haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè.
18 Yaû laai faān‐tsaì‐kè yeūng‐kung laī: À‐lūn kûng k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź, òn shaú tsoî yeūng‐kung t‘aū‐sheûng.
19 T‘ong‐hiu yeūng; Moh‐sai tsaû tseung huetₒ shá‐lōk t‘aān‐sheûng sz̀‐waī.
20 Yaû ts‘itₒ‐hoi yeūng; Moh‐sai tsaû tseung t‘aū kûng yūk‐faaì, k‘āp mŏng‐yaū shiu‐saaì.
21 Yûng shuí saí‐tsêng ch‘eūng tsông kûng peí; Moh‐sai tseung ts‘uēn yeūng to shiu tsoî t‘aān‐sheûng: ni‐ti haî faān‐tsaì, haî hing‐heung, haî hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kè fóh‐tsaì; tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
22 Tsoì laai taî‐î chekₒ yeūng‐kung, tsik‐haî kuk‐yīng‐laĭ‐kè yeūng: À‐lūn kûng k‘uĭ chùng‐tsź, òn shaú tsoî yeūng‐kung t‘aū‐sheûng.
23 T‘ong‐niu yeūng; Moh‐sai tsaû ning huetₒ ch‘ā‐lōk À‐lūn‐kè yaû‐ĭ‐chue‐sheûng, yaû ch‘ā yaû‐shaú kûng yaû‐keukₒ‐kè taaî‐chí.
24 Moh‐sai yaû taaì À‐lūn‐kè chùng‐tsź laī, tseung huetₒ ch‘ā k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè yaû‐ĭ‐chue‐sheûng, yaû ch‘ā yaû‐shaú kûng yaû‐keukₒ‐kè taaî‐chí: yīk tseung huetₒ shá‐lōk t‘aān‐kè sz̀‐waī chi‐sheûng.
25 Yaû ning yaū kûng meĭ, k‘oì‐tsông‐kè mŏng‐yaū, kon‐p‘ìn, leŭng‐kòh noî‐shăn, k‘āp iu‐kaan‐kè yaū, līn yaû‐t‘uí:
26 Yaû ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn, tsoì mō‐kaaù‐péng‐kè lōh‐chung, ning mō‐kaaù‐péng yat‐kòh, ka‐yaū‐kè péng yat‐kòh, pōk‐péng yat‐kòh, fòng‐lōk yaū kûng yaû‐t‘uí chi‐sheûng:
27 Tseung yat‐ts‘aì k‘aaĭ‐laī kaau‐kwòh À‐lūn, kûng k‘uĭ chùng‐tsź‐kè shaú, táng k‘uĭ iū‐paaí, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn tsô iū‐tsaì.
28 Moh‐sai ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî shaú‐chung ning‐lōk‐laī, shiu tsoî t‘aān‐kè faān‐tsaì chi‐sheûng: ni‐ti haî kuk‐yīng‐laĭ: haî hing‐heung, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kè fóh‐tsaì.
29 Moh‐sai ning yeūng‐kè hung iū‐paaí, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn tsô iū‐tsaì: yan‐waî ni‐ti haî kuk‐yīng‐laĭ‐kè yeūng‐kung, Moh‐sai ying‐tak‐kè fân; to haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
30 Moh‐sai k‘aaĭ ko‐yaū, k‘āp t‘aān‐sheûng‐kè huetₒ, shá‐lōk À‐lūn shan kûng i‐fūk, yaû shá‐lōk k‘uĭ chùng‐tsź‐kè shan kûng i‐fūk; tseung À‐lūn‐kè shan kûng i‐fūk, k‘uĭ chùng‐tsź‐kè shan kûng i‐fūk, to pīt‐hoi waī shìng.
31 Moh‐sai tuì À‐lūn kûng k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź wâ, Tsoî ooî‐mōk moōn‐ts‘īn chué tsaì yūk: yīk haí kòh‐ch‘uè shīk, k‘āp shīk lōh‐noî kuk‐yīng‐laĭ kè péng, tsun‐i ngŏh shóh‐fan‐foò wâ, À‐lūn, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź ying‐tong shīk kè.
32 Uē‐shîng‐kè yūk kûng péng, neĭ tong yûng fóh fān‐shiu.
33 Ts‘at‐yāt‐kaan, neĭ‐teî m̄‐hó ch‘ut ooî‐mōk moōn‐haú, chì‐tò moŏn‐hiu neĭ kuk‐yīng‐laĭ‐kè yāt‐tsź: yan‐waî ts‘at‐yāt‐kaan, k‘uĭ pit kûng neĭ hāng kuk‐yīng‐laĭ.
34 Kam‐yāt shóh‐hāng‐kè, haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kè mîng‐lîng, waî neĭ‐teî shūk‐tsuî.
35 Koò‐ts‘ź neĭ‐teî ying‐tong ts‘at yāt ts‘at yê, chuê tsoî ooî‐mōk moōn‐haú, kán shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐p‘aaì‐kè chik‐fân, mĭn‐chì neĭ‐teî sź‐mōng: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kóm‐yeûng fan‐foò ngŏh à.
36 Ue‐shî À‐lūn kûng k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsź, tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ Moh‐sai shaú, faān shóh‐fan‐foò kè laī hāng.
