1 YEUKₒ‐SAI‐À tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ: tsoî ching‐uēt shāp‐sz̀ yāt, chùng‐yān tsaû t‘ong uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè yeūng‐tsaí.
2 Wōng fan‐p‘aaì chùng‐tsaì‐sz kán‐shaú chik‐yīk, ī‐ch‘é iù k‘uĭ‐teî kong‐ngaî kung‐fûng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè yīk‐sẑ,
3 Yaû wâ, Iù shīng shìng‐kitₒ tsoî‐ue‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, kaaù‐fàn Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān wâ, Iù tseung shìng‐kwaî chai‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Taaî‐waî‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn kìn‐chuk‐kè tîn‐uk‐chung; kóm‐tsaû m̄‐shaí neĭ‐teî kin‐pokₒ taam‐t‘oī: uē‐kam neĭ‐teî iù fûng‐sẑ neĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘āp kòh‐ti tsź‐mān Ĭ‐shik‐līt.
4 Ī‐ch‘é iù tsẑ‐keí uê‐peî, tsun‐i neĭ chùng‐foô‐ka, chiù‐chuê neĭ‐teî‐kè paan‐luēn, tsun‐ts‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Taaî‐waî shóh‐keì‐kè shue, uĕ‐k‘āp kûng k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn shóh‐keì‐kè shue.
5 Shî‐laāp tsoî shìng‐shóh, i‐chuê neĭ‐teî hing‐taî, tsik‐haî ni‐ti tsź‐mān chùng‐foô‐ka shóh‐tsô‐kè ts‘z̀‐tsuî, ī‐ch‘é chiù‐i Leî‐meî‐yān chùng‐foô‐ka shóh‐hāng‐kè ts‘z̀‐tsuî.
6 Iù t‘ong uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè yeūng‐tsaí, tsẑ‐keí shìng‐pīt, ī‐ch‘é shaí neĭ hing‐taî uēn‐ts‘uēn uê‐peî, táng k‘uĭ‐teî tsun‐shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ Moh‐sai shaú shóh‐kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ.
7 Yeukₒ‐sai‐à sheúng‐ts‘z̀ tsź‐mān, tsaû yaū kw‘ān‐chung k‘aaĭ yeūng‐tsaí, k‘āp shaan‐yeūng‐tsaí, kûng‐kaì‐shò saam‐maân‐chekₒ, ngaū saam‐ts‘in‐chekₒ, ni‐ti haî waî faān‐t‘ūng‐tsoî‐kè, shaú‐uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè tsaì‐māt: kaai‐yaū wōng tsẑ‐keí māt‐īp ch‘ut‐kè à.
8 Yaû k‘uĭ‐kè kwan‐cheúng, yīk lōk‐ì shue‐naāp ts‘z̀‐kwòh tsź‐mān, kûng tsaì‐sz k‘āp Leî‐meî‐yān: Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐kè chùng‐tsoí Hei‐lāk‐ka, Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à, Yē‐hitₒ, k‘aaĭ yeūng î‐ts‘in‐lūk‐paakₒ‐chekₒ, ngaū saam‐paakₒ‐chekₒ, ts‘z̀‐kwòh tsaì‐sz tsô uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè tsaì‐māt.
9 Leî‐meî‐yān tsuì‐hín‐ché Koh‐nōh‐neī, kûng k‘uĭ hing‐taî Shî‐mâ‐ngă, Nā‐taân‐īp, uĕ‐k‘āp Hap‐sha‐peì, Yē‐īp, Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐paatₒ, k‘aaĭ ko‐yeūng nğ‐ts‘in‐chekₒ, ngaū nğ‐paakₒ‐chekₒ, ts‘z̀‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān, tsô uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè tsaì‐māt.
10 Kóm‐tsaû kung‐fûng‐kè sẑ kaai‐ts‘aī‐peî, tsaì‐sz k‘eĭ‐tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè, Leî‐meî‐yān k‘eĭ haí paan‐luēn, i‐chuê wōng shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè à.
11 Leî‐meî‐yān t‘ong uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè yeūng‐tsaí, tsaì‐sz tseung shóh‐shaû‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè huetₒ k‘aaĭ‐laī shá, Leî‐meî‐yān tsaû mokₒ shóh‐t‘ong‐kè hei‐shang.
12 Yaû tseung faān‐tsaì ning‐huì, táng k‘uĭ‐teî hóh‐ĭ i‐chuê tsź‐mān chùng‐foô‐ka shóh‐hāng‐kè ts‘z̀‐tsuî laī fan‐p‘aaì, chì k‘uĭ‐teî hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsoî Moh‐sai‐shue shóh‐sé‐lōk‐kè. Shóh‐t‘ong‐kè ngaū, yīk‐haî kóm‐yeûng.
13 K‘uĭ‐teî yīk chiù‐i sheūng‐laî, k‘aaĭ fóh laī‐shiu uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè ko‐yeūng: waī‐haî k‘eī‐uē‐kè shìng‐māt, yûng foó, yûng woh, yûng wōk laī chué, tsaû kap‐ts‘uk fan‐p‘aaì kwòh‐chùng‐tsź‐mān.
14 Īn‐haû waî tsẑ‐keí k‘āp waî tsaì‐sz uê‐peî shīk: yan‐waî tsaì‐sz À‐lūn haû‐yuî, chuen hìn faān‐tsaì kûng ko chì‐tò yê‐maăn; koò‐ts‘ź Leî‐meî‐yān waî tsẑ‐keí, yīk waî tsaì‐sz À‐lūn haû‐yuî uê‐peî shīk.
15 Au‐koh‐ché À‐saatₒ‐kè haû‐yuî, kokₒ‐k‘eĭ‐tsoî tong‐sẑ‐chi‐shóh, chiù‐i Taaî‐waî, À‐saatₒ, Hei‐maân, kûng wōng‐kè sin‐kìn‐ché, Yē‐t‘ó‐tûn shóh‐fan‐foò; sz‐moōn‐ché, hon‐shaú kokₒ‐moōn; m̄‐shaí leī‐hoi kòh‐ti chik‐sẑ: yan‐waî k‘uĭ hing‐taî Leî‐meì‐yān, waî k‘uĭ‐teî uê‐peî shīk à.
16 Tong yāt Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yīk‐sẑ kaai‐ts‘aī‐peî, tsun‐shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ, ī‐ch‘é hìn faān‐tsaì tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè t‘aān‐ts‘īn, i‐chuê Yeukₒ‐sai‐à wōng shóh‐fan‐foò.
17 Tong‐shī tsoî‐kòh‐ch‘uè‐kè Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ, kûng mō‐kaaù‐tsitₒ, ts‘at yāt.
18 Tsẑ sin‐chi Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ‐chi‐yāt ĭ‐haû, tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt, meî yaŭ hó‐ts‘z̆ uē‐kam shóh‐shaú‐kè uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ; ī‐ch‘é Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng, kaai meî‐ts‘āng shaú‐kwòh yat‐kòh uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ hó‐ts‘z̆ Yeukₒ‐sai‐à, k‘āp tsaì‐sz Leî‐meî‐yān, kûng tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān shóh‐shaú‐kè.
19 Tong Yeukₒ‐sai‐à tang‐waî shāp‐paatₒ nīn, tsaû shaú ni‐ti‐uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ à.
20 ¶ Faān ts‘ź‐sẑ‐chi‐haû, Yeukₒ‐sai‐à sau‐ts‘ap tîn‐uk, ĭ‐king tsô‐uēn, yaŭ Oi‐k‘āp wōng Neī‐koh sheŭng‐laī, seúng kung‐tá kân‐chuê Paakₒ‐laāt‐hōh Ka‐kei‐māt: Yeukₒ‐sai‐à tsaû ch‘ut‐huì kung‐kik k‘uĭ.
21 Taân k‘uĭ tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché laī kìn Yeukₒ‐sai‐à wâ, Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng à, ngŏh uĕ neĭ hōh‐kon ni? Ngŏh kam‐yāt m̄‐haî ch‘ut‐laī kung‐kik neĭ, haî kung‐kik ngŏh shóh‐kûng‐chaang‐chìn‐kè ka che: yan‐waî Sheûng‐Taì ĭ‐king fan‐foò ngŏh, iù kap‐ts‘uk: neĭ iù pâ‐shaú, maĭ‐kung Sheûng‐Taì, mĭn k‘uĭ paaî‐waaî neĭ, yan‐waî Sheûng‐Taì t‘ūng maaī ngŏh à.
22 Taân‐haî Yeukₒ‐sai‐à m̄‐háng chuèn‐huì leī‐hoi k‘uĭ, koí‐chong táng hóh‐ĭ kûng k‘uĭ chaang‐chìn, m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Neī‐koh yaū Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐t‘okₒ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsaû laī‐tò Maĭ‐kat‐toh shaan‐kuk seúng chaang‐chìn.
23 Shê‐tsìn‐kè kwan‐sẑ, shê‐chùng Yeukₒ‐sai‐à wōng; wōng tuì k‘uĭ pūk wâ, Taaì ngŏh huì; yan‐waî ngŏh shaû‐ch‘ŭng‐sheung lokₒ.
24 Kóm‐tsaû k‘uĭ pūk foō‐k‘uĭ‐ch‘ut kòh‐shîng‐ch‘e, chai‐tsoî k‘uĭ‐kè taî‐î shîng‐ch‘e: taaì k‘uĭ tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, k‘uĭ tsaû sź, yaû tsòng‐tsoî k‘uĭ līt‐tsó‐kè chùng‐fān‐mô. Faān Yaū‐t‘aaì kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng chùng‐yān, waî Yeukₒ‐sai‐à pei‐oi.
25 Yē‐leî‐maĭ to waî Yeukₒ‐sai‐à pei‐oi: shóh‐yaŭ‐au‐koh‐kè naām‐nuĭ, yīk ch‘eùng oi‐koh, tsoî kóh‐ti oi‐ts‘z̄, k‘aai‐kóng‐k‘āp Yeukₒ‐sai‐à, chì‐tò kam‐yāt, ī‐ch‘é tseung ts‘ź‐sẑ shīng‐waī yat laî tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt: t‘aí‐a, ni‐ti‐shuetₒ‐wâ tsoì‐lōk oi‐koh‐chung‐laī à.
26 Yeukₒ‐sai‐à shóh‐hāng k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, k‘āp k‘uĭ kokₒ‐shîn‐tô, i‐chuê sé‐lōk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lūt‐faatₒ‐kè shue.
27 K‘āp k‘uĭ tsẑ‐ch‘í‐ch‘oh chì‐meĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, t‘aí‐a, kaai‐tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì līt‐wōng keí‐leūk‐kè shue à.
