1 YEUKₒ‐SHAT‐KÈ tsź‐suen, Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè tsaí Mă‐kat, Mă‐kat‐kè tsaí Kei‐līt, kokₒ‐chi‐p‘aaì chùng‐tsūk‐kè t‘aū‐mūk, laī‐tò Moh‐sai k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt kokₒ‐tsūk‐cheúng mūk‐cheúng‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn: wâ,
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò ngŏh chué chap‐ch‘aū fan teî, peí‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsô ch‘aán‐īp: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû fan‐foò ngŏh chué, tseung ngŏh‐teî hing‐taî Sai‐lōh‐fei‐hap‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, fan‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐kè nuĭ.
3 K‘uĭ‐teî yeūk kà‐hiu Ĭ‐shik‐līt pīt‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè yān, tsaû pit tseung ngŏh tsó‐tsung‐kè ch‘aán‐īp taaì‐huì: kóm k‘uĭ cheûng‐foo chi‐p‘aaì‐kè ch‘aán‐īp ka‐toh, ngŏh‐teî shóh‐fan‐kè ch‘aán‐īp tsaû kaám‐shiú.
4 Táng‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān hei‐nīn kóh‐shī, kóh‐ti nuĭ‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, pit kwai‐ue cheûng‐foo‐kè chi‐p‘aaì, kóm‐yeûng, tsaû kaám‐hiu ngŏh‐teî tsó‐tsung chi‐p‘aaì‐kè ch‘aán‐īp.
5 Moh‐sai tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mîng‐lîng, tsaû fan‐foò Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān wâ, Yeukₒ‐shat tsź‐suen‐kè chi‐p‘aaì, shóh‐kóng haî cheūk à.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lûn‐k‘āp Sai‐lōh‐fei‐hap‐kè nuĭ, yaŭ kóm fan‐foò, yâm‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî kà shóh‐uên‐kà‐kè yān; tūk‐iù kà t‘ūng‐tsung chi‐p‘aaì‐kè yān.
7 Kóm Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, tsaû m̄‐shaí leī‐hoi yat‐chi‐p‘aaì, kwai‐maaī pīt‐chi‐p‘aaì: yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè chùng‐yān, pit‐iù kokₒ‐shaú tsó‐tsung chi‐p‘aaì‐kè ch‘aán‐īp.
8 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐chung, faān yaŭ shīng‐kaì ch‘aán‐īp‐kè nuĭ, pit iù kà t‘ūng‐tsung chi‐p‘aaì‐kè yān, táng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān hóh‐ĭ kokₒ‐shaú tsó‐tsung‐kè ch‘aán‐īp.
9 Kóm‐yeûng, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, tsaû m̄‐shaí leī‐hoi yat chi‐p‘aaì, kwai‐maaī pīt chi‐p‘aaì; yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐yān, pit iù kokₒ‐shaú tsẑ‐keí‐kè ch‘aán‐īp à.
10 Sai‐lōh‐fei‐hap‐kè nuĭ, tsaû tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai kóm laī tsô:
11 Sai‐lōh‐fei‐hap‐kè nuĭ, tsik‐haî Mă‐la, Tak‐saatₒ, Hotₒ‐la, Māt‐ka, Nōh‐à, to kà k‘uĭ paakₒ‐shuk‐kè chùng‐tsź:
12 K‘uĭ‐teî kà Yeukₒ‐shat tsaí Mă‐nā‐sai chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsź, kóm‐tsaû ch‘aán‐īp laū‐ts‘uēn tsoî poón‐tsūk‐kè chi‐p‘aaì lokₒ.
13 ¶ Ni‐ti haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî Moh‐aapₒ kw‘òng‐yĕ, Yē‐leî‐koh tuì‐mîn Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐pin, t‘okₒ Moh‐sai shaú shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè kaaì‐mîng lūt‐laî.
