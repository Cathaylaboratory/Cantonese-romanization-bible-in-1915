1 WAĪ‐HAÎ tong shau‐meĭ‐kè yāt‐tsź, pit‐shīng‐tsaû haî kóm, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè shaan, pit‐tsoî‐chùng‐shaan‐chi‐téng‐ch‘uè tak‐kin‐laāp, yaû tak‐shing‐ko‐kwòh chùng‐kong‐lĭng; ī‐ch‘é līt‐mān ooĭ‐laū‐lōk kwai‐shūk‐k‘uĭ à.
2 Yaŭ toh‐kwokₒ pit‐tîng‐huì‐tò, yaû wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù‐laī‐t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh, sheŭng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shaan, tò Ngă‐kokₒ‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì kè tîn‐uk la; k‘uĭ ooĭ‐k‘aaĭ‐k‘uĭ‐kè lô‐t‘ō chí‐kaaù, ngŏh‐teî yīk‐ooĭ‐hāng‐k‘uĭ‐kè lô‐kìng à: yan‐waî lūt‐faatₒ pit‐yaū Sun‐shaan‐ch‘uè faatₒ‐ch‘ut, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, yīk‐yaū Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng faatₒ‐ch‘ut à.
3 K‘uĭ pit‐tsoî toh‐kwokₒ‐mān‐chi‐chung shi‐shám‐p‘oòn, yaû chaakₒ‐shīng uĕn‐fong k‘eūng‐chòng‐kè kwokₒ; k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐k‘aaĭ‐to‐kìm pìn‐tsokₒ‐laī‐p‘ā, k‘aaĭ‐maū‐ts‘eung pìn‐tsô‐līm‐to; ni‐kwokₒ‐m̄‐heí‐to‐kìm kung‐tá‐kóh‐kwokₒ, yaû m̄‐tsoì hōk tá‐cheùng‐kè sẑ à.
4 Waī‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî kokₒ‐yān, tsoî tsẑ‐keí p‘ō‐t‘aī‐shuê‐hâ, uĕ‐k‘āp mō‐fa‐kwóh‐shuê‐hâ ts‘ŏh‐lokₒ; ī‐ch‘é mŏ‐yān ooĭ‐lîng‐k‘uĭ king‐fong: yan‐waî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè haú, ts‘āng‐kóng ni‐ti là.
5 Chùng‐mān kokₒ‐tsīk‐laaî keí‐shān‐kè mîng‐lîng laī‐hāng, ngŏh‐teî wĭng‐uĕn‐chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn, tsīk‐laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ngŏh‐teî Sheûng‐Taì‐kè mîng‐lîng laī‐hāng à.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Tong‐kòh‐yāt ngŏh pit‐tsuî‐ooî kòh‐ti‐pai‐kè, yaû tsaāp‐maaī kòh‐ti‐peî‐kón‐chūk‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp ngŏh ts‘āng‐shaí‐k‘uĭ shaû‐foó‐naân‐kè;
7 Ngŏh yīk‐ooĭ‐shaí‐kòh‐ti pai‐kè, uē‐shîng‐yat‐fân, yaû shaí‐kòh‐ti peî‐kón‐chūk‐kè, tsô‐k‘eūng‐shîng‐kè‐kwokₒ: ī‐ch‘é Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî Sun‐shaan‐ch‘uè, ooĭ‐koón‐hāt k‘uĭ‐teî, tsẑ‐kam‐ĭ‐haû chì‐wĭng‐uĕn‐kè.
8 Chì‐ue neĭ tsô‐kw‘ān‐yeūng‐kè shaú‐laū, tsô Sun‐nuĭ‐kè kong, kwokₒ‐k‘uēn pit‐tîng‐kwai‐ue‐neĭ; ī‐ch‘é ts‘īn‐shī‐kè kwokₒ‐k‘uēn, tsik‐haî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐chi‐nuĭ‐kè kwokₒ to‐kwai‐ue‐neĭ.
9 Uē‐kam neĭ‐yan‐hōh taaî‐sheng haàm‐foó ni? tsoî neĭ‐chi‐chung mŏ‐wōng me? neĭ waî‐ch‘aakₒ‐sẑ sź‐mōng‐t‘ùng‐foó, luî‐neĭ hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘uī‐lō‐kè foŏ‐yān me?
10 Sun‐shaan‐chi‐nuĭ à, iù k‘uī‐lō shang‐ch‘aán, hó‐ts‘z̆ shang‐ch‘aán‐kè foŏ‐yān kóm: yan‐waî neĭ iù‐yaū‐shēng‐chung‐ch‘ut‐huì, tsoî‐t‘īn‐kaan kui‐chuê, ī‐ch‘é laī‐tò Pa‐peí‐lūn‐ch‘uè, tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè pit‐tak‐kaù‐t‘uetₒ; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ooĭ‐kaù‐shūk‐neĭ, t‘uetₒ‐leī‐ch‘aū‐tīk‐kè shaú à.
11 Uē‐kam yaŭ‐toh‐kwokₒ tsuî‐tsaāp seúng‐kung‐kik neĭ, k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Iù tìm‐yūk‐k‘uĭ, táng ngŏh‐kè ngaăn tsoî Sun‐shēng‐ch‘uè, t‘aí‐kìn shóh‐seúng‐kè.
12 Waī‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐shik‐tak Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè ì‐nîm, yaû m̄‐mīng‐paāk k‘uĭ shóh‐maū‐tîng‐kè: yan‐waî k‘uĭ ts‘āng tsuî‐maaī k‘uĭ‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆‐ch‘eūng‐sheûng‐wōh‐pá yat‐yeûng.
13 Sun‐shēng‐kè nuĭ à, iù‐heí‐laī‐tá‐wōh‐kuk: yan ngŏh tseung‐lîng‐neĭ‐kokₒ shīng‐t‘itₒ, shaí neĭ‐keukₒ‐t‘aī‐tsô‐t‘ūng‐kè kóm: neĭ pit‐ooĭ‐tá‐suì chùng‐toh‐līt‐mān: yaû k‘aaĭ‐k‘uĭ‐shóh‐tsik‐kè ts‘oī‐paāk, kwai‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘uĭ‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, kwai‐ue ts‘uēn‐teî‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à.
