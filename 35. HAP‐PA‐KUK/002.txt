1 NGŎH iù k‘eĭ‐tsoî shaú‐mông‐chi‐shóh, laāp‐tsoî pó‐t‘oī‐chi‐sheûng, yaû mông‐chuê t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ‐t‘ūng‐ngŏh‐kóng‐mat‐yĕ, ngŏh yaû tím‐yeûng k‘aaĭ‐sò‐uen‐chi‐sẑ‐taapₒ‐yìng k‘uĭ ni?
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû‐taapₒ ngŏh wâ, Iù‐k‘aaĭ î‐tseûng sé‐lōk shēk‐pei‐chi‐sheûng, k‘aaĭ‐laī mīng‐paāk hak‐lōk, táng‐tò pan‐tsaú hāng‐kwòh‐kè, ts‘uī‐pîn t‘aí‐tak‐ch‘ut.
3 Yan‐waî kòh‐î‐tseûng sheûng‐ch‘é táng‐haû, yaŭ tîng‐shāt‐chi‐k‘eī, yaû kón‐faaì lām‐chung‐kòh‐shī, yīk‐pit m̄‐haî‐hui‐ká‐kè: sui‐īn ch‘ī‐maân, to‐iù‐táng‐haû; yan‐waî tò‐taí ooĭ‐laī, m̄‐ooĭ‐tsoì‐ch‘ī à.
4 T‘aí‐a, k‘uĭ‐kè sam‐shān tsẑ‐ko, tsoî k‘uĭ‐ch‘ung‐noî yīk‐m̄‐chìng‐chīk: waī‐haî î‐yān yaū k‘uĭ‐kè sùn tsaû‐tak‐shang lokₒ.
5 Fòng‐ch‘é tsaú haî‐hei‐p‘ìn‐kè, kiu‐ngô‐kè yān, yaû m̄‐chuê‐tsoî ka‐chung, shang‐heí sz‐yūk, hó‐ts‘z̆‐yam‐kaan kòm‐footₒ, yaû hó‐ts‘z̆‐sź‐mōng pat‐nāng‐moŏn‐ì, waī‐haî tsuî‐maaī‐maân‐kwokₒ, ch‘ūng‐tīp‐maân‐mān, kwai‐shūk tsẑ‐keí‐kè lokₒ:
6 Ni‐ti‐taaî‐chùng, heí‐m̄‐ooĭ yûng p‘eì‐uê tuì‐k‘uĭ‐kóng, heí‐m̄‐ooĭ k‘aaĭ‐kei‐ts‘iù‐kè cham‐īn laī‐tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Ka‐tsang‐m̄‐shūk tsẑ‐keí‐kè māt, kòh‐yān yaŭ‐wôh‐lokₒ! iù‐tò‐keí‐noî ni? k‘aaĭ‐yān‐tín‐aapₒ‐kè māt, tsokₒ‐waī‐tsẑ‐keí taaî‐toh‐kè, kòh‐yān yaŭ‐wôh‐lokₒ!
7 Ngaaŭ‐waaî neĭ‐kè, heí‐m̄‐ooĭ fat‐īn heí‐laī me? Kaaú‐iŭ neĭ‐kè, heí‐m̄‐ooĭ síng‐nĝ me? Neĭ heí‐m̄‐shīng‐waī lŏ‐leūk‐kè māt, kwai‐shūk k‘uĭ‐teî me?
8 Yan‐waî neĭ‐ts‘āng lŏ‐leūk‐toh‐kwokₒ, tsoî‐līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung shóh‐uē‐shîng‐kè yān, yīk‐pit‐lŏ‐leūk‐faan‐neĭ; haî yan neĭ laū‐yān‐chi‐huetₒ, yaû heùng‐kíng‐noî‐shēng‐yap, k‘āp k‘eī‐chung kui‐chuê‐kè, laī‐hāng‐k‘eūng‐okₒ‐sẑ‐kè uēn‐koò à.
9 ¶ Waî‐poón‐ka wán‐tó okₒ‐hâng‐kè leî‐yik, seúng‐on‐chì k‘uĭ ch‘aaū‐tsoî ko‐ch‘uè, táng‐k‘uĭ hóh‐ĭ‐t‘uetₒ‐hiu hung‐okₒ‐yān‐kè shaú, kòh‐yān yaŭ‐wôh‐lokₒ!
10 Neĭ‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt līt‐mān hó‐toh, tsaû haî tsẑ‐ts‘uí‐līng‐yūk, kwai‐ue‐poón‐ka, yaû faân‐tsuî t‘oh‐luî tsẑ‐keí‐līng‐wān.
11 Yan‐waî kaû‐shēk yaū‐ts‘eūng‐pekₒ‐ch‘uè ooĭ‐hoi‐sheng, ī‐ch‘é tùng‐leūng yaū‐mūk‐liû‐ch‘uè taapₒ‐yìng à.
12 ¶ K‘aaĭ‐huetₒ‐laī‐heí‐heung‐ts‘uen, yaû‐k‘aaĭ‐tsuî‐hin laī‐laāp‐shēng‐yap, kòh‐yān‐yaŭ‐wôh‐lokₒ!
13 T‘aí‐a, līt‐mān waî‐fóh‐lō‐līk, līt‐kwokₒ waî‐hui‐wā shaí‐tsẑ‐keí‐kaù‐kooî, heí‐m̄‐haî ch‘ut‐ue‐maân‐kwan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè ì‐chí me?
14 Yan‐waî ts‘uēn‐teî pit‐tak‐shik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐wīng‐kwong‐kè chì‐shik‐ch‘ung‐moŏn, hó‐ts‘z̆‐chùng‐shuí‐che‐k‘oì‐taaî‐hoí‐yat‐yeûng.
15 ¶ Peí k‘uĭ lūn‐shè yám‐tsaú, yaû‐ka‐tūk‐māt lōk‐huì‐kè, yaû shaí k‘uĭ yám‐tò‐tsuì, táng hóh‐ĭ t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ ch‘ikₒ‐shan‐kè sau‐kw‘aì, kòh‐yān‐yaŭ‐wôh‐lokₒ!
16 Neĭ tak‐līng‐yūk, m̄‐haî tak‐wīng‐kwong‐ch‘ung‐moŏn lokₒ: neĭ‐teî iù‐yám, yaû iù‐hōk‐faan meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ‐kè yān kóm‐yeûng: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû‐shaú‐kè pooi, pit‐chuèn‐heùng‐kwai‐neĭ, ī‐ch‘é oo‐waì‐kè līng‐yūk, pit‐ka‐toh tsoî‐neĭ‐wīng‐kwong‐chi‐sheûng.
17 Ī‐ch‘é heùng Leî‐pa‐nuên shóh‐hāng k‘eūng‐okₒ‐kè sẑ, uĕ‐k‘āp paaî‐waaî tsaú‐shaù húng‐haakₒ‐kè sẑ, pit‐che‐k‘oì neĭ, haî yan laū‐yān‐huetₒ, yaû yan heùng‐kíng‐teî, heùng‐shēng‐yap, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung shóh‐kui‐chuê‐kè, shóh‐hāng k‘eūng‐okₒ‐kè uēn‐koò à.
18 ¶ Tiu‐hak‐kè tseûng, yaŭ‐mat‐yik‐ch‘uè ni? chì‐lîng chíng‐shīng‐kè yān, tiu‐hak k‘uĭ ch‘ut‐laī ni? waāk chuè‐tseûng k‘āp‐ká‐ngaî‐kaaù‐sz, yaŭ‐mat‐yik‐ch‘uè ni? chì‐lîng chíng‐shīng‐kè yān, chiù‐k‘uĭ shóh‐tsô‐kè kung‐foo; laī‐tsô á‐haú‐kè ngaŭ‐tseûng ni?
19 Heùng‐mūk‐t‘aū wâ, Iù‐síng‐nĝ la; yaû tuì á‐haú‐kè shēk‐t‘aū wâ, Heí‐laī, kòh‐yān‐yaŭ‐wôh‐lokₒ! ts‘ź‐māt ooĭ‐kaaù‐fàn me? T‘aí‐a, t‘ung‐to‐haî peí‐ngān kûng kam‐seung‐chuê‐kè, yat‐ti‐heì‐sik to‐mŏ à.
20 Waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî k‘uĭ shìng‐tîn‐chi‐chung: ts‘uēn‐teî to‐iù tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn suk‐tsîng lokₒ.
