1 TS‘Ź‐HAÛ, À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng, tseung À‐kaapₒ‐yān, Hap‐maĭ‐taāt‐taaî‐kè tsaí Hap‐maân, shing‐ko‐waî, laāp k‘uĭ‐kè tseukₒ‐waî, kàng‐ko‐kwòh faān t‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ‐kè kwan‐cheúng.
2 Wōng‐kè shān‐pūk tsoî wōng‐moōn‐kè to kwaî‐tó kìng‐paaì Hap‐maân: yan‐waî wōng yă‐ts‘āng fan‐foò kóm‐yeûng toî k‘uĭ. Tūk‐haî Mūk‐taí‐koí m̄‐kwaî‐tó, yīk m̄‐kìng‐paaì.
3 Ue‐shî wōng‐kè shān‐pūk tsoî wōng‐moōn‐kè, tuì Mūk‐taí‐koí wâ, Neĭ waî‐mat waī‐pooì wōng‐kè mîng‐lîng ni?
4 Chùng‐yān yāt‐yāt huèn k‘uĭ, k‘uĭ yaû m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng, chùng‐yān tsaû k‘aaĭ‐laī wâ‐kwòh Hap‐maân chi, seúng kìn Mūk‐taí‐koí‐kè sẑ‐ts‘īng tak‐shìng m̄‐tak‐shìng: yan Mūk‐taí‐koí ts‘āng tseung tsẑ‐keí haî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān kóng‐kwòh chùng‐yān‐chi à.
5 Hap‐maân kìn Mūk‐taí‐koí m̄‐kwaî‐tó kìng‐paaì k‘uĭ, tsaû ch‘ung‐moŏn nau‐nô.
6 Chùng‐yān ĭ‐king tseung Mūk‐taí‐koí‐kè tsung‐tsūk wâ Hap‐maân chi; Hap‐maân ĭ‐waī taan‐tūk lōk‐shaú hoî Mūk‐taí‐koí yat‐yān haî siú‐sẑ: tsaû seúng tsiú‐mīt À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ kwokₒ‐noî shóh‐yaŭ Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, tsik‐haî Mūk‐taí‐koí t‘ūng‐tsūk‐kè.
7 Tong À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng taî‐shāp‐î nīn ching‐uēt, tsik‐haî Neī‐saàn‐uēt, chùng‐yān haí Hap‐maân mîn‐ts‘īn, yāt‐yāt uēt‐uēt, tò shāp‐î‐uēt, haî À‐taāt‐uēt, chaì Pò‐ĭ, tsik‐haî chap‐ch‘aū.
8 Hap‐maân tuì À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng wâ, Yaŭ yat chúng‐tsź‐mān, saàn‐pò tsoî wōng kwokₒ kokₒ‐shaáng‐kè tsź‐mān‐chi‐chung; k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè lūt‐faatₒ, kûng maân‐mān‐kè lūt‐faatₒ m̄‐t‘ūng; yīk m̄‐shaú wōng‐kè lūt‐faatₒ: koò‐ts‘ź wōng yūng k‘uĭ‐teî, shāt haî mŏ‐yik.
9 Wōng yeūk ĭ‐waī shîn, hóh‐ĭ ch‘ut‐sheûng‐uê tsiú‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî: ngŏh tsaû tseung ngān yat‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ‐maân, kaau‐kwòh koón‐foò‐yān‐kè shaú, chai‐yāp wōng‐kè foò‐fōng.
10 Ue‐shî wōng tsẑ shaú t‘uetₒ kaaì‐chí, ts‘z̀‐kwòh Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk À‐kaapₒ‐yān, Hap‐maĭ‐taāt‐taaî‐kè tsaí Hap‐maân.
11 Wōng tuì Hap‐maân wâ, Ts‘ź‐ngān ngŏh ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ, ts‘ź mān ngŏh yīk kaau‐kwòh neĭ, yâm‐ì hon‐toî k‘uĭ‐teî.
12 Ching‐uēt shāp‐saam yāt, tsaû kiù wōng‐kè shîn‐sé‐ché laī, t‘okₒ À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng‐kè mēng, i‐chuê Hap‐maân shóh‐fan‐foò, yûng kokₒ‐shaáng, kokₒ‐tsūk, mān‐tsẑ t‘ó‐yam sé, t‘ung‐chi wōng‐kè taaî‐shān, kokₒ‐shaáng‐kè fong‐paakₒ, kûng kokₒ‐tsūk‐kè kwan‐cheúng; yaû yûng wōng‐kè kaaì‐chí yàn‐hiu.
13 Sé‐shue kaau‐kwòh yīk‐tsut, ch‘uēn‐tò wōng‐kè kokₒ‐shaáng, fan‐foò tseung Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān mō‐lûn lŏ‐shiù nuên‐tsaí foŏ‐nuĭ, tsoî yat‐yāt‐noî, tsik‐haî shāp‐î‐uēt shāp‐saam yāt, mēng‐kiù À‐taāt‐uēt, tsûn‐teî tsiú‐mīt shaatₒ‐lūk, lîng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān sź‐mōng, yaû ts‘eúng‐saaì‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè ts‘oī‐māt.
14 Yaû ch‘aau‐sé ni‐ti‐shue, tòng tsokₒ‐sheûng‐uê paan‐hāng kokₒ‐shaáng, t‘ung‐chi kokₒ‐tsūk, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè uê‐peî táng‐haû kòh‐yāt.
15 Yīk‐tsut fûng wōng mîng tsik‐hak heí‐hāng, ts‘ź‐sheûng‐uê ts‘ūng Shue‐shaan‐kung ch‘uēn‐ch‘ut. Wōng kûng Hap‐maân ts‘ŏh‐ch‘uè yám‐tsaú; taân Shue‐shaan‐kè shēng iŭ‐luên.
