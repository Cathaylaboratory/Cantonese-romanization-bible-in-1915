1 PAT‐TS‘ŪNG okₒ‐yān‐chi‐kaì‐maū, pat‐hāng tsuî‐yān‐chi‐tô‐lô, pat‐ts‘ŏh mŏ‐maân‐yān‐chi‐tsôh‐waî, kóm‐yeûng‐chi‐yān, chan‐haî yaŭ‐fuk.
2 K‘uĭ foon‐heí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ; tseung ts‘ź‐lūt‐faatₒ laī yāt yê sz‐seúng.
3 Kóm‐yeûng‐chi‐yān, hó‐peí shuê‐mūk, chùng tsoî hōh‐pin, i‐shī kitₒ‐kwóh; k‘uĭ īp m̄‐līng‐lōk; shóh‐hāng‐chi‐sẑ, mō‐pat‐shûn‐leî.
4 Kòh‐ti okₒ‐yān, uĕ k‘uĭ m̄‐t‘ūng: hó‐peí kuk‐hong, peî‐fung‐ch‘ui‐saàn.
5 Shóh‐ĭ okₒ‐yān shaû‐shám, mŏ shuetₒ‐wâ tuì‐taapₒ, tsuî‐yān m̄‐yāp‐tak î‐yān ooî‐chung.
6 Yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chi‐tò î‐yān‐chi‐lô: waī‐haî okₒ‐yān‐chi‐lô, shî‐pit mīt‐mōng.
