1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ kóm‐yeûng hín‐shî kwòh‐ngŏh; t‘aí‐a, tong hâ‐kwaì shóh‐chùng‐kè, heí‐shaú faatₒ‐ngā kòh‐shī, k‘uĭ‐tsô‐wōng‐ch‘ūng; yaû‐t‘aí‐a, haî‐wōng‐shau‐kotₒ‐chi‐haû hâ‐kwaì‐shóh‐chùng‐kè lokₒ.
2 Tong wōng‐ch‘ūng shīk‐saaì teî‐sheûng‐kè ts‘eng‐ts‘ó, ngŏh‐tsaû‐kóm‐wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, hán‐ts‘itₒ k‘aū neĭ shè‐mĭn: Ngă‐kokₒ tím yaŭ‐nāng‐k‘eĭ‐laāp ni? yan‐waî k‘uĭ haî saì‐siú‐kè à.
3 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waî‐ts‘ź‐sẑ tsẑ‐fooì: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû‐wâ, Ts‘ź‐sẑ pit‐m̄‐shīng‐tsaû kà.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐yeûng hín‐shî‐kwòh‐ngŏh: t‘aí‐a, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘aaĭ‐fóh seung‐chaang, fóh‐tsaû‐t‘an‐mīt taaî‐sham‐uen, yaû seúng‐t‘an kíng‐teî.
5 Ngŏh tsaû wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, hán‐ts‘itₒ‐k‘aū‐neĭ pâ‐shaú: Ngă‐kokₒ tím‐yaŭ‐nāng k‘eĭ‐laāp ni? yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐haî‐saì‐siú‐kè à.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû waî‐ts‘ź‐sẑ tsẑ‐fooì: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû wâ, Ts‘ź‐sẑ to‐m̄‐shīng‐tsaû kà.
7 K‘uĭ yaû kóm‐yeûng‐hín‐shî kwòh‐ngŏh: t‘aí‐a, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî i‐chún‐shīng‐shóh‐heí‐kè ts‘eūng‐pin k‘eĭ‐laāp, k‘uĭ‐shaú‐cha‐chuê‐chún‐shīng.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû tuì ngŏh wâ, À‐moh‐sẑ à, neĭ t‘aí‐kìn mat‐yĕ ni? Ngŏh wâ, Yat‐t‘iū‐chún‐shīng lokₒ, Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh‐tseung yat‐t‘iū‐chún‐shīng, màng‐chīk‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsź‐mān‐chi‐chung: ngŏh‐pit‐m̄‐tsoì‐king‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî à:
9 Ī‐ch‘é Ĭ‐saatₒ‐kè shūng‐yau, ooĭ‐shīng‐waī‐fong‐faì‐kè, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè shìng‐shóh, pit‐peî‐ts‘aān‐hoî‐kè: ngŏh iù‐k‘aaĭ‐to‐kìm heí‐laī kung‐kik Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè ka.
10 ¶ Tong‐shī Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî kè tsaì‐sz, À‐mă‐tsê, shaí‐yān‐huì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè wōng Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm wâ, À‐moh‐sẑ tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka‐chung maū‐faán: ts‘ź‐teî pat‐nāng‐taí‐tóng k‘uĭ‐shóh‐kóng‐kòm‐toh‐shuetₒ‐wâ là.
11 Yan‐waî À‐moh‐sẑ kóm‐wâ, Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm pit‐peî‐to‐kìm‐laī‐shaatₒ, ī‐ch‘é Ĭ‐shik‐līt pit‐haí‐poón‐t‘ó peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐huì‐kè.
12 À‐mă‐tsê yīk‐tuì À‐moh‐sẑ wâ, Sin‐kìn à, iù‐t‘ō‐huì Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kíng‐teî la, tsoî‐kòh‐ch‘uè shīk‐péng, yaû‐tsoî‐kòh‐ch‘uè kóng‐uê‐īn‐kè sẑ la:
13 Waī‐haî m̄‐hó‐haí Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî tsoì‐kóng‐uê‐īn‐kè sẑ: yan‐ts‘ź‐haî wōng‐kè shìng‐shóh, yaû‐haî wōng‐ka‐kè kung‐shat à.
14 Ue‐shî À‐moh‐sẑ taapₒ‐yìng tuì À‐mă‐tsê wâ, Ngŏh‐poón‐loī m̄‐haî sin‐chi, yaû‐m̄‐haî sin‐chi‐kè tsaí; haî‐tsô‐mūk‐yān kim‐tá‐leĭ‐song‐shuê‐kè:
15 Ngŏh‐kan‐ts‘ūng kw‘ān‐yeūng‐kòh‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû suén‐hiu‐ngŏh, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû‐tuì‐ngŏh‐wâ, Huì la, tuì‐ngŏh‐tsź‐mān Ĭ‐shik‐līt kóng‐uê‐īn‐kè sẑ la.
16 Koò‐ts‘ź neĭ‐uē‐kam iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ: Neĭ‐wâ, M̄‐hó‐kóng‐uê‐īn laī‐kung‐kik Ĭ‐shik‐līt, yaû‐m̄‐hó‐ch‘ut‐īn‐uĕ laī‐kung‐kik Ĭ‐saatₒ‐kè ka.
17 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐yeûng‐wâ; Neĭ‐kè ts‘ai tseung‐tsoî‐shēng‐chung tsô‐keî‐foŏ, neĭ‐kè tsaí‐nuĭ peî‐kìm‐chaám‐sź, neĭ‐kè kíng‐teî peí‐shīng‐leūng‐kwòh‐laī‐fan‐kotₒ; neĭ tsẑ‐keí sź‐tsoî pat‐kitₒ‐chi‐teî: ī‐ch‘é Ĭ‐shik‐līt pit‐leī‐pīt‐poón‐t‘ó, peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐huì‐lokₒ.
