1 TONG Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka taî‐shāp nīn, tsik‐haî Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ shāp‐paatₒ nīn kòh‐shī, yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐kè īn‐uĕ.
2 Tong kòh‐shī, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè kwan‐ping, waī‐kw‘àn‐chuê Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng: Yē‐leî‐maĭ yaû kw‘àn‐chuê tsoî oô‐waî‐ping‐kè uên‐noî, tsik‐haî Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng‐kè uk‐luĭ‐t‘aū.
3 Yan‐waî Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka ĭ‐king kw‘àn‐chuê k‘uĭ, wâ, Neĭ yan‐hōh kóng‐uê‐īn wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐tseung ts‘ź‐shēng, kaau‐kwòh Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè shaú, k‘uĭ pit‐tîng ts‘uí huì‐kè;
4 Ī‐ch‘é Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka, pat‐nāng t‘ō‐peî t‘uetₒ‐leī Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè shaú, taân‐haî pit‐peî‐kaau‐lōk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè shaú, yaû haú‐tuì‐haú kûng‐k‘uĭ‐kóng, yaû ngaăn‐tuì‐ngaăn laī‐t‘aí‐chuê‐kè;
5 K‘uĭ to‐ooĭ‐taaì Sai‐taí‐ka tò Pa‐peí‐lūn, k‘uĭ pit‐tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè táng‐toî ngŏh t‘aàm‐kìn k‘uĭ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ: sui‐īn neĭ‐teî kûng Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān chaang‐chìn, heí‐ooĭ‐leî‐taāt me?
6 ¶ Yē‐leî‐maĭ tsaû wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐nĕ, kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
7 T‘aí‐a, neĭ‐kè à‐shuk Sha‐lūng‐chi‐tsź Hap‐nā‐mīt, tseung‐loī‐tò neĭ‐ch‘uè wâ, Iù neĭ maaĭ ngŏh t‘īn, tsoî À‐nā‐tāt‐kè: yan haî maaĭ‐shūk ts‘ź‐t‘īn, haî neĭ ying‐fân‐kè.
8 Ue‐shî ngŏh shuk‐chi‐tsź Hap‐nā‐mīt, i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐kè, laī‐tò oô‐waî‐ping‐kè uên‐noî, kìn ngŏh, yaû tuì‐ngŏh‐wâ, Ts‘éng‐k‘aū neĭ maaĭ ngŏh t‘īn, tsik‐haî tsoî À‐nā‐tāt shūk Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐chi‐teî‐kè: yan‐waî shīng‐shaû ka‐īp, haî neĭ ying‐fân kè, maaĭ‐shūk‐kè sẑ, to haî neĭ ying‐koi tá‐leĭ; neĭ‐iù‐waî tsẑ‐keí‐maaĭ ts‘ź‐t‘īn à. Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh‐chi‐ts‘ź‐īn, yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘ut‐kè.
9 Ngŏh tsaû heùng ngŏh shuk‐tsź Hap‐nā‐mīt maaĭ‐tsoî À‐nā‐tāt kòh‐faaì‐t‘īn, yaû tuî‐ngān‐leŭng kwòh‐k‘uĭ, tsuk‐shò‐ngān shāp‐ts‘at shī‐haakₒ.
10 Ngŏh tsoî kòh‐cheung‐k‘aì ts‘im‐mēng; ī‐ch‘é k‘ap‐yàn, yaû ts‘éng chìng‐yān, tsaû k‘aaĭ t‘in‐p‘īng tuî‐ngān‐leŭng‐kwòh k‘uĭ.
11 Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh tseung kaau‐yīk kòh‐cheung‐k‘aì, tsik‐haî chiù‐lūt‐laî, yaŭ yat‐cheung ĭ‐king yàn‐fung‐kè, t‘ūng‐maaī yat‐cheung meî‐ts‘āng yàn‐fung‐kè:
12 Tong ngŏh à‐shuk‐chi‐tsź Hap‐nā‐mīt mîn‐ts‘īn, yaû tong ts‘im‐mēng lōk kaau‐yīk‐k‘aì‐kè chùng‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn, yaû tong tsoî oô‐waî‐ping uên‐noî taaî‐chùng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn, tseung shóh‐kaau‐yīk‐chi‐k‘aì, kaau‐kwòh Mă‐sai‐ngă‐chi‐suen Neī‐leî‐à‐chi‐tsź Pa‐lūk‐kè shaú.
13 Yaû tong taaî‐chùng‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn, ngŏh cheūk‐līk fan‐foò Pa‐lūk wâ,
14 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ; Iù‐tseung ts‘ź‐k‘aì, tsik‐haî ts‘ź‐kaau‐yīk‐chi‐k‘aì, kòh‐cheung yàn‐fung‐kè, līn‐maaī kòh‐cheung meî‐yàn‐fung‐kè; k‘aaĭ‐laī ts‘ōng‐lōk yat‐kîn ngă‐heì, táng hóh‐ĭ ts‘uēn‐tó‐ch‘uè, chì‐tò toh‐yāt.
15 Yan‐waî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ; Uk‐uĕ kûng t‘īn‐teî, k‘āp p‘ō‐t‘aī‐uēn, tseung‐loī tsoî ts‘ź‐teî, pit‐tsoì peî‐maaĭ‐kè lokₒ.
16 ¶ Ngŏh tseung kaau‐yīk‐kè k‘aì, kaau‐kwòh Neī‐leî‐à‐kè tsaí Pa‐lūk‐chi‐haû, tsaû heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘eī‐k‘aū, wâ,
17 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à! t‘aí‐a, neĭ yă‐ts‘āng tseung taaî‐k‘uēn‐nāng yaû tseung shan‐hoi‐kè shaú, ch‘òng‐tsô t‘in kûng teî, to mŏ yat‐kîn‐sẑ suèn‐haî neĭ naān‐tsô‐kè:
18 Neĭ shi‐king‐sut kwòh‐ts‘in‐shò‐yān, neĭ k‘aaĭ līt‐tsó‐kè tsuî‐hin, tsoî‐haû pò‐yìng, kwai‐ue k‘uĭ tsź‐suen‐kè hung‐waaī: Taaî‐Waî Ts‘uēn‐Nāng‐Chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaû haî neĭ‐kè mēng à,
19 Tsoî maū‐tîng‐kè sẑ ĭ‐waī‐taaî, tsoî hāng‐waī yaŭ ts‘uēn‐nāng‐kè: neĭ‐ngaăn mīng‐kìn‐yān chùng‐tsź‐kè lô‐t‘ō: tsaû chiù‐i yān‐shóh‐hāng‐kè lô, yaû chiù‐i k‘uĭ hāng‐waī shóh‐kitₒ‐shāt‐kè pò‐faan kwòh‐kokₒ‐yān:
20 Yă‐ts‘āng tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐teî, ch‘itₒ‐laāp î‐tseûng kûng k‘eī‐nāng‐kè sẑ, tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐mān kûng chùng‐yān‐chi‐chung; yaû waî tsz̀‐keí tsô‐ch‘ut mīng‐shing, hó‐ts‘z̆ kam‐yāt yat‐yeûng;
21 Yaû tseung î‐tseûng kûng k‘eī‐nāng‐kè sẑ, tseung taaî‐līk‐kè shaú, kûng shan‐hoi‐kè peì, k‘āp taaî‐king‐hoī‐kè sẑ, taaì Ĭ‐shik‐līt neĭ‐kè tsź‐mān ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp‐teî;
22 Yaû peí ts‘ź‐teî kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî, tsik‐haî neĭ heùng‐k‘uĭ līt‐tsó faatₒ‐shaî laī‐peí‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè, haî laū‐uĕ‐k‘āp‐māt‐t‘ōng‐chi‐teî;
23 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû yāp‐laī shīng‐shaû ts‘ź‐teî; waī‐haî m̄‐shûn‐ts‘ūng neĭ‐kè sheng‐yam, yaû m̄‐i neĭ‐kè lūt‐faatₒ laī‐hāng; neĭ shóh‐fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè, yat‐ti to m̄‐tsô: koò‐ts‘ź neĭ lîng ni‐ti‐tsoi‐wôh, kòng‐lām‐ue k‘uĭ‐teî:
24 T‘aí‐hă ti‐chìn‐t‘oī, k‘uĭ‐teî laī‐tò seúng‐kung‐tá ts‘uí‐hiu ts‘ź‐shēng; ī‐ch‘é ts‘ź‐shēng, ĭ‐king kaau‐lōk kung‐tá k‘uĭ kòh‐ti Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè shaú, haî yan to‐kìm, k‘āp kei‐fong, kûng wan‐yīk; neĭ heùng k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐kóng‐kè; ĭ‐king shīng‐tsaû, t‘aí‐a, neĭ to kìn‐kè lokₒ.
25 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ ts‘āng tuì ngŏh wâ, Iù k‘aaĭ ngān maaĭ ni‐faaì‐t‘īn, yaû ts‘éng‐yān tsô‐chìng; waī‐haî ts‘ź‐shēng, ĭ‐king kaau‐lōk Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè shaú lokₒ.
26 ¶ Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ, wâ,
27 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐yaŭ huetₒ‐yūk‐ché‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì: pin‐yat‐kîn‐sẑ haî naān, chì‐tò ngŏh pat‐nāng‐tsô‐kè ni?
28 ¶ Koò‐ts‘ź Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐tseung ts‘ź‐shēng, kaau‐lōk Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè shaú, yaû kaau‐lōk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ‐kè shaú, k‘uĭ ooĭ‐laī ts‘uí‐hiu:
29 Kung‐tá ts‘ź‐shēng‐kè Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān ooĭ‐laī, yaû fòng‐fóh shiu‐mīt ts‘ź‐shēng, līn‐maaī kòh‐ti uk‐uĕ, tsik‐haî tsoî uk‐pooì kòh‐ch‘uè, shiu‐heung hìn‐kwòh Pa‐līk, yaû tîn‐tsaú paaì pīt‐î‐kòh‐shān, laī‐kik ngŏh faatₒ‐nô‐kè.
30 Yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè tsź‐suen, tsẑ nīn‐shiù‐kòh‐shī, tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn, tūk‐haî hāng‐okₒ‐kè che: yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè tsź‐suen, tūk‐haî k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ‐shaú shóh‐tsokₒ‐kè, kik ngŏh faatₒ‐nô, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
31 Yan‐waî tsẑ kìn‐heí kòh‐yāt, chì‐tò kam‐yāt; ni‐kòh shēng tsô kik ngŏh faatₒ‐nô yaû kik ngŏh făn‐hân‐chi‐māt, táng‐tò ngŏh iù‐ch‘uē‐huì k‘uĭ leī‐hoi ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn,
32 Haî yan Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsź‐suen, kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì tsź‐suen, yat‐k‘oì‐kè okₒ‐hâng, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐tsô laī‐kik ngŏh faatₒ‐nô‐kè, haî k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí, kûng līt‐wōng, k‘āp kwan‐cheúng, tsaì‐sz, sin‐chi, shūk Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè yān, k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān.
33 K‘uĭ‐teî chuèn‐pooì m̄‐chuèn‐mîn heùng‐ngŏh: sui‐īn ngŏh kaaù‐fàn k‘uĭ‐teî, hó‐tsó heí‐shan kaaù‐fàn k‘uĭ‐teî, k‘uĭ‐teî sheûng‐ch‘é m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng laī‐tsipₒ‐shaû kaaù‐fàn.
34 K‘uĭ‐teî faán‐chuèn tsoî òn ngŏh mēng laī‐ch‘ing‐kè tîn‐uk, on‐chì k‘uĭ hóh‐oò‐chi‐māt, laī‐chíng‐oo k‘uĭ.
35 K‘uĭ‐teî kìn‐heí Pa‐līk‐kè shūng‐yau, tsik‐haî tsoî Heí‐năm‐tsaí‐kè kuk kòh‐ch‘uè, seúng peí k‘uĭ chùng‐tsaí k‘āp chùng‐nuĭ king‐kwòh Moh‐lōk‐kè fóh; ts‘ź‐haî ngŏh mŏ‐fan‐foò‐kè, yīk mŏ‐tsoî sam‐chung faatₒ‐ch‘ut ì‐sz̀, lîng k‘uĭ teî hāng kòm‐hóh‐oò‐kè sẑ, yaû lîng Yaū‐t‘aaì faân‐tsuî.
36 ¶ Koò‐ts‘ź uē‐kam lûn‐k‘āp ni‐kòh‐shēng, tsik‐haî neĭ‐teî shóh‐kóng‐k‘āp wâ, Ĭ‐king peî‐to‐kìm, peî‐kei‐fong, peî‐wan‐yīk, kaau‐lōk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng shaú‐kè, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ;
37 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh faatₒ‐nau‐nô, yaû făn‐hân, yaû līt‐nô hó‐taaî kòh‐shī, kón‐chūk‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐huì kokₒ‐kwokₒ; tseung‐loī pit‐ts‘ūng kòh‐ch‘uè tsuî‐maaī k‘uĭ‐teî, yaû taaì‐k‘uĭ‐teî tò‐ni‐ch‘uè teî‐fong, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî on‐īn kui‐chuê:
38 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tsô ngŏh tsź‐mān, ngŏh yīk pit‐tsô k‘uĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì:
39 Ngŏh to ooĭ‐lîng k‘uĭ‐teî yaŭ yat‐t‘iū‐sam, yat‐t‘iū‐lô, táng k‘uĭ‐teî kìng‐waì ngŏh, lîng k‘uĭ uĕ‐k‘āp tsoî‐haû‐kè tsź‐suen tak‐shîn‐yik:
40 Yaû kûng k‘uĭ‐teî laāp‐wĭng‐uĕn‐kè yeukₒ, laāp‐ì ts‘z̀‐k‘uĭ‐teî tak shîn‐yik, tsúng‐mŏ‐chuèn‐heùng leī‐hoi k‘uĭ‐teî; yaû peí k‘uĭ‐teî yaŭ kìng‐waì‐ngŏh‐kè sam, táng‐tò mŏ‐leī‐hoi ngŏh à.
41 Fòng‐ch‘é ngŏh tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî faaì‐lōk, ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ‐teî tak‐shîn‐yik, ngŏh shāt‐tsoî tsûn‐ngŏh‐kè sam, tsûn‐ngŏh‐kè shān, tsoî ts‘ź‐teî‐chi‐chung, chùng‐chīk k‘uĭ‐teî,
42 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Hó‐ts‘z̆ ngŏh taaì‐ts‘ź‐taaî‐tsoi‐wôh, kòng‐lām‐ue ni‐ti‐tsź‐mān, tsaû pit‐chiù‐kóm‐yeûng taaì ngŏh shóh‐ying‐shīng kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè shîn‐yik, kòng‐lām‐ue k‘uĭ‐teî.
43 Ī‐ch‘é neĭ shóh‐kóng‐k‘āp‐kè teî, wâ, Haî fong‐faì, līn‐yān kûng ch‘uk‐shang to mŏ; haî kaau‐lōk Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè shaú, tsoî ts‘ź‐teî‐kè t‘īn, pit‐yaŭ‐yān maaĭ‐kè.
44 Chùng‐yān pit‐k‘aaĭ ngān‐leŭng maaĭ‐t‘īn, yaû ts‘im‐mēng lōk k‘aì, yīk yàn‐hūng‐k‘aì, yaû ts‘éng yān tsô‐chìng, tsoî Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐chi‐teî, yaû tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsóh‐yaû‐kè teî‐fong, tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè chùng‐shēng‐yap, tsoî shaan‐teî‐kè shēng‐yap, kûng p‘īng‐uēn‐kè shēng‐yap, yaû tsoî naām‐fong‐kè shēng‐yap: yan‐waî ngŏh pit‐ooĭ‐lîng lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān faan‐laī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
