1 K‘AŪ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘eng ngŏh‐chi‐t‘ó‐kò, chún ngŏh foo‐kiù‐chi‐sheng, taāt‐tò neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn.
2 Ngŏh shaû‐foó‐chi‐yāt, k‘aū neĭ mōk che‐chuê‐kòh‐mîn m̄‐koò ngŏh; sui iù chak‐ĭ‐t‘eng ngŏh: ngŏh foo‐kiù‐chi‐shī, kap‐ts‘uk ying‐shīng ngŏh.
3 Ngŏh‐chi‐suì‐uēt, hó‐ts‘z̆ in‐chi‐siu‐saàn, ngŏh‐chi‐kwat kon‐shaù, hó‐ts‘z̆ peî‐fóh‐shiu‐kwòh.
4 Ngŏh sam peî‐chaakₒ, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ó‐chi‐kon‐foo; ngŏh ĭ‐king mōng‐keì yám‐shīk.
5 Yan ngŏh shī‐sheūng t‘aàn‐sik foo‐kiù, ngŏh p‘eī‐yūk shaù tò t‘ipₒ‐kwat.
6 Ngŏh hó‐ts‘z̆ kw‘òng‐yĕ‐chi‐t‘aī‐oō: hó‐ts‘z̆ laân‐teî‐chi‐ch‘i‐hiu.
7 Ngŏh shīng‐maăn m̄‐fàn‐tak‐cheūk, hó‐ts‘z̆ uk‐téng‐chi‐koo‐niŭ tūk‐suk.
8 Ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, shīng‐yāt hei‐miŭ ngŏh; hó‐ch‘eung‐kw‘ōng kung‐kik ngŏh, chí‐chuê ngŏh laī‐shaî‐uên.
9 Ngŏh shīk fooi‐naī, yaū‐uē shīk péng, shóh‐yám‐ché yīk‐yaŭ ngaăn‐luî k‘au‐maaī,
10 Yan neĭ‐chi‐nô‐heì, kûng neĭ‐chi‐tsang‐oò: neĭ yă‐ts‘āng kuí‐ko ngŏh, yaû wing‐tai.
11 Ngŏh‐chi‐nīn‐keí, hó‐ts‘z̆ yāt‐yíng keì‐ts‘ē; yīk hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ó kòm kon‐foo.
12 Waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wĭng‐uĕn ts‘ŏh‐waî; peî‐yān‐keì‐nîm, shaì‐shaì mō‐tsûn.
13 Neĭ pit hing‐heí līn‐sut Sun‐shēng: yan shi‐yan chi‐shī ĭ‐kân, shóh‐tîng‐chi‐k‘eī ĭ‐tò.
14 Yan‐waî neĭ‐chi‐pūk‐yān, chung‐ì Sun‐shēng‐chi‐waī‐shēk, līn naī‐ch‘ān to t‘aí‐chûng.
15 Kokₒ‐kwokₒ to pit kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mēng, teî‐sheûng līt‐wōng, to kìng‐waì neĭ‐chi‐tsuen‐wīng.
16 Yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoì kìn Sun‐shēng, k‘uĭ mēng‐chi‐wīng‐wā, hín‐în‐ch‘ut‐laī.
17 K‘uĭ pit t‘eng p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐ché‐chi‐k‘eī‐t‘ó, m̄‐hing‐fat k‘uĭ‐chi‐shóh‐k‘aū.
18 Ni‐ti‐ying‐tong sé‐lōk shue‐sheûng, ch‘uēn‐kwòh tsź‐suen: táng haû‐loī shaû‐tsô‐chi‐mān, tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
19 Yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yă‐ts‘āng ts‘ūng chì‐ko‐chi‐shìng‐shóh foó‐shan; ts‘ung t‘in‐sheûng kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ teî‐hâ;
20 Uên t‘eng peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐ché‐chi‐t‘aàn‐sheng; fòng shang tsaû‐sź‐chi‐yān;
21 Táng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mēng, ch‘uēn‐yeūng Sun‐shēng, táng k‘uĭ‐chi‐tsaàn‐tsûng, ch‘uēn‐yeūng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng;
22 Tsik‐haî chùng‐mān kûng līt‐kwokₒ tsuî‐tsaāp, fūk‐sẑ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐shī.
23 Chûng tsoî poòn‐lô, k‘uĭ lîng ngŏh‐teî līk‐leûng shui‐yeūk; yīk kaám‐shiú ngŏh‐teî nīn‐suì.
24 Ngŏh tsaû wâ, Ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì à, m̄‐hó lîng ngŏh chung‐nīn huì‐shaì: neĭ‐chi‐nīn‐suì, haî shaì‐shaì‐mō‐tsûn.
25 Neĭ tsoî sheûng‐koó, laāp teî‐chi‐kei: t‘in yīk haî neĭ shaú‐tsô‐chi‐kung.
26 T‘in‐teî to ooĭ‐paaî‐waaî, waī‐tūk neĭ haî sheūng‐ts‘uēn: t‘in‐teî hó‐ts‘z̆ i‐fūk tsîm‐laân; neĭ tseung k‘uĭ oôn‐kwòh, hó‐ts‘z̆ oôn i‐fūk; ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ pit‐tîng koí‐pìn:
27 Waī‐haî neĭ mŏ‐kang‐koí, neĭ‐chi‐nīn‐suì mō‐tsûn.
28 Neĭ pūk‐chi‐tsź‐suen, pit‐tîng sheūng‐ts‘uēn, k‘uĭ‐chi‐haû‐yuî, shî‐pit tsoî neĭ‐chi‐ts‘īn k‘eĭ‐wán.
