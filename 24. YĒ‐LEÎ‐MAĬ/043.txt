1 YĒ‐LEÎ‐MAĬ heùng chùng‐tsź‐mān kóng‐uēn‐saaì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘uĭ‐teî Sheûng‐Taì‐kè īn‐uĕ, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐t‘okₒ‐lōk k‘uĭ kóng kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ kòh‐shī,
2 Ue‐shî Hōh‐sha‐ngă‐chi‐tsź À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, kûng Ka‐leĭ‐à‐chi‐tsź Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, t‘ūng‐maaī taaî‐chùng kiu‐ngô‐kè yān, tuì Yē‐leî‐maĭ kóng, wâ, Neĭ kóng hui‐ká‐kè: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ngŏh‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, m̄‐ts‘āng tá‐faatₒ‐neĭ‐laī wâ, Neĭ‐teî pat‐hóh yāp‐tò Oi‐k‘āp, tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè keì‐kui à:
3 Taân‐haî Neī‐leĭ‐à‐chi‐tsź Pa‐lūk, kaaú‐iŭ neĭ kung‐kik ngŏh‐teî, seúng kaau ngŏh‐teî lōk Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐chi‐shaú, táng k‘uĭ‐teî hóh‐ĭ shaatₒ ngŏh‐teî, yaû lŏ‐leūk ngŏh‐teî, poon‐huì Pa‐peí‐lūn.
4 Kóm‐tsaû Ka‐leĭ‐à‐chi‐tsź Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, kûng ping‐ka chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, t‘ūng chùng‐tsź‐mān, m̄‐shûn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sheng‐yam, laī‐chuê‐tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐chi‐teî.
5 Waī‐haî Ka‐leĭ‐à‐chi‐tsź Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, kûng ping‐ka chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, tseung‐saaì‐Yaū‐t‘aaì uē‐shîng‐kè yān, tsik‐haî ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî peî‐kón‐chūk tò‐kokₒ‐kwokₒ‐ch‘uè faán‐laī keì‐chuê Yaū‐t‘aaì‐chi‐teî‐kè;
6 Yaŭ chùng‐yān, k‘āp foŏ‐nuĭ, k‘āp hoī‐t‘ūng, kûng‐maaī wōng‐ka‐kè chùng‐nuĭ, k‘āp oô‐waî ping‐cheúng Neī‐pò‐saatₒ‐la‐taam shóh‐laū‐lōk Sha‐faan‐chi‐suen À‐hei‐kom‐chi‐tsź K‘eī‐taaî‐leî, kûng sin‐chi Yē‐leî‐maĭ, k‘āp Neī‐leî‐à‐chi‐tsź Pa‐lūk‐ch‘uè‐kè kokₒ‐yān.
7 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû yāp‐tò Oi‐k‘āp‐teî: yan k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐shûn‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sheng‐yam: k‘uĭ‐teî laī‐tò Taapₒ‐peí‐nik‐ch‘uè.
8 Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, tsoî Taapₒ‐peí‐nik‐ch‘uè, kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ, wâ,
9 Iù peí‐shaú chap‐chuê taaî‐shēk‐t‘aū, tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān ngaăn‐ts‘īn k‘aaĭ‐laī ts‘ōng‐maaī tsoî chuen‐liû fooi‐naī‐chi‐chung, tsoî Taapₒ‐peí‐nik yāp Faatₒ‐lŏ wōng‐kung‐chi‐shóh;
10 Yaû tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐shaí‐yān‐huì ts‘uí ngŏh pūk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, yaû tsoî ngŏh shóh‐ts‘ōng‐maaī ni‐ti‐shēk‐chi‐sheûng, kìn‐laāp k‘uĭ wōng‐waî; k‘uĭ pit‐cheung‐hoi‐k‘uĭ‐kè wā‐meĭ‐waī‐mōk, tsoî ts‘ź‐shēk‐chi‐sheûng.
11 K‘uĭ pit‐ooĭ‐laī, yaû kung‐tá Oi‐k‘āp‐teî, tîng‐tuēt shaû‐sź‐kè, tsaû lîng k‘uĭ sź‐mōng; tîng‐tuēt peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè, tsaû lŏ‐leūk k‘uĭ‐teî; tîng‐tuēt peî‐kìm‐laī‐hoî‐kè, tsaû peí‐kìm‐laī‐hoî.
12 Ngŏh pit tsoî Oi‐k‘āp chùng‐shān‐kè uk, faatₒ‐cheūk‐fóh shiu‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî; k‘uĭ yaû lŏ‐leūk poon‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐huì: k‘uĭ pit‐k‘aaĭ Oi‐k‘āp‐kè‐teî laī‐ch‘uen‐cheukₒ, hó‐ts‘z̆ mūk‐yān cheukₒ i‐fūk kóm; ī‐ch‘é yaū kòh‐ch‘uè p‘īng‐on ch‘ut‐huì.
13 Tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐teî Paakₒ‐shî‐māk‐kè ch‘uĕ‐tseûng, k‘uĭ to ooĭ‐waí‐ch‘aakₒ; yaû peí‐fóh‐shiu‐mīt Oi‐k‘āp chùng‐shān‐kè uk.
