1 KÒM shīng‐sam hāng‐ts‘ź‐sẑ‐chi‐haû, À‐shūt wōng Sai‐nā‐kei‐laāp laī‐ts‘am‐yāp Yaū‐t‘aaì, ch‘itₒ‐laāp yīng‐p‘oōn kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐teî pó‐cheùng‐kè shēng, seúng ts‘uí‐hiu kwai‐shūk tsẑ‐keí.
2 Hei‐sai‐ka kìn Sai‐nā‐kei‐laāp laī‐tò, tîng‐ì chaang‐chìn kung‐kik Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng,
3 Tsaû kûng k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, k‘āp yŭng‐sẑ, taaî‐chùng sheung‐ĭ, seúng sak shēng‐ngoî kokₒ‐uēn‐ts‘uēn‐kè shuí: chùng‐yān tsaû laī pong‐chôh.
4 Kóm‐tsaû tsuî‐tsaāp hó‐toh tsź‐mān, t‘īn‐sak kokₒ‐uēn‐ts‘uēn, yīk t‘īn‐sak t‘ung‐laū ni‐ch‘uè‐teî‐kè k‘ai, tsaû wâ, À‐shūt wōng laī‐chi‐shī, shaí‐mat peí k‘uĭ wán‐tak kòm‐toh shuí à?
5 Hei‐sai‐ka tsẑ‐k‘eūng, tsaû kìn‐chuk kòh‐ti k‘ing‐titₒ‐kè shēng‐oōn, chuk‐ko chì‐tò shuè‐laū; ī‐ch‘é yaû chuk‐yat ts‘eūng‐oōn tsoì‐ngoî‐pîn, yaû sau‐ts‘ap Taaî‐waî‐shēng‐chung‐kè Maĭ‐lōh, yaû tsô hó‐toh kwan‐heì kûng t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī.
6 K‘uĭ ch‘itₒ‐laāp mŏ‐sẑ‐cheúng, chì koón‐hāt tsź‐mān, tsuî‐tsaāp tò tsẑ‐keí‐ch‘uè, tsoî‐shēng‐moōn‐kè footₒ‐teî, yûng shîn‐īn on‐waì k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè sam, wâ,
7 Iù faatₒ‐k‘eūng kong‐ngaî, maĭ yan À‐shūt wōng k‘āp kan‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng, tsaû waì‐kuî sòng‐taám: yan‐waî t‘ūng‐maaī‐ngŏh‐teî‐kè shìng‐kwòh t‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ‐kè à:
8 T‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ‐kè haî yūk‐peì; t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh‐teî, pong‐chôh ngŏh‐teî, waî ngŏh‐teî chaang‐chìn, haî ngŏh‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à. Tsź‐mān í‐laaî Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Hei‐sai‐ka‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsaû on‐lōk à.
9 ¶ Ts‘ź‐haû, À‐shūt wōng Sai‐nā‐kei‐laāp, kûng k‘uĭ ts‘uēn‐kwan, (kung‐kik La‐kat‐chi‐shī, tsaû tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ pūk huì Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng,) huì kìn Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Hei‐sai‐ka, kûng faān‐tsoî‐Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, wâ,
10 À‐shūt wōng Sai‐nā‐kei‐laāp kóm‐yeûng wâ, Neĭ‐teî í‐laaî mat‐shuī, chì neĭ chûng‐kui‐tsoî waī‐kw‘àn‐kè Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng ni?
11 Hei‐sai‐ka wâ, Ngŏh‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, pit ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh‐teî, t‘uetₒ‐leī À‐shūt wōng‐kè shaú, heí‐m̄‐haî kóm huèn neĭ‐teî, tiu‐heì‐tsz̀‐keí shaû‐kei‐hotₒ laī‐sź me?
12 Ni‐kòh Hei‐sai‐ka heí‐m̄‐haî ch‘uē‐huì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yau‐t‘aān, kûng k‘uĭ tsaì‐t‘aān, fan‐foò Yaū‐t‘aaì k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù tsoî yat‐t‘aān shūng‐paaì; ī‐ch‘é tsoî kòh‐t‘aān‐sheûng fān‐heung me?
13 Neĭ‐teî heí‐m̄‐chi ngŏh kûng ngŏh līt‐tsó heùng î‐teî‐kè chùng‐tsź‐mān shóh‐hāng me? ts‘ź līt‐pong‐chi‐teî‐kè chùng‐shān, sik‐yāt heí‐nāng‐kaù‐tak k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè kwokₒ t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh shaú me?
14 Ngŏh līt‐tsó shóh‐tsûn‐mīt‐kè līt‐kwokₒ, k‘eī‐chùng‐shān‐chung shuī‐nāng‐kaù‐tak k‘uĭ tsź‐mān t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh shaú ni? chì neĭ‐teî‐kè Shān, yaŭ‐nāng ch‘íng‐kaù neĭ‐teî t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh shaú ni?
15 Koò‐ts‘ź uē‐kam maĭ yūng Hei‐sai‐ka kóm‐yeûng hei‐moōn neĭ‐teî, yaŭ‐waāk neĭ‐teî, kóm‐yeûng maĭ‐sùn k‘uĭ: yan‐waî meî‐yaŭ hōh‐mān, hōh‐kwokₒ‐kè shān, nāng‐ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsź‐mān t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh shaú, k‘āp t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh līt‐tsó‐kè shaú: hōh‐fòng neĭ‐teî‐kè Shān, heí‐nāng ch‘íng‐kaù neĭ‐teî t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh shaú me?
16 Yaû k‘uĭ‐kè pūk, kàng‐yaŭ shuetₒ‐wâ waí‐p‘òng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Sheûng‐Taì, k‘āp Sheûng‐Taì‐kè pūk Hei‐sai‐ka.
17 K‘uĭ yīk sé‐shue p‘òng‐tūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, waí‐p‘òng kóm wâ, Hó‐ts‘z̆ līt‐pong‐teî‐kè chùng‐shān pat‐nāng kaù k‘uĭ tsź‐mān t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh shaú, Hei‐sai‐ka‐kè Shān, yīk pat‐nāng ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ tsź‐mān t‘uetₒ‐leī ngŏh shaú lokₒ.
18 K‘uĭ‐teî yaû yûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān t‘ó‐wâ, heùng tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng shēng‐oōn‐kè tsź‐mān, taaî‐sheng‐foo‐kiù, seúng haakₒ‐fong k‘uĭ‐teî, iŭ‐luên k‘uĭ‐teî; chì‐î ts‘uí kòh‐ti‐shēng.
19 Yaû k‘uĭ‐teî kóng‐k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, hó‐ts‘z̆ kóng‐k‘āp teî‐sheûng chùng‐tsź‐mān‐kè shān, yān‐shaú‐shóh‐tsô‐kè kóm‐yeûng.
20 Yan‐ts‘ź Hei‐sai‐ka wōng kûng À‐moh‐sẑ‐kè tsaí sin‐chi Ĭ‐ts‘oì‐à heùng‐t‘in foo‐k‘aū.
21 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû tá‐faatₒ yat‐kòh t‘in‐sz̀ huì‐tò À‐shūt wōng‐kè yīng, mīt‐tsuēt shóh‐yaŭ taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ kûng k‘uĭ chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, k‘āp chùng‐tseùng‐shuì. À‐shūt wōng tsaû sau‐kw‘aì faan‐kwai k‘uĭ poón‐t‘ó. Keì‐faan‐tò yāp k‘uĭ shān‐kè tîn‐uk, ch‘ut‐tsoî tsẑ‐keí‐iu‐kè tsaí, tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè k‘aaĭ to‐kìm laī‐shaatₒ k‘uĭ lokₒ.
22 Kóm‐yeûng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘íng‐kaù Hei‐sai‐ka kûng kui‐tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè tsź‐mān, t‘uetₒ‐leī À‐shūt wōng Sai‐nā‐kei‐laāp‐kè shaú, yīk t‘uetₒ‐leī k‘uĭ‐teî chùng‐ch‘aū‐tīk‐kè shaú; ī‐ch‘é sz̀‐waī to pó‐yaû k‘uĭ.
23 Yaŭ hó‐toh‐yān taaì laĭ‐māt tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ī‐ch‘é k‘aaĭ pó‐māt sùng‐kwòh Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Hei‐sai‐ka: kóm‐tsaû Hei‐sai‐ka wōng tsoî līt‐kwokₒ ngaăn‐ts‘īn ch‘eung‐shîng tsuen‐taaî à.
24 ¶ Tong kòh‐yāt‐tsź Hei‐sai‐ka yaŭ‐pêng tseung‐kân‐sź, tsaû k‘eī‐t‘ó Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kûng‐k‘uĭ‐kóng, ī‐ch‘é k‘aaĭ yau‐ching chí‐shî k‘uĭ.
25 Taân‐haî Hei‐sai‐ka m̄‐i‐chuê k‘uĭ shóh‐shaû‐kè yan‐tín laī‐pò‐taapₒ; yan‐waî k‘uĭ sam‐chung kiu‐ngô: koò‐ts‘ź yaŭ nau‐nô lām‐tò k‘uĭ, k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng.
26 Taân‐haî Hei‐sai‐ka kokₒ‐tak sam‐chung kiu‐ngô, tsaû tsẑ‐keí him‐pei, Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān kaai‐haî kóm‐yeûng, chì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā nau‐nô tsoî Hei‐sai‐ka‐kè yāt‐tsź, m̄‐kòng‐lām‐tò k‘uĭ‐teî.
27 Hei‐sai‐ka tak‐fòh‐ts‘oī, shaû tsuen‐wīng shâm‐toh: waî tsẑ‐keí tsô foó‐foò, shau‐ts‘ōng kam, ngān, pó‐shēk, heung‐liû, kûng tóng‐p‘aaī, k‘āp kokₒ‐yeûng hó‐kè heì‐mĭng;
28 Yaû waî ka‐tsang‐kè kuk, yaū, tsaú, tsô ts‘ong‐lăm; yaû waî kokₒ‐ch‘uk‐shang k‘āp yeūng‐kw‘ān tsô laān.
29 Kim‐chi waî tsẑ‐keí ch‘itₒ‐laāp kokₒ‐shēng, kûng ngaū‐yeūng‐kw‘ān‐kè ch‘aán‐īp shâm‐toh: yan‐waî Sheûng‐Taì ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ hó‐toh ch‘aán‐īp à.
30 Ni‐kòh Hei‐sai‐ka yīk sak K‘eī‐fàn sheûng uēn‐ts‘uēn‐kè shuí, tseung‐laī yăn‐hâ laū‐yāp Taaî‐waî‐shēng‐kè sai‐pîn. Hei‐sai‐ka faān‐shóh‐hāng‐kè sẑ leî‐taāt à.
31 Waī‐haî Pa‐peí‐lūn chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, shóh‐tá‐faatₒ‐tò k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè, seúng mân tsoî ts‘ź‐teî shóh‐tsô‐kè yau‐ching, ni‐ti‐sẑ Sheûng‐Taì tsaû yâm Hei‐sai‐ka, seúng shì k‘uĭ, chì‐lîng chi‐tò k‘uĭ sam‐chung faān‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè à.
32 Hei‐sai‐ka shóh‐hāng k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, k‘āp k‘uĭ shîn‐sẑ, t‘aí‐a, kaai‐tsoì‐lōk sin‐chi À‐moh‐sẑ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐ts‘oì‐à hín‐shî‐kè shue, k‘āp tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue.
33 Hei‐sai‐ka kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám, chùng‐yān tsòng k‘uĭ tsoî Taaî‐waî‐kè tsaí fān‐mô‐shaan‐pin‐ch‘uè à: Yaū‐t‘aaì chùng‐yān k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān, kaai tsoî k‘uĭ‐sź‐chi‐shī tsuen‐kìng k‘uĭ. K‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Mă‐nā‐sai, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
