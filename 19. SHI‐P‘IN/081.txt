1 SHEÛNG‐TAÌ haî ngŏh‐teî‐chi‐līk‐leûng: ying‐tong tsaàn‐tsûng k‘uĭ, ying‐tong foon‐heí tsaàn‐meĭ Ngă‐kokₒ‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì.
2 Ch‘eùng‐shi tá‐koó, t‘aān hó‐k‘ām‐shat.
3 Mooĭ‐fūng ch‘oh‐yat shāp‐nğ, haî tsitₒ‐k‘eī‐chi‐yāt, ch‘ui‐heí heúng‐kokₒ.
4 Ni‐ti haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt sheūng‐laî, Ngă‐kokₒ‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, shóh‐laāp‐chi‐laĭ‐ī.
5 K‘uĭ kung‐kik Oi‐k‘āp‐teî chi‐shī, yă‐ts‘āng kûng Yeukₒ‐shat laāp ni‐ti tsô‐chìng‐kuì: tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè ngŏh shóh‐t‘eng‐kìn‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, ngŏh to m̄‐mīng‐paāk.
6 K‘uĭ wâ, ngŏh lîng paakₒ‐sìng pokₒ‐t‘aū, m̄‐shaí taam‐yĕ: shaú‐sheûng m̄‐shaí waăn‐laām.
7 Tong foó‐naân‐chi‐shī, neĭ foo‐kiù ngŏh, ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng kaù neĭ; ts‘ūng luī‐sheng‐chi‐māt‐ch‘uè ying wăn neĭ: tsoî Maĭ‐leî‐pa shuí‐pin laī‐shì neĭ.
8 Ngŏh‐chi‐paakₒ‐sìng à, neĭ ying‐iù t‘eng ngŏh wâ: Ĭ‐shik‐līt à, neĭ yeūk‐haî háng t‘eng ngŏh wâ, ngŏh tsaû peí chìng‐kuì kwòh‐neĭ;
9 Tsoî neĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung, m̄‐hó yaŭ pīt‐ti‐shān; ī‐ch‘é m̄‐hó paaì‐kwaî î‐pong‐chi‐shān.
10 Ngŏh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, yă‐ts‘āng taaì neĭ ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp: neĭ tá‐hoi kòh‐haú, ngŏh tsaû lîng neĭ shīk‐paaú.
11 Mō‐n î ngŏh‐chi‐paakₒ‐sìng, m̄‐t‘eng ngŏh wâ; Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, m̄‐kwai‐shûn ngŏh.
12 Koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh yaū‐tak k‘uĭ sam‐chung pooì‐yīk: cha tsẑ‐keí chué‐ì laī‐tsô.
13 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh‐chi‐paakₒ‐sìng, t‘eng‐ts‘ūng ngŏh wâ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, háng hāng ngŏh lô!
14 Ngŏh tsaû ts‘uk‐ts‘uk chaì‐fūk k‘uĭ‐chi‐tīk‐yān, tsoì shan‐shaú kung‐kik‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐ch‘aū‐yān.
15 Kóm‐tsaû uèn‐hân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ché, pit‐tîng kwai‐fūk: ngŏh‐chi‐paakₒ‐sìng, wĭng‐uĕn laū‐ts‘uēn.
16 Ngŏh pit tseung hó‐māk laī‐yeŭng‐yūk k‘uĭ: lîng k‘uĭ shīk‐paaú p‘oōn‐shēk shóh‐ch‘ut‐chi‐māt‐t‘ōng.
