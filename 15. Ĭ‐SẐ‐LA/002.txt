1 PA‐PEÍ‐LŪN wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, sik‐yāt shóh‐lŏ‐leūk tò Pa‐peí‐lūn‐kè, tsik‐haî chuê‐tsoî shaáng‐noî peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè tsź‐mān, haî peî‐shik‐fòng, tsoì faan‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì, kokₒ‐kwai‐ooī poón‐shēng, tsik‐haî t‘ūng‐maaī Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ laī‐kè;
2 K‘eī shò‐mūk līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû: Yē‐shue‐à, Neī‐hei‐maĭ, Sai‐loî‐à, Leĭ‐loī‐à, Mūk‐taí‐koí, Pit‐shaan, Māt‐paatₒ, Pit‐waai, Leĭ‐wāng, Pa‐nā. Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsź‐mān yān‐shò līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû:
3 Pa‐lūk‐kè tsź‐suen î‐ts‘in‐yat‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐î yān.
4 Shî‐faatₒ‐t‘aī‐à‐kè tsź‐suen saam‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐î yān.
5 À‐la‐kè tsź‐suen ts‘at‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐nğ yān.
6 Yē‐shue‐à kûng Yeuk‐aapₒ‐kè haû‐yuî, tsik‐haî Pa‐hap‐moh‐aapₒ‐kè tsź‐suen î‐ts‘in‐paatₒ‐paakₒ‐shāp‐î yān.
7 Ĭ‐laān‐kè tsź‐suen yat‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐sz̀ yān.
8 Saatₒ‐t‘ó‐kè tsź‐suen kaú‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐nğ yān.
9 Saatₒ‐koí‐kè tsź‐suen ts‘at‐paakₒ‐lūk‐shāp yān.
10 Pa‐neī‐kè tsź‐suen lūk‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐î yān.
11 Peí‐paaì‐kè tsź‐suen lūk‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐saam yān.
12 Aapₒ‐kaapₒ‐kè tsź‐suen yat‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐î yān.
13 À‐toh‐neī‐kon‐kè tsź‐suen lūk‐paakₒ‐lūk‐shāp‐lūk yān.
14 Pit‐waai‐kè tsź‐suen î‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐lūk yān.
15 À‐ting‐kè tsź‐suen sz̀‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐sz̀ yān.
16 Shūk Hei‐sai‐ka, À‐tāk‐kè tsź‐suen kaú‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
17 Peí‐ts‘oì‐kè tsź‐suen saam‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐saam yān.
18 Yeukₒ‐la‐kè tsź‐suen yat‐paakₒ‐shāp‐î yān.
19 Hap‐shûn‐kè tsź‐suen î‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐saam yān.
20 Kat‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen kaú‐shāp‐nğ yān.
21 Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng‐kè tsź‐mān yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐saam yān.
22 Neī‐t‘ōh‐faatₒ‐kè tsź‐mān nğ‐shāp‐lūk yān.
23 À‐nā‐tāt‐kè tsź‐mān yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
24 Aapₒ‐mă‐fat‐kè tsź‐mān sz̀‐shāp‐î yān.
25 Kei‐līt‐à‐lām kûng Kei‐fei‐la k‘āp Peí‐lūk‐kè tsź‐mān kûng ts‘at‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐saam yān.
26 La‐mă k‘āp Ka‐pa‐kè tsź‐mān kûng lūk‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐yat yān.
27 Māk‐mă‐kè tsź‐mān yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐î yān.
28 Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî k‘āp Oi‐kè tsź‐mān kûng î‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐saam yān.
29 Neī‐p‘òh‐kè tsź‐mān nğ‐shāp‐î yān.
30 Moōt‐pit‐kè tsź‐mān yat‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐lūk yān.
31 K‘eī‐ts‘z̀ Ĭ‐laān‐kè tsź‐suen yat‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐sz̀ yān.
32 Hap‐lām‐kè tsź‐mān saam‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp yān.
33 Lūt kûng Hap‐tāk k‘āp À‐nōh‐kè tsź‐mān kûng ts‘at‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐nğ yān.
34 Yē‐leî‐koh‐kè tsź‐mān saam‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐nğ yān.
35 Sai‐nā‐kè tsź‐mān saam‐ts‘in‐lūk‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp yān.
36 Tsaì‐sz shūk Yē‐shue‐à ka Yē‐t‘aaì‐à‐kè tsź‐suen kaú‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐saam yān.
37 Yam‐māk‐kè tsź‐suen yat‐ts‘in‐nğ‐shāp‐î yān.
38 Pa‐shūt‐kè tsź‐suen yat‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐ts‘at yān.
39 Hap‐lām‐kè tsź‐suen yat‐ts‘in‐shāp‐ts‘at yān.
40 Leî‐meî‐yān yaŭ Hōh‐t‘a‐wai‐kè haû‐yuî, tsik‐haî Yē‐shue‐à kûng Kaapₒ‐mīt‐kè tsź‐suen ts‘at‐shāp‐sz̀ yān.
41 Au‐koh‐kè: yaŭ À‐saatₒ‐kè tsź‐suen yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
42 Hon‐shaú‐moōn‐kè tsź‐suen: yaŭ Sha‐lūng‐kè tsź‐suen, À‐tāk‐kè tsź‐suen, Taāt‐moōn‐kè tsź‐suen, À‐kuk‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐taí‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen, Shuetₒ‐paaì‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng yat‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐kaú yān.
43 Kung‐fûng tîn‐yīk: yaŭ Sai‐hap‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐so‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen, Taapₒ‐pūk‐kè tsź‐suen,
44 K‘eī‐lūk‐kè tsź‐suen, Sai‐à‐kè tsź‐suen, Pa‐tûn‐kè tsź‐suen,
45 Leî‐pa‐nā‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐ka‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen, À‐kuk‐kè tsź‐suen,
46 Hap‐kaapₒ‐kè tsź‐suen, Saatₒ‐maaĭ‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐naān‐kè tsź‐suen,
47 Kat‐tāk‐kè tsź‐suen, Ka‐hap‐kè tsź‐suen, Leî‐à‐ngă‐kè tsź‐suen,
48 Leĭ‐sùn‐kè tsź‐suen, Neī‐koh‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen, Ka‐saàn‐kè tsź‐suen,
49 Oo‐saatₒ‐kè tsź‐suen, Pa‐sai‐à‐kè tsź‐suen, Peí‐ts‘oì‐kè tsź‐suen,
50 Aapₒ‐nā‐kè tsź‐suen, Maĭ‐oô‐nîng‐kè tsź‐suen, Neī‐p‘ó‐sam‐kè tsź‐suen,
51 Paatₒ‐pat‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐koó‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐fat‐kè tsź‐suen,
52 Paatₒ‐lūk‐kè tsź‐suen, Maĭ‐hei‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐sha‐kè tsź‐suen,
53 Paatₒ‐kuk‐kè tsź‐suen, Sai‐sai‐la‐kè tsź‐suen, T‘a‐mă‐kè tsź‐suen,
54 Neī‐ts‘ē‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐taí‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen.
55 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn chùng‐pūk‐kè tsź‐suen: yaŭ Sóh‐t‘aaì‐kè tsź‐suen, Sóh‐peí‐līt‐kè tsź‐suen, Peí‐lô‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen,
56 Ngă‐la‐kè tsź‐suen, Taāt‐kwan‐kè tsź‐suen, Kat‐tāk‐kè tsź‐suen,
57 Shî‐faatₒ‐t‘aī‐à‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐tik‐kè tsź‐suen, Sai‐pa‐yam, P‘òh‐kei‐lāk‐kè tsź‐suen, À‐maĭ‐kè tsź‐suen.
58 Taân‐faān kung‐fûng tîn‐yīk k‘āp Shóh‐lōh‐moōn chùng‐pūk‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng saam‐paakₒ‐kaú‐shāp‐î yān.
59 Ts‘ūng Tāk‐maĭ‐la, Tāk‐hap‐saatₒ, Kei‐lūk, Aapₒ‐taân, Yam‐māk, sheŭng‐laī‐kè: pat‐nāng piú‐mīng k‘uĭ tsó‐foô ka k‘āp tsung‐tsūk haî shūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān m̄‐haî, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû:
60 Teî‐loī‐ngă‐kè tsź‐suen, Toh‐peí‐kè tsź‐suen, Neī‐koh‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng lūk‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐î yān.
61 Tsaì‐sz‐chung yaŭ Hap‐pa‐ngă‐kè tsź‐suen, Haakₒ‐koh‐sẑ‐kè tsź‐suen, Pa‐sai‐loī‐kè tsź‐suen; yan‐waî tsó‐foô ts‘uì‐hiu K‘eī‐līt‐yān Pa‐sai‐loī‐kè nuĭ tsô ts‘ai, koò‐ts‘ź k‘uĭ k‘aaĭ Pa‐sai‐loī‐kè mēng tsô mēng à:
62 Ts‘ź ka‐kè yān, haí tsūk‐p‘ó shóh‐tsoì‐chi‐chung ch‘ā‐kwòh, wán tsẑ‐keí‐kè uēn‐tsīk to m̄‐kìn: koò‐ts‘ź chùng‐yān ĭ‐waī k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng, m̄‐chún k‘uĭ‐teî tong tsaì‐sz‐kè chik‐yīk
63 Fong‐paakₒ tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Pat‐hóh shīk chì‐shìng‐kè māt, chì‐tò yaŭ Oo‐līng kûng T‘ó‐mīng‐chi‐tsaì‐sz k‘eĭ‐laāp‐kè.
64 Ooî‐chùng kûng‐kaì sz̀‐maân‐î‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ‐lūk‐shāp yān,
65 Ts‘ź‐ngoî chûng‐yaŭ k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè pūk‐p‘eĭ ts‘at‐ts‘in‐saan‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐ts‘at yān: k‘eī‐chung yaŭ au‐koh‐kè naām‐nuĭ î‐paakₒ yān.
66 K‘uĭ‐teî yaŭ mă ts‘at‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐lūk‐chekₒ; lōh î‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐nğ chekₒ;
67 Lokₒ‐t‘ōh sz̀‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐nğ‐chekₒ; luī lūk‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐chekₒ.
68 Tong tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng chi‐shī, yaŭ keí‐kòh tsó‐foô‐kè ka‐cheúng, waî Sheûng‐Taì‐kè tîn‐uk, lōk‐ì kuen‐chôh, seúng tsoì kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk tsoî kaû‐chi‐shóh:
69 Chùng‐yān chiù‐i līk‐leûng kaau‐kwòh kung‐tsokₒ‐kè foò‐fōng kam saam‐shāp‐lūk‐maân‐lūk‐ts‘in leŭng, ngān î‐shāp‐nğ‐maân leŭng, tsaì‐sz‐kè laĭ‐i yat‐paakₒ kîn.
70 Kóm‐tsaû tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān k‘āp yaŭ‐ti tsź‐mān, yaû au‐koh‐kè, hon‐shaú‐moōn‐kè, kung‐fûng tîn‐yīk‐kè, k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, kaai chuê‐tsoî kokₒ‐yān poón‐shēng.
