1 NEĬ tong waân‐naân‐chi‐shī, uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘eng neĭ k‘eī‐k‘aū; uên Ngă‐kokₒ Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐mēng pó‐oô neĭ;
2 Ts‘ūng shìng‐tîn shi yan pong‐chôh neĭ, ts‘ūng Sun‐shaan shi yan foō‐ch‘ī neĭ;
3 Keì‐nîm neĭ kokₒ‐yeûng laĭ‐māt, tsipₒ‐naāp neĭ kokₒ‐yeûng hìn‐tsaì;
4 Shûn‐ts‘ūng neĭ‐chi‐sam uên, shīng‐tsaû neĭ‐chi‐kaì‐maū.
5 Mūng neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù, ngŏh‐teî tsaû ko‐sheng foon‐heí, tseung ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐mēng, shuê‐heí taaî‐k‘eī: mông Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, wăn‐chún neĭ faān‐shóh‐k‘aū.
6 Uē‐kam ngŏh chi‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, pit kaù k‘uĭ shóh‐fung‐laāp‐ché; ts‘ūng shìng‐t‘in‐sheûng t‘eng k‘uĭ shóh‐k‘aū, yûng yaû‐shaú chi‐taaî‐līk laī‐ch‘íng‐kaù.
7 Waāk yaŭ‐yān ch‘ing‐tsaàn‐ch‘e, waāk yaŭ‐yān ch‘ing‐tsaân mă: waī‐haî ngŏh ch‘ing‐tsaàn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐mēng.
8 K‘uĭ‐teî pūk‐tó titₒ‐tó: ngŏh‐teî haî heí‐shan k‘eĭ‐wán.
9 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘íng‐kaù: ngŏh k‘eī‐t‘ó‐shī, mông ngŏh wōng shuī‐t‘eng.
