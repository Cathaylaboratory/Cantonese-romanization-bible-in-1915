1 NGŎH yă‐ts‘āng yaŭ wâ, Ngŏh pit iù kán‐shân hāng‐waī, m̄‐peí haú‐shīt faân‐tsuî: okₒ‐yān tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn‐chi‐shī, ngŏh‐pit k‘īm‐chaì‐chuê kòh‐haú.
2 Ngŏh hó‐ts‘z̆ á‐yān, mŏ shuetₒ‐wâ‐ch‘ut, tsik‐sź shîn‐īn, ngŏh yīk m̄‐kóng; ngŏh chan shaū‐foó‐chi‐kīk.
3 Ngŏh sam‐chung īt‐līt, sz‐nîm heí‐laī, tsaû hó‐ts‘z̆ fóh‐shiu: ue‐shî yûng haú t‘ó‐kò, wâ,
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū lîng ngŏh chi‐tò ngŏh‐chi‐kitₒ‐kūk, uĕ‐k‘āp ngŏh‐chi‐nīn‐shaû keí‐ch‘eūng: táng ngŏh chi‐tò hōh‐shī chí‐sik.
5 Neĭ ts‘z̀ ngŏh‐chi‐shang‐mîng, pat‐kwòh yat‐chekₒ shaú‐paán kòm‐ch‘eūng; ngŏh tsoî‐shaì‐chi‐kaú, kuì neĭ shóh‐t‘aí, hó‐ts‘z̆ mō māt yat‐yeûng: faān‐yān sui k‘eūng‐kîn, to haî yat‐t‘iū heì.
6 Yān shang tsoî shaì, tsûn shūk hui‐miŭ: pa‐paì yat‐shaì, yīk haî t‘ō‐lō: tsik‐ch‘uk fòh‐ts‘oī, pat‐chi shuī tak.
7 Uē‐kam ngŏh yaŭ hōh‐chí‐mông? Chué à, waī‐haî chí‐mông neĭ.
8 Kaù ngŏh t‘uetₒ ch‘ut kokₒ‐yeûng tsuî‐okₒ, m̄‐hó peí uē‐ch‘ún‐chi‐yān mŏ‐lûng ngŏh.
9 Yan ni‐ti tsoi‐naân, haî neĭ shóh‐tîng; ngŏh á m̄‐hoi‐haú.
10 K‘aū neĭ ch‘uē‐huì neĭ shóh‐kòng‐kwòh ngŏh‐chi‐chaakₒ‐fāt: yan ngŏh peî‐neĭ‐shaú shóh‐chaakₒ, kei‐oō mīt‐mōng.
11 Neĭ yan tsuî laī fāt yān, k‘uĭ shóh‐seúng‐ché, neĭ to lîng k‘uĭ siu‐saàn, hó‐ts‘ź ch‘ūng siu‐fà‐māt yat‐yeûng: shaì‐yān chan‐haî hui‐miŭ.
12 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘eng ngŏh k‘eī‐t‘ó, chak‐ĭ t‘eng ngŏh foo‐kiù; ngŏh laū ngaăn‐luî, mông neĭ t‘aí‐koò ngŏh: tsoî neĭ‐chi‐ts‘īn, keì‐kui tsô haakₒ, hó‐ts‘z̆ ngŏh‐chi‐līt‐tsó yat‐yeûng.
13 K‘aū Chué foon‐yūng ngŏh, táng ngŏh hitₒ‐sik heì‐līk, īn‐haû yat huì pat ooī.
