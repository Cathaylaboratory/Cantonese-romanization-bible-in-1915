1 PA‐LAĀN tuì Pa‐lāk wâ, Neĭ iù ni‐ch‘uè waî ngŏh heí ts‘at‐kòh‐t‘aān, yaû waî ngŏh uê‐peî ts‘at‐chekₒ kung‐ngaū‐tsaí, ts‘at‐chekₒ yeūng‐kung.
2 Pa‐lāk tsaû chiù Pa‐laān shóh‐kóng laī‐hāng; Pa‐lāk kûng Pa‐laān tsoî mooĭ‐kòh‐t‘aān‐sheûng, hìn yat‐chekₒ kung‐ngaū‐tsaí, yat‐chekₒ yeūng‐kung.
3 Pa‐laān tuì Pa‐lāk wâ, Neĭ kân‐chuè faān‐tsaì k‘eì‐ch‘uè, ngòh tsaâm‐shī heùng‐ts‘īn‐huì: waāk‐ché Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām ngŏh‐ch‘uè, tsaû k‘uĭ shóh‐chí‐shî ngŏh‐kè, ngŏh tsaû wâ‐kwòh‐neĭ‐chi. Ue‐shí huì‐tò yat‐taatₒ ch‘ekₒ‐kong.
4 Sheûng‐Taì kòng‐lām Pa‐laān‐ch‘uè: Pa‐laān tuì Sheûng‐Taì wâ, Ngòh ĭ‐king heí ts‘at‐kòh‐t‘aān, mooĭ‐kòh‐t‘aān hìn yat‐chekₒ kung‐ngaū‐tsaí, yat‐chekₒ yeūng‐kung.
5 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung shuetₒ‐wâ ch‘uēn‐yāp Pa‐laān haú‐chung, yaû wâ, Neĭ faan‐huì Pa‐lāk‐ch‘uè, iù kóm‐yeûng kóng.
6 Pa‐laān tsaû faan‐huì Pa‐lāk‐ch‘uè, t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ t‘ūng‐maaī Moh‐aapₒ‐kè mūk‐cheúng, kân‐chuê faān‐tsaì k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè.
7 Pa‐laān tsaû tsokₒ‐koh, wâ, Moh‐aapₒ wōng Pa‐lāk taaì ngŏh yaū À‐laān laī, ts‘ūng tung‐pîn‐kè shaan ī ch‘ut, wâ, Laī la, waî‐ngŏh chaù‐chòh Ngă‐kokₒ, waî‐ngŏh nô‐mâ Ĭ‐shik‐līt.
8 Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐pat‐chaù‐chòh‐kè, ngŏh tím‐nāng chaù‐chòh k‘uĭ? Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐pat‐nô‐mâ‐kè, ngŏh tím‐nāng nô‐mâ k‘uĭ?
9 Ngŏh ts‘ūng p‘oōn‐shēk chi‐sheûng t‘aí k‘uĭ, yaû ts‘ūng shaan‐téng kóh‐ch‘uè mông k‘uĭ: kìn kóh‐ti paakₒ‐sìng tūk‐tsẑ kui‐chuê, m̄‐yāp līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐shò.
10 Mat‐shuī nāng kaì Ngă‐kokₒ‐yān uē sha‐ch‘ān chi‐chùng, mat‐shuī nāng kaì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān sz̀‐fân‐chi‐yat ni? Uên ngŏh chi‐sź, uē shîn yān chi‐sź, uên ngŏh chi‐chung, uē shîn‐yān chi‐chung!
11 Pa‐lāk tuì Pa‐laān wâ, Neĭ haî tím‐yeûng toî ngŏh ni? ngŏh ts‘éng neĭ laī chaù‐chòh ngŏh‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk, neĭ faán‐chuèn waî k‘uĭ‐teî chuk‐fuk.
12 Pa‐laān taapₒ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐ch‘uēn‐yāp‐ngŏh‐haú‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, ngŏh heí‐m̄‐kán‐shân laī ch‘uēn‐kóng me?
13 Pa‐lāk wâ, Ts‘éng neĭ t‘ūng ngŏh huì pīt‐ch‘uè, tsoî kòh‐taatₒ t‘aí‐hă k‘uĭ‐teî: taân neĭ pat‐nāng kìn‐saaì taaî‐chùng, pat‐kwòh t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ‐teî yat‐pin‐che: uên neĭ haí‐kòh‐ch‘uè chaù‐chòh k‘uĭ‐teî.
14 Tsaû taaì Pa‐laān huì‐tò Sóh‐feī‐t‘īn, sheŭng Peí‐sẑ‐ka shaan‐téng, tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè heí ts‘at‐kòh t‘aān, mooĭ‐kòh‐t‘aān‐sheûng, hìn yat‐chekₒ kung‐ngaū‐tsaí, yat‐chekₒ yeūng‐kung.
15 Pa‐laān tuì Pa‐lāk wâ, Neĭ kân‐chuê faān‐tasì k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè, táng‐toî ngŏh huì kìn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
16 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām Pa‐laān‐ch‘uè, tseung shuetₒ‐wâ ch‘uēn‐yāp k‘uĭ haú‐chung, wâ, Neĭ tsoì faan‐huì Pa‐lāk‐ch‘uè, iù kóm‐yeûng kóng.
17 Pa‐laān faan‐tò Pa‐lāk‐ch‘uè, kìn k‘uĭ kûng Moh‐aapₒ‐kè mūk‐cheúng, kân‐chuê faān‐tsaì k‘eĭ ch‘uè, Pa‐lāk mân k‘uĭ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kóng mat‐yĕ ni?
18 Pa‐laān tsaû tsokₒ‐koh wâ, Pa‐lāk à, neĭ heí‐shan laī t‘eng; Sai‐poōt‐kè tsaí à, chak ĭ t‘eng ngŏh kóng:
19 Sheûng‐Taì m̄‐haî yān, pit mŏ kóng taaî‐wâ; Sheûng‐Taì m̄‐haî yān‐chi‐tsź, pit mŏ haû‐fooì‐sam: k‘uĭ ĭ‐king kóng‐kè, heí yaŭ m̄‐hāng me? ĭ‐king ying‐shīng‐kè, heí m̄‐yìng‐îm me?
20 Ngŏh fûng Sheûng‐Taì mîng: chuk‐fuk k‘uĭ shóh‐chuk‐fuk‐kè; ngŏh pat‐nāng koí‐chuèn.
21 Taân meî‐ts‘āng kìn Ngă‐kokₒ yaŭ‐mat‐kwòh‐shat, meì‐ts‘āng kìn Ĭ‐shik‐līt yaŭ‐mat‐tsuî‐okₒ: k‘uĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ, yaŭ foo‐wōng‐kè sheng tsoî k‘eī‐chung.
22 Sheûng‐Taì taaì k‘uĭ‐teî ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp; k‘uĭ‐teî k‘eūng‐kîn hó‐ts‘z̆ yĕ‐ngaū.
23 Pit mŏ ts‘ē‐shūt hoî‐tak Ngă‐kokₒ, yīk mŏ faatₒ‐shūt hoî‐tak Ĭ‐shik‐līt, tong‐shī lûn‐k‘āp Ngă‐kokₒ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt, wâ, Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐hāng, hōh‐táng k‘eī‐miû!
24 Ni‐ti paakₒ‐sìng pit hing‐heí, hó‐ts‘z̆ mŏ‐sz, yaû t‘iù‐yeūk hó‐ts‘z̆ kung‐sz: yeūk meî‐ts‘āng shīk peî‐chukₒ‐kè māt, meî‐ts‘āng yám peî‐sheung‐kè huetₒ, tsaû m̄‐pūk‐ch‘uè fàn.
25 Pa‐lāk tuì Pa‐laān wâ, Neĭ maĭ chaù‐chòh k‘uĭ‐teî, yīk maĭ chuk‐fuk k‘uĭ‐teî lokₒ.
26 Pa‐laān taapₒ Pa‐lāk wâ, Ngŏh heí‐m̄‐haî yă‐ts‘āng tuì neĭ kóng, wâ, Faān Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò ngŏh‐kè, ngŏh pit tsun‐i laī hāng me?
27 Pa‐lāk tuì Pa‐laān wâ, Ts‘éng laī, ngŏh taaì neĭ huì pīt‐ch‘uè, waāk‐ché Sheûng‐Taì foon‐heí neĭ, peí neĭ tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè, waî ngŏh chaù‐chòh k‘uĭ‐teî.
28 Pa‐lāk tsaû taaì Pa‐laān huì‐tò heùng‐kw‘òng‐yĕ‐kè Peí‐ĭ‐shaan‐téng‐sheûng.
29 Pa‐laān wâ, Neĭ waî ngŏh haí‐ni‐ch‘uè heí ts‘at‐kòh‐t‘aān, uê‐peî ts‘at‐chekₒ kung‐ngaū‐tsaí, ts‘at‐chekₒ yeūng‐kung.
30 Pa‐lāk tsaû chiù Pa‐laān shóh‐kóng laī hāng, mooĭ‐kòh‐t‘aān‐chi‐sheûng, hìn yat‐chekₒ kung‐ngaū‐tsaí, yat‐chekₒ yeūng‐kung.
