1 NGŎH‐chi‐līng‐wān, ying‐tong kóm‐tsê Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: ngŏh‐chi‐sam‐ch‘eūng, ying‐tong tsaàn‐tsûng k‘uĭ‐chi‐shìng‐mēng.
2 Ngŏh‐chi līng‐wān, ying‐tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, m̄‐hó mōng‐keì k‘uĭ‐chi‐yan‐waî:
3 K‘uĭ shè‐mĭn ngŏh kokₒ‐yeûng tsuî‐okₒ; i‐hó ngŏh kokₒ‐yeûng tsāt‐pêng;
4 Kaù‐shūk ngŏh mîng, mĭn‐chì sź‐mōng; tseung yan‐ch‘úng ts‘z̄‐pei, ts‘z̆ koon ka ngŏh;
5 K‘aaĭ hó‐māt ts‘z̀ ngŏh shīk‐paaú; lîng ngŏh pìn‐faan shiù‐nuên, hó‐ts‘z̆ mā‐ying.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shi‐hāng kung‐î, waî kòh‐ti shaû‐foó‐chi‐yān shan‐uen.
7 Tseung tsẑ‐keí tô‐lô chí‐shî Moh‐sai, tseung tsẑ‐keí tsokₒ‐waī, chí‐shî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî ts‘z̄‐pei līn‐măn, faatₒ‐nô m̄‐kap‐ts‘uk; ī‐ch‘é taaî‐shi‐yan‐ch‘úng.
9 K‘uĭ mŏ shī‐sheūng chaakₒ‐fāt yān: mŏ wĭng‐uĕn waaī‐ts‘ōng nô‐heì.
10 Mŏ chiù ngŏh‐teî‐chi‐tsuî, laī‐chaakₒ‐shīng ngŏh‐teî; mŏ‐chiù ngŏh‐teî‐chi‐okₒ, laī pò‐yìng ngŏh‐teî.
11 Taân‐faān kìng‐waì k‘uĭ‐chi‐yān, k‘uĭ taaî‐shi‐yan kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘in‐kaakₒ‐teî kòm‐ko.
12 K‘uĭ lîng ngŏh‐teî‐chi‐tsuî leī‐hoi ngŏh‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆ tung‐kīk kaakₒ sai‐kīk kòm‐uĕn.
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā oì‐līn kìng‐waì‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐yān, hó‐ts‘z̆ foô‐ts‘an oì‐līn tsaí‐nuĭ yat‐yeûng.
14 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ chi‐tò ngŏh‐teî‐chi‐t‘aí‐chat; keì‐nîm ngŏh‐teî haî naī‐t‘ó.
15 Yān‐chi‐tô‐yāt, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ó kóm: shang‐cheúng yīk hó‐ts‘z̆ yĕ‐fa.
16 Peî‐fung‐yat‐ch‘ui, tsik‐shī līng‐lōk; kaû‐shī teî‐pô, to m̄‐yîng‐tak.
17 Waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐yan‐tín, ts‘ūng wĭng‐uĕn chì wĭng‐uĕn, shi‐kwòh faān waì‐kuî k‘uĭ, shaú k‘uĭ kaaì‐mîng, kán‐keì k‘uĭ lūt‐faatₒ‐ché;
18 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐kung‐î, yīk shi‐kwòh k‘uĭ tsź‐suen.
19 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî t‘in‐sheûng, laāp‐shāt tsôh‐waî; chap‐k‘uēn koón‐leĭ maân‐māt.
20 Yaŭ taaî‐nāng‐līk, i k‘uĭ shuetₒ‐wâ, t‘eng k‘uĭ fan‐foò‐chi t‘in‐sz̀, yīk tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
21 Tsun‐hāng k‘uĭ chí‐ì tsô k‘uĭ‐chi‐pūk‐ché; yīk tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
22 Taân‐faān Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐tsô‐chi‐maân‐māt, tsoî k‘uĭ shóh‐koón‐leĭ‐chi‐kokₒ‐ch‘uè‐ché, to tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: ngŏh‐chi‐līng‐wān, yīk tong tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
