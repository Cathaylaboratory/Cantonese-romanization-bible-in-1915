1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT‐YĀN ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp, Ngă‐kokₒ‐chi‐ka, leī‐hoi î‐pong chi‐shī;
2 Yaū‐taaî tsô Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐shìng‐tîn, Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsô Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐king‐shēng.
3 Taaî‐hoí yat‐kìn tsaû t‘ō‐tsaú: Yeukₒ‐taân yīk t‘uì‐haû.
4 Taaî‐shaan t‘iù‐heí‐laī hó‐ts‘z̆ yeūng‐kung, siú‐shaan t‘iù‐heí‐laī hó‐ts‘z̆ yeūng‐tsaí.
5 Ts‘éng mân taaî‐hoi, Neĭ yan‐hōh t‘ō‐tsaú? yaû mân Yeukₒ‐taan, Neĭ yan‐hōh t‘uì‐haû?
6 Taaî‐shaan, neĭ yan‐hōh t‘iù‐heí, hó‐ts‘z̆ yeūng‐kung; siú‐shaan, neĭ yan‐hōh t‘iù‐heí, hó‐ts‘z̆ yeūng‐tsaí?
7 Taaî‐teî tsoî Chué‐chi‐ts‘īn, tsoî Ngă‐kokₒ‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì ts‘īn, yīk pit chan‐tûng;
8 Sheûng‐Taì nāng lîng p‘oōn‐shēk pìn‐waī ch‘ī‐t‘ōng, fóh‐shēk pìn‐waī ts‘uēn‐shuí.
