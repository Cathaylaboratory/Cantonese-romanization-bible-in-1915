1 NGŎH‐CHI‐SHEÛNG‐TAÌ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ngŏh tūk í‐laaî neĭ: k‘aū neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐leī taân‐faān pik‐hoî‐ngŏh‐ché:
2 P‘à k‘uĭ hó‐ts‘z̆ sz‐tsź laī‐kwá ngŏh, tseung ngŏh kwá‐suì, chì‐tò mŏ‐kaù.
3 Ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, t‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh yaŭ hāng‐kwòh ni‐ti; yeūk ngŏh shaú‐chung yaŭ pat‐î;
4 Yān kûng ngŏh seung‐hó, ngŏh yeūk faán‐k‘aaĭ okₒ pò k‘uĭ; (yān mō‐koò laī‐kung ngŏh, ngŏh yeūk kipₒ‐faan k‘uĭ:)
5 Kóm‐tsaû yâm‐ts‘ūng tīk‐yān chui‐tó ngŏh; tseung ngŏh shang‐mîng ch‘aaí‐lōk‐teî, lîng ngŏh‐chi‐mîn‐mūk, kwai‐ue naī‐ch‘ān.
6 Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faatₒ‐nô heí‐shan, k‘eĭ‐tsoî tīk‐yān fán‐nô‐chi‐chung: síng‐nĝ laī pong‐chôh ngŏh, i neĭ tîng‐laî laī‐shám‐p‘oòn.
7 Līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐yān, sz̀‐mîn waī‐chuê neĭ: uên neĭ ts‘ŏh‐faan ko‐waî, tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐sheûng.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shî‐pit shám‐p‘oòn maân‐kwokₒ: uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā i‐chuê ngŏh‐chi‐kung‐î chìng‐chīk, laī p‘oòn‐tuèn ngŏh.
9 Kung‐î‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, neĭ nāng kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ yān sam‐ch‘eūng: uên neĭ t‘uĕn‐tsuēt okₒ‐yān‐chi‐okₒ; lîng shîn‐yān tak‐kin‐laāp.
10 Sheûng‐Taì haî ngŏh t‘āng‐p‘aaī, shî‐pit ch‘íng‐kaù chīk‐sam‐chi‐yān.
11 Sheûng‐Taì waî shîn‐yān shan‐uen, Sheûng‐Taì yāt‐yāt yaû faatₒ‐nô.
12 Okₒ‐yān yeūk pat‐ooī‐t‘aū, Sheûng‐Taì shî‐pit mōh‐leî cheung‐to; hoi‐tîng pá‐kung laī‐táng‐haù.
13 Ī‐ch‘é shaatₒ‐yān‐kè heì‐haaî, ĭ‐king heùng‐chuê k‘uĭ; shóh‐yûng‐chi‐tsìn, k‘ui‐shî fóh‐tsìn.
14 K‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐ts‘ōng haî tsuî‐okₒ, shóh‐waaī haî ts‘aān‐hoî, shóh‐shang haî chà‐ngaî.
15 K‘uĭ kwāt‐chi‐sham‐haang, faán‐t‘ui tsẑ‐keí lōk‐huì.
16 K‘uĭ chi tūk‐kaì, pit kwai tsoî tsẑ‐keí t‘aū‐sheûng, k‘uĭ‐chi‐hung‐okₒ, pit lām‐tò tsẑ‐keí téng‐sheûng.
17 Ngŏh i‐chuê Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐kung‐î laī tsaàn‐meĭ: ī‐ch‘é koh‐tsûng chì‐sheûng‐ché Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mēng.
