1 NGŎH t‘aí‐kìn ts‘ŏh‐waî‐sheûng‐kè, yaû‐shaú cha‐chuê yat‐kuén‐shue, noî‐ngoî to‐yaŭ tsẑ sé, k‘ap ts‘at‐kòh yàn fung‐māt.
2 Yaû t‘aí‐kìn taaî‐līk‐kè t‘in‐sz̀‐taaî‐sheng iu‐wâ, Mat‐shuī hom‐tong laī‐hoi kòh‐kuén‐shue, k‘itₒ‐hoi kòh‐yàn ni?
3 Tsoî t‘in, tsoî teî, uĕ‐k‘āp tsoî‐teî‐chi‐hâ‐kè, to mŏ yān nāng hoi kòh‐kuén‐shue, waāk t‘aí k‘uĭ‐kè.
4 Yan mŏ‐yān hom‐tong hoi kòh‐kuén‐shue laī t‘aí‐hă, ngŏh tsaû hó pei‐ts‘itₒ huk‐yap.
5 Cheúng‐lŏ‐chi‐chung yat‐kòh tuì ngŏh wâ, Maĭ huk: Yaū‐taaî chi‐p‘aaì‐kè Sz, Taaî‐waî‐chi‐Kan, ĭ‐king tak‐shìng, hoi kòh‐kuén‐shue, ī‐ch‘é k‘itₒ‐hoi kòh‐ts‘at‐yàn.
6 Ngŏh t‘aí‐kìn tsôh‐waî, kûng sz̀‐chekₒ shang‐māt, k‘āp cheúng‐lŏ‐chi‐chung, yaŭ Ko‐Yeūng k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè, hó‐ts‘z̆ ĭ‐king shaû‐shaatₒ‐kè, yaŭ ts‘at‐chekₒ kokₒ, ts‘at‐chekₒ ngaăn, tsik‐haî Sheûng‐Taì‐kè ts‘at Shān, shaû tá‐faatₒ huì p‘ó‐t‘in‐hâ‐kè.
7 Kòh‐chekₒ Ko‐Yeūng hāng‐ts‘īn‐laī, haí ts‘ŏh‐waî‐ché‐kè yaû‐shaú lóh kòh‐kuén‐shue.
8 K‘uĭ lóh kòh‐kuén‐shue‐chi‐shī, sz̀‐chekₒ shang‐māt, kûng î‐shāp‐sz̀ cheúng‐lŏ, to pūk‐tó Ko‐Yeūng mîn‐ts‘īn, kokₒ‐cha‐chuê k‘ām, kûng tsoì‐moŏn‐heung‐kè kam‐lō, ni‐ti heung, tsik‐haî shìng‐t‘ō‐kè k‘eī‐t‘ó.
9 Tsaû ch‘eùng san‐shi, wâ, Neĭ hom‐tong lóh kòh‐kuén‐shue, k‘itₒ‐hoi kòh‐ti‐yàn: yan neĭ yă‐ts‘āng peî‐shaatₒ, k‘aaĭ neĭ‐kè huetₒ, ts‘ūng kokₒ‐tsūk, kakₒ‐yam, kokₒ‐mān, kokₒ‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung, maaĭ‐faan‐yān kwai‐fuk Sheûng‐Taì;
10 Yaû lîng k‘uĭ‐teî waī‐kwokₒ, waī tsaì‐sz, kwai ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì: tsoî teî‐sheûng waī wōng.
11 Ngŏh t‘aí‐kìn, yaû t‘eng‐mān waī‐chuê‐kòh‐waî, kûng shang‐māt k‘āp cheúng‐lŏ: sz̀‐pin‐kè chùng‐t‘in‐sz̀ yaŭ sheng, k‘eī‐shò yaŭ ts‘in‐ts‘in maân‐maân;
12 Taaî‐sheng kóng‐wâ, Peî‐shaatₒ‐kè Ko‐Yeūng, hom‐shaû k‘uēn‐shaì, foò‐haû, chì‐waî, ts‘oī‐nāng, tsuen‐kwaì, wīng‐kwong, tsûng‐chuk à.
13 Ngŏh yaû t‘eng‐mān tsoî t‘in, tsoî teî, tsoî teî‐chi‐hâ, tsoî hoí‐chi‐sheûng, taân‐faān shaû‐tsô‐chi‐māt, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè, to wâ, Tsûng‐chuk, tsuen‐kwaì, wīng‐kwong, k‘uēn‐shaì, kwai‐ue ts‘ŏh‐waî‐sheûng‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp Ko‐Yeūng, chì‐tò shaì‐shaì.
14 Kòh sz̀‐chekₒ shang‐māt, wâ, À‐moōn. Î‐shāp‐sz̀ cheúng‐lŏ, yīk pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè laī paaì.
