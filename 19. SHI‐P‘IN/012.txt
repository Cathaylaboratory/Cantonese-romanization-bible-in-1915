1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ à; shaì‐yān‐chi‐chung, kìng‐k‘īn‐ché mŏ saaì; chung‐sùn‐ché yīk tsîm‐shiú, k‘aū neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù.
2 Yān kûng yān seung‐kóng, k‘ui sheûng ká‐ngaî: moŏn‐haú ch‘ím‐meî, sam waaī leŭng‐yeûng.
3 Taân‐faān haú hò‐ch‘ím‐meî, shīt sheûng king‐kw‘a‐chi‐yān, uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mīt‐tsuēt k‘uĭ:
4 K‘uĭ yă‐ts‘āng wâ, Ngŏh shīt tîng iù ch‘īng‐k‘eūng; haú haî tsz̀‐keí‐kè: shuī‐nāng‐koón ngŏh?
5 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Kw‘àn‐foó‐chi‐yān shaû‐hoì, p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐chi‐yān tse‐t‘aàn; ngŏh pit hing‐heí, on‐chì k‘uĭ tsoî wán‐chân teî‐fong, t‘uetₒ‐lat faān waí‐p‘òng‐chi‐ché.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐shuetₒ‐wâ, haî hó‐tsing‐kitₒ: yaū‐uē paāk‐ngān tsoî lō, lîn‐kwòh ts‘at ts‘z̀.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ pit wĭng‐uĕn pó‐oô kw‘àn‐foó‐chi‐yān, mĭn shaû shaì‐yān haâm‐hoî.
8 Okₒ‐yān tò‐ch‘uè yaū‐hāng, kaan‐okₒ‐chi‐laū ts‘ŏh‐ko‐waî.
