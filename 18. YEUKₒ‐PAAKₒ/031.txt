1 NGŎH kûng ngŏh‐kè ngaăn laāp‐yeukₒ; kóm‐tsaû ngŏh tím‐ooĭ t‘aí‐chuê t‘ūng‐nuĭ ni?
2 Yan‐waî ts‘ūng‐sheûng yaū Sheûng‐Taì‐kè fân‐īp? ts‘ūng sheûng‐ko yaū kòh‐waî Ts‘uēn‐Nāng‐Ché shóh‐shīng‐shaû‐kè ka‐īp, haî tím‐yeûng‐kè ni?
3 Heí‐m̄‐haî tsoi‐naân kwai‐ue pat‐î yān? yaû hung‐sẑ kwai‐heùng tsuî‐hin‐kè yān me?
4 K‘uĭ heí‐m̄‐t‘aí‐kìn ngŏh‐kè lô‐t‘ō, heí‐m̄‐shó‐saaì ngŏh‐kè keukₒ‐pô me?
5 T‘óng‐yeūk i hui‐mŏng laī‐hāng, waāk ngŏh keukₒ kap‐ts‘uk huì tsô kwaí‐chà‐kè;
6 Uên ngŏh peí tuî‐kwòh‐kè t‘in‐p‘īng laī‐ch‘ìng, táng Sheûng‐Taì hóh‐ĭ chi‐tò ngŏh chìng‐chīk.
7 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh keukₒ‐pô p‘in‐chuèn leī‐hoi kòh‐lô‐t‘ō, ngŏh sam yaû‐i ngŏh ngaăn laī hāng, t‘óng‐yeūk yaŭ hā‐ts‘z ch‘i‐chuê ngŏh shaú‐sheûng;
8 Kóm‐tsaû peí ngŏh pòh‐chúng yaû peí pīt‐yān shīk; shāt‐haî iù ngŏh t‘īn‐shóh‐shang‐ch‘aán‐kè peî‐pāt‐heí laī.
9 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh sam peî‐foŏ‐yān yaŭ‐waāk, waāk ngŏh tsoî lūn‐shè moōn‐haú táng‐haû;
10 Kóm‐tsaû iù ngŏh‐kè ts‘ai waî pīt‐yān t‘ui‐môh, yaû peî‐pīt‐yān fūk‐tsoî k‘uĭ‐sheûng.
11 Yan‐waî ts‘ź haî kīk‐chi hóh‐oò‐kè taaî‐tsuî; shāt‐haî sẑ‐sz shóh‐tong yīng‐fāt‐kè tsuî‐hin.
12 Yan‐ts‘ź haî fóh tsiú‐mīt‐tò paaî‐waaî, yaû ooĭ pāt‐heí‐saaì ngŏh‐kè shang‐ch‘aán.
13 Ngŏh pūk waāk p‘eĭ kûng ngŏh chaang‐lûn kòh‐shī, t‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh miŭ‐shî k‘uĭ‐kè kò‐tsûng;
14 Kóm‐tsaû Sheûng‐Taì heí‐laī kòh‐shī, ngŏh ooĭ‐tím‐tsô ni? k‘uĭ laī t‘aàm‐ch‘aatₒ ngŏh kòh‐shī, ngŏh ooĭ‐tím‐yeûng taapₒ‐yìng k‘uĭ ni?
15 Tsoî t‘oi‐chung tsô ngŏh kòh‐waî, heí‐m̄‐haî tsô k‘uĭ me? heí‐m̄‐haî yat‐waî tsoî t‘oi‐chung tsokₒ‐ch‘ut ngŏh‐teî leŭng‐kòh me?
16 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh chóh‐chuê p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐kè tak k‘uĭ sam‐shóh‐seúng, waāk lîng kwá‐foŏ‐kè ngaăn shui‐paaî;
17 Waāk‐haî tūk‐tsẑ shīk ngŏh meĭ‐māt, yaû koo‐tsź m̄‐ts‘āng shīk;
18 (M̄‐tak‐a, k‘uĭ ts‘z̆ ngŏh shiù‐nīn‐shī kûng ngŏh cheúng‐taaî, hó‐ts‘z̆ foô‐ts‘an yat‐yeûng, ngŏh yaû tsẑ‐mŏ‐t‘oi to tsô ts‘ź‐foŏ‐yān‐kè chí‐lô‐chi‐yān;)
19 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh kìn yān yan mŏ i‐fūk sź‐mōng, waāk yan kw‘àn‐fāt‐kè mŏ‐i‐fūk che‐k‘oì;
20 T‘óng‐yeūk k‘uĭ‐kè iu m̄‐ts‘āng chuk‐fuk ngŏh, waāk k‘uĭ m̄‐ts‘āng tak ngŏh yeūng‐kè mō pó‐nuĕn k‘uĭ;
21 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh ts‘āng kuí‐shaú kung‐kik koo‐tsź, haî yan ngŏh t‘aí‐kìn pong‐chôh‐ngŏh‐kè tsoî shēng‐moōn‐ch‘uè:
22 Kóm‐tsaû uên ngŏh pokₒ‐t‘aū haí pokₒ‐t‘aū kwat titₒ‐lōk, ngŏh‐kè shaú‐peì chitₒ‐t‘uĕn t‘uetₒ‐leī‐ti‐kwat.
23 Yan‐waî ts‘ūng Sheûng‐Taì laī‐kè waân‐naân húng‐haakₒ ngŏh, yaû yan k‘uĭ‐kè meĭ‐shîn ngŏh pat‐nāng tsô māt‐yĕ sẑ.
24 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh tseung kam tsô ngŏh‐kè chí‐mông, waāk ts‘āng tuì tsing‐kam wâ, Neĭ haî ngŏh shóh‐í‐k‘aaù‐kè;
25 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh yan ts‘oī‐paāk‐toh, yaû yan ngŏh shaú tak‐tó taaî‐toh tsaû faaì‐lōk;
26 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh mông‐kìn t‘aaì‐yeūng faatₒ‐kwong kòh‐shī, waāk uēt i leûng‐kwong laī‐hāng;
27 Ngŏh sam tsoî òm‐chung peî‐yăn‐yaŭ, waāk ngŏh haú kûng ngŏh shaú ts‘an‐tsuí:
28 Ni‐ti to haî sẑ‐sz ying‐tong yīng‐fāt‐kè sẑ: yan ngŏh kóm‐tsaû ooĭ‐heùng‐tsoî‐sheûng kòh‐waî Sheûng‐Taì kóng taaî‐wâ la.
29 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh kìn tsang‐oò ngŏh‐kè yān paaî‐waaî tsaû foon‐heí, waāk kìn tsoi‐wôh lām‐tò k‘uĭ shan‐sheûng, tsaû kuí‐ko tsẑ‐keí:
30 Ngŏh shāt‐haî m̄‐peí ngŏh haú laī‐faân‐tsuî, k‘aū‐tó k‘uĭ shang‐mîng pìn‐waī chaù‐chòh à.
31 T‘óng‐yeūk shūk ngŏh cheùng‐mōk‐kè yān mŏ‐wâ, Mat‐shuī ooĭ‐wán‐tó yat‐kòh m̄‐ts‘āng k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ‐kè yūk‐ts‘īng tak‐tsuk‐ì ni!
32 Luĭ‐haakₒ m̄‐tsoî kaai‐ch‘uè hitₒ‐suk: waī‐haî ngŏh hoi‐moōn tsipₒ‐naāp kwòh‐lô‐kè yān.
33 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh óm‐chuê shóh‐faân‐kè tsuî hó‐ts‘z̆ À‐tong, tseung ngŏh tsuî‐hin ts‘ōng‐maaī sam‐waaī‐ch‘uè:
34 Haî yan ngŏh p‘à taaî‐kw‘ān‐chùng, yaû yān‐ka‐kè miŭ‐shî haakₒ‐fong ngŏh, táng‐tò ngŏh suk‐tsîng m̄‐kóm ch‘ut‐moōn?
35 Uên ngŏh tak‐yat‐kòh ooĭ‐t‘eng‐mān‐kè! t‘aí‐a, ts‘ź‐haî ngŏh ts‘im‐mēng‐chi‐hô, iù kòh‐waî Ts‘uēn‐Nāng‐Ché taapₒ‐yìng ngŏh a, yaû uên ngŏh yaŭ ngŏh ch‘aū‐tīk shóh‐sé‐kè chông‐ts‘z̄.
36 Ngŏh k‘okₒ‐shāt ooĭ‐taam‐tsoî pokₒ‐t‘aū‐sheûng, yaû póng‐tsoî ngŏh‐sheûng hó‐ts‘z̆ koon‐mĭn.
37 Ngŏh ooĭ heùng k‘uĭ shing‐mīng ngŏh keukₒ‐pô‐kè shò‐mūk; ngŏh ooĭ‐ts‘an‐kân k‘uĭ hó‐ts‘z̆ kwan‐cheúng.
38 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh‐kè t‘īn‐t‘ó faatₒ‐sheng kung‐kik ngŏh, t‘īn‐kaan‐kè laī‐p‘ā lô t‘ūng yat‐ts‘aī huk‐yap;
39 T‘óng‐yeūk ngŏh mŏ‐ts‘īn shīk‐kwòh t‘īn‐teî shóh‐kitₒ‐kè kwóh‐tsź, waāk lîng t‘īn‐chué shat‐hiu k‘uĭ shang‐mîng:
40 Kóm‐tsaû iù tsāt‐laī t‘aì‐toî māk shang‐heí, yaû paî‐ts‘ó t‘aì‐toî taaî‐māk à. ¶ Yeukₒ‐paakₒ‐kè īn‐uĕ kóng‐uĕn lokₒ.
