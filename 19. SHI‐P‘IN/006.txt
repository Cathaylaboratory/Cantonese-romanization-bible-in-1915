1 K‘AŪ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, m̄‐hó shi neĭ‐chi‐nô, laī kíng‐chaakₒ ngŏh, m̄‐hó faatₒ taaî‐nô, laī chap‐chaakₒ ngŏh.
2 Ngŏh shan uĕn‐yeūk: k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā king‐sut ngŏh: ngŏh kwat tá‐chàn; k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā i‐leĭ ngŏh.
3 Ngŏh sam yīk kīk‐tá‐chàn: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ iù tò‐hōh‐shī ni?
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, uên neĭ faan‐laī, ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh wān: shi neĭ‐chi‐yan, laī ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh.
5 Yan tsoî sź‐teî, mŏ yān keì‐nîm neĭ: tsoî yam‐kaan, shuī‐nāng ch‘ing‐tsaàn neĭ?
6 Ngŏh kw‘àn‐kuên t‘aàn‐sik; shīng‐yê laū‐luî; laū‐shap ch‘ōng‐tsīk.
7 Ngŏh yan yau‐shaū, ngaăn‐mūk fan‐fa; yan tīk chùng‐toh, tsẑ kokₒ‐shui‐lŏ.
8 Neĭ‐teî hāng‐okₒ‐chi‐yān, iù leī‐hoi‐saaì ngŏh; yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘eng‐mān ngŏh oi‐huk‐chi‐sheng.
9 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ĭ‐king t‘eng ngŏh chi t‘ó‐kò; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shî‐pit ying‐wăn ngŏh shóh‐pán‐k‘aū.
10 Ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, shî‐pit húng‐kuî sau‐ch‘í: fat‐īn t‘uì‐haû king‐fong.
