1 TONG Taaî‐leî‐oo wōng taî‐î‐nīn, paatₒ‐uēt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, lām‐tò Yīk‐toh‐kè‐suen Peí‐leĭ‐ka‐kè tsaí sin‐chi Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à wâ,
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shāp‐fan‐m̄‐foon‐heí‐neĭ‐kè‐līt‐tsó.
3 Shóh‐ĭ neĭ‐iù‐tuì‐k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐wâ; Neĭ iù‐kwai‐faan ngŏh‐ch‘uè, maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ lokₒ, kóm‐ngŏh‐pit‐kwai‐faan‐neĭ‐ch‘uè, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
4 M̄‐hó‐hōk‐faan neĭ‐kè‐līt‐tsó, tsik‐haî ts‘īn‐shī‐kè sin‐chi heùng k‘uĭ iu‐wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ. Neĭ‐teî‐iù‐leī‐pīt okₒ‐hâng‐kè lô‐t‘ō, yaû leī‐pīt shóh‐tsô‐kè okₒ‐sẑ, ooī‐t‘aū‐faan‐laī la: taân‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐t‘eng‐cheūk, yaû‐m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng‐ngŏh à, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kóm wâ.
5 Neĭ‐teî‐kè līt‐tsó haí‐pin‐ch‘uè‐ni? kòh‐ti‐sin‐chi ooĭ‐shang‐tò‐wĭng‐uĕn me?
6 Waī‐haî ngŏh‐kè īn‐uĕ, uĕ‐k‘āp ngŏh‐kè lūt‐laî, tsik‐haî ngŏh‐fan‐foò chùng‐sin‐chi ngŏh‐kè pūk‐yān, heí‐m̄‐lām‐tò neĭ‐līt‐tsó‐ch‘uè me? k‘uĭ‐teî‐tsaû‐chuèn‐heùng wâ, Chiù‐i‐maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng‐ngŏh‐shóh‐seúng‐tsô‐kè, tsun‐i‐ngŏh‐teî‐kè lô‐t‘ō, yaû‐chiù‐i‐ngŏh‐teî‐kè hāng‐waī, k‘uĭ‐tsaû chiù‐kóm‐yeûng laī‐hon‐toî‐ngŏh lokₒ.
7 ¶ Tong Taaî‐leî‐oo wōng taî‐î‐nīn shāp‐yat‐uēt, tsik‐haî Sai‐paatₒ‐uēt î‐shāp‐sz̀‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, lām‐tò Yīk‐toh‐kè suen Peí‐leĭ‐ka‐kè tsaí, sin‐chi Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à wâ,
8 Ngŏh‐tsoî‐yê‐kaan mông‐kìn, yaû‐t‘aí‐hă, yat‐kòh‐yān k‘ē‐chuê‐yat‐chekₒ‐hūng‐mă, haí‐kuk‐taí kong‐nim‐shuê‐chi‐chung k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè; tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐haû‐mîn yīk‐yaŭ‐hūng‐kè, tsź‐kè, k‘āp paāk‐shik‐kè mă.
9 Ue‐shî‐ngŏh‐wâ, Ngŏh‐kè Chué à, ni‐ti‐haî‐mat‐yĕ‐ni? T‘ūng‐ngŏh‐kóng‐shuetₒ‐wâ‐kè t‘in‐sz̀ tsaû‐tuì‐ngŏh‐wâ, Ngŏh tseung‐hín‐mīng lîng‐neĭ‐chi‐tò ni‐ti‐haî‐mat‐yĕ à.
10 K‘eĭ‐tsoî kong‐nim‐shuê‐chung‐kè yān tuì‐taapₒ‐yaû‐wâ, Ni‐ti‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐tá‐faatₒ, yaû‐loī yaû‐huì, hāng‐t‘ung‐teî‐sheûng.
11 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû‐taapₒ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sz̀‐ché, tsik‐haî k‘eĭ‐tsoî kong‐nim‐shuê‐chi‐chung‐kè, yaû‐wâ, Ngŏh‐teî‐ĭ‐king‐yaû‐huì yaû‐loī, hāng‐t‘ung‐teî‐sheûng, yaû t‘aí‐hă ts‘uēn‐teî‐on‐tsîng yaû‐t‘īng‐sik à.
12 Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sz̀‐ché tuì‐taapₒ‐wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ‐m̄‐háng‐shi‐king‐sut‐kwòh Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, uē‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè chùng‐yap, tsik‐haî neĭ ĭ‐king ts‘at‐shāp nīn kòm‐noî, heùng‐chuê‐k‘uĭ faatₒ‐nô, kóm‐chûng‐keí‐noî‐ni?
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû k‘aaĭ‐yān‐ts‘z̄‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, yaû k‘aaĭ‐on‐waì‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tuì‐taapₒ‐t‘ūng‐ngŏh‐kóng‐kè t‘in‐sz̀.
14 Kóm‐tsaû t‘ūng‐ngŏh‐kóng‐kè t‘in‐sz̀ tuì‐ngŏh‐wâ, Neĭ‐iù‐faatₒ‐sheng kiù‐wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐kóng; Ngŏh‐waî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tò‐keî, yaû‐waî Sun‐shaan tò‐keî‐shâm‐taaî à.
15 Ngŏh hó‐m̄‐foon‐heí kòh‐ti‐on‐īn kui‐chuê‐kè līt‐pong: yan ngŏh‐shiú‐shiú‐m̄‐foon‐heí kòh‐chân‐shī, k‘uĭ‐teî‐ka‐toh‐foó‐naân lokₒ.
16 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh k‘aaĭ‐king‐sut faan‐ooī‐Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐ch‘uè: maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh‐kè tîn‐uk pit‐tsoî‐k‘eī‐chung‐kìn‐heí, chún‐shīng pit‐màng‐ch‘ut tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐chi‐sheûng à.
17 Iù‐tsoì‐yat‐ooī faatₒ‐sheng kiù‐wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh‐kè chùng‐shēng‐yap, yan tak‐leî‐taāt tseung‐pit‐chì‐shang‐kwóng‐footₒ; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā sheûng‐ch‘é on‐waì Sun‐shaan, yaû‐kaán‐suén Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng.
18 ¶ Ngŏh kuí‐ngaăn laī‐mông, yaû‐t‘aí‐hă sz̀‐chekₒ‐kokₒ.
19 Tsaû‐tuì t‘ūng‐ngŏh‐kóng‐kè t‘in‐sz̀ wâ, Ni‐ti‐haî‐mat‐yĕ ni? K‘uĭ‐tsaû‐taapₒ wâ, Ni‐ti‐haî tá‐saàn Yaū‐t‘aaì, Ĭ‐shik‐līt, kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kokₒ pòh.
20 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû‐peí‐ngŏh t‘aí sz̀‐kòh‐t‘itₒ‐tseûng.
21 Ue‐shî ngŏh wâ, Ni‐ti‐yān laī‐tsô‐mat‐yĕ ni? K‘uĭ‐tsaû tuì‐ngŏh‐wâ, Ni‐ti‐haî tá‐saàn Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè kokₒ, chì‐tò‐mŏ‐yān taam‐ko‐t‘aū: waī‐haî ni‐ti‐yān laī‐king‐haakₒ‐k‘uĭ‐teî, yaû‐k‘ing‐titₒ Līt‐pong‐kè kokₒ, tsik‐haî kuí‐kokₒ laī‐kung‐saàn Yaū‐t‘aaì kíng‐teî‐kè lokₒ.
