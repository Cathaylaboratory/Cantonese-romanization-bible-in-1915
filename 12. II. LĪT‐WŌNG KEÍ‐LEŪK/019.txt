1 HEI‐SAI‐KA wōng t‘eng‐mān, tsaû maakₒ‐līt i‐fūk, k‘aaĭ ts‘o‐mā‐pò cheukₒ‐tsoî shan‐sheûng, yāp‐huì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn.
2 Tá‐faatₒ ka‐tsoí Ĭ‐leî‐à‐kam, shîn‐sé‐ché Ch‘itₒ‐nā, k‘āp tsaì‐sz‐chung‐kè cheúng‐lŏ, to shan‐cheukₒ ts‘o‐mā‐pò, huì kìn À‐moh‐sẑ‐kè tsaí sin‐chi Ĭ‐ts‘oì‐à.
3 Tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Hei‐sai‐ka kóm wâ, Kam‐yāt haî waân‐naân chaakₒ‐fāt, kûng sau‐yūk‐kè yāt‐tsź: hó‐ts‘z̆ so‐tsaí lām‐ch‘aán, mŏ līk shang‐ch‘aán k‘uĭ.
4 Waāk‐ché neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, t‘eng‐mān Laāp‐sha‐kei shóh‐kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsik‐haî tseung k‘uĭ chué‐yān À‐shūt wōng tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ laī waí‐p‘òng shang‐oōt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì; waāk‐ché neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā háng chaakₒ‐fāt k‘uĭ shóh‐t‘eng‐mān‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ: koò‐ts‘ź neĭ iù waî ni‐ti shîng‐hâ‐kè uē‐mān, t‘aī‐heí neĭ‐kè k‘eī‐t‘ó.
5 Kóm‐tsaû Hei‐sai‐ka wōng‐kè shān‐pūk, tsaû tò Ĭ‐ts‘oì‐à‐ch‘uè.
6 Ĭ‐ts‘oì‐à tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Iù kóm‐yeûng ooī‐fuk neĭ‐teî chué‐yān wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Maĭ‐yan neĭ shóh‐t‘eng‐mān‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ tsaû kuî‐p‘à, tsik‐haî À‐shūt wōng‐kè pūk‐yān, waí‐p‘òng ngŏh kóh‐ti shuetₒ‐wâ.
7 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit shaí k‘uĭ yan t‘eng‐mān fung‐shing, tsaû laāp‐ì faan‐huì poón‐teî; ngŏh tseung lîng k‘uĭ tsoî poón‐teî, peî‐kìm mīt‐mōng.
8 ¶ Laāp‐sha‐kei t‘eng‐mān À‐shūt wōng leī‐pīt La‐kat, tsaû faan huì: kong‐ngaam uê‐kìn wōng kung‐tá Laāp‐nā.
9 Yaû t‘eng‐mān yān kóm wâ, Koó‐shāt wōng Tāk‐hap‐ka ch‘ut‐laī, seúng kûng neĭ chaang‐chìn: tsaû tsoì tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché huì kìn Hei‐sai‐ka wâ,
10 Iù kóm‐yeûng tuì Yaū‐taaî wōng Hei‐sai‐ka wâ, Maĭ‐t‘eng neĭ shóh‐í‐laaî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì hei‐p‘ìn neĭ, wâ Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng pit m̄‐kaau‐kwòh À‐shūt wōng‐kè shaú.
11 T‘aí‐a, neĭ yă‐ts‘āng t‘eng‐mān À‐shūt‐kè līt‐wōng, heùng līt‐kwokₒ tím‐yeûng hāng, tím‐yeûng tsûn‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî: kóm neĭ nāng tak‐kaù me?
12 Ngŏh līt‐tsó shóh‐mīt‐kè; Hóh‐saàn, Hap‐laān, Leĭ‐sitₒ, kûng T‘aī‐la‐saatₒ‐teî shóh‐chuê Oi‐t‘īn‐yān, ni‐ti līt‐kwokₒ‐kè shān, heí kaù‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî me?
13 Hap‐moōt‐wōng, À‐paatₒ wōng. Sai‐faatₒ‐ngă‐yam‐shēng‐kè wōng, Hei‐nā k‘āp Ĭ‐ngă‐kè wōng, to haí pin‐ch‘uè ni?
14 Hei‐sai‐ka ts‘ūng sz̀‐ché‐kè shaú tsipₒ‐hiu shue‐sùn, t‘aí‐uēn: Hei‐sai‐ka tsaû sheŭng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn, chín‐hoi shue‐sùn tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn.
15 Hei‐sai‐ka tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn k‘eī‐t‘ó wâ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, chuê‐tsoî kei‐lô‐ping‐kè, tsô shaì‐sheûng maân‐kwokₒ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, tūk‐haî neĭ; t‘in‐teî yīk haî neĭ shóh‐ch‘òng‐tsô.
16 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, chak‐ĭ shuī‐t‘èng: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, hoi‐ngaăn t‘aí‐koò: t‘eng Sai‐nā‐kei‐laāp‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, yan k‘uĭ tá‐faatₒ sz̀‐ché waí‐p‘òng shang‐oōt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì.
17 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, À‐shūt‐kè līt‐wōng, kwóh‐haî mīt līt‐kwokₒ kûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè teî.
18 Ī‐ch‘é wing k‘uĭ‐teî chùng‐shān tsoî fóh‐chung: yan kóh‐ti m̄‐haî sheûng‐taì, haî yān shaú shóh‐tsô‐kè kung‐tsokₒ mūk‐shēk‐che: shóh‐ĭ peî‐waí‐mīt.
19 Ngŏh‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, yan‐ts‘ź k‘aū neĭ kaù ngŏh‐teî t‘uetₒ‐lat k‘uĭ‐kè shaú, táng t‘in‐hâ līt‐kwokₒ, to chi‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ tūk‐haî Sheûng‐Taì à.
20 ¶ Kóm‐tsaû À‐moh‐sẑ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐ts‘oì‐à, tá‐faatₒ yān huì kìn Hei‐sai‐ka, wâ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Neĭ heùng ngŏh k‘eī‐t‘ó, kung À‐shūt wōng Sai‐nā‐kei‐laāp‐kè, ngŏh ĭ‐king t‘eng‐mān lokₒ.
21 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐lûn‐k‘āp k‘uĭ haî kóm; Sun‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ miŭ‐shî neĭ, hei‐siù neĭ; Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè nuĭ heùng neĭ nîng‐t‘aū.
22 Neĭ shóh‐waí‐p‘òng‐kè, sitₒ‐tūk‐kè, mat‐shuī ni? neĭ suen‐yeūng neĭ sheng, ko‐ngô neĭ ngaăn, kung mat‐shuī ni? Heí‐m̄‐haî kung Ĭ‐shik‐līt Tūk‐yat‐kè Shìng‐Ché me?
23 Neĭ t‘okₒ neĭ sz̀‐ché, waí‐p‘òng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh tuk‐taaì hó‐toh chìn‐ch‘e, ĭ‐king sheŭng shaan‐téng, tò Leî‐pa‐nuên‐kè noî‐teî, ngŏh tseung chaám‐fāt ko‐taaî‐kè paakₒ‐heung‐shuê, kûng meĭ‐hó‐kè ts‘ūng‐shuê: ngŏh pit yāp k‘uĭ kíng‐teî‐kè haakₒ‐uê, ī‐ch‘é lōk‐tò k‘uĭ feī‐t‘īn‐kè maû‐lām.
24 Ngŏh ts‘āng kwāt tsaû yám î‐pong‐kè shuí, ī‐ch‘é yûng ngŏh keukₒ‐chaang taāp kon Oi‐k‘āp yat‐ts‘aì‐kè hōh.
25 Ngŏh taaî‐tsó tsô‐shīng ni‐kîn, neĭ heí meî‐ts‘āng t‘eng‐mān haî ngŏh, tsẑ‐koó‐ĭ‐loī tîng‐tuēt ts‘ź‐sẑ me? uē‐kam haî ngŏh shīng‐tsaû, shaí neĭ mīt kin‐koò‐kè shēng, pìn‐waī fong‐teî.
26 Koò‐ts‘ź k‘eī‐chung kui‐mān uĕn‐yeūk mŏ‐līk, king‐fong, sau‐ts‘aām, haî hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘īn‐ts‘ó, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘eng‐ts‘oì, hó‐ts‘z̆ uk‐sheûng‐kè ts‘ó‐kon, hó‐ts‘z̆ meî cheúng‐shīng tsaû kon‐foo‐kè wōh‐suî.
27 Ngŏh chi‐tò neĭ‐kè ts‘ŏh‐ch‘uè, kûng neĭ‐kè ch‘ut‐yāp, k‘āp neĭ heùng‐ngŏh‐kè p‘ŏ‐hân.
28 Yan neĭ heùng ngŏh p‘ŏ‐hân, kûng neĭ‐kè huen‐wa, ĭ‐king shing‐sheŭng yāp‐tsoî ngŏh ĭ‐chung, koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh pit yûng ngau laī ngau‐chuê neĭ peî, pit yûng ngŏh lāk chai‐tsoî neĭ haú, lîng neĭ t‘uì‐haû, chuèn‐tò neĭ shóh‐laī‐kè lô.
29 Ni‐ti waî neĭ tsô chìng‐kuì à. Kam nīn neĭ‐teì hóh‐ĭ shīk tsẑ‐īn shang‐kè māt, taî‐î nīn, shīk yaū ni‐ti māt shang‐ch‘aán‐kè; taî‐saam nīn, hóh‐ĭ saatₒ‐chúng, hóh‐ĭ shau‐shīng, hóh‐ĭ chùng‐p‘ō‐t‘ō‐uēn, shīk k‘eī‐chung‐kè kwóh.
30 Yaū‐taaî ka shîng‐hâ peî‐naân‐kè mān, pit hâ‐t‘aū sham‐kan, sheûng‐t‘aū kitₒ‐kwóh.
31 Yan‐waî pit yaŭ uē‐shîng‐kè mān, ts‘ūng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng ch‘ut‐laī, pit yaŭ peî‐naân‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, ch‘ut tsẑ Sun‐shaan: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yan īt‐sam, pit shīng‐tsaû ts‘ź‐sẑ.
32 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lûn À‐shūt wōng wâ, K‘uĭ pit‐m̄‐tak‐yāp ts‘ź‐shēng, yīk m̄‐tak tsoî shēng‐noî shê yat tsìn, m̄‐tak ning t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī tò shēng‐ts‘īn, yīk m̄‐tak chuk‐luĭ kung shēng.
33 Pit ts‘ūng k‘uĭ shóh‐laī‐kè lô t‘uì‐haû faan‐huì, pit m̄‐tak‐yāp ts‘ź shēng, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kóm wâ lokₒ.
34 Yan‐waî ngŏh pit waî tsẑ‐keí k‘āp waî ngŏh pūk Taaî‐waî‐kè uēn‐koò, oô‐waî ts‘ź‐shēng, ch‘íng‐kaù ts‘ź‐shēng.
35 ¶ Shuī‐chi kóh‐maăn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè t‘in‐sz̀ ch‘ut‐huì tsoî À‐shūt wōng yīng‐chung, kung‐kik chíng‐sź kwan‐ping shāp‐paatₒ‐maân‐nğ‐ts‘in yān: chiu‐tsó heí‐shan t‘aí‐kìn, kaai‐haî sź‐shi lokₒ.
36 À‐shūt wōng Sai‐nā‐kei‐laāp, tsaû leī‐hoi ts‘ź‐teî faan‐huì, chuê‐tsoî Neī‐neī‐meī.
37 Tong yāt k‘uĭ tsoî k‘uĭ shān Nīk‐lōk miû shūng‐paaì, k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí À‐tak‐maĭ‐lāk, Sha‐leĭ‐sz: yûng kìm kik‐sź k‘uĭ, t‘ō‐peî‐huì À‐la‐laāp‐teî. K‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐saatₒ‐hap‐tûn, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
