1 TS‘AAM‐SUEN huì‐tò Ka‐saatₒ haí kóh‐ch‘uè t‘aí‐kìn yat‐kòh keî‐foŏ, tsaû kûng k‘uĭ ts‘an‐kân.
2 Yaŭ‐yān wâ‐kwòh Ka‐saatₒ‐yān chi, Ts‘aam‐suen ĭ‐king laī‐tò ni‐ch‘uè. Chùng‐yān tsaû sz̀‐waī hon‐shaú, haí shēng‐moōn maaī‐fūk, chung‐yê tsîng‐tsĭng wâ, Táng‐tò t‘in‐kwong, ngŏh‐teî pit‐tîng shaatₒ k‘uĭ.
3 Ts‘aam‐suen fàn‐tò poòn‐yê, tsoî poòn‐yê heí‐shan, tseung shēng‐moōn, k‘āp moōn leŭng‐kuetₒ, līn moōn‐shaan, yat‐ts‘aī ch‘aakₒ‐hiu, taam tsoî pokₒ‐sheûng, t‘oī‐tò Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn tuì‐mîn‐kè shaan‐téng.
4 ¶ Haû‐loī Ts‘aam‐suen oì yat‐kòh foŏ‐yān, mēng‐kiù Taaî‐leî‐la, haí Sóh‐līt‐shaan‐kuk chuê.
5 Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, sheŭng‐huì kìn kòh‐foŏ‐yān, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, K‘aū neĭ yăn‐yaŭ Ts‘aam‐suen, shì‐t‘aàm k‘uĭ tím yaŭ kòm‐taaî heì‐līk, táng ngŏh‐teî chi‐tò tím‐yeûng shìng‐tak‐k‘uĭ, kw‘án‐póng‐k‘uĭ, foó‐naân‐k‘uĭ: ngŏh‐teî tsaû mooĭ‐yān peí ngān yat‐ts‘in‐yat‐paakₒ kwòh‐neĭ.
6 Taaî‐leî‐la tuì Ts‘aam‐suen wâ, K‘aū neĭ kóng‐kwòh ngŏh t‘eng, neĭ‐kè heì‐līk tím‐yaŭ kòm‐taaî ni? iù yûng mat‐fong‐faatₒ nāng kw‘án‐póng neĭ, foó‐naân neĭ ni?
7 Ts‘aam‐suen taapₒ wâ, Yeūk yaŭ yān k‘aaĭ ts‘at‐t‘iū meî‐kon‐foo‐kè ts‘eng‐shīng laī póng ngŏh, ngŏh tsaû uĕn‐yeūk, hó‐ts‘z̆ pīt‐yān yat‐yeûng lokₒ.
8 Ue‐shî Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, ning ts‘at‐t‘iū m̄‐ts‘āng kon‐foo‐kè ts‘eng‐shīng kaau‐kwòh foŏ‐yān, foŏ‐yān tsaû k‘aaĭ‐laī póng Ts‘aam‐suen.
9 Yaŭ maaī‐fūk‐kè yāu, uê‐sin ts‘ōng‐hiu foŏ‐yān‐kè fōng luĭ. Foŏ‐yān tuì Ts‘aam‐suen wâ, Ts‘aam‐suen à, Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān laī chukₒ neĭ là. Ts‘aam‐suen tsaû chàng‐t‘uĕn shīng, hó‐ts‘z̆ chàng‐t‘uĕn king‐kwòh‐fóh‐kè mā‐sz yat‐yeûng. Kóm‐tsaû yīng‐īn m̄‐chi‐tò k‘uĭ heì‐līk‐kè loī‐yaū.
10 Taaî‐leî‐la tuì Ts‘aam‐suen wâ, Neĭ hei‐moōn ngŏh, tuì ngŏh kóng taaî‐wâ: uē‐kam k‘aū neĭ wâ‐ngŏh‐chi, yûng mat‐yĕ fong‐faatₒ chì‐póng‐tak neĭ ni?
11 Taapₒ wâ, Yeūk yaŭ‐yān k‘aaĭ meî‐ts‘āng‐shaí‐kwòh‐kè san‐shīng póng‐kán ngŏh, ngŏh tsaû uĕn‐yeūk, hó‐ts‘z̆ pīt‐yān yat‐yeûng.
12 Taaî‐leî‐la tsaû tseung san‐shīng póng k‘uĭ, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ts‘aam‐suen à, Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān laī chukₒ neĭ lòh. Yaŭ yān maaī‐fūk haí fōng‐luĭ. Ts‘aam‐suen tsaû tseung peì‐pokₒ‐kè shīng chàng‐t‘uĕn, hó‐ts‘z̆ chàng‐t‘uĕn yat‐t‘iū sìn kóm‐yeûng.
13 Taaî‐leî‐la tuì Ts‘aam‐suen wâ, Neĭ tò uē‐kam chûng haî hei‐moōn ngŏh, tuì ngŏh kóng taaî‐wâ‐che uên neĭ wâ‐ngŏh‐chi, yûng mat‐yĕ fong‐faatₒ póng‐tak neĭ ni? Ts‘aam‐suen wâ, Neĭ yeūk k‘aaĭ ngŏh t‘aū‐sheûng‐kè faatₒ‐pin ts‘at‐t‘iū, yûng waî‐sìn t‘ūng‐chik, kóm‐tsaû hóh‐ĭ lokₒ.
14 Taaî‐leî‐la tseung k‘uĭ‐kè t‘aū‐faatₒ uĕ‐waî‐sìn t‘ūng‐chik, yûng teng chíng‐shāt, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ts‘aam‐suen, Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān laī chukₒ neĭ là. Ts‘aam‐suen tsaû síng‐kokₒ, tseung chik‐kei‐kè teng k‘āp waî‐sìn màng‐hiu.
15 Taaî‐leî‐la tuì Ts‘aam‐suen wâ, Neĭ sam m̄‐heùng ngŏh, tím‐kóng‐tak‐wâ oì ngŏh ni? neĭ ĭ‐king hei‐moōn ngŏh saam‐ts‘z̀, m̄‐wâ ngŏh chi neĭ tím yaŭ kòm‐taaî heì‐līk.
16 Taaî‐leî‐la yāt‐yāt yûng shuetₒ‐wâ ts‘ui‐pik k‘uĭ, chì k‘uĭ sam faān‐moôn seúng‐sź;
17 Ts‘aam‐suen tsaû tsûn‐sam kóng‐saaì, wâ, Heùng‐loī tsoî t‘aū‐sheûng meî‐ts‘āng yûng t‘aì‐to, yan‐waî ngŏh tsẑ mŏ‐t‘oi kwai‐heùng Sheûng‐Taì tsô Nōh‐saì‐līt‐yān: yeūk t‘aì‐hiu ngŏh‐kè t‘aū‐faatₒ, ngŏh‐kè heì‐līk pit leī‐hoi ngŏh, chì ngŏh uĕn‐yeūk hó‐ts‘z̆ pīt‐yān yat‐yeûng lokₒ.
18 Taaî‐leî‐la kìn k‘uĭ tsûn‐sam kóng‐saaì, tsaû tá‐faatₒ‐yān huì kiù Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè mūk‐paakₒ laī, wâ, K‘uĭ ĭ‐king tuì ngŏh tsûn‐sam kóng‐saaì, uē‐kam neĭ‐teî tsoì sheŭng‐laī yat‐ts‘z̀. Kóm‐tsaû Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, shaú ning‐chuê ngān, sheŭng‐tò foŏ‐yān kòh‐ch‘uè.
19 Taaî‐leî‐la lîng Ts‘aam‐suen chàm‐chuê k‘uĭ‐kè sat laī‐fàn, kiù yat‐kòh yān laī t‘aì k‘uĭ t‘aū‐sheûng kóh‐ts‘at‐t‘iū faatₒ‐pin, Taaî‐leî‐la tsaû foó‐naân k‘uĭ, k‘uĭ‐kè līk kwóh‐īn leī‐hoi k‘uĭ.
20 Taaî‐leî‐la wâ, Ts‘aam‐suen à, Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān laī chukₒ neĭ là. Ts‘aam‐suen tsaû síng‐kokₒ, sam kóm wâ, Ngŏh iù hōk‐faan ts‘īn keí‐ts‘z̀, ch‘ut‐huì yuk‐tûng‐hă shan. Taân k‘uĭ m̄‐chi‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ĭ‐king leī‐hoi k‘uĭ à.
21 Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān chukₒ‐chuê k‘uĭ, waatₒ‐hiu k‘uĭ‐kè ngaăn‐tsing, yaû taaì k‘uĭ lōk‐tò Ka‐saatₒ, k‘aaĭ t‘ūng‐līn póng k‘uĭ; kóm‐tsaû k‘uĭ haí kaam‐luĭ t‘ui‐môh.
22 Taân k‘uĭ‐kè t‘aū‐faatₒ peî‐t‘aì‐chi‐haû, yaû tsîm‐tsĭm cheúng‐heí‐laī.
23 ¶ Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè mūk‐paakₒ tsuî‐tsaāp, seúng kûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shān Taaî‐kung hìn taaî‐tsaì, ī‐ch‘é heí‐lōk: tsaû kóm wâ, Ngŏh‐teî‐kè shān, ĭ‐king tseung ngŏh‐teî ch‘aū‐tīk Ts‘aam‐suen, kaau‐kwòh ngŏh‐teî shaú‐chung lokₒ.
24 Paakₒ‐sìng t‘aí‐kìn Ts‘aam‐suen, tsaû tsaàn‐meĭ k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shān, wâ, Ngŏh‐teî‐kè ch‘aū‐yān, waí‐waaî ngŏh‐teî‐kè teî‐t‘ó; ī‐ch‘é shaatₒ hó‐toh yān, ngŏh‐teî‐kè shān, ĭ‐king tseung k‘uĭ kaau‐kwòh ngŏh‐teî‐kè shaú lokₒ.
25 Chùng‐yān sam kīk‐foon‐heí, tsaû wâ iù kiù Ts‘aam‐suen laī, táng k‘uĭ waî ngŏh‐teî heì‐shá. Kóm‐tsaû kiù Ts‘aam‐suen ts‘ūng kaam‐chung ch‘ut‐laī; Ts‘aam‐suen haí chùng‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn heì‐shá: k‘uĭ‐teî shaí k‘uĭ k‘eĭ‐tsoî leŭng‐t‘iū ch‘uĕ‐kè chung‐kaan.
26 Ts‘aam‐suen mân t‘oh k‘uĭ shaú kòh‐haû‐shang‐tsaí wâ, Seúng neĭ t‘oh ngŏh shaú moh‐chuê t‘okₒ‐uk‐kè ch‘uĕ, táng ngŏh aai‐chuê‐hă.
27 Kòh‐chân uk‐luĭ ch‘ung‐moŏn naām‐nuĭ; Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè chùng‐mūk‐paakₒ to haí‐ch‘uè; laū‐sheûng yeukₒ‐yaŭ saam‐ts‘in naām‐nuĭ, laī t‘aí Ts‘aam‐suen heì‐shá.
28 Ts‘aam‐suen t‘ó‐kò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū neĭ keì‐nîm ngŏh, Sheûng‐Taì à, k‘aū neĭ kin‐chòng ngŏh tūk‐haî ni‐yat‐ts‘z̀, táng ngŏh haí Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān shan‐sheûng, shan‐hiu waatₒ ngŏh sheung‐ngaăn‐kè uen.
29 Ts‘aan‐suen tsaû cha‐chuê t‘okₒ‐uk‐leŭng‐t‘iū ch‘uĕ, yaû‐shaú cha yat‐t‘iū, tsóh‐shaú cha yat‐t‘iū.
30 Ts‘aan‐suen wâ, Ngŏh ts‘īng‐uên t‘ūng Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān yat‐ts‘aī sź. Tsaû tsûn‐līk wat‐shan; uk tsaû k‘ing‐titₒ, aatₒ‐chuê mūk‐paakₒ k‘āp uk‐luĭ shóh‐yaŭ‐kè paakₒ‐sìng. Ts‘aam‐suen sź‐kòh‐shī, shóh‐shaatₒ‐kè yān, peí k‘uĭ tsoî‐shang‐kòh‐shī shóh‐shaatₒ‐kè chûng‐toh à.
31 Ts‘aam‐suen‐kè hing‐taî, t‘ūng k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an‐kè ts‘uēn‐ka, to lōk‐huì wán k‘uĭ‐kè shi, t‘oī‐faan‐sheŭng‐laī, tsòng‐tsoî Sóh‐la Ĭ‐shāt‐tô chung‐kaan, haí k‘uĭ‐kè foô Mă‐nōh‐à‐kè fān‐mô‐ch‘uè. Ts‘aam‐suen tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān sẑ‐sz î‐shāp nīn.
