1 YĒ‐OÔ taî‐ts‘at nīn, Yeukₒ‐hāt tang‐waî; tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng sz̀‐shāp nīn. K‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an haî Pīt‐shî‐pa‐yān, mēng‐kiù Sai‐peì‐à.
2 Yeukₒ‐hāt tak tsaì‐sz Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî kaaù‐fàn k‘uĭ‐kè kòm‐noî yāt‐tsź, hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐foon‐heí‐kè sẑ.
3 Tūk‐haî mŏ ch‘uē‐huì yau‐t‘aān: chùng‐yān yīng‐īn haí yau‐t‘aān‐sheûng fān‐heung, hìn‐tsaì.
4 ¶ Yeukₒ‐hāt tuì tsaì‐sz wâ, Faān yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk, fan‐pīt‐waī‐shìng‐chi‐māt, shóh‐chīk t‘ung‐hāng‐kè ngān, m̄‐lûn haî tsaì‐sz shóh‐koó‐kè shan‐kà ngān, waāk ts‘ūng‐sam shóh‐uên, lōk‐kuen‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè ngān;
5 Tsaì‐sz iù kokₒ‐yaū kokₒ‐shóh seung‐shik‐kè: yûng laī sau‐chíng shóh‐yaŭ p‘òh‐waaî‐chi‐ch‘uè.
6 Līk‐chì Yeukₒ‐hāt wōng taî‐î‐shāp‐saam nīn, tsaì‐sz yīng meî sau‐chíng tîn‐uk‐kè p‘òh‐waaî‐ch‘uè.
7 Koò‐ts‘ź Yeukₒ‐hāt wōng kiù tsaì‐sz cheúng Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî, k‘āp chùng‐tsaì‐sz laī, tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî waî‐mat m̄‐sau‐chíng tîn‐uk, p‘òh‐waaî‐chi‐ch‘uè ni? uē‐kam pat‐chún neĭ‐teî ts‘ūng seung‐shik‐kè yān, tsoì shaû ngān lokₒ, iù tseung kóh‐ti ngān laī sau‐chíng tîn‐uk‐kè p‘òh‐waaî‐ch‘uè.
8 Chùng‐tsaì‐sz ts‘ūng‐mîng, m̄‐tsoì shaû mān‐kè ngān, yīk m̄‐sau‐chíng tîn‐uk p‘òh‐waaî chi‐ch‘uè.
9 Waī‐haî tsaì‐sz‐cheúng Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî, tseung yat‐kòh kwaî, haí kwaî‐koì tsuèn‐hiu‐kòh‐lung, fòng‐tsoî t‘aān‐kè chak‐pin, haí yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk moōn‐kè yaû‐pîn: shaú‐moōn‐kè chùng‐tsaì‐sz, tseung faān‐yaŭ sùng‐yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè ngān, tsûn‐chai kwaî‐noî.
10 Kìn kwaî‐chung‐kè ngān ĭ‐king toh, wōng‐kè shîn‐sé yān, k‘āp tsaì‐sz‐cheúng tò, kaì‐suèn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐noî shóh‐tak‐kè ngān, yûng toî chong‐chuê.
11 Yaû k‘aaĭ shóh‐ch‘ing‐kwòh‐kè ngān, kaau‐kwòh chùng‐kung‐tsokₒ, tsik‐haî tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn paân‐sẑ‐kè yān: paân‐sẑ‐kè yān, tseung ngān kaau‐kwòh sau‐chíng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè mūk‐tseûng, k‘āp kìn‐tsô‐kè yān.
12 Yaû kaau‐kwòh ngă‐tseûng, shēk‐tseûng, maaĭ mūk‐liû, k‘āp tsōk‐shīng‐kè shēk, laī‐sau‐chíng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk p‘òh‐waaî‐chi‐ch‘uè, pìng maaĭ sau‐chíng tîn‐uk kokₒ‐yeûng‐ts‘oī‐liû.
13 Shóh‐sùng‐yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè ngān, mŏ‐k‘aaĭ‐laī tsô tîn‐chung shóh‐yûng‐kè ngān‐p‘oōn, ngān‐chuk‐tsín, ngān‐uē, ngān‐heúng‐kokₒ, pìng shóh‐yaŭ pīt‐yeûng‐kam‐heì, ngān‐heì:
14 Yan k‘uĭ‐teî tseung kóh‐ti ngān, kaau‐kwòh tsô‐kung‐kè yān, sau‐chíng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk.
15 Kim‐chi paân‐sẑ‐kè yān, yan‐waî paân‐sẑ shīng‐shāt, shóh‐ĭ kaau‐ngān‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî sheūng‐kung‐kà, m̄‐kûng k‘uĭ‐teî suèn‐shò.
16 Waī‐haî shūk‐hin‐kè ngān, shūk‐tsuî‐kè ngān, kwai‐ue tsaì‐sz: m̄‐fûng‐yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk.
17 ¶ Tong‐shī À‐laān wōng Hap‐sitₒ, sheŭng‐huì kung‐tá Ka‐tāk, ts‘uí‐hiu: tsaû laāp‐ì sheŭng‐huì kung‐tá Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng.
18 Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt tseung k‘uĭ līt‐tsó Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ, Yeukₒ‐laān, À‐hap‐tsê, shóh‐fan‐pīt‐waī‐shìng‐kè ts‘oī‐māt, k‘āp k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí shóh‐fan‐pīt‐waī‐shìng‐kè ts‘oī‐māt, pìng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk foò‐chung, kûng wōng‐kung‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè kam, tsûn‐teî sùng‐huì À‐laān wōng Hap‐sitₒ; Hap‐sitₒ tsaû pīt Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng huì‐hiu.
19 Yeukₒ‐hāt k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, k‘āp k‘uĭ faān shóh‐hāng‐kè, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
20 Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè shān‐pūk, maū‐faán, shaatₒ Yeukₒ‐hāt tsoî kân Sik‐la‐kè Maĭ‐lōh kung.
21 K‘uĭ‐kè shān‐pūk, tsik‐haî Shî‐maĭ‐aapₒ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐kaapₒ, kûng Shuetₒ‐māk‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐paatₒ, kik‐k‘uĭ‐chì‐sź; chùng‐yān tseung k‘uĭ tsòng‐tsoî Taaî‐waî shēng, haí k‘uĭ līt‐tsó‐kè fān‐teî: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí À‐mă‐tsê kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
