1 TS‘Ź‐HAÛ, À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng‐kè nau‐nô‐chí‐sik chi‐shī, k‘uĭ keì‐nîm Ngă‐shāt‐teî, kûng k‘uĭ shóh‐tsô‐kè, ī‐ch‘é heùng k‘uĭ shóh‐tîng‐kè sheûng‐uê.
2 Ue‐shî wōng‐kè shî‐shān tsaù wōng wâ, Pat‐uē waî wōng chaaú‐ts‘ām meĭ‐maaû‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ:
3 Yaû ts‘éng wōng p‘aaì koon tsoî kwokₒ‐chung‐kè kokₒ‐shaáng, chiu‐tsaāp shóh‐yaŭ meĭ‐maaû‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ, tò Shue‐shaan‐kung, tò nuĭ‐uên kaau‐kwòh koón‐leĭ kung‐nuĭ‐kè t‘aaì‐kaàm Hei‐k‘eī shaú; k‘aaĭ kitₒ‐shan‐kè māt ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî:
4 Wōng shóh‐foon‐heí‐kè shiù‐nuĭ, hóh‐ĭ laāp k‘uĭ toî Ngă‐shāt‐teî tsô wōng‐haû. Wōng ĭ ts‘ź‐sẑ waī hó; tsaû chiù‐kóm‐yeûng laī tsô.
5 ¶ Haí Shue‐shaan‐kung‐noî, yaŭ yat‐kòh Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, mēng kiù Mūk‐taí‐koí, haî Pîn‐ngă‐măn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè Kei‐sẑ‐kè tsang suen, Shî‐mooĭ‐kè suen, Ngaaī‐ĭ‐kè tsaí;
6 Sik‐yāt Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng, Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, tseung Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam, kûng tsź‐mān ts‘ūng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng lŏ‐leūk‐huì, Mūk‐taí‐koí yīk tsoî k‘eī‐chung peî lŏ‐leūk.
7 Mūk‐taí‐koí yeŭng‐yūk k‘uĭ shuk‐kè nuĭ, Hap‐taaî‐sha, yaû mēng Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ: yan‐waî ts‘ź‐nuĭ mŏ‐foô‐mŏ, yaû shâm‐meĭ‐maaû, k‘uĭ foô‐mŏ sź‐hiu; Mūk‐taí‐koí ts‘uí k‘uĭ tòng‐tsokₒ tsẑ‐keí‐kè nuĭ.
8 Wōng‐kè sheûng‐uê ĭ‐king p‘ìn‐ch‘uēn, yaŭ hó‐toh shiù‐nuĭ haí Shue‐shaan‐kung chiu‐tsaāp, kaau‐kwòh Hei‐k‘eī, Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ yīk sùng‐yāp wōng‐kung, kaau‐kwòh Hei‐k‘eī, tsik‐haî koón‐leĭ kung‐nuĭ‐kè.
9 Hei‐k‘eī foon‐heí ts‘ź‐shiù‐nuĭ, tsaû shi yān‐ts‘z̄ kwòh‐k‘uĭ; tsik‐hak tseung kitₒ‐shan‐kè māt, kûng ying‐tak‐kè fân, k‘āp k‘uĭ shóh‐tong‐tak‐kè ts‘at‐kòh kung‐nuĭ peí‐kwòh k‘uĭ: yaû shaí k‘uĭ kûng kóh‐ti kung‐nuĭ, poon‐yāp tsoî nuĭ‐uên kīk‐hó‐kè teî‐fong.
10 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ meî‐ts‘āng tseung tsẑ‐keí tsung‐tsūk ts‘an‐shūk kóng‐kwòh‐yān‐chi: yan‐waî Mūk‐taí‐koí kaaù k‘uĭ maĭ kóng à.
11 Mūk‐taí‐koí yāt‐yāt haí nuĭ‐uên‐chi‐ts‘īn loī‐wŏng, seúng chi‐tò Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ‐kè sẑ‐ts‘īng, k‘āp tím‐yeûng ch‘ué‐chì k‘uĭ.
12 (Shiù‐nuĭ kitₒ‐shan‐chi‐shī, kaì shāp‐î‐kòh uēt, lūk‐kòh uēt yûng moōt‐yeūk‐yaū, lūk‐kòh uēt yûng heung‐liû, k‘āp nuĭ‐yān kitₒ‐shan‐kè māt;) shiù‐nuĭ ĭ‐king pīt kui shāp‐î‐kòh uēt, òn‐chuê foŏ‐nuĭ‐kè laî, tsaû i ts‘z̀‐tsuî yāp‐huì kìn À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng.
13 Shiù‐nuĭ haî kóm‐yeûng yāp‐huì kìn wōng; faān k‘uĭ shóh‐seúng‐kè, tsaû peí‐kwòh k‘uĭ, taaì‐maaī ts‘ūng nuĭ‐uên yāp‐tò wōng‐kung.
14 Yê‐maăn yāp‐huì, mīng‐yāt faan‐tò, nuĭ‐yāp taî‐î uên, kaau‐kwòh koón‐leĭ kung‐fei, wōng‐kè t‘aaì‐kaàm Sha‐kaapₒ, ch‘uē‐fei wōng foon‐heí k‘uĭ, tsoì t‘aī k‘uĭ‐kè mēng chiû k‘uĭ, m̄‐tsoì huì kìn wōng.
15 Mūk‐taí‐koí‐kè shuk, À‐peì‐hoī‐kè nuĭ, tsik‐haî Mūk‐taí‐koí tsokₒ‐waī tsẑ‐keí‐kè nuĭ, Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ òn ts‘z̀‐tsuî tong yāp wōng‐kung chi‐shī, ch‘uē‐hiu koón‐leĭ kung‐nuĭ wōng‐kè t‘aaì‐kaàm Hei‐k‘eī shóh‐fan‐foò peí‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ, k‘uĭ mŏ‐lóh pīt‐yeûng. Taân‐faān kìn k‘uĭ‐kè tsaû foon‐heí k‘uĭ.
16 Kóm‐tsaû wōng taî‐ts‘at nīn shāp‐uēt, tsik‐haî Taí‐paakₒ‐uēt, Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ peî‐taaì‐yāp wōng‐kung, kìn À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng.
17 Wōng oì Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ toh‐kwòh oì chùng‐nuĭ, k‘uĭ haí wōng mîn‐ts‘īn tak yan‐ch‘úng, peí‐kaaù chùng‐t‘ūng‐nuĭ kàng‐toh; wōng tsaû k‘aaĭ wōng‐haû mĭn‐laū peí‐kwòh k‘uĭ taaì, laāp k‘uĭ toî Ngă‐shāt‐teî tsô wōng‐haû.
18 Ue‐shî wōng waî chùng‐kwan‐cheúng shān‐pūk paaí‐ch‘itₒ taaî‐īn‐tsīk, tsik‐haî tsô Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ‐kè īn‐tsīk; chiù‐wōng‐foò‐haû‐kè yan‐tín, kaám‐mĭn kokₒ‐shaáng ts‘īn‐leūng, yaû taaî‐paan sheúng‐ts‘z̀.
19 Chùng‐t‘ūng‐nuĭ taî‐î ts‘z̀ chi‐shī, Mūk‐taí‐koí ts‘ŏh‐tsoî wōng‐kè moōn.
20 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ yīng‐īn i‐chuê Mūk‐taí‐koí shóh‐fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐kè; meî‐ts‘āng tseung k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí‐kè tsung‐tsūk ts‘an‐shūk kóng‐kwòh‐yān‐chi: yan‐waî Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ tsun‐i Mūk‐taí‐koí‐kè mîng, hó‐ts‘z̆ sik‐yāt foó‐yeŭng k‘uĭ chi‐shī yat‐yeûng.
21 Tong kòh‐yāt Mūk‐taí‐koí ts‘ŏh‐tsoî wōng‐moōn chi‐shī, wōng‐kè t‘aaì‐kaàm‐chung, yaŭ hon‐shaú‐moōn‐kè leŭng‐yān, P‘ik‐taan, Taí‐līk, uèn‐hân seúng ch‘itₒ faatₒ lōk‐shaú hoî À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng.
22 Mūk‐taí‐koí chi‐tò, tsaû t‘ung‐chi wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ; Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ t‘okₒ Mūk‐taí‐koí‐kè mēng, tseung ts‘ź‐sẑ tsaù‐chi wōng.
23 Ch‘ā‐ch‘aatₒ ts‘ź‐sẑ, kwóh‐haî chan‐ts‘īng; tsaû tiù‐heí leŭng‐kòh‐yān tsoî mūk‐sheûng: yaû tseung ts‘ź‐sẑ tong wōng mîn‐ts‘īn, sé‐tsoî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue.
