1 UE‐SHÎ chùng‐tsź‐mān hó‐ts‘z̆ yat‐kòh‐yān, haí Shuí‐moōn‐ts‘īn footₒ‐teî tsuî‐tsaāp; ts‘éng sẑ‐tsź Ĭ‐sẑ‐la, ning Moh‐sai lūt‐faatₒ‐chi‐shue, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐mîng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè.
2 Ts‘at uēt ch‘oh‐yat yāt, tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐sẑ‐la, taaì lūt‐faatₒ tò kung‐ooî‐ts‘īn, waāk naām waāk nuĭ, uĕ‐k‘āp taân‐faān t‘eng‐kwòh‐nāng‐mīng‐paāk‐kè.
3 Tsaû tsoî Shuí‐moōn‐ts‘īn footₒ‐teî, tsẑ t‘in‐kwong tò aàn‐chaù, tong chùng‐naām‐nuĭ, uĕ‐k‘āp t‘eng‐kwòh‐nāng‐mīng‐paāk‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn tūk‐ch‘ut‐laī; chùng‐tsź‐mān chak‐ĭ t‘eng‐mān kòh‐ti lūt‐faatₒ‐chi‐shue.
4 Sẑ‐tsź Ĭ‐sẑ‐la, k‘eĭ‐tsoî waî ts‘ź‐sẑ tāk‐laāp‐kè mūk t‘oī‐sheûng; Mă‐tak‐taí, Shî‐mă, À‐noî‐ngă, Oo‐leĭ‐à, Hei‐lāk‐ka, Mă‐sai‐ngă, k‘eĭ tsoî k‘uĭ yaû‐pîn; Peí‐t‘aaì‐à, Maĭ‐sha‐leî, Mă‐k‘eī‐à, Hap‐shûn, Hap‐paatₒ‐taaî‐nā, Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à, Maĭ‐shue‐laān, k‘eĭ tsoî k‘uĭ tsóh‐pîn.
5 Ĭ‐sẑ‐la, k‘eĭ tsoî chùng‐tsź‐mān‐chi‐sheûng; haí chùng‐tsź‐mān ngaăn‐ts‘īn, tá‐hoi‐kóh‐pô shue; tá‐hoi chi‐shī, chùng‐tsź‐mān to k‘eĭ‐heí‐shan:
6 Ĭ‐sẑ‐la tsaû tsaàn‐meĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā. Chùng‐tsź‐mān to kuí‐heí shaú yìng sheng wâ, Shīng‐sam‐shóh‐uên, Shīng‐sam‐shóh‐uên: tsaû shuī‐tai‐t‘aū heùng‐teî laī‐paaì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
7 Yē‐shue‐à, Pa‐neī, Shî‐leĭ‐peí, Ngă‐peí, À‐kuk, Sha‐peí‐t‘aaì, Oô‐teî‐ngă, Mă‐sai‐ngă, K‘eī‐leî‐taaî, À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐paatₒ, Hap‐naān, Peí‐loī‐à, kûng Leî‐meî‐yān, to lîng tsź‐mān tsing‐t‘ung lūt‐faatₒ: tsź‐mān k‘eĭ tsoî k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí kóh‐taatₒ.
8 K‘uĭ‐teî līng‐leî tūk‐ch‘ut Sheûng‐Taì‐kè lūt‐faatₒ‐chi‐shue, kaaí‐mīng ì‐sz̀, lîng taaî‐chùng‐hiú‐tak shóh‐tūk‐ch‘ut‐kè.
9 Fong‐paakₒ Neī‐hei‐maĭ, kûng tsô‐tsaì‐sz‐kè sẑ‐tsź Ĭ‐sẑ‐la, uĕ‐k‘āp kaaù‐fàn tsź‐mān‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān, tsaû tuì tsź‐mān wâ, Kam‐yāt haî neĭ‐teî Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shìng‐yāt; maĭ‐yau‐shaū, maĭ‐haàm. Yan‐waî chùng‐tsź‐mān t‘eng‐mān lūt‐faatₒ kòh‐shī, tsaû haàm‐heí‐laī.
10 K‘uĭ yaû tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Iù faan‐huì shīk feī‐meĭ‐kè, yám kom‐t‘īm‐kè, meî‐ts‘āng uê‐peî‐kè tsaû iù fan‐kwòh k‘uĭ: yan ts‘ź‐yāt waî ngŏh‐teî‐kè Chué shīng‐waī‐shìng‐yāt: neĭ‐teî maĭ‐yau‐shaū; yan tak Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè foon‐heí, haî neĭ‐teî‐kè līk‐leûng à.
11 Leî‐meî‐yān kóm‐yeûng on‐waì chùng‐tsź‐mān, wâ, Ts‘ź‐yāt haî shìng‐yāt; iù kaam‐haú, m̄‐hó yau‐shaū.
12 Chùng‐tsź‐mān faan‐huì yám‐shīk, yīk fan sùng‐kwòh yān, ī‐ch‘é taaî‐taaî heí‐lōk, yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî mīng‐paāk shóh‐suen‐ch‘uēn k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ.
13 ¶ Taî‐î yāt, chùng‐tsź‐mān‐kè tsó‐foô ka‐cheúng, kûng tsaì‐sz, uĕ‐k‘āp Leî‐meî‐yān, to ts‘aī‐tsaāp laī‐kìn sẑ‐tsź Ĭ‐sẑ‐la, seúng tsing‐t‘ung lūt‐faatₒ‐chi‐īn.
14 Tsoî lūt‐faatₒ chi‐chung, kìn yaŭ sé‐lōk wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yă‐ts‘āng fan‐foò Moh‐sai, lîng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsoî ts‘at‐uēt tsitₒ‐chi‐shī, iù chuê haí maaū‐uk‐ch‘uè:
15 To iù ch‘uēn‐wān kokₒ‐shēng kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù sheŭng shaan, lóh kóm‐laám‐shuê‐chi, k‘āp yĕ‐kóm‐laám‐shuê‐chi, yaū‐mūk‐chi, kong‐nim‐shuê‐chi, tsó‐shuê‐chi, kûng kokₒ‐maû‐shîng‐kè shuê‐chi, chiù‐i lūt‐faatₒ shóh‐sé‐lōk‐kè, tseung‐laī taapₒ‐maaū‐uk.
16 Kóm‐tsaû tsź‐mān ch‘ut‐huì, lóh‐hiu shuê‐chi laī, kokₒ‐yān haí tsẑ‐keí uk‐sheûng‐mîn, waāk haí uên‐noî, yaû haí Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐kè uên‐noî, waāk haí Shuí‐moōn‐kè footₒ‐teî, waāk haí Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐moōn‐kè footₒ‐shēng, to taapₒ maaū‐uk.
17 Peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐faan‐laī‐kè ooî‐chùng, tsaû taapₒ maaū‐uk kui‐chuê: yan‐waî tsẑ À‐nuên‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kòh‐shī, chì‐tò kam‐yāt, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ts‘ūng‐loī meî‐yaŭ kóm‐yeûng tsô. Ue‐shî chùng‐yān taaî‐taaî foon‐heí.
18 Tsẑ‐taî‐yat yāt, shīk‐tò shau‐meĭ kòh‐yāt, Ĭ‐sẑ‐la yāt‐yāt tūk‐ch‘ut Sheûng‐Taì‐kè lūt‐faatₒ‐chi‐shue. Chùng‐yān shaú tsitₒ ts‘at yāt; taî‐paatₒ yāt, chiù‐sheūng‐laî waī shìng‐ooî.
