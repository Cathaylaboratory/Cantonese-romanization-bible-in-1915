1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT‐YĀN k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ tuî‐nğ, tsoî Moh‐sai, À‐lūn k‘uēn‐hâ t‘ūng‐ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp‐kè, shóh‐king‐kwòh‐kè lô‐ch‘īng haî kóm.
2 Moh‐sai tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò, keì mīng k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐king‐kwòh‐kè lô‐ch‘īng: tsẑ ch‘í‐ch‘oh heí‐hāng, chì‐tò kokₒ‐ch‘uè‐kè teî‐fong, to līt‐mīng tsoî haû.
3 Ching‐uēt shāp‐nğ yāt, k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘ūng Laān‐sat heí‐ch‘īng‐huì; tsik‐haî uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè ts‘z̀ yāt, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsoî chùng Oi‐k‘āp‐yān‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn, ngōng‐īn ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp.
4 Kóh‐shī, Oi‐k‘āp‐yān yīk tsòng‐maaī shóh‐yaŭ‐kè cheúng‐tsź, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐chíng‐sź‐kè: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yīk shám‐p‘oòn chaakₒ‐fāt k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shān‐tseûng.
5 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ts‘ūng Laān‐sat heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Shò‐kotₒ.
6 Ts‘ūng Shò‐kotₒ heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ chak pin‐kè Ĭ‐t‘āng.
7 Ts‘ūng Ĭ‐t‘āng heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, chuèn tò Peí‐hap‐hei‐lūk, haî tsoî Pa‐līk‐saí‐fan tuì‐mîn: tsaû on‐yīng tsoî Māt‐tuēt.
8 Ts‘ūng Peí‐hap‐hei‐lūk heí‐ch‘īng‐huì‐kwòh‐hoí, tsoî Ĭ‐t‘āng kw‘òng‐yĕ hāng lô saam‐yāt, on‐yīng tsoî Mă‐la.
9 Ts‘ūng Mă‐la heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, hāng‐tò Ĭ‐lām: Ĭ‐lām yaŭ shāp‐î shuí‐ts‘uēn, ts‘at‐shāp p‘oh tsó‐shuê; tsaû tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè on‐yīng.
10 Ts‘ūng Ĭ‐lām heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Hūng‐hoí‐pin.
11 Ts‘ūng Hūng‐hoí‐pin heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Sùn‐kè kw‘òng‐yĕ.
12 Ts‘ūng Sùn‐kè kw‘òng‐yĕ heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî T‘uetₒ‐ka.
13 Ts‘ūng T‘uetₒ‐ka heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî À‐lūk.
14 Ts‘ūng À‐lūk heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Leĭ‐fei‐t‘īng, tsik‐haî paakₒ‐sìng mŏ shuí yám kóh‐ch‘uè.
15 Ts‘ūng Leĭ‐fei‐t‘īng heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Sai‐naaĭ kw‘òng‐yĕ.
16 Ts‘ūng Sai‐naaĭ kw‘òng‐yĕ heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Hat‐līk‐hâ‐taaî‐faatₒ.
17 Ts‘ūng Hat‐līk‐hâ‐taaî‐faatₒ heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Hap‐sai‐lūk.
18 Ts‘ūng Hap‐sai‐lūk heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Lāk‐mă.
19 Ts‘ūng Lāk‐mă heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Lām‐moōn‐p‘à‐līt.
20 Ts‘ūng Lām‐moōn‐p‘à‐līt heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Laāp‐nā.
21 Ts‘ūng Laāp‐nā heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Lāk‐saatₒ.
22 Ts‘ūng Lāk‐saatₒ heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Kei‐pò‐la‐taaî.
23 Ts‘ūng Kei‐pò‐la‐taaî heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Sha‐paakₒ‐shaan.
24 Ts‘ūng Sha‐paakₒ‐shaan heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Hap‐la‐taaî.
25 Ts‘ūng Hap‐la‐taaî heí‐ch‘īng‐huĭ, on‐yīng tsoî Mă‐hei‐lūk.
26 Ts‘ūng Mă‐hei‐lūk heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Taaî‐hap.
27 Ts‘ūng Taaî‐hap heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Taaî‐la.
28 Ts‘ūng Taaî‐la heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Māt‐ka.
29 Ts‘ūng Māt‐ka heí‐ch‘īng huì, on‐yīng tsoî Hap‐moh‐nā.
30 Ts‘ūng Hap‐moh‐nā heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Moh‐sai‐lūk.
31 Ts‘ūng Moh‐sai‐lūk heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Peì‐neī‐ngă‐kon.
32 Ts‘ūng Peì‐neī‐ngă‐kon heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Hotₒ‐hap‐k‘āp‐kaapₒ.
33 Ts‘ūng Hotₒ‐hap‐k‘āp‐kaapₒ heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Yeukₒ‐pa‐taaî.
34 Ts‘ūng Yeukₒ‐pa‐taaî heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Ĭ‐p‘òh‐nā.
35 Ts‘ūng Ĭ‐p‘òh‐nā heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Ĭ‐ts‘ūn‐ka‐pīt.
36 Ts‘ūng Ĭ‐ts‘ūn‐ka‐pīt heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Sùn‐kè kw‘òng‐yĕ, tsik‐haî Ka‐t‘itₒ.
37 Ts‘ūng Ka‐t‘itₒ heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Hōh‐ĭ‐shaan, kân Ĭ‐tung‐kè pin‐kaaì.
38 Tsaì‐sz À‐lūn tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò, tsaû sheŭng Hōh‐ĭ‐shaan, sź‐tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè, kóh‐yāt haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp‐teî chi‐haû sz̀‐shāp nīn, nğ uēt ch‘oh‐yat yāt.
39 À‐lūn sź tsoî Hōh‐ĭ‐shaan kóh‐shī, yaŭ yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐saam suì.
40 Chuê tsoî Ka‐naām‐teî naām‐pîn‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān À‐laāp wōng, t‘eng‐mān Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān laī‐tò.
41 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû ts‘ūng Hōh‐ĭ‐shaan heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Saatₒ‐moh‐nā.
42 Ts‘ūng Saatₒ‐moh‐nā heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Pô‐nuên.
43 Ts‘ūng Pô‐nuên heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Oh‐pōk.
44 Ts‘ūng Oh‐pōk heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Ĭ‐ngă‐à‐pa‐lām, kân Moh‐aapₒ kíng‐kaaì.
45 Ts‘ūng Ĭ‐ngă‐à‐pa‐lām heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Taí‐poón‐ka‐tak.
46 Ts‘ūng Taí‐poón‐ka‐tak heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî À‐moōn‐tāk‐la‐t‘aaì‐yam.
47 Ts‘ūng À‐moōn‐tāk‐la‐t‘aaì‐yam heí‐ch‘īng‐huì, on‐yīng tsoî Neī‐p‘òh ts‘īn‐pîn‐kè À‐pa‐lām‐shaan.
48 Ts‘ūng À‐pa‐lām‐shaan heí‐ch‘īng huì, on‐yīng tsoî Moh‐aapₒ kw‘òng‐yĕ, tsoî Yē‐leî‐koh tuì‐mîn Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐pin.
49 K‘uĭ‐teî on‐yīng tsoî Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐pin, tsik‐haî Moh‐aapₒ kw‘òng‐yĕ, tsẑ Paakₒ‐yē‐sai‐moōt chì‐tò À‐paakₒ‐shî‐t‘īng.
50 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî Moh‐aapₒ kw‘òng‐yĕ, tsoî Yē‐leî‐koh tuì‐mîn Yeukₒ‐taân hōh‐pin, tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
51 Neĭ iù tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kóng wâ, Neĭ‐teî kwòh Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh yāp Ka‐naām kíng‐teî kóh‐shī;
52 Pit iù tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn kón‐chūk kóh‐ch‘uè‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, waí‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐tiu‐haak‐kè shēk‐pei, yūng‐chuè‐kè ngaŭ‐tseûng, ch‘aakₒ‐laân k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè ko‐kong‐tsaì‐t‘aān:
53 Neĭ‐teî iù chìm‐tuēt kòh‐ti‐teî, táng tsẑ‐keí kui‐chuê: yan‐waî ngŏh tseung ni‐taatₒ‐teî ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ‐teî.
54 Iù chap‐ch‘aū laī fan kóh‐ti‐teî, tsô neĭ‐teî kokₒ‐tsūk‐kè ch‘aán‐īp: yān‐toh‐kè tsaû peí toh, yān‐shiú‐kè tsaû peí shiú: kokₒ‐yān tseung ch‘aū shóh‐tîng‐kè laī fan teî; yaû iù chiù neĭ tsó‐tsung‐kè chi‐p‘aaì laī shīng‐shaû.
55 Neĭ‐teî yeūk m̄‐tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn kón‐chūk kóh‐ti paakₒ‐sìng; tsaû neĭ shóh‐laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè yān, pit tsô neĭ ngaăn‐chung‐kè ts‘z̀, lāk‐hâ‐kè king‐kik, tsoî neĭ‐teî shóh‐chuê‐kè teî‐fong kaaú‐iŭ neĭ.
56 Ī‐ch‘é ngŏh p‘īng‐sò seúng hon‐toî k‘uĭ‐teî, tsaû iù kóm‐yeûng hon‐toî neĭ‐teî.
