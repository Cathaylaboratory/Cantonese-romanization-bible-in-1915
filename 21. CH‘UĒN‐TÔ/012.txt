1 TONG shiù‐nīn‐kè yāt‐tsź, iù keì‐nîm neĭ Tsô‐Fà‐Kè‐Chué, tsik‐haî waân‐naân‐kè yāt‐tsź meî‐tò‐chi‐sin, yaû tak neĭ wâ, Ngŏh tsoî ts‘ź‐shī mŏ‐kìn‐lōk‐ì‐kè sẑ chi‐nīn‐meî‐tò‐chi‐sin;
2 Yaû t‘aaì‐yeūng kûng kwong, uēt kûng sing‐suk, meî‐pìn‐hak‐òm‐chi‐sin, kim‐maaī lōk‐uĕ‐chi‐haû, wān meî‐faan‐laī‐chi‐sin:
3 Tong‐shī hon‐shaú‐uk‐kè chàn‐tûng, yaŭ nāng‐līk‐kè wat‐shan, mōh‐yĕ‐kè yan‐shiú tsaû‐chí‐sik, yaū ch‘eung‐haú mông‐kìn pìn hak‐òm.
4 T‘ung‐kaai‐kè moōn to shaan‐maaī, tong‐shī mōh‐yĕ‐chi‐sheng hó‐meī, yaŭ tseukₒ‐tsaí‐kè sheng, ooĭ‐lîng‐yān‐heí‐shan, ch‘eùng‐koh‐chùng‐nuĭ, ooĭ‐shīng‐waī pei‐meī;
5 Ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ yan‐waî shing‐ko‐kè kuî‐p‘à, lô‐kaan yaŭ king‐fong‐kè sẑ, hâng‐yān‐shuê ooĭ‐hoi‐fa, ts‘ó‐maăng shīng‐waī chûng‐yâm, sam‐kè uên‐seúng yīk shui‐paaî: yan‐waî yān huì‐kwai tò wĭng‐uĕn‐kè chuê‐shóh, ī‐ch‘é tiù‐song‐kè yān tsoî‐kaai‐wŏng‐loī:
6 Tong ngān‐lîn meî‐kaaí‐lat, waāk kam‐chung meî‐chíng‐laân, waāk shuí‐p‘īng tsoî ts‘uēn meî‐līt, waāk ch‘e‐lūn tsoî tséng‐ch‘uè meî‐laân‐chi‐sin.
7 Naī‐ch‘ān kwai‐faan teî‐t‘ó: chiù‐uēn yat‐yeûng, līng‐wān kwai‐faan ts‘z̀‐peí‐k‘uĭ kóh‐waî‐Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐sin, iù keì‐nîm k‘uĭ.
8 Ch‘uēn‐tô‐ché wâ, Hung‐hui‐chi‐hung‐hui; to‐t‘ung‐haî hung‐hui‐kè.
9 ¶ Fòng‐ch‘é yan ch‘uēn‐tô‐ché yaŭ chì‐waî, k‘uĭ yīng‐īn kaaù‐fàn chùng‐yān tak‐kìn‐shik; ī‐ch‘é ts‘aam‐seúng ch‘ā‐ch‘aatₒ, yaû i ts‘āng‐ts‘z̀ ch‘ān‐ch‘itₒ toh‐tuen cham‐īn.
10 Ch‘uēn‐tô‐ché seúng‐wán‐tak hōp‐ì‐kè īn‐uĕ: ī‐ch‘é i‐chìng‐chīk shóh‐sé‐lōk‐kè, tsik‐haî chan‐shāt‐kè īn‐uĕ.
11 ¶ Chì‐waî‐yān‐kè īn‐uĕ, hó‐ts‘z̆ tsim‐leî‐kè ts‘eung, yaû ts‘z̆ chùng‐ooî‐lŏ shóh‐tîng‐shāt‐kè teng, yaū‐ue yat‐kòh mūk‐ché shóh‐peí‐kè.
12 Fòng‐ch‘é ngŏh‐kè tsaí à, neĭ iù shaû huèn‐kaàn: tsokₒ‐shue‐toh haî mō‐chung‐kīk‐kè sẑ; ī‐ch‘é tūk‐shue‐toh haî lîng yūk‐t‘aí kaù‐kooî‐kè sẑ à.
13 ¶ Yat‐k‘oì sẑ‐ts‘īng‐kè taaî‐ì haî kóm, ĭ‐king t‘eng‐kwòh‐saaì shóh‐ĭ‐lûn‐kè: Iù kìng‐waì Sheûng‐Taì, yaû shaú‐chuê k‘uĭ kaaì‐mîng: yan ni‐ti haî yān ts‘uēn‐peî‐kè poón‐fân.
14 Yan‐waî Sheûng‐Taì pit lîng kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè tsokₒ‐waī, t‘ūng‐maaī faān‐haî yán‐ts‘ōng‐kè sẑ‐ts‘īng, waāk shîn waāk okₒ, to shaû shám‐p‘oòn‐kè lokₒ.
