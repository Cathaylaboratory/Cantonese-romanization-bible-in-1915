1 TONG shāp‐î‐nīn, shāp‐î‐uēt, ch‘oh‐yat‐yāt, yaŭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue‐ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù waî Oi‐k‘āp wōng Faatₒ‐lŏ kuí‐oi‐koh, yaû tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Neĭ p‘īng‐sò peî‐ch‘ing‐waī‐hó‐ts‘z̆ līt‐pong‐kè siú‐sz, neĭ sheûng‐ch‘é shīng‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ taaî‐hoí‐kè‐lūng: tsoî neĭ kong‐hōh‐chi‐chung faatₒ‐līk, peí‐neĭ keukₒ kaaú‐iŭ chùng‐shuí, yaû chíng‐chūk k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè kong‐hōh.
3 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh pit‐yûng toh‐mān‐kè tuî‐nğ saatₒ‐mŏng tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐sheûng; k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tsoî ngŏh‐mŏng‐chung, ts‘uí‐heí‐neĭ à.
4 Ngŏh pit‐laū‐lōk‐neĭ tsoî hŏn‐teî, yaû chukₒ‐neĭ ch‘ut‐huì fong‐t‘īn‐ch‘uè, yaû lîng‐t‘in‐hung chùng‐fei‐niŭ, ts‘ai‐tsoî neĭ‐sheûng, yaû ts‘uēn‐teî tsûn‐teî‐kè tsaú‐shaù, haí neĭ‐ch‘uè tak‐paaú‐uè.
5 Ngŏh pit‐fòng neĭ‐kè yūk tsoî‐shaan‐sheûng, tseung‐neĭ‐ko‐taaî‐kè shan ch‘ung‐moŏn‐shaan‐kuk.
6 Ngŏh to‐iù‐tseung neĭ‐kè huetₒ lām‐laaî‐neĭ, tsoî‐k‘eī‐chung ooĭ‐yaū‐chi‐teî chì‐tò‐shaan‐ch‘uè; yaû‐yûng‐neĭ ch‘ung‐moŏn shuí‐lô.
7 Ngŏh mīt‐tsuēt‐neĭ kòh‐shī, tsaû ooĭ‐che‐k‘oì kòh‐t‘in, yaû lîng t‘in‐sheûng‐kè sing‐suk pìn‐waī hak; ngŏh ooĭ‐k‘aaĭ‐wān che‐paì t‘aaì‐yeūng, uēt to‐m̄‐faatₒ‐ch‘ut k‘uĭ‐kè kwong.
8 Ngŏh to‐tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐sheûng, lîng t‘in‐kè chùng‐kwong pìn‐waī hak, yaû lîng hak‐òm kòng‐lām neĭ‐teî‐ch‘uè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
9 Ngŏh lîng‐neĭ‐kè paaî‐mōng lām‐tò līt‐mān‐chi‐chung, yaû lām‐tò neĭ‐shóh‐meî‐shik‐chi‐kwokₒ‐teî kòh‐shī, tsaû kaaú‐iŭ toh‐mān‐chi‐sam.
10 Ngŏh iū‐paaí ngŏh‐to‐kìm tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn kòh‐shī, shāt‐haî ooĭ‐lîng‐toh‐mān yan‐neĭ‐king‐k‘eī, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè līt‐wōng, yan‐neĭ‐king‐fong‐tak‐hó‐kaau‐kwaan; tong neĭ k‘ing‐paaî‐chi‐yāt, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kokₒ‐yān, waî‐tsẑ‐keí‐kè shang‐mîng, shī‐hak ooĭ‐chìn‐lūt.
11 Yan‐waî Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè to‐kìm, pit‐kòng‐lām‐ue neĭ‐ch‘uè.
12 Ngŏh pit‐yûng taaî‐nāng‐līk‐ché‐chi‐to‐kìm, lîng‐neĭ‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng k‘ing‐paaî, k‘uĭ‐teî t‘ung‐to‐haî līt‐pong‐chi‐chung hóh‐kuî‐p‘à‐kè; k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐paaî‐waaî Oi‐k‘āp‐kè kiu‐ngô, k‘eī‐chung kw‘ān‐chùng to‐peî‐mīt‐mōng.
13 Ngŏh to ts‘ūng toh‐shuí‐chi‐p‘ōng, tseung k‘eī‐chung‐tsaú‐shaù mīt‐tsuēt‐saaì; yān‐kè keukₒ‐pô m̄‐tsoì‐kaaú‐iŭ k‘uĭ‐teî, tsaú‐shaù‐kè keukₒ‐t‘aī, to mŏ‐kaaú‐iŭ k‘uĭ‐teî.
14 Ue‐shî ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè chùng‐shuí pìn‐waī ts‘ing, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè kong‐hōh, hó‐ts‘z̆ yaū kóm‐laū‐lōk, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
15 Ngŏh lîng Oi‐k‘āp‐teî pìn‐waī waī‐heì‐fong‐faì‐kè, shiú‐k‘uetₒ‐saaì p‘īng‐yāt shóh‐ch‘ung‐moŏn‐kè kòh‐shī, yaû kung‐tá‐saaì k‘eī‐chung shóh‐kui‐chuê‐kè yān kòh‐shī, k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
16 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐yûng ts‘ź‐oi‐koh laī‐oi‐huk: līt‐pong‐kè chùng‐nuĭ, pit‐tseung‐ts‘ź laī‐oi‐huk: pit‐tseung‐ts‘ź‐waî Oi‐k‘āp yaû waî‐k‘uĭ‐kw‘ān‐chùng‐laī‐oi‐huk, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ à.
17 ¶ Yaû tong shāp‐î‐nīn ching‐uēt shāp‐nğ‐yāt, yaŭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
18 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐waî Oi‐k‘āp kw‘ān‐chùng huk‐yap, yaû tseung k‘uĭ‐teî tsik‐haî ts‘ź‐teî k‘āp yaŭ‐mēng‐chi‐līt‐pong‐kè chùng‐nuĭ, fòng‐lōk‐teî‐chung sham‐taí‐ch‘uè, t‘ūng‐maaī kòh‐ti‐lōk‐sham‐haang‐kè yān.
19 Neĭ meĭ‐laî shìng‐kwòh mat‐shuī ni? iù‐lōk‐huì t‘ūng‐maaī meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ‐kè yān fàn‐tó‐ch‘uè.
20 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tsoî‐peî‐to‐kìm‐shaatₒ‐ché‐chi‐chung pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè: k‘uĭ peî‐kaau‐kwòh to‐kìm: iù‐laai k‘uĭ t‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng huì‐lokₒ.
21 K‘eūng‐chòng‐kè, haí yaŭ‐nāng‐ché‐chi‐chung, yaū yam‐foó‐chi‐kaan, t‘ūng‐pong‐chôh k‘uĭ‐kòh‐ti‐yān, ooĭ‐tuì‐k‘uĭ‐kóng: k‘uĭ‐teî ĭ‐king lōk‐huì yaû‐fàn‐tsîng, tsik‐haî meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ‐kè, peî‐to‐kìm‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè.
22 À‐shūt t‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ kw‘ān‐chùng to‐haí‐ch‘uè: k‘uĭ fān‐mô tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐sz̀‐waī: t‘ung‐to‐haî peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, peî‐to‐kìm pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè.
23 K‘uĭ‐teî‐kè fān‐mô, on‐tsoî‐sham‐haang kīk‐taí‐ch‘uè, k‘uĭ‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng, tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐fān‐mô‐sz̀‐waī: t‘ung‐to‐haî peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, peî‐to‐kìm‐pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè, tsik‐haî p‘īng‐yāt tsoî‐shang‐yān‐chi‐teî, lîng‐yān‐king‐fong‐kè.
24 Yaŭ À‐laān kûng k‘uĭ shóh‐yaŭ‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng, tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐fān‐mô‐sz̀‐waī, t‘ung‐to‐haî peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, peî‐to‐kìm pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè, meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ, lōk‐huì teî‐chung sham‐taí‐ch‘uè, p‘īng‐yāt tsoî‐shang‐yān‐chi‐teî, lîng‐yān king‐fong; tsaû t‘ūng‐maaī kòh‐ti lōk‐sham‐haang‐kè yān, taam‐tong‐k‘uĭ‐kè sau‐kw‘aì.
25 Yān‐teî iù‐tîng k‘uĭ yat‐cheung‐ch‘ōng, tsoî peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐yān‐chi‐chung, t‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ shóh‐yaŭ‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng: k‘uĭ‐fān‐mô‐tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐sz̀‐waī: t‘ung‐to‐haî meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ, yaû peî‐to‐kìm‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè: yan k‘uĭ tsoî‐shang‐yān‐chi‐teî, lîng‐yān‐king‐fong, yaû t‘ūng‐maaī kòh‐ti lōk‐sham‐haang‐ch‘uè‐kè yān, taam‐tong k‘uĭ‐kè sau‐kw‘aì: k‘uĭ tsaû peî‐on‐tsoî peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐yān‐chi‐chung.
26 Kòh‐ch‘uè to‐yaŭ Maĭ‐ch‘itₒ T‘ó‐paatₒ kûng k‘uĭ‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng: t‘ung‐to‐haî meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ, yaû‐peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè: yan k‘uĭ tsoî‐shang‐yān‐chi‐teî, lîng‐yān‐king‐fong.
27 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐m̄‐tak‐kûng kòh‐ti yaŭ‐nāng‐kè, meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè‐kè yān t‘ūng‐fàn, tsik‐haî taaì‐maaī kwan‐heì lōk‐yam‐kaan: yaû tseung k‘uĭ pá‐kìm fòng‐tsoî t‘aū‐hâ‐kè yān, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsuî‐hin, to tsoî k‘uĭ hoī‐kwat‐chi‐sheûng, haî yan k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî‐shang‐yān‐chi‐teî, lîng yaŭ‐nāng‐ché king‐fong.
28 Waī‐haî neĭ pit‐tsoî‐meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ‐kè peî‐chitₒ‐t‘uĕn, yaû kûng kòh‐ti peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè t‘ūng‐fàn.
29 Yīk yaŭ Ĭ‐tung, k‘uĭ līt‐wōng t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, sui‐īn yaŭ‐nāng‐līk‐kè, to t‘ūng‐maaī‐kòh‐ti‐peî‐to‐kìm shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐kûng meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ‐kè, k‘āp lōk‐sham‐haang‐kè yān t‘ūng‐fàn.
30 Kòh‐ch‘uè to‐yaŭ pak‐fong‐kè chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, t‘ūng‐maaī taaî‐chùng Sai‐tûn‐yān, tsik‐haî kûng peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè lōk‐huì; sui‐īn yaŭ‐nāng‐līk lîng‐yān king‐fong, k‘uĭ‐teî to‐kìn‐sau‐kw‘aì; k‘uĭ‐teî meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ, kûng‐kòh‐ti peî‐to‐kìm‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè t‘ūng‐fàn, yaû‐t‘ūng‐maaī kòh‐ti lōk‐sham‐haang‐ch‘uè‐kè, taam‐tong k‘uĭ‐kè sau‐kw‘aì.
31 Faatₒ‐lŏ pit‐t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ‐teî, yaû waî‐k‘uĭ shóh‐yaŭ‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng shaû‐on‐waì, tsik‐haî Faatₒ‐lŏ k‘āp k‘uĭ ts‘uēn‐kwan, peî‐to‐kìm shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
32 Yan ngŏh ts‘āng fòng k‘ui king‐hoī‐chi‐sẑ tsoî shang‐yān‐chi‐teî: Faatₒ‐lŏ k‘āp k‘uĭ ts‘uēn‐kwan, pit‐peî‐fòng‐lōk meî‐shaû‐kotₒ‐laĭ‐yān‐chi‐chung, t‘ūng‐maaī‐kòh‐ti peî‐to‐kìm‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ à.
