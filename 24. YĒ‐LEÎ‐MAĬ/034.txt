1 TONG Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ kûng k‘uĭ ts‘uēn‐kwan, uĕ‐k‘āp teî‐sheûng shóh‐shūk‐k‘uĭ‐k‘uēn‐hâ‐chi‐chùng‐kwokₒ, t‘ūng‐maaī līt‐mān, yat‐ts‘aī tá‐cheùng kung‐kik Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, yaû kung‐kik shūk‐k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐yap kòh‐shī, yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, wâ,
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì kóm‐wâ; Iù huì kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka kóng, yaû tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐tseung‐ni‐kòh‐shēng, kaau‐lōk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐chi‐shaú, k‘uĭ pit‐k‘aaĭ‐fóh laī‐shiu‐mīt‐kè:
3 Neĭ to pat‐nāng tóh‐peî t‘uetₒ‐leī k‘uĭ‐shaú, waī‐haî peî‐chukₒ‐tó kaau‐lōk k‘uĭ‐kè shaú; neĭ ts‘an‐ngaăn ooĭ‐t‘aí‐kìn Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè ngaăn, k‘uĭ to‐ooĭ‐haú‐tuì‐haú kûng‐neĭ‐kóng, neĭ tsaû pit‐huì‐tò Pa‐peí‐lūn.
4 Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka à, neĭ chûng‐iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lûn‐k‘āp neĭ, kóm wâ,
5 Neĭ pit‐m̄‐peî kìm laī‐sź‐mōng: neĭ pit‐on‐īn kwòh‐shaì: ka‐yān pit‐waî‐neĭ shiu‐heung, hó‐ts‘z̆ waî neĭ‐chi‐ts‘īn‐kè līt‐wōng yat‐yeûng; k‘uĭ‐teî yīk waî neĭ oi‐huk, wâ, Oi‐tsoi ngŏh‐chué à! yan‐waî ngŏh ts‘āng kóng ts‘ź‐īn, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
6 Ue‐shî sin‐chi Yē‐leî‐maĭ, tseung ni‐ti‐shuetₒ‐wâ, kóng‐saaì Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka t‘eng,
7 Haî Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè kwan‐ping, tá‐cheùng kung‐kik Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, yaû kung‐kik shūk Yaū‐t‘aaì shóh‐uē‐shîng‐kè shēng‐yap, yaû kung‐kik La‐kitₒ k‘āp À‐sai‐ka kòh‐shī: yan‐waî shūk‐Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè shēng‐yap, tūk‐haî ni‐ti‐laū‐ts‘uēn, tsô pó‐cheùng‐kè shēng‐yap che.
8 ¶ Yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐chi‐īn‐uĕ, tsoî Sai‐taí‐ka wōng kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng chùng‐yān laāp‐yeukₒ‐chi‐haû, shing‐mīng k‘uĭ‐teî hóh‐ĭ tak‐shik‐fòng kòh‐shī;
9 Laāp‐tîng iù‐kokₒ‐yān shik‐fòng k‘uĭ pūk‐yān, yaû shik‐fòng k‘uĭ p‘eĭ‐nuĭ, tsô Hei‐paakₒ‐loī‐kè naām waāk nuĭ; táng mŏ‐yān shaí k‘uĭ‐teî laī‐fūk‐sẑ tsẑ‐keí, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ hing‐taî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān kóm.
10 Taaî‐chùng kwan‐cheúng kûng chùng‐tsź‐mān, t‘ūng‐maaī‐laāp ts‘ź‐yeukₒ, laāp‐tîng kokₒ‐yān iù‐shik‐fòng k‘uĭ pūk‐yān, yaû shik‐fòng k‘uĭ p‘eĭ‐nuĭ, táng mŏ‐yān shaí k‘uĭ tsoì fūk‐sẑ tsẑ‐keí, yaû shûn‐ts‘ūng ts‘ź‐yeukₒ, shik‐fòng k‘uĭ‐teî.
11 Waī‐haî haû‐loī, k‘uĭ‐teî faán‐chuèn yaû lîng k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐shik‐fòng‐kè pūk‐p‘eĭ faan‐laī, yaû lîng k‘uĭ‐teî tsoì fūk‐ts‘ūng tsô‐pūk‐yān kûng p‘eĭ‐nuĭ.
12 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ, wâ,
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ; Ngŏh taaì neĭ līt‐tsó ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp teî, ch‘ut nō‐pūk‐chi‐ka kòh‐yāt, kûng k‘uĭ‐teî laāp‐yeukₒ, wâ,
14 Uē‐kwóh neĭ hing‐taî tsô Hei‐paakₒ‐loī‐yān‐kè, peî‐maaî‐kwòh‐neĭ; yaû fūk‐sẑ‐neĭ lūk nīn, tsaû kwòh‐hiu ts‘at‐nīn‐chi‐haû, iù shik‐fòng k‘uĭ, yaû lîng k‘uĭ tak‐tsẑ‐chué leī‐hoi neĭ: waī‐haî neĭ‐teî līt‐tsó, m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng ngŏh, m̄‐heùng ngŏh chak‐ĭ.
15 Neĭ‐teî tsoî sin, to faán‐chuèn tsoî ngŏh ngaăn‐ts‘īn hāng chìng‐chīk‐kè, tsik kokₒ‐yān shing‐mīng k‘uĭ lūn‐shè hóh‐ĭ tak‐shik‐fòng; yaû tong ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn, haí òn‐ngŏh mēng laī‐ch‘ing‐foo‐kè tîn‐uk laāp‐yeukₒ:
16 Waī‐haî neĭ‐teî tsoì faán‐chuèn yaû waí‐p‘òng ngŏh‐kè mēng, kokₒ‐yān tseung k‘uĭ shóh‐ĭ‐king shik‐fòng yâm‐ì huì‐hiu‐kè pūk‐yān kûng p‘eĭ‐nuĭ, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî faan‐laī, yaû lîng k‘uĭ‐teî tsoì fūk‐ts‘ūng neĭ tsô pūk‐p‘eĭ.
17 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Neĭ‐teî m̄‐ts‘āng t‘eng‐ts‘ūng ngŏh, iù kokₒ‐yān shing‐mīng k‘uĭ hing‐taî, waāk lūn‐shè, hóh‐ĭ tak‐shik‐fòng: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh heùng neĭ shing‐mīng iù‐fòng‐ch‘ut to‐kìm, wan‐yīk, kûng kei‐fong; yaû lîng neĭ‐teî tsoî teî‐sheûng maân‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung, p‘ùng‐loī p‘ùng‐huì.
18 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng faān‐haî pooì‐yīk‐ngŏh‐yeukₒ‐kè yān, tsik‐haî m̄‐shīng‐tsaû tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn shóh‐laāp‐chi‐yeukₒ‐kè īn‐uĕ, haî k‘uĭ‐teî kotₒ‐hoi ngaū‐tsaí, tsoî leŭng‐pin chung‐kaan hāng‐kwòh‐kòh‐shī shóh‐laāp‐kè,
19 Tsik‐haî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐chi‐kwan cheúng, kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐chi‐kwan‐cheúng, kûng waân‐koon, kûng tsaì‐sz, uĕ‐k‘āp kíng‐noî chùng‐tsź‐mān, hāng‐kwòh‐ngaū‐tsaí leŭng‐pin chung‐kaan‐kè;
20 Ngŏh tseung‐loī pit‐k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ‐teî kaau‐lōk ch‘aū‐tīk‐chi‐shaú, yaû kaau‐lōk chaaú‐ts‘ām k‘uĭ shang‐mîng‐ti‐yān‐chi‐shaú: k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè sź‐shi, ooĭ‐waī t‘in‐hung‐kè fei‐niŭ k‘āp teî‐sheûng‐kè tsaú‐shaù tsô‐shīk‐māt.
21 Ī‐ch‘é ngŏh ooĭ‐tseung Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka kûng k‘uĭ‐kè kwan‐cheŭng kaau‐lōk ch‘aū‐tīk‐chi‐shaú, yaû kaau‐lōk chaaú‐ts‘ām k‘uĭ shang‐mîng‐ti‐yān‐chi‐shaú, yaû kaau‐lōk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng kwan‐ping‐chi‐shaú, tsik‐haî ĭ‐king leī‐hoi faan‐sheŭng‐huì‐kè.
22 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐ch‘ut‐mîng‐lîng, yaû lîng k‘uĭ‐teî faan‐laī tò‐ni‐kòh‐shēng; k‘uĭ‐teî ooĭ‐tá‐cheùng kung‐kik ts‘uí‐hiu ni‐kòh‐shēng, yaû peí‐fóh‐laī shiu‐mīt: ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐lîng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐chi‐chùng‐yap tsô fong‐faì‐kè, tsúng‐mŏ‐yān kui‐chuê tsoî‐noî à.
