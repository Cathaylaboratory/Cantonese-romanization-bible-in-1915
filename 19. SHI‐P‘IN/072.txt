1 SHEÛNG‐TAÌ à, k‘aū tseung neĭ‐chi‐p‘oòn‐uĕ, ts‘z̀‐kwòh kwan‐wōng, tseung neĭ‐chi‐kung‐î, ts‘z̀‐kwòh wōng‐tsź.
2 K‘uĭ tsaû tseung kung‐î shám mân neĭ‐chi‐paakₒ‐sìng, tseung chìng‐chīk p‘oòn‐tuèn k‘ūng‐foó‐chi yān.
3 Taaî‐shaan, siú‐shaan, tsaû pit chùng‐kung‐î, ch‘aán wōh‐p‘īng ue mān.
4 Wōng pit waî shaû‐foó‐chi‐yān shan‐uen, ch‘íng‐kaù p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐chi‐yān, paaî‐waaî k‘eūng‐okₒ‐chi‐yān.
5 Yān kìng‐waì k‘uĭ, yāt‐kaú uēt‐ch‘eūng, chì‐tò maân‐shaì.
6 K‘uĭ‐chi‐kòng‐lām, hó‐ts‘z̆ kom‐uĕ kòng ĭ kotₒ‐chi‐ts‘ó‐teî: hó‐ts‘z̆ taaî‐uĕ tsz‐yûn teî‐t‘ó.
7 Tong kòh‐chân‐shī, shîn‐yān pit‐tîng hing faatₒ; toh‐tak p‘īng‐on, hó‐ts‘z̆ uēt kòm ch‘eūng‐uĕn.
8 Ts‘ūng ni‐pîn hoí, chì‐tò kóh‐pîn hoí, ts‘ūng taaî‐hōh chì‐tò teî‐kīk, to kwai k‘uĭ koón‐leĭ.
9 Chuê‐tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ‐ché, pit kwaî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn; k‘uĭ‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, pit pūk‐tsoî ch‘ān‐sheûng.
10 Taaî‐shat kûng hoí‐tó‐chi‐līt‐wōng, k‘ui‐sùng laĭ‐māt: Shî‐pa kûng Sai‐pa‐chi‐kwan‐wōng, pit laī tsùn‐kùng.
11 Līt‐wōng to iù paaì‐kwaî k‘uĭ: kokₒ‐kwokₒ to iù fūk‐sẑ k‘uĭ.
12 P‘ān‐k‘ūng‐chi‐yān foo‐kiù, k‘uĭ pit ch‘íng‐kaù; foó‐ch‘óh‐chi‐yān, mō‐shóh‐í‐k‘aaù, k‘uĭ yīk pit pó‐yaû.
13 K‘uĭ pit līn‐măn shui‐yeūk p‘ān‐foó‐chi‐yān, ch‘íng‐kaù k‘ūng‐kw‘àn‐yān‐chi‐shang‐mîng.
14 Kaù k‘uĭ shang‐mîng, t‘uetₒ‐ch‘ut kaan‐chà k‘eūng‐okₒ: ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐huetₒ, tsoî k‘uĭ ngaăn‐ts‘īn, ĭ‐waī pó‐pooì.
15 K‘uĭ‐teî pit tak‐shang, yaû tseung Shî‐pa‐chi‐kam sùng‐kwòh k‘uĭ: shī‐sheūng waî k‘uĭ k‘eī‐t‘ó; ī‐ch‘é shīng‐yāt tsaàn‐meĭ k‘uĭ.
16 Shaan‐sheûng nğ‐kuk fung‐shūk, kwóh‐tsź iū‐yaî; hó‐ts‘z̆ Leî‐pa‐nuên‐chi‐shuê‐lām: shēng‐chung yān‐mān hing‐wông, hó‐ts‘z̆ teî‐sheûng‐chi‐ts‘ó.
17 K‘uĭ‐chi‐mēng, shî‐pit wĭng‐uĕn shīng‐ts‘uēn: hó‐ts‘z̆ yāt kòm‐ch‘eūng‐kaú: yān pit yan k‘uĭ tak‐fuk: maân‐kwokₒ yīk ch‘ing k‘uĭ waī yaŭ‐fuk.
18 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Sheûng‐Taì, tsik‐haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt Sheûng‐Taì, tūk hāng î‐tsik, uên k‘uĭ shaû yān tsûng‐chuk.
19 Uên k‘uĭ‐chi‐wīng‐mēng, wĭng‐uĕn shaû yān tsûng‐chuk: uên k‘uĭ‐chi‐wīng‐kwong, ch‘ung‐moŏn t‘in‐teî; Shīng‐sam‐shóh‐uên. Yē‐sai‐chi‐tsź Taaî‐waî, k‘eī‐t‘ó‐chi‐shi uēn lokₒ.
