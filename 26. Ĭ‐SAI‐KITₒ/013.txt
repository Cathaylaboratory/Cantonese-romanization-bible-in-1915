1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ‐KÈ īn‐uĕ tsoì kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐kóng‐uê‐īn kung‐kik Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chung kóng‐uê‐īn‐kè sin‐chi, yaû tuì kòh‐ti‐yaū‐tsẑ‐keí‐sam‐chung‐faatₒ‐uê‐īn‐kè, wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ;
3 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Uē‐ch‘ún‐kè sin‐chi, i‐ts‘ūng tsẑ‐keí‐kè sam‐ì, yaû mŏ‐kìn‐kwòh‐mat‐yĕ‐kè, shaû wôh la!
4 Ĭ‐shik‐līt à, neĭ‐kè sin‐chi, ts‘āng‐shīng‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ fong‐faì‐teî‐kè oō‐leī kóm.
5 Neĭ‐teî m̄‐ts‘āng sheŭng‐huì p‘òh‐laân‐chi‐ch‘uè, yaû m̄‐ts‘āng chuk‐heí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè waī‐leī, táng‐hóh‐ĭ tong Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐yāt lām‐chân‐kòh‐shī k‘eĭ‐laāp.
6 Kòh‐táng‐yān t‘aí‐kìn hung‐hui‐chi‐sẑ, k‘āp ká‐ngaî‐kè chim‐puk, tsik‐haî wâ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā sheûng‐ch‘é mŏ‐tá‐faatₒ‐k‘uĭ‐teî: yaû lîng yān‐teî mông‐kòh‐ti‐īn‐uĕ ooĭ‐tak‐kin‐laāp.
7 Neĭ‐teî kíng‐īn wâ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ; ngŏh‐shāt‐tsoî m̄‐ts‘āng‐kóng‐kè, neĭ‐teî heí‐m̄‐ts‘āng kìn‐hung‐hui‐kè î‐tseûng me? Heí‐m̄‐ts‘āng kóng‐ká‐ngaî‐kè chim‐puk me?
8 ¶ Shóh‐ĭ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐wâ; Yan neĭ ts‘āng kóng‐hung‐hui‐kè sẑ, yaû t‘aí‐kìn‐ká‐ngaî‐kè î‐tseûng, koò‐ts‘ź t‘aí‐a, ngŏh tsaû kung‐kik neĭ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ,
9 Ngŏh shaú pit‐kung‐kik kòh‐ti‐t‘aí‐kìn hung‐hui‐kè, yaû chim‐puk ká‐ngaî‐kè sin‐chi: k‘uĭ‐teî‐m̄‐tak‐shūk‐ngŏh‐tsź‐mān peì‐māt‐chi‐ĭ, yīk m̄‐peí‐chuè‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka‐kè ch‘aakₒ, yīk‐m̄‐yāp‐tak Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chi‐teî; neĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
10 Haî‐yan‐k‘uĭ‐teî yaŭ‐waāk‐ngŏh tsź‐mān, wâ, P‘īng‐on; k‘eī‐shāt mŏ‐p‘īng‐on; waāk yān‐heí‐fuk‐ts‘eūng kòh‐shī, k‘uĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ‐shang‐fooi laī‐tông‐kwòh:
11 Iù tuì kòh‐ti k‘aaĭ‐shang‐fooi laī‐tông‐kwòh‐kè wâ: pit‐ooĭ‐titₒ‐lōk, yaŭ yat‐chân‐uĕ‐tsàm‐kwòh; taaî‐pōk‐shēk à, neĭ‐teî iù‐kòng‐lōk; ī‐ch‘é kw‘ōng‐fung ooĭ‐ch‘ui‐waaî.
12 T‘aí‐a, fuk‐ts‘eūng ĭ‐king titₒ‐lōk‐kòh‐shī, heí‐m̄‐haî yaŭ tuì‐neĭ‐wâ, Shóh‐yûng laī‐tông‐kwòh‐kè fooi haí‐pin‐ch‘uè‐ni?
13 Shóh‐ĭ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh făn‐hân kòh‐shī, pit‐k‘aaĭ kw‘ōng fung laī‐ch‘ui‐waaî; ngŏh nau‐nô kòh‐shī, pit‐yaŭ yat‐chân‐uĕ tsàm‐kwòh, yīk faatₒ‐līt‐nô, k‘aaĭ taaî‐pōk tsiú‐mīt‐k‘uĭ à.
14 Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh pit‐waí‐laân k‘aaĭ‐shang‐fooi laī‐tông‐kwòh‐kè kòh‐fuk‐ts‘eūng, yaû ch‘aakₒ‐tai‐tò‐p‘īng‐teî, táng‐tò kei‐chí to‐lô‐ch‘ut tsaû‐titₒ‐lōk, neĭ tsoî‐k‘eī‐chung to peî‐tsiú‐mīt: neĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
15 Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh pit‐tsoî‐fuk‐ts‘eūng‐ch‘uè, yaû tsoî‐kòh‐ti k‘aaĭ‐shang‐fooi laī‐tông‐kwòh‐kè yān‐ch‘uè, shīng‐tsaû ngŏh făn‐hân, ngŏh to ooĭ‐tuì‐neĭ‐wâ, Fuk‐ts‘eūng mŏ‐hiu, tông‐fooi‐kè yān yīk‐mŏ‐hiu;
16 Tsik‐haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè sin‐chi, lûn‐k‘āp Yē‐lō‐saatₒ‐laăng kóng‐uê‐īn‐kè, yaû waî‐k‘uĭ‐kìn‐p‘īng‐on‐kè î‐tseûng, k‘eī‐shāt mŏ‐p‘īng‐on‐kè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
17 ¶ Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐tîng‐neĭ‐mîn heùng‐kung‐kik‐neĭ‐tsź‐mān‐kè chùng‐nuĭ, tsik‐haî chiù‐tsẑ‐keí‐sam‐ì kóng‐uê‐īn‐kè; neĭ‐iù‐kóng‐uê‐īn kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐teî,
18 Yaû wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Kòh‐ti haí chùng‐shaú‐chaang luēn‐maaī yūk‐tsź, yaû chíng‐kan waāk‐ko waāk‐aí‐chi‐yān‐kè t‘aū, laī‐chaaú‐ts‘ām‐k‘uĭ‐līng‐wān‐kè foŏ‐yān, yaŭ wôh lokₒ! Neĭ‐teî heí‐ooĭ‐chaaú‐ts‘ām ngŏh tsź‐mān‐kè līng‐wān, yaû waî‐tsẑ‐keí kaù‐shang k‘uĭ‐līng‐wān me?
19 Neĭ‐teî heùng‐ngŏh tsź‐mān kóng‐taaî‐wâ, lîng k‘uĭ t‘eng‐ts‘ūng neĭ taaî‐wâ? tsaû tsoî‐ngŏh‐tsź‐mān‐chi‐chung, tsô yat‐cha‐cha‐kè maū‐māk, k‘āp yat‐faaì faaì‐kè péng, sitₒ‐tūk ngŏh, laī‐seúng‐shaatₒ kòh‐ti m̄‐ying‐fân shaû‐shaatₒ‐kè, yaû seúng‐kaù‐shang m̄‐ying‐fân tak‐shang‐kè līng‐wān.
20 Koò‐ts‘ź Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik neĭ‐kè yūk‐tsź, tsik‐haî neĭ shóh‐yûng‐laī‐chaaú‐ts‘ām‐yān‐kè līng‐wān, lîng k‘uĭ fei‐hiu, ngŏh yīk haí neĭ shaú‐peì‐ch‘uè mokₒ‐hiu‐k‘uĭ, yaû iù‐fòng‐kòh‐ti līng‐wān‐huì, tsik‐haî neĭ shóh‐chaaú‐ts‘ām kòh‐ti līng‐wān, lîng‐k‘uĭ‐fei‐hiu‐kè.
21 Ngŏh to‐iù‐maakₒ‐līt neĭ‐kè kan, yaû kaù‐ngŏh‐tsź‐mān t‘uetₒ‐leī neĭ‐shaú, k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐tsoì haí‐neĭ‐shaú‐chung peî‐chaaú‐ts‘ām‐kè; neĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
22 Yan‐waî neĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ taaî‐wâ lîng î‐yān‐kè sam‐yau‐shaū, tsik‐haî ngŏh m̄‐ts‘āng lîng k‘uĭ kìn paì‐aì‐kè; yaû kin‐koò hāng‐okₒ‐yān‐kè shaú, táng k‘uĭ m̄‐faan‐chuèn leī‐hoi‐okₒ‐lô, laī‐tak‐kaù tak‐shang.
23 Shóh‐ĭ neĭ m̄‐tsoì ooĭ‐kìn‐hung‐hui‐kè sẑ, yaû m̄‐tsô chim‐puk‐kè sẑ: ngŏh pit‐kaù‐ngŏh‐tsź‐mān t‘uetₒ‐leī neĭ shaú: neĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
