1 À‐TONG, Ch‘itₒ, Ĭ‐nōh‐sẑ,
2 Koi‐naām, Mă‐lāk‐līt, Yē‐līt,
3 Ĭ‐nōk, Mă‐t‘ó‐saatₒ‐la, La‐māk,
4 Nōh‐à, Shím, Hōm, Ngă‐fat.
5 ¶ Ngă‐fat‐kè tsaí Hóh‐mīt, Mă‐kokₒ, Mă‐t‘aaì, Ngă‐faan, T‘ó‐paatₒ, Maĭ‐ch‘itₒ, T‘aī‐laāp.
6 Hóh‐mīt‐kè tsaí À‐shāt‐kei‐nā, Leĭ‐faatₒ, T‘ōh‐ka‐mă.
7 Ngă‐faan‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐sha, Taaî‐shat, Kei‐taí, Toh‐taan.
8 ¶ Hōm‐kè tsaí Koó‐shāt, Māk‐sai, Fat, Ka‐naām.
9 Koó‐shāt‐kè tsaí Sai‐pa, Hap‐feī‐la, Saapₒ‐taaî, La‐mă, Saapₒ‐t‘aī‐ka. La‐mă‐kè tsaí Shî‐pa, Taí‐taân.
10 Koó‐shāt shang Nīng‐lūk: k‘uĭ tsoî teî ch‘í‐ch‘oh tsô ying‐hūng.
11 Māk‐sai shang Lô‐ting, On‐naām, Leî‐hap, Naāp‐t‘ó,
12 Paatₒ‐lŏ, Kaapₒ‐lô, Kopₒ‐t‘okₒ, (Kaapₒ‐lô shang Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān.)
13 Ka‐naām shang cheúng‐tsź Sai‐tûn, kûng Haak,
14 Uĕ‐k‘āp Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐yān, À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān, Kaak‐ka‐sai‐yān,
15 Hei‐meî‐yān, À‐kei‐yān, Sai‐neī‐yān,
16 À‐ngă‐taí‐yān, Saí‐mă‐leĭ‐yān, Hap‐moōt‐yān.
17 ¶ Shím‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐laān, À‐shūt, À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ, Lô‐tak, À‐laān, Oo‐sẑ, Oô‐lāk, K‘eī‐t‘ipₒ, Maĭ‐ch‘itₒ,
18 À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ shang Shî‐la, Shî‐la shang Hei‐paakₒ.
19 Hei‐paakₒ shang leŭng‐kòh tsaí: yat‐kòh mēng‐kiù Faatₒ‐lāk; yan‐waî tong‐shī shaì‐sheûng‐yān to fan‐saàn‐hiu: k‘uĭ‐kè saì‐ló mēng‐kiù Yeukₒ‐taan.
20 Yeukₒ‐taan shang À‐moh‐taāt, Sha‐nap, Hap‐saatₒ, Mă‐fei, Yē‐la,
21 Hap‐toh‐laān, Oo‐saatₒ, Tāk‐la,
22 Ĭ‐paatₒ, À‐peì‐mă‐leî, Shî‐pa,
23 Oh‐fei, Hap‐feī‐la, Yeukₒ‐paatₒ. Ts‘ź yat‐k‘oì haî Yeukₒ‐taan‐kè tsaí.
24 ¶ Shím, À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ, Shî‐la,
25 Hei‐paakₒ, Faatₒ‐lāk, La‐kaú,
26 Saatₒ‐lūk, Nā‐hōk, T‘a‐la,
27 À‐paakₒ‐laān; À‐paakₒ‐laān tsik‐haî À‐paakₒ‐la‐hón.
28 À‐paakₒ‐la‐hón‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐saatₒ, Ĭ‐shāt‐mă‐leî.
29 ¶ K‘uĭ‐teî‐kè haû‐toî, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû: Ĭ‐shāt‐mă‐leî‐kè cheúng‐tsź Neī‐p‘aaī‐yeukₒ; k‘eī‐ts‘z̀ Kei‐taāt, Aapₒ‐pīt, Māt‐shaam.
30 Māt‐mă, T‘ó‐mă, Mă‐saatₒ, Hap‐taāt, T‘aī‐mă,
31 Yik‐tuk, Nā‐fei‐shāt, K‘eī‐taí‐mă. Ts‘ź‐haî Ĭ‐shāt‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí.
32 ¶ À‐paakₒ‐la‐hón‐kè ts‘ipₒ Kei‐to‐la shóh‐shang‐kè tsaí, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû: k‘uĭ shang Sam‐laān, Yeukₒ‐shaan, Maĭ‐t‘īn, Yik‐paatₒ, Shue‐à. Yeukₒ‐shaan‐kè tsaí Shî‐pa, Taí‐taân.
33 Maĭ‐t‘īn‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐faatₒ, Ĭ‐fat, Hap‐nōk, À‐peì‐taaî, Ĭ‐lāk‐taaî. Ts‘ź‐yat‐k‘oì haî Kei‐to‐la‐kè tsaí.
34 ¶ À‐paakₒ‐la‐hón shang Ĭ‐saatₒ. Ĭ‐saatₒ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐sò, Ĭ‐shik‐līt.
35 ¶ Ĭ‐sò‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐faatₒ, Laū‐ĭ, Yē‐shî, Ngă‐laān, Hóh‐la.
36 Ĭ‐leî‐faatₒ‐kè tsaí T‘aī‐maân; Oh‐moōt, Sai‐poh, Ka‐taân, Kei‐naāp, T‘īng‐naāp, À‐mă‐līk.
37 Laū‐ĭ‐kè tsaí Nā‐hāt; Sai‐la, Sha‐mă, Maĭ‐saatₒ.
38 Sai‐ĭ‐kè tsaí Lōh‐taan, Shuetₒ‐paatₒ, Tsaì‐pîn, À‐nā, Taí‐shûn, Ĭ‐ch‘aatₒ, Taí‐shaan.
39 Lōh‐taan‐kè tsaí Oh‐leî, Hei‐maân: Lōh‐taan‐kè mooî T‘īng‐naāp.
40 Shuetₒ‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí À‐lāk‐mān, Mă‐nā‐hāt, Ĭ‐paatₒ, Shî‐poh, Oh‐naām. Tsaì‐pîn‐kè tsaí À‐ngă, À‐nā.
41 À‐nā‐kè tsaí Taí‐shûn. Taí‐shûn‐kè tsaí Òm‐laān; Yat‐shî‐paan, Yik‐laān, Kei‐laān.
42 Ĭ‐ch‘aatₒ‐kè tsaí P‘ik‐hón, Saatₒ‐faan, Yē‐kon. Taí‐shaan‐kè tsaí Oo‐sẑ, À‐laān.
43 ¶ Meî‐ts‘āng yaŭ wōng koón‐leĭ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chi‐sin, koón‐leĭ Ĭ‐tung‐teî‐kè līt‐wōng, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû; Peì‐ĭ‐kè tsaí Peì‐la: k‘uĭ‐kè shēng‐mēng kiù T‘īng‐hap‐pa.
44 Peì‐la sź‐haû, P‘òh‐sz‐la‐yān Sai‐la‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐paatₒ, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
45 Yeukₒ‐paatₒ sź‐haû, shūk T‘aī‐maân‐teî‐kè yān, Oô‐shaan kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
46 Oô‐shaan sź‐haû, Peì‐t‘aatₒ‐kè tsaí Hap‐t‘aatₒ, tsik‐haî ts‘āng tsoî Moh‐aapₒ‐p‘īng‐uēn kung‐kik Maĭ‐t‘īn‐yān‐kè, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng: k‘uĭ‐kè shēng‐mēng kiù À‐meî‐tak.
47 Hap‐t‘aatₒ sź‐haû, Mă‐sẑ‐leĭ‐ka‐yān Saam‐la, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
48 Saam‐la sź‐haû, hōh‐pin‐kè Leĭ‐hōh‐paakₒ‐yān Sò‐lōh, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
49 Sò‐lōh sź‐haû, À‐kaakₒ‐p‘òh‐kè tsaí Pa‐lāk‐hap‐naām, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
50 Pa‐lāk‐hap‐naām sź‐haû, Hap‐t‘aatₒ kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng: k‘uĭ‐kè shēng‐mēng kiù Pó; k‘uĭ ts‘ai mēng‐kiù Maĭ‐hei‐taaî‐pīt, haî Mă‐tāk‐līt‐kè nuĭ, Mă‐tāk‐līt haî Maĭ‐saatₒ‐hōp‐kè nuĭ.
51 Hap‐t‘aatₒ sź‐haû. Tsoî Ĭ‐tung tsô kung‐tseukₒ‐kè, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû; T‘īng‐nā kung‐tseukₒ, À‐lāk‐ngă kung‐tseukₒ, Yē‐t‘ipₒ kung‐tseukₒ,
52 À‐hōh‐leî‐pa‐mă kung‐tseukₒ, Ĭ‐la kung‐tseukₒ, Peí‐nuên kung‐tseukₒ,
53 Kei‐naāp kung‐tseukₒ, T‘aī‐maân kung‐tseukₒ, Māt‐saatₒ kung‐tseukₒ,
54 Māk‐t‘itₒ kung‐tseukₒ, Ĭ‐laān kung‐tseukₒ. Ts‘ź haî Ĭ‐tung chùng‐kung‐tseukₒ.
