1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Neĭ kûng À‐lūn, Nā‐taapₒ, À‐peì‐oô, k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt cheúng‐lŏ‐chung chi ts‘at‐shāp yān, k‘ui iù sheŭng laī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ch‘uè; uĕn‐uĕn kwaî‐paaì.
2 Tūk‐haî Moh‐sai hóh‐ĭ kân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: k‘eī‐uē kè yān pat‐tak‐kân; paakₒ‐sìng yīk m̄‐hó t‘ūng Moh‐sai sheŭng laī.
3 Moh‐sai lōk laī, k‘aaĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kokₒ‐yeûng shuetₒ‐wâ lūt‐laî tuì paakₒ‐sìng kóng: paakₒ‐sìng ts‘aī sheng taapₒ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faān shóh‐kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, ngŏh pit i‐ts‘ūng laī tsô.
4 Moh‐sai tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng sé tsoî shue‐chung, hó‐tsó heí‐shan, haí shaan‐hâ heí t‘aān, yaû laāp shāp‐î t‘iū shēk‐ch‘uĕ, chiù‐i Ĭ‐shik‐līt kè shāp‐î chi‐p‘aaì.
5 Tá‐faatₒ Ĭ‐shik‐līt chung kè shiù‐nīn yān, tseung faān‐tsaì k‘āp ch‘aū‐yan‐tsaì kè ngaū, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
6 Moh‐sai ning huetₒ yat‐poòn tsoì lōk p‘oōn‐chung; yat‐poòn shá lōk t‘aān‐sheûng.
7 Yaû ning yeukₒ‐shue tūk‐kwòh paakₒ‐sìng t‘eng: taaî‐chùng wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faān shóh‐kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, ngŏh‐teî pit t‘eng‐ts‘ūng tsun‐hāng.
8 Moh‐sai ning huetₒ shá lōk paakₒ‐sìng shan‐sheûng, wâ, T‘aí‐hă yeukₒ kè huetₒ, naaĭ‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘aaĭ ni‐ti shuetₒ‐wâ, kûng neĭ‐teî shóh‐laāp‐kè.
9 Moh‐sai, À‐lūn, Nā‐taapₒ, À‐peì‐oô, t‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt cheúng‐lŏ‐chung chi ts‘at‐shāp yān to sheŭng shaan:
10 Ch‘iū‐kìn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì: k‘uĭ keukₒ‐hâ yaŭ hó‐ts‘z̆ teukₒ shīng kè laām‐yūk‐shēk, yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘in‐shik kòm‐ts‘ing.
11 Taân Sheûng‐Taì m̄‐yûng shaú òn‐hă Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè tsuen‐cheúng: k‘uĭ‐teî t‘aí‐kìn Sheûng‐Taì, yaû yám yaû shīk.
12 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Neĭ sheŭng shaan tò ngŏh‐ch‘uè, chuê tsoî shaan‐sheûng: ngŏh iù k‘aaĭ shēk‐pei k‘āp lūt‐faatₒ kaaì‐mîng ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ, tsik‐haî ngŏh shóh‐sé‐kè; táng neĭ hóh‐ĭ kaaù‐fàn k‘uĭ‐teî.
13 Moh‐sai kûng k‘uĭ taaî shān Yeukₒ‐shue‐à: t‘ūng sheŭng Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shaan.
14 Tuì cheúng‐lŏ wâ, Neĭ‐teî hóh‐ĭ chuê tsoî ni‐ch‘uè, táng‐toî ngŏh‐teî faan laī: ni‐ch‘uè yaŭ À‐lūn, Oô‐ĭ, t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ: faān yaŭ sẑ‐ts‘īng hóh‐ĭ huì k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè.
15 Moh‐sai tsaû sheŭng shaan, shaan peî wān che‐chuê.
16 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè wīng‐iû, chí tsoî Sai‐naaĭ shaan, wān che‐chuê lūk yāt: tò taî‐ts‘at yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haí wān‐chung chiû Moh‐sai.
17 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wīng‐kwong‐kè yīng‐chông, hó‐ts‘z̆ fóh shiu shaan‐téng, tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ngaăn‐ts‘īn.
18 Moh‐sai yāp wān sheŭng shaan: Moh‐sai chuê tsoî shaan‐sheûng sz̀‐shāp yāt sz̀‐shāp yê.
