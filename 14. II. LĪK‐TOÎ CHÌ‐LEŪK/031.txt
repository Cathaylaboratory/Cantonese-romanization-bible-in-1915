1 FAĀN ni‐ti‐sẑ keì tsô‐shīng, tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè chùng‐yān ch‘ut‐huì‐tò Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐shēng, waí‐ch‘aakₒ ngaŭ‐tseûng, chaám‐t‘uĕn À‐sha‐laāt, faì‐huì shūng‐yau, kûng tsaì‐t‘aān, tsoì Yaū‐t‘aaì, Pìn‐ngă‐măn, Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn, Mă‐nā‐sai ts‘uēn‐teî, chì tsûn‐teî tsuēt‐saaì. Ue‐shî Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān faan‐huì poón‐t‘ó‐kè shēng, kokₒ‐kwai tsẑ‐keí‐kè ch‘aán‐īp.
2 Hei‐sai‐ka ch‘itₒ‐laāp tsaì‐sz paan‐luēn, yaû fan‐p‘aaì Leî‐meî‐yān i‐chuê lūn‐paan, kokₒ‐yān chiù‐ì kóh‐ti chik‐yīk, tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān, hìn faān‐tsaì k‘āp hìn ch‘aū‐yan‐tsaì; ī‐ch‘é fûng‐sẑ kóm‐tsê tsaàn‐meĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yīng‐p‘oōn‐kè moōn.
3 Wōng yaû tîng wōng‐īp‐chi‐fân waî faān‐tsaì, tsik‐haì tsô chiu‐maăn‐kè faān‐tsaì, yaû waî kokₒ‐on‐sik‐yāt, mooĭ uēt ch‘oh‐yat k‘āp tsitₒ‐k‘eī‐kè faān‐tsaì, tsun‐i shóh‐tsoì‐lōk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐lūt‐faatₒ‐kè.
4 Kim‐chi fan‐foò kui‐tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè tsź‐mān, kung‐k‘ap tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān chi‐fân, chì k‘uĭ‐teî faatₒ‐făn tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè lūt‐faatₒ.
5 Uê‐chí yat‐ch‘ut, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû shâm‐toh taaì ch‘oh‐shāt‐kè‐kwóh, waāk kuk, waāk tsaú, waāk māt‐t‘ōng, kûng t‘īn‐chung shóh‐ch‘aán‐kè, yaû k‘aaĭ kokₒ‐māt shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat, tsaû kīk‐toh taaì‐laī.
6 Kui‐chuê Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐shēng‐kè Ĭ‐shik‐līt, k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, yīk taaì ngaū‐yeūng shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat; ī‐ch‘é shìng‐pīt hìn‐kwòh Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô shìng‐māt, tseung shāp‐fàn‐chi‐yat, taai‐tò tsik‐shīng tui‐luĭ.
7 Tsẑ saam‐uēt heí‐shaú, chuk kokₒ‐tui‐luĭ‐chi‐kei, chì‐tò ts‘at‐uēt tsô‐shīng.
8 Hei‐sai‐ka kûng chùng‐kwan‐cheúng laī‐kìn‐tui‐luĭ‐chi‐shī, tsaû chuk‐tsê Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; ī‐ch‘é chuk‐fuk k‘uĭ tsź‐mān Ĭ‐shik‐līt.
9 Ue‐shî Hei‐sai‐ka lûn kokₒ‐tui‐luĭ, tsaû mân tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān.
10 Yaŭ shūk Saatₒ‐tuk ka‐kè tsaì‐sz‐cheúng À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, taapₒ k‘uĭ wâ. Tsz̀ tsź‐mān taaì laĭ‐māt yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk ĭ‐loī, ngŏh‐teî sheūng‐shī kaù‐shīk, shìng‐hâ laū‐ts‘uēn shâm‐toh: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ĭ‐king chuk‐fuk k‘uĭ‐kè tsź‐mān; shóh‐uē‐shîng‐kè kòm‐fung‐shîng à.
11 Kòh‐shī Hei‐sai‐ka fan‐foò uê‐peî ts‘ong‐lăm tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk; tsaû uê‐peî hó,
12 K‘uĭ‐teî shīng‐sam tseung laĭ‐māt, k‘āp shóh‐shue‐naāp shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat, kûng shìng‐pīt‐chi‐māt taaì‐yāp‐luĭ‐pîn: ni‐ti‐sẑ haî Leî‐meî‐yān Koh‐nōh‐neī koón‐hāt, k‘uĭ‐kè hing‐taî Shî‐mooĭ foò koón‐hāt.
13 Yē‐hitₒ, À‐saatₒ‐sai, Nā‐hap, À‐saatₒ‐hak, Yē‐leĭ‐moōt, Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐paatₒ, Ĭ‐līt, Yik‐mă‐kei, Mă‐hāt, Peì‐nā‐ngă, tsô tuk‐leĭ‐kè, tsoî Koh‐nōh‐neī k‘āp k‘uĭ hing‐taî Shî‐mooĭ‐kè shaú‐hâ‐yān, tsun‐i Hei‐sai‐ka wōng k‘āp Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐chi‐tsoí À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐laāp‐kè.
14 Hon‐shaú tung‐moōn‐kè haî Leî‐meî‐yān, Yam‐nā‐kè tsaí Hóh‐la, cheúng‐koón faān lōk‐ì sùng‐kwòh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè laĭ‐māt, fân shóh‐hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè, k‘āp kokₒ‐chì‐shìng‐chi‐māt.
15 Tsoî k‘uĭ shaú‐hâ‐kè, yaŭ Oi‐t‘īn‐yān Maĭ‐nā‐mān, Yē‐shue‐à, Shî‐mà‐ngă, À‐mă‐leĭ, Shî‐ka‐neī, tsoî tsaì‐sz‐kè kokₒ‐shēng, kung‐fûng chik‐yīk, i‐chuê paan‐luēn; ī‐ch‘e fan‐faatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî hing‐taî, taaî kûng siú, kaai‐ch‘ut k‘uĭ‐teî fan‐p‘aaì‐wān:
16 Tsoì‐tsoî tsūk‐p‘ó‐kè naām‐ting, tsẑ saam‐suì‐ĭ‐sheûng, yīk fan‐p‘aaì, faān yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐tîn‐uk‐kè, tseung k‘uĭ‐teî chik‐yīk i‐chuê paan‐luēn, ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ‐teî mooĭ yāt‐kè chik‐fân, peí‐tak kung‐fûng yīk‐sẑ;
17 Yaû fan‐faatₒ chùng‐tsaì‐sz tsoì‐lōk tsūk‐p‘ó‐kè, i‐chuê k‘uĭ līt‐tsó‐kè ka, k‘āp Leî‐meî‐yān, tsẑ î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, tseung k‘uĭ‐teî chik‐yīk i‐chuê paan‐luēn;
18 Yaû fan‐faatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî tsoì‐lōk tsūk‐p‘ó‐kè chùng shiù‐ché, k‘eī‐ts‘ai, k‘eī‐tsź, k‘eī‐nuĭ, ts‘uēn‐kung‐ooî: yan k‘uĭ‐teî kung‐fûng chik‐yīk; ī‐ch‘é shìng‐kitₒ iù tsẑ‐keí shìng‐pīt à:
19 Yaû waî fan‐faatₒ shūk À‐lūn‐yān kui‐tsoî kokₒ‐shēng kaau‐yĕ‐kè tsaì‐sz, taaî‐chùng tsoî kokₒ‐shēng ch‘itₒ‐mēng sé‐tsoî‐ch‘aakₒ‐sheûng‐kè yān, shaí hóh‐fan‐p‘aaì tsaì‐sz‐kè chùng‐naām‐ting, k‘āp faān tsoì‐tsoî tsūk‐p‘ó‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān.
20 Hei‐sai‐ka tsoî ts‘uēn Yaū‐t‘aaì kóm‐yeûng hāng, yaû tsoî k‘uĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng shîn‐tô, chìng‐chīk chan‐shāt.
21 Taân‐faān k‘uĭ heí‐shaú shóh‐hāng‐kè kung‐tsokₒ, tsoî Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐kè yīk‐sẑ, tsun lūt‐faatₒ, shaú mîng‐lîng, ts‘ām‐k‘aū Sheûng‐Taì, k‘uĭ tsûn‐sam laī‐hāng, yaû leî‐taāt à.
