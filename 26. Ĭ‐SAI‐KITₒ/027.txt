1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ‐KÈ īn‐uĕ tsoì‐kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù waî T‘ui‐lōh kuí‐oi‐koh;
3 Yaû tuì T‘ui‐lōh wâ, Chuê‐tsoî‐taaî‐hoí‐ch‘ut‐yāp‐chi‐ch‘uè‐kè à, heùng‐toh‐hoí‐tó‐kè līt‐mān tsô‐maû‐yīk‐kè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘ui‐lōh à, neĭ ts‘āng wâ, Ngŏh haî shāp‐tsuk meĭ‐laî kè.
4 Neĭ‐kè kíng‐kaaì, tsoî‐hoí‐chung‐sam, kìn‐tsô‐neĭ‐kè, tsaû lîng‐neĭ‐wā‐laî‐uēn‐ts‘uēn.
5 K‘uĭ‐teî yûng À‐nīk‐kè ts‘ūng‐shuê, tsô neĭ‐kè paán; yaû tseung Leî‐pa‐nuên‐kè paakₒ‐heung‐shuê, tsô neĭ‐kè waī.
6 K‘uĭ‐teî yûng Pa‐shaan‐kè tseûng‐shuê, tsô neĭ‐kè tseúng; yaû yûng Kei‐taí hoí‐tó‐laī‐kè pín‐paakₒ‐mūk, peí‐tseûng‐ngā‐seung‐chuê‐kè, tsô neĭ ts‘ŏh‐keí.
7 Neĭ kòh‐cheùng‐leĭ, tsẑ Oi‐k‘āp laī‐kè, saù‐fa yaù‐saì‐mā‐pò tsô‐kè; táng hóh‐ĭ‐waî‐neĭ tsô‐hô‐k‘eī, neĭ‐kè pò‐cheùng, yûng tsẑ Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐hoí‐tó‐kè ts‘eng‐kûng‐laām‐pò‐tsô‐kè.
8 Sai‐tûn kûng À‐faatₒ‐kè kui‐mān, waî‐neĭ chaaû‐tseúng; T‘ui‐lōh à, neĭ‐chì‐waî‐yān tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung, tsô‐neĭ‐kè shaau‐kung.
9 K‘eī‐paatₒ‐kè lŏ‐taaî‐yān, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung‐kè chì‐waî‐yān, waî‐neĭ‐tá‐ch‘aang: hoí‐sheûng shóh‐yaŭ‐shuēn‐chekₒ, t‘ūng‐maaī shuēn‐ka, tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung liû‐leĭ neĭ‐kè fòh‐māt.
10 Pa‐sai‐yān, Lô‐tak‐yān, kûng Fat‐yān, tsoî neĭ‐kwan‐chung tsô‐mŏ‐sẑ: yaû tsoî‐neĭ‐ch‘uè kwà‐heí t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī‐kûng‐t‘aū‐kw‘ai; k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû hín‐ch‘ut neĭ‐meĭ‐laî‐chi‐ch‘uè.
11 À‐faatₒ‐yān tsoî neĭ kwan‐ping‐chi‐chung, chau‐waī tsoî neĭ ts‘eūng‐oōn‐chi‐sheûng, Ka‐mă‐tîng tóng‐tsoî neĭ‐shēng‐laū‐chi‐chung: haí neĭ ts‘eūng‐oōn‐ch‘uè chau‐waī kwà‐heí k‘uĭ‐t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī; tsaû lîng‐neĭ wā‐laî shīng‐waī uēn‐ts‘uēn.
12 Taaî‐shat yan‐k‘uĭ shóh‐faatₒ kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè ts‘oī‐māt taaî‐toh, tsaû tsô‐neĭ sheung‐ka; k‘aaĭ‐ngān, t‘itₒ, sekₒ, uēn, waî‐neĭ‐shóh‐faatₒ‐kè fòh‐māt kaau‐yīk.
13 Ngă‐faan, T‘ó‐paatₒ, Maĭ‐ch‘itₒ, shūk‐neĭ maaĭ‐maaî‐kè: tseung yān‐haú kûng t‘ūng‐heì, waî‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt kaau‐yīk.
14 T‘ōh‐ka‐mă‐yān tseung mă‐p‘at, chìn‐mă, k‘āp‐luī, waî‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt kaau‐yīk.
15 Taí‐taân‐yān, shūk‐neĭ tsô‐maaĭ‐maaî‐kè; toh‐hoí‐tó tsô‐neĭ‐shaú‐hâ‐kè shĭ‐chàn: yaû ning tseûng‐ngā‐kokₒ, k‘āp oo‐mūk, kûng neĭ kaau‐yīk.
16 Yan neĭ‐kung‐tsokₒ‐kīk‐toh, À‐laān tsô‐neĭ‐sheung‐ka: tsaû tseung lūk‐yūk, tsź‐pò, saù‐fa‐kè kung‐tsokₒ, yaù‐saì‐kè‐mā‐pò, shaan‐oō, k‘āp hūng‐pó‐shēk, waî‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt kaau‐yīk.
17 Yaū‐t‘aaì kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐teî, to‐shūk‐neĭ tsô‐maaĭ‐maaî‐kè: tseung Maĭ‐nīk‐kè māk, t‘ōng‐kwóh, māt‐t‘ōng, yaū, k‘āp uĕ‐heung, waî‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt kaau‐yīk.
18 Taaî‐mă‐shik yan‐neĭ‐kung‐tsokₒ‐kīk‐toh, yaû yan‐neĭ‐kokₒ‐yeûng‐ts‘oī‐māt‐kīk‐toh, tsô‐neĭ‐sheung‐ka; tseung Hak‐poón‐kè tsaú, kûng paāk‐yeūng‐mō laī‐kaau‐yīk.
19 Feī‐taân k‘āp Ngă‐faan tseung yūng‐sìn waî‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt kaau‐yīk: kwong‐t‘itₒ, yūk‐kwaì, k‘āp ch‘eung‐p‘ō, tsoî‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt‐chi‐chung.
20 Taí‐taân shūk‐neĭ‐maaĭ‐maaî kòh‐ti tsô‐mă‐ch‘e‐chin kwaì‐chûng‐kè pò.
21 À‐la‐paakₒ k‘āp Kei‐taāt‐kè chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, tsô‐neĭ‐shaú‐hâ‐kè sheung‐ka, tseung yeūng‐tsaí, yeūng‐kung, k‘āp shaan‐yeūng: kûng‐neĭ‐maû‐yīk.
22 Shūk Shî‐pa kûng La‐mă maaĭ‐maaî‐kè, to‐tsô‐neĭ maaĭ‐maaî‐kè: tseung kokₒ‐yeûng chì‐hó‐kè heung‐liû, k‘āp pó‐shēk, kûng wōng‐kam, waî‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt kaau‐yīk.
23 Hap‐laān, Kon‐neī, kûng Oi‐t‘īn, tsik‐haî Shî‐pa, À‐shūt, kûng Kat‐mootₒ maaĭ‐maaî‐kè, to‐tsô neĭ‐maaĭ‐maaî‐kè.
24 Ts‘ź‐yān tseung‐neĭ‐tíng‐hó‐kè fòh‐māt maaĭ‐maaî, tseung laām‐pò‐p‘at, k‘āp saù‐fa‐kè‐kung‐foo, kwaì‐chûng‐kè i‐fūk, chong‐tsoî paakₒ‐heung‐mūk‐tsô‐kè seung, peí‐shīng‐kw‘án‐chuê, tsoî‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt‐chi‐chung.
25 Taaî‐shat shuēn‐chekₒ poon‐wân neĭ‐fòh‐māt: ī‐ch‘é neĭ tak‐fung‐shîng‐ka‐toh, tsoî chùng‐hoí‐chung‐sam, shīng‐waī‐kīk‐wīng‐wā‐kè.
26 Neĭ chaaû‐tseúng‐kè yān, taaì‐neĭ‐yāp taaî‐shuí‐ch‘uè: tung‐fung tsoî‐chùng‐hoí‐chung‐sam tá‐waaî‐neĭ.
27 Neĭ‐kè ts‘oī‐paāk, neĭ‐kè kung‐tsokₒ k‘āp fòh‐māt, neĭ‐shuēn‐ka k‘āp‐shaau‐kung, tá‐ch‘aang‐kè, k‘āp liû‐leĭ‐neĭ‐fòh‐māt‐kè, t‘ūng‐maaī‐neĭ chùng‐mŏ‐sẑ tsoî‐neĭ‐ch‘uè‐kè, kûng‐neĭ‐chi‐chung‐kè taaî‐chùng‐yān, pit‐tsoî‐neĭ‐paaî‐mōng‐chi‐chung, haâm‐lōk chùng‐hoí‐chung‐sam.
28 Yan neĭ shaau‐kung haàm‐foó‐chi‐sheng, tsóh‐yaû‐teî to‐ooĭ‐chàn‐tûng.
29 Faān neĭ‐cha‐tseúng‐kè, tsô‐shuēn‐ka‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp hoí‐sheûng‐kè chùng‐shaau‐kung, pit‐yaū‐k‘uĭ shuēn‐chekₒ lōk‐laī k‘eĭ‐tsoî hŏn‐teî;
30 Yaû lîng k‘uĭ sheng‐yam tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐sheûng peî‐t‘eng‐mān, yaû haàm‐foó‐ts‘ai‐leūng, fòng‐ch‘ān‐tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐t‘aū‐sheûng, yaû‐tseung‐tsẑ‐keí luk‐tsoî‐fooi‐ch‘uè:
31 Yaû waî‐neĭ lîng‐k‘uĭ‐teî t‘aū‐t‘uk, peí‐ts‘o‐mā‐pò ch‘uk‐chuê, sam‐shān foó‐ts‘itₒ, laū‐luî‐oi‐huk‐tak‐ts‘ai‐leūng.
32 K‘uĭ‐teî huk‐yap kòh‐shī, tsaû waî‐neĭ‐kuí‐oi‐koh, yaû waî‐neĭ‐oi‐huk, wâ, Mat‐shuī ts‘z̆‐T‘ui‐lōh, tsik‐haî ts‘z̆‐k‘uĭ tsoî‐taaî‐hoí‐chung‐kaan, shīng‐waī‐tsīk‐mōk kè?
33 Neĭ fòh‐māt yaū‐chùng‐hoí‐sheŭng‐laī kòh‐shī, neĭ tsaû ch‘ung‐moŏn chùng‐toh‐līt‐mān; tseung‐neĭ‐kè taaî‐toh‐ts‘oī‐māt, k‘āp‐neĭ‐kè fòh‐māt, lîng‐teî‐sheûng līt‐wōng shīng‐waī‐foò‐haû.
34 Neĭ tsoî‐hoí‐pin chùng‐shuí‐chi‐sham‐ch‘uè peî‐tá‐waaî‐kòh‐shī, neĭ‐kè fòh‐māt, uĕ‐k‘āp neĭ‐kè chùng‐yān, tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung, to‐k‘ing‐paaî‐lokₒ.
35 Hoí‐tó‐kè chùng‐kui‐mān, waî‐neĭ‐king‐k‘eī, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè wōng, to king‐hoī‐kuî‐p‘à, k‘uĭ‐teî mîn‐maaû to‐yau‐moôn.
36 Līt‐mān‐chi‐chung‐kè sheung‐ka, heùng‐neĭ‐faatₒ‐ch‘i‐sheng; neĭ shīng‐waī king‐hoī‐chi‐māt, neĭ‐tseung‐loī pit‐m̄‐tak sheūng‐ts‘uēn.
