1 KÒH‐MAĂN wōng m̄‐fàn‐tak‐cheūk, tsaû fan‐foò lóh līk‐toî‐chì‐leūk laī; yaŭ yān tūk‐kwòh k‘uĭ t‘eng.
2 K‘eī‐chung sé‐lōk yaŭ wâ, Wōng‐kè t‘aaì‐kaàm hon‐shaú‐kung‐moōn‐kè î yān, P‘ik‐taan, Taí‐līk, seúng maū‐hoî À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng, Mūk‐taí‐koí tseung kòh‐kîn‐sẑ pò‐wōng‐chi.
3 Wōng tsaû wâ, Mūk‐taí‐koí tsô‐hiu kòh‐kîn‐sẑ, yaŭ mat‐yĕ tsuen‐wīng tseukₒ‐waî ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ mŏ ni? Fūk‐sẑ‐wōng‐kè shān‐pūk taapₒ wâ, Mŏ‐k‘aaĭ mat‐yĕ shi‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ.
4 Wōng wâ, Mat‐shuī haí uên‐chung ni? Hap‐maân kong‐ngaam yāp‐tò wōng‐kung‐kè ngoî‐uên, seúng k‘aū wōng chún k‘uĭ tiù Mūk‐taí‐koí, haí k‘uĭ shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐kè mūk‐kà‐sheûng.
5 Wōng‐kè shān‐pūk taapₒ wâ, Hap‐maân k‘eĭ‐tsoî uên‐noî. Wōng wâ, Hóh‐yūng k‘uĭ yāp‐laī.
6 Hap‐maân tsaû yāp‐huì. Wōng tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Wōng shóh‐foon‐heí tsuen‐wīng‐kè yān, tong tím‐yeûng toî k‘uĭ ni? Hap‐maân sam‐ì wâ, Wōng shóh‐foon‐heí tsuen‐wīng‐kè yān, mat‐shuī peí‐ngŏh‐kàng‐shìng ni?
7 Hap‐maân tsaû tuì wōng wâ, Wōng shóh‐foon‐heí tsuen‐wīng‐kè yān,
8 Iù tseung wōng shóh‐sheūng‐cheukₒ‐kè wōng‐fūk, wōng shóh‐sheūng‐k‘ē‐kè mă, wōng shóh‐taaì‐tsoî t‘aū‐sheûng‐kè koon‐mĭn:
9 Tseung ts‘ź i‐fūk kûng mă, kaau‐kwòh wōng‐kè kwan‐cheúng‐chung yat‐kòh, kīk‐tsuen‐kwaì‐shān‐kè shaú, mîng k‘uĭ tseung i‐fūk peí‐wōng‐shóh‐foon‐heí tsuen‐wīng‐kòh‐yān cheukₒ‐heí, lîng k‘uĭ k‘ē‐sheŭng mă, haí shēng‐luĭ‐kè kaai‐sheûng laī‐hāng, haí k‘uĭ ts‘īn‐t‘aū suen‐īn wâ, Wōng shóh‐foon‐heí‐tsuen‐wīng‐kè yān, haî kóm‐yeûng toî k‘uĭ.
10 Wōng tsaû tuì Hap‐maân wâ, Neĭ kap‐ts‘uk tseung ts‘ź i‐fūk kûng mă, i‐chuê neĭ shóh‐kóng‐kè, heùng tsoî kung‐moōn‐ts‘ŏh‐ch‘uè‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān Mūk‐taí‐koí laī tsô: neĭ faān shóh‐kóng‐kè, m̄‐hó hìm‐k‘uetₒ yat‐yeûng.
11 Hap‐maân tsaû tseung i‐fūk peí Mūk‐taí‐koí cheukₒ‐heí, lîng k‘uĭ k‘ē‐sheŭng mă, tsoî shēng‐luĭ‐kè kaai‐sheûng laī‐hāng, haí k‘uĭ ts‘īn‐t‘aū suen‐īn wâ, Wōng shóh‐foon‐heí tsuen‐wīng‐kè yān, haî kóm‐yeûng toî k‘uĭ.
12 Mūk‐taí‐koí yīng faan‐huì wōng‐moōn. Waī Hap‐maân huk‐yap mūng‐t‘aū, faaì‐ti tsaú‐faan‐huì‐kwai.
13 Yaû tseung tsẑ‐keí shóh‐uê‐cheūk‐kè sẑ, kóng‐saaì kwòh‐k‘uĭ ts‘ai Sai‐lāk, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè p‘āng‐yaŭ t‘eng. K‘uĭ chì‐waî‐kè yaŭ, k‘āp k‘uĭ ts‘ai Sai‐lāk tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ keì‐haí Mūk‐taí‐koí mîn‐ts‘īn shaû‐wat, k‘uĭ uē‐kwóh haî shūk Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, neĭ tsaû pit‐pat‐nāng shìng k‘uĭ, pit haí k‘uĭ‐ts‘īn shaû‐wat tò‐taí à.
14 K‘uĭ‐teî t‘ūng Hap‐maân kóng wâ chi‐shī, wōng‐kè t‘aaì‐kaàm laī ts‘ui Hap‐maân, faì‐huì foô Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐kè ìn.
