1 KUNG‐Î chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, ngŏh t‘ó‐kò neĭ, mông neĭ shuī‐t‘eng ngŏh: sik‐yāt shaû kw‘àn‐foó, neĭ kaaí‐lat ngŏh; uē‐kam k‘aū neĭ līn‐sut ngŏh, t‘eng ngŏh shóh‐k‘aū.
2 Neĭ‐teî kokₒ‐yān miŭ‐shî ngŏh‐chi‐wīng‐waî? neĭ foon‐heí hui‐ká, hò‐hāng kaan‐chà, tò‐keí shī ni?
3 Neĭ tong chi Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, kaán‐chaāk kìng‐k‘īn‐chi‐yān, kwai k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí: ngŏh faān t‘ó‐kò, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit‐t‘eng.
4 Neĭ‐teî sui tong waì‐kuî, m̄‐hó waī‐pooì: tsoî ch‘ōng‐chi‐shī, leūng‐sam tsẑ‐mân, m̄‐shaí ch‘ut‐sheng.
5 Tseung kung‐î tòng‐tsokₒ tsaì‐māt, laī í‐k‘aaù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
6 Hó‐toh‐yān wâ, Mat‐shuī nāng tseung hó‐ch‘uè shi‐peí ngŏh‐teî? waī uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ mîn‐sheûng‐chi‐wīng‐kwong, chiù‐lām ngŏh‐teî.
7 Neĭ ts‘z̀ foon‐lōk, tsoî ngŏh sam‐chung, hó‐kwòh shaì‐yān tsaú maĭ fung‐shîng‐chi‐shī.
8 Ngŏh tak on‐sam, yat‐fàn tsaû cheūk: yan tūk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lîng ngŏh on‐kui.
