1 KWAAN‐UE À‐moōn tsź‐suen‐chi‐sẑ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ĭ‐shik‐līt mŏ‐tsaí me? mŏ‐shīng‐shaû ka‐īp‐kè tsaí me? kóm‐tsaû yan‐hōh kìn Mă‐kam shīng‐shaû Ka‐tak ni? k‘uĭ tsź‐mān chuê‐tsoî k‘eī‐chung‐kè shēng‐yap ni?
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Shóh‐ĭ iù‐t‘aí‐hă, yāt‐tsź tseung‐kân‐laī, ngŏh tsaû haî lîng tá‐cheùng king‐fong‐kè sheng, kung‐kik shūk À‐moōn tsź‐suen‐kè La‐pa faatₒ‐ch‘ut; peî‐yān t‘eng‐mān, tsaû ooĭ‐shīng‐waī fong‐faì‐kè tui, k‘uĭ chùng‐nuĭ to peî‐fóh‐shiu‐mīt: ue‐shî Ĭ‐shik‐līt ooĭ‐shīng‐shaû kòh‐ti yă‐ts‘āng shīng‐shaû kòh‐ch‘uè‐kè yān, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
3 Hei‐shāt‐poón à, iù taaî‐sheng‐haàm, yan‐waî Oi‐yap ĭ‐king paaî‐waaî lokₒ: La‐pa‐kè chùng‐nuĭ à, iù‐hō‐foo, peí‐ts‘o‐mā‐pò ch‘uk‐chuê; iù‐oi‐huk, yaû tsoî waī‐leī‐chi‐kaan, tsaú‐huì tsaú‐loī; yan‐waî Mă‐kam līn‐maaī k‘uĭ tsaì‐sz kûng kwan‐cheúng, yat‐ts‘aī iù‐peî‐lŏ‐leūk huì‐lokₒ.
4 Poōt‐yīk‐kè nuĭ à, neĭ yan‐hōh waî‐neĭ‐kè chùng‐kuk, neĭ laū‐shuí‐kè kuk king‐kw‘a ni? í‐laaî neĭ‐kè ts‘oī‐māt wâ, Mat‐shuī ooĭ‐laī‐neĭ‐ch‘uè ni?
5 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh ts‘ūng waī‐chuê neĭ‐kè chùng‐yān, lîng‐king‐fong kòng‐lām‐ue neĭ; neĭ‐teî kokₒ‐yān, yat‐chīk peî‐kón‐ch‘ut‐huì; tsúng‐mŏ‐yān laī‐tsuî‐maaī kòh‐ti‐laū‐leī shat‐shóh‐kè yān.
6 Waī‐haî haû‐loī, ngŏh pit‐lîng À‐moōn tsź‐suen peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān faan‐laī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
7 ¶ Kwaan‐ue Ĭ‐tung‐kè sẑ, maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Tsoî T‘aī‐maân m̄‐tsoì‐yaŭ chì‐waî me? maū‐tîng‐kè chi‐shik, haí t‘ung‐taāt‐yān‐ch‘uè t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐kè me? k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè chì‐waî m̄‐kìn‐hiu me?
8 Taí‐taân‐kè kui‐mān à, iù‐t‘ō‐tsaú faán‐chuèn t‘uì‐shuk, kui‐chuê sham‐māt‐ch‘uè; yan‐waî ngŏh t‘aàm‐ch‘aatₒ k‘uĭ kòh‐shī, tsaû‐lîng Ĭ‐sò‐kè wôh‐waân, kòng‐lām‐ue k‘uĭ.
9 Uē‐kwóh chaāk‐p‘ō‐t‘aī‐tsź‐kè‐yān laī‐neĭ‐ch‘uè, heí‐m̄‐laū‐hâ‐ti waī‐lōk‐kè p‘ō‐t‘aī‐tsź me? uē‐kwóh yê‐kaan yaŭ‐ts‘aāk‐laī, k‘uĭ‐teî heí‐m̄‐paaî‐waaî chì‐tò kìn tsuk me?
10 Waī‐haî ngŏh lîng Ĭ‐sò pìn‐waī ch‘ekₒ‐t‘aí, lô‐ch‘ut peì‐māt‐chi‐ch‘uè, k‘uĭ pat‐nāng yán‐ts‘ōng tsẑ‐keí: k‘uĭ haû‐yuî peî‐paaî‐waaî, k‘uĭ hing‐taî‐kûng‐lūn‐shè to‐haî, tsẑ‐keí yīk mŏ‐hiu lokₒ.
11 Iù laū‐lōk neĭ mō‐foô‐kè tsź‐nuĭ, ngŏh ooĭ‐pó‐ts‘uēn k‘uĭ tak‐shang; to‐iù peí neĭ kwá‐foŏ í‐laaî ngŏh à.
12 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, poón‐loī m̄‐kwaan k‘uĭ yám‐kòh‐pooi‐kè yān, yat‐tîng iù‐yám à; neĭ heí‐haî kòh‐ti tò‐taí‐m̄‐shaû‐yīng‐fāt‐kè yān me? neĭ shāt‐tsoî m̄‐mĭn‐tak yīng‐fāt, waī‐haî yat‐tîng iù‐yám‐kè lokₒ.
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh chí‐tsẑ‐keí faatₒ‐shaî wâ, P‘òh‐sz‐la shīng‐waī‐king‐hoī‐kè, līng‐yūk‐kè, fong‐faì‐kè, chaù‐chòh‐kè māt; k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐chi‐shēng‐yap, tò‐wĭng‐uĕn pit‐shīng‐waī fong‐faì.
14 Ngŏh yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘eng‐mān siu‐sik, yīk yaŭ tá‐faatₒ ch‘aai‐huì līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung, wâ, Iù yat‐ts‘aī tsuî‐maaī laī‐kung‐kik‐k‘uĭ, yaû heí‐laī tá‐cheùng à.
15 Yan‐waî t‘aí‐a, ngŏh lîng‐neĭ tsoî līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung shīng‐waī meī‐siú, yaû‐tsoî yān‐chi‐chung‐peî‐miŭ‐shî.
16 Chuê‐tsoî shēk‐là, yaû chìm‐chuê shaan‐lĭng‐chì‐téng‐chi‐ch‘uè: chì‐ue neĭ king‐haakₒ‐yān‐kè sz‐ts‘īng, neĭ sam‐kiu‐ngô, tsaû hei‐p‘ìn tsẑ‐keí, sui‐īn on‐chuê neĭ‐kè ch‘aaū, hó‐ts‘z̆ mā‐ying kòm‐ko, ngŏh pit‐ts‘ūng kòh‐ch‘uè laai‐neĭ lōk‐laī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
17 Ĭ‐tung pit‐shīng‐waī king‐hoī‐chi‐māt: taân‐faān king‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐kè tsaû king‐k‘eī, yaû heùng k‘eī‐chung‐kè tsoi‐wôh‐faatₒ‐ch‘i‐sheng.
18 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘ing‐paaî Shóh‐toh‐mă k‘āp Ngōh‐moh‐la, kûng‐maaī kaakₒ‐lūn‐kè shēng‐yap, tsaû mŏ‐yān chuê‐tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè, tseung‐loī to‐mŏ‐yān‐kè tsaí tsoî k‘eī‐chung keì‐kui.
19 T‘aí‐a, k‘uĭ ts‘ūng Yeukₒ‐taân shing‐ko kòh‐ch‘uè, hó‐ts‘z̆ sz‐tsź, ooĭ‐sheŭng‐laī kung‐kik k‘uĭ kin‐koò‐kè chuê‐shóh: taân‐haî ngŏh fat‐īn‐kaan ooĭ‐lîng k‘uĭ tsaú‐huì leī‐hoi: taân‐faān peî‐kaán‐suén‐kè, ngŏh tsaû ch‘itₒ‐laāp kòh‐yān koón‐hāt k‘uĭ, yan‐waî yaŭ mat‐shuī hó‐ts‘z̆ ngŏh ni? mat‐shuī ooĭ‐waî ngŏh tîng‐tuēt yāt‐k‘eī ni? k‘eĭ‐tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn‐kè mūk‐yān, haî mat‐shuī ni?
20 Shóh‐ĭ neĭ‐teî iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐maū‐tîng kóng‐heí kung‐kik Ĭ‐tung‐kè sẑ; uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ tîng‐ì‐kè sẑ, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ shóh‐tîng‐tuēt kung‐kik T‘aī‐maân‐kè kui‐mān: Yat‐tîng‐iù laai‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐huì, tsik‐haî kw‘ān‐chùng‐chi‐shiù‐nuên kè: yat‐tîng līn‐maaī k‘uĭ‐teî, lîng k‘uĭ chuê‐shóh, pìn‐waī fong‐faì kè.
21 Yan k‘uĭ k‘ing‐titₒ‐kè sheng, teî‐k‘aū to‐chàn‐tûng, yaû‐yaŭ haàm‐foó‐kè sheng, tò Hūng‐hoí to t‘eng‐mān lokₒ.
22 T‘aí‐a, k‘uĭ pit‐sheŭng‐laī, hó‐ts‘z̆ mā‐ying kóm‐yeûng fei‐laī, yaû ch‘aang‐hoi k‘uĭ‐kè yīk, kung‐kik P‘òh‐sz‐la: tong‐kòh‐yāt, shūk Ĭ‐tung yŭng‐sẑ‐kè sam, shīng‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ foŏ‐yān t‘ùng‐foó‐chi‐shī‐kè sam.
23 ¶ Kwaan‐ue Taaî‐mă‐shik‐chi‐sẑ. Hap‐moōt kûng À‐paatₒ kìn‐sau‐kw‘aì lokₒ: yan‐waî k‘uĭ t‘eng‐mān tsoi‐wôh‐kè siu‐sik: tsaû yūng‐fà laū‐huì; hoí‐sheûng yaŭ yau‐luî‐chi‐sẑ; pat‐nāng‐tak‐on‐tsîng.
24 Taaî‐mă‐shik pìn‐waī mŏ‐līk‐kè, tsẑ‐chuèn t‘ō‐tsaú, chìn‐lūt chukₒ‐chuê k‘uĭ: t‘ùng‐foó kûng yau‐shaū chap‐chuê k‘uĭ, hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘uī‐lō‐kè foŏ‐yān kóm.
25 Shaû‐tsaàn‐meĭ‐kè shēng, ngŏh faaì‐lōk‐kè yap, tím‐yeûng m̄‐kìn waī‐heì ni!
26 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Koò‐ts‘ź tong‐kòh‐yāt, k‘uĭ nīn‐shiù‐kè yān, tsoî‐kaai‐chung ooĭ‐pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè, taaî‐chùng mŏ‐sẑ, to‐ooĭ‐suk‐tsîng m̄‐ch‘ut‐sheng.
27 Ngŏh iù‐faatₒ‐fóh tsoî Taaî‐mă‐shik shēng‐ts‘eūng‐ch‘uè, tsaû t‘an‐mīt Pîn‐hap‐taāt‐kè kung‐shat.
28 ¶ Kwaan‐ue Kei‐taāt kûng Hâ‐shokₒ‐kè chùng‐kwokₒ, tsik‐haî Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ shóh‐kung‐kik‐kè, ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Neĭ‐iù heí‐ch‘īng, sheŭng‐huì‐tò Kei‐taāt, yaû paaî‐waaî tung‐fong‐kè chùng‐yān.
29 K‘uĭ‐teî‐kè waī‐mōk kûng yeūng‐kw‘ān iù‐ts‘uí‐huì: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐waî tsẑ‐keí taaì‐huì cheùng‐līm, kûng yat‐ts‘aī‐kè heì‐mĭng, t‘ūng‐maaī lokₒ‐t‘ōh; yaû heùng k‘uĭ‐teî kiù‐sheng wâ, Sz̀‐waī yaŭ king‐haakₒ‐kè à.
30 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Hâ‐shokₒ‐chi‐kui‐mān à, iù‐t‘ō‐tsaú hāng hó‐uĕn, chuê‐lōk sham‐māt‐ch‘uè; yan‐waî Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ ĭ‐king maū‐tîng hoî neĭ, yaû laāp‐shāt chué‐ì kung‐kik neĭ.
31 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Iù heí‐ch‘īng‐huì‐tò yat‐kòh on‐tsîng mŏ‐kwà‐luî kui‐chuê‐kè kwokₒ, tsik‐haî mŏ‐moōn, mŏ‐waāng‐aapₒ, tūk‐tsẑ kui‐chuê‐kè.
32 K‘uĭ‐kè lokₒ‐t‘ōh, shīng‐waī ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐chi‐māt, k‘uĭ‐kè ch‘uk‐shang, taaî‐toh to‐shīng‐waī leūk‐māt: kòh‐ti t‘aū‐kokₒ tsín‐faatₒ‐kè yān, ngŏh pit‐heùng chùng‐fung fan‐saàn k‘uĭ‐teî; yaû ts‘ūng sz̀‐waī lîng wôh‐waân kòng‐lām‐ue k‘uĭ‐teî, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
33 Hâ‐shokₒ pit‐shīng‐waī ch‘aaī‐kaú‐kè chuê‐shóh, wĭng‐uĕn tsô‐fong‐faì‐kè: tsúng‐mŏ‐yān‐kè tsaí tsoî‐k‘eī‐chung keì‐kui lokₒ.
34 ¶ Tong Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Sai‐taí‐ka heí‐ch‘oh tsô‐wōng kòh‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lûn‐k‘āp Ĭ‐laān, shóh‐kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐kè īn‐uĕ, wâ,
35 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh‐pit‐waí‐ch‘aakₒ Ĭ‐laān kòh‐pá‐kung, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ nāng‐līk‐kè t‘aū‐mūk.
36 Ngŏh yīk‐tseung t‘in‐kè sz̀‐pîn fung‐ch‘ui‐laī, yaû heùng ni‐ti chùng‐fung fan‐saàn k‘uĭ‐teî; ī‐ch‘é pit‐mŏ‐yat‐kwokₒ m̄‐tak‐yaū Ĭ‐laān chūk‐ch‘ut‐kè yān laī‐tò kòh‐ch‘uè.
37 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh to ooĭ‐lîng Ĭ‐laān‐yān tsoî ch‘aū‐tīk mîn‐ts‘īn, yaû tsoî chaaú‐ts‘ām‐k‘uĭ‐shang‐mîng‐kè yān mîn‐ts‘īn king‐tûng: ngŏh yaû lîng tsoi‐wôh tsik‐haî ngŏh‐kè līt‐nô, kòng‐lām‐ue k‘uĭ‐teî; ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐shaí to‐kìm chui‐kón k‘uĭ‐teî, táng‐tò ngŏh tsiú‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî:
38 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh iù ch‘itₒ‐laāp ngŏh ts‘ŏh‐waî tsoî Ĭ‐laān, ts‘ūng kòh‐ch‘uè paaî‐waaî kòh‐wōng kûng kwan‐cheúng à.
39 Waī‐haî tò‐moōt‐shaì‐kè yāt‐tsź, pit‐haî‐kóm, ngŏh tsaû lîng Ĭ‐laān peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān faan‐laī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
