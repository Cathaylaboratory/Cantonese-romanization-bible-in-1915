1 TONG Hei‐lūt wōng kòh‐shī, Yē‐So keì‐haí Yaū‐t‘aaì Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng ch‘ut‐shaì, yaŭ keí‐waî pokₒ‐sẑ, haí tung‐pîn laī‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng,
2 Wâ, Shang‐ch‘ut‐laī tsô Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐Wōng‐kè, haí pin‐ch‘uè ni? yan ngŏh‐teî haí tung‐pîn, mông‐kìn k‘uĭ nap‐sing, koò‐ts‘ź laī‐paaì k‘uĭ.
3 Hei‐lūt‐wōng t‘eng‐mān, tsaû fong‐heí‐laī, t‘ung Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng to‐haî.
4 Kóm‐tsaû kiù‐ts‘aī chùng‐tsaì‐sz‐cheúng, t‘ūng mān‐kaan tūk‐shue‐yān laī‐mân, wâ, Kei‐Tuk ying‐koi haí pin‐ch‘uè ch‘ut‐shaì ni?
5 Chùng‐yān tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Haí Yaū‐t‘aaì Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng: yan‐waî sin‐chi yaŭ sé‐lōk kóm‐wâ,
6 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐teî Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng à, neĭ haí Yaū‐t‘aaì foó‐uên‐chung, m̄‐haî chì‐siú‐kè: yan‐waî tseung‐loī yaŭ kòh Kwan‐Wōng, haí neĭ‐ch‘uè ch‘ut, laī pó‐yeŭng ngŏh Ĭ‐shik‐līt paakₒ‐sìng‐kè.
7 Ue‐shî, Hei‐lūt tsîng‐tsĭng kiù kòh‐ti‐pokₒ‐sẑ laī, saì‐mân kòh‐nap‐sing, haî keí‐shī în‐ch‘ut.
8 Tsaû tá‐faatₒ‐k‘uĭ‐teî huì Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng, wâ, Neĭ huì lōk‐līk ch‘ā‐mân kòh‐saì‐man‐tsaí; wán‐cheūk, tsaû wâ‐kwòh‐ngŏh chi, táng ngŏh yīk‐huì paaì k‘uĭ.
9 Pokₒ‐sẑ t‘eng‐kìn wōng shóh‐kóng, tsaû heí‐hāng‐huì lokₒ; fat‐īn tsoî tung‐pîn shóh‐kìn kòh‐nap‐sing, haí ts‘īn‐t‘aū yăn‐k‘uĭ‐teî, yăn‐tò kòh‐saì‐man‐tsaí shóh‐chuê kòh‐ch‘uè, tsaû chí‐tsoî k‘uĭ‐sheûng‐t‘aū.
10 Pokₒ‐sẑ t‘aí‐kìn‐kòh nap‐sing, foon‐heí tò liŭ‐pat‐tak.
11 Yāp‐tò uk‐luĭ, kìn saì‐man‐tsaí, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an Mă‐leî‐à, tsaû kwaî‐tó paaì kòh‐saì‐man‐tsaí: tá‐hoi k‘uĭ‐kè pó‐hōp, k‘aaĭ laĭ‐māt hìn‐sheŭng; tsik‐haî wōng‐kam, uĕ‐heung, moōt‐yeūk.
12 Pokₒ‐sẑ haí mûng‐chung tak māk‐shî, kiù‐maĭ faan‐huì Hei‐lūt‐ch‘uè, tsaû haí pīt‐t‘iū‐lô faan‐kwai poón‐t‘ó.
13 ¶ K‘uĭ‐teî huì‐chi‐haû, Chué‐kè sz̀‐ché, tsoî mûng‐chung hín‐în‐kwòh‐Yeukₒ‐shat, wâ, Heí‐shan, taaì kòh‐saì‐man‐tsaí, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an, tsaú‐huì Oi‐k‘āp, haí kòh‐ch‘uè táng ngŏh chí‐tím neĭ: yan‐waî Hei‐lūt tseung‐iù chaaú‐ts‘ām kòh‐saì‐man‐tsaí laī‐shaatₒ‐k‘uĭ.
14 Yeukₒ‐shat tsik‐shī heí‐shan, taaì kòh‐saì‐man‐tsaí, t‘ūng k‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an, laû‐yê tsaú‐huì Oi‐k‘āp.
15 Haí‐kòh‐ch‘uè, chì‐tò Hei‐lūt sź‐hiu, kóm‐tsaû yìng‐hiu Chué t‘okₒ sin‐chi‐shóh‐kóng, wâ, Ĭ‐king kiù ngŏh Tsź ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp lokₒ.
16 Kòh‐chân, Hei‐lūt keì chi‐tò peî pokₒ‐sẑ hei‐p‘ìn‐tó, tsaû kīk‐nau, shaí yān tseung Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng, uĕ‐k‘āp kòh‐ch‘uè foô‐kân‐kè teî‐fong, shóh‐yaŭ saì‐man‐tsaí, i‐chuê saì‐mân pokₒ‐sẑ kòh‐shī, taân‐faān tsẑ‐leŭng‐suì‐ĭ‐hâ‐kè, tsûn‐teî shaatₒ‐saaì.
17 Kóm‐tsaû yìng‐hiu sin‐chi Yē‐leî‐maĭ shóh‐kóng, wâ.
18 Haí La‐mă teî‐fong, t‘eng‐mān kiù‐haàm sham‐sheung‐kè sheng, La‐kitₒ‐shî haàm k‘uĭ tsaí, yan mŏ‐tak laū‐ts‘uēn, shóh‐ĭ m̄‐shaû on‐waì.
19 Hei‐lūt sź‐hiu, Chué‐kè sz̀‐ché, haí Oi‐k‘āp ch‘uè, tsoî mûng‐chung hín‐în‐kwòh‐Yeukₒ‐shat,
20 Wâ, Heí‐shan, taaì kòh‐saì‐man‐tsaí, t‘ūng k‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an, huì Ĭ‐shik‐līt teî‐fong, yan kòh‐ti chaaú‐ts‘ām saì‐man‐tsaí sìng‐mîng‐kè, ĭ‐king sź lokₒ.
21 K‘uĭ tsaû heí‐shan, taaì kòh‐saì‐man‐tsaí, t‘ūng k‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an, laī‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt teî‐fong.
22 Taân‐haî mān‐tak À‐kei‐lŏ, haí Yaū‐t‘aaì kaì‐chuê foô‐ts‘an Hei‐lūt tsô wōng, tsaû p‘à huì kòh‐ch‘uè, waī‐haî tsoî‐mûng‐chung tak‐māk‐shî, koò‐ts‘ź faan‐huì Ka‐leî‐leî kíng‐teî.
23 Tò yat‐kòh shēng, mēng‐kiù Nā‐saatₒ‐lāk, tsaû haí‐ch‘uè kui‐chuê, kóm‐tsaû yìng‐hiu chùng‐sin‐chi shóh‐kóng, Yān‐teî tseung‐iù kiù k‘uĭ tsô Nā‐saatₒ‐lāk‐yān lokₒ.
