1 NEĬ‐TEÎ tong‐ch‘eùng shaú‐san‐shi; koh‐tsûng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; yan k‘uĭ ĭ‐king tsô‐kwòh k‘eī‐sẑ: yûng yaû‐shaú kûng shìng‐peì, ĭ‐king ch‘íng‐kaù kwòh‐yān.
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yă‐ts‘āng tseung‐kaù‐yan suen‐kóng‐ch‘ut‐laī: tong līt‐pong‐yān ngaăn‐ts‘īn, ts‘āng hín‐ch‘ut k‘uĭ‐chi‐kung‐î.
3 K‘uĭ yīk keì‐nîm shi‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐chi‐ts‘z̄‐pei, uĕ‐k‘āp chan‐shāt: teî‐chi‐sz̀‐kīk‐kè yān, to kìn‐kwòh ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì chi‐kaù‐yan.
4 P‘ó‐t‘in‐hâ‐yān, to tong heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā foon‐sheng foo‐kiù: faatₒ‐sheng tsokₒ‐ngōk laī‐koh‐tsûng.
5 T‘aān‐k‘ām laī‐ch‘ing‐tsaàn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; t‘aān‐k‘ām yaû wôh shi.
6 Ch‘ui‐kokₒ ch‘ui‐siu, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Taaî‐Kwan chi‐ts‘īn, laī‐foon‐heí tsaàn‐tsûng.
7 Taaî‐hoí kûng k‘eī‐chung‐chi‐maân‐māt; ts‘uēn‐teî kûng teî‐sheûng‐chi‐kui‐mān, to tong taaî‐sheng ch‘ing‐tsûng.
8 Tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ts‘īn, kong‐hōh ying‐tong p‘aakₒ‐cheúng: shaan‐lĭng ying‐iù foon‐kiù.
9 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ kòng‐lām shám‐p‘oòn t‘in‐hâ; píng‐kung‐î shám‐p‘oòn shaì‐kaaì: p‘āng chìng‐chīk shám‐p‘oòn līt‐kwokₒ.
