1 IÙ chai‐hô‐t‘ūng yāp‐neĭ‐kè haú la. K‘uĭ laī‐kung‐tá Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, hó‐ts‘z̆‐mā‐ying‐kóm, yan‐waî‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐pooì‐yīk ngŏh‐kè yeukₒ, yaû‐waī‐faân ngŏh‐kè lūt‐faatₒ à.
2 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tuì‐ngŏh haàm‐foó wâ, Ngŏh‐kè Sheûng‐Taì à, ngŏh‐teî Ĭ‐shik‐līt shik‐tak‐neĭ à.
3 Ĭ‐shik‐līt ĭ‐king‐tiu‐heì shîn‐leūng‐kè: ch‘aū‐tīk pit‐chui‐kón‐k‘uĭ lokₒ.
4 K‘uĭ‐teî ts‘āng‐ch‘itₒ‐laāp kwan wōng, waī‐haî m̄‐tsīk‐laaî‐ngŏh‐kè: yaû kûng‐laāp‐kwan‐cheúng, waī‐haî‐ngŏh m̄‐chi‐tò‐kè: yaû k‘aaĭ‐ngān‐kûng‐kam waî‐tsẑ‐keí‐tsokₒ‐shīng ngaŭ‐tseûng, táng‐k‘uĭ hóh‐ĭ t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐kè lokₒ.
5 Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à à, k‘uĭ ts‘āng‐tiu‐heì neĭ‐kè ngaū‐tsaí: ngŏh‐kè nau‐nô heùng‐k‘uĭ‐teî shiu‐cheūk, chûng‐iù‐keí‐noî: chì‐k‘uĭ‐teî tak‐tó‐mō‐tsuî‐chi‐teî‐waî ni?
6 Yan‐waî ni‐ti to‐yaū Ĭ‐shik‐līt ch‘ut‐kè: haî kûng‐yān‐tsokₒ‐shīng‐kè; m̄‐shīng‐tak‐waī Sheûng‐Taì: ī‐ch‘é Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐kè kam‐ngaū, pit‐peî‐chaakₒ‐suì.
7 K‘uĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ‐fung saatₒ‐chúng‐kè, tsaû pit‐shau‐shīng suên‐fung à: k‘uĭ kíng‐īn‐mŏ‐wōh: kòh‐ti‐yeung m̄‐kitₒ‐kuk‐shīng‐fán: uē‐kwóh‐yaŭ‐kitₒ‐shāt‐kè, ngoî‐yān ooĭ‐laī‐t‘an‐shīk à.
8 Ĭ‐shik‐līt kìn‐t‘an‐mīt: k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî‐līt‐mān‐chi‐chung, hó‐ts‘z̆ m̄‐lōk‐ì‐kè heì‐mĭng kóm.
9 K‘uĭ‐teî sheŭng‐huì À‐shūt‐ch‘uè, hó‐ts‘z̆‐yĕ‐luī, taan‐tūk‐tsẑ‐keí‐kè kóm: Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn tsẑ‐ts‘éng‐koò‐kung‐kè ts‘īng‐lōng.
10 K‘uĭ‐teî sui‐īn haí‐līt‐mān‐chi‐chung, ts‘éng‐koò‐kung‐kè, ngŏh uē‐kam ooĭ‐tsuî‐maaī‐k‘uĭ‐teî, yaû yan‐kwan‐cheúng‐chi‐wōng‐kè ch‘ŭng‐yâm, k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû heí‐shaú kaám‐shiú lokₒ.
11 Yan‐waî Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn ka‐toh‐tsôh‐t‘aān laī‐faân‐tsuî, kóh‐ti‐t‘aān heùng‐k‘uĭ shīng‐waī‐tsuî‐okₒ.
12 Ngŏh sui‐īn waî‐k‘uĭ tseung‐ngŏh‐lūt‐faatₒ sé‐ch‘ut‐yat‐maân‐kw‘ai‐t‘iū, to‐suèn‐haî k‘eī‐î‐kè sẑ che.
13 Chì‐ue ngŏh‐laĭ‐māt‐kè tsaì‐tsẑ à, k‘uĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ‐ngaū‐yūk hìn‐tsaì, yaû ning‐huì‐laī‐shīk‐kè; waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā m̄‐tsipₒ‐naāp‐k‘uĭ‐teî; tò‐uē‐kam tsaû keì‐nîm k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè hin‐yaū, yaû kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ‐pò‐yìng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsuî‐okₒ: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐m̄‐faan‐ooī Oi‐k‘āp lokₒ.
14 Ĭ‐shik‐līt mōng‐keì‐hiu k‘uĭ‐Tsô‐Fà‐Kè Chué, yaû kìn‐heí kung‐shat; Yaū‐t‘aaì yaû ka‐toh‐yaŭ‐pó‐cheùng‐kè shēng‐yap: waī‐haî ngŏh shaí‐fóh‐lōk‐k‘uĭ‐kè shēng‐yap, laī‐t‘an‐mīt‐k‘uĭ‐kè pó‐cheùng à.
