1 SHEÛNG‐CHUÉ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè Shān kòng‐lām‐ue‐ngŏh; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘aaĭ‐ko mootₒ‐ngŏh, heùng wan‐yaū‐kè yān suen‐ch‘uēn ka‐yam; yaû tá‐faatₒ ngŏh chaatₒ‐hó‐sheung‐sam‐kè, kóng‐mīng shaû lŏ‐leūk‐kè chi, hóh‐ĭ tak‐shik‐fòng, tuì peî‐póng‐chuê‐kè wâ, Kaam‐fōng ĭ‐king hoi‐hiu lokₒ;
2 Yaû suen‐ch‘uēn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hóh‐tsipₒ‐naāp‐kè nīn, uĕ‐k‘āp ngŏh‐kè Sheûng‐Taì pò‐ch‘aū‐chi‐yāt; yaû on‐waì taân‐faān taam‐yau‐kè;
3 Yeukₒ‐tîng haí Sun‐shēng taam‐yau‐kè yān, peí k‘uĭ tak yat‐téng‐fa‐tsô‐kè mô t‘aì‐toî naī‐fooi, tak faaì‐lōk‐kè ko t‘aì‐toî yau‐shaū, tak‐tsaàn‐meĭ‐kè i‐fūk t‘aì‐toî paì‐aì‐kè sam‐shān; táng k‘uĭ hóh‐ĭ tak‐ch‘ing‐waī‐kung‐î‐kè shuê‐mūk, haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐chùng‐kè, lîng k‘uĭ hóh‐ĭ tak‐wīng‐kwong à.
4 K‘uĭ‐teî ooĭ‐kìn‐heí kaû‐shī fong‐faì‐chi‐ch‘uè, yaû hing‐heí ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn shaû ts‘aān‐hoî‐kè, yaû sau‐chíng fong‐faì‐kè shēng‐yap, līk‐toî shóh‐ts‘aān‐hoî‐chi‐ch‘uè.
5 Ngoî‐pong‐kè yān ooĭ‐k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè yeŭng‐yūk neĭ‐kè kw‘ān‐yeūng, waî neĭ kaang‐t‘īn, waî neĭ sau‐leĭ p‘ō‐t‘aī‐shuê.
6 Waī‐haî neĭ pit‐tak‐ch‘ing‐waī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tsaì‐sz: chùng‐yān yīk‐ch‘ing‐neĭ waī ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shān‐pūk: neĭ‐teî ooĭ shīk Līt‐kwokₒ‐kè ts‘oī‐māt, yaû tak k‘uĭ wīng‐wā laī‐tsẑ‐kw‘a‐kè.
7 Yan neĭ shóh‐shaû‐kè līng‐yūk; tsaû ka‐sheung‐p‘ooĭ peí neĭ, yaû yan shaû king‐luên, k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî shóh‐tak‐kòh‐fân‐īp faaì‐lōk: shóh‐ĭ tsoî kíng‐teî‐chi‐noî, k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tak‐tó sheung‐p‘ooĭ: wĭng‐uĕn‐kè faaì‐oōt, to‐kwai‐ue k‘uĭ‐teî lokₒ.
8 Yan‐waî ngŏh tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā oì kung‐î‐kè shám‐p‘oòn, yaû tsang‐oò tsuî‐hin k‘eūng‐ts‘eúng; k‘uĭ pit tsīk chan‐shāt laī‐pò‐yìng, yaû kûng k‘uĭ‐teî laāp wĭng‐uĕn‐kè yeukₒ.
9 K‘uĭ‐teî‐kè haû‐yuî, tsoî Līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung, k‘uĭ‐teî shóh‐shang‐kè, tsoî līt‐mān‐chi‐chung: to peî‐yān shik‐tak: shóh‐yaŭ t‘aí‐kìn‐kè, tsaû yîng‐shāt k‘uĭ‐teî haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐chuk‐fuk‐kè haû‐yuî à.
10 Ngŏh tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ch‘uè ooĭ‐lōk‐ì shâm‐taaî, ngŏh līng‐wān haí ngŏh‐kè Sheûng‐Taì‐ch‘uè ooĭ‐faaì‐oōt; yan‐waî k‘uĭ ts‘āng k‘aaĭ tak‐kaù‐kè i‐sheūng cheukₒ‐lōk‐ngŏh, peí kung‐î‐kè laĭ‐fūk che‐k‘oì ngŏh, hó‐ts‘z̆ san‐lōng tsẑ‐taaì yat‐téng fa‐tsô‐kè mô, yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ san‐foŏ k‘aaĭ chan‐pó laī‐chong‐shik tsẑ‐keí kóm.
11 Hó‐ts‘z̆ teî‐sheûng faatₒ‐ngā, hó‐ts‘z̆ uēn‐chung‐kè chúng shang‐heí kóm‐yeûng; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit lîng kung‐î tsûng‐yeūng tsoî maân‐kwokₒ‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn shang‐heí‐laī à.
