1 TSOÎ Maĭ‐t‘aaì‐yān Taaî‐leî‐oo‐kè uēn‐nīn, ngŏh keì‐heí‐laī, lîng‐k‘uĭ‐kin‐koò kong‐k‘eūng.
2 ¶ Uē‐kam ngŏh tseung chan‐shāt‐kè sẑ chí‐shî‐neĭ. T‘aí‐a, Pa‐sai chûng‐yaŭ saam‐kòh‐wōng hing‐heí‐kìn‐laāp; kòh‐taî‐sz̀‐kè tseung‐ooĭ‐foò‐haû‐chi‐kīk, shìng‐kwòh taaî‐chùng: tsaû‐k‘aaĭ‐foò‐haû shīng‐waī‐k‘eūng, tsaû kaaú‐iŭ taaî‐chùng heí‐laī, kung‐kik Ngă‐faan‐kè kwokₒ.
3 Pit‐yaŭ yat‐kòh‐yŭng‐līk‐kè wōng hing‐heí‐kìn‐laāp, k‘aaĭ‐taaî k‘uēn‐laī chî‐leĭ; ī‐ch‘é yâm‐ì‐laī‐tsô.
4 K‘uĭ hing‐heí‐kìn‐laāp‐chi‐shī, tsaû k‘uĭ kwokₒ pit‐tîng‐shaû‐chitₒ laī‐fan‐saàn heùng t‘in‐kè sz̀‐fung; taân‐m̄‐kwai k‘uĭ miū‐yuî, yīk‐m̄‐shûn‐chuê k‘uĭ‐shóh‐cha‐kè k‘uēn laī‐fan‐hoi: yan‐waî k‘uĭ kwokₒ pit‐pāt‐heí kwai‐ue‐pīt‐yān, tsik‐haî ni‐ti‐taaî‐chùng‐chi‐ngoî‐kè à.
5 Naām‐fong‐kè wōng, pit‐tîng k‘eūng‐chòng, taân‐k‘uĭ yān‐kwan‐chi‐chung; yat‐kòh pit‐k‘eūng‐chòng‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ, tsaû tak‐tó‐kòh‐k‘uēn; ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐k‘uēn tseung‐shīng‐waī‐taaî‐k‘uēn.
6 Kwòh‐keí‐nīn‐chi‐haû, taaî‐ka ooĭ‐kaau‐kitₒ; naām‐fong‐kòh‐wōng‐kè nuĭ, tseung‐ts‘an‐tsaû pak‐fong‐kè wōng laī‐laāp‐wōh‐yeukₒ: taân‐haî ts‘ź‐nuĭ shóh‐í‐laaî‐pong‐chôh‐k‘uĭ‐k‘uēn, m̄‐tak‐laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè; kòh‐wōng kûng‐k‘uĭ‐shóh‐í‐laaî‐kè, to‐pat‐nāng k‘eĭ‐laāp: ī‐ch‘é ni‐kòh‐nuĭ kûng‐taaì‐k‘uĭ‐laī‐kè, k‘āp shang‐k‘uĭ‐kè, t‘ūng‐maaī tong‐shī foō‐k‘uĭ‐kè, to‐pit‐tîng peî‐chukₒ‐ch‘ut‐lokₒ.
7 Waī‐haî ni‐kòh‐nuĭ‐chi‐kan shóh‐ch‘ut‐kè chi, pit‐yaŭ yat‐kòh tsoî‐wōng‐kè poón‐waî k‘eĭ‐laāp, k‘uĭ‐pit‐laī‐tò yat‐taaî‐tuî‐ping; ī‐ch‘é yāp‐pak‐fong kòh‐wōng‐kè pó‐cheùng, kung‐kik‐k‘uĭ‐teî, yaû tá‐shìng‐k‘uĭ‐t‘im:
8 Kim‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shān, līn‐maaī‐chuè‐kè tseûng, uĕ‐k‘āp‐ti‐ngān‐kûng‐kam‐kè pó‐heì, t‘ung‐to‐lŏ‐leūk‐huì Oi‐k‘āp; īn‐haû‐leī‐hoi‐pak‐fong‐kòh‐wōng keí‐nīn‐kòm‐noî.
9 Ue‐shî yāp‐naām‐fong kòh‐wōng‐kè kwokₒ, tsaû‐faan‐huì‐tsz̀‐keí‐poón‐t‘ó.
10 Chì‐ue‐k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsaí, tseung‐ooĭ‐tá‐cheùng, tsuî‐maaī‐taaî‐tuî‐ping: k‘uĭ‐ts‘īn‐tsùn‐moŏn‐teî‐t‘ung‐hāng: haû‐loī tsoì‐faan‐laī‐tá‐cheùng, chīk‐tò k‘uĭ‐kè pó‐cheùng.
11 Kóm‐tsaû naām‐fong‐kè wōng, tseung‐ooĭ‐faatₒ‐nô‐kīk‐līt, pit‐tîng‐ch‘ut‐laī, kûng‐pak‐fong‐kè wōng tá‐cheùng: ī‐ch‘é ooĭ‐k‘aaĭ‐taaî‐tuî‐ping p‘aaī‐hoi; ni‐ti‐taaî‐tuî‐ping, pit‐tîng kaau‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐shaú.
12 Taaî‐chùng‐pit‐peî‐ch‘uē‐mīt, haû‐loī k‘uĭ‐sam‐tsẑ‐ko; k‘uĭ‐ooĭ‐lîng‐maân‐shò‐yān kwaàn‐tó: taân‐haî pat‐nāng‐tak‐shìng.
13 Pak‐fong‐kè wōng, ooĭ‐faan‐laī, yīk‐ooĭ‐p‘aaī‐hoi taaî‐tuî‐ping, peí‐chi ts‘īn‐shī kàng‐taaî, moŏn‐hiu‐nīn‐shò, pit‐tîng taaì‐taaî‐tuî‐ping, kûng‐fung‐shîng‐kè tsz‐ts‘oī‐kóm‐laī.
14 Tsoî‐kòh‐chân‐shī, pit‐yaŭ‐hó‐toh‐yān k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè kung‐tá naām‐fong‐kè wōng: ī‐ch‘é paakₒ‐sìng ti‐k‘eūng‐pô‐kè, to‐ooĭ‐hing‐heí, seúng‐shīng‐tsaû‐î‐tseûng‐kè î‐sẑ; waī‐haî‐k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tîng‐kwaàn‐tó.
15 Kóm‐tsaû pak‐fong‐kè wōng, ooĭ‐laī ch‘itₒ‐laāp‐yīng‐luĭ, yaû ts‘uí‐kin‐koò‐kè shēng: waī‐haî naām‐fong‐kè taaî‐tuî‐ping, pit‐pat‐nāng taí‐tóng‐k‘uĭ, kaán‐suén‐kè ping, to‐pit‐haî tsúng‐mŏ‐līk‐nāng taí‐tóng.
16 Waī‐haî laī‐kung‐tá‐k‘uĭ‐kè, ooĭ‐yâm‐ì laī‐hāng, mŏ‐yān ooĭ‐k‘eĭ‐laāp haí‐k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn: k‘uĭ tseung‐haí‐meĭ‐teî‐k‘eĭ‐laāp, haí‐k‘uĭ‐shaú‐hâ pit‐mŏ‐ts‘aān‐hoî,
17 Pit‐tîng yat‐mîn k‘aaĭ‐ts‘uēn‐kwokₒ‐kè līk, uĕ‐k‘āp chìng‐chīk‐kè yān laī: yaû tseung‐k‘aaĭ‐foŏ‐yān‐kè nuĭ peí‐k‘uĭ, ì‐sz̀ seúng‐paaî‐waaî‐k‘uĭ: taân‐ni‐ti‐pit‐m̄‐tak‐laāp, to‐m̄‐tak yan‐waî k‘uĭ shīng‐tsaû.
18 Tsẑ‐ts‘ź‐chi‐haû, k‘uĭ ooĭ‐tiû‐chuèn‐mîn heùng‐kokₒ‐hoí‐tó, yaû‐ooĭ‐toh‐ts‘uí k‘uĭ: taân‐pit‐yaŭ yat‐kòh‐yān‐kwan, tseung‐k‘uĭ‐shóh‐shi‐kè līng‐yūk‐ch‘uē‐huì; yaû ooĭ‐lîng‐k‘uĭ līng‐yūk faan‐chuèn kwai‐ue‐tsẑ‐keí à.
19 Ue‐shî ooĭ‐tiû‐chuèn‐mîn, heùng‐tsẑ‐keí kíng‐noî‐kè pó‐cheùng: taân‐pit‐tîng titₒ‐tó kwaàn‐cheūk, haû‐loī wán‐k‘uĭ‐to‐m̄‐kìn.
20 Ue‐shî yaŭ‐yat‐kòh kaì‐k‘uĭ‐laī kìn‐laāp, tseung‐ooĭ‐lîng hoh‐hak‐kè laī‐shau‐heúng‐kè, hāng‐t‘ung‐kwokₒ‐kè wīng‐iû: waī‐haî keí‐yāt‐chi‐kaan, pit‐peî‐tá‐paaî, taân‐m̄‐haî k‘aaĭ‐nau‐nô, yīk‐m̄‐haî k‘aaĭ‐tá‐cheùng à.
21 Yaû‐yaŭ yat‐kòh hâ‐laū‐kè yān, kaì‐k‘uĭ‐laī‐kìn‐laāp, taaî‐chùng m̄‐ts‘āng k‘aaĭ‐kwokₒ‐kè tsuen‐wīng peí‐k‘uĭ: waī‐haî k‘uĭ tong t‘aaì‐p‘īng‐chi‐shī, k‘aaĭ‐ch‘ím‐meî‐shuetₒ‐wâ, laī‐tak‐k‘uĭ‐kwokₒ‐k‘uēn.
22 Taân‐haî chùng‐yān tsoî k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn peî‐ch‘uē‐mīt, hó‐ts‘z̆ taaî‐shuí‐ch‘uē‐mīt‐kóm; ī‐ch‘é wōh‐yeukₒ‐kè yān‐kwan‐to‐tsoî‐noî.
23 Kûng‐k‘uĭ‐laāp‐yeukₒ‐chi‐haû, k‘uĭ‐tseung ooĭ‐hāng‐chà‐ngaî: yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐tsaû sheŭng‐laī, k‘aaĭ‐se‐shiú tsź‐mān, shīng‐waī chì‐k‘eūng.
24 Tong‐t‘aaì‐p‘īng‐kè shī, k‘uĭ laī‐tò‐shaáng‐chung tsuì‐ko‐uē‐kòh‐ch‘uè; ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐līt‐tsó kûng‐k‘uĭ‐tsó‐foô‐chi‐tsó shóh‐meî‐ts‘āng‐hāng‐kwòh‐kè; k‘uĭ‐ooĭ‐hāng, tsik‐haî tá‐kipₒ‐k‘eūng‐ts‘eúng; ī‐ch‘é k‘aaĭ‐ts‘īn‐ts‘oī saàn‐kwòh‐taaî‐chùng: k‘uĭ‐yīk‐ooĭ‐ch‘itₒ‐kaì laī‐kung‐tá ti‐pó‐cheùng, chì‐tò moŏn‐k‘eī kòh‐shī à.
25 K‘uĭ tseung‐ooĭ‐kik‐faatₒ k‘uĭ‐kè līk, kûng‐maaī‐yŭng‐heì, k‘aaĭ‐taaî‐tuî‐ping laī‐kung‐tá naām‐fong‐kòh‐wōng; naām‐fong‐kòh‐wōng to‐k‘aaĭ‐hó‐taaî‐tuî ī‐ch‘é‐yaŭ‐poón‐sẑ‐kè ping, kûng‐k‘uĭ‐tá‐cheùng; taân‐pat‐nāng‐tong‐tak‐chuê: yan‐waî taaî‐chùng tseung‐ch‘itₒ‐kaì‐laī‐kung‐k‘uĭ à.
26 Shīk‐k‘uĭ‐tsẑ‐keí‐ti‐ngaaū‐chaân‐kè, tseung‐ooĭ‐chue‐mīt, k‘uĭ‐kòh‐taaî‐tuî‐ping, to‐ooĭ‐ooî saàn: ī‐ch‘é taaî‐toh ooĭ‐tó‐paî lokₒ.
27 Chì‐ue ni‐leŭng‐kòh‐wōng, yat‐sam ch‘itₒ‐kaì seúng‐maū‐hoî, t‘ūng‐tsīk‐kòh‐shī, tsaû‐kóng‐taaî‐wâ; taân‐shóh‐maū m̄‐tak‐shīng‐tsaû: yan‐waî tò‐shóh tîng‐kè yāt‐k‘eī, moōt‐chung‐pit‐tîng‐kòng‐lām.
28 K‘uĭ tsaû‐ooĭ‐taaì‐hó‐toh ts‘īn‐ts‘oī faan‐laī‐poón‐t‘ó; k‘uĭ‐sam ooĭ‐pooì‐yīk‐shìng‐yeukₒ: k‘uĭ tsaû‐ooĭ‐yâm‐ì‐laī‐hāng, tsaû‐faan‐huì‐k‘uĭ‐poón‐t‘ó.
29 Haí‐shóh‐tîng‐kè yāt‐k‘eī, k‘uĭ‐ooĭ‐tsoì‐faan‐laī‐tò‐naām‐fong‐ch‘uè; taân‐haî tsoî‐haû‐chi‐shī, pit‐m̄‐ts‘z̆ tsoî‐ts‘īn‐chi‐shī‐kè.
30 Yan‐waî Kei‐taí‐kè shuēn ooĭ‐laī‐tò kung‐tá‐k‘uĭ; shóh‐ĭ‐k‘uĭ‐ooĭ‐yau‐shaū, tsaû‐faan‐huì yīk‐pit‐ooĭ‐nau‐nô‐shìng‐yeukₒ: yaû‐ooĭ‐yâm‐ì‐laī‐tsô; to‐ooĭ‐faan‐laī, tsoì‐kûng‐pooì‐yīk‐shìng‐yeukₒ‐kè t‘ūng‐maū.
31 Tseung‐pit‐yaŭ‐taaî‐tuî‐ping‐kûng‐k‘uĭ‐k‘eĭ‐laāp, sitₒ‐tūk shìng‐shóh, tsik‐haî kin‐koò‐chi‐shóh, ch‘uē‐huì‐yāt‐yāt shóh‐hìn‐kè tsaì‐pán, laī‐chì‐laāp‐shi‐fong‐mō‐hóh‐tsang‐oò‐kè.
32 Waī‐yīk‐shìng‐yeukₒ‐kè, k‘uĭ‐ooĭ‐k‘aaĭ‐ch‘ím‐meî‐shuetₒ‐wâ, laī‐kaaú‐waaî‐k‘uĭ: waī‐haî shik‐tsẑ‐keí‐kè Sheûng‐Taì ti‐tsź‐mān, ooĭ‐faatₒ‐k‘eūng laī‐shīng‐tsaû‐k‘uĭ‐shóh‐waī à.
33 Mān‐kaan‐ti‐ts‘ung‐mīng‐kè, ooĭ‐kaaù‐fàn hó‐toh‐yān: waī‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî hó‐toh‐yāt, to‐pit‐peî‐kìm, waāk fóh, waāk lŏ‐leūk, waāk ts‘eúng‐kipₒ, laī‐k‘ing‐titₒ à.
34 K‘uĭ k‘ing‐titₒ‐kòh‐shī, to‐leūk‐leūk yaŭ‐ti‐pong‐chôh à: taân‐haî hó‐toh‐ooĭ‐kûng‐kòh‐ti‐kóng‐ch‘ím‐meî‐kè yān laī‐kitₒ‐tóng à.
35 Kòh‐ti ts‘ung‐mīng‐kè yān chi‐chung, to‐yaŭ‐ti ooĭ‐kwaàn‐tó, ĭ‐chì‐shì‐lîn‐k‘uĭ, chíng‐kitₒ‐k‘uĭ, chíng‐paāk‐k‘uĭ, chīk‐chì‐tò‐moōt‐yāt à: yan‐waî haí‐shóh‐tîng‐kè yāt‐k‘eī, pit‐tîng‐ooĭ‐tò.
36 Chì‐ue‐kòh‐wōng pit‐tîng yâm‐ì laī‐hāng; ooĭ‐tsẑ‐ko, tsẑ‐taaî, shìng‐kwòh‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè shān, yīk‐ooĭ‐k‘aaĭ‐fei‐sheūng‐kè king‐kw‘a laī‐kóng, kung‐kik‐maân‐shān‐kè Chué, k‘uĭ‐ooĭ‐tak‐tó‐leî‐taāt, táng‐tò nau‐nô‐kè sẑ ĭ‐king‐moŏn‐hiu: yan‐waî shóh‐tîng‐kè sẑ pit‐shīng‐tsaû là.
37 K‘uĭ pit‐m̄‐koò‐k‘uĭ‐līt‐tsó‐kè Shān, kûng‐maaī foŏ‐nuĭ ti‐k‘eī‐mô‐kè, mō‐lûn‐mat‐shān, k‘uĭ‐to‐m̄‐koò: yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐ooĭ‐tsẑ‐taaî‐ko‐kwòh‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè à.
38 Waī‐haî tsẑ‐keí‐paaî‐waaî, k‘uĭ‐tsaû‐ooĭ‐kìng pó‐cheùng‐kè Shān: ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐līt‐tsó‐m̄‐shik‐kè shān, k‘uĭ tseung‐k‘aaĭ‐kam, k‘aaĭ‐ngān, k‘aaĭ‐yūk‐shēk, k‘aaĭ‐pó‐māt‐laī‐kìng‐k‘uĭ.
39 K‘uĭ‐pit‐í‐laaî pīt‐î‐kè shān, laī‐pong‐chôh‐k‘uĭ, taân‐faān‐yîng‐k‘uĭ‐kè, k‘uĭ tsaû ooĭ‐k‘aaĭ‐wīng‐iû laī‐ka‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ: ī‐ch‘é lìng‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐koón‐hāt‐taaî‐chùng, k‘aaĭ‐t‘ó‐teî‐fan‐hoi‐laī‐tsô‐sheúng‐ts‘z̀.
40 Moōt‐chung‐kòh‐shī, naām‐fong‐kòh‐wōng ooĭ‐ch‘uk‐faân‐k‘uĭ: pak‐fong‐kòh‐wōng ooĭ‐kung‐kik‐k‘uĭ, hó‐ts‘z̆ faan‐taaî‐suên‐fung, k‘aaĭ‐ch‘e k‘aaĭ‐mă‐ping, k‘aaĭ‐hó‐toh‐shuēn‐chekₒ; yaû‐ooĭ‐yāp‐līt‐kwokₒ, tsaû‐moŏn‐teî‐hāng‐t‘ung.
41 To‐ooĭ‐yāp‐huì wā‐meĭ‐chi‐teî, k‘ing‐titₒ‐kè kwokₒ: yaŭ‐hó‐toh, taân‐haî nāng‐t‘uetₒ‐lat‐k‘uĭ‐shaú‐kè, tsik‐haî Ĭ‐tung Moh‐aapₒ, kûng‐maaī À‐moōn‐yān ti‐kwaì‐hín‐kè.
42 K‘uĭ to‐ooĭ‐shan‐shaú‐kung‐kik‐līt‐kwokₒ: chì‐ue Oi‐k‘āp‐teî to‐pat‐nāng‐mĭn‐k‘uĭ.
43 Tseung‐cha‐k‘uēn laī‐ts‘eúng‐maaī Oi‐k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ‐kam‐ngān‐kè ts‘oī‐foò, kûng‐maaī kokₒ‐yeûng pó‐māt: ī‐ch‘é Luĭ‐peí‐à‐yān, kûng Koó‐shāt‐yān, to‐ooĭ‐kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ‐keukₒ‐pô.
44 Taân‐haî tsẑ‐sai tsẑ‐tung, pit‐ooĭ‐yaŭ‐fung‐shing laī‐kaaú‐iŭ‐k‘uĭ; koò‐ts‘ź k‘uĭ ooĭ‐faatₒ‐nô‐tò‐kīk, tsaû‐ch‘ut‐laī seúng‐tsûn‐teî‐mīt‐tsuēt‐taaî‐chùng.
45 K‘uĭ‐tseung‐k‘aaĭ‐k‘uĭ‐wōng‐kung‐kè waī‐cheùng, ch‘ān‐ch‘itₒ‐tsoî‐hoí, kûng wā‐meĭ shìng‐shaan leŭng‐p‘ōng‐chi‐chung‐kaan; taân‐k‘uĭ‐pit‐chì‐tò‐moōt‐chung, to‐mŏ‐tak‐pong‐chôh‐k‘uĭ‐kè lokₒ.
