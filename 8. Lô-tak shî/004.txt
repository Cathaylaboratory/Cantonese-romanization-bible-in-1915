1 TONG‐SHĪ Poh‐sẑ huì‐tò shēng moōn ts‘ŏh‐tó ch‘uè: kong‐ngaam Poh‐sẑ shóh‐kóng tsô ts‘an‐shūk‐kè yān king‐kwòh; Poh‐sẑ wâ, Maŭ‐yān à! hāng‐maaī la, ni‐ch‘uè ts‘ŏh‐hă. Kòh‐yān tsaû maaī‐huì ts‘ŏh.
2 Poh‐sẑ yaû haí shūk shēng‐kè cheúng‐lŏ chi‐chung kaán‐chaāk shāp‐kòh yān, tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Ts‘éng neĭ‐teî ts‘ŏh‐tó ni‐ch‘uè, k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû ts‘ŏh lōk.
3 Poh‐sẑ tsaû tuì kòh‐tsô‐ts‘an‐shūk‐kè yān wâ, Ngŏh‐teî tsūk‐hing Ĭ‐leî‐maĭ‐līk kóh‐faaì‐t‘īn, uē‐kam ts‘ūng Moh‐aapₒ‐teî faan‐laī‐kè Nā‐oh‐maĭ iù ch‘ut maaî:
4 Ngŏh seúng tuì neĭ kóng, wâ, Neĭ háng haí ts‘ŏh‐tó ni‐ch‘uè‐kè yān mîn‐ts‘īn, k‘āp ngŏh mān‐kè cheúng‐lŏ mîn‐ts‘īn maaĭ kóh‐faaì t‘īn m̄‐ni? Yeūk neĭ háng shūk tsaû shūk: neĭ yeūk m̄‐háng shūk, tsaû wâ‐ngŏh‐chi: yan‐waî ch‘uē‐neĭ‐chi‐ngoî, k‘eī‐ts‘z̀ haî ngŏh; tsoì mŏ pin‐kòh hóh‐nāng shūk kóh‐faaì‐t‘īn‐kè. K‘uĭ taapₒ wâ, Ngŏh pit‐tîng shūk lokₒ.
5 Poh‐sẑ wâ, Neĭ yaū Nā‐oh‐maĭ shaú maaĭ kóh‐faaì‐t‘īn chi‐yāt, yīk iù yaū sź‐yān‐kè ts‘ai, Moh‐aapₒ‐nuĭ Lô‐tak, to maaĭ waî sź‐yān haí ch‘aán‐īp‐sheûng, laū‐ts‘uēn k‘uĭ‐kè mēng.
6 Kòh‐ts‘an‐shūk‐kè wâ, Kóm‐yeûng ngŏh tsaû pat‐nāng shūk lokₒ, húng‐p‘à waaî ngŏh‐kè ch‘aán‐īp: neĭ hóh‐ĭ shūk ngŏh shóh‐tong‐shūk‐kè; yan‐waî ngŏh pat‐nāng shūk à.
7 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn yaŭ‐tîng‐laî, yeūk iù shūk mat‐yĕ, waāk kaau‐yīk, seúng tîng‐shāt, tsaû ni‐kòh‐yān t‘uetₒ haaī, peí‐kwòh kóh‐kòh‐yān: Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haî kóm‐yeûng tsô p‘āng‐kuì‐kè.
8 Kòh‐ts‘an‐shūk‐kè tuì Poh‐sẑ wâ, Neĭ tsẑ‐keí maaĭ pâ la. Kóm‐tsaû tseung haaī t‘uetₒ‐hiu.
9 Poh‐sẑ tuì cheúng‐lŏ kûng chùng‐yān wâ, Neĭ‐teî kam‐yāt tsô kìn‐chìng, faān shūk Ĭ‐leî‐maĭ‐līk, kûng Kei‐līn, Mă‐lūn‐kè, ngŏh to yaū Nā‐oh‐maĭ shaú‐chung chì‐maaĭ.
10 Ī‐ch‘é Moh‐aapₒ nuĭ Lô‐tak, tsik‐haî Mă‐lūn‐kè ts‘ai, ngŏh yīk maaĭ‐hiu tsô ngŏh‐kè ts‘ai, táng haí sź‐yān‐kè ch‘aán‐īp‐sheûng, laū‐ts‘uēn k‘uĭ‐kè mēng, táng k‘uĭ‐kè mēng haí k‘uĭ poón‐tsūk poón‐heung pat‐chì mīt‐tsuēt: neĭ‐teî kam‐yāt tsô kìn‐chìng.
11 Haí shēng‐moōn‐haú ts‘ŏh‐ch‘uè‐kè chùng‐yān, t‘ūng kokₒ‐cheúng‐lŏ to wâ, Ngŏh‐teî tsô kìn‐chìng. Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lîng yāp‐neĭ uk‐k‘eí‐kòh nuĭ, hó‐ts‘z̆ La‐kitₒ kûng Leî‐à, tsik‐haî kìn‐laāp Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka‐kè kóh‐leŭng‐kòh‐nuĭ‐yān kóm: uên neĭ haí Ĭ‐faatₒ‐taaî tsokₒ‐sẑ shìng‐ì, haí Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng tak mīng‐shing:
12 Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaū ni‐kòh nuĭ ts‘z̀ neĭ haû‐yuî, táng neĭ‐kè ka, hó‐ts‘z̆ Faatₒ‐lāk‐sẑ‐ka yat‐yeûng, tsik‐haî Taaî‐mă ts‘ūng Yaū‐taaî shóh‐shang‐kè.
13 Ue‐shî Poh‐sẑ ts‘uì‐hiu Lô‐tak waī ts‘ai: ĭ‐king kûng k‘uĭ t‘ūng‐shat, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ waaī‐yân, shang‐hiu yat‐kòh tsaí.
14 Chùng‐foŏ‐yān tuì Nā‐oh‐maĭ wâ, Iù tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yan‐waî k‘uĭ kam‐yāt m̄‐laū‐lōk neĭ mŏ ts‘an‐shūk‐kè yān, uên k‘uĭ‐kè mēng haí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chung taaî‐hín‐chuè.
15 K‘uĭ tseung‐loī pit ka‐tsang neĭ tsing‐shān, yeŭng neĭ lŏ‐nīn: k‘uĭ haî oì neĭ sik‐foŏ shóh‐shang‐kè, peí‐kaaù neĭ yaŭ ts‘at‐kòh‐tsaí chûng‐hó à.
16 Nā‐oh‐maĭ tsaû tseung kòh‐so‐tsaí p‘ŏ tsoî waaī‐chung, tsô k‘uĭ‐kè yeŭng‐mŏ.
17 Lūn‐shè‐kè foŏ‐yān tsaû koí‐mēng kwòh so‐tsaí, wâ, Nā‐oh‐maĭ tak‐hiu tsaí lokₒ; tsaû kiù so‐tsaí kòh‐mēng tsô Oh‐paakₒ: kòh‐Oh‐paakₒ haî Yē‐sai‐kè foô‐ts‘an, Yē‐sai haî Taaî‐waî‐kè foô‐ts‘an.
18 ¶ Faatₒ‐lāk‐sẑ‐kè shaì‐toî līt mīng tsoî‐haû: Faatₒ‐lāk‐sẑ shang Ĭ‐sẑ‐lūn,
19 Ĭ‐sẑ‐lūn shang À‐laān, À‐laān shang À‐maĭ‐nā‐taāt,
20 À‐maĭ‐nā‐taāt shang Nā‐shûn, Nā‐shûn shang Saatₒ‐moōn,
21 Saatₒ‐moōn shang Poh‐sẑ, Poh‐sẑ shang Oh‐paakₒ,
22 Oh‐paakₒ shang Yē‐sai, Yē‐sai shang Taaî‐waî.
