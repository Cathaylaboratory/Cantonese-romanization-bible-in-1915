1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ‐KÈ īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue‐ngŏh wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐ch‘ut yat‐kuì yán‐uĕ, yaû tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka kóng‐p‘eì‐uê; wâ,
3 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Yaŭ yat‐chekₒ taaî‐mā‐ying, yaŭ‐taaî‐yīk kûng‐ch‘eūng‐uĕ, shang‐moŏn nğ‐shik‐kè mō, laī‐tò Leî‐pa‐nuên‐ch‘uè, yaû ts‘oí‐hiu paakₒ‐heung‐shuê‐kè téng:
4 Yaû ngaaŭ‐chitₒ‐k‘eī‐sheûng chì‐ko‐kè nuên‐chi, taaì‐yāp maû‐yīk‐kè teî; fòng‐tsoî‐sheung‐koó‐kè shēng‐yap.
5 K‘uĭ to ts‘uí‐hiu kíng‐noî‐kè chúng, pòh‐lōk feī‐meĭ‐kè t‘īn‐teî; yaû fòng‐tsoî toh‐shuí‐chi‐p‘ōng ch‘aapₒ‐hó‐k‘uĭ, hó‐ts‘z̆ laŭ‐shuê kóm.
6 Tsaû tsîm‐tsĭm shang‐cheúng, shīng‐waī kwóng‐footₒ pat‐ko‐chi p‘ō‐t‘aī‐shuê, kòh‐ti shuê‐chi chuèn‐heùng‐k‘uĭ, kòh‐ti kan‐k‘eŭng tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐hâ‐taí‐ch‘uè: kóm‐tsaû shīng‐waī p‘ō‐t‘aī‐shuê, shang‐ch‘ut shuê‐chi, yaû faatₒ‐ch‘ut miū‐yuî.
7 Lîng‐ngoî yaŭ chekₒ‐taaî‐mā‐ying, yaŭ taaî‐yīk kûng toh‐mō: yaû t‘aí‐a, ni‐p‘oh‐p‘ō‐t‘aī‐shuê, yaū‐tsẑ‐keí uēn‐teî‐kòh‐faaì‐t‘īn, tsaû peí kan‐k‘eŭng heùng‐k‘uĭ shang‐hoi, yaû faatₒ‐ch‘ut shuê‐chi heùng‐chuê‐k‘uĭ, táng‐k‘uĭ hóh‐ĭ lām‐laaî.
8 Ts‘ź‐shuê chùng‐lōk‐feī‐teî tsoî‐toh‐shuí‐chi‐p‘ōng, táng hóh‐ĭ‐faatₒ‐ch‘ut shuê‐chi, yaû hóh‐ĭ kitₒ‐kwóh, shīng‐waī meĭ‐shîn‐kè p‘ō‐t‘aī‐shuê.
9 Neĭ iù wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Heí ooĭ‐tak‐maû‐shîng me? k‘uĭ heí‐m̄‐haî līn‐kan‐k‘eŭng pāt‐heí, yaû‐kotₒ‐t‘uĕn k‘eī‐sheûng‐kè kwóh‐tsź, táng k‘uĭ ooĭ‐foo‐kó, yaû táng‐tò k‘uĭ san‐ch‘ut‐kè nuên‐īp to‐foo‐kó‐saaì me? yān m̄‐yûng taaî‐līk, yaû m̄‐yûng toh‐yān, tsaû hóh‐ĭ līn‐kan‐k‘eŭng pāt‐heí k‘uĭ lokₒ.
10 T‘aí‐a, sui‐īn chùng‐lōk, heí‐ooĭ‐maû‐shîng me? tung‐fung ch‘ui‐tò‐k‘uĭ kòh‐shī, heí‐m̄‐ooĭ tsûn‐teî foo‐kó me? k‘uĭ yīng‐īn tsoî‐shang‐heí kòh‐faaì‐t‘īn‐ch‘uè, pit‐tîng foo‐kó lokₒ.
11 ¶ Fòng‐ch‘é Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh‐wâ,
12 Chikₒ‐koón tuì poôn‐yīk‐chi‐ka wâ, Neĭ‐teî heí‐m̄‐shik‐tak ts‘ź‐sẑ‐kè ì‐sz̀ me? iù‐wâ‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi, T‘aí‐a, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng laī‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, yaû ts‘uí‐hiu k‘uĭ wōng kûng k‘uĭ kwan‐cheúng, taaì‐huì Pa‐peí‐lūn, tò‐k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè;
13 K‘uĭ ts‘uí‐hiu wōng‐ka‐kè haû‐yuî, yaû kûng k‘uĭ laāp‐yeukₒ, k‘uĭ‐teî i‐shóh‐faatₒ‐shaî‐kè taaì‐k‘uĭ‐laī: yaû ts‘uí‐hiu kíng‐noî taaî‐nāng‐līk‐kè:
14 Táng‐tò kòh‐kwokₒ‐shīng‐waī pei‐tsîn, yaû táng k‘uĭ pat‐hóh‐tsẑ‐ko‐heí‐laī, taân yan shaú‐chuê k‘uĭ māng‐yeukₒ, tsaû hóh‐ĭ k‘eĭ‐laāp.
15 Waī‐haî k‘uĭ pooì‐yīk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng, tá‐faatₒ waí‐ch‘aai lōk Oi‐k‘āp, seúng k‘uĭ‐teî peí‐mă‐p‘at kûng toh‐yān kwòh‐yān. K‘uĭ heí‐ooĭ‐hing‐shîng me? tsô kóm‐yeûng‐kè sẑ, kòh‐yān heí‐ooĭ‐tóh‐peî me? k‘uĭ pooì‐yīk māng‐yeukₒ, kíng‐īn ooĭ‐tóh‐peî me?
16 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Chiù‐ngŏh‐shang‐oōt, tsoî laāp k‘uĭ tsô wōng, kòh‐waî‐wōng shóh‐chuê‐kòh‐ch‘uè, kòh‐waî‐wōng shóh‐faatₒ‐kè shaî, haî k‘uĭ shóh‐miŭ‐shî, yaû pooì‐yīk k‘uĭ māng‐yeukₒ‐kè, tsik‐haî tsoî Pa‐peí‐lūn‐chi‐chung haí‐kòh‐wōng‐ch‘uè, k‘uĭ pit‐tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè ooĭ‐sź‐mōng.
17 Fòng‐ch‘é Faatₒ‐lŏ tseung taaî‐kwan‐ping kûng taaî‐kw‘ān‐chùng tá‐cheùng, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî chuk‐luĭ, yaû heí‐chìn‐t‘oī, seúng‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐toh‐yān‐kòh‐shī, to‐pat‐nāng yik‐ue‐k‘uĭ:
18 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ pooì‐yīk māng‐yeukₒ, tsaû miŭ‐shî shóh‐faatₒ‐kè shaî, yaû t‘aí‐a, k‘uĭ ĭ‐king kaau‐shaú, to yīng‐īn tsô ni‐ti kokₒ‐yeûng, k‘uĭ tsúng‐pat‐hóh‐tóh‐peî.
19 Shóh‐ĭ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ; Chiù‐ngŏh‐shang‐oōt, k‘uĭ keì‐īn miŭ‐shî‐ngŏh‐kè māng‐shaî, yaû pooì‐yīk ngŏh‐kè māng‐yeukₒ, ngŏh‐pit‐tseung ts‘ź‐waān‐faan tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐t‘aū‐sheûng.
20 Ngŏh ooĭ‐saatₒ‐ngŏh‐mŏng tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐sheûng‐t‘aū, k‘uĭ‐pit‐tsoî‐ngŏh‐kè‐koó peî‐chukₒ‐tó, ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐taaì‐k‘uĭ‐huì Pa‐peí‐lūn, yaû tsoî‐kòh‐ch‘uè waî k‘uĭ shóh‐faân‐tsuî, kung‐kik‐ngŏh‐kè‐kwòh‐shat, huèn‐mĭn k‘uĭ.
21 Shūk k‘uĭ taaî‐chùng tuî‐nğ t‘ō‐tsaú‐kè yān, t‘ung‐to‐ooĭ peî‐to‐kìm pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè, shóh‐laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè, to‐heùng‐kokₒ‐fung‐ch‘uè peî‐fan‐saàn: neĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, kóng‐kwòh ts‘ź‐sẑ lokₒ.
22 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐ts‘uí paakₒ‐heung‐shuê‐kè ko‐téng, yaû ch‘aapₒ‐hó k‘uĭ; ngŏh haí k‘uĭ‐yaū‐t‘iū‐chi‐chung, aaú‐chitₒ yat‐chi nuên‐kè, k‘aaĭ‐laī chùng‐lōk yat‐kòh‐ko‐tsùn yaŭ‐mīng‐kè shaan‐sheûng:
23 Tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt ko‐tsùn‐kè shaan chùng‐lōk‐k‘uĭ: tsaû shang‐ch‘ut shuê‐chi, yaû kitₒ‐kwóh, yīk shīng‐waī meĭ‐hó‐kè paakₒ‐heung‐shuê: kokₒ‐yeûng‐yīk‐kè tseukₒ‐niŭ ts‘ai‐ue‐k‘eī‐hâ; yaû tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐shuê‐chi yíng‐yàm‐chi‐chung ts‘ai‐chí.
24 T‘īn‐kaan shuê‐mūk, t‘ung to‐ooĭ‐chi‐tò ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ts‘āng‐fāt‐lōk ko‐tsùn‐kè shuê, yaû kuí‐ko pei‐hâ‐kè shuê, yaû‐foo‐kó ts‘eng‐lūk‐kè shuê, yīk lîng‐foo‐kó‐kè shuê shīng‐waī maû‐shîng‐kè: ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ts‘āng‐kóng‐kwòh, yaû‐tsô‐shīng ts‘ź‐sẑ lokₒ.
