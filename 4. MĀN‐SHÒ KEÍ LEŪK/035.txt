1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tsoî Moh‐aapₒ kw‘òng‐yĕ, Yē‐leî‐koh tuì‐mîn Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐pin, tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Neĭ iù fan‐foò Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, ts‘ūng shóh‐tak‐kè ch‘aán‐īp chi‐chung, tseung keí‐kòh‐shēng peí‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān; táng k‘uĭ‐teî kui‐chuê, yīk tseung kokₒ‐shēng‐ngoî‐kè kaau‐yĕ, peí‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān.
3 K‘uĭ‐teî hóh‐ĭ chuê‐tsoî ts‘ź‐shēng; ngaū yeūng lūk‐ch‘uk, k‘āp faān shóh‐yaŭ‐kè, hóh‐ĭ tsoî kaau‐yĕ.
4 Peí‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè kaau‐yĕ, iù tsoî shēng‐ngoî sz̀‐pîn leūng yat‐paakₒ cheûng.
5 Yaû tung leūng î‐paakₒ cheûng, naām leūng î‐paakₒ cheûng, sai leūng î‐paakₒ cheûng, pak leūng î‐paakₒ cheûng; shēng tsoî chung‐kaan: ni‐ti tsaû haî shēng‐ngoî‐kè kaau‐yĕ.
6 Shóh peí‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè shēng, k‘eī‐chung iù tseung lūk tsôh tsô t‘ō‐peî‐kè shēng, uê‐sin ch‘itₒ‐laāp, táng m̄‐koò‐ì shaatₒ‐yān‐kè, hóh‐ĭ tsaú‐yāp kòh‐ch‘uè: ts‘ź‐lūk shēng chi‐ngoî, chûng iù peí sz̀‐shāp‐î shēng.
7 Neĭ‐teî shóh‐peí‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè, kûng yaŭ sz̀‐shāp‐paatₒ shēng: yaû t‘ūng‐maaī shēng‐ngoî‐kè kaau‐yĕ.
8 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tseung shóh‐tak‐kè ch‘aán‐īp fan‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān: tak‐toh‐kè tsaû peí toh; tak‐shiú‐kè tsaû peí shiú: to chiù‐shóh‐tak‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, peí‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān tsô shēng.
9 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
10 Neĭ iù tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kóng wâ, Neĭ‐teî kwòh Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh, yāp Ka‐naām teî;
11 Pit iù ch‘itₒ‐laāp keí‐kòh shēng tsô t‘ō‐peî‐kè shēng; táng shaatₒ‐yān‐kè yān, tsik‐haî m̄‐koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐kè, hóh‐ĭ tsaú‐yāp kóh‐ch‘uè.
12 M̄‐koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè, chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung, peî‐hoi‐pò‐ch‘aū‐kè yān, mĭn‐chì ooĭ sź; táng tsoî ooî‐chùng mîn‐ts‘īn shaû shám‐p‘oòn.
13 Neĭ shóh‐peí‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè shēng, k‘eī‐chung tong iù uê‐peî lūk‐tsôh tsô t‘ō‐peî‐kè shēng.
14 Tsoî Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh ni‐pîn‐ngôn, uê‐peî saam‐tsôh shēng, tsoî Ka‐naām‐teî, uê‐peî saam‐tsôh shēng, to tsô t‘ō‐peî‐kè shēng.
15 Faān Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kûng k‘eī‐chung keì‐kui‐kè luĭ‐haakₒ, hóh‐ĭ tsoî ni‐lūk‐tsôh shēng t‘ō‐peî: táng taân‐faān m̄‐koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè, hóh‐ĭ yāp‐huì t‘ō‐peî.
16 Yān yeūk yûng t‘itₒ‐heì tá‐sź yān, tsaû haî koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè: pit‐iù shaû‐sź.
17 Yeūk yûng hóh‐ĭ‐tèng‐sź‐yān‐kè shēk‐t‘aū laī tèng yān, kòh‐yān sź‐hiu, tsaû haî koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè: pit iù shaû sź.
18 Yeūk yûng hóh‐ĭ‐tá‐sź‐yān‐kè mūk‐heì laī tá yān, kòh‐yān sź‐hiu, tsaû‐haî koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè: pit‐iù shaû sź.
19 Pò‐ch‘aū‐kè, tsẑ‐keí iù shaatₒ kòh‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè: uê‐cheūk chi‐shī, tsaû tseung k‘uĭ shaatₒ‐sź.
20 Yeūk uèn‐hân yān, tseung yān t‘ui titₒ, waāk tāk‐tang yûng māt tèng‐sź yān;
21 Waāk yaŭ ch‘aū‐hân, yûng shaú tá‐sź yān: tsaû‐haî koò‐ì‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè; pit‐iù shaû‐sź: pò‐ch‘aū‐kè uê‐cheūk k‘uĭ, iù tseung k‘uĭ shaatₒ sź.
22 Yeūk mŏ‐ch‘aū‐hân, fat‐īn tseung yān t‘ui‐titₒ, waāk mŏ‐sam yûng māt laī‐tèng‐yān,
23 Waāk m̄‐kìn‐kokₒ kóh‐shī, yûng hóh‐ĭ‐tèng‐sź‐yān‐kè shēk‐t‘aū, tèng‐lōk yān shan‐sheûng, kòh‐yān sź‐hiu, poón‐loī m̄‐haî waî ch‘aū‐hân, yīk m̄‐haî seúng hoî k‘uĭ:
24 Ooî‐chùng tsaû iù chiù laî, tsoî shaatₒ‐yān‐kè k‘āp pò‐ch‘aū‐kè chung‐kaan, laī shám‐p‘oòn:
25 Ooî‐chùng pit kaù kòh‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè, t‘uetₒ‐leī pò‐ch‘aū‐kè yān shaú‐sheûng, lîng k‘uĭ yāp‐huì shóh‐t‘ō‐peî‐kè shēng: k‘uĭ iù chuê‐tsoî shēng‐chung, táng‐tò shaû shìng‐ko‐kè tsaì‐sz‐cheúng sź‐hiu.
26 Shaatₒ‐yān‐kè yeūk ch‘ut‐huì shóh‐t‘ō‐peî‐kè shēng‐kíng‐ngoî;
27 Peî pò‐ch‘aū‐kè tsoî t‘ō‐peî‐kè shēng‐kíng‐ngoî uê‐cheūk, tsaû tseung ni‐kòh shaatₒ‐yān‐kè laī‐shaatₒ‐sź; pò‐ch‘aū‐kè yān tsaû mŏ tsuî:
28 Yan‐waî shaatₒ‐yān‐kè, iù chuê‐tsoî shóh‐t‘ō‐peî‐kè shēng‐luĭ, táng‐tò tsaì‐sz‐cheúng sź‐hiu: tsaì‐sz‐cheúng sź‐chi‐haû, shaatₒ‐yān‐kè chì‐hóh‐ĭ faan‐huì tsẑ‐keí poón‐t‘ó.
29 Ni‐ti tsoî neĭ‐teî kokₒ‐ka tò līk‐toî, tsô shám‐p‘oòn‐kè tîng‐laî.
30 Faān‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè, iù p‘āng‐yān tsô‐kìn‐chìng, tsaû shaû‐sź: yeūk tsêng‐haî yat‐kòh‐yān tsô‐kìn‐chìng, tsaû pat‐hóh lîng k‘uĭ shaû‐sź.
31 Shaatₒ‐yān‐kè yeūk faân‐sź‐tsuî, pat‐hóh chún k‘uĭ shūk‐tsuî: k‘uĭ pit iù shaû sź.
32 Tong tsaì‐sz‐cheúng meî‐sź‐chi‐ts‘īn, yeūk yaŭ yān tsaú‐yāp t‘ō‐peî‐shēng‐kè, pat‐hóh chún k‘uĭ shūk‐tsuî, faan‐huì tsẑ‐keí poón‐t‘ó.
33 Kóm‐tsaû neĭ shóh‐kui‐chuê‐kè teî pat ĭm oo‐waì: yan‐waî huetₒ ooĭ ĭm‐oo kòh‐teî: yeūk yaŭ yān shaatₒ yān laū huetₒ tsoî kòh‐teî, ch‘uē‐hiu laū kòh‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè huetₒ, mŏ‐pīt‐yeûng faatₒ hóh‐ĭ kitₒ‐tsîng teî.
34 M̄‐hó shaí neĭ‐teî shóh‐chuê‐kè teî ĭm oo‐waì, tsik‐haî ngŏh shóh‐chuê‐kè: yan‐waî ngŏh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chuê‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chi‐chung à.
