1 NEĬ yeūk kán‐shân t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, chiù ngŏh kam‐yāt shóh‐fan‐foò neĭ‐kè, kán‐shaú k‘uĭ kokₒ‐t‘iū kaaì‐mîng laī hāng, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì tsaû pit shaí neĭ ko‐ch‘ut‐kwòh teî‐hâ maân‐kwokₒ chi‐sheûng:
2 Neĭ yeūk t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsaû ni‐ti kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè fuk, pit lām‐tò neĭ shan‐sheûng.
3 Tsoî shēng‐luĭ pit tak fuk, tsoî t‘īn‐kaan pit tak fuk.
4 Waaī‐t‘oi shóh‐shang‐kè, t‘īn‐t‘ó shóh‐ch‘aán‐kè, ch‘uk‐shang shóh‐ch‘ut‐kè, k‘āp ngaū‐kw‘ān yeūng‐kw‘ān to tak fuk.
5 Neĭ‐kè lōh, k‘āp tsoì‐kaaù‐kè p‘oōn, to tak fuk.
6 Tong neĭ ch‘ut‐huì tsaû tak fuk, yāp‐laī yīk tak fuk.
7 Ch‘aū‐tīk laī kung‐tá neĭ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí k‘uĭ‐teî peî‐neĭ tá‐paaî: k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘ūng yat‐t‘iū‐lô laī tá neĭ, pit tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn ts‘ūng ts‘at‐t‘iū lô t‘ō‐tsaú.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí neĭ tsoî ts‘ong‐lăm, k‘āp faān shaú shóh‐tsô‐kè sẑ, to tak fuk, peí‐neĭ yaû tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ‐kè teî‐fong chuk‐fuk neĭ.
9 Neĭ yeūk kán‐shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè kaaì‐mîng, tsun‐hāng k‘uĭ‐kè tô, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû pit chiù k‘uĭ heùng neĭ shóh‐faatₒ‐shaî‐kè, laāp neĭ tsô tsẑ‐keí‐kè shìng‐mān.
10 Teî‐sheûng‐kè maân‐mān, kìn neĭ tak ch‘ing‐waī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mān; tsaû to kuî‐p‘à neĭ.
11 Neĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng neĭ līt‐tsó faatₒ‐shaî ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ‐kè teî‐sheûng, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí neĭ ts‘īn‐ts‘oī foò‐haû, k‘āp neĭ shan shóh‐shang‐kè, neĭ ch‘uk‐shang shóh‐ch‘ut‐kè, neĭ t‘īn‐t‘ó shóh‐ch‘aán‐kè, to ka‐toh.
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit waî neĭ hoi t‘in‐kè foò‐fōng, i‐chuê shī‐haû lōk uĕ tsoî neĭ‐kè teî‐sheûng, yaû heùng neĭ shaú faān‐shóh‐tsô‐kè sẑ laī‐chuk‐fuk neĭ: neĭ ooĭ tsè‐t‘aaì‐kwòh toh‐kwokₒ, taân m̄‐shaí kûng pīt‐yān tsè‐t‘aaì.
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit laāp neĭ tsô t‘aū, m̄‐tsô meĭ, shaí neĭ kui‐sheûng, m̄‐kui hâ, neĭ yeūk t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè kokₒ‐t‘iū kaaì‐mîng, tsik‐haî ngŏh kam‐yāt shóh‐fan‐foò‐neĭ‐kè, kán‐shaú tsun‐hāng: waāk tsóh waāk yaû, mŏ leī‐hoi ngŏh kam‐yāt shóh‐fan‐foò neĭ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, shûn‐ts‘ūng pīt‐ti shān, laī fūk‐sẑ, tsaû yaŭ kóm‐yeûng.
14 
15 ¶ Neĭ yeūk m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, m̄‐chiù ngŏh kam‐yāt shóh‐fan‐foò neĭ‐kè laī‐kán‐shaú tsun‐hāng, shóh‐yaŭ‐kè kaaì‐mîng faatₒ‐tô: kóm‐tsaû ni‐ti kokₒ‐yeûng chaù‐chòh pit lām‐tò neĭ shan‐sheûng:
16 Tsoî shēng‐luĭ shaû chaù‐chòh, tsoî t‘īn‐kaan shaû chaù‐chòh.
17 Neĭ‐kè lōh k‘āp tsoì‐kaaù‐kè p‘oōn, to shaû chaù‐chòh.
18 Waaī‐t‘oi shóh‐shang‐kè, t‘īn‐t‘ó shóh‐ch‘aán‐kè, ch‘uk‐shang shóh‐ch‘ut‐kè, k‘āp ngaū‐kw‘ān yeūng‐kw‘ān, to shaû chaù‐chòh.
19 Tong neĭ yāp‐laī tsaû shaû chaù‐chòh, ch‘ut‐huì yīk shaû chaù‐chòh.
20 Yan neĭ hāng okₒ pooì‐heì ngŏh, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí neĭ shaú faān shóh‐tsô‐kè sẑ peî chaù‐chòh; king‐húng, chaakₒ‐fāt, lām‐tò neĭ shan‐sheûng chì‐tò neĭ ts‘uk‐ts‘uk mīt‐mōng.
21 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí wan‐yīk ĭm‐ts‘an neĭ, chì‐tò neĭ shóh‐shīng‐shaû‐kè teî peî‐mīt‐tsuēt.
22 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit ts‘ui‐pik neĭ waân lō‐pêng, īt‐pêng, yeūk‐pêng, faatₒ‐shiu, uê to‐ping, yaû shaí neĭ‐kè wōh‐kà ts‘aān‐yeūk, foo‐kó; ni‐ti kokₒ‐yeûng pik‐paak neĭ, chì‐tò neĭ sź‐mōng.
23 Neĭ t‘aū‐sheûng‐kè t‘in, pit hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘ūng, keukₒ‐hâ‐kè teî pit hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘itₒ.
24 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí ni‐ti uĕ pìn‐waī sha: ts‘ūng t‘in kòng‐lōk‐laī, shaí neĭ mīt‐mōng.
25 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí neĭ peî‐ch‘aū‐tīk tá‐paaî: neĭ ts‘ūng yat‐t‘iū lô ch‘ut‐huì, kûng ch‘aū‐tīk tá‐cheùng, pit tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn, ts‘ūng ts‘at‐t‘iū lô t‘ō‐tsaú: ī‐ch‘é neĭ pit shaû teî‐sheûng maân‐kwokₒ līng‐yūk.
26 T‘in‐hung‐kè tseukₒ, teî‐sheûng‐kè shaú, pit tseung neĭ‐kè shi‐hoī tsô shīk‐māt, mŏ yān kón‐chūk.
27 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit tseung Oi‐k‘āp‐yān shóh‐waân‐kè tūk‐ting, okₒ‐ch‘ong, kaaì‐sín, ka‐tsoî neĭ shan‐sheûng, mŏ‐nāng i‐hó.
28 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí neĭ tin‐kw‘ōng, ngaăn‐maāng, sam‐king:
29 Neĭ pit tsoî aàn‐chaù t‘aàm‐mōk, hó‐ts‘z̆ maāng‐ngaăn‐yān tsoî haak‐òm‐chung t‘aàm‐mōk, neĭ shóh‐tsô‐kè sẑ, pit m̄‐t‘ung‐taāt: sheūng‐shī uê‐cheūk kw‘àn‐pik, ts‘eúng‐tuēt, mŏ yān ch‘íng‐kaù.
30 Neĭ p‘ìng‐tîng kòh‐ts‘ai, peî‐pīt‐yān ts‘uì‐huì t‘ūng‐fōng: neĭ heí‐hó kaan‐uk, m̄‐tak kui‐chuê: neĭ chùng p‘ō‐t‘aī‐uēn, m̄‐tak chaakₒ kwóh‐tsź.
31 Neĭ‐kè ngaū, tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn peî t‘ong‐hiu, neĭ m̄‐tak‐shīk: neĭ‐kè luī‐peî‐yān ts‘eúng‐tuēt, m̄‐tak k‘uĭ faan‐laī: neĭ‐kè yeūng, kwai neĭ ch‘aū‐tīk, mŏ yān waî neĭ huì kaù‐chôh.
32 Neĭ‐kè tsaí‐nuĭ, pit kwai‐ue ngoî‐pong, neĭ shīng‐yāt p‘aàn‐mông, chì‐tò ngaăn‐mūk fan‐maī: mŏ līk ch‘íng‐kaù.
33 Neĭ‐kè t‘ó‐ch‘aán, k‘āp neĭ lō‐foó shóh‐tak‐kè, pit peî neĭ p‘īng‐sò m̄‐yîng‐shik‐kè ngoî‐pong‐yān ts‘am‐t‘an; neĭ sheūng‐shī shaû kw‘àn‐pik pô‐yeūk:
34 Neĭ yan ngaăn‐chung shóh‐kìn‐kè sẑ, pit ooĭ tin‐kw‘ōng.
35 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit tseung okₒ‐ch‘ong ka‐tsoî neĭ sat‐sheûng t‘uí‐hâ, ts‘ūng keukₒ‐taí tò t‘aū‐téng, to haî, pat‐nāng i‐hó.
36 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí neĭ k‘āp neĭ shóh‐laāp‐kè wōng, huì‐tò yat kwokₒ, haî neĭ k‘āp neĭ līt‐tsó shóh‐meî‐yîng‐shik‐kè; haí kóh‐ch‘uè, neĭ pit fūk‐sẑ mūk‐tiu shēk‐tsōk‐kè shān.
37 Neĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā taaì neĭ huì‐tò‐kè kwokₒ, pit peî‐yān ch‘à‐î, peî‐yān siù‐t‘aām, peî‐yān kei‐ts‘iù.
38 Neĭ tseung hó‐toh‐chúng saatₒ‐lōk t‘īn, taân shau‐kotₒ‐kè haî shiú; yan‐waî peî‐wōng‐ch‘ūng shīk‐hiu.
39 Neĭ chùng‐chīk p‘ooī‐yeŭng p‘ō‐t‘aī‐uēn, taân m̄‐tak chaakₒ kwóh‐tsź, tsô tsaú yám; yan‐waî peî‐ch‘ūng shīk‐hiu.
40 Neĭ yaŭ kóm‐laám‐shuê tsoî neĭ kíng‐kaaì kokₒ‐ch‘uè taân m̄‐tak yaū ch‘ā‐lōk neĭ shan‐t‘aí; yan‐waî shuê‐sheûng‐kè kóm‐laám m̄‐shūk tsẑ lōk à.
41 Neĭ shang tsaí‐nuĭ, m̄‐tak shaû k‘uĭ fûng‐yeŭng; yan‐waî k‘uĭ pit‐peî‐lŏ‐leūk.
42 Neĭ‐kè shuê k‘āp teî‐sheûng‐kè ch‘ut‐ch‘aán, pit‐peî‐wōng‐ch‘ūng shīk‐saaì.
43 Tsoî neĭ‐ch‘uè‐kè luĭ‐haakₒ, pit tsîm‐tsĭm shing‐waī ko; neĭ pit tsîm‐tsĭm kòng‐waī pei.
44 K‘uĭ pit tsè‐t‘aaì kwòh‐neĭ, neĭ pat‐nāng tsè‐t‘aaì‐kwòh k‘uĭ: k‘uĭ pit tsô t‘aū, neĭ pit tsô meĭ.
45 Ni‐ti chaù‐chòh, pit chui‐pik neĭ, lām‐tò neĭ shan‐sheûng, chì‐tò neĭ mīt‐mōng; yan neĭ m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, m̄‐kán‐shaú k‘uĭ shóh‐fan‐foò neĭ‐kè faatₒ‐tô, lūt‐laî:
46 Ni‐ti chaù‐chòh pit tsoî neĭ k‘āp neĭ haû‐yuî‐kè shan‐sheûng, lîng yān tsokₒ waī k‘eī‐sẑ, î‐tsik, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn.
47 Yan‐waî neĭ foò‐tsuk kòh‐shī, m̄‐háng foon‐sam lōk‐ì laī‐fūk‐sẑ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì;
48 Shóh‐ĭ neĭ pit shaû kei‐hotₒ, ch‘ekₒ‐shan, sui‐yûng hìm‐k‘uetₒ, fūk‐sẑ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐tá‐faatₒ laī kung‐tá neĭ‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk: k‘uĭ pit tseung t‘itₒ‐aakₒ fòng‐tsoî neĭ kéng‐sheûng, chì‐tò neĭ mīt‐tsuēt.
49 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit ts‘ūng uĕn‐fong teî‐kīk, taaì yat kwokₒ‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, hó‐ts‘z̆ mā‐ying kóm, fei‐laī kung‐kik neĭ; kòh‐kwokₒ‐kè t‘ó‐t‘aām, neĭ m̄‐hiú‐tak;
50 K‘uĭ‐teî mîn‐maaû hung‐okₒ, m̄‐t‘aí‐koò lŏ‐yān, m̄‐līn‐măn nuên‐tsaí:
51 K‘uĭ‐teî pit shīk‐saaì neĭ‐kè ch‘uk‐shang shóh‐ch‘ut, teî‐t‘ó shóh‐ch‘aán: yaû shīk neĭ‐kè kuk, tsaú, yaū, ngaū‐kw‘ān, yeūng‐kw‘ān, m̄‐laū‐lōk peí neĭ, chì‐tò neĭ mīt‐tsuēt.
52 K‘uĭ‐teî pit tseung neĭ kw‘àn‐tsoî‐neĭ‐kè kokₒ‐shēng‐luĭ, chì‐tò neĭ shóh‐í‐laaî‐kè ts‘uēn‐teî ko‐taaî‐shēng‐ts‘eūng, to peî‐tá‐titₒ: yaû tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ‐kè ts‘uēn‐teî kw‘àn neĭ tsoî kokₒ‐shēng‐luĭ.
53 Neĭ peî‐ch‘aū‐tīk waī‐kw‘àn: shaû kw‘àn‐pik foó‐naân kòh‐shī, neĭ pit shīk neĭ shóh‐shang‐kè, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ‐kè tsaí‐nuĭ chi‐yūk:
54 Neĭ‐teî chi‐chung yaū‐yeūk saì‐neî‐kè yān, yīk pit nô‐shî k‘uĭ‐kè hing‐taî, kûng ts‘an‐oì‐kè ts‘ai, k‘āp shóh‐shîng‐hâ‐kè tsaí‐nuĭ.
55 M̄‐tseung k‘uĭ shóh‐shīk tsaí‐nuĭ‐kè yūk, peí‐kwòh k‘eī‐chung yat yān: yan neĭ peî‐kw‘àn‐tsoî kokₒ‐shēng‐luĭ, shaû ch‘aū‐tīk kw‘àn‐pik kóh‐shī, mŏ yĕ laū‐lōk‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ.
56 Neĭ‐teî chi‐chung, yaū‐yeūk saì neî‐kè foŏ‐yān, p‘īng‐sò m̄‐háng yûng keukₒ taāp‐teî‐kè, yīk pit nô‐shî ts‘an‐oì‐kè cheûng‐foo, k‘āp tsaí‐nuĭ,
57 Yaû sz shīk k‘uĭ shan‐noî ch‘ut‐laī‐kè paau‐i, k‘āp shóh‐shang‐kè tsaí‐nuĭ: yan neĭ peî‐kw‘àn‐tsoî kokₒ‐shēng‐luĭ, shaû ch‘aū‐tīk kw‘àn‐pik kòh‐shī, k‘uĭ shīk‐māt hìm‐k‘uetₒ, shóh‐ĭ iù shīk.
58 Neĭ yeūk m̄‐kán‐shaú tsun‐hāng ni‐ti lūt‐faatₒ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsik‐haî ni‐kuén‐shue shóh‐sé‐lōk‐kè, yīk m̄‐kìng‐waì YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ NEĬ‐KÈ SHEÛNG‐TAÌ chì‐tsuen chì‐kwaì‐kè mēng;
59 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit tseung k‘eī‐î‐kè tsoi‐wôh ka‐tsoî neĭ shan‐sheûng, k‘āp neĭ haû‐yuî shan‐sheûng, to haî chì‐taaî chì‐noî‐kè tsoi, chì‐ch‘ŭng chì‐kaú‐kè pêng.
60 Yaû shaí neĭ shóh‐kuî‐p‘à‐kè Oi‐k‘āp‐yān shóh‐waân‐kè tsāt‐pêng; ch‘īn‐iŭ neĭ shan.
61 Chûng‐yaŭ meî‐ts‘āng sé‐lōk ni‐ti lūt‐faatₒ‐shue shóh‐yaŭ‐kè tsoi‐wôh tsāt‐pêng, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí‐saaì lām‐kân neĭ shan, chì‐tò neĭ mīt‐tsuēt.
62 Neĭ‐teî ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn sui‐īn hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘in‐sing kòm‐toh; yan neĭ m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, shóh‐ĭ mŏ keí‐toh yān laū‐ts‘uēn.
63 Ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hó hon‐toî neĭ‐teî, haî uēt‐ì shaí neĭ‐teî chùng‐toh; uē‐kam Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsiú‐mīt neĭ‐teî, haî uēt‐ì shaí neĭ‐teî sź‐mōng; k‘uĭ pit ts‘ūng neĭ shóh‐huì shīng‐shaû‐kè teî‐fong, tseung neĭ pāt‐ch‘uē.
64 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí neĭ‐teî saàn‐hoi tsoî maân‐kwokₒ‐chung, ts‘ūng ni‐pîn teî‐kīk, tò kóh‐pîn teî‐kīk; neĭ‐teî pit tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè fūk‐sẑ mūk‐tiu shēk‐tsōk‐kè shān, tsik‐haî neĭ k‘āp neĭ līt‐tsó shóh‐meî‐yîng‐shik‐kè.
65 Neĭ tsoî ni‐ti kwokₒ‐chung, pit mŏ on‐lōk, m̄‐tak laāp‐keukₒ‐chi‐teî: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, shaí neĭ tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè sam‐luĭ king‐fong, ngaăn‐mūk fan‐maī, ì‐nîm yau‐waân:
66 Neĭ‐kè shang‐mîng pit mō tîng, yāt‐yê king‐fong, pat‐nāng pó neĭ shang‐mîng.
67 Neĭ yan sam‐luĭ king‐fong‐kè, ngaăn‐chung t‘aí‐kìn‐kè, chiu‐tsó pit wâ, Uên kap‐ti tò yê‐maăn! yê‐maăn pit wâ, Uên kap‐ti tò chiu‐tsó!
68 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit shaí neĭ‐teî ts‘ŏh shuēn faan‐huì Oi‐k‘āp, heùng ngŏh ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn tuì neĭ kóng wâ, Neĭ m̄‐tsoì kìn kóh‐t‘iū‐lô hāng, haí kóh‐ch‘uè: neĭ pit peî‐maaî‐kwòh ch‘aū‐tīk tsô nō‐pūk, taân mŏ yān háng‐maaĭ neĭ.
