1 NGŎH iù shī‐shī chuk‐tsê Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: ngŏh haú‐chung iù sheūng‐shī tsaàn‐tsûng k‘uĭ.
2 Ngŏh sam pit tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā laī king‐kw‘a: him‐sùn‐chi‐yān t‘eng‐mān, pit‐tîng foon‐heí.
3 Neĭ kûng ngŏh to ying‐tsuen Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waī‐taaî, ngŏh‐teî yat‐ts‘aī tong tsaàn k‘uĭ‐chi‐mēng.
4 Ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yaû mūng k‘uĭ yìng ngŏh; ī‐ch‘é tsoî húng‐kuî‐chi‐chung kaù ngŏh.
5 Faān yeŭng‐mông‐Chué‐ché, mîn‐sheûng tsaû yaŭ kwong‐ts‘oí: pat chì sau‐kw‘aì.
6 Ts‘ź k‘ūng‐foó‐chi‐yān foo‐kiù, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shuī‐t‘èng, ts‘ūng kokₒ‐yeûng waân‐naân‐chi‐chung, ts‘āng kaù k‘uĭ.
7 Faān kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐yān, yaŭ t‘in‐sz̀ sz̀‐mîn chaatₒ‐yīng, oô‐waî ch‘íng‐kaù.
8 Shì‐t‘aí‐hă Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaû chi k‘uĭ haî‐chì‐shîn: waī í‐laaî k‘uĭ‐ché, pit‐tîng yaŭ‐fuk.
9 Taân‐faān shìng‐kitₒ‐chi‐yān, tong kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: yan‐waî kìng‐waì‐k‘uĭ‐ché, mŏ‐ti k‘uetₒ‐fāt.
10 Chòng‐kîn sz‐tsź, yīk yaŭ‐shī k‘uetₒ‐fāt kei‐ngôh: waī k‘eī‐k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ché, pit tak hó‐ch‘uè moŏn‐tsuk.
11 Chùng‐siú‐tsź à, neĭ laī‐t‘eng ngŏh: ngŏh tsaû tseung kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐leĭ kaaù neĭ.
12 Pat‐lûn hōh‐yān, seúng tak‐shang‐mîng, seúng heúng ch‘eūng‐shaû, seúng tak‐hó‐ch‘uè?
13 Tsaû tong kàm‐chaì haú‐shīt m̄‐kóng okₒ‐īn, haú‐shūn m̄‐kóng ká‐wâ.
14 Leī‐okₒ ts‘ūng‐shîn: chuen ts‘ām wōh‐mūk laī‐hāng.
15 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chi‐ngaăn, t‘aí‐koò shîn‐yān, chak‐ĭ t‘eng k‘uĭ‐chi‐foo‐kiù.
16 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā făn‐nô‐chi‐mîn, tuì‐chuê hāng‐okₒ‐ché, tsoî shaì‐sheûng pit ch‘uē‐mīt k‘uĭ mēng‐hô.
17 Yān foo‐kiù, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘eng k‘uĭ shóh‐k‘aū, kaù k‘uĭ ch‘ut kokₒ‐yeûng waân‐naân.
18 Sam‐chung sheung‐pei‐ché, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘an‐kân k‘uĭ; sam‐chung t‘ùng‐fooì‐ché, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ.
19 Shîn‐yān‐chi‐foó‐naân shâm‐toh: waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘ūng‐chung yat‐k‘oì kaù‐oô.
20 Pó‐ts‘uēn k‘uĭ‐chi‐kwat: yat‐t‘iū to m̄‐chitₒ.
21 Okₒ‐yān pit sź‐tsoî wôh‐waân‐chi‐chung: tsang‐oò shîn‐yān‐ché, pit iù tîng tsuî.
22 Faān‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐pūk, pit mūng k‘uĭ kaù‐shūk līng‐wān: shóh‐yaŭ í‐laaî k‘uĭ‐ché, tsúng mŏ‐tîng‐tsuî.
