1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ à, neĭ ĭ‐king shi yan‐tín kwòh‐neĭ kwokₒ: ĭ‐king shaí Ngă‐kokₒ tsūk‐chung peî‐lŏ‐chi‐yān faan‐kwai.
2 Neĭ ĭ‐king shè‐mĭn neĭ tsź‐mān‐chi‐tsuî, che‐k‘oì k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐okₒ.
3 Ch‘uē‐saaì neĭ‐chi‐taaî‐nô: t‘īng‐sik neĭ‐chi făn‐heì.
4 Hán‐k‘aū Sheûng‐Taì, ngŏh‐teî Kaù‐Chué, waăn‐ooī ngŏh‐teî, mātₒ tsoì‐heùng ngŏh‐teî faatₒ‐nô.
5 Neĭ heí‐haî wĭng‐uĕn nau‐nô ngŏh‐teî me? heí‐haî shaì‐shaì to heì‐făn me?
6 Heí‐haî m̄‐faan‐laī lîng ngŏh‐teî faan‐shang me: neĭ heí‐pat lîng neĭ‐chi‐mān yan neĭ ī foon‐heí?
7 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tseung neĭ‐chi‐līn‐măn peí‐kwòh ngŏh‐teî‐kìn, tseung neĭ‐chi‐ch‘íng‐kaù ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh‐teî.
8 Ngŏh pit‐tîng t‘eng Ts‘uēn‐Nāng Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐īn: yan‐waî k‘uĭ ying‐shīng ts‘z̀ p‘īng‐on kwòh‐k‘uĭ paakₒ‐sìng, uĕ‐k‘āp kìng‐k‘īn chi yān: taân k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐hó tsoì‐hāng hui‐mŏng.
9 Chué‐chi‐kaù‐yan, haî kûng kìng‐waì‐chi‐yān seung‐kân; wīng‐kwong yīk chuê‐tsoî ngŏh‐teî kíng‐noî.
10 Yan‐tín kûng chan‐leĭ, peí‐ts‘ź seung‐līn; kung‐î kûng wōh‐p‘īng, peí‐ts‘ź seung‐ts‘an‐tsuí.
11 Chan‐leĭ ts‘ūng teî shang‐ch‘ut, kung‐î ts‘ūng t‘in shuī‐koò.
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit tseung hó‐māt laī‐ts‘z̀; lîng ngŏh‐teî t‘ó‐teî, ch‘ut‐ch‘aán fung‐shîng.
13 Kung‐î pit‐tîng sin hāng; lîng ngŏh‐teî kwai Chué shóh‐hāng‐chi‐lô.
