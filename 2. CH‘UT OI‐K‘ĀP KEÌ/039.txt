1 YÛNG laām‐shik, tsź‐shik, kòng‐shik sìn, tsô yaù‐saì kè i‐fūk, tsik‐haî tsoî shìng‐shóh sẑ‐fûng kè laĭ fūk, yīk waî À‐lūn tsô shìng i; haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
2 ¶ Yûng kam sìn, kûng laām‐shik tsź‐shik, kòng‐shik, k‘āp saì mā sìn, tsô kòh‐ti ĭ‐fat.
3 Yûng kam tá shīng pōk p‘ìn, tsín shīng sìn, k‘aaĭ miû shaú kung‐tseûng shóh yûng kè laām‐shik, tsź‐shik, kòng‐shik, saì mā sìn laī chik shīng.
4 Yaû tsoî ĭ‐fat kè leŭng p‘ōng: tsô leŭng t‘iū seung‐līn kè kin taaì.
5 Ĭ‐fat sheûng miû shaú chik kè fa taaì, kûng chik ĭ‐fat kè faatₒ‐tsź seung t‘ūng; tsik‐haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai; k‘aaĭ kam sìn, kûng laām‐shik, tsź‐shik, kòng‐shik, saì‐mā sìn laī chik shīng kè.
6 ¶ Yaû teukₒ pik‐yūk seung tsoî kam fong sheûng, chiù‐i haak yàn kè faatₒ‐tsź, tseung Ĭ‐shik‐līt chi‐p‘aaì kè mēng haak lōk huì.
7 Tseung yūk on tsoî ĭ‐fat kè kin taaì sheûng, waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsô keì‐hô; to haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
8 ¶ Yaû yûng kam sìn kûng laām‐shik, tsź‐shik, kòng‐shik, k‘āp saì‐mā sìn; chiù‐i chik faatₒ laī chik p‘ó‐tsź, hó‐ts‘z̆ chik ĭ‐fat yat‐yeûng.
9 P‘ó‐tsź haî sz̀ fong kè; chipₒ‐maaī leŭng ts‘āng: ch‘eūng footₒ to haî shaú chí yat‐naàm waī tô.
10 Sheûng‐mîn seung yūk‐shēk sz̀ hōng: taî‐yat hōng haî mă‐nŏ, wōng‐pó shēk, ts‘ung‐yūk.
11 Taî‐î hōng haî lūk‐yūk, ts‘ing‐yūk, kam‐kong shēk.
12 Taî‐saam hōng haî hūng‐pó shēk, paāk mă‐nŏ, tsź‐yūk.
13 Taî‐sz̀ hōng haî wōng‐pó shēk, pik‐yūk, pikₒ‐yūk: to seung tsoî kam fong chi‐chung.
14 Ni shāp‐î faaì yūk, chiù Ĭ‐shik‐līt chi‐p‘aaì kè mēng, yûng haak‐yàn faatₒ‐tsź, mooĭ yat faaì yūk haak yat mēng, shāp‐î chi‐p‘aaì to haî kóm.
15 Yûng tsing‐kam sìn nōh shīng lîn, on tsoî p‘ó‐tsź leŭng pin.
16 Yaû yûng kam chíng leŭng‐kòh kam fong; leŭng‐kòh kam waān, tseung kam waān on tsoî p‘ó‐tsź leŭng pin.
17 Tseung leŭng t‘iū kam lîn, ch‘uen yāp p‘ó‐tsź leŭng pin kè kam waān noî.
18 Tseung leŭng t‘iū kam lîn t‘aū, póng tsoî leŭng‐kòh kam fong sheûng, on tsoî ĭ‐fat kè kin taaì ts‘īn‐pîn.
19 Yaû chíng leŭng‐kòh kam waān, on tsoî p‘ó‐tsź luĭ kè leŭng pin, kân ĭ‐fat chi‐noî.
20 Yaû chíng leŭng‐kòh kam waān, on tsoî ĭ‐fat leŭng pin kin taaì chi‐hâ, kûng luēn‐hōp kòh‐ch‘uè seung tuì, tsoî ĭ‐fat kè fa taaì sheûng.
21 Yûng laām‐shik shīng, tseung p‘ó‐tsź kè waān, kûng ĭ‐fat kè waān póng‐chuê, tsoî fa taaì chi‐sheûng, táng p‘ó‐tsź m̄ leī‐hoi ĭ‐fat; haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
22 ¶ Yûng chik faatₒ tsô ĭ‐fat luĭ kè ngoî p‘ō, haî ts‘uēn laām‐shik kè.
23 Ngoî p‘ō chung‐kaan hoi yat‐kòh lung, lung‐kè sz̀‐waī chik ch‘ut pin laān, hó‐ts‘z̆ kaapₒ i kè lung, lîng k‘uĭ m̄ ooĭ līt laân.
24 Haí p‘ō keukₒ sz̀‐waī kè pin laān sheûng, yûng laām‐shik, tsź‐shik, kòng‐shik, k‘āp mā sìn chik ch‘ut shēk‐laū fa.
25 Yaû yûng tsing‐kam chíng shīng kam līng, on tsoî p‘ō keukₒ sz̀‐waī shēk‐laū fa kòh‐ch‘uè, táng kam līng kûng shēk‐laū fa seung kaàn;
26 Yat‐kòh kam līng, yat té shēk‐laū fa, k‘eī‐ts‘z̀ yīk haî kóm‐yeûng, p‘ō keukₒ sz̀‐waī to yaŭ, táng k‘uĭ cheukₒ heí ni‐ti laī sẑ‐fûng; haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
27 ¶ Yaû yûng saì‐mā sìn, waî À‐lūn k‘āp k‘uĭ chùng‐tsź, chik shīng noî p‘ō,
28 Yaû yûng saì‐mā pò tsô koon kûng chaatₒ t‘aū kan, kûng saì‐mā sìn shóh chik kè foò,
29 Yaû yûng laām‐shik, tsź‐shik, kòng‐shik, saì‐mā sìn, tsô saù fa kè taaî taaì; haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
30 ¶ K‘uĭ yûng tsing kam tsô shìng koon kè p‘aaī, chiù haak‐yàn kè faatₒ‐tsź, haak tsẑ tsoî p‘aaī sheûng, wâ, SHÌNG‐KITₒ KWAI‐UE YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ.
31 Yaû yûng laām shik‐shīng, tseung kam p‘aaī póng tsoî koon sheûng; haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
32 ¶ Kóm‐tsaû ooî mōk kè kokₒ‐yeûng kung‐foo tsô uēn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān to tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faān shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè laī hāng.
33 ¶ Chùng‐yān tseung ooî mōk kûng ooî mōk kè yat‐ts‘aì heì‐māt, ning huì Moh‐sai ch‘uè, tsik‐haî kau, paán, shaan, ch‘uĕ, tsôh,
34 Yeūng‐kung p‘eī, ĭm hūng kè koì, kûng oō‐hōk p‘eī kè sheûng koì, k‘āp che‐paì kè līm,
35 Yeukₒ‐kwaî kûng t‘oī kwaî kè kòng, k‘āp shūk‐tsuî‐shóh,
36 T‘oī kûng t‘oī kè yat‐ts‘aì heì‐kuî, k‘āp ch‘ān‐ch‘itₒ kè péng,
37 Tsing kam tang t‘oī kûng tang chaán, tsik‐haî paaí‐līt kè tang, k‘āp tang t‘oī kè yat‐ts‘aì heì‐kuî, tím tang kè yaū,
38 Kam t‘aān, ko‐yaū, hing‐heung, waī‐mōk kè moōn līm,
39 T‘ūng t‘aān, kûng t‘aān ngoî kè t‘ūng mŏng, t‘oī t‘aān kè kòng, t‘aān kè yat‐ts‘aì heì‐kuî, saí‐yūk kè p‘oōn k‘āp p‘oōn keukₒ,
40 Uên kè pò‐waī kûng ch‘uĕ, kûng tsôh, uên moōn kè līm kûng shīng uĕ teng, ooî mōk shóh yûng kè yat‐ts‘aì heì‐māt,
41 Tsoî shìng‐shóh sẑ‐fûng kè laĭ i, tsaì‐sz À‐lūn kè shìng i, k‘āp k‘uĭ chùng‐tsź tsô tsaì‐sz shóh cheukₒ kè i‐fūk.
42 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faān shóh fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè, laī tsô kokₒ‐yeûng kung‐foo.
43 Moh‐sai t‘aí‐kìn kokₒ‐yeûng kung‐foo, k‘ui haî chiù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh fan‐foò, kóm‐yeûng tsô shīng kè: Moh‐sai tsaû chuk‐fuk chùng‐yān.
