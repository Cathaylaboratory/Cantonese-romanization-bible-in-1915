1 NGŎH i‐chuê chìng‐chīk laī‐hāng; k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā p‘oòn‐tuèn ngŏh: ngŏh í‐laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; tsaû pit tak wán‐koò.
2 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, shì ngŏh ch‘aatₒ ngŏh; tuèn‐lîn ngŏh‐chi‐sam‐ch‘eūng.
3 Neĭ‐chi‐yan‐tín, sheūng tsoî ngŏh ngaăn‐ts‘īn: ngŏh i‐chuê neĭ‐chi‐chan‐leĭ laī‐hāng.
4 Ngŏh sò‐yāt m̄‐kûng hui‐mŏng‐chi‐yān ts‘ŏh, m̄‐t‘ūng kwaí‐kwat‐chi‐yān hāng.
5 Tsokₒ‐okₒ kòh‐tóng‐yān, ngŏh shâm‐tsang‐oò; yīk pit m̄‐t‘ūng okₒ‐yān ts‘ŏh.
6 Shaú yaŭ pat‐î, pit‐tîng saí‐tsîng: īn‐haû chau‐waī hāng Chué‐chi‐t‘aān:
7 Taaî sheng tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ, ī‐ch‘é ch‘uēn‐yeūng neĭ‐chi‐î‐tsik.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh foon‐heí neĭ shóh‐chuê‐chi‐tîn, uĕ‐k‘āp neĭ wīng‐kwong shóh‐tsoî‐chi‐ch‘uè.
9 K‘aū neĭ māt lîng ngŏh līng‐wān t‘ūng‐maaī faân‐tsuî‐yān yat‐yeûng, māt lîng ngŏh shang‐mîng t‘ūng‐maaī hung‐okₒ‐yān yat‐yeûng:
10 K‘uĭ shaú shóh‐tsô‐ché haî kaan‐ts‘ē, yaû‐shaú ch‘ung‐moŏn‐ché haî tsong‐māt.
11 Waī‐haî ngŏh pit hāng chìng‐chīk: uên neĭ kaù‐shūk ngŏh, līn‐sut ngŏh.
12 Ngŏh keukₒ k‘eĭ‐laāp p‘īng‐teî: tsoî shìng‐ooî‐chi‐chung, ngŏh pit chuk‐tsê Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
