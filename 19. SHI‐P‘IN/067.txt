1 UÊN Sheûng‐Taì līn‐măn ngŏh‐teî, chuk‐fuk ngŏh‐teî; peí‐k‘uĭ mîn‐sheûng‐chi‐kwong chiù‐chuê ngŏh‐teî.
2 Táng shaì‐sheûng‐chi‐yān, chi‐tò neĭ tô‐lô, līt‐pong‐chi‐mān, chi neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù.
3 Sheûng‐Taì à, līt‐kwokₒ ying chuk‐tsê neĭ; maân‐kwokₒ to ying chuk‐tsê neĭ.
4 Līt‐kwokₒ pit foon‐heí koh‐tsûng: yan‐waî neĭ i‐kung‐î shám‐p‘oòn maân‐mān, ch‘ī‐leĭ shaì‐sheûng līt‐kwokₒ.
5 Sheûng‐Taì à, līt‐kwokₒ ying chuk‐tsê neĭ; maân‐kwokₒ to ying chuk‐tsê neĭ.
6 Teî ĭ‐king ch‘ut‐ch‘aán fung‐tsuk; Sheûng‐Taì tsik ngŏh tsẑ‐keí‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, shî‐pit chuk‐fuk ngŏh‐teî.
7 Sheûng‐Taì pit chuk‐fuk ngŏh‐teî; teî‐chi‐sz̀‐kīk, mō‐pat‐kìng‐waì.
