1 TONG taî‐shāp‐nīn shāp‐uēt shāp‐î‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐tîng‐neĭ mîn heùng Oi‐k‘āp wōng Faatₒ‐lŏ, yaû‐kóng‐uê‐īn kung‐kik‐k‘uĭ, yaû kung‐kik Oi‐k‘āp ts‘uēn‐teî:
3 Iù kóng wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, Oi‐k‘āp wōng Faatₒ‐lŏ à, ngŏh kung‐kik neĭ, kòh‐t‘iū taaî‐lūng p‘oōn‐tsoî k‘uĭ‐kong‐hōh‐chung‐kaan, tsik‐haî ts‘āng wâ, Ngŏh t‘iū‐hōh haî tsẑ‐keí‐kè, ngŏh tsô‐ch‘ut waî‐tsẑ‐keí yûng.
4 Ngŏh pit‐k‘aaĭ ngau fòng‐lōk neĭ‐ngā‐ch‘ōng, yaû lîng neĭ kong‐hōh‐kè uē ch‘i‐chuê‐neĭ‐lūn‐ch‘uè, ngŏh pit‐yaū‐neĭ‐kong‐hōh‐chung‐kaan, laai‐neĭ sheŭng‐laī, t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ kong‐hōh shóh‐ch‘i‐chuê‐neĭ‐lūn‐kè chùng‐uē.
5 Ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐laū‐lōk‐neĭ peî‐wing‐lōk kw‘òng‐yĕ, neĭ kûng‐neĭ kong‐hōh‐kè chùng‐uē: neĭ‐pit‐titₒ‐lōk fong‐t‘īn‐ch‘uè; yaû m̄‐peî‐tsuî‐ooî waāk shau‐maaī kè: ngŏh ĭ‐king tseung‐neĭ peí‐kwòh t‘īn‐kaan‐kè tsaú‐shaù k‘āp t‘in‐hung‐kè fei‐niŭ tsô‐shīk‐māt.
6 Oi‐k‘āp‐kè chùng‐kui‐mān, pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî heùng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka, shīng‐waī lō‐waĭ‐kè kwaaí‐cheûng.
7 K‘uĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ‐shaú‐cha‐chuê‐neĭ kòh‐shī, neĭ tsaû chitₒ‐t‘uĕn, yaû maakₒ‐līt‐saaì k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè pokₒ‐t‘aū: k‘uĭ‐teî aai‐chuê‐neĭ kòh‐shī, neĭ tsaû chitₒ‐t‘uĕn, yaû lîng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè chùng‐iu chàn‐tûng.
8 Shóh‐ĭ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐taaì‐to‐kìm kòng‐lām‐neĭ‐ch‘uè, yaû haí neĭ‐ch‘uè t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐yān kûng ch‘uk‐shang.
9 Oi‐k‘āp‐teî pit‐shīng‐waī waī‐heì k‘āp fong‐faì‐chi‐teî; k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: yan k‘uĭ ts‘āng‐wâ, Kòh‐t‘iū‐hōh haî‐ngŏh‐kè, haî‐ngŏh‐tsô‐ch‘ut‐kè à.
10 T‘aí‐a, yan‐ts‘ź ngŏh‐kung‐kik‐neĭ, yaû kung‐kik‐neĭ‐kè kong‐hōh, yīk‐pit‐lîng Oi‐k‘āp‐teî tsûn‐teî shīng‐waī‐fong‐faì waī‐heì‐kè teî, tsẑ Tsuî‐ĭ‐neī‐kè shuè‐laū, chì‐tò Koó‐shāt kaau‐kaaì.
11 Tsúng‐mŏ‐yān‐kè keukₒ ooĭ‐king‐kwòh, k‘āp ch‘uk‐shang‐kè keukₒ hāng‐kwòh, yīk‐m̄‐tak yaŭ‐kui‐mān sz̀‐shāp‐nīn kòm‐noî.
12 Ngŏh pit‐lîng Oi‐k‘āp‐teî tsoî fong‐faì‐chi‐kwokₒ teî‐chung, tsô‐fong‐faì‐kè, k‘uĭ‐kè shēng‐yap, tsoî fong‐faì‐shēng‐yap‐chi‐chung, tsô fong‐faì‐kè, sz̀‐shāp‐nīn kòm‐noî: ngŏh ooĭ‐tseung Oi‐k‘āp‐yān fan‐saàn tsoî‐līt‐pong‐chi‐chung, yaû p‘aaì‐hoi k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung.
13 Yan‐waî Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Moŏn‐hiu sz̀‐shāp‐nīn kòh‐shī, ngŏh pit‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî peî‐fan‐saàn tò‐līt‐mān‐ch‘uè, tsuî‐maaī Oi‐k‘āp‐yān:
14 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng Oi‐k‘āp peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān faan‐laī, yaû lîng‐k‘uĭ‐teî faan‐yāp Paatₒ‐lōh‐teî, yāp k‘uĭ poón‐kíng‐chi‐noî; yaû tsoî‐kòh‐ch‘uè shīng‐waī hâ‐tsìn‐kè kwokₒ.
15 Tsaû tsoî līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐chung, tsô‐chì‐hâ‐tsîn‐kè; m̄‐tsoì shing‐ko tsẑ‐keí tsoî‐līt‐pong‐chi‐sheûng: ngŏh‐pit‐tîng kaám‐shiú k‘uĭ‐teî, táng‐tò m̄‐tsoì‐cha‐k‘uēn koón‐hāt‐līt‐pong.
16 Yīk m̄‐tsoì tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka shóh‐í‐laaî‐kè, tong‐chuèn‐ngaăn heùng‐mông k‘uĭ‐teî kòh‐shī, lìng‐yān keì‐nîm faan‐tsuî‐hin‐kè sẑ: k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû‐chi‐tò ngŏh‐haî Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
17 ¶ Tong î‐shāp‐ts‘at nīn, ching‐uēt, ch‘oh‐yat‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
18 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, shaí‐k‘uĭ kwan‐ping kung‐kik T‘ui‐lōh: tsaû tsô‐kwòh yat‐kîn taaî‐fūk‐yīk‐kè sẑ, kokₒ‐t‘aū pìn‐waī t‘uk‐kè, kokₒ‐kin peî‐ch‘aatₒ‐sheung: k‘uĭ‐sheûng‐ch‘é yaū T‘ui‐lōh waî‐k‘uĭ shóh‐tsô taaî‐fūk‐yīk‐kè sẑ kung‐kik‐k‘uĭ, meî‐shaû‐kung‐kà, k‘uĭ‐kwan‐ping to mŏ‐kung‐ts‘īn:
19 Shóh‐ĭ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐tseung Oi‐k‘āp‐teî peí‐kwòh Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ; k‘uĭ iù‐chukₒ‐huì Oi‐k‘āp‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng, yaû ts‘uí‐huì k‘uĭ shóh‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐chi‐māt, k‘āp k‘uĭ shóh‐lŏ‐leūk‐chi‐māt; ts‘ź‐tsaû‐haî tsô‐k‘uĭ kwan‐ping‐kè kung‐ts‘īn.
20 Ngŏh tseung Oi‐k‘āp‐teî peí‐kwòh k‘uĭ, pò‐sheúng k‘uĭ‐fūk‐yīk shóh‐tsô‐kè, yan k‘uĭ‐teî haî waî‐ngŏh tsokₒ‐kung, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
21 ¶ Tong kòh‐yāt ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng yat‐chekₒ‐kokₒ heùng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka faatₒ‐ngā, yaû lîng‐neĭ‐teî tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî‐chung‐kaan, yaŭ‐nāng hoi‐haú; k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
