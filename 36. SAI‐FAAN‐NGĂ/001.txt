1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ‐KÈ īn‐uĕ, tsik‐haî tong Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng À‐moōn‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐sai‐à‐kè yāt‐tsź, lām‐tò Koó‐shî‐kè tsaí Sai‐faan‐ngă, Koó‐shî haî K‘eī‐taaî‐leî‐kè tsaí, K‘eī‐taaî‐leî haî À‐mă‐leĭ‐kè tsaí, À‐mă‐leĭ haî Hei‐sai‐ka‐kè tsaí à.
2 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh‐pit‐tseung‐maân‐māt ch‘uē‐huì, mīt‐tsuēt‐ue‐teî‐sheûng.
3 Ngŏh‐iù‐mīt‐tsuēt‐yān kûng‐ch‘uk‐shang; t‘in‐hung‐kè tseukₒ‐niŭ, hoí‐chung‐kè uē‐luî, uĕ‐k‘āp ngoî‐p‘oōn‐kûng‐okₒ‐yān; ngŏh pit‐mīt‐tsuēt‐yān ue‐teî‐sheûng, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
4 Ngŏh pit‐heùng Yaū‐t‘aaì k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng kui‐mān‐chi‐sheûng, shan‐ch‘ut ngŏh‐shaú; yīk‐pit‐haí‐ni‐ch‘uè‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt Pa‐līk shóh‐uē‐shîng‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp Chi‐mă‐līn‐kè mēng, kûng‐tsaì‐sz yat‐ts‘aī kè.
5 Haí uk‐koì paaì sing‐kwan‐kè; chí‐chuê Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kûng Mootₒ‐kon laī‐faatₒ‐shaî‐k‘āp‐shūng‐paaì‐kè.
6 Pooì‐heùng m̄‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè; yaû m̄‐ts‘ām‐k‘aū m̄‐fóng‐mân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè, kóh‐táng‐yān to‐pit‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐kè lokₒ.
7 ¶ Neĭ iù‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn kaam‐haú; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yāt‐tsź laī‐kân‐lokₒ: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ĭ‐king uê‐peî tsaì‐tsẑ, yaû shìng‐pīt k‘uĭ‐kè luĭ‐haakₒ.
8 Tong Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaì‐tsẑ kòh‐yāt, pit‐shīng‐tsaû kóm, ngŏh‐iù‐yīng‐fāt‐kwan‐cheúng uĕ‐k‘āp‐wōng‐kè chùng‐tsź, kûng‐shóh‐yaŭ ngoî‐pong‐kè i‐sheūng cheukₒ‐lōk k‘uĭ‐shan‐kè.
9 Yaû tong‐kòh‐yāt, ngŏh‐pit‐yīng‐fāt taân‐faān t‘iù‐kwòh moōn‐haú‐kè, tsik‐haî k‘aaĭ‐k‘eūng‐okₒ‐kûng‐ngōh‐chà‐kè sẑ, ch‘ung‐moŏn chué‐yān‐chi‐uk‐kè yān à.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû wâ, Tong‐kòh‐yāt pit‐yaŭ haàm‐foó‐kè sheng, yaū‐uē‐moōn‐ch‘ut, yīk yaŭ‐hō‐foó‐kè sheng, yaū‐taî‐î‐shóh‐ch‘ut, kàng‐yaŭ waí‐laân‐kè sheng, yaū‐chùng‐shaan‐ch‘uè‐laī.
11 Mootₒ‐taāt‐kè kui‐mān à, iù‐taaî‐sheng‐haàm, yan‐waî Ka‐naām‐kè tsź‐mān paaî‐waaî lokₒ; shóh‐yaŭ taam‐t‘oī‐ngān‐kè, to‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐hiu.
12 Tong‐kòh‐chân‐shī pit‐tîng haî kóm, ngŏh iù‐k‘aaĭ‐tang‐fóh fóng‐ch‘ā Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, faān‐tîn‐tsaú‐cha‐kè: yaû tsoî‐sam‐chung wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit‐m̄‐hāng‐shîn, yīk‐m̄‐hāng‐okₒ‐kè, ngŏh‐pit‐yīng‐fāt‐k‘uĭ‐lokₒ.
13 K‘uĭ‐teî‐kè ts‘oī‐paāk, ooĭ‐shīng‐waī‐leūk‐māt, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè uk‐uĕ, pìn‐waī‐fong‐faì‐kè: ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐teî ooĭ‐heí‐uk, taân‐m̄‐tak kui‐chuê tsoî‐noî; yaû ooĭ‐chùng‐p‘ō‐t‘aī‐uēn, taân m̄‐yám shóh‐ch‘ut‐kè tsaú à.
14 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè taaî‐yāt‐tsź laī‐kân, shāt‐haî laī‐kân, yaû hó‐faaì chui‐kón‐laī‐kè, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yāt‐tsź‐kè sheng faatₒ‐ch‘ut: yŭng‐sẑ to‐haí‐kòh‐ch‘uè kiù‐haàm hó‐foó‐ts‘itₒ‐kè.
15 Kòh‐yāt haî‐nau‐nô‐kè yāt, haî‐wôh‐waân k‘āp foó‐ch‘óh‐kè yāt, haî‐fong‐faì k‘āp ts‘aān‐hoî‐kè yāt, haî‐mūng‐mooî k‘āp yau‐òm‐kè yāt, haî‐māt‐wān kûng sham‐hak‐kè yāt.
16 Haî‐hô‐t‘ūng k‘āp king‐haakₒ‐kè yāt, kung‐kik‐pó‐cheùng‐kè shēng‐yap, yaû kung‐kik shing‐ko‐kè shēng‐laū.
17 Ngŏh‐iù‐shaí‐foó‐ch‘óh lām‐yān‐ka, lîng k‘uĭ hó‐ts‘z̆ maāng‐ngaăn‐kè kóm‐hāng, yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faân‐tsuî‐kè: k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè huetₒ, pit‐hó‐ts‘z̆‐naī‐ch‘ān, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè yūk, hó‐ts‘z̆‐fàn‐t‘ó kóm tó‐ch‘ut.
18 Tong Yē‐Wōh‐Wā nau‐nô‐kè yāt‐tsź, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè ngān waāk kam, to‐pat‐nāng kaù‐t‘uetₒ k‘uĭ‐teî à: waī‐haî ts‘uēn‐teî pit‐peî k‘uĭ făn‐līt‐kè fóh t‘an‐mīt; ī‐ch‘é shóh‐yaŭ‐chuê‐tsoî‐kíng‐teî‐chi‐noî, chì‐chung hó‐kaau‐kwaan‐kè.
