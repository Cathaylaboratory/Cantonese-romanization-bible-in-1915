1 À‐TONG kûng kòh ts‘ai Hâ‐wa t‘ūng‐fōng; Hâ‐wa waaī‐yân, tsaû shang Koi‐yán, wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pó‐yaû ngŏh, tak kòh naām‐tsaí lokₒ.
2 Haû‐loī yaû shang k‘uĭ saì‐ló À‐paakₒ. À‐paakₒ haî hon‐yeūng kè, Koi‐yán haî kaang‐t‘īn kè.
3 Tò yat yāt, Koi‐yán k‘aaĭ ti t‘ó‐ch‘aán hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
4 À‐paakₒ ning taî‐yat shang kè yeūng‐tsaí, kûng yeūng kè chi‐ko, hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā. Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘aí‐koò À‐paakₒ, kûng k‘uĭ shóh hìn kè tsaì‐māt:
5 M̄ t‘aí‐koò Koi‐yán, kûng k‘uĭ shóh hìn kè tsaì‐māt. Koi‐yán tsaû hó nau, pìn‐hiu mîn‐shik.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mân Koi‐yán wâ, Neĭ waî‐mat kòm nau, pìn‐hiu mîn‐shik ni?
7 Neĭ yeūk‐haî hāng shîn, heí yaŭ m̄ hing‐heí me? yeūk m̄ hāng shîn, tsuî‐okₒ tsaû lām neĭ moōn ts‘īn lokₒ. Waī‐haî k‘uĭ pit heùng‐mô‐chuê neĭ, neĭ yīk koón‐leĭ k‘uĭ.
8 Haû‐loī Koi‐yán kûng saì‐ló À‐paakₒ seung kóng: chìng tsoî t‘īn‐kaan, Koi‐yán tsaû tseung saì‐ló À‐paakₒ shaatₒ‐hiu.
9 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Koi‐yán wâ. Neĭ saì‐ló À‐paakₒ haí pin‐ch‘uè? Koi‐yán wâ, M̄‐chi à: Ngŏh heí haî hon‐shaú saì‐ló kè me?
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Neĭ kwóh‐shāt tsô kwòh mat‐yĕ ni? neĭ saì‐ló kè huetₒ, yaŭ sheng ts‘ūng teî‐sheûng laī oi‐k‘aū ngŏh à.
11 Neĭ keì‐īn peí shaú shaatₒ kòh saì‐ló, teî hoi haú laī tsipₒ k‘uĭ kè huetₒ, neĭ uē‐kam pit shaû teî‐kè chaù‐chòh;
12 Neĭ kaang‐t‘īn, teî m̄‐t‘aì neî ch‘ut‐līk; neĭ pit‐tîng laū‐leī teî‐sheûng.
13 Koi‐yán tuì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh‐kè tsuî‐okₒ kīk ch‘ŭng, m̄‐shīng‐shaû tak heí lokₒ.
14 Kam yāt neĭ kón ngŏh ch‘ut ni taatₒ teî‐fong m̄ peí ngŏh kìn neĭ mîn; ngŏh laū‐leī teî‐sheûng; taân‐faān uê‐cheūk ngŏh‐kè pit‐tîng shaatₒ ngŏh.
15 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Taân‐faān shaatₒ Koi‐yán kè, pit iù shaû ts‘at p‘ooĭ kè yīng‐fāt. Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā peí yat‐kòh keì‐hô kwòh Koi‐yán, mĭn‐chì uê‐cheūk‐kè shaatₒ k‘uĭ.
16 ¶ Koi‐yán tsaû leī‐hoi Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn, huì Oi‐t‘īn uēn tung‐pin, Nōh‐tak kè teî‐fong kui‐chuê.
17 Koi‐yán kûng ts‘ai t‘ūng‐fōng; k‘uĭ ts‘ai waaī‐yân, tsaû shang Ĭ‐nōk: yaû heí yat‐tsôh shēng, tsaû tseung kòh tsaí kè mēng, kiù kòh shēng tsô Ĭ‐nōk.
18 Ĭ‐nōk shang Ĭ‐laāp: Ĭ‐laāp shang Maĭ‐oô‐ngă‐leî: Maĭ‐oô‐ngă‐leî shang Mă‐t‘ó‐saatₒ‐leî: Mă‐t‘ó‐saatₒ‐leî shang La‐māk.
19 La‐māk ts‘uì leŭng‐kòh ts‘ai: yat‐kòh mēng kiù À‐taaî, yat‐kòh mēng kiù Saí‐la.
20 À‐taaî shang Ngă‐paatₒ: haî chuê cheùng‐mōk laī yeŭng ch‘uk‐shang kè ch‘í‐tsó.
21 K‘uĭ saì‐ló mēng kiù Yaū‐paatₒ: haî t‘aān‐k‘ām ch‘ui‐siu kè ch‘í‐tsó.
22 Saí‐la shang T‘ó‐paatₒ‐koi‐yán, haî tá‐t‘ūng tá‐t‘itₒ kè tsó‐sz: k‘uĭ kè mooî mēng kiù Nā‐mă.
23 La‐māk tuì leŭng‐kòh ts‘ai À‐taaî kûng Saí‐la wâ, La‐māk kè foŏ à, t‘eng ngŏh chi sheng, mān ngŏh chi īn; ngŏh shaatₒ yān laī tsẑ sheung: shaatₒ t‘ūng‐tsź laī tsẑ hoî.
24 T‘óng‐yeūk shaatₒ Koi‐yán haî shaû ts‘at p‘ooĭ kè yīng‐fāt, kóm‐tsaû shaatₒ La‐māk, pit‐tîng ts‘at‐shāp‐ts‘at p‘ooĭ lokₒ.
25 ¶ À‐tong yaû kûng k‘uĭ ts‘ai t‘ūng‐fōng; kòh ts‘ai shang tsaí, koí mēng waī Ch‘itₒ: wâ, Sheûng‐Taì tsoì ts‘z̀ yat‐kòh tsaí kwòh ngŏh, laī toî Koi‐yán shóh shaatₒ kè À‐paakₒ.
26 Ch‘itₒ yīk shang kòh tsaí; mēng kiù Ĭ‐nōh‐sẑ: kòh‐chân‐shī, yān chì heí‐shaú ooĭ ch‘ing Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kè mēng laī shūng‐paaì.
