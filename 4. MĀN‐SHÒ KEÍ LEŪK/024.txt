1 PA‐LAĀN kìn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā foon‐heí chuk‐fuk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, k‘uĭ m̄‐tsoì huì wán ts‘ē‐shūt, tsaû chuèn‐mîn heùng kw‘òng‐yĕ.
2 Pa‐laān kuí‐ngaăn kìn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, òn‐chuê chi‐p‘aaì chuè‐chaapₒ; Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐Shān tsaû kóm‐tûng Pa‐laān.
3 K‘uĭ tsaû tsokₒ‐koh, wâ, Peì‐ĭ‐kè tsaí Pa‐laān yaŭ wâ, Ngaăn‐mūk hoi‐hiu‐kè yān yaŭ wâ:
4 Tak‐mān Sheûng‐Taì kóng‐kè, tak‐kìn Ts‘uēn‐Nang‐Chi‐Sheûng‐Taì hín‐în‐kè, shan‐t‘aí pūk‐to‐ch‘uè, ngaăn‐mūk hoi‐hiu‐kè yān yaŭ wâ:
5 Ngă‐kokₒ à, neĭ‐kè pò‐cheùng meĭ‐hó, Ĭ‐shik‐līt à, neĭ‐kè cheùng‐mōk wā‐laî!
6 Hó‐ts‘z̆ shaan‐kuk tsipₒ‐līn, hó‐ts‘z̆ hōh‐pin‐kè fa‐uēn, hó‐ts‘z̆ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐chùng‐kè ch‘ām‐heung‐shuê, hó‐ts‘z̆ shuí‐pin‐kè paakₒ‐heung‐shuê.
7 Shuí ts‘ūng k‘uĭ heì‐mĭng laū‐ch‘ut, k‘uĭ saatₒ‐chúng tsoî toh‐taaî‐chaāk, k‘uĭ‐kè wōng shìng‐kwòh À‐kaapₒ, k‘uĭ‐kè kwokₒ pit hing‐wông.
8 Sheûng‐Taì taaì k‘uĭ ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp; k‘uĭ k‘eūng‐kîn hó‐ts‘z̆ yĕ‐ngaū: pit t‘an‐shīk ch‘aū‐tīk‐kè kwokₒ, chitₒ‐t‘uĕn ch‘aū‐tīk‐kè kwat, yaû yûng tsìn shê‐sheung ch‘aū‐tīk.
9 K‘uĭ mau‐ch‘uè waāk pūk‐ch‘uè, hó‐ts‘z̆ kung‐sz, yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ mŏ‐sz: mat‐shuī kóm‐liū‐faân k‘uĭ? Chuk‐fuk‐neĭ‐kè pit‐peî‐chuk‐fuk, chaù‐chòh‐neĭ‐kè pīt‐peî‐chaù‐chòh.
10 ¶ Pa‐lāk hó‐nau‐nô, chap‐chaakₒ Pa‐laān: yaû p‘aakₒ shaú tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ngŏh chiû neĭ laī chaù‐chòh ngŏh‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk, neĭ faán‐chuèn saam‐ts‘z̀ chuk‐fuk k‘uĭ.
11 Shóh‐ĭ neĭ faan‐huì poón‐heung la: ngŏh seúng ka‐toh neĭ tsuen‐wīng; taân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chóh‐chuê neĭ tak‐tsuen‐wīng.
12 Pa‐laān tuì Pa‐lāk wâ, Neĭ shóh‐tá‐faatₒ huì‐ngŏh‐ch‘uè‐kè sz̀‐ché, ngŏh heí meî‐ts‘āng tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ,
13 Tsik‐sź Pa‐lāk k‘aaĭ moŏn‐uk‐kè kam‐ngān ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh, ngŏh to pat‐nāng kwòh‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mîng‐lîng, shîn‐tsẑ hāng shîn‐sẑ okₒ‐sẑ; waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò‐ngŏh‐kè, ngŏh pit chiù‐kóm‐kóng‐che?
14 Uē‐kam ngŏh iù faan‐huì poón‐tsūk: ngŏh tseung ni‐ti paakₒ‐sìng yāt‐haû toî neĭ paakₒ‐sìng‐kè, wâ‐kwòh neĭ chi.
15 Tsaû tsokₒ‐koh wâ, Peì‐ĭ‐kè tsaí Pa‐laān yaŭ wâ, Tsik‐haî ngaăn‐mūk hoi‐hiu‐kè yān yaŭ wâ:
16 Tak‐mān Sheûng‐Taì kóng‐kè, tak‐chi Chì‐Sheûng‐Ché‐kè chì‐shik‐kè, tak‐kìn Ts‘uēn‐Nāng‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì hín‐în‐kè, shan‐t‘aí pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè, ngaăn‐mūk yīng‐īn hoi‐kè yān yaŭ wâ:
17 Ngŏh pit‐tak‐kìn k‘uĭ, taân m̄‐haî în‐shī: ngŏh pit‐tak‐koon k‘uĭ, taân m̄‐haî kân‐ngaăn: pit‐yaŭ yat‐nap‐sing, ts‘ūng Ngă‐kokₒ ch‘ut‐în, yat‐kòh cha‐k‘uēn‐kè, ts‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt hing‐heí, pit kung‐tá Moh‐appₒ‐kè sz̀‐waī, chue‐mīt Ch‘itₒ‐kè haû‐yuî.
18 K‘uĭ pit tak Ĭ‐tung tsô ch‘aán‐īp, yīk pit tak ch‘aū‐tīk‐kè teî Sai‐ĭ tsô ch‘aán‐īp; Ĭ‐shik‐līt pit faatₒ‐ch‘ut yŭng‐līk.
19 Cha‐k‘uēn‐kè pit ts‘ūng Ngă‐kokₒ ch‘ut‐laī, tsiú‐mīt shēng‐chung uē‐shîng‐kè paakₒ‐sìng.
20 Pa‐laān yaû heùng À‐mă‐līk‐yān mông‐hă tsaû tsokₒ‐koh wâ, À‐mă‐līk poón‐loī haî kokₒ‐kwokₒ‐kè t‘aū‐mūk; taân shau‐meĭ pit‐peî‐mīt‐tsuēt.
21 Yaû heùng Kei‐neī‐yān mông‐hă tsaû tsokₒ‐koh wâ, Neĭ‐teî kui‐chuê kin‐koò, neĭ‐kè ch‘aaū‐uēt tsoî shēk‐ngaām chi‐chung.
22 Waī Kei‐neī pit shui‐paaî, chì‐tò peî À‐shūt chukₒ‐huì.
23 Yaû tsokₒ‐koh wâ, Hóh‐sik lokₒ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hāng ts‘ź‐sẑ chi‐shī, mat‐shuī tak‐shang ni!
24 Pit yaŭ shuēn ts‘ūng Kei‐taí‐kè pin‐kaaì laī, k‘uĭ‐teî foó‐pik À‐shūt, foó‐pik Hei‐paakₒ, k‘uĭ‐teî to pit‐peî‐mīt‐tsuēt.
25 Pa‐laān ue‐shî heí‐shan, faan‐huì poón‐heung: Pa‐lāk yīk huì.
