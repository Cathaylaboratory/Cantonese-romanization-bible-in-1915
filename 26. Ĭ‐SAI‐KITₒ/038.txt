1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ‐CHI‐ĪN‐UĔ kòng‐lām‐ue‐ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐tîng‐neĭ‐mîn heùng Mă‐kokₒ‐teî‐kè Kokₒ, tsik Lōh‐sz̀, Maĭ‐ch‘itₒ, kûng T‘ó‐paatₒ‐kè kwan‐cheúng, yaû‐kóng‐uê‐īn, kung‐kik‐k‘uĭ.
3 Wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Kokₒ à, tsô Lōh‐sz̀, Maĭ‐ch‘itₒ, kûng T‘ó‐paatₒ‐kè kwan‐cheúng à, t‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik neĭ:
4 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng neĭ faan‐chuèn‐t‘aū, yaû fòng‐kòh‐ngau tsoî‐neĭ‐ngā‐ch‘ōng‐ch‘uè, yaû taaì‐neĭ‐ch‘ut‐laī t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ ts‘uēn‐kwan, mă‐p‘at‐kûng‐mă‐ping, t‘ung‐to‐haî cheukₒ‐saaì mŏ‐kaapₒ‐kè, yat‐kòh taaî‐tuî‐nğ, yaŭ‐t‘ŭn kûng p‘aaī, t‘ung‐to‐shaí to‐kìm:
5 Pa‐sai, Koó‐shāt k‘āp Puk, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ‐teî; t‘ung‐to‐yaŭ t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī kûng t‘aū‐kw‘ai:
6 Yaŭ Hóh‐mīt‐kûng‐k‘uĭ chùng‐tuî‐nğ; yaŭ pak‐fong‐chi‐kīk‐teî‐kè T‘ōh‐ka‐mă‐chi‐ka, kûng‐k‘uĭ‐chùng‐tuî‐nğ; ī‐ch‘é kûng‐neĭ yaŭ taaî‐toh‐tsź‐mān.
7 Neĭ iù uê‐peî, shāt‐haî iù‐uê‐peî‐tsẑ‐keí, neĭ‐kûng‐shóh‐yaŭ tsuî‐maaī‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng, neĭ iù‐heùng‐k‘uĭ‐teî tsô‐oô‐waî‐kè kwan.
8 Kwòh‐hó‐toh‐yāt‐chi‐haû, neĭ tsaû peî‐t‘aàm‐ch‘aatₒ‐kwòh‐kè: tong moōt‐shī‐kè nīn‐kaan, neĭ pit‐yāp‐tò to‐kìm shóh‐ts‘uí‐faan‐chi‐teî, yaū‐toh‐mān‐ch‘uè tsuî‐maaī‐kè, tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐shaan‐sheûng, tsik‐haî, sheūng‐shī‐peî‐fong‐faì‐kè; waī‐haî yaū‐līt‐mān‐ch‘uè ts‘uí‐faan‐laī, k‘uĭ‐teî taaî‐chùng pit‐on‐īn kui‐chí.
9 Neĭ pit‐sheŭng‐huì, hó‐ts‘ kw‘ōng‐fung ooĭ‐laī, hó‐ts‘z̆‐wān che‐k‘oì teî‐mîn, neĭ‐kûng‐neĭ‐taaî chùng tuî‐nğ, k‘āp t‘ūng‐maaī‐laī‐kè toh‐tsź‐mān.
10 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Tong‐kòh‐yāt shīng‐tsaû haî kóm, yaŭ‐ì‐sz̀ yāp‐tò neĭ‐sam‐chung, neĭ pit‐tîng ch‘itₒ‐okₒ‐kè kaì‐maū:
11 Neĭ pit‐ooĭ‐wâ, Ngŏh iù‐sheŭng‐tò mŏ‐ts‘eūng‐oōn‐kè ts‘uen‐heung; ngŏh iù‐huì‐tò‐kòh‐ti p‘īng‐tsîng‐kui‐chuê‐kè, t‘ung‐to‐mŏ‐ts‘eūng‐oōn kui‐chuê‐kè, yaû mŏ‐chaāp mŏ‐moōn‐kè,
12 Seúng‐ts‘eúng‐tuēt yaû seúng‐lŏ‐leūk; seúng‐chuèn‐neĭ‐shaú kung‐kik p‘īng‐yāt fong‐faì, uē‐kam yaŭ‐kui‐mān‐chi‐teî, yaû kung‐kik kòh‐ti yaū līt‐mān‐ch‘uè tsuî‐maaī‐kè, yaŭ‐shang‐ch‘uk‐kûng‐ka‐ts‘oī, yaû‐chuê‐tsoî‐teî chung‐kaan‐kè.
13 Shî‐pa kûng Taí‐taân k‘āp Taaî‐shat‐kè sheung‐ka, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè siú‐sz, ooĭ‐tuì‐neĭ wâ, Neĭ‐sheŭng‐laī ts‘eúng‐tuēt me? neĭ tsuî‐tsaāp tuî‐nğ laī‐lŏ‐leūk me? seúng‐ts‘uí‐tó ngān‐kûng‐kam, ts‘uí‐huì shang‐ch‘uk‐kûng‐ka‐ts‘oī, ts‘uí‐tuēt taaî‐toh‐leūk‐māt me?
14 ¶ Yān‐chi‐tsź à, koò‐ts‘ź iù‐kóng‐uê‐īn, yaû tuì Kokₒ wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Tong‐kòh‐yāt, ngŏh tsź‐mān Ĭ‐shik‐līt on‐īn kui‐chuê kòh‐ch‘uè, neĭ heí‐m̄‐tak‐chi me?
15 Neĭ pit‐ts‘ūng neĭ poón‐ch‘uè yaū‐pak‐fong‐chi‐kīk‐teî‐ch‘uè laī, neĭ k‘āp t‘ūng‐maaī‐neĭ‐kè toh‐mān, t‘ung‐to‐k‘ē‐tsoî‐mă‐sheûng, yaŭ‐kòh‐shâm‐taaî tuî‐nğ, yaŭ taaî‐nāng‐kè kwan‐ping:
16 Neĭ‐ooĭ‐sheŭng‐laī kung‐kik ngŏh‐tsź‐mān Ĭ‐shik‐līt, hó‐ts‘z̆ wān‐che‐k‘oì‐teî‐mîn‐kóm; tong moōt‐yāt haî kóm, ngŏh pit‐taaì neĭ laī‐kung‐kik ngŏh‐kè teî, táng līt‐kwokₒ hóh‐ĭ‐shik‐tak neĭ, Kokₒ à, haî tong ngŏh tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐ngaăn‐ts‘īn, haí‐neĭ‐ch‘uè tak‐shīng‐waī‐shìng‐kitₒ kòh‐shī à.
17 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Neĭ heí‐haî ngŏh koó‐shī t‘okₒ ngŏh pūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè sin‐chi shóh‐kóng‐k‘āp‐kè me? tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî tong‐kòh‐chân‐shī, king‐līk hó‐toh‐nīn, kóng ngŏh pit‐tîng lîng‐neĭ‐laī‐kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐teî me?
18 Tong‐kòh‐yāt pit‐haî‐kóm, haí Kokₒ‐ch‘uè laī‐kung‐kik Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐teî kòh‐shī, ngŏh făn‐hân pit‐sheŭng‐tò ngŏh peî‐lung‐ch‘uè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
19 Yan ngŏh faatₒ‐keî‐ts‘ē yaû faatₒ‐nau‐nô‐kè fóh kòh‐shī kóng‐kwòh, wâ, Tong‐kòh‐yāt pit‐tîng tsoî‐Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐teî yaŭ taaî‐chàn‐tûng;
20 Chì‐tò hoí‐chung‐kè uē, t‘in‐hung‐kè fei‐niŭ, t‘īn‐kaan‐kè tsaú‐shaù, laan‐tsoî‐teî‐mîn‐kè chùng‐laan‐māt, tsoî‐teî‐mîn‐kè taaî‐chùng‐yān, tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn chàn‐tûng, shaan‐lĭng to peî‐wing‐lōk, k‘ei‐k‘ui‐chi‐ch‘uè ooĭ‐titₒ‐lōk, kokₒ‐ts‘eūng‐oōn ooĭ‐k‘ing‐tó‐teî‐hâ.
21 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ: Ngŏh ooĭ‐heùng‐ngŏh‐kè chùng‐shaan kiù‐sheng faatₒ‐to‐kìm kung‐kik k‘uĭ, kokₒ‐yān‐kè to‐kìm, pit‐kung‐kik tsẑ‐keí‐hing‐taî.
22 Ngŏh ooĭ‐tseung‐wan‐yīk kûng huetₒ p‘oòn‐tuèn‐k‘uĭ; ngŏh ooĭ‐lōk‐uĕ tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐sheûng, tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐tuî‐nğ k‘āp t‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ‐kè toh‐mān‐chi‐sheûng, yaŭ ch‘ām‐moōt‐kè chân‐uĕ, yaŭ‐pōk‐shēk, fóh, kûng laū‐wōng.
23 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng‐tsẑ‐keí shing‐ko, yaû lîng tsẑ‐keí shīng‐waī shìng‐kitₒ; yaû tsoî‐toh‐kwokₒ‐chi ngaăn‐chung hín‐mīng tsẑ‐keí, k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
