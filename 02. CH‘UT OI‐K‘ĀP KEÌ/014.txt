1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Neĭ tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kóng wâ, K‘uĭ‐teî iù faan‐chuèn‐t‘aū haí Peí‐hap‐hei‐lūk ts‘īn‐pîn, hoí‐pin Māt‐tuēt‐kè chung‐kaan on‐yīng, tsik‐haî tuì‐chuê Pa‐līk‐saí‐fan: kân hoí‐pin‐ch‘uè on‐yīng.
3 Yan Faatₒ‐lŏ pit ĭ‐lûn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān wâ, K‘uĭ‐teî maī‐shat lô‐t‘ō, kw‘àn tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ lokₒ.
4 Ngŏh tsaû lîng Faatₒ‐lŏ sam‐chung kong‐ngaâng, táng k‘uĭ chui‐kón Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān; ngŏh pit‐yan Faatₒ‐lŏ kûng k‘uĭ ts‘uēn‐kwan tak wīng‐iû; lîng Oi‐k‘āp‐yān chi ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā. Chùng‐yān tsaû tsun‐i kóm‐yeûng tsô.
5 Yaŭ yān tuì Oi‐k‘āp wōng wâ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān t‘ō‐tsaú: Faatₒ‐lŏ k‘āp shān‐pūk, heùng kòh‐ti paakₒ‐sìng koí‐pìn kòh‐sam, wâ, Tsô‐mat ngŏh shik‐fòng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐fūk‐sẑ ngŏh ni?
6 Ue‐shî uê‐peî mă‐ch‘e, taaì Oi‐k‘āp‐yān t‘ūng huì:
7 Kaán‐chaāk hó mă‐ch‘e lūk‐paakₒ shîng, t‘ūng‐maaī Oi‐k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ‐kè ch‘e, kokₒ‐ch‘e k‘ui yaŭ ping‐cheúng taaì‐lĭng.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lîng Oi‐k‘āp wōng Faatₒ‐lŏ sam‐chung kong‐ngaâng, táng k‘uĭ chui‐kón Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān: Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān yeūng peì tsaû ch‘ut‐huì.
9 Oi‐k‘āp‐yān tsik‐haî Faatₒ‐lŏ‐kè mă kûng mă‐ch‘e, ts‘uēn kwan chui‐kón Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, tsaû haí hoí pin kân Peí‐hap‐hei‐lūk, Pa‐līk‐saí‐fan, on‐yīng kòh‐ch‘uè, chui‐tó k‘uĭ‐teî.
10 Faatₒ‐lŏ ĭ‐king kân kòh‐ch‘uè, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kuí ngaăn t‘aí‐kìn Oi‐k‘āp‐yān chui‐laī; tsaû hó fong: t‘ó‐kò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
11 Chùng‐yān tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Neĭ taaì ngŏh‐teî ch‘ut‐laī sź tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ‐ch‘uè, heí haî yan‐waî tsoî Oi‐k‘āp mŏ fān‐teî me? neĭ waî‐mat kóm‐yeûng toî ngŏh? taaì ngŏh ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp ni?
12 Ngŏh‐teî haí Oi‐k‘āp kòh‐shī, heí m̄‐tuì neĭ wâ, Neĭ maĭ kaaú‐iŭ ngŏh, yaū‐tak ngŏh‐teî fūk‐sẑ Oi‐k‘āp‐yān me? Yan‐waî fūk‐sẑ Oi‐k‘āp‐yān, peí‐kaaù sź tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ chûng‐hó à.
13 Moh‐sai tuì paakₒ‐sìng wâ, Maĭ fong, neĭ‐teî k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè táng‐hă, t‘aí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè ch‘íng‐kaù, tsik‐haî kam‐yāt iù shi‐kwòh neĭ‐teî: yan‐waî kam‐yāt shóh‐kìn‐kè Oi‐k‘āp‐yān, haû‐loī wĭng m̄‐tsoì kìn lokₒ.
14 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit waî neĭ‐teî tá‐cheùng, neĭ‐teî hóh‐ĭ on‐tsîng.
15 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Neĭ waî‐mat t‘ó‐kò ngŏh ni? iù kiù Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān heùng‐ts‘īn‐huì:
16 Neĭ kuí‐heí kwaaí‐cheûng, shan shaú tsoî hoí‐sheûng, fan‐hoi hoí shuí: táng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haí hoí‐chung hāng hŏn‐teî.
17 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit lîng Oi‐k‘āp‐yān sam‐chung kong‐ngaâng, chui‐kón Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān: ngŏh tsaû yan Faatₒ‐lŏ kûng k‘uĭ kè ts‘uēn‐kwan, mă‐ch‘e mă‐ping tak wīng‐iû.
18 Ngŏh keì yan Faatₒ‐lŏ kûng k‘uĭ‐kè mă‐ch‘e mă‐ping tak wīng‐iû, Oi‐k‘āp‐yān tsaû chi ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
19 Sheûng‐Taì chi sz̀‐ché, tsik‐haî tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt kwan ts‘īn‐hāng‐kè, chuèn‐tò k‘uĭ‐teî haû‐pîn hāng; wān‐ch‘uĕ yīk yaū k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘īn‐pîn chuèn‐tò haû‐pîn:
20 Wān laāp tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐yān kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè chung‐kaan; haí kòh‐pin tsaû‐haî wān, haî haak‐òm, haí ni‐pin yê‐kaan faatₒ‐kwong: táng peí‐ts‘ź shīng maăn to pat‐nāng seung‐kân.
21 Moh‐sai shan shaú heùng hoí‐mîn; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lîng taaî‐tung‐fung shīng‐yê ch‘ui hoí shuí t‘uì‐huì, kòh‐ti shuí fan‐hoi, hoí tsaû pìn‐waī hŏn‐teî.
22 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān hāng hoí‐chung hŏn‐teî: shuí tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî tsóh yaû, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘eūng‐pekₒ yat‐yeûng.
23 Oi‐k‘āp‐yān chui‐kón k‘uĭ‐teî, Faatₒ‐lŏ shóh‐yaŭ‐kè mă kûng mă‐ch‘e k‘āp mă‐ping k‘ui kan‐chuê k‘uĭ‐teî yāp hoí‐chung.
24 T‘in‐mung‐kwong chi‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haí fóh‐ch‘uĕ wān‐ch‘uĕ chi‐chung, mông‐hă Oi‐k‘āp kwan‐ping, tsaû lîng k‘uĭ‐teî iŭ‐luên,
25 Chíng‐lat k‘uĭ‐kè ch‘e‐lūn, lîng k‘uĭ naān hāng: ue‐shî Oi‐k‘āp‐yān wâ, Ngŏh‐teî iù t‘ō‐peî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐lokₒ; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘aì k‘uĭ‐teî kung‐tá Oi‐k‘āp‐yān à.
26 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ, Neĭ shan shaú heùng hoí‐mîn, shaí hoí shuí hōp‐faan‐maaī, tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐yān k‘āp k‘uĭ mă‐ch‘e mă‐ping chi‐sheûng.
27 Moh‐sai shan shaú heùng hoí‐mîn, tò t‘in‐kwong, hoí shuí tsoì yŭng heí; Oi‐k‘āp‐yān heùng shuí t‘ō‐tsaú; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû tseung Oi‐k‘āp‐yān t‘ui lōk hoí‐chung.
28 Hoí shuí chuèn‐laū, châm‐hiu mă‐ch‘e mă‐ping, k‘āp kan‐chuê Faatₒ‐lŏ ts‘uēn kwan yāp hoí kè; to mŏ yat‐kòh laū‐faan.
29 Taân‐haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān hāng hoí‐chung hŏn‐teî; shuí tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî tsóh yaû, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘eūng‐pekₒ yat‐yeûng.
30 Kóm‐yeûng, tong kòh yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘íng‐kaù Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān t‘uetₒ‐leī Oi‐k‘āp‐yān shaú; Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kìn Oi‐k‘āp‐yān‐kè shi tsoî hoí‐pin.
31 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kìn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng Oi‐k‘āp‐yān shóh‐hāng‐kè taaî‐nāng: tsaû kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, sùn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kûng k‘uĭ pūk Moh‐sai.
