1 À‐MOH‐SẐ‐KÈ tsaí Ĭ‐ts‘oì‐à shóh‐tak māk‐shî‐chi‐īn, lûn‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè.
2 Tseung‐kân moōt‐k‘eī‐chi‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shìng‐tîn‐kè shaan, pit kìn‐laāp tsoî chùng‐shaan‐chi‐téng, yaû shing‐ko‐kwòh chùng‐shaan‐lĭng‐chi‐sheûng; maân‐kwokₒ pit t‘ūng‐maaī laū kwai k‘uĭ à.
3 Yān‐mān taaî‐toh wŏng‐loī kóm wâ, Taaî‐ka laī‐maaī, táng ngŏh‐teî yat‐ts‘aī sheŭng‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shaan, tò Ngă‐kokₒ‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì‐kè tîn; k‘uĭ ooĭ kaaù‐fàn ngŏh‐teî shik k‘uĭ‐kè lô‐t‘ō, ngŏh‐teî tsaû tsùn‐hāng k‘uĭ‐kè lô‐kìng: yan‐waî lūt‐faatₒ‐pit yaū Sun‐shaan faatₒ‐ch‘ut, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐īn, yaū Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng ch‘uēn‐hoi.
4 K‘uĭ tsoî līt‐kwokₒ chi‐chung, shi shám‐p‘oòn tuk‐chaakₒ chùng‐mān: lîng k‘uĭ‐teî tseung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè to‐kìm tá‐shīng laī‐p‘ā, chi‐ts‘eung tá‐tsô līm‐to: kwokₒ pit m̄‐tsoì kuí to‐kìm laī kung kwokₒ, yīk pit m̄‐hōk kaau‐chìn‐kè sẑ lokₒ.
5 ¶ Ngă‐kokₒ‐kè ka à, ts‘éng neĭ laī, táng ngŏh‐teî yat‐ts‘aī haí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè kwong‐ch‘uè laī‐hāng.
6 Sheûng‐Chué shóh‐ĭ neĭ ts‘āng tiu‐heì neĭ tsź‐mān Ngă‐kokₒ‐kè ka, haî yan k‘uĭ‐teî ch‘ung‐moŏn tsẑ‐tung‐fong‐laī‐kè fung‐tsūk, tsô mō‐shūt‐kè, hó‐ts‘z̆ Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān yat‐yeûng, yaû kûng ngoî‐pong‐kè yān līn‐p‘āng kitₒ‐tóng.
7 Tsaû yaŭ kam‐ngān ch‘ung‐moŏn k‘uĭ‐kè kwokₒ, k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘oī‐paāk to mŏ‐haân‐leûng; yaŭ mă‐p‘at ch‘ung‐tsuk k‘uĭ‐kè teî, mă‐ch‘e yīk mō‐shò‐kòm‐toh:
8 Yīk yaŭ ngaŭ‐tseûng ch‘ung‐moŏn k‘uĭ kíng‐noî; chùng‐yān paaì tsẑ‐keí shaú shóh‐tsokₒ‐kè, tsẑ‐keí shaú‐chí shóh‐chíng‐ch‘ut‐kè:
9 Kóm‐tsaû pei‐meī‐kè yān to wat‐sat, taaî‐waî‐kè yān yīk hâ‐kwaî: shóh‐ĭ m̄‐hó shè‐mĭn k‘uĭ‐teî.
10 Neĭ‐teî iù tsaú‐yāp shēk‐ngaām, tsoî naī‐ch‘ān ni‐maaī, tóh‐peî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè king‐haakₒ, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ wai‐īm‐kè wīng‐kwong.
11 Kòh‐chân‐shī, yān‐ngaăn‐kokₒ‐ko‐kè, pit‐tîng tôh‐lōk pei‐tsîn, yān‐ka kiu‐ngô‐kè sìng‐ts‘īng, pit kòng‐lōk hâ‐laū, tūk‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tak‐shing‐ko lokₒ.
12 Yan‐waî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā laāp‐tîng yat‐yāt, kung‐kik faān‐haî tsẑ‐kiu kûng ngô‐maân‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp faān‐haî tsẑ‐ko‐kè; k‘uĭ tsaû pit tôh‐lōk pei‐tsîn kà:
13 Yaû kung‐kik Leî‐pa‐nuên‐shaan shing‐ko‐kè paakₒ‐heung‐shuê, uĕ‐k‘āp Pa‐shaan shóh‐yaŭ‐kè tseûng‐shuê,
14 Yaû kung‐kik shóh‐yaŭ ko‐shaan, uĕ‐k‘āp shóh‐yaŭ shing‐ko‐kè shaan‐lĭng,
15 Yaû kung‐kik ko‐kìn‐kè mông‐laū, kûng‐maaī kokₒ‐shēng‐oōn,
16 Yaû kung‐kik Taaî‐shat shóh‐yaŭ‐kè shuēn‐chekₒ, t‘ūng‐maaī kokₒ‐yeûng hóh‐oì‐kè meĭ‐tseûng.
17 Kóh‐chân‐shī, yān tsẑ‐ko‐kè sìng‐ts‘īng, pit kòng‐lōk hâ‐laū, yān‐ka kiu‐ngô‐kè sìng‐ts‘īng, pit tôh‐lōk pei‐tsîn: tūk‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tak shing‐ko lokₒ.
18 Kòh‐ti ngaŭ‐tseûng, to iù tsûn‐teî sò‐ch‘uē à.
19 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yûng‐heí taaî‐līk tá‐chàn teî‐k‘aū, kòh‐chân yān‐ka, pit tsaú‐yāp shēk‐ngaām, waāk yāp teî‐lung laī‐tóh‐peî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè king‐haakₒ, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ wai‐īm‐kè wīng‐kwong.
20 Tong kòh‐yāt, yān pit k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ‐ngān‐tsô‐kè ngaŭ‐tseûng, waāk kam‐tsô‐kè ngaŭ‐tseûng, tsik shóh‐chíng laī‐paaì‐kè, wing‐huì peí so‐shué kûng fei‐shué laī‐yûng;
21 Tsaú‐yāp shēk‐luĭ‐kè ngaām, waāk yāp līt‐laân‐kè shēk‐là, laī‐tóh‐peî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè king‐haakₒ, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ wai‐īm‐kè wīng‐kwong, tong k‘uĭ yûng‐heí taaî‐līk tá‐chàn teî‐k‘aū kòh‐shī à.
22 Yān‐ka tsoî mat‐yĕ kaì‐suèn k‘uĭ‐hó ni? pat‐kwòh yaŭ shang‐heì haí peî‐koh‐lung ch‘ut‐yāp che, pat‐hóh í‐laaî k‘uĭ à.
