1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Neĭ iù tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kóng wâ, Foŏ‐yān waaī‐t‘oi shang tsaí: pit ts‘at yāt tòng haî pat‐kitₒ; chiù k‘uĭ hāng‐king kè pat‐kitₒ yāt‐k‘eī yat‐yeûng.
3 Tò taî‐paatₒ yāt, so‐tsaí‐kè yeūng‐p‘eī iù shaû kotₒ.
4 Foŏ‐yān shang‐ch‘aán laū huetₒ, pit táng‐toî saam‐shāp‐saam yāt; īn‐haû fong waī kitₒ‐tsîng, pat‐hóh moh shìng māt, pat‐hóh yāp shìng‐shóh, chì‐tò pat‐kitₒ‐kè yāt‐k‘eī moŏn‐hiu.
5 Yeūk‐haî shang nuĭ, foŏ‐yān iù shāp‐sz̀ yāt, tòng waī pat‐kitₒ, hó‐ts‘z̆ hāng‐king kè yāt‐k‘eī yat‐yeûng: shang‐ch‘aán laū huetₒ, pit tsoì táng lūk‐shāp‐lūk yāt, īn‐haû fong waī kitₒ‐tsîng.
6 Waāk shang tsaí, waāk shang nuĭ, foŏ‐yān shīng waī kitₒ‐tsîng kè yāt‐k‘eī moŏn‐hiu, tsaû iù ning meî‐moŏn yat‐suì‐kè ko‐yeūng tsô faān‐tsaì, yaû yûng yat‐chekₒ paāk‐kopₒ‐tsaí, waāk yat‐chekₒ paan‐kau, tsô shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì, ning tò ooî‐mōk moōn‐ts‘īn, kaau‐kwòh tsaì‐sz:
7 Tsaì‐sz iù hìn tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn, waî foŏ‐yān shūk‐tsuî; kóm‐tsaû foŏ‐yān waî laū huetₒ kè sẑ, shīng waī shìng‐kitₒ kè. Ni‐ti haî foŏ‐yān shang tsaí‐nuĭ kè tîng‐laî.
8 Yeūk‐haî foŏ‐yān ts‘īn‐leūng m̄‐kaù hìn ko‐yeūng, tsaû iù ning leŭng‐chekₒ paan‐kau, waāk leŭng‐chekₒ paāk‐kopₒ‐tsaí; yat‐chekₒ tsô faān‐tsaì, yat‐chekₒ tsô shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì: tsaì‐sz iù waî k‘uĭ shūk‐tsuî, lîng k‘uĭ tak kitₒ‐tsîng.
