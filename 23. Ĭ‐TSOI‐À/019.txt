1 OI‐K‘ĀP ch‘ŭng‐foo‐kè î‐tseûng. ¶ T‘aí‐a, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shīng‐chuê hing‐faaì‐kè wān, laī‐tò Oi‐k‘āp‐ch‘uè: Oi‐k‘āp‐kè ngaŭ‐tseûng, haí k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn ooĭ chàn‐tûng, Oi‐k‘āp‐kè sam, tsoî k‘uĭ ch‘ung ooĭ sòng‐taám à.
2 Ngŏh ooĭ soh‐súng Oi‐k‘āp‐yān: kung‐kik Oi‐k‘āp‐yān: kokₒ‐yān kung‐tá k‘uĭ hing‐taî, yaû kokₒ‐yān kung‐tá lūn‐shè; shēng‐yap kung‐tá shēng‐yap, kwokₒ kung‐tá kwokₒ à.
3 Oi‐k‘āp‐kè sam‐shān tsoî k‘uĭ ch‘ung pit‐shīng‐waī hung‐hui; k‘uĭ shóh‐sheung‐leūng maū‐tîng‐kè, ngŏh ooĭ t‘an‐mīt: k‘uĭ tsaû heùng ngaŭ‐tseûng laī‐k‘aū, waāk heùng mō‐shūt, waāk heùng p‘āng‐kwaí‐kè, waāk heùng naām‐mōh‐kè.
4 Ngŏh pit tseung Oi‐k‘āp‐yān kaau‐kwòh hak‐pōk‐yān‐kè shaú; yīk pit yaŭ k‘eūng‐maăng‐kè wōng koón‐hāt k‘uĭ‐teî, Sheûng‐Chué Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kóm wâ là.
5 Ts‘ūng taaî‐hoí laī‐kè shuí ooĭ tsuēt‐hiu, kóh‐t‘iū‐hōh tsîm‐tsĭm mŏ‐shuí; ī‐ch‘é ooĭ‐kon‐k‘okₒ‐kè.
6 Chùng‐hōh ooĭ‐faatₒ‐ch‘aù; Oi‐k‘āp kokₒ‐shuí‐lô, tsîm‐tsĭm ooĭ‐saì, yaû ooĭ‐kon t‘im: shóh‐yaŭ maaū‐ts‘ó lō‐waĭ foo‐kó‐hiu.
7 Kân Neī‐lōh‐hōh ts‘ó‐t‘īn, tsik‐haî t‘ipₒ‐kân Neī‐lōh‐hōh‐pin‐kè, ī‐ch‘é kân Neī‐lōh‐hōh tsóh‐yaû shóh‐saatₒ‐chúng‐kè, to ooĭ kon‐foo, tsaû peî‐ch‘ui‐huì shīng‐waī mŏ‐hiu.
8 Lóh‐uē‐kè yān pit‐pei‐oi, faān haí Neī‐lōh‐hōh tiù‐uē‐kè ooĭ t‘aī‐huk, saatₒ‐mŏng‐tsoî‐shuí‐sheûng‐kè ooĭ shui‐paaî.
9 Kim‐ch‘é lîng maakₒ‐mā chik‐pò‐kè yān, k‘āp chik‐paāk‐pò‐kè, pit shat‐mông kìn sau‐yūk.
10 Shing‐heí k‘uĭ kokₒ‐ch‘uĕ pit‐faì‐ch‘aakₒ‐kè, faān koò‐kung‐yān ooĭ‐yau‐shaū lokₒ.
11 Sóh‐on‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, pìn‐saaì uē‐ch‘ún, Faatₒ‐lŏ chì chì‐waî‐kè ch‘aakₒ‐sẑ, shóh‐sheung‐leūng maū‐tîng‐kè, to suèn‐haî shaù‐sam shóh‐faatₒ‐kè che: neĭ tím‐kaaí tuì Faatₒ‐lŏ wâ, Ngŏh haî chì‐waî‐yān‐kè tsaí, haî koó‐īn wōng‐kè suen ni?
12 Kóm‐tsaû chì‐waî‐yān haí pin‐ch‘uè ni? iù k‘uĭ uē‐kam wâ‐neĭ‐chi, yaû iù lîng k‘uĭ chi‐tò Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, heùng Oi‐k‘āp shóh‐tîng‐tuēt‐kè sẑ à.
13 Sóh‐on‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, ts‘āng pìn‐waī uē‐ch‘ún, Nōh‐fat‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, peî‐yān‐hei‐p‘ìn; tsô k‘uĭ chùng‐tsūk uk‐kokₒ‐kè shēk, ts‘āng lîng Oi‐k‘āp hāng ts‘òh lokₒ.
14 Tsoî k‘uĭ chung‐kaan, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng kaaú‐wān waâng‐yīk‐kè shān: táng‐tsoî faān‐shóh‐tsô‐kè, k‘uĭ tsaû lîng Oi‐k‘āp hāng‐ts‘òh‐lokₒ, hó‐ts‘z̆ tsuì‐tsaú‐kè yān aú‐t‘ò kòh‐shī, hāng m̄‐tak‐wán yat‐yeûng.
15 Ī‐ch‘é waî Oi‐k‘āp pit mŏ‐yat‐yeûng kung‐foo hóh‐ĭ tsô, tsik‐haî t‘aū waāk meĭ, tsó‐shuê‐kè chi, waāk lō‐waĭ ts‘ó hóh‐tsô.
16 ¶ Tong kóh‐yāt, Oi‐k‘āp tsaû hó‐ts‘z̆ nuĭ‐yān kóm: yīk pit‐yan maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kuí‐tûng k‘uĭ shaú, tsik‐haî tsoî k‘uĭ sheûng‐t‘aū kuí‐tûng‐kè, tsaû chìn‐lūt king‐fong à.
17 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐teî to ooĭ‐tsô Oi‐k‘āp shóh‐kuî‐p‘à‐kè, taân‐faān t‘eng‐yān‐kóng‐k‘āp to ooĭ‐king‐fong, haî yan‐waî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng k‘uĭ shóh‐tîng‐tuēt‐kè chí‐ì lokₒ.
18 ¶ Tong kóh‐yāt, tsoî Oi‐k‘āp yaŭ nğ‐kòh shēng‐yap, kóng Ka‐naām‐kè t‘ó‐wâ, yaû heùng maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faatₒ‐shaî; yat‐kòh tsaû kiù‐tsô Tseung‐mīt‐kè shēng.
19 ¶ Tong kóh‐yāt, tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐teî chi‐chung, tsaû yaŭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yat‐tsôh‐t‘aān, yaû tsoî k‘uĭ kíng‐kaaì waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaŭ yat‐t‘iū‐ch‘uĕ.
20 Tsaû haî tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐teî waî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô keì‐hô, yaû tsô chìng‐kuì: yan k‘uĭ‐teî shî‐pit waî naān‐waī k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè uēn‐koò, heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haàm‐kiù, k‘uĭ pit tá‐faatₒ yat‐kòh kaù‐chué, yat‐kòh yaŭ‐nāng pó‐oô‐kè, tsaû pit‐tîng ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ‐teî.
21 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung pit‐peî‐Oi‐k‘āp shóh‐shik‐kè, tong kóh‐yāt, Oi‐k‘āp‐yān tsaû ooĭ‐shik‐tak Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yaû k‘aaĭ hìn‐tsaì kûng laĭ‐māt laī‐kìng‐paaì; yaû heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaî‐uên; ī‐ch‘é tsô‐shīng shóh‐shaî‐kè uên lokₒ.
22 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit ooĭ kik‐tá Oi‐k‘āp: yaû tá yaû i‐hó: k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû ooĭ chuèn‐heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘uĭ pit chún k‘uĭ‐teî k‘aū‐tó, tsaû i‐hó k‘uĭ‐teî là.
23 ¶ Tong kóh‐yāt, ts‘ūng Oi‐k‘āp tò À‐shūt, pit yaŭ yat‐t‘iū taaî‐lô, À‐shūt tseung ooĭ yāp Oi‐k‘āp, yaû Oi‐k‘āp‐yān tseung ooĭ‐yāp À‐shūt‐teî; ī‐ch‘é Oi‐k‘āp‐yān pit‐kûng À‐shūt‐yān t‘ūng‐sẑ Chué à.
24 ¶ Tong kóh‐yāt, Ĭ‐shik‐līt kûng Oi‐k‘āp, À‐shūt, hōp‐maaī waī‐saam‐ché‐chi‐yat, tsoî teî‐chung shi‐fuk‐kè:
25 Yan‐waî maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng chuk‐fuk k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Oi‐k‘āp ngŏh‐kè tsź‐mān, À‐shūt ngŏh shaú shóh‐tsô‐kè, Ĭ‐shik‐līt ngŏh‐kè kei‐īp, kaai‐tak‐fuk lokₒ.
