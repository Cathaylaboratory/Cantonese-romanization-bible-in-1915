1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ à, k‘aū neĭ m̄‐hó sam‐nau chap‐chaakₒ ngŏh: m̄‐hó faatₒ taaî‐nô chaakₒ‐fāt ngŏh.
2 Neĭ‐chi‐tsìn kat‐chuê ngŏh, neĭ‐chi‐shaú aatₒ‐chuê ngŏh.
3 Yan neĭ‐chi‐nô, shóh‐ĭ ngŏh yūk k‘ui shaû suén‐sheung; yan ngŏh‐chi‐tsuî, shóh‐ĭ ngŏh kwat tsúng m̄‐on‐lōk.
4 Ngŏh tsuî ko‐kwòh ngŏh t‘aū: yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ taam‐chuê ch‘ŭng‐māt, ch‘ŭng‐kwòh‐ngŏh‐chi‐līk.
5 Yan ngŏh uē‐ch‘ún mŏng‐waī, shan shaû ch‘ŭng‐sheung, laân ī‐ch‘é ch‘aù.
6 Ngŏh hó‐foó‐ch‘óh; wat‐luen kòh‐shan laī‐hāng; shīng‐yāt shaū‐moôn.
7 Ngŏh iu‐kwat hó‐ts‘z̆ fóh‐shiu: ngŏh yūk‐tò‐ch‘uè to laân.
8 Ngŏh shan‐tsź hui‐hōn shaû‐sheung: sam‐chung yau‐shaū, taaî‐sheng foo‐kiù.
9 Chué à, ngŏh shóh‐seúng‐ché, to tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn; ngŏh‐chi‐tse‐t‘aàn, yīk pat‐nāng che‐moōn neĭ.
10 Ngŏh sam t‘iù‐tûng, ngŏh līk shui‐yeūk: ngaăn‐tsing mō kwong, mūng‐mooî‐saaì.
11 Ngŏh sui shaû‐sheung, ngŏh‐chi p‘āng‐yaŭ leī‐hoi ngŏh k‘eĭ; ngŏh‐chi‐ts‘an‐ts‘ik, yīk kaakₒ‐uĕn k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè.
12 Seúng sheung‐ngŏh‐shang‐mîng‐ché, ch‘itₒ‐laāp huen‐t‘ò; seúng‐hoî‐ngŏh‐ché, haú ch‘ut okₒ‐īn, shīng‐yāt maū‐tōk kwaí‐kaì.
13 Taân ngŏh hó‐ts‘z̆ ĭ‐lūng m̄‐t‘eng‐kìn; hó‐ts‘z̆ haú‐á m̄‐ooĭ kóng.
14 Ngŏh hó‐ts‘z̆ yat‐kòh m̄‐t‘eng‐tak, haú tsúng mŏ chaakₒ‐fāt yān.
15 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, ngŏh waī‐haî yeŭng‐mông neĭ: ngŏh Chué Sheûng‐Taì, k‘aū neĭ t‘eng ngŏh.
16 Ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng wâ, P‘à k‘uĭ‐teî yan ngŏh shaû‐naân tsaû foon‐heí: yan ngŏh keukₒ‐shím, k‘uĭ tsaû king‐kw‘a, laī kung‐kik ngŏh.
17 Ngŏh shī‐shī to tseung‐kân hitₒ‐pô, ngŏh‐chi‐yau‐luî, sheūng‐tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn.
18 Ngŏh chiu‐yîng tsẑ‐keí‐chi‐okₒ; ī‐ch‘é yan‐ngŏh‐chi‐tsuî, sam‐chung yau‐wat.
19 Taân hoî ngŏh shang‐mîng‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, k‘ui haî k‘eūng‐yŭng: mŏ uēn‐koò laī‐tsang‐oò‐ngŏh‐ché, k‘eī yān kīk‐toh.
20 K‘uĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ okₒ pò shîn, ngŏh haî ts‘ūng shîn; koò‐ts‘ź k‘uĭ tsô ngŏh ch‘aū‐tīk.
21 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, m̄‐hó tiu‐heì ngŏh: ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì à, m̄‐hó uĕn‐leī ngŏh.
22 Chué à, neĭ haî ngŏh‐chi‐kaù‐ché, faaì laī kaù ngŏh.
