1 K‘AŪ Sheûng‐Taì tseung neĭ‐chi‐mēng kaù ngŏh, tseung neĭ‐chi‐līk, waî ngŏh shan‐uen.
2 K‘aū Sheûng‐Taì t‘eng ngŏh‐chi t‘ó‐kò; chak‐ĭ‐t‘eng ngŏh haú‐chung īn‐uĕ.
3 Ngoî‐yān heí‐laī kung‐kik ngŏh, k‘eūng‐waāng‐chi‐yān, seúng hoî ngŏh sìng‐mîng: k‘uĭ m̄‐k‘aaĭ Sheûng‐Taì chai‐tsoî ngaăn‐ts‘īn.
4 Sheûng‐Taì tseung ngŏh pó‐oô: Chué yīk pó‐yaû kòh‐ti foō‐chôh‐ngŏh‐chi‐yān.
5 Ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, neĭ pit kòng‐tsoi kwòh‐k‘uĭ: k‘aū Chué i neĭ‐chi‐chung‐sùn mīt‐tsuēt k‘uĭ.
6 Ngŏh pit kom‐sam tsaì‐tsẑ neĭ: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mēng haî chì‐hó; ngŏh iù tsaàn‐tsûng neĭ mēng.
7 Yan neĭ ĭ‐king kaù ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐leī kokₒ‐naân: ngŏh ĭ‐king ts‘an‐ngaăn kìn ch‘aū‐tīk shaû‐pò.
