1 TONG kòh‐yāt, À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng, tseung Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè ch‘aū‐tīk, Hap‐maân‐kè uk, ts‘z̀‐kwòh wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ. Mūk‐taí‐koí yīk laī‐tò wōng‐mîn‐ts‘īn; yan‐waî Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ ĭ‐king tseung Mūk‐taí‐koí, haî k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘an‐ts‘ik kóng‐kwòh‐wōng‐chi.
2 Wōng ch‘uē‐lat tsẑ‐keí‐kè kaaì‐chí, tsik‐haî ts‘ūng Hap‐maân lóh‐faan‐kè, tsaû ts‘z̀‐kwòh Mūk‐taí‐koí. Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ laāp Mūk‐taí‐koí koón‐leĭ Hap‐maân‐kè ka.
3 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ tsoì pūk‐tsoî wōng‐keukₒ‐hâ, laū‐ngaăn‐luî hán‐k‘aū k‘uĭ faì‐ch‘uē À‐kaapₒ tsūk Hap‐maân‐kè hoî, k‘āp k‘uĭ maū‐kung Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè okₒ‐maū.
4 Wōng heùng Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ kuí‐heí kam‐kwai. Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ tsaû heí‐shan k‘eĭ‐tsoî wōng mîn‐ts‘īn.
5 Wâ, À‐kaapₒ tsūk, Hap‐maân ch‘itₒ‐kaì ch‘uēn‐chí, iù shaatₒ‐mīt haí wōng kokₒ‐shaáng‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, uē‐kam ngŏh yeūk tak wōng yan‐ch‘úng, wōng yeūk ĭ‐waī shîn, yeūk foon‐heí ngŏh, ts‘éng wōng lîng ch‘ut‐yat‐chí, koí‐yīk Hap‐maân shóh‐ch‘uēn kòh‐ti chí‐ì:
6 Yan‐waî ngŏh tím yán kìn tsẑ‐keí poón‐tsūk‐kè yān kóm shaû hoî? tím yán kìn tsẑ‐keí poón‐ka‐kè yān peî‐mīt ni?
7 Ue‐shî À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng tuì wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān Mūk‐taí‐koí wâ, Yan Hap‐maân seúng hoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, ngŏh ĭ‐king tseung k‘uĭ‐kè ka ts‘z̀‐kwòh Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ, chùng‐yān ĭ‐king tiù Hap‐maân tsoî mūk‐kà‐sheûng.
8 În‐tsoî neĭ‐teî waî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, hóh‐ĭ yâm‐ts‘ūng neĭ‐teî‐kè ì, t‘okₒ ngŏh mēng sé‐sheûng‐uê, yûng wōng‐kè kaaì‐chí laī‐yàn: yan‐waî faān t‘okₒ‐wōng‐mēng‐laī‐sé, yûng wōng‐kaaì‐chí laī‐yàn‐kè uê‐chí, mŏ‐yān hóh‐kang‐koí à.
9 Tong‐shī shik‐chīk taî‐saam‐uēt, tsik‐haî Sai‐faan‐uēt‐kè î‐shāp‐saam yāt; chiû wōng‐kè shîn‐sé‐ché laī, i‐chuê Mūk‐taí‐koí shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè laī‐sé sheûng‐uê, ch‘uēn‐kwòh ts‘ūng Yàn‐tô chīk‐tò Koó‐shāt, yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐ts‘at shaáng‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, kûng taaî‐shān fong‐paakₒ kwan‐cheúng, to òn kokₒ‐shaáng kokₒ‐tsūk‐kè mān‐tsẑ t‘ó‐yam, yīk òn Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè mān‐tsź t‘ó‐yam.
10 Mūk‐taí‐koí t‘okₒ À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng‐kè mēng sé‐sheûng‐uê, yûng wōng‐kè kaaì‐chí laī‐yàn, tsaû kaau‐kwòh k‘ē‐faaì‐mă‐kè, waāk k‘ē‐luī, waāk siú‐lokₒ‐t‘ōh‐kè yīk‐tsut, ch‘uēn‐tò kokₒ‐ch‘uè:
11 Tsoî sheûng‐uê, wōng chún‐tsoî kokₒ‐shēng‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān tsuî‐tsaāp, pó‐oô tsẑ‐keí, tsiú‐mīt shaatₒ‐lūk, faān‐kokₒ‐shaáng kokₒ‐mān, seúng kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐teî, lîng k‘uĭ uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ nuên‐tsaí, kûng foŏ‐nuĭ sź‐mōng‐kè, ī‐ch‘é ts‘eúng‐tuēt kòh‐ti‐yān‐kè ts‘oī‐māt,
12 Tsoî yat‐yāt‐noî laī‐tsô, haí À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng‐kè kokₒ‐shaáng, tsik‐haî tsoî shāp‐î‐uēt, mēng‐kiù À‐taāt‐uēt‐kè shāp‐saam yāt.
13 Shóh‐ch‘aau‐sé‐kè, tsik‐haî sheûng‐uê paan‐hāng kokₒ‐shaáng, ch‘uēn‐shî kokₒ‐tsūk, shaí Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān tsẑ‐keí uê‐peî, táng‐haû kòh‐yāt, heùng k‘uĭ‐teî ch‘aū‐tīk pò‐ch‘aū.
14 Kóm‐tsaû k‘ē‐faaì‐mă‐kè yīk‐tsut, yan‐waî fûng wōng‐mîng, kap‐ts‘uk hāng‐huì. Ts‘ź‐sheûng‐uê haî ts‘ūng Shue‐shaan‐kung faatₒ‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè.
15 Mūk‐taí‐koí cheukₒ laām‐shik, paāk‐shik‐kè ch‘iū‐fūk, t‘aū taaì taaî‐kam‐koon‐mĭn, yaû cheukₒ tsź‐shik, yaù‐saì‐mā‐pò‐kè ngoî‐p‘ō, ts‘ūng wōng mîn‐ts‘īn ch‘ut‐huì: Shue‐shaan shēng‐kè tsź‐mān, taaî foo foon‐heí.
16 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān kaai‐yaŭ kwong‐wā, yaû foon‐heí, yaû faaì‐lōk, yaû tak tsuen‐kwaì.
17 Wōng‐kè sheûng‐uê, m̄‐lûn tò pin‐shaáng pin‐shēng, Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān to‐foon‐heí faaì‐lōk, paaí‐ch‘itₒ tsaú‐ìn, ĭ kóh‐yāt waī hó‐yāt‐tsź. Yaû yan‐waî kuî‐p‘à Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān; hó‐toh t‘ó‐mān yāp Yaū‐t‘aaì‐tsīk.
