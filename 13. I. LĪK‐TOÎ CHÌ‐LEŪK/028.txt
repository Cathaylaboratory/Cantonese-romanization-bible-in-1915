1 TAAÎ‐WAÎ tsuî‐tsaāp‐kè chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, tsik‐haî kokₒ‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè kwan‐cheúng, fūk‐sẑ wōng‐kè paan‐cheúng, k‘āp ts‘in‐foo‐cheúng, paakₒ‐foo‐cheúng, koón‐leĭ wōng‐kè ka‐ts‘oī, kûng wōng‐tsź‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, k‘āp yaŭ koon‐k‘uēn, kûng taaî‐nāng‐kè, tsik‐haî faān yaŭ taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng.
2 Taaî‐waî wōng tsaû k‘eĭ‐laāp wâ, Ngŏh hing‐taî, k‘āp ngŏh tsź‐mān à, iù t‘eng ngŏh la: Chì‐ue ngŏh, ngŏh sam‐chung seúng waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeukₒ‐kwaî, ī‐ch‘é waî ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè keukₒ‐taāp‐tàng laī chuk tîn‐uk, on‐chì yeukₒ‐kwaî, ngŏh ĭ‐king uê‐peî kìn‐chuk‐kè ts‘oī‐liû lokₒ:
3 Taân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì ngŏh wâ, Yan neĭ haî chìn‐sẑ, ts‘āng laū yān huetₒ, koò‐ts‘ź m̄‐hó waî ngŏh mēng kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk à.
4 Waī‐haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, sik‐yāt kaán‐suén ngŏh tsoî ngŏh foô ts‘uēn‐ka, shaí ngŏh wĭng‐uĕn tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè wōng: yan‐waî k‘uĭ kaán‐suén Yaū‐t‘aaì chi‐p‘aaì tsô kwan‐cheúng; tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐chung kaán‐suén ngŏh foô‐kè ka; haí ngŏh foô‐kè chùng‐tsź‐chi‐chung, tsaû foon‐heí laāp ngŏh tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chùng‐chi‐wōng:
5 (Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘z̀ ngŏh yaŭ hó‐toh tsaí,) haí ngŏh tsaí‐chi‐chung, kaán‐suén ngŏh tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, ts‘ŏh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè kwokₒ‐waî, koón‐leĭ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì ngŏh wâ, Neĭ tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, pit tseung kìn‐chuk ngŏh tîn‐uk, k‘āp kokₒ‐uên: yan‐waî ngŏh ĭ‐king kaán‐suén k‘uĭ tsô ngŏh‐kè tsaí, ngŏh tseung pit tsô k‘uĭ‐kè foô.
7 K‘uĭ t‘óng‐yeūk shī‐sheūng tsun ngŏh kaaì‐mîng faatₒ‐tô, hó‐ts‘z̆ kam‐yāt, ngŏh pit kin‐laāp k‘uĭ kwokₒ, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn.
8 Koò‐ts‘ź tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐chùng, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ooî‐chùng‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn, ngŏh‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì yīk t‘eng‐mān ngŏh uē‐kam huèn neĭ‐teî ts‘ām‐k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yat‐ts‘aì kaaì‐mîng: táng neĭ‐teî tak‐tó ts‘ź‐meĭ‐teî, laū‐ts‘uēn neĭ‐kè haû‐toî, wĭng‐uĕn tsô ch‘aán‐īp.
9 Ngŏh tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn à, neĭ iù shik neĭ foô‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, shīng‐sam‐lōk‐ì laī‐fūk‐sẑ k‘uĭ: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ yān‐sam, chi‐tò faān‐shóh‐seúng‐ch‘ut‐kè nîm‐t‘aū: neĭ yeūk ts‘ām‐k‘aū k‘uĭ, k‘uĭ pit peí neĭ wán‐tó; neĭ yeūk tiu‐heì k‘uĭ, k‘uĭ pit wĭng‐uĕn heì‐tsuēt neĭ.
10 Neĭ iù kán‐shân; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng kaán‐suén neĭ kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk k‘āp shìng‐shóh: neĭ iù kin‐k‘eūng huì‐tsô.
11 ¶ Ue‐shî Taaî‐waî tseung tîn‐uk yaū‐lōng, foó‐foò, laū‐fōng, noî‐tîn, k‘āp shūk‐tsuî‐chi‐shóh kòh‐kaan, kûng yat‐ts‘aì‐kè foón‐shik, kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn,
12 Yaû kaau peî Shìng‐Shān kóm‐tûng, shóh‐tak‐kè foón‐shik, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè kokₒ‐uên, sz̀‐waī‐kè fōng, Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐kè foó‐foò, k‘āp shìng‐pīt‐chi‐māt‐kè foó‐foò:
13 Yīk tseung tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè paan‐ts‘z̀, uĕ‐k‘āp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk fūk‐yīk‐kè kung‐foo, kûng tîn‐uk‐noî yat‐ts‘aì heì‐mĭng‐kè foón‐shik, kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ.
14 Tseung kam ch‘ìng‐kwòh, ts‘z̀‐peí k‘uĭ tsô kokₒ‐yeûng shóh‐yûng‐kè kam‐heì; yaû tseung ngān ch‘ìng‐kwòh, tsô kokₒ‐yeûng shóh‐yûng‐kè ngān‐heì:
15 Tsik‐haî tsô kam‐tang‐t‘oī: ni‐ti tang‐t‘oī kûng tang, yīk haî chiù‐i tsô kokₒ‐chuk t‘oī shóh‐yûng‐kè‐à.
16 Yaû tseung kam ch‘ìng‐kwòh tsô paaí‐ch‘itₒ‐péng‐kè t‘oī, tsik‐haî tsô kokₒ‐t‘oī; yaû tseung ngān tsô ngān‐t‘oī:
17 Yûng tsing‐kam laī tsô yūk‐ngau, k‘āp p‘oōn, pooi: yaû ch‘ìng kam tsô kokₒ‐kam‐oón; ch‘ìng ngān tsô kokₒ‐ngān‐oón:
18 Yaû yûng tsing‐kam ch‘ìng‐kwòh tsô heung‐t‘aān; yaû yûng kam tsô ch‘e‐kè foón‐shik, tsik‐haî kei‐lô‐ping shan‐hoi‐leŭng yīk, che‐koì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeukₒ‐kwaî.
19 Taaî‐waî wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yûng shaú waāk‐ch‘ut‐laī, lîng ngŏh hiú‐tak‐saaì ts‘ź shóh‐yaŭ‐kè foón‐shik.
20 Taaî‐waî yaû tuì k‘uĭ tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wâ, Neĭ iù kin‐k‘eūng kong‐ngaî laī tsô: m̄‐hó kuî‐p‘à, m̄‐hó king‐fong: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Sheûng‐Taì tsik‐haî ngŏh‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, haî t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ; k‘uĭ pit m̄‐leī‐hoi neĭ, m̄‐tiu‐heì neĭ, chì‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk fūk‐yīk kung‐foo tsô‐shīng.
21 Neĭ t‘aí‐a, yaŭ tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè kokₒ‐paan, tsô Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè chik‐yīk: yaû‐yaŭ keî‐ngaî tsing‐haaú‐kè yān, pit foon‐heí t‘ūng neĭ tsô kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè kung‐foo: chùng‐paan‐cheúng uĕ‐k‘āp chùng‐tsź‐mān, to pit‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng neĭ mîng‐lîng à.
