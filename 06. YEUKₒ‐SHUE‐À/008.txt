1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Neĭ m̄‐shaí king‐fong, m̄‐shaí kuî‐p‘à: neĭ heí‐shan taaì‐lĭng chùng‐kwan‐ping, sheŭng‐huì kung‐tà Aai‐shēng: ngŏh ts‘āng tseung Aai‐kè wōng, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, k‘uĭ‐kè shēng, k‘uĭ‐kè teî, to kaau‐kwòh‐neĭ shaú:
2 Neĭ iù heùng Aai‐shēng kûng Aai‐kè wōng, hó‐ts‘z̆ ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn chuen‐heùng Yē‐leî‐koh, kûng Yē‐leî‐koh wōng shóh‐tsô‐kè laī‐tsô: taân shóh ts‘eúng‐tuēt‐kè ts‘oī‐māt k‘āp ch‘uk‐shang, neĭ‐teî hóh‐ĭ tseung‐laī kwai‐ue tsẑ‐keí‐kè yûng: neĭ iù maaī‐fūk ping‐ting tsoî shēng‐haû.
3 Ue‐shî Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kûng chùng‐kwan‐ping heí‐shan, seúng sheŭng‐huì kung‐tá Aai‐shēng: Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kaán‐suén saam‐maân taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ, yê‐maăn tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî huì.
4 Fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù maaī‐fūk kwan‐ping tsoî shēng‐haû‐pîn: chùng‐yān iù uê‐peî, m̄‐hó leī shēng kòm‐uĕn:
5 Ngŏh kûng ngŏh‐shóh tsaāp‐lĭng‐kè chùng‐yān, to pit hāng kân shēng: k‘uĭ‐teî yeūk hó‐ts‘z̆ sin‐shī ch‘ut shēng laī kung‐tá ngŏh‐teî, ngŏh‐teî tsaû tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘īn‐pîn t‘ō‐tsaú, táng yăn‐yaŭ k‘uĭ‐teî ch‘ut shēng,
6 (Yan k‘uĭ‐teî pit chui‐kón ngŏh‐teî,) peí‐ts‘ź seung‐kóng, wâ, Ti‐yān hó‐ts‘z̆ sin‐shī tsoî ngŏh‐teî ts‘īn‐pîn t‘ō‐tsaú lokₒ: shóh‐ĭ ngŏh‐teî iù tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî ts‘īn‐pîn t‘ō‐tsaú.
7 Kòh‐shī, neĭ‐teî tsaû ts‘ūng maaī‐fūk kóh‐ch‘uè ch‘ut‐laī chìm‐tuēt kòh‐shēng: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit tseung shēng kaau‐kwòh neĭ‐teî shaú.
8 Neĭ‐teî tuēt‐hiu shēng, iù yûng fóh laī shiu: pit tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò laī‐tsô. Ngŏh ĭ‐king fan‐foò neĭ‐teî lokₒ.
9 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî ch‘ut‐huì: chùng‐yān tsaû huì‐tò maaī‐fūk‐kè teî‐fong laī chuè‐chaatₒ, tsik‐haî tsoî Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî kûng Aai‐shēng‐kè chung‐kaan, Aai‐shēng‐kè sai‐pîn: kóh maăn Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsoî paakₒ‐sìng‐chung hitₒ‐suk.
10 ¶ Mīng‐yāt chiu‐tsó, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à hó‐tsó heí‐shan, tím‐kwòh yān‐shò, t‘ūng‐maaī Ĭ‐shik‐līt kokₒ‐cheúng‐lŏ, taaì‐lĭng chùng‐yān sheŭng‐huì Aai‐shēng.
11 T‘ūng‐maaī Yeukₒ‐shue‐à‐kè chùng‐kwan, to sheŭng‐huì kân‐chuê shēng, on‐yīng tsoî Aai‐shēng pak‐pîn: tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî kûng Aai‐shēng‐kè chung‐kaan, yaŭ kòh‐shaan‐kuk.
12 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kaán‐chaāk yān taaî‐yeukₒ yaŭ nğ‐ts‘in, shaí k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî, k‘āp Aai‐shēng‐kè chung‐kaan maaī‐fūk, tsaû heùng shēng‐kè sai‐pîn.
13 Paakₒ‐sìng ts‘uēn‐kwan to paaí‐līt tsoî shēng‐pak, fūk ping paaí‐līt tsoî shēng‐sai, kóh‐maăn, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à yāp‐huì shaan‐kuk‐chung.
14 Aai‐shēng‐kè wōng t‘aí‐kìn, tsaû kûng shēng‐luĭ‐kè yān, chiu‐tsó kap‐ts‘uk heí‐shan, yeukₒ‐tîng shī‐haû, ch‘ut‐huì kw‘òng‐yĕ ts‘īn‐pîn, kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tá‐cheùng, wōng kûng taaî‐chùng paakₒ‐sìng t‘ūng huì; k‘uĭ m̄‐chi yaŭ fūk ping tsoî shēng‐haû, ooĭ kung‐tá k‘uĭ‐teî à.
15 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî mîn‐ts‘īn chà‐paaî, heùng kw‘òng‐yĕ‐kè lô t‘ō‐tsaú.
16 Shóh‐yaŭ Aai‐shēng‐kè yān, to tsuî‐maaī huì chui‐kón Yeukₒ‐shue‐à, kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān: k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû peî yăn‐yaŭ, leī‐hoi kòh‐shēng.
17 Aai‐shēng kûng Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî‐shēng‐chung, mŏ yat‐kòh‐yān m̄‐ch‘ut‐huì chui‐kón Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān: mŏ kwaan shēng‐moōn, huì chui‐kón Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
18 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Neĭ kuí‐heí shaú‐sheûng shóh‐cha‐kè ts‘eung, heùng‐chuê Aai‐shēng; yan‐waî ngŏh pit tseung ni‐kòh shēng kaau‐kwòh neĭ shaú. Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsaû kuí‐heí shaú‐sheûng‐kè ts‘eung heùng‐chuê Aai‐shēng.
19 K‘uĭ shaú yat‐kuí, fūk‐ping tsaû ts‘ūng maaī‐fūk‐kè teî‐fong, kap‐ts‘uk heí‐shan: tsaú‐yāp shēng, tuēt‐hiu, tsaû yûng fóh laī‐shiu.
20 Aai‐shēng‐yān ooī‐t‘aū mông‐hă chi‐shī, tsaû‐kìn‐shēng‐chung in‐îm ch‘ung t‘in, m̄‐lûn heùng pin‐t‘iū lô, to pat‐nāng t‘ō‐tsaú: tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ chà‐paaî t‘ō‐tsaú‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, to faan‐chuèn‐shan, laī kung‐tá Aai‐shēng‐yān.
21 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, t‘aí‐kìn maaī‐fūk‐kè ping tak‐hiu kòh‐shēng, shēng‐chung in‐îm fei‐heí, tsaû faan‐chuèn‐shan kung‐shaatₒ Aai‐shēng‐yān.
22 Maaī‐fūk‐kè ping, yīk ch‘ut shēng kung‐tá Aai‐shēng‐yān; shaí k‘uĭ‐teî kw‘àn‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chung‐kaan; ni‐pîn kóh‐pîn, to yaŭ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān: tseung k‘uĭ‐teî shaatₒ‐saaì, mŏ yat‐kòh laū‐lōk.
23 Chùng‐yān tsaû kaàm‐shang chukₒ‐chuê Aai‐wōng, laai k‘uĭ tò Yeukₒ‐shue‐à‐ch‘uè.
24 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsoî t‘īn‐ngoî, tsik‐haî chui‐kón k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè kw‘òng‐yĕ, shaatₒ shóh‐yaŭ‐kè Aai‐yān, chì‐tò tsûn‐teî tsiú‐mīt, īn‐haû Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān faan‐chuèn yāp Aai‐shēng, yûng to shaatₒ‐saaì shēng‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè yān.
25 Kòh‐yāt, sź‐mōng‐kè naām nuĭ, kûng‐kaì yaŭ yat‐maân‐î‐ts‘in yān, tsik‐haî Aai‐shēng faān‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè yān.
26 Yan Yeukₒ‐shue‐à m̄‐shau‐ooī kuí ts‘eung‐kè shaú, chì‐tò mīt‐saaì faān chuê‐tsoî Aai‐shēng‐kè yān.
27 Tūk‐haî ch‘uk‐shang k‘āp shóh‐ts‘eúng‐shēng‐luĭ‐kè ts‘oī‐māt, tsaû kwai‐ue tsẑ‐keí, haî tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Yeukₒ‐shue‐à‐kè mîng‐lîng à.
28 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tseung Aai‐shēng fān‐shiu, shaí k‘uĭ wĭng‐uĕn tsô t‘ó‐tui, hung‐kw‘òng chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
29 Yaû tseung Aai‐wōng kwà‐tsoî shuê‐sheûng: tò aai‐maăn īt‐t‘aū‐lōk‐kóh‐shī, chùng‐yān i Yeukₒ‐shue‐à shóh‐fan‐foò, tsaû tseung shi shaú ning‐lōk‐laī, wing‐yāp shēng‐moōn‐haú, yaû tseung shēk‐t‘aū tui tsoî k‘uĭ shan‐sheûng, shīng‐waī ko‐kong, laū‐ts‘uēn tò kam‐yāt.
30 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsoî Ĭ‐paatₒ shaan, waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heí yat‐kòh‐t‘aān,
31 Haî yûng uēn‐ts‘uēn‐kè shēk, yān meî‐ts‘āng k‘aaĭ t‘itₒ‐heì tsōk‐kwòh‐kè laī‐heí kòh‐t‘aān, tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pūk‐yān Moh‐sai shóh‐fan‐foò Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, hó‐ts‘z̆ Moh‐sai‐kè lūt‐faatₒ‐shue shóh‐tsoì kóm: chùng‐yān tsoî ni‐kòh t‘aān‐sheûng, tseung faān‐tsaì hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yaû hìn ch‘aū‐yan‐tsaì.
32 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à haí kóh‐ch‘uè tseung Moh‐sai‐kè lūt‐faatₒ, tong Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn, chiù‐shik sé‐tsoî shēk‐sheûng.
33 Ĭ‐shīk‐līt chùng‐yān, kûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè cheúng‐lŏ, koon‐cheúng, shám‐sẑ‐koon, waāk luĭ‐haakₒ, waāk poón‐t‘ó‐yān, to k‘eĭ‐tsoî yeukₒ‐kwaî‐kè leŭng‐p‘ōng, tsoî t‘oī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yeukₒ‐kwaî‐kè Leî‐meî‐yān kokₒ‐tsaì‐sz mîn‐ts‘īn; yat poòn tuì‐chuê K‘eī‐leĭ‐sam‐shaan, yat‐poòn tuì‐chuê Ĭ‐paatₒ‐shaan; tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pūk‐yān Moh‐sai ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè, sin waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chuk‐fuk.
34 Ts‘ź‐haû, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tseung lūt‐faatₒ shóh‐yaŭ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ suen‐tūk, lûn chuk‐fuk, lûn chaù‐chòh, to tsun‐ts‘ūng lūt‐faatₒ‐shue faān‐shóh‐tsoì‐kè.
35 Faān Moh‐sai shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt ooî‐chùng kûng foŏ‐yān tsaí‐nuĭ, k‘āp k‘eī‐chung wŏng‐loī‐kè luĭ‐haakₒ, mŏ yat kuì m̄‐suen‐tūk à.
