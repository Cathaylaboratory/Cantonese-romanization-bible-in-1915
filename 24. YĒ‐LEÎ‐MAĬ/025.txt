1 TONG Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐sai‐à‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam taî‐sz̀ nīn, ts‘ź‐nīn haî Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ‐chi‐uēn‐nīn, yaû lûn Yaū‐t‘aaì chùng‐mān‐kè īn‐uĕ, kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ;
2 Tsik‐haî sin‐chi Yē‐leî‐maĭ shóh‐kóng‐kwòh Yaū‐t‘aaì chùng‐mān, uĕ‐k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng chùng‐kui‐mān‐kè, wâ,
3 Tsẑ‐ts‘ūng Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng À‐moōn‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐sai‐à taî‐shāp‐saam nīn, chì‐tò kam‐yāt, î‐shāp‐saam nīn kòm‐noî, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, ts‘āng kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, ngŏh yaû ch‘uēn‐kóng‐kwòh‐neĭ‐teî, hó‐tsó heí‐shan laī‐kóng; waī‐haî neĭ‐teî mŏ‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng.
4 Ī‐ch‘é Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ chùng‐pūk, tsik‐haî sin‐chi, tò‐neĭ‐teî‐ch‘uè, hó‐tsó heí‐shan, tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî; waī‐haî neĭ‐teî mŏ‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng, yaû mŏ‐chak‐ĭ‐laī‐t‘eng.
5 K‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ‐teî kokₒ‐yān iù leī‐hoi hāng‐okₒ‐kè lô faan‐ooī, yaû leī‐hoi neĭ shóh‐tsô‐kè okₒ‐sẑ, chuê‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsẑ‐sheûng‐koó‐chi‐shī, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn, ts‘z̀‐kwòh neī k‘āp neĭ līt‐tsó‐chi‐teî:
6 M̄‐hó kan‐ts‘ūng fûng‐sẑ pīt‐î‐kòh‐shān, waāk kìng‐paaì k‘uĭ, k‘aaĭ neĭ‐shaú shóh‐tsô‐chi‐māt, laī‐kik‐ngŏh‐faatₒ‐nô; neĭ mŏ‐kóm‐tsô, ngŏh m̄‐haâm‐hoî neĭ‐teî.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Neĭ‐teî sheûng‐ch‘é m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng ngŏh; tsaû k‘aaĭ neĭ‐shaú shóh‐tsô‐chi‐māt, kik‐ngŏh‐faatₒ‐nô, haâm‐hoî‐neĭ‐teî.
8 Shóh‐ĭ maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Yan neĭ mŏ‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng ngŏh‐kè īn‐uĕ.
9 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh tseung shaí‐yān huì‐ts‘uí‐hiu pak‐fong‐kè chùng‐ka, yaû shaí‐yān huì‐tò ngŏh pūk Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, tsaû taaì k‘uĭ‐teî laī‐kung‐kik ts‘ź‐teî, yaû kung‐kik ts‘ź‐teî‐kè kui‐mān, yaû kung‐kik‐saaì sz̀‐waī‐kè līt‐pong, ngŏh‐to tseung tsûn‐teî mīt‐tsuēt‐saaì k‘uĭ‐teî, lîng k‘uĭ tsô king‐k‘eī‐chi‐māt, faatₒ‐ch‘i‐sheng‐chi‐māt, tsô wĭng‐uĕn‐kè fong‐faì.
10 Fòng‐ch‘é ngŏh tseung tsoî k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè chí‐sik foon‐lōk kûng faaì‐oōt‐kè sheng, san‐lōng kûng san‐foŏ‐kè sheng, shēk‐môh‐kè sheng, k‘āp tang‐fóh‐kè kwong.
11 Ī‐ch‘é ts‘ź‐teî t‘ung‐to‐tsô‐fong‐faì‐kè, yaû tsô‐king‐k‘eī‐chi‐māt; ni‐ti līt‐pong to‐iù fūk‐sẑ Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng ts‘at‐shāp‐nīn kòm‐noî.
12 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Tseung pit‐haî‐kóm, kwòh‐hiu ts‘at‐shāp nīn kòh‐shī, ngŏh tsaû yan‐k‘uĭ‐tsuî‐hin yīng‐fāt Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng, k‘āp kokₒ‐kwokₒ kûng Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐chi‐teî, yaû lîng k‘uĭ wĭng‐uĕn fong‐faì.
13 Ngŏh tseung‐tsoî kòh‐kwokₒ shīng‐tsaû‐saaì ngŏh shóh‐ch‘ut‐kung‐kik‐k‘uĭ‐kè īn‐uĕ, tsik‐haì shóh‐sé‐lōk ts‘ź‐kuén‐shue‐kè, haî Yē‐leî‐maĭ heùng‐maân‐kwokₒ shóh‐kóng uê‐īn‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ.
14 Yan‐waî toh‐kwokₒ uĕ‐k‘āp taaî‐wōng, ooĭ‐lîng‐yān fūk‐ts‘ūng tsẑ‐keí: ngŏh pit‐chiù‐i k‘uĭ‐kè hāng‐waī, yaû chiù‐i k‘uĭ‐shaú‐shóh‐tsokₒ‐kè kung‐foo, pò‐yìng k‘uĭ‐teî.
15 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì yaû tuì ngŏh wâ; Neĭ iù‐heùng ngŏh‐shaú ts‘uí ni‐chekₒ făn‐hân‐kè pooi, yaû lìng ngŏh tá‐faatₒ‐neĭ‐huì‐tò‐kè chùng‐kwokₒ yám‐ts‘ź‐pooi.
16 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû iù‐yám, yaû chuèn‐loī chuèn‐huì, shīng‐waī tin‐kw‘ōng, haî yan ngŏh shaí‐yāp k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung to‐kìm‐kè uēn‐koò à.
17 Ue‐shî ngŏh heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaú ts‘uí‐kòh‐chekₒ‐pooi, yaû lîng k‘uĭ tá‐faatₒ ngŏh huì‐tò‐kè chùng‐kwokₒ yám‐hiu:
18 Tsik‐haî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì chùng‐yap, k‘āp k‘eī‐chung‐kè līt‐wōng kûng kwan‐cheúng, lîng k‘uĭ tsô‐fong‐faì tsô‐king‐k‘eī‐chi‐māt, tsô‐faatₒ‐ch‘i‐sheng kûng chaù‐chòh‐kè; hó‐ts‘z̆ kam‐yāt shīng‐tsaû‐kè yat‐yeûng;
19 Yaû Oi‐k‘āp wōng Faatₒ‐lŏ, k‘āp k‘uĭ chùng‐pūk, kûng k‘uĭ kwan‐cheúng, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ taaî‐chùng tsź‐mān;
20 Yaû tsaāp‐tsūk‐kè tsź‐mān, Oo‐sẑ‐teî‐kè chùng‐wōng, Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān teî‐kè chùng‐wōng, À‐pan, Kei‐lūn, Ka‐saatₒ, Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn, kûng À‐shāt‐tāt uē‐shîng‐kè,
21 Ĭ‐tung, Moh‐aapₒ, kûng À‐moōn‐kè tsź‐suen,
22 Yaû T‘ui‐lōh‐kè chùng‐wōng, k‘āp Sai‐tûn‐kè chùng‐wōng, uĕ‐k‘āp taaî‐hoí‐chi‐ngoî hoí‐tó‐kè līt‐wōng,
23 Taí‐taân, T‘aī‐mă, Pò‐sz, uĕ‐k‘āp tsín‐t‘aū‐kokₒ‐chi‐faatₒ‐kè,
24 À‐la‐paakₒ‐kè chùng‐wōng, uĕ‐k‘āp chuê‐kw‘òng‐yĕ tsaāp‐tsūk tsź‐mān‐kè chùng‐wōng,
25 Sam‐leĭ‐kè chùng‐wōng, kûng Ĭ‐laān‐kè chùng‐wōng, uĕ‐k‘āp Maĭ‐t‘aaì‐kè chùng‐wōng,
26 Pak‐fong‐kè chùng‐wōng, waāk‐uĕn waāk‐kân, peí‐ts‘ź t‘ūng‐maaī, uĕ‐k‘āp t‘in‐hâ‐maân‐kwokₒ; tsoî‐haû Shî‐shaatₒ wōng to‐iù yám lokₒ.
27 Ī‐ch‘é neĭ iù tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm wâ; Neĭ‐teî iù‐yám tò‐tsaú‐tsuì, yaû aú‐t‘ò, yaû k‘ing‐titₒ, yīk m̄‐tsoì‐faan‐heí‐laī, haî yan‐ngŏh‐shaí‐yāp neĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung to‐kìm‐kè uēn‐koò à.
28 Tseung pit‐haî‐kóm, t‘óng‐yeūk k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐háng haí neĭ‐shaú lóh‐pooi laī‐yám, neĭ tsaû iù‐tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Neĭ‐teî pit‐iù yám à.
29 Yan ngŏh kíng‐īn haí òn‐ngŏh‐mēng‐laī ch‘ing‐foo‐kè shēng‐ch‘uè, heí‐shaú hāng tsoi‐naân‐kè sẑ, neĭ‐teî t‘ung‐to‐m̄‐shaí shaû yīng‐fāt‐kè me? Neĭ‐teî k‘okₒ‐shāt m̄‐mĭn‐tak‐yīng‐fāt: yan ngŏh pit‐chiu‐to‐kìm kòng‐lōk‐ts‘uēn‐teî‐kè chùng‐mān, maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
30 Koò‐ts‘ź neĭ iù‐ch‘uēn‐saaì ts‘ź‐īn‐heùng k‘uĭ‐kóng‐uê‐īn‐kè, yaû‐tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsẑ‐sheûng ooĭ‐p‘aaū‐haau, yaû tsẑ‐shìng‐shóh faatₒ‐ch‘ut‐sheng‐yam; yaû heùng k‘uĭ yeūng‐laān kīk‐taaî‐sheng p‘aaū‐haau, k‘uĭ tseung‐faatₒ‐ch‘ut‐taaî‐sheng; hó‐ts‘z̆ ch‘aaí‐taāp p‘ō‐t‘aī‐tsź‐kè sheng, laī kung‐kik ts‘uēn‐teî‐kè chùng‐mān.
31 Tsẑ‐teî‐chi‐kīk yaŭ ch‘aaú‐naaû‐sheng; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kûng līt‐pong yaŭ yat‐kîn chaang‐lûn‐kè sẑ, k‘uĭ tseung‐kûng faān‐yaŭ‐huetₒ‐yūk‐kè chaang‐lûn; chì‐ue okₒ‐yān, tsaû k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ‐teî kaau‐kwòh to‐kìm, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
32 ¶ Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, T‘aí‐a, pit‐yaŭ tsoi‐wôh tsẑ‐yat‐kwokₒ faatₒ‐ch‘ut huì‐tò pīt‐kwokₒ, yīk‐yaŭ taaî‐pô‐fung tsẑ‐teî‐kīk ch‘ui‐heí‐laī.
33 Tong‐kòh‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, tsẑ‐ni‐pin‐teî‐kīk tò kòh‐pin‐teî‐kīk: to m̄‐tak‐yān waî‐k‘uĭ‐oi‐huk, yaû m̄‐peî‐tsuî‐maaī waāk tsòng‐maaī; tsaû shīng‐waī fàn‐t‘ó tsoî‐teî‐mîn.
34 Neĭ‐teî mūk‐yān à, iù taaî‐sheng‐haàm, yaû foo‐kiù; neĭ‐teî tsô‐yeūng‐kw‘ān‐kè t‘aū‐mūk à, iù‐tsoî‐fooi‐chung tsẑ‐luk: yan‐waî shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè yāt‐tsź haân‐k‘eī laī‐tò, ngŏh pit‐chaakₒ‐suì neĭ‐teî; neĭ‐teî titₒ‐waaî, hó‐ts‘z̆ hóh‐lōk‐kè heì‐mĭng kóm.
35 Ī‐ch‘é mūk‐yān mŏ‐lô hóh‐tsaú, yeūng‐kw‘ān‐kè t‘aū‐mūk pat‐nāng tóh‐peî.
36 Yaŭ mūk‐yān kiù‐foó‐kè sheng, yeūng‐kw‘ān‐kè t‘aū‐mūk yaû taaî‐sheng‐haàm: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fong‐faì k‘uĭ ts‘ó‐t‘īn à.
37 Ī‐ch‘é yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè līt‐nô, wōh‐p‘īng‐kè yeūng‐kw‘ān to‐pìn‐waī tsīk‐tsîng lokₒ.
38 K‘uĭ ĭ‐king leī‐hoi yán‐ts‘ōng‐chi‐shóh, hó‐ts‘z̆ sz‐tsź kóm: yan to‐kìm shi‐foó‐naân kòm‐kwaan‐haî, yan k‘uĭ līt‐nô‐kè uēn‐koò, kíng‐teî to‐shīng‐waī king‐hoī‐chi‐māt lokₒ.
