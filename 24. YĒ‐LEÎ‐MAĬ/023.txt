1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ wâ, Ts‘aān‐hoî fan‐saàn ngŏh‐ts‘ó‐t‘īn‐chi‐yeūng‐kè mūk‐yān shaû‐wôh lokₒ!
2 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, kung‐kik yeŭng‐ngŏh‐tsź‐mān‐kè mūk‐yān, kóm wâ; Neĭ ĭ‐king fan‐saàn ngŏh kw‘ān‐yeūng, yaû kón‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐huì, tsúng‐mŏ‐t‘aàm‐mông k‘uĭ‐teî: t‘aí‐a, ngŏh tseung neĭ tsokₒ‐waī‐kè okₒ‐ts‘īng, haí‐neĭ‐shan‐sheûng pò‐faan, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ,
3 Yaū ngŏh shóh‐kón‐chūk‐k‘uĭ‐huì‐kè kokₒ‐kwokₒ, to‐ooĭ tsuî‐maaī ngŏh kw‘ān‐yeūng shóh‐shîng‐kè, yaû taaì k‘uĭ‐teî faan‐yāp yeūng‐laān‐ch‘uè; k‘uĭ‐teî shî‐pit shang‐ch‘aán yaû ka‐toh.
4 Ngŏh tseung ch‘itₒ‐yeŭng k‘uĭ‐kè mūk‐yān sz‐leĭ k‘uĭ‐teî: k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐tsoì king‐fong, yaû m̄‐tsoì wân‐luên, yīk m̄‐tsoì shiú‐yat‐kòh, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
5 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, yāt‐tsź laī‐kân, ngŏh waî Taaî‐waî hing‐heí yat‐kòh kung‐î‐kè Miū‐Yuî, k‘uĭ pit‐cha‐k‘uēn tsô Wōng, yaû yûng chì‐waî hāng‐sẑ, yīk‐tsoî kíng‐noî shi‐shám‐p‘oòn k‘āp kung‐î.
6 Tong k‘uĭ‐kè yāt‐tsź, Yaū‐t‘aaì tsaû tak‐kaù, Ĭ‐shik‐līt yīk on‐īn kui‐chuê: shóh‐òn‐kòh‐mēng laī ch‘ing‐foo k‘uĭ‐kè, tsaû‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waī ngŏh‐teî‐kè kung‐î.
7 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, yāt‐tsź laī‐kân, yān m̄‐tsoì wâ, Chiù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsik‐haî taaì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp‐teî‐kè shang‐oōt;
8 Waī‐haî wâ, Chiù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsik‐haî yăn‐taaì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka haû‐yuî ch‘ut‐pak‐fong‐chi‐teî, yaû ts‘ūng ngŏh shóh‐kón‐chūk‐k‘uĭ‐huì kokₒ‐kwokₒ‐ch‘uè‐kè shang‐oōt; k‘uĭ‐teî tseung chuê‐tsoî tsẑ‐keí kíng‐noî.
9 ¶ Lûn‐k‘āp sin‐chi‐kè, ngŏh‐sam tsoî ngŏh ch‘ung‐noî suén‐sheung; ngŏh kwat‐t‘ung hó‐chàn‐tûng; ngŏh hó‐ts‘z̆ tsuì‐tsaú‐yān, yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ yān peî‐tsaú‐shìng‐hiu, haî yan Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yaû yan k‘uĭ shìng‐īn‐kè uēn‐koò.
10 Kíng‐noî k‘okₒ‐haî moŏn‐saaì hāng‐kaan‐yām‐kè yān; yan chaù‐chòh‐kè sẑ, kíng‐teî to oi‐huk; kw‘òng‐yĕ‐kè ts‘ó‐t‘īn kon‐foo‐hiu, yan k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè lô haî‐okₒ, k‘uĭ‐kè nāng‐līk waî pat‐chìng‐kè sẑ faatₒ‐ch‘ut.
11 Yan‐waî sin‐chi līn‐maaī tsaì‐sz, to‐haî sitₒ‐tūk‐kè; ī‐ch‘é tsoî ngŏh tîn‐uk‐chi‐chung, ngŏh kìn‐cheūk k‘uĭ‐kè okₒ‐ts‘ing, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
12 Koò‐ts‘ź k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè lô, pit‐shīng‐uē hak‐òm‐chi‐chung yaū‐waāt‐chi‐teî: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐peî‐chui‐kón, yaû titₒ‐lōk‐huì: yan ngŏh iù‐lîng tsoi‐wôh kòng‐lām k‘uĭ sheûng, tsik‐haî t‘aàm‐ch‘aatₒ yīng‐fāt‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐nīn, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ,
13 Tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à sin‐chi, to‐kìn‐kwòh uē‐ch‘ún‐kè sẑ; k‘uĭ‐teî í‐laaî Pa‐līk kóng uê‐īn, yaû lîng Ĭ‐shik‐līt ngŏh tsź‐mān hāng‐ts‘òh.
14 Tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng sin‐chi, to‐kìn‐kwòh yat‐kîn kīk‐hóh‐oò‐kè sẑ: k‘uĭ‐teî hāng‐kaan‐yām, kim kóng‐taaî‐wâ: yaû kin‐koò okₒ‐yān‐chi‐shaú, táng‐tò mŏ‐yān leī‐hoi okₒ‐sẑ faan‐laī: taaî‐chùng heùng‐ngŏh, hó‐ts‘z̆ shīng‐waī Shóh‐toh‐mā, k‘eī‐chung‐kè kui‐mān, hó‐ts‘z̆ Ngōh‐moh‐la kóm.
15 ¶ Shóh‐ĭ maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē Wōh‐Wā lûn‐k‘āp sin‐chi, kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh tseung peí neĭ shīk yan‐ch‘ān, yaû lìng k‘uĭ‐teî yám foó‐taám‐kè shuí: yan‐waî sitₒ‐tūk‐kè sẑ, yaū Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè sin‐chi‐ch‘uè faatₒ‐ch‘ut, hāng t‘ung‐ts‘uēn‐teî.
16 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, M̄‐hó t‘eng‐ts‘ūng kóh‐ti sin‐chi shóh‐kóng‐kwòh‐neĭ‐kè īn‐uĕ: k‘uĭ‐teì tseung hui‐ká kaaù‐neĭ: yaû i tsẑ‐keí sam‐ì, m̄‐haî yaū‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè haú‐kóng ì‐tseûng.
17 K‘uĭ‐teî pat‐hitₒ tuì kòh‐ti ìm‐heì‐ngŏh‐kè yān wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng wâ, Neĭ‐teî pit‐tak‐p‘īng‐on; yaû tuì taân‐faān i tsẑ‐keí waān‐káng laī‐hāng‐kè wâ, Pit‐mŏ‐tsoi‐wôh lām‐tò neĭ shan‐sheûng à.
18 Mat‐shuī k‘eĭ‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ĭ‐sẑ‐ooî‐chung, tsaû hóh‐ĭ kokₒ‐tak chan‐shāt, yaû t‘eng‐cheūk k‘uĭ‐kè īn‐uĕ ni? mat‐shuī ts‘āng‐kokₒ‐tak chan‐shāt, yaû t‘eng‐mān ts‘ź‐īn ni?
19 T‘aí‐a, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè pô‐fung, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐kè făn‐hân, ĭ‐king faatₒ‐ch‘ut, shāt‐haî suên‐chuèn ch‘ui‐kè pô‐fung: pit‐faatₒ‐ch‘ut kòng‐lōk okₒ‐yān t‘aū‐sheûng.
20 Meì‐ts‘āng hāng‐tsûn, yaû meì‐ts‘āng shīng‐tsaû k‘uĭ‐sam‐kè ì‐chí: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè nau‐nô, pit‐m̄‐faan‐chuèn, tò moōt‐yāt‐chi‐shī, neĭ‐teî pit‐tsûn‐teî mīng‐paāk ts‘ź‐sẑ lokₒ.
21 Ngŏh mŏ‐tá‐faatₒ ni‐ti‐sin‐chi, k‘uĭ‐teî sheûng‐ch‘é pan‐tsaú: ngŏh m̄‐ts‘āng tuì k‘uĭ‐teî kóng, k‘uĭ‐teî sheûng‐ch‘é ch‘uēn‐uê‐īn.
22 T‘óng‐yeūk k‘eĭ‐tsoî ngŏh ĭ‐sẑ‐ooî‐chung, k‘uĭ tsaû pit‐lîng tsź‐mān t‘eng ngŏh īn‐uĕ, yaû lîng k‘uĭ chuèn‐heùng leī‐hoi k‘uĭ shóh‐tsokₒ‐kè okₒ‐hâng.
23 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh heí‐haî kân‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, m̄‐haî kaakₒ‐uĕn‐kè Sheûng‐Taì me?
24 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Yān heí‐yaŭ nāng‐tsoî yán‐māt‐ch‘uè ni‐maaī, táng ngŏh m̄‐t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ me? Ngŏh heí‐m̄‐haî ch‘ung‐moŏn t‘in‐teî me? Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ,
25 Ngŏh ĭ‐king t‘eng‐mān sin‐chi shóh‐kóng‐kè, tsik‐haî t‘okₒ‐ngŏh‐mēng kóng‐taaî‐wâ tsokₒ‐uê‐īn, wâ, Ngŏh faatₒ‐kwòh‐mûng à, ngŏh faatₒ‐kwòh‐mûng à.
26 Ch‘uēn‐kóng taaî‐wâ tòng‐tsokₒ‐uê‐īn‐chi‐sin‐chi, tsik‐haî kóng tsẑ‐keí‐sam‐chung kwaí‐chà‐kè sin‐chi, ts‘ōng ts‘ź‐ì tsoî k‘uĭ‐sam iù‐keí‐noî ni?
27 Yaû ĭ‐waī yaŭ‐nāng tseung tsẑ‐keí shóh‐faatₒ‐kè mùng, tsik‐haî kokₒ‐tuì lūn‐shè shóh‐kóng‐kè mûng, lîng‐ngŏh tsź‐mān mōng‐keì ngŏh‐mēng, hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘uĭ līt‐tsó waî Pa‐līk mōng‐keì ngŏh‐mēng yat‐yeûng.
28 Taàn‐faān sin‐chi faatₒ‐kwòh‐mûng‐kè, iù‐k‘uĭ kóng‐ch‘ut shóh‐faatₒ‐kwòh‐kè mûng; yaû taân‐faān tak‐tó ngŏh‐kè īn‐uĕ, iù‐k‘uĭ chiù‐shāt kóng‐ch‘ut ngŏh‐kè īn‐uĕ. Kon‐ts‘ó peí‐kaaù‐māk, suèn‐haì‐tím‐yeûng ni? Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
29 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ; Ngŏh‐kè īn‐uĕ, heí‐m̄‐haî hó‐ts‘z̆‐fóh me? Yaû hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘itₒ‐ch‘uī tá‐suì p‘oōn‐shēk me?
30 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh yan ts‘ź kung‐kik kòh‐ti sin‐chi, tsik‐haî kokₒ‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ lūn‐shè t‘au‐hiu‐ngŏh‐īn‐uĕ‐kè.
31 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik kòh‐ti‐sin‐chi, tsik‐haî shaí‐leì‐laī‐wâ, Chué‐wâ‐kè.
32 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik kòh‐ti‐kóng‐hui‐faū‐kè mûng, tòng‐tsokₒ uê‐īn, yaû kóng k‘uĭ mûng‐seúng, lìng‐ngŏh tsź‐mān yan k‘uĭ taaî‐wâ, yaû yan k‘uĭ hui‐faū‐kè king‐kw‘a, tsaû hāng‐ts‘òh‐lô; ngŏh k‘okₒ‐haî mŏ‐tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ, yaû mŏ‐fan‐foò k‘uĭ: tsúng‐pat‐nāng‐yaŭ yik‐ue tsź‐mān, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
33 Waāk ni‐ti‐tsź‐mān, waāk sin‐chi, waāk tsaì‐sz, ooĭ‐mân neĭ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè īn‐uĕ haî mat‐yĕ? neĭ iù tuì‐k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Chan‐yaŭ ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè īn‐uĕ, ngŏh pit‐tiu‐heì neĭ‐teî à, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ,
34 Chì‐ue sin‐chi, k‘āp tsaì‐sz, kûng tsź‐mān, tsik‐haî kóm wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè īn‐uĕ, ngŏh pit‐yīng‐fāt kóh‐kòh‐yān, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ ts‘uēn‐ka.
35 Neĭ‐teî kokₒ‐yān iù‐tuì lūn‐shè, yaû kokₒ‐yān iù‐tuì hing‐taî kóm wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā taapₒ‐yìng mat‐yĕ ni? Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóng mat‐yĕ ni?
36 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè īn‐uĕ, m̄‐shaí tsoì‐kóng: yan‐waî kokₒ‐yān tsẑ‐keí‐kè īn‐uĕ, pit‐shīng‐waī k‘uĭ‐kè ch‘ŭng‐yâm; yan neĭ‐teî ĭ‐king lûng‐waaî shang‐oōt‐Sheûng‐Taì‐kè īn‐uĕ, maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ngŏh‐teî‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì‐kè īn‐uĕ à.
37 Neĭ iù‐tuì kòh‐sin‐chi kóm wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā taapₒ‐yìng‐neĭ mat‐yĕ ni? Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóng‐kwòh‐mat‐yĕ ni?
38 T‘óng‐yeūk neĭ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè īn‐uĕ; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû yan‐ts‘ź kóm wâ; Haî‐yan neĭ kóng‐ts‘ź‐īn, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè īn‐uĕ, ngŏh kíng‐īn tá‐faatₒ t‘ung‐chi‐neĭ, pat‐hóh wâ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘ŭng‐foó‐kè īn‐uĕ;
39 Shóh‐ĭ t‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐tsûn‐teî mōng‐keì, yaû tiu‐heì‐neĭ, k‘āp ngŏh shóh‐ts‘z̀‐neĭ kûng neĭ‐līt‐tsó‐kè shēng, lîng k‘uĭ leī‐hoi ngŏh‐mîn‐ts‘īn:
40 Yaû tseung lîng wĭng‐uĕn‐kè līng‐yūk, kòng‐lām neĭ sheûng‐t‘aū, uĕ‐k‘āp pat‐hitₒ‐kè sau‐kw‘aì, tsúng‐pat‐nāng mōng‐keì lokₒ.
