1 Ĭ‐FAT sź‐hiu chi‐haû, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān yaû haí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ.
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung k‘uĭ‐teî kaau‐kwòh Ka‐naām wōng Yē‐pan‐kè shaú, tsik‐haî tsoî Hâ‐shokₒ tsô‐wōng‐kè; k‘uĭ‐kè tseung‐kwan mēng kiù‐tsô Sai‐sai‐la, tsik‐haî chuê‐tsoî Î‐pong‐yān‐kè Hâ‐lōh‐ch‘itₒ.
3 Yē‐pan wōng yaŭ t‘itₒ‐ch‘e kaú‐paakₒ leŭng, foó‐ch‘óh haak‐pōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān î‐shāp nīn kòm‐noî: Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû foo‐yeūk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
4 ¶ Kóh‐shī yaŭ yat‐waî nuĭ‐sin‐chi, kiù‐tsô Taí‐p‘òh‐la, haî La‐peí‐t‘uetₒ‐kè ts‘ai, tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè sẑ‐sz.
5 K‘uĭ haí Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn shaan‐teî, chuê‐tsoî La‐mă kûng Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî chung‐kaan‐kè, Taí‐p‘òh‐la tsó‐shuê‐hâ: Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān to sheŭng k‘uĭ kóh‐ch‘uè shaû‐shám‐p‘oòn.
6 Taí‐p‘òh‐la tá‐faatₒ yān huì Kei‐tāk Naāp‐taaî‐leî, kiù À‐peì‐nōh‐òm‐kè tsaí Pa‐lāk laī, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, m̄‐haî yaŭ fan‐foò wâ, Neĭ‐teî haí Taaî‐pōk‐shaan chiu‐tsaāp, tuk‐sut Naāp‐taaî‐leî‐yān, Sai‐pò‐lūn‐yān, kûng yat‐maân t‘ūng neĭ huì me?
7 Ngŏh pit yăn Yē‐pan‐kè tseung‐kwan Sai‐sai‐la, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè ch‘e, k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐yān, huì‐tò Kei‐shûn‐hōh kìn neĭ; tsaû tseung k‘uĭ kaau‐kwòh neĭ shaú.
8 Pa‐lāk taapₒ wâ, Neĭ t‘ūng ngŏh huì, ngŏh tsaû huì: neĭ m̄‐t‘ūng ngŏh huì, ngŏh tsaû m̄‐huì.
9 Taí‐p‘òh‐la wâ, Ngŏh pit‐tîng t‘ūng neĭ huì: taân ni‐chân‐shī ch‘ut moōn pit‐m̄‐tak wīng‐kwong; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tseung‐kân maaî Sai‐sai‐la kwòh‐foŏ‐yān‐kè shaú là. Kóm‐tsaû Taí‐p‘òh‐la heí‐shan, t‘ūng‐maaī Pa‐lāk huì‐tò Kei‐tāk.
10 Pa‐lāk chiu‐maaī Sai‐pò‐lūn, Naāp‐taaî‐leî, huì‐tò Kei‐tāk; yaŭ yat‐maân yān kan k‘uĭ sheŭng‐huì: Taí‐p‘òh‐la to t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ sheŭng‐huì.
11 Kei‐neī‐kè‐yān Hei‐paakₒ, yă‐ts‘āng leī‐hoi Kei‐neī‐kè yān, tsik‐haî Moh‐sai‐kè ngoî‐foô, Hōh‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen, laī laāp cheùng‐mōk haí Kei‐tāk, kân‐chuê Saatₒ‐nā‐yam‐kè tseûng‐shuê p‘ōng‐pin.
12 Yaŭ yān kóng‐kwòh Sai‐sai‐la chi, À‐peì‐nōh‐òm‐kè tsaí Pa‐lāk, ĭ‐king sheŭng‐tò Taaî‐pōk‐shaan.
13 Sai‐sai‐la tsaû tsuî‐tsaāp shóh‐yaŭ‐kè ch‘e, tsik‐haî kaú‐paakₒ‐leŭng t‘itₒ‐ch‘e, uĕ‐k‘āp ts‘ūng Î‐pong‐kè Hâ‐lōh‐ch‘itₒ, chì‐tò Kei‐shûn‐hōh, shóh‐yaŭ kan‐ts‘uī‐k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐yān.
14 Taí‐p‘òh‐la tuì Pa‐lāk wâ, Heí‐shan; uē‐kam haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kaau Sai‐sai‐la kwòh‐neĭ‐shaú‐kè shī‐haû: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heí‐m̄‐haî tsoî neĭ ts‘īn‐t‘aū hāng me? Pa‐lāk tsaû haí Taaî‐pōk‐shaan lōk‐laī, kan‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐kè yaŭ yat‐maân‐yān.
15 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yûng to laī‐shaí Sai‐sai‐la k‘āp k‘uĭ shóh‐yaŭ‐leŭng‐ch‘e, ts‘uēn‐kwan tsoî Pa‐lāk mîn‐ts‘īn king‐luên; Sai‐sai‐la hă‐ch‘e pô‐hāng t‘ō‐tsaú.
16 Pa‐lāk chui‐kón k‘uĭ‐kè ch‘e kûng ping, chīk‐tò Î‐pong‐yān‐kè Hâ‐lōh‐ch‘itₒ: Sai‐sai‐la‐kè ts‘uēn‐kwan, to peî to shaatₒ‐saaì; mŏ‐yat‐kòh laū‐ts‘uēn.
17 ¶ Taân Sai‐sai‐la pô‐hāng t‘ō‐tsaú huì‐tò Kei‐neī‐yān Hei‐paakₒ‐chi‐ts‘ai Ngă‐yik‐kè cheùng‐mōk: yan‐waî Hâ‐shokₒ wōng Yē‐pan, kûng Kei‐neī‐yān Hei‐paakₒ leŭng‐ka wōh‐hó à.
18 Ngă‐yik ch‘ut‐huì yīng‐tsipₒ Sai‐sai‐la, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ngŏh chué à, yāp huì le, ts‘éng neĭ yāp ngŏh‐ch‘uè; m̄‐shaí‐fong. Sai‐sai‐la tsaû yāp‐huì k‘uĭ cheùng‐mōk‐luĭ. Ngă‐yik tsaû ning cheung p‘eĭ k‘ám‐chuê k‘uĭ.
19 Sai‐sai‐la tuì Ngă‐yik wâ, Ngŏh kéng‐hotₒ à; k‘aū neĭ peí‐ti‐shuí kwòh‐ngŏh‐yám. Ngă‐yik tsaû tá‐hoi p‘eī‐toî, peí naaĭ k‘uĭ yám, ning cheung‐p‘eĭ k‘ám‐chuê k‘uĭ.
20 Sai‐sai‐la tuì Ngă‐yik wâ, Neĭ k‘eĭ‐tsoî cheùng‐mōk‐moōn‐haú, t‘óng‐yeūk yaŭ yān laī mân, wâ, Ni‐ch‘uè yaŭ yān mŏ? neĭ tsaû wâ, Mŏ‐yān à.
21 Sai‐sai‐la kaù‐kooî fàn‐nâm: Hei‐paakₒ‐kè ts‘ai Ngă‐yik, ning cheùng‐mōk‐kè teng, shaú cha‐chuê ch‘uī, tsîng‐tsĭng hāng‐maaī‐huì, k‘aaĭ teng tá‐yāp ngaāk‐kokₒ, tá‐ch‘uen‐yāp teî‐ch‘uè. Sai‐sai‐la tsaû sź‐hiu.
22 Kóh‐chân Pa‐lāk chui‐kón Sai‐sai‐la, Ngă‐yik ch‘ut‐huì yīng‐tsipₒ k‘uĭ, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Laī la, ngŏh ooĭ peí neĭ t‘aí neĭ‐shóh‐wán‐kè yān. Pa‐lāk yāp‐hiu cheùng‐mōk, t‘aí‐kìn Sai‐sai‐la sź‐hiu fàn‐ch‘uè, cheùng‐mōk teng yīng‐tsoî k‘uĭ ngaāk‐kokₒ‐chung.
23 Kóm‐tsaû Sheûng‐Taì haí kóh‐yāt tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt mîn‐ts‘īn tá‐paaî Ka‐naām wōng Yē‐pan.
24 Ts‘ź‐haû Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè shaú, uēt‐faatₒ yaŭ līk yēng‐hiu Ka‐naām wōng Yē‐pan, táng‐tò mīt‐tsuēt k‘uĭ à.
