1 NGŎH sui ooĭ kóng pīt‐yān‐kè t‘ó‐t‘aām, uĕ‐k‘āp t‘in‐sz̀‐kè wâ, yeūk mŏ yān‐oì, tsaû haî hó‐ts‘z̆ mīng‐lōh heúng‐poōt yat‐yeûng.
2 Ngŏh sui yaŭ sin‐chi ch‘uēn‐tô‐kè nāng‐kòn, yaû mīng‐paāk kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè ò‐miû, k‘āp kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè chì‐shik; ī‐ch‘é yaŭ taaî‐sùn‐tak, chì‐tò nāng ī‐hoi shaan‐kong, yeūk mŏ yān‐oì, ngŏh tsaû m̄‐haî mat‐yĕ.
3 Ngŏh sui k‘aaĭ faān shóh‐yaŭ‐kè laī chau‐tsaì p‘ān‐k‘ūng, ī‐ch‘é shé‐shan shaû shiu, yeūk mŏ yān‐oì, ngŏh to mŏ yik.
4 Yān‐oì haî foon‐yūng, ts‘z̄‐pei; yān‐oì haî m̄‐tò‐keî; m̄‐king‐kw‘a, m̄‐kiu‐ngô,
5 M̄‐tsô fei‐laĭ‐kè sẑ, m̄‐k‘aū leî‐keí, m̄‐hing‐î faatₒ‐nô, m̄‐ch‘uén‐tōk yān okₒ;
6 M̄‐foon‐heí pat‐î, waī foon‐heí chan‐leĭ;
7 Faān‐sẑ foon‐yūng, faān‐sẑ sùn‐shāt, faān‐sẑ yeŭng‐mông, faān‐sẑ yán‐noî.
8 Yān‐oì haî wĭng‐uĕn m̄‐shat‐chuî kè: taân sin‐chi ch‘uēn‐tô‐kè nāng‐kòn, pit‐faì‐waaî; kóng pīt‐ch‘uè t‘ó‐t‘aām‐kè nāng‐kòn, pit‐t‘īng‐sik; chì‐shik yīk pit‐faì‐waaî.
9 Yan‐waî ngŏh‐teî‐kè chì‐shik, meî‐tak ts‘aī‐ts‘uēn, tsô sin‐chi ch‘uēn‐tô‐kè, yīk meî‐tak ts‘aī‐ts‘uēn.
10 Taân ts‘aī‐ts‘uēn‐kè laī‐tò, meî‐ts‘aī‐ts‘uēn‐kè, tsaû pit faì‐waaî.
11 Ngŏh tsô t‘ūng‐tsź‐chi‐shī, shuetₒ‐wâ hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘ūng‐tsź, kìn‐shik hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘ūng‐tsź, sam‐sz yīk hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘ūng‐tsź: taân ngŏh keì shīng‐hiu yān, tsaû tiu‐heì t‘ūng‐tsź‐kè‐sẑ lokₒ.
12 Ngŏh‐teî uē‐kam tuì‐chuê kèng laī‐t‘aí, m̄‐toh mīng‐paāk; tò kòh‐chân‐shī, tsaû peí‐ts‘ź tīk‐mîn lokₒ: uē‐kam ngŏh‐kè chì‐shik, meî tak ts‘aī‐ts‘uēn; tò kòh‐chân‐shī, ngŏh tsaû sham shik, hó‐ts‘z̆ Chué shik ngŏh yat‐yeûng.
13 Uē‐kam shóh‐ts‘uēn‐kè, yaŭ ts‘ź‐saam‐yeûng, tsik‐haî sùn‐tak, yeŭng‐mông, yān‐oì; k‘eī‐chung chì‐taaî‐kè, haî yān‐oì à.
