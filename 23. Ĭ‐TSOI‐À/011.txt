1 Ī‐CH‘É yaū Yē‐sai‐kè shuê‐t‘aū ooĭ shang‐ch‘ut yat‐chi, yaû k‘uĭ kan‐k‘eŭng shang‐ch‘ut‐kè miū‐yuî ooĭ kitₒ‐shāt:
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shān chí‐tsoî k‘uĭ sheûng‐t‘aū, tsik‐haî chì‐waî k‘āp ts‘ung‐mīng‐kè shān, maū‐tōk kûng ts‘oī‐nāng‐kè shān, kìn‐shik uĕ kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shān;
3 K‘uĭ shóh‐heí‐lōk‐kè, haî shūk kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sẑ: k‘uĭ m̄‐haî chiù‐ngaăn‐shóh‐kìn laī‐shám‐p‘oòn, m̄‐haî chiù‐ĭ‐shóh‐mān laī‐tuk‐chaakₒ:
4 Waī‐haî k‘aaĭ kung‐î laī‐shám‐p‘oòn p‘ān‐k‘ūng, k‘aaĭ chìng‐leĭ laī‐tuk‐chaakₒ teî‐sheûng wan‐yaū‐kè: k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ‐haú‐kè kwàn laī‐kung‐tá teî‐sheûng, k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ haú‐shóh‐ch‘ut‐kè heì‐laī shaatₒ okₒ‐yān.
5 Kung‐î ooĭ tsô k‘uĭ iu‐taaì, chung‐sùn ooĭ tsô k‘uĭ shan‐sheûng‐kè ch‘uk‐shīng.
6 Ch‘aaī‐lōng kûng yeūng‐tsaí ooĭ t‘ūng‐chuê, paaù‐tsź kûng shaan‐yeūng‐tsaí fàn‐tó yat‐ch‘uè: ngaū‐tsaí kûng sz‐tsź, t‘ūng‐maaī yeŭng‐feī‐kè ch‘uk‐shang waī‐kw‘ān; yat‐kòh t‘ūng‐tsź ooĭ t‘oh‐ts‘aī k‘uĭ.
7 Ngaū kûng hūng‐yān t‘ūng‐maaī shīk; k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsaí, t‘ūng‐maaī yat‐ts‘aī fàn‐ch‘uè: sz ooĭ‐shīk ts‘ó, hó‐ts‘z̆ ngaū yat‐yeûng.
8 Waì‐naaĭ‐kè tsaí, haí tūk‐shē‐kè lung oôn‐shá, t‘uĕn‐naaĭ‐kè tsaí, chai‐shaú lōk fuk kè taû.
9 Tsoî ngŏh shìng‐shaan tsúng mŏ‐suén‐sheung, mŏ‐ts‘aān‐hoî kè: yan‐waî teî‐sheûng ch‘ung‐moŏn shik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè chì‐shik, hó‐ts‘z̆ shuí che‐k‘oì taaî‐hoí yat‐yeûng.
10 ¶ Tong kòh‐yāt pit haî kóm, Yē‐sai‐kè kan shīng‐laāp, tsô līt‐kwokₒ‐kè hô‐k‘eī; Līt‐kwokₒ‐pit‐ooĭ‐ts‘ām‐k‘aū k‘uĭ: k‘uĭ on‐sik‐chi‐shóh, pit shīng‐waī wīng‐kwong‐kè.
11 ¶ Tong kòh‐yāt pit haî kóm, Sheûng‐Chué tsaû‐ooĭ taî‐î‐ts‘z̀ maaī‐shaú kaù‐shūk k‘uĭ tsź‐mān shóh‐uē‐shîng‐kè yān, tsik‐haî yaū À‐shūt, yaū Oi‐k‘āp, yaū Paatₒ‐lōh, yaū Koó‐shāt, yaū Ĭ‐laān, yaū Shî‐nā, yaū Hap‐māt, yaū taaî‐hoí‐kè chau‐tó shóh‐laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè.
12 K‘uĭ pit waî līt‐kwokₒ ch‘itₒ‐laāp hô‐k‘eī, laī tsuî‐maaī Ĭ‐shik‐līt tiu‐heì‐kè mān, yaû tsẑ‐teî‐chi‐sz̀ kokₒ, tsuî‐maaī Yaū‐t‘aaì saàn‐hoi‐kè mān.
13 Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐kè tò‐keî, to ooĭ leī‐hoi, kaaú‐iŭ Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè, pit‐peî‐mīt‐tsuēt: Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn m̄‐ooĭ tò‐keî Yaū‐t‘aaì, Yaū‐t‘aaì m̄‐ooĭ kaaú‐iŭ Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn.
14 K‘uĭ‐teî heùng sai‐pîn fei‐lōk Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè pokₒ‐t‘aū‐sheûng; t‘ūng‐maaī yat‐ts‘aī lŏ‐leūk tung‐fong‐chi‐tsź: k‘uĭ‐teî shan‐shaú ts‘uí Ĭ‐tung kûng Moh‐aapₒ, À‐moōn‐kè tsź‐suen, yīk shûn‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî.
15 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ooĭ tsûn‐teî mīt‐tsuēt Oi‐k‘āp hoí‐leî; yaû ooĭ yûng kīk‐īt‐kè fung, yuk‐shaú tsoî‐ue taaî‐hōh‐chi‐sheûng, p‘aakₒ k‘uĭ fan‐hoi ts‘at‐t‘iū shuí, lîng yān m̄‐shaí shap‐keukₒ hāng‐kwòh.
16 Waî k‘uĭ‐kè tsź‐mān shóh‐uē‐shîng, tsik‐haî yaū À‐shūt laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè, pit yaŭ t‘iū taaî‐lô; hó‐ts‘z̆ Ĭ‐shik‐līt ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp sheŭng‐laï shóh‐yaŭ yat‐yeûng.
