1 TONG Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Oo‐sai‐à, Yeukₒ‐taam, À‐hap‐sz̀, Hei‐sai‐ka nīn‐kaan, uĕ‐k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm nīn‐kaan, kòh‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, kòng‐lām‐ue Pīt‐leĭ‐kè tsaí Hōh‐sai.
2 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tong‐ch‘oh‐chi‐shī, t‘okₒ Hōh‐sai kóng. Tsaû tuì Hōh‐sai wâ, Iù huì‐ts‘uì keî‐foŏ tsô‐ts‘ai, ī‐ch‘é tak‐yām‐shang‐kè tsaí: yan‐waî ts‘ź‐teî leī‐hoi Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, hāng‐taaî‐kaan‐yām‐kè sẑ à.
3 Ue‐shî k‘uĭ‐huì‐ts‘uì Tik‐la‐yān‐kè nuĭ Hóh‐mīt; k‘uĭ tsaû waaī‐t‘oi‐shang‐tsaí kwòh‐k‘uĭ lokₒ.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû‐tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Iù on‐k‘uĭ‐mēng‐kiù‐tsô Yē‐sz‐līt; yan‐waî hitₒ‐mŏ‐noî ngŏh pit‐haí Yē‐oô‐ka pò‐ch‘aū Yē‐sz‐līt‐kè huetₒ, yaû‐lîng shūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka‐kè kwokₒ sik‐mīt‐lokₒ.
5 Tong‐kòh‐yāt ngŏh pit‐haí Yē‐sz‐līt‐kè kuk, ch‘aakₒ‐t‘uĕn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kòh‐pá‐kung.
6 Hóh‐mīt yaû‐waaī‐t‘oi shang‐yat‐kòh‐nuĭ. Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yaû‐tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Iù‐on‐k‘uĭ‐mēng‐kiù‐tsô Lōh‐lô‐hap‐mă: yan‐waî ngŏh m̄‐tsoì shi‐king‐sut‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè ka; tuèn‐pat‐hóh shè‐mĭn k‘uĭ‐teî lokₒ.
7 Waī‐haî, iù‐shi‐king‐sut kwòh Yaū‐t‘aaì‐ka, yaû tsīk‐laaî k‘uĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ‐teî, m̄‐haî k‘aaĭ‐kung, waāk kìm, waāk kaau‐chìn‐kè sẑ, waāk mă‐ping laī‐ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ‐teî à.
8 Hóh‐mīt kûng‐k‘uĭ‐nuĭ Lōh‐lô‐hap‐mă t‘uĕn‐naaĭ‐kòh‐shī, waaī‐t‘oi yaû‐shang‐kòh‐tsaí.
9 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû wâ, Iù‐on‐k‘uĭ‐mēng‐kiù‐tsô Lōh‐à‐maĭ: yan‐waî neĭ‐teî‐m̄‐tsô ngŏh tsź‐mān, ngŏh yīk‐m̄‐háng‐tsô‐neĭ Sheûng‐Taì à.
10 ¶ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè shò, sheûng‐ch‘é ooĭ‐shīng‐waī hoí‐pin‐kè sha, pat‐nāng leūng‐kwòh, waāk shó‐kwòh à: tseung‐pit‐haî‐kóm, yă‐ts‘āng wâ Neĭ‐teî‐m̄‐haî ngŏh‐tsź‐mān, haí kòh‐ch‘uè tsaû‐ooĭ‐tuì‐k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ‐teî haî Oōt‐Sheûng‐Taì kè tsaí‐là.
11 Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, iù‐tsuî‐maaī leŭng‐ka, hōp‐ì ch‘itₒ‐laāp yat‐kòh‐tsô‐t‘aū‐mūk‐kè, yat‐ts‘aī yaū‐kíng‐teî sheŭng‐huì: yan‐waî Yē‐sz‐līt‐chi‐yāt, pit‐tîng haî taaî‐yāt‐tsź à.
