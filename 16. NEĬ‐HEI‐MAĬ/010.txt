1 YÀN‐MĒNG‐KÈ, haî Hap‐ka‐leî‐kè tsaí, fong‐paakₒ Neī‐hei‐maĭ, kûng Sai‐taí‐ka,
2 Sai‐loî‐à, À‐saatₒ‐leî‐à, Yē‐leî‐maĭ,
3 Pa‐shūt, À‐mă‐leĭ, Mă‐k‘eī‐à,
4 Hap‐tāt, Shî‐pa‐neī, Mă‐lūk,
5 Hap‐lām, Maĭ‐leĭ‐moōt, Oh‐pa‐taí,
6 Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ, K‘eī‐neī‐tûn, Pa‐lūk,
7 Maĭ‐shue‐laān, À‐peì‐ngă, Maĭ‐à‐mān,
8 Mă‐ts‘oì‐à, Pit‐koi, Shî‐mă‐ngă; ts‘ź kaai‐haî tsaì‐sz.
9 Yaû‐yaŭ Leî‐meî‐yān; tsik‐haî À‐saàn‐neī‐kè tsaí Yē‐shue‐à, Hei‐nā‐taāt‐kè tsaí Pán‐noî, Kaapₒ‐mīt;
10 Kûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè hing‐taî Shî‐pa‐neī, Hōh‐teî‐ngă, K‘eī‐leî‐taaî, Peí‐loī‐à, Hap‐naān,
11 Maĭ‐ka, Leĭ‐hōp, Hap‐sha‐peí,
12 Saatₒ‐haak, Shî‐leĭ‐peí, Shî‐pa‐neī,
13 Hōh‐teî‐ngă, Pa‐neī, Peí‐neī‐nō.
14 Yaû‐yaŭ tsź‐mān‐kè cheúng‐ché; tsik‐haî Pa‐lūk, Pa‐hap‐moh‐aapₒ, Ĭ‐laān, Saatₒ‐t‘ó, Pa‐neī,
15 Pò‐neī, Aapₒ‐kaapₒ, Peí‐paaì,
16 À‐toh‐neī‐ngă, Pit‐waai, À‐ting,
17 À‐tāk, Hei‐sai‐ka, Aapₒ‐shuetₒ,
18 Hōh‐teî‐ngă, Hap‐shûn, Peí‐ts‘oì,
19 Hap‐la, À‐nā‐tāt, Neī‐paaì,
20 Moōt‐peí‐aapₒ, Maĭ‐shue‐laān, Hei‐sik,
21 Maĭ‐shî‐saatₒ‐pīt, Saatₒ‐tuk, Aapₒ‐t‘ó‐à,
22 Peí‐la‐taí, Hap‐naān, À‐noî‐ngă,
23 Hōh‐sai, Hap‐nā‐neī‐à, Hap‐shūt,
24 Hap‐lōh‐haak, Peí‐leî‐hap, Shuetₒ‐paakₒ,
25 Leĭ‐wāng, Hap‐sha‐nā, Mă‐sai‐ngă,
26 À‐hei‐ngă, Hap‐naān, À‐naān,
27 Mă‐lūk, Hap‐lām, Pa‐nā.
28 ¶ K‘eī‐uē‐kè tsź‐mān, tsaì‐sz Leî‐meî‐yān, hon‐shaú‐moōn‐kè, au‐koh‐kè, tîn‐uk fūk‐yīk‐kè, kûng faān tsẑ‐keí pīt‐hoi ts‘ź‐teî‐kè tsź‐mān, kwai‐fūk Sheûng‐Taì lūt‐faatₒ‐kè, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè ts‘ai, kûng tsaí‐nuĭ, faān yaŭ chi‐shik, nāng‐mīng‐paāk‐kè;
29 K‘uĭ‐teî kaau‐ts‘at k‘uĭ‐kè hing‐taî, tsik‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî tseukₒ‐waî‐kè, chaù‐chòh faatₒ‐shaî, pit tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè lūt‐faatₒ, tsik‐haî Sheûng‐Taì‐kè pūk Moh‐sai shóh‐ch‘uēn‐shaû‐kè, ī‐ch‘é pit kán‐shaú tsun‐hāng ngŏh‐teî‐kè Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yat‐ts‘aì kaaì‐mîng, faatₒ‐tô, kw‘ai‐t‘iū;
30 Yaû pit m̄‐tseung ngŏh‐teî‐kè nuĭ, kà‐kwòh ts‘ź‐teî‐kè mān, yīk m̄‐waî ngŏh‐teî‐kè tsaí laī ts‘uì k‘uĭ teî‐kè nuĭ:
31 Waāk ts‘ź‐teî‐kè mān tsoî on‐sik‐yāt, taaì heì‐kuî kûng shīk‐māt laī‐maaî‐kwòh ngŏh‐teî, tsoî on‐sik‐yāt, waāk shìng‐yāt, tsaû ngŏh‐teî pit‐m̄‐yaū k‘uĭ maaĭ: mooĭ‐fūng taî‐ts‘at nīn, pit m̄‐kaang‐chùng, faān hìm‐foô ngŏh‐teî‐kè, m̄‐tsoì t‘ó‐ts‘uí.
32 Yaû ngŏh‐teî waî tsẑ‐keí tîng laî, mooĭ‐nīn kokₒ‐yān kuen‐ngān saam‐fân‐yat shī‐haakₒ, waî ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐chi‐yûng;
33 Tsik‐haî paaí‐ch‘itₒ‐kè péng, tsô sheūng‐hìn‐kè sò‐tsaì, faān‐tsaì, on‐sik‐yāt k‘āp uēt‐shokₒ, shóh‐hìn‐kè tsaì, yaû waî tsitₒ‐k‘eī, waî shìng‐māt, waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān hìn shūk‐tsuî‐kè tsuî‐tsaì, k‘āp waî ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk shóh‐sui‐yûng‐kè.
34 Ngŏh‐teî waî tsaì‐sz kûng Leî‐meî‐yān k‘āp tsź‐mān to chap‐ch‘aū, mooĭ‐nīn pin‐kòh iù i‐tîng‐k‘eī tseung hìn‐faān‐tsaì‐kè ch‘aaī, chiù‐i ngŏh līt‐tsó‐kè ka, taaì‐yāp ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè tîn‐uk, shiu‐tsoî ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè t‘aān‐sheûng, i‐chuê lūt‐faatₒ shóh‐tsoì‐kè:
35 Yaû mooĭ‐nīn pit tseung ngŏh‐teî t‘īn‐teî ch‘oh‐shīng‐shūk‐kè t‘ó‐ch‘aán, kûng kokₒ‐yeûng shuê‐mūk ch‘oh‐shīng‐shūk‐kè kwóh, to taaì‐tò ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè tîn‐uk:
36 Yaû i‐chuê lūt‐faatₒ shóh‐tsoì‐kè, tseung ngŏh‐teî ch‘oh‐t‘oi‐kè tsaí, kûng shaú‐shang‐kè ch‘uk‐shang k‘āp ngaū‐yeūng, taaì‐tò ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè tîn‐uk, kaau‐kwòh haí Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk fûng‐sẑ‐kè tsaì‐sz.
37 Yaû tseung ch‘oh‐shīng‐shūk‐kè māk‐fán, uĕ‐k‘āp ngŏh‐teî kuí‐heí‐kè laĭ‐māt, kokₒ‐yeûng shuê‐sheûng ch‘oh‐shīng‐shūk‐kè kwóh, k‘āp tsaú yaū chi‐heí‐shaú chà‐kè, taaì‐tò tsaì‐sz ch‘uè, chai‐tsoî ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐kè foò‐fōng; k‘aaĭ ngŏh‐teî t‘īn shóh‐ch‘aán‐kè shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat, taaì‐tò Leî‐meî‐yān, yan‐waî Leî‐meî‐yān haí ngŏh‐teî shēng‐shóh‐ch‘aán‐kè ts‘uí shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat.
38 Tong Leî‐meî‐yān ts‘uí shāp‐fân‐yat chi‐shī, tsaì‐sz À‐lūn‐kè tsaí, iù kûng Leî‐meî‐yān t‘ūng‐maaī haí‐ch‘uè: yaû Leî‐meî‐yān iù k‘aaĭ shóh‐tak shāp‐fân‐yat chi‐chung, tseung shāp‐fân‐chi‐yat, taaì‐tò ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐uk‐kè fōng, chai‐yāp foó‐foò.
39 Yan‐waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kûng Leî‐meî‐yān, pit iù k‘aaĭ k‘uĭ shóh‐hìn‐kè kuk, san tsaú, kûng yaū, taaì‐tò shìng‐shóh‐kè heì‐mĭng, kung‐fûng‐yīk kè tsaì‐sz, hon‐shaú‐moōn‐kè, au‐koh‐kè, shóh‐tsoî‐kè fōng: tsaû ngŏh‐teî pit m̄‐faì‐heì ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐kè tîn‐uk à.
