1 TONG Pa‐sai wōng Koó‐līt uēn‐nīn, seúng yìng‐îm Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐chi‐haú shóh‐kóng‐kè, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐tûng Pa‐sai wōng Koó‐līt‐kè sam, lîng k‘uĭ ch‘uēn‐wān t‘ung kwokₒ, tsik‐haî ch‘ut‐uê‐chí wâ,
2 Pa‐sai wōng Koó‐līt kóm wâ, T‘in‐sheûng‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ĭ‐king tseung teî‐sheûng līt‐kwokₒ ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh; yaû mîng ngŏn tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng waî k‘uĭ kìn‐chuk tîn‐uk.
3 Neĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung, taân‐faān tsô k‘uĭ tsź‐mān‐kè? hóh‐ĭ sheŭng‐tò Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng kìn‐chuk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, uên k‘uĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ‐teî, (ts‘ź Sheûng‐Taì chan‐haî Sheûng‐Taì à.)
4 Faān shūk neĭ‐teî tsź‐mān, laū‐ts‘uēn tsoî keì‐kui kóh‐ch‘uè, iù kóh‐ch‘uè‐kè yān pong‐chôh k‘uĭ‐teî, sùng kam‐ngān, fòh‐ts‘oī, ch‘uk‐shang, lîng‐ngoî yīk hóh‐lōk‐ì kuen‐chôh, kung‐fûng tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐tîn‐uk.
5 Ue‐shî Yaū‐t‘aaì k‘āp Pîn‐ngă‐măn līt‐tsó‐foô‐kè ka‐cheúng, kûng tsaì‐sz k‘āp Leî‐meî‐yān, tsik‐haî faān shaû Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐kóm‐tûng k‘uĭ sam‐kè, kaai‐heí‐laī seúng sheŭng‐huì kìn‐chuk tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk.
6 Faān k‘uĭ‐teî sz̀‐waī‐kè yān, yaû k‘aaĭ ngān‐heì, wōng‐kam, fòh‐ts‘oī, ch‘uk‐shang, uĕ‐k‘āp pó‐māt, kin‐chôh k‘uĭ‐kè shaú, lîng‐ngoî yīk lōk‐ì kuen‐chôh.
7 Koó‐līt wōng yīk tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè heì‐mĭng ning‐ch‘ut‐laī, tsik‐haî Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, sik‐yāt ts‘ūng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng taaì‐ch‘ut chai‐tsoî tsẑ‐keí chùng‐shān‐kè miû‐chung;
8 Ni‐ti‐heì‐mĭng Pa‐sai wōng Koó‐līt t‘okₒ foò‐koon Māt‐leĭ‐t‘aatₒ‐kè shaú taaì‐ch‘ut‐laī shó‐kwòh, kaau‐peí Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè kwan‐cheúng Ch‘itₒ‐pa‐saatₒ.
9 Kòh‐ti shò‐mūk līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû: kam‐uē saam‐shāp, ngān‐uē yat‐ts‘in, to î‐shāp‐kaú pá,
10 Kam‐pooi saam‐shāp, ngān‐pooi‐chi‐ts‘z̀‐kè, sz̀‐paakₒ‐yat‐shāp, pīt‐yeûng heì‐mĭng yat‐ts‘in.
11 Kam‐ngān kokₒ‐heì‐mĭng, kûng‐kaì nğ‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ kîn. Taaì‐lĭng peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè yān, ts‘ūng Pa‐peí‐lūn faan‐sheŭng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐chi‐shī, Ch‘itₒ‐pa‐saatₒ tseung‐saaì ni‐ti heì‐mĭng taaì‐sheŭng‐laī.
