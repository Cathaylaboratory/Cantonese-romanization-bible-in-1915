1 TAAÎ‐NĀNG chi‐tsź à, neĭ ying‐koi tseung tsuen‐wīng k‘uēn‐līk kwai‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
2 Neĭ ying‐koi tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mēng‐chi‐wīng‐iû kwai‐faan k‘uĭ; tsoî wīng‐wā‐chi‐shìng‐shóh shūng‐paaì k‘uĭ.
3 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐sheng tsoî shuí‐sheûng: wīng‐kwong‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì hāng‐luī: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî taai shuí‐chi‐sheûng.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐sheng kīk‐yaŭ‐līk; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐sheng‐kīk‐wai‐mŏ.
5 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐sheng chàn‐t‘uĕn paakₒ‐heung‐shuê; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐sheng chàn‐t‘uĕn Leî‐pa‐nuên‐chi paakₒ‐heung‐shuê.
6 Lîng k‘eī t‘iù‐yeūk hó‐ts‘z̆ tūk‐ngaū; lîng Leî‐pa‐nuên k‘āp Sai‐līn‐shaan t‘iù‐yeūk, hó‐ts‘z̆ yĕ‐ngaū.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐luī‐sheng, shím‐ch‘ut fóh‐îm.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐luī‐sheng, chàn‐tûng kw‘òng‐yĕ; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chàn‐tûng Ka‐t‘itₒ‐chi‐kw‘òng‐yĕ.
9 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐luī‐sheng, lîng p‘ăn‐lūk shang‐tsź, yaû‐nāng chàn‐tûng lām‐chung shuê‐mūk: faān‐tsoî k‘uĭ tîn‐chung‐ché, to tsaàn‐meĭ k‘uĭ‐kè wīng‐iû.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘ŏh‐tsoî hūng‐shuí‐chi‐sheûng; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘ŏh‐tsoî pó‐tsôh, wĭng‐uen waī Wōng.
11 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit tseung līk ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐kè paakₒ‐sìng; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit chuk‐fuk k‘uĭ‐kè paakₒ‐sìng p‘īng‐on.
