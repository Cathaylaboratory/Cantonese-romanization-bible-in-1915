1 OH‐PA‐TAÍ‐chi‐î‐tseûng à. ¶ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóng‐k‘āp Ĭ‐tung kóm wâ; Ngŏh‐teî yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘eng‐mān siu‐sik, yīk‐yaŭ waí‐ch‘aai tá‐faatₒ‐huì līt‐mān‐chi‐chung wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù‐heí‐laī, yaû t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh‐teî hing‐heí laī‐tá‐cheùng, kung‐kik k‘uĭ la.
2 T‘aí‐a, ngŏh shaí‐neĭ tsoî‐līt‐mān‐chi‐chung: saì‐siú‐kè, neĭ kìn‐miŭ‐shî to hó‐kaau‐kwaan.
3 Chuê‐tsoî shēk‐ngaām‐ch‘uè‐kè, yaû tsoî‐tsùn‐ko‐ch‘uè yaŭ chuê‐shóh‐kè; sam‐chung tsẑ‐wâ, Mat‐shuī ooĭ‐laai‐ngŏh‐lōk‐teî ni? neĭ‐teî sam‐chung‐kè kiu‐ngô, tsaû‐haî hei‐p‘ìn‐neĭ à.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Neĭ sui‐īn shing‐sheŭng hó‐ts‘z̆ mā‐ying kóm sui‐īn kìn‐neĭ‐kè ch‘aaū kitₒ‐tsoî līt‐sing‐chi‐kaan, ngŏh pit‐ts‘ūng kòh‐ch‘uè laai‐neĭ‐lōk‐laī à.
5 Uē‐kwóh yaŭ‐ts‘aāk‐laī‐tò‐neĭ‐ch‘uè, waāk tsoî‐yê‐kaan tá‐kipₒ, k‘uĭ‐teî heí‐m̄‐t‘au‐sitₒ chì‐tò sam‐tsuk me? (neĭ kìn‐t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐kè haî‐chiù‐kóm‐yeûng à!) waāk yaŭ‐chaāk‐p‘ō‐t‘aī‐tsź‐kè laī‐neĭ‐ch‘uè, k‘uĭ‐teî heí‐m̄‐laū‐lōk ti‐uē‐shîng‐kè me?
6 Ĭ‐sò‐kè sẑ‐ts‘īng, i‐tím‐yeûng kìn‐ch‘aatₒ ni! k‘uĭ yán‐ts‘ōng‐kè pó‐māt, i tím‐yeûng chaaú‐ts‘ām ni!
7 Kûng‐neĭ kitₒ‐tóng‐kè chùng‐yān, taaì‐neĭ‐hāng‐tò kíng‐kaaì: t‘ūng neĭ seung‐wōh‐kè haî‐hei‐p‘ìn‐neĭ, yaû shìng‐tīk‐neĭ; shīk‐neĭ‐péng‐kè, ch‘itₒ‐kei‐laâm hoî‐neĭ: tsoî k‘uĭ noî‐ch‘ung yat‐ti‐to‐mŏ‐ts‘ung‐mīng.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Tong‐kòh‐yāt, ngŏh heí‐m̄‐haí Ĭ‐tung mīt‐tsuēt chì‐waî‐kè yān, haí Ĭ‐sò‐kè shaan mīt‐tsuēt ts‘ung‐mīng‐kè yān me?
9 T‘aī‐maân à, neĭ‐kè yŭng‐sẑ ooĭ‐kìn‐king‐hoī, chì‐tò haí Ĭ‐sò‐kè shaan, kokₒ‐yān peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk mīt‐tsuēt‐kè lokₒ.
10 Yan‐waî neĭ heùng‐hing‐taî Ngă‐kokₒ shóh‐hāng‐k‘eūng‐okₒ, pit‐yaŭ‐sau‐yūk‐lām‐neĭ, yīk‐tò wĭng‐uĕn t‘uĕn‐tsuēt‐kè lokₒ.
11 Tong neĭ haí tuì‐mîn k‘eĭ‐laāp kòh‐yāt, yaŭ ngoî‐yān lŏ‐leūk‐k‘uĭ‐kè ka‐ch‘aán, ngoî‐pong‐kè yān yāp‐k‘uĭ‐shēng‐moōn, chap‐ch‘aū seúng‐tak Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐chi‐shī, neĭ kûng ngoî‐pong‐yān tsô‐poôn‐yat‐yeûng.
12 Tong neĭ hing‐taî‐chi‐yāt‐tsź, tsik‐haî shaû‐naân‐chi‐shī, neĭ m̄‐hó‐haí‐p‘ōng‐pin t‘aí‐chuê‐k‘uĭ; tong Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè tsź‐suen peî‐ts‘aān‐hoî kòh‐yāt, neĭ m̄‐hó‐yan‐ts‘ź‐sẑ foon‐heí, yaû tsoî‐foó‐naân‐kè shī, m̄‐hó‐kóng‐kiu‐ngô‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ.
13 Tong ngŏh‐tsź‐mān shaû‐wôh‐waân‐kè yāt, neĭ m̄‐hó‐yāp k‘uĭ‐shēng‐moōn; ī‐ch‘é tong k‘uĭ shaû‐tsoi‐naân‐kòh‐yāt, m̄‐hó‐t‘aí‐chuê‐k‘uĭ‐foó‐ch‘óh, yaû tong k‘uĭ‐shaû‐tsoi‐naân‐kòh‐yāt, m̄‐hó‐peí‐shaú‐ts‘uí‐k‘uĭ‐ts‘oī‐māt;
14 M̄‐hó tsoî‐shāp‐tsẑ‐lô‐haú t‘uĕn‐tsuēt k‘uĭ t‘ō‐tsaú‐kè; tsoî‐foó‐naân‐kòh‐yāt, yaū k‘uĭ uē‐shîng‐kè, m̄‐hó‐kaau‐ch‘ut‐laī lokₒ.
15 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yāt‐tsź, lām‐kân‐maân‐kwokₒ‐kè: i‐neĭ‐shóh‐tsô, tsaû chiù‐kóm‐yeûng tsô‐kwòh neĭ: neĭ‐shóh‐hāng‐kè sẑ, pit‐kwai‐faan neĭ‐t‘aū‐sheûng à.
16 Hó‐ts‘z̆ neĭ‐tsoî‐ngŏh‐shìng‐shaan‐ch‘uè yám‐kwòh, maân‐kwokₒ‐tsaû‐kóm‐yeûng‐pat‐hitₒ‐laī‐yám, k‘uĭ‐teî shāt‐haî ooĭ‐yám, ooĭ‐t‘an‐lōk, yaû pìn‐waī hó‐ts‘z̆ m̄‐ts‘āng‐yaŭ‐kè kóm.
17 Waī‐haî tsoî Sun‐shēng pit‐yaŭ tóh‐peî‐hiu‐kè yān, to‐shīng‐waī‐shìng‐kitₒ‐kè; Ngă‐kokₒ‐kè ka yīk‐pit‐shīng‐shaû k‘uĭ‐kè teî‐īp lokₒ.
18 Ngă‐kokₒ‐kè ka, ooĭ‐shīng‐waī‐fóh, Yeukₒ‐shat‐kè ka tsô‐fóh‐îm, ī‐ch‘é Ĭ‐sò‐kè ka ooĭ‐tsô ts‘ó‐kan, fóh tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung shiu‐heí t‘an‐mīt‐kè; Ĭ‐sò‐kè ka, to‐pit mŏ‐yat‐kòh laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè; yan‐waî ni‐ti haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐kè lokₒ.
19 Naām‐fong‐kè yān, ooĭ‐tak‐tó Ĭ‐sò‐kè shaan‐tsô‐teî‐īp; p‘īng‐teî‐kè yān, tseung‐tak‐tó Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè teî: k‘uĭ‐teî ooĭ‐tak Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐kè t‘īn, kûng Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐kè t‘īn tsô‐teî‐īp: Pîn‐ngă‐măn yaû‐shīng‐shaû Kei‐līt à.
20 Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsź‐suen‐chi‐chung, yaū ts‘ź‐kwan‐chung peî‐lŏ‐leūk, tsik‐haî tsoî Ka‐naām‐yān‐chi‐chung‐kè, ooĭ‐tak‐tó Saatₒ‐lāk‐faatₒ‐kè kíng‐kaaì tsô‐teî‐īp lokₒ; Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè, tsik‐haî tsoî Sai‐faatₒ‐la, ooĭ‐tak‐tó naām‐fong‐kè chùng‐yap tsô‐teî‐īp.
21 Ī‐ch‘é kaù‐ché yaū Sun‐shaan‐ch‘uè ch‘ut, laī‐shám‐p‘oòn Ĭ‐sò‐kè shaan; kòh‐kwokₒ tsaû kwai‐shūk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lokₒ.
