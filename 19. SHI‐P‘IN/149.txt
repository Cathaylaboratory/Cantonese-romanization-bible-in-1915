1 NEĬ‐TEÎ tong‐tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā. Ch‘eùng san‐shi koh‐tsûng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsoî kìng‐k‘īn‐chi‐yān ooî‐chung laī‐ch‘ing‐tsûng.
2 Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsūk, tong yan ch‘òng‐tsô neĭ‐chi‐Chué laī‐foon‐heí: Sun‐shēng‐chi‐mān, tong yan neĭ‐chi‐Wōng laī‐tsokₒ‐ngōk.
3 Ying‐tong shaú mŏ‐tsuk‐tô, tsaàn‐meĭ Chué mēng: tá‐koó t‘aān‐k‘ām, koh‐tsûng Sheûng‐Taì.
4 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā foon‐heí k‘uĭ‐chi‐paakₒ‐sìng: ch‘íng‐kaù him‐pei‐ché, lîng k‘uĭ tsuen‐wīng, kìng‐k‘īn‐chi‐yān tak‐wīng‐kwong, ying‐tong foon‐lōk.
5 Sui tsoî‐ch‘ōng‐sheûng: yīk foon‐lōk koh‐tsûng.
6 Haú tsaàn Sheûng‐Taì, shaú chap‐leî‐kìm;
7 Iù pò līt‐pong‐chi‐ch‘aū, hāng maân‐mān‐chi‐yīng;
8 Tseung shīng‐sokₒ laī‐póng līt‐wōng, tseung sóh‐lîn laī‐sóh k‘uĭ‐chi‐shān‐tsź;
9 I‐chuê lūt‐faatₒ, p‘oòn‐tuèn k‘uĭ‐teî: faān haî kìng‐k‘īn‐chi‐yān, to yaŭ kóm‐yeûng tsuen‐wīng. Neĭ‐teî tong‐tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
