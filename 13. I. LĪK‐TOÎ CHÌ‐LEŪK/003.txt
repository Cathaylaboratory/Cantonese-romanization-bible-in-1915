1 TAAÎ‐WAÎ tsoî Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn shóh‐shang‐kè tsaí, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû; cheúng‐tsź Òm‐nuên, ts‘ūng Yē‐sz‐līt‐teî‐kè nuĭ À‐hei‐nuĕn shóh‐shang‐kè; k‘eī‐ts‘z̀ Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ, ts‘ūng Ka‐māt‐teî‐kè nuĭ À‐peì‐koi shóh‐shang‐kè:
2 K‘eī‐saam Aapₒ‐sha‐lūng, ts‘ūng Kei‐shūt wōng Taāt‐maaĭ‐kè nuĭ Mă‐ka shóh‐shang‐kè: k‘eī‐sz̀ À‐toh‐neī‐à, ts‘ūng Hap‐k‘āp shóh‐shang‐kè:
3 K‘eī‐nğ Shî‐faatₒ‐t‘aī‐à, ts‘ūng À‐peì‐taāt shóh‐shang‐kè: k‘eī‐lūk Ĭ‐tāk‐nîm, ts‘ūng Taaî‐waî‐kè ts‘ai Ĭ‐haakₒ‐la shóh‐shang‐kè.
4 Ts‘ź‐lūk‐kòh haî Taaî‐waî tsoî Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn shóh‐shang‐kè; k‘uĭ tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè tsô wōng, ts‘at nīn līng lūk‐kòh uēt: yaû tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng saam‐shāp‐saam nīn.
5 Tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng shóh‐shang‐kè tsaí; haî Shî‐mooĭ, Shuetₒ‐pa, Nā‐taan, Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, ts‘ź‐sz̀‐kòh haî ts‘ūng Maĭ‐leî‐à‐kè nuĭ, Paāt‐shue‐à shóh‐shang‐kè:
6 Yaû‐yaŭ Yik‐hāt, Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă, Ĭ‐leî‐faatₒ‐līt,
7 Nā‐ka, Neī‐fat, Yē‐fei‐à,
8 Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă, Ĭ‐leî‐à‐t‘aaì, Ĭ‐leî‐faatₒ‐līt, kûng‐kaì kaú‐kòh.
9 Ts‘ź‐yat‐k‘oì haî Taaî‐waî‐kè tsaí, k‘uĭ ts‘ipₒ‐kè chùng‐tsź m̄‐tsoî‐noî, k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè mooî haî Taaî‐mă.
10 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè tsaí Lōh‐poh‐òm, Lōh‐poh‐òm‐kè tsaí À‐peì‐à, À‐peì‐à‐kè tsaí À‐saatₒ, À‐saatₒ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ,
11 Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐laān, Yeukₒ‐laān‐kè tsaí À‐hap‐tsê, À‐hap‐tsê‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hāt,
12 Yeukₒ‐hāt‐kè tsaí À‐mă‐tsê, À‐mă‐tsê‐kè tsaí À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐taam,
13 Yeukₒ‐taam‐kè tsaí À‐hap‐sẑ, À‐hap‐sẑ‐kè tsaí Hei‐sai‐ka, Hei‐sai‐ka‐kè tsaí Mă‐nā‐sai,
14 Mă‐nā‐sai‐kè tsaí À‐moōn, À‐moōn‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐sai‐à.
15 Yeukₒ‐sai‐à‐kè tsaí, cheúng‐tsź haî Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, k‘eī‐ts‘z̀ Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam, k‘eī‐saam Sai‐taí‐ka, k‘eī‐sz̀ Sha‐lūng.
16 Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam‐kè tsaí Yē‐koh‐neī‐à: Yē‐koh‐neī‐à‐kè tsaí Sai‐taí‐ka.
17 Peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè Yē‐koh‐neī‐à‐kè tsaí, Saatₒ‐la‐t‘itₒ,
18 Mă‐k‘eī‐laān, Peí‐t‘aaì‐à, Shî‐nā‐saatₒ, Yē‐ka‐maĭ, Hōh‐sha‐mă, Neī‐t‘a‐peì.
19 Peí‐t‘aaì‐à‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ, Shî‐mooĭ: Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ‐kè tsaí Maĭ‐shue‐laān, Hap‐nā‐neī, k‘eī‐mooî‐haî Shî‐lōh‐māt:
20 Yaû yaŭ Hap‐shue‐pa, Oh‐hak, Peí‐leĭ‐ka, Hap‐saatₒ‐taí, Uē‐saatₒ‐hak‐shik, kûng‐kaì nğ‐kòh.
21 Hap‐nā‐neī‐kè tsaí, haî Peí‐la‐taí kûng Yē‐ts‘oì‐à: yaû‐yaŭ Leĭ‐pa‐ngă‐kè tsaí, À‐ĭ‐naān‐kè tsaí, Oh‐pa‐taí‐kè tsaí, Shî‐ka‐neī‐kè tsaí.
22 Shî‐ka‐neī‐kè tsaí haî Shî‐mâ‐ngă: Shî‐mâ‐ngă‐kè tsaí haî Hap‐tāt, Ĭ‐kaapₒ, Pa‐leĭ‐à, Neī‐leĭ‐à, Sha‐faatₒ, kûng‐kaì lūk‐kòh.
23 Neī‐leĭ‐ngă‐kè tsaí haî Ĭ‐leî‐oh‐naaĭ, Hei‐sai‐ka, Aapₒ‐leî‐kom, kûng‐kaì saam‐kòh.
24 Ĭ‐leî‐oh‐naaĭ‐kè tsaí haî Hōh‐t‘aaì‐ngă, Ĭ‐leî‐à‐shāt, Peí‐loī‐à, À‐kuk, Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, T‘a‐loī‐à, À‐nā‐neī, kûng‐kaì ts‘at‐kòh.
