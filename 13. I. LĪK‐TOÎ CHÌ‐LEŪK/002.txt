1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT‐KÈ tsaí, haî Laū‐pîn, Sai‐mîn, Leî‐meî, Yaū‐taaî, Ĭ‐saatₒ‐ka, Sai‐pò‐lūn,
2 Taân, Yeukₒ‐shat, Pîn‐ngă‐măn, Naāp‐taaî‐leî, Ka‐tak, À‐ch‘itₒ.
3 ¶ Yaū‐taaî‐kè tsaí Ĭ, Oh‐naām, Shî‐la: ni‐saam‐kòh ts‘ūng Ka‐naām foŏ‐yān Paāt‐shue‐à shóh‐shang‐kè. Yaū‐taaî‐kè cheúng‐tsź Ĭ, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû shaatₒ k‘uĭ.
4 Yaū‐taaî‐kè sik‐foŏ Taaî‐mă, waî k‘uĭ shang Faatₒ‐lāk‐sẑ, kûng Sai‐la. Yaū‐taaî faān shóh‐yaŭ‐kè tsaí haî nğ‐kòh.
5 Faatₒ‐lāk‐sẑ‐kè tsaí Hei‐sz‐lūn, Hap‐mŏ‐lāk.
6 Sai‐la‐kè tsaí Sam‐leĭ, Ĭ‐t‘aàm, Hei‐maân, Kaapₒ‐kokₒ, Taāt‐taaî: kûng‐kaì nğ‐kòh.
7 Ka‐maĭ‐kè tsaí À‐kon, k‘uĭ iŭ‐luên Ĭ‐shik‐līt, haî yan tong‐mīt‐kè māt, ĭ‐chì faân‐tsuî.
8 Ĭ‐t‘aàm‐kè tsaí À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à.
9 Hei‐sz‐lūn shóh‐shang‐kè tsaí haî Yē‐la‐mīt, Laān, Ka‐lāk‐fei.
10 Laān shang À‐maĭ‐nā‐t‘aatₒ; À‐maĭ‐nā‐t‘aatₒ shang Nā‐shûn, tsô Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè kwan‐cheúng;
11 Nā‐shûn shang Saatₒ‐moōn, Saatₒ‐moōn shang Poh‐sẑ,
12 Poh‐sẑ shang Oh‐pīt, Oh‐pīt shang Yē‐sai,
13 Yē‐sai shang cheúng‐tsź Ĭ‐leî‐aapₒ, k‘eī‐ts‘z̀ À‐peì‐nā‐t‘aatₒ, k‘eī‐saam Shî‐mooĭ,
14 K‘eī‐sz̀ Nā‐taân‐īp, k‘eī‐nğ La‐t‘aaì.
15 K‘eī‐lūk Oh‐sin, k‘eī‐ts‘at Taaî‐waî:
16 K‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsź‐mooî haî Sai‐lŏ‐ngă, kûng À‐peì‐koi. Sai‐lŏ‐ngă‐kè tsaí À‐peì‐shai, Yeukₒ‐aapₒ, À‐saatₒ‐haak, kûng‐kaì saam‐kòh.
17 À‐peì‐koi shang À‐mă‐saatₒ: À‐mă‐saatₒ‐kè foô‐ts‘an, haî Ĭ‐shāt‐mă‐leî‐yān Yik‐t‘ipₒ.
18 Hei‐sz‐lūn‐kè tsaí Ka‐lāk, ts‘ūng k‘uĭ ts‘ai À‐so‐pa, yīk ts‘ūng Yē‐leūk shang tsaí: À‐so‐pa‐kè tsaí Yē‐ch‘itₒ, Shuetₒ‐pa, Aapₒ‐tun.
19 À‐so‐pa sź‐haû, Ka‐lāk yaû ts‘uì Ĭ‐faatₒ‐taaî ts‘ūng k‘uĭ shang Oô‐ĭ.
20 Oô‐ĭ shang Oo‐leî, Oo‐leî shang Peì‐saatₒ‐līt.
21 Ts‘ź‐haû Hei‐sz‐lūn kûng Kei‐līt‐kè tsó‐foô Mă‐kat‐kè nuĭ t‘ūng‐fōng, Hei‐sz‐lūn lūk‐shāp suì chì ts‘uì k‘uĭ; ts‘ūng k‘uĭ shang Sai‐kotₒ.
22 Sai‐kotₒ shang Ngaaī‐ĭ, k‘uĭ tsoî Kei‐līt‐teî yaŭ î‐shāp‐saam‐kòh‐shēng.
23 Haû‐loī Kei‐shūt‐yān, kûng À‐laān‐yān, tuēt‐hiu Ngaaī‐ĭ‐kokₒ‐shēng, k‘āp Kei‐naāp‐shēng, kûng kòh‐ti ts‘uen‐heung, kûng‐kaì lūk‐shāp‐kòh‐shēng. Ts‘ź yat‐k‘oì haî Kei‐līt‐kè tsó‐foô, Mă‐kat‐kè tsaí.
24 Hei‐sz‐lūn tsoî Ĭ‐faatₒ‐taaî‐ka‐lāk sź‐haû, k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘ai À‐peì‐à waî k‘uĭ shang À‐shūt, tsik‐haî T‘aī‐koh‐à‐kè tsó‐foô.
25 Hei‐sz‐lūn‐kè cheúng‐tsź, Yē‐la‐mīt‐kè tsaí, k‘uĭ cheúng‐tsź haî Laān, yaû shang Pò‐nā, Oh‐līn, Oh‐sin, À‐hei‐ngă.
26 Yē‐la‐mīt yaû ts‘uì pīt‐î‐kòh ts‘ai, mēng‐kiù À‐t‘a‐la: k‘uĭ haì Oh‐naām‐kè mŏ‐ts‘an.
27 Yē‐la‐mīt‐kè cheúng‐tsź, Laān‐kè tsaí, haî Mă‐sẑ, Ngă‐mān, Ĭ‐kitₒ.
28 Oh‐naām‐kè tsaí haî Sha‐maaĭ, Ngă‐t‘a. Sha‐maaĭ‐kè tsaí‐Nā‐taapₒ, À‐peì‐shūt.
29 À‐peì‐shūt‐kè ts‘ai, mēng‐kiù À‐peì‐koi, À‐peì‐koi waî k‘uĭ shang À‐pîn, Moh‐laāp.
30 Nā‐taapₒ‐kè tsaí Sai‐lāk. À‐p‘ìn: taân Sai‐lāk meì‐ts‘āng yaŭ tsaí, tsaû sź‐hiu.
31 À‐p‘ìn‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐shî. Ĭ‐shî‐kè tsaí Shî‐shaan. Shî‐shaan‐kè tsaí À‐loī.
32 Sha‐maaĭ‐kè saì‐ló Ngă‐t‘a‐kè tsaí Yik‐t‘ipₒ, Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan: Yik‐t‘ipₒ meî‐ts‘āng yaŭ tsaí tsaû sź‐hiu.
33 Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan‐kè tsaí Peí‐lāk. Saatₒ‐saatₒ. Ts‘ź‐haî Yē‐la‐mīt‐kè tsź‐suen.
34 Shî‐shaan mŏ‐tsaí, waī‐yaŭ nuĭ. Shî‐shaan yaŭ yat‐kòh‐pūk, mēng‐kiù Yē‐la, haî Oi‐k‘āp‐yān.
35 Shî‐shaan tseung k‘uĭ nuĭ peí‐kwòh k‘uĭ pūk Yē‐la tsô ts‘ai; ts‘ūng k‘uĭ shang‐hiu À‐t‘aaì.
36 À‐t‘aaì shang Nā‐taan, Nā‐taan shang Saatₒ‐paatₒ,
37 Saatₒ‐paatₒ shang Ĭ‐fat‐laāp, Ĭ‐fat‐laāp shang Oh‐paakₒ,
38 Oh‐paakₒ shang Yē‐oô, Yē‐oô shang À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à,
39 À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à shang Hei‐līk, Hei‐līk shang Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ,
40 Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ shang Sai‐sha‐maaĭ, Sai‐sha‐maaĭ shang Sha‐lūng,
41 Sha‐lūng shang Yē‐ka‐maĭ, Yē‐ka‐maĭ shang Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă.
42 Yē‐la‐mīt saì‐ló Ka‐lāk‐kè tsaí, cheúng‐tsź Maĭ‐sha, tsik‐haî Sai‐fat‐kè tsó‐foô; yaû shang Mă‐leĭ‐sha chùng‐tsź, Mă‐leĭ‐sha haî Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn‐kè tsó‐foô.
43 Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn‐kè tsaí Hóh‐la, Taaî‐pò‐à, Leĭ‐kam, Shî‐mă.
44 Shî‐mă shang La‐hōm, tsik‐haî Yeukₒ‐koòn‐kè tsó‐foô: Leĭ‐kam shang Sha‐maaĭ.
45 Sha‐maaĭ‐kè tsaí haî Mă‐wān: Mă‐wān haî Paakₒ‐suk‐kè tsó‐foô.
46 Ka‐lāk‐kè ts‘ipₒ Ĭ‐faatₒ, shang Hap‐laān, Moh‐saatₒ, Ka‐sitₒ: Hap‐laān shang Ka‐sitₒ.
47 Ngă‐t‘aaì‐kè tsaí Leĭ‐kìm, Yeukₒ‐taam, Kei‐shaan, Peí‐līk, Ĭ‐faatₒ, Sha‐aapₒ.
48 Ka‐lāk‐kè ts‘ipₒ Mă‐ka shang Shî‐pīt, Tak‐hap‐nā.
49 Yaû shang Māk‐mă‐nā‐kè tsó‐foô Sha‐aapₒ, Moōt‐peí‐nā k‘āp Kei‐peì‐à‐kè tsó‐foô Shî‐faatₒ: Ka‐lāk‐kè nuĭ haî Aapₒ‐saatₒ.
50 Ĭ‐faatₒ‐taaî‐kè cheúng‐tsź Oô‐ĭ, Oô‐ĭ tsaí Ka‐lāk‐kè tsź‐suen, līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû; Kei‐līt‐yē‐lām‐kè tsó‐foô Shuetₒ‐paatₒ,
51 Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng‐kè tsó‐foô Saatₒ‐mă, Paakₒ‐ka‐tak‐kè tsó‐foô Yām‐lāk.
52 Kei‐līt‐yē‐lām‐kè tsó‐foô Shuetₒ‐paatₒ, yīk‐yaŭ tsaí, tsik‐haî Hap‐lōh‐ĭ k‘āp yat‐poòn Mă‐nā‐haak‐yān.
53 Kei‐līt‐yē‐lām‐kè chùng‐tsūk; haî Ĭ‐tak‐yān, Pò‐yān, Shue‐mă‐yān, Māt‐loī‐yān, ts‘ūng ts‘ź‐tsūk‐chung shang‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè; yaŭ Saatₒ‐laāp‐yān, k‘āp Yik‐līk‐yān.
54 Saatₒ‐mă‐kè tsź‐suen, haî Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng‐yān, Neī‐t‘ōh‐faatₒ‐yān, À‐taaî‐lūk, Paakₒ‐yeukₒ‐aapₒ chi ka, yat‐poòn Mă‐nā‐haak‐yān, k‘āp Shóh‐leĭ‐yān.
55 Chuê‐tsoî Ngă‐pit chùng‐shîn‐sé‐kè ka; tsik‐haî Tak‐la‐yān, Shî‐maĭ‐aapₒ‐yān, So‐kaapₒ‐yān. Ts‘ź‐haî Kei‐neī‐yān ts‘ūng Leĭ‐kaapₒ ka‐kè tsó‐foô Hap‐mootₒ shóh‐shang‐kè.
