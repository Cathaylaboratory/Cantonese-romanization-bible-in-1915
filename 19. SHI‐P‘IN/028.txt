1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ haî ngŏh p‘oōn‐shēk; ngŏh t‘ó‐kò neĭ, k‘aū neĭ t‘eng‐mān: t‘óng‐yeūk neĭ m̄‐yìng‐taapₒ, ngŏh tsaû hó‐ts‘z̆ yāp‐haâm‐haang‐ché yat‐yeûng.
2 Ngŏh heùng‐chuê neĭ‐chi‐shìng‐tîn, kuí‐heí‐shaú laī‐foo‐kiù neĭ, k‘aū neĭ t‘eng ngŏh k‘eī‐t‘ó‐chi‐sheng.
3 Ts‘itₒ‐mōk heì ngŏh uē heì tsuî‐yān, uĕ‐k‘āp kaan‐okₒ‐ché yat‐yeûng, k‘uĭ‐teî kûng‐yān‐kóng shuetₒ‐wâ shâm‐t‘īm, sam‐luĭ shâm‐hím.
4 Uên neĭ i k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐chi‐okₒ ĭ pò k‘uĭ: i k‘uĭ shaú shóh‐tsô; i k‘uĭ shóh‐toî‐yān‐ché ĭ sheūng k‘uĭ.
5 Yan k‘uĭ m̄‐koò‐nîm Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hāng‐waī, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ shaú‐chi shóh‐tsokₒ, koò‐ts‘ź Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit tsiú‐mīt k‘uĭ, m̄‐hing‐heí k‘uĭ.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng t‘eng ngŏh k‘eī‐t‘ó‐chi‐sheng, uên neĭ shaû ngŏh shóh‐chuk.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî ngŏh‐chi‐līk‐leûng, haî ngŏh‐chi‐t‘āng‐p‘aaī; ngŏh sam í‐laaî k‘uĭ, tsaû tak foō‐chôh: ngŏh sam hó‐taaî‐foon‐heí; ngŏh pit koh‐shi laī‐tsaàn‐tsûng.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî k‘uĭ paakₒ‐sìng‐chi‐līk‐leûng, yaû haî kin‐koò shóh, laī‐ch‘íng‐kaù faān‐k‘uĭ shóh‐fung‐laāp‐ché.
9 Uên neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù neĭ‐chi‐suén‐mān, ts‘z̀ fuk neĭ‐chi‐ch‘aán: wĭng‐uĕn mūk‐yeŭng k‘uĭ, foō‐ch‘ī k‘uĭ.
