1 SHEÛNG‐TAÌ à, tsoî Sun‐shēng‐chi‐yān, suk‐tsîng laī‐tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ: naaĭ‐haî poón‐ying: huí‐kwòh‐uên‐ché, yīk pit tuì neĭ sheūng‐waān.
2 T‘eng‐t‘ó‐kò‐chi‐Chué à, chùng‐yān pit laī ts‘an‐tsaû neĭ.
3 Ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘ŭng‐tsuî, m̄‐tong‐tak‐heí: ngŏh‐chi‐kwòh‐okₒ, neĭ pit ch‘uē‐huì.
4 Neĭ shóh‐kaán‐chaāk shaí k‘uĭ laī‐ts‘an‐kân neĭ, chuê‐tsoî‐neĭ‐uk‐chung‐ché, kòh‐ti‐yān haî yaŭ‐fuk: neĭ uk‐chung‐chi‐meĭ‐fuk, shìng‐tîn‐chi‐taaî‐yan, pit lîng ngŏh‐teî paaú‐tsuk.
5 Kaù ngŏh‐teî‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì à, neĭ pit píng kung‐î, hāng īm‐wai laī‐yìng ngŏh‐teî; teî‐kīk hoí‐kokₒ, uĕn‐fong‐chi‐yān, to í‐laaî neĭ:
6 Sheûng‐Taì k‘aaĭ taaî‐k‘uēn‐nāng laī ch‘uk‐iu; yûng keí‐chi‐līk laī‐kin‐tîng shaan‐ngōk:
7 Neĭ nāng chí‐sik hoí‐sheûng poh‐lông‐chi‐sheng, chí‐sik maân‐mān huen‐wa‐chi‐shaì.
8 Kòh‐shī chuê‐tsoî kīk‐uĕn chi‐yān, kìng‐waì neĭ î‐tsik: yāt‐ch‘ut, yāt‐yāp‐teî‐fong‐chi‐yān, mō‐pat‐koh‐tsûng neĭ.
9 Neĭ ĭ‐king kuèn‐koò teî‐t‘ó, kòng uĕ tsz‐yûn, shaí k‘uĭ shīng‐waī feī‐yûn‐chi‐teî: Sheûng‐Taì chi‐hōh, sheūng‐shī shuí‐moŏn: táng paakₒ‐kuk shang‐cheúng, ni‐ti haî neĭ shóh‐uê‐peî.
10 Neĭ lām‐laaî t‘īn‐maŭ: chíng‐p‘īng k‘eī yuk‐yeūng: k‘aaĭ meī‐uĕ tsz‐yûn k‘uĭ: neĭ chuk‐fuk k‘uĭ, lîng wōh‐miū shang‐cheúng.
11 Shīng‐nīn tak neĭ yan‐waî ka‐meĭ; neĭ shóh‐hāng‐kwòh‐chi‐teî, tsaû ts‘z̀ yan‐ko.
12 Tsik‐sź ts‘ó‐yĕ‐chi‐teî, yīk shi yan‐chaāk: chùng‐siú‐shaan to lîng k‘uĭ foon‐lōk.
13 Ts‘ó‐teî che‐moŏn yeūng‐kw‘ān; shaan‐kuk p‘o‐moŏn wōh‐māk; maân‐mān foon‐kiù, ī‐ch‘é koh‐tsûng.
