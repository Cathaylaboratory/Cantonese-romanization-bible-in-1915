1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ‐KÈ īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue‐ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐kóng‐uê‐īn kung‐kik Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè mūk‐yān, yaû tuì‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐wâ, Tsik‐haî tuì‐kòh‐ti‐mūk‐yān‐wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè mūk‐yān, tūk‐haî yeŭng‐tsẑ‐keí‐kè, yaŭ‐wôh‐lokₒ! mūk‐yān heí‐m̄‐ying‐koi waì‐kw‘ān‐yeūng me?
3 Neĭ‐teî shīk‐feī‐kè, yaû tsẑ‐keí cheukₒ‐kòh‐ti‐yeūng‐mō, yaû t‘ong‐feī‐kè yeūng‐tsaí: waī‐haî mŏ‐waì‐kw‘ān‐yeūng à.
4 Neĭ‐teî m̄‐ts‘āng yeŭng‐chòng kòh‐ti‐yaŭ‐chìng‐kè, m̄‐ts‘āng i‐hó kòh‐ti‐yaŭ‐pêng‐kè, m̄‐ts‘āng‐chaatₒ‐hó kòh‐ti‐chitₒ‐sheung‐kè, m̄‐taaì‐faan kòh‐ti‐peî‐kón‐chūk‐huì‐kè, m̄‐wán‐faan kòh‐ti‐mōng‐shat‐lô‐t‘ō‐kè; waī‐haî k‘aaĭ‐k‘eūng‐līk īm‐lîng koón‐hāt k‘uĭ.
5 Yan mŏ‐mūk‐yān, k‘uĭ‐teî peî‐fan‐saàn: yaû shīng‐waī t‘īn‐kaan chùng‐tsaú‐shaù‐kè shīk‐māt, yīk‐peî‐fan‐saàn‐kè.
6 Ngŏh‐kè yeūng mōng‐tsaú chùng‐shaan‐chi‐chung, tsoî‐kokₒ‐ko‐kong‐chi‐sheûng: ngŏh‐kè yeūng shāt‐haî peî‐fan‐saàn tsoî ts‘uēn‐teî‐mîn‐sheûng, tsúng‐mŏ‐yān fóng‐ts‘ām, waāk wán‐faan‐k‘uĭ‐teî.
7 Koò‐ts‘ź neĭ‐teî mūk‐yān à, iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ;
8 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Chiù‐ngŏh‐shang‐oōt‐kè, ngŏh‐kè yeūng k‘eī‐shāt shīng‐waī leūk‐māt, ngŏh‐kè yeūng heùng‐t‘īn‐kaan chùng‐tsaú‐shaù shīng‐waī shīk‐māt, haî yan mŏ‐mūk‐yān, ngŏh‐kè mūk‐yān, to mŏ‐fóng‐ts‘ām‐ngŏh‐yeūng, waī‐haî kòh‐ti‐mūk‐yān, taân‐yeŭng‐feī‐tsẑ‐keí, tsúng‐mŏ‐waì‐ngŏh‐kè yeūng;
9 Koò‐ts‘ź neĭ‐teî mūk‐yān à, iù‐t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ;
10 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik kòh‐ti‐mūk‐yān; ngŏh pit‐heùng‐k‘uĭ shaú‐t‘ó‐mân ngŏh‐yeūng, yaû lîng‐k‘uĭ‐teî chí‐sik‐waì‐ngŏh‐yeūng; kòh‐ti‐mūk‐yān, m̄‐tsoì‐nāng‐yeŭng‐feī‐tsẑ‐keí; ngŏh pit‐kaù‐ngŏh‐yeūng t‘uetₒ‐leī‐k‘uĭ‐haú, táng‐m̄‐shīng‐waī mūk‐yān‐kè shīk‐māt.
11 Yan‐waî Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh tsẑ‐keí, tsik‐haî ngŏh, ooĭ‐fóng‐ts‘ām yaû wán‐tó ngŏh‐yeūng.
12 Hó‐ts‘z̆ mūk‐yān tsoî‐k‘uĭ fan‐saàn‐kè yeūng‐kòh‐yāt, fóng‐ts‘ām k‘uĭ kw‘ān‐yeūng; ngŏh pit‐chiù‐kóm‐yeûng fóng‐ts‘ām ngŏh‐yeūng, ngŏh‐pit‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî‐māt‐wān hak‐òm kòh‐yāt, peî‐fan‐saàn kokₒ‐ch‘uè‐kè, kaù‐t‘uetₒ‐k‘uĭ‐teî.
13 Ngŏh pit‐ts‘ūng‐līt‐mān‐ch‘uè taaì‐k‘uĭ‐ch‘ut‐laī, yaû ts‘ūng‐līt‐kwokₒ‐ch‘uè tsuî‐maaī‐k‘uĭ‐teî, taaì‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐faan‐yāp tsẑ‐keí‐poón‐kíng, yaû tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè chùng‐shaan, tsoî‐chùng‐shuí‐lô‐p‘ōng‐pin, yaû tsoî‐kíng‐noî kokₒ‐ch‘uè yaŭ‐kui‐mān‐chi‐teî, yeŭng‐yūk‐k‘uĭ‐teî.
14 Ngŏh pit‐k‘aaĭ‐meĭ‐hó‐kè ts‘ó‐t‘īn waì‐k‘uĭ‐teî, k‘uĭ‐kè yeūng‐laān pit‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐shaan ko‐tsùn‐ch‘uè: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tsoî‐meĭ‐hó‐kè yeūng‐laān fàn‐tó‐ch‘uè, yīk‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐shaan‐sheûng feī‐meĭ‐kè ts‘ó‐t‘īn‐ch‘uè shīk‐paaú.
15 Ngŏh‐tsẑ‐keí ooĭ‐waì‐ngŏh‐yeūng, yaû lîng‐k‘uĭ‐teî fàn‐tó‐ch‘uè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
16 Ngŏh pit‐fóng‐ts‘ām mōng‐shat‐kè, yaû tseung‐peî‐kón‐chūk‐huì‐kè taaì‐faan‐laī, yaû chaatₒ‐hó‐tá‐sheung‐kè, yaû yeŭng‐chòng yaŭ‐pêng‐kè; ī‐ch‘é mīt‐mōng kòh‐ti‐feī‐kè, k‘āp chòng‐kîn‐kè; ngŏh i‐p‘oòn‐tuèn‐chi‐leĭ, ooĭ‐waì‐k‘uĭ‐teî.
17 Chì‐ue neĭ, ngŏh‐kè kw‘ān‐yeūng à, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh tsoî‐ch‘uk‐shang kûng‐ch‘uk‐shang‐chi‐chung p‘oòn‐tuèn, līn‐yeūng‐kung kûng‐shaan‐yeūng‐kung.
18 Tsoî feī‐meĭ‐kè ts‘ó‐t‘īn shīk‐paaú, neĭ ĭ‐waī haî‐hó‐siú‐sẑ, waī‐haî to‐iù k‘aaĭ‐keukₒ‐ch‘aaí‐taāp k‘eī‐uē‐kè‐ts‘ó‐t‘īn me? yaû yám‐ts‘ing‐kitₒ‐kè shuí, waī‐haî to‐iù‐k‘aaĭ‐keukₒ‐chíng‐chūk k‘eī‐uē‐kè shuí me?
19 Chì‐ue ngŏh‐kè yeūng, k‘uĭ‐teî shīk neĭ‐teî peí‐keukₒ‐shóh‐ch‘aaí‐taāp‐kè; yaû yám neĭ‐teî peí‐keukₒ‐shóh‐chíng‐chūk‐kè.
20 ¶ Koò‐ts‘ź Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh, tsik‐haî‐ngŏh ooĭ‐tsoî feī‐ch‘uk‐shang kûng‐shaù‐ch‘uk‐shang‐chung‐kaan p‘oòn‐tuèn.
21 Yan neĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ‐pin‐shan kûng pokₒ‐t‘aū t‘ui‐úng, yaû k‘aaĭ‐kokₒ‐laī‐taí‐ch‘uk faān‐yaŭ‐pêng‐kè, chì‐tò‐fan‐saàn k‘uĭ‐teî;
22 Shóh‐ĭ ngŏh‐iù ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh‐kw‘ān‐yeūng, k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐tsoì tsô‐leūk‐māt; ngŏh pit‐tsoî‐ch‘uk‐shang kûng‐ch‘uk‐shang‐chi‐kaan p‘oòn‐tuèn.
23 Ngŏh pit‐ch‘itₒ‐laāp yat‐kòh‐mūk‐yān, tsik‐haî ngŏh‐pūk Taaî‐waî, tuk‐leĭ‐k‘uĭ‐teî; k‘uĭ pit‐waì‐k‘uĭ‐teî, yaû tsô‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè mūk‐yān.
24 Ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ooĭ‐tsô‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, ngŏh pūk Taaî‐waî, tsaû haí k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung tsô‐kwan‐cheúng: ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ts‘āng‐kóng‐kwòh lokₒ.
25 Ngŏh pit‐kûng‐k‘uĭ‐teî laāp‐wōh‐p‘īng‐kè māng‐yeukₒ, yaû lîng‐okₒ‐shaù tsoî‐kíng‐noî‐chí‐sik: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tsoî‐kw‘òng‐yĕ on‐īn‐kui‐chuê, yaû fàn‐tsoî shuê‐lām‐chung.
26 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng‐k‘uĭ‐teî, uĕ‐k‘āp ngŏh shaan‐kong sz̀‐waī teî‐fong, shīng‐waī shi‐fuk‐kè; yaû lîng‐chân‐uĕ i‐hōp‐shī‐haû kòng‐lōk; pit‐yaŭ‐fuk hó‐ts‘z̆ chân‐uĕ kóm.
27 T‘īn‐kaan‐kè shuê ooĭ‐kitₒ‐kwóh, teî to‐ooĭ‐shang t‘ó‐ch‘aán, k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî poón‐kíng tak‐on‐īn, tong‐ngŏh‐ch‘aakₒ‐waí aakₒ‐kè waāng‐mūk, yaû kaù‐k‘uĭ‐teî t‘uetₒ‐leī kòh‐ti yûng‐k‘uĭ‐laī‐fūk‐sẑ tsẑ‐keí‐kè yān‐chi‐shaú, kòh‐shī k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
28 K‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐tsoì heùng‐î‐pong‐yān tsô‐leūk‐māt, t‘īn‐kaan‐kè tsaú‐shaù, yaû‐m̄‐t‘an‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî; waī‐haî pit‐on‐īn‐kui‐chuê, tsúng‐mŏ‐yān lîng‐k‘uĭ‐teî king‐fong.
29 Ngŏh pit‐heùng‐k‘uĭ‐teî hing‐heí‐yat‐kòh yaŭ‐mēng‐kè chùng‐chīk‐uēn, k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî kíng‐noî, m̄‐tsoì peî‐wan‐yīk mīt‐mōng, yaû m̄‐tsoì taam‐tong î‐pong‐yān‐kè sau‐kw‘aì.
30 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh tsik‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, t‘ūng‐maaī‐k‘uĭ‐teî; ī‐ch‘é k‘uĭ‐teî tsik‐haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka, haî‐ngŏh‐kè tsź‐mān, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ:
31 Ī‐ch‘é neĭ‐teî tsô‐ngŏh‐kè yeūng, ngŏh ts‘ó‐t‘īn‐kè yeūng to‐haî‐yān, ngŏh yaû‐haî neĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ à.
