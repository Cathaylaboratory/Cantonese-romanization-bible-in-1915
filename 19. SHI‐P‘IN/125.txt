1 Í‐K‘AAÙ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐yān, yaū‐uē Sun‐shaan, wĭng‐uĕn laāp‐shāt, tsúng m̄‐iū‐tûng.
2 Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, sz̀‐pin yaŭ shaan waī‐chuê, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yīk haî kóm‐yeûng oô‐waî k‘uĭ paakₒ‐sìng, tsẑ kam‐yāt chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn.
3 Yan okₒ‐yān k‘uēn‐pìng, tsúng m̄‐koón‐leĭ‐tak shîn‐yān ch‘aán‐īp; taan‐p‘à shîn‐yān yīk shan‐shaú laī‐tsô okₒ‐sẑ.
4 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, neĭ pit shîn toî faān‐hāng‐shîn, k‘āp sam‐chung chìng‐chīk‐chi‐yān.
5 Chì‐ue chuèn‐t‘aū laī‐hāng tsẑ‐keí ts‘ē‐tô‐ché, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit tseung k‘uĭ t‘ūng okₒ‐yān yat‐yeûng hon‐toî: uên Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tak heúng p‘īng‐on.
