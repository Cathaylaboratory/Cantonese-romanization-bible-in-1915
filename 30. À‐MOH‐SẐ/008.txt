1 SHEÛNG‐CHUÉ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐yeûng hín‐shî‐kwòh‐ngŏh: t‘aí‐a, yaŭ‐yat‐lōh‐hâ‐t‘in‐kè kwóh.
2 K‘uĭ tsaû wâ, À‐moh‐sẑ à, neĭ t‘aí‐kìn mat‐yĕ ni? Ngŏh tsaû wâ, Yat‐lōh‐hâ‐t‘in‐kè kwóh lokₒ. Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì‐ngŏh‐wâ, Moōt‐shī ĭ‐king‐lām‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt, ngŏh‐kè tsź‐mān‐ch‘uè; ngŏh pit‐m̄‐tsoì‐king‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî à.
3 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Tong‐kòh‐yāt tîn‐uk‐kè koh, pìn‐waī haàm‐foó‐kè sheng: pit‐yaŭ taaî‐toh‐shi‐hoī, tsoî kokₒ‐ch‘uè‐wing‐ch‘ut; yaû m̄‐ch‘ut‐sheng lokₒ.
4 Neĭ‐teî seúng‐t‘an‐shīk kw‘àn‐fāt‐kè, yaû lîng kíng‐noî p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐yān shui‐paaî,
5 Wâ, Uēt‐shokₒ keí‐shī‐kwòh‐hiu, táng‐ngŏh‐hóh‐ĭ‐maaî‐kuk ni? on‐sik‐yāt keí‐shī‐kwòh‐hiu, táng‐ngŏh hóh‐ĭ‐t‘aan‐hoi‐ti‐māk ni? k‘aaĭ‐ĭ‐faatₒ laī‐chíng‐saì, k‘aaĭ shī‐haakₒ laī‐ka‐taaî, yaû‐yûng‐kwaí‐kaì shaí‐ká‐ngaî‐kè t‘in‐p‘īng.
6 Seúng‐k‘aaĭ‐ti‐ngān maaĭ‐hiu pei‐tsîn‐yān, seúng‐k‘aaĭ‐tuì‐haaī maaĭ‐hiu‐p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐yān; yaû‐maaî‐māk shóh‐shîng‐kè hong‐ch‘ān, ts‘ź‐táng‐yān iù‐t‘eng‐ni‐ti‐shuetₒ‐wâ lokₒ.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng‐chí‐chuê Ngă‐kokₒ‐kè shîn‐faatₒ‐shaî‐wâ, Ngŏh‐pit‐wĭng‐uĕn m̄‐mōng‐keì k‘uĭ‐kè hāng‐waī à.
8 Kíng‐teî m̄‐haî‐waî‐ts‘ź‐sẑ chàn‐tûng me? chuê‐tsoî‐k‘eī‐chung m̄‐haî‐oi‐huk me? ī‐ch‘é ts‘uēn‐teî cheùng‐heí, hó‐ts‘z̆ t‘iū‐hōh‐kóm‐iŭ‐luên; yaû‐tsoì hâ‐lōk, hó‐ts‘z̆ Oi‐k‘āp kòh‐t‘iū‐hōh kóm.
9 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Tong‐kòh‐yāt pit‐haî‐kóm, ngŏh‐haí aàn‐chaù‐shī ooĭ‐shaí‐yāt sai‐lōk, yaû tsoî‐kwong‐mīng‐chi‐yāt, shaí‐teî‐sheûng hak‐òm:
10 Ngŏh ooĭ‐shaí‐neĭ‐kè tsitₒ‐īn pìn‐waī‐pei‐huk, neĭ‐kè foon‐ch‘eùng pìn‐waī‐oi‐koh; ngŏh tseung‐shaí ts‘o‐mā‐pò ch‘uk‐chuê kokₒ‐yān‐kè iu, yaû lîng kokₒ‐yān t‘aū‐sheûng lat‐hiu‐faatₒ; ngŏh ooĭ‐shaí‐ts‘ź‐yāt oi‐huk, hó‐ts‘z̆ waî‐tūk‐tsź sòng‐mōng‐kè oi‐huk, k‘āp‐tò‐taí oi‐huk‐foó‐ch‘óh‐kè yāt kóm.
11 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, T‘aí‐a, yāt‐tsź‐pit‐tò, ngŏh tseung‐shaí‐kei‐fong lām‐ue‐ts‘ź‐teî, m̄‐haî yan‐mŏ‐péng‐kei‐ngôh, yīk‐m̄‐haî yan‐mŏ‐shuí kéng‐hotₒ, waī‐haî‐m̄‐tak‐t‘eng‐mān Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kei‐fong à:
12 K‘uĭ‐teî tsẑ‐tung‐hoí tò‐sai‐hoí, yaû tsẑ‐pak‐fong chì‐naām‐fong, pan‐tsaú‐wŏng‐loī, ts‘ām‐k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, tsúng‐m̄‐k‘aū‐tak‐tó à.
13 Tong‐kòh‐yāt meĭ‐laî‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ, kûng‐nīn‐shiù‐kè naām‐yān, kaai‐yan‐kéng‐hotₒ tsaû‐kw‘àn‐kuên lokₒ.
14 Yaŭ‐chí‐chuê Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à‐kè tsuî laī‐faatₒ‐shaî, wâ, Taân à, neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì shang‐oōt‐kè; yaû wâ, Pīt‐shî‐pa‐kè lô‐t‘ō shang‐oōt‐kè; kòh‐táng‐yān to‐ooĭ‐k‘ing‐paaî, yaû‐tò‐wĭng‐uĕn m̄‐ooĭ‐tsoì‐heí‐laī lokₒ.
