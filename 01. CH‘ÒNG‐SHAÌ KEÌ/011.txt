1 SIK‐YĀT t‘in‐hâ kè haú‐yam haî yat‐yeûng, shuetₒ‐wâ yīk haî yat‐yeûng.
2 Tsẑ‐ts‘ūng haí tung‐pin poon‐ts‘in chi shī, tsoî Shî‐nā teî‐fong, uê‐cheūk yat taatₒ p‘īng‐uēn; tsaû haí‐ch‘uè kui‐chuê.
3 Peí‐ts‘ź seung kóng wâ, Ngŏh‐teî iù tsô ti chuen, yûng fóh shiu shīng k‘uĭ. Kóm‐tsaû yaŭ chuen laī t‘aì shēk, yaû yaŭ shēk‐yaū laī t‘aì fooi.
4 Yaû wâ, Ngŏh‐teî iù kìn‐laāp yat‐kòh shēng, k‘āp heí yat‐tsôh t‘aapₒ, t‘aapₒ‐téng iù tò t‘in kòm ko; ch‘uēn‐yeūng ngŏh‐teî kè mēng, mĭn‐chì fan‐saàn tsoî t‘in‐hâ.
5 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām laī t‘aí‐hă yān shóh kìn‐laāp kè shēng kûng t‘aapₒ.
6 Tsaû wâ, Shaì‐yān haî yat tsūk, shuetₒ‐wâ yīk haî yat‐yeûng kè; uē‐kam keì tsô ch‘ut ni‐kîn‐sẑ: haû‐loī k‘uĭ shóh seúng tsô kè, tím nāng kàm‐chí tak ni?
7 Ngŏh‐teî iù kòng‐lām, chíng luên k‘uĭ‐teî kè haú‐yam, lîng k‘uĭ shuetₒ‐wâ m̄‐seung‐t‘ung.
8 Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haí kòh‐ch‘uè, saàn‐hoi chùng‐yān tsoî sz̀ fong teî mîn: kóm‐tsaû pâ shaú, mŏ kìn‐laāp kòh shēng lokₒ.
9 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haí kòh‐ch‘uè chíng‐luên t‘in‐hâ yān kè shuetₒ‐wâ; fan‐saàn k‘uĭ huì sz̀ fong teî mîn: shóh‐ĭ kiù kóh‐kòh shēng tsô Pa‐pīt.
10 ¶ Shím kè tsūk‐p‘ó, līt mīng tsoî tsóh: hūng‐shuí chi haû î nīn, Shím ĭ‐king yat‐paakₒ suì, shang À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ:
11 Shím shang À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ chi haû, king‐līk nğ‐paakₒ nīn, chûng shang tsaí nuĭ.
12 À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ saam‐shāp‐nğ suì, shang Saatₒ‐la:
13 À‐faatₒ‐saatₒ shang Saatₒ‐la chi haû, king‐līk sz̀‐paakₒ‐līng‐saam nīn, chûng shang tsaí nuĭ.
14 Saatₒ‐la saam‐shāp suì shang Hei‐paakₒ:
15 Saatₒ‐la shang Hei‐paakₒ chi haû, king‐līk sz̀‐paakₒ‐līng‐saam nīn, chûng shang tsaí nuĭ.
16 Hei‐paakₒ saam‐shāp‐sz̀ suì shang Faatₒ‐lāk:
17 Hei‐paakₒ shang Faatₒ‐lāk chi haû, king‐līk sz̀‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp nīn, chûng shang tsaí nuĭ.
18 Faatₒ‐lāk saam‐shāp suì shang La‐kaú:
19 Faatₒ‐lāk shang La‐kaú chi haû, king‐līk î‐paakₒ‐līng‐kaú nīn, chûng shang tsaí nuĭ.
20 La‐kaú saam‐shāp‐î suì shang Saatₒ‐lūk:
21 La‐kaú shang Saatₒ‐lūk chi haû, king‐līk î‐paakₒ‐līng‐ts‘at nīn, chûng shang tsaí nuĭ.
22 ¶ Saatₒ‐lūk saam‐shāp suì shang Nā‐hōk:
23 Saatₒ‐lūk shang Nā‐hōk chi haû, king‐līk î‐paakₒ nīn, chûng shang tsaí nuĭ.
24 Nā‐hōk î‐shāp‐kaú suì shang T‘a‐la:
25 Nā‐hōk shang T‘a‐la chi haû, king‐līk yat‐paakₒ‐shāp‐kaú nīn, chûng shang tsaí nuĭ.
26 ¶ T‘a‐la ts‘at‐shāp suì shang À‐paakₒ‐laān, Nā‐hōk, Hap‐laān.
27 ¶ T‘a‐la kè tsūk‐p‘ó, līt mīng tsoî tsóh: T‘a‐la shang À‐paakₒ‐laān, Nā‐hōk, Hap‐laān; Hap‐laān shang Lōh‐tak.
28 K‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an T‘a‐la tsoî shaì chi yāt, Hap‐laān sź tsoî poón‐teî, tsik‐haî Ka‐lāk‐taí kè Ĭ shēng.
29 À‐paakₒ‐laān, Nā‐hōk kokₒ ts‘uì ts‘ai‐tsź: À‐paakₒ‐laān kè ts‘ai, mēng kiù Saatₒ‐loî; Nā‐hōk kè ts‘ai, mēng kiù Māt‐ka, Māt‐ka kûng Ĭ‐sẑ‐ka to haî Hap‐laān kè nuĭ.
30 Saatₒ‐loî m̄‐shīng‐tak yân kè; shóh‐ĭ mŏ tsaí.
31 T‘a‐la taaì k‘uĭ tsaí À‐paakₒ‐laān kûng sik‐foŏ Saatₒ‐loî; k‘āp suen Lōh‐tak, tsik‐haî Hap‐laān kè tsaí, to ch‘ut‐hiu Ka‐lāk‐taí kè Ĭ shēng, seúng poon‐huì Ka‐naām teî; tò‐hiu Hap‐laān tsaû haí kòh‐ch‘uè chuê.
32 T‘a‐la heúng‐shaû î‐paakₒ‐līng‐nğ suì: sź tsoî Hap‐laān.
