1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ haî ngŏh‐chi‐līk‐leûng, ngŏh kìng‐oì neĭ,
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā oô‐waî ngŏh, hó‐ts‘z̆ ko‐shaan, hó‐ts‘z̆ pó‐cheùng; Sheûng‐Taì haî ngŏh p‘oōn‐shēk, ngŏh pit í‐laaî k‘uĭ; yīk haî che ngŏh‐chi‐t‘āng‐p‘aaī, kaù ngŏh‐chi‐kin‐kokₒ, oô ngŏh‐chi‐ko‐chaaî.
3 Ngŏh foo‐kiù tong tsaàn‐tsûng‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: tsaû pit kaù ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐leī ch‘aū‐tīk.
4 Sź‐mōng‐chi‐mŏng‐shīng iŭ‐chuê ngŏh, feí‐luî‐chi‐yān, hó‐ts‘z̆ poh‐lông haakₒ ngŏh.
5 Yam‐kaan‐chi‐shīng‐sokₒ k‘ui‐ts‘uk ngŏh: sź‐mōng‐chi‐lōh‐mŏng heùng‐chuê ngŏh.
6 Ngŏh tsoî waân‐naân‐chi‐chung, k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, foo‐kiù ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì: k‘uĭ tsoî tîn‐luĭ, pit t‘eng ngŏh‐chi‐sheng, ngŏh pán‐k‘aū‐chi‐īn, pit yāp k‘uĭ ĭ.
7 K‘uĭ faatₒ‐heí nô laī; taaî‐kei‐teî tsaû iū‐yuk, shaan yīk chàn‐tûng.
8 K‘uĭ peî ch‘ut‐heì, uē‐in yat‐poon, haú ch‘ut maăng‐fóh: uē t‘aàn shiu‐hūng.
9 K‘uĭ shaí kòh‐t‘in tai‐shuī, ts‘an‐tsẑ kòng‐lām: keukₒ‐hâ hak‐òm.
10 K‘ē‐chuê kei‐lô‐ping fei‐laī: tsè fung tsô yīk laī‐fei‐ts‘eūng.
11 Sz̀‐waī yam‐òm oô‐waî; hak shuí oo‐wān che‐paì.
12 Chué mîn‐ts‘īn‐chi‐kwong‐fai, ch‘ung‐hoi māt‐wān, yaŭ pōk kûng fóh‐t‘aàn kòng lōk.
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî t‘in hāng‐luī, chì‐ko‐chi‐Chué faatₒ‐sheng; yaŭ pōk yaŭ t‘aàn‐fóh kòng‐lōk.
14 Shê‐tsìn kón‐saàn ch‘aū‐tīk; shím‐tîn tseung k‘uĭ tá‐paaî.
15 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yat nô, yat p‘àn taaî‐heì, hoí‐taí în‐ch‘ut, teî‐kei hín‐lô.
16 K‘uĭ tsoî sheûng‐pîn shuī‐shaú t‘oh ngŏh, kaù ngŏh ch‘ut taaî‐shuí‐chi‐chung.
17 Tsang ngŏh‐chi‐tīk‐yān, k‘uĭ līk k‘eūng‐kwòh ngŏh: Chué ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung kaù ngŏh.
18 Tong ngŏh waân‐naân‐chi‐shī, ch‘aū‐tīk heùng‐cheūk ngŏh: waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā foō‐ch‘ī ngŏh.
19 Taaì ngŏh tò kwóng‐footₒ teî‐fong, yan k‘uĭ foon‐heí ngŏh, shóh‐ĭ ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh.
20 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit chiù ngŏh‐chi‐shîn‐î laī‐pò‐yìng ngŏh; chiù‐ngŏh shaú shóh‐tsô ts‘ing‐kitₒ‐chi‐sẑ laī‐sheúng‐ts‘z̀ ngŏh.
21 Yan ngŏh tsun‐shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐tô, meî‐ts‘āng tsô‐kwòh okₒ‐sẑ, laī‐leī‐hoi Sheûng‐Taì.
22 K‘uĭ lūt‐faatₒ sheūng‐tsoî ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn, k‘uĭ lūt‐laî ngŏh tsúng m̄‐tiu‐heì.
23 Ngŏh tsoî k‘uĭ ts‘īn, waī uēn‐ts‘uēn‐chi‐yān, okₒ‐loī ngŏh tsz̄‐keí kán‐shaú pat‐faân.
24 Shóh‐ĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chiù‐ngŏh‐chi‐shîn, i ngŏh tsoî k‘uĭ ngaăn‐ts‘īn shóh‐hāng ts‘ing‐kitₒ‐chi‐sẑ, laī pò‐yìng ngŏh.
25 Līn‐sut‐chi‐yān, neĭ pit tseung līn‐sut toî k‘uĭ; uēn‐ts‘uēn‐chi‐yān, neĭ pit tseung uēn‐ts‘uēn toî k‘uĭ.
26 Ts‘ing‐kitₒ‐chi‐yān, neĭ pit tseung ts‘ing‐kitₒ toî k‘uĭ: pooì‐yīk‐chi‐yān, neĭ pit tseung pooì‐yīk toî k‘uĭ.
27 Yan‐waî neî pit kaù shaû‐naân‐chi‐mān: ngaăn‐ko‐chi‐yān, neĭ pit aatₒ‐tai k‘uĭ.
28 Neĭ pit tím‐cheūk ngŏh tang: ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, lîng ngŏh hak‐òm pìn‐waī kwong‐mīng.
29 Ngŏh í‐laaî neĭ, shaatₒ‐yāp tīk‐chân; í‐laaî Sheûng‐Taì, t‘iù kwòh ko‐ts‘eūng.
30 Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐hāng haî uēn‐ts‘uēn: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐īn haî tsing‐kam: taân‐faān í‐laaî‐k‘uĭ‐ché, k‘uĭ pit pó‐oô.
31 Ch‘uē Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐ngoî, pin‐kòh haî Sheûng‐Taì? ch‘uē‐ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐ngoî, pin‐kòh haî p‘oōn‐shēk?
32 Sheûng‐Taì ts‘z̀ līk‐leûng kwòh‐ngŏh, lîng ngŏh shóh‐hāng waī uēn‐ts‘uēn.
33 Lîng ngŏh keukₒ‐faaì, t‘ūng lūk yat‐yeûng, taaì ngŏh sheŭng‐huì ko‐shaan k‘eĭ‐ch‘uè.
34 Kaaù ngŏh shaú nāng chìn‐taù, táng ngŏh peì‐hoi tak t‘ūng‐kung.
35 Neĭ peí t‘āng‐p‘aaī laī‐kaù‐oô ngŏh: tseung yaû‐shaú foō‐ch‘ī ngŏh, neĭ‐chi‐wan‐yaū, lîng ngŏh waī taaî.
36 Neĭ pit lîng ngŏh keukₒ‐pô foon‐footₒ, neĭ pit lîng ngŏh keukₒ‐kwat k‘eĭ‐wán.
37 Ngŏh chui tīk‐yān, shî‐pit chui‐tó: ngŏh meî mīt tīk, pat‐háng ooī‐t‘aū.
38 Ngŏh tá tīk‐yān, lîng k‘uĭ pat‐nāng heí‐shan: iù titₒ‐tsoî ngŏh‐chi‐keukₒ‐hâ.
39 Neĭ tseung līk‐leûng ts‘z̀ ngŏh, lîng ngŏh nāng chìn‐taù: neĭ lîng kòh‐ti kung‐kik‐ngŏh‐ché, k‘ui kwai‐fūk ngŏh.
40 Neĭ lîng ngŏh tá‐t‘uì ngŏh‐chi‐tīk‐yān; tsiú‐mīt ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘aū‐yān:
41 Ch‘aū‐tīk pit foo‐kiù, taân mŏ yān kaù‐oô: tsik‐sź foo‐kiù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yīk m̄‐yìng‐taapₒ k‘uĭ.
42 Ngŏh tá‐suì k‘uĭ‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆ fung‐ch‘ui‐chi‐ch‘ān: tiu‐heì k‘uĭ‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆ kaai‐sheûng‐chi‐naī.
43 Î‐pong‐yān kûng ngŏh chìn‐taù, neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh; laāp ngŏh‐tsô līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐kwan: meî shik ngŏh‐chi‐mān, yīk fūk‐sẑ ngŏh.
44 K‘uĭ ĭ yat‐t‘eng, tsaû tsun‐i ngŏh: î‐pong‐chi‐yān, to kwai‐fūk ngŏh.
45 Î‐pong‐yān pit‐tîng shui‐paaî, sam‐king taám‐chàn, t‘ō‐tsaú ch‘ut‐shēng.
46 Wĭng‐shang Sheûng‐Taì haî ngŏh p‘oōn‐shēk; uên tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ; ch‘íng‐kaù‐ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng Taì, uên neĭ tak taaî‐tsaàn‐tsûng.
47 Ts‘uēn‐nāng‐chi‐Chué, t‘aì ngŏh shan‐uen, lîng līt‐kwokₒ kwai‐fūk ngŏh.
48 Neĭ kaù ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐leī ch‘aū‐tīk; kuí ngŏh tsoî kung‐ngŏh‐ché‐chi‐sheûng: kaù ngŏh ch‘ut k‘eūng‐okₒ‐yān‐chi‐shaú.
49 Koò‐ts‘ź ngŏh tsoî līt‐kwokₒ tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ, ī‐ch‘é koh‐tsûng neĭ mēng.
50 Neĭ shóh‐fung‐laāp‐chi‐Taaî‐waî wōng; neĭ shi‐yan ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ, kim k‘uĭ tsź‐suen, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn.
