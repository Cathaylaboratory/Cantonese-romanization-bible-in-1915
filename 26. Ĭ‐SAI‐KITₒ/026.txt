1 TONG shāp‐yat‐nīn uēt‐chi‐ch‘oh‐yat‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, yan T‘ui‐lōh kung‐kik Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng wâ, Aai‐yă, tsô‐līt‐mān‐chi‐moōn‐kè, ĭ‐king‐waaî‐laân: k‘uĭ faan‐chuèn kwai‐heùng‐ngŏh: k‘uĭ kíng‐īn peî‐fong‐faì, ngŏh tsaû tak‐ka‐toh‐fung‐shîng:
3 Shóh‐ĭ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘ui‐lōh à, t‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik neĭ, yaû lîng toh‐kwokₒ sheŭng‐laī kung‐kik neĭ, hó‐ts‘z̆ taaî‐hoí lîng‐poh‐lông yŭng‐heí‐laī yat‐yeûng.
4 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tsiú‐mīt T‘ui‐lōh‐kè ts‘eūng‐oōn, yaû waí‐ch‘aakₒ k‘uĭ shēng‐laū: ngŏh yīk yaū k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè kwaatₒ‐ts‘ing naī‐ch‘ān, lìng‐k‘uĭ pìn‐waī kwong‐lô‐kè shēk.
5 K‘uĭ pit‐tsô hoí‐chung p‘aaī‐mŏng‐chi‐shóh: yan ngŏh ĭ‐king‐kóng‐kè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ: K‘uĭ pit‐shīng‐waī î‐pong‐kè leūk‐māt.
6 Shūk‐k‘uĭ‐kè nuĭ, tsoî t‘īn‐kaan‐kè, ooĭ‐peî‐to‐kìm shaatₒ‐lūk; k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
7 Yan‐waî Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh pit‐lîng Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, chue‐wōng‐chi‐wōng ts‘ūng‐pak‐fong‐laī, taaì‐mă‐p‘at, kûng mă‐ch‘e, k‘āp mă‐ping, k‘āp tuî‐nğ, t‘ūng‐maaī toh‐yān, lōk‐laī T‘ui‐lōh‐ch‘uè.
8 K‘uĭ pit‐tseung to‐kìm tsoî‐t‘īn‐kaan shaatₒ‐neĭ‐chùng‐nuĭ: k‘uĭ to ooĭ‐heí‐chìn‐t‘oī, yaû‐chuk‐luĭ, kuí‐heí t‘ŭn‐p‘aaī, laī‐kung‐kik‐neĭ.
9 K‘uĭ to‐ooĭ‐on‐k‘uĭ‐lông‐ch‘uī kung‐kik neĭ ts‘eūng‐oōn, yaû yûng‐chìn‐foó waí‐ch‘aakₒ‐neĭ‐shēng‐laū.
10 Yan k‘uĭ mă‐p‘at‐kīk‐toh, k‘uĭ ch‘ān‐oi ooĭ‐che‐paì‐neĭ: k‘uĭ yāp‐neĭ‐shēng‐moōn, hó‐ts‘z̆ yaū ĭ‐king p‘òh‐waaî‐kè shēng ī‐yāp kóm‐yeûng, kòh‐shī neĭ‐kè ts‘eūng‐oōn, yan mă‐ch‘e kûng fóh‐ch‘e, k‘āp mă‐ch‘e‐kè ts‘ō‐sheng ooĭ‐chàn‐tûng.
11 K‘uĭ ooĭ‐tseung mă‐kè t‘aī, ch‘aaí‐taāp‐saaì neĭ‐kè kaai‐hông: k‘uĭ pit‐k‘aaĭ to‐kìm shaatₒ‐saaì neĭ‐tsź‐mān, neĭ‐nāng‐līk‐kè ch‘uĕ, pit‐k‘ing‐titₒ‐ue‐teî‐hâ.
12 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐kipₒ‐leūk neĭ‐kè ts‘oī‐paāk, yaû ts‘eúng‐tuēt neĭ‐kè fòh‐māt: k‘uĭ‐teî ooĭ‐waí‐ch‘aakₒ neĭ‐kè ts‘eūng‐oōn, yaû tsiú‐mīt neĭ uēt‐ì‐kè uk‐shè: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐tseung neĭ‐kè shēk‐liû, kûng mūk‐liû, k‘āp ch‘ān‐oi, fòng‐lōk chùng‐shuí‐chi‐chung.
13 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng neĭ shi‐koh‐chi‐sheng chí‐sik; neĭ‐k‘ām‐shat‐kè yam, m̄‐tsoì peî‐t‘eng‐mān.
14 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng‐neĭ tsô‐kwong‐t‘uk‐chi‐shēk: tsô‐lông‐mŏng‐chi‐shóh; m̄‐tsoì peî‐kìn‐heí‐kè: yan‐waî ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng kóng‐kwòh‐kè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ à.
15 ¶ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì T‘ui‐lōh kóm wâ; T‘eng neĭ‐k‘ing‐titₒ‐chi‐yam, shaû‐sheung‐kè‐hō‐foo kòh‐shī, tsoî‐neĭ‐chi‐chung yaŭ‐shaatₒ‐lūk kòh‐shī, hoí‐tó heí‐m̄‐ooĭ‐chàn‐tûng me?
16 Kòh‐shī hoí‐sheûng‐chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, yaū wōng‐waî lōk‐hâ, yaû‐ch‘uē‐huì‐laĭ‐fūk, mokₒ‐t‘uetₒ‐saù‐fa‐i‐sheūng: k‘aaĭ‐chìn‐lūt‐laī‐ch‘uen‐cheukₒ; yaû‐ts‘ŏh‐lōk‐teî‐ch‘uè, shī‐hak‐chìn‐lūt, yaû waî‐neĭ‐king‐hoī.
17 K‘uĭ‐teî pit‐waî‐neĭ kuí‐oi‐koh, yaû tuì‐neĭ‐wâ, Neĭ chan‐chìng peî‐tsiú‐mīt‐kè p‘īng‐yāt yaŭ p‘iu‐hoí‐kè yān kui‐chuê, haî yaŭ‐mēng‐kè shēng, tsoî hoí‐sheûng shīng‐waī‐kin‐k‘eūng‐kè, ts‘ź‐shēng kûng‐kui‐mān, p‘īng‐sò lîng‐faān‐kân‐k‘uĭ‐ch‘uè‐kè, peî‐k‘uĭ‐king‐haakₒ!
18 Uē‐kam tsoî‐neĭ‐k‘ing‐paaî‐chi‐yāt; hoí‐tó‐ooĭ‐chìn‐lūt; shāt‐tsoî hoí‐chung‐kè chau‐tó, kìn‐neĭ huì‐hiu, to ooĭ king‐hoī.
19 Yan‐waî Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh lîng‐neĭ tsô‐fong‐faì‐kè shēng, hó‐ts‘z̆ mŏ‐kui‐mān‐kè shēng‐yap kòh‐shī; ngŏh lîng‐sham‐uen‐sheŭng‐tò‐neĭ‐ch‘uè yaû peí‐taaî‐shuí che‐k‘oì neĭ kòh‐shī;
20 Ngŏh tsaû lîng neĭ t‘ūng‐maaī kòh‐ti lōk‐sham‐haang‐kè, lōk‐huì tò‐koó‐shī‐kè tsź‐mān‐ch‘uè, yaû lîng‐neĭ tsoî‐teî‐chung‐chi‐hâ, kui‐chuê tsoî‐tsẑ‐koó fong‐faì‐kè teî‐fong, kûng‐maaī kòh‐ti lōk‐sham‐haang kè, táng neĭ m̄‐tak‐yaŭ kui‐mān; ī‐ch‘é ngŏh pit‐on‐chì wīng‐wā tsoî‐shang‐yān‐chi‐teî à;
21 Ngŏh pit‐lîng‐neĭ tsô‐king‐haakₒ‐kè, neĭ m̄‐tsoì tak‐laū‐ts‘uēn: sui‐īn yaŭ‐yān seúng‐wán‐neĭ, sheûng‐ch‘é tò‐wĭng‐uĕn m̄‐tsoì wán‐tak‐tó, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ lokₒ.
