1 TONG À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ chi‐shī shīng‐tsaû haî kóm, (ni‐kòh À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ, t‘úng koón‐leĭ yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐ts‘at shaáng, ts‘ūng Yàn‐tô chīk‐tò Koó‐shāt:)
2 Tong kòh‐yāt, À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng haí Shue‐shaan kung‐noî ts‘ŏh k‘uĭ kwokₒ‐waî chi‐shī.
3 Tsoî waî taî‐saam‐nīn, waî chùng‐kwan‐cheúng shān‐pūk paaí‐ch‘itₒ īn‐tsīk; Pa‐sai kûng Maĭ‐t‘aaì yaŭ k‘uēn‐shaì‐kè, kokₒ‐shaáng‐kè tseukₒ‐waî, k‘āp kwan‐cheúng, kaai tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn:
4 Ts‘ź‐shī hín‐ch‘ut k‘uĭ wīng‐wā kwokₒ‐chung‐kè foò‐haû, kûng tsẑ‐keí wai‐īm‐kè tsuen‐kwaì hó‐toh‐yāt, tsik‐haî yat‐paakₒ‐paatₒ‐shāp yāt.
5 Ts‘ź‐yāt moŏn‐hiu, wōng waî shóh‐yaŭ tsoî Shue‐shaan‐kung‐kè taaî‐siú tsź‐mān, paaí‐ch‘itₒ īn‐tsīk ts‘at yāt, tsoî wōng‐kung uēn‐chi‐uên;
6 Yaŭ paāk‐shik, ts‘eng‐shik, laām‐shik‐kè pò‐līm, yûng saì‐yaù‐mā‐shīng, tsź‐shik‐shīng, ts‘ūng ngān‐waān póng‐tsoî wān‐shēk ch‘uĕ‐sheûng: ti‐ch‘ōng haî kam‐ngān tsô‐kè, paaí‐tsoî p‘o hūng‐shik, paāk‐shik, wōng‐shik, hak‐shik, wān‐shēk‐kè teî‐sheûng.
7 Yûng kam heì‐mĭng ts‘z̀‐yám, (heì‐mĭng kokₒ‐yaŭ yat shik,) uê‐tsaú yīk hó‐toh, chiù‐i wōng‐kè foò‐haû.
8 Yám haî i tîng‐laî; tsúng mŏ mĭn‐k‘eŭng yān: yan wōng kóm fan‐foò chùng‐ka‐tsoí, chún kokₒ‐yān ts‘uī‐keí‐ì.
9 Wōng‐haû Ngă‐shāt‐teî haí À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng‐kè kung‐noî, yīk waî foŏ‐nuĭ paaí‐ch‘itₒ īn‐tsīk.
10 Taî‐ts‘at yāt, wōng yám tsaú sam‐lōk, tsaû fan‐foò haí wōng mîn‐ts‘īn shî‐laāp‐kè ts‘at‐kòh t‘aaì‐kaàm, Maĭ‐oô‐maân, Pit‐sẑ‐taaî, Hap‐p‘òh‐nā, P‘ik‐taaî, À‐paatₒ‐taaî, Sai‐taāt, Kaapₒ‐ka.
11 Taaì wōng‐haû Ngă‐shāt‐teî, t‘aū taaì wōng‐haû mĭn‐laū, laī‐tò wōng mîn‐ts‘īn seúng peí līt‐mān kûng kwan‐cheúng t‘aí k‘uĭ‐kè meĭ‐maaû: yan‐waî k‘uĭ yūng‐maaû shâm meĭ à.
12 Taân wōng‐haû Ngă‐shāt‐teî m̄‐háng i t‘aaì‐kaàm shóh‐ch‘uēn‐kè wōng‐mîng: koò‐ts‘ź wōng shâm‐făn‐nô, sam‐chung fóh‐heí.
13 Ue‐shî wōng mân shik shī‐mô‐kè chì‐waî‐yān, (yan‐waî wōng‐kè sheūng‐kw‘ai, heùng chi‐laî mīng‐faatₒ‐kè yān haî kóm‐yeûng tsô:
14 Ts‘z̀‐ue wōng‐kè sheūng kìn wōng mîn, tsoî kwokₒ‐chung ts‘ŏh‐ko‐waî‐kè, yaŭ Pa‐sai Maĭ‐t‘aaì‐kè ts‘at kwan‐cheúng, tsik‐haî Kaapₒ‐shî‐nā, Shî‐taāt, Aapₒ‐mă‐t‘aaì, Taaî‐shat, Maĭ‐līk, Mă‐sai‐nā, Maĭ‐mŏ‐kon:)
15 Wōng mân wâ, Wōng‐haû Ngă‐shāt‐teî m̄‐tsun t‘aaì‐kaàm shóh‐ch‘uēn‐kè wōng‐mîng, ngŏh‐teî chiù‐kwokₒ‐faatₒ iù tím‐yeûng ch‘ué‐chì k‘uĭ ni?
16 Maĭ‐mŏ‐kon tsoî wōng k‘āp chùng‐kwan‐cheúng mîn‐ts‘īn taapₒ wâ, Wōng‐haû Ngă‐shāt‐teî shóh‐tsô‐kè, m̄‐chí haî‐tak‐tsuî wōng, to tak‐tsuî shūk À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng, tsoî kokₒ‐shaáng shóh‐yaŭ‐kwan‐cheúng kûng tsź‐mān.
17 Yan‐waî wōng‐haû shóh‐tsô ni‐kîn‐sẑ, pit ch‘uēn‐wān tò chùng‐foŏ‐yān, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî t‘aí‐hing cheûng‐foo, yan‐waî t‘eng‐mān ch‘uēn‐kóng‐wâ, À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng, chiû wōng‐haû Ngă‐shāt‐teî laī‐tò wōng mîn‐ts‘īn, taân k‘uĭ m̄‐laī.
18 Ī‐ch‘é kam‐yāt Pa‐sai Maĭ‐t‘aaì‐kè chùng‐kung‐chué, mān‐tak wōng‐haû shóh‐tsô‐kè, pit heùng wōng chùng‐kwan‐cheúng laī kóm‐yeûng kóng. Kóm‐tsaû kàng‐ka miŭ‐shî nau‐nô shang‐heí‐laī lokₒ.
19 Wōng yeūk ĭ‐waī‐hó, tsaû iù ch‘ut sheûng‐uê, sé‐tsoî Pa‐sai Maĭ‐t‘aaì‐kè lūt‐faatₒ‐chung, chì pat‐nāng kang‐koí, tsúng m̄‐chún Ngă‐shāt‐teî tsoì‐tò wōng mîn‐ts‘īn yaû tseung k‘uĭ‐kè haû‐waî, ts‘z̀‐kwòh kàng‐hó‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐kè.
20 Shóh‐ch‘ut sheûng‐uê, ch‘uēn p‘ìn t‘ung‐kwokₒ‐chi‐shī, (yan‐ts‘ź‐kwokₒ haî kwóng‐taaî,) shóh‐yaŭ‐kè foŏ‐yān, m̄‐lûn cheûng‐foo kwaì‐tsîn, to pit tsuen‐kìng k‘uĭ.
21 Wōng kûng chùng‐kwan‐cheúng kaai‐foon‐heí shóh‐kóng‐kè; wōng tsaû chiù‐i Maĭ‐mŏ‐kon‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ laī‐tsô:
22 Yaû sé‐sùn t‘ung‐chi wōng‐kè kokₒ‐shaáng, chiù‐kokₒ‐shaáng‐kè mān‐tsẑ, yīk t‘ung‐chi kokₒ‐mān, chiù‐kokₒ‐mān‐kè t‘ó‐yam, lîng kokₒ‐yān tsoî tsẑ‐keí ka‐chung tsô chué, yīk lîng ts‘ź‐sheûng‐uê, chiù‐kokₒ‐mān‐kè t‘ó‐yam laī‐ch‘uēn‐wān à.
