1 NGŎH uê waân‐naân‐chi‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yan‐hōh yán‐maaī, leī uĕn k‘eĭ‐laāp?
2 Okₒ‐yān kiu‐waāng, pik‐hoî p‘ān‐yān: uên k‘uĭ haâm‐lōk tsẑ‐keí shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐chi‐kaì‐chung.
3 Okₒ‐yān suî‐hiu kòh‐sam tsaû king‐kw‘a, t‘aam‐ts‘oī laī leī‐hoi, miŭ‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
4 Okₒ‐yān kiu‐ngô, m̄‐k‘aū Sheûng‐Taì: k‘uĭ sam ĭ‐waī mŏ Sheûng‐Taì.
5 K‘uĭ‐chi‐hāng‐waī, shī‐sheūng shûn‐leî; neĭ‐chi‐shám‐p‘oòn, ko tsoî k‘uĭ‐sheûng, pat‐nāng mông‐kìn: chì‐ue ch‘aū‐tīk, k‘uĭ tsaû miŭ‐shî.
6 K‘uĭ sam ĭ‐waī tsẑ‐keí wán‐chân: shaì‐shaì pat‐uê tsoi‐naân.
7 K‘uĭ moŏn‐haú to haî chaù‐chòh hei‐p‘ìn ts‘aān‐hoî: leî‐sheûng ts‘uēn‐shî lōng‐tūk ká‐ngaî.
8 K‘uĭ tsoî heung‐kaan ni‐maaī: tsîng‐chung tseung mō‐koo‐ché laī‐shaatₒ: peí ngaăn chong kw‘àn‐foó‐chi‐yān.
9 Ni‐maaī òm‐teî, hó‐ts‘z̆ sz‐tsź tsoî ngaām yat‐yeûng: iù chukₒ‐wōk kw‘àn‐foó‐chi‐yān: yăn kw‘àn‐foó‐chi‐yān yāp mŏng, tsaû laī chukₒ‐wōk.
10 Kw‘àn‐foó‐chi‐yān, suì‐laân titₒ‐tó, peî‐k‘eūng‐chaaú sheung‐hoî.
11 K‘uĭ sam ĭ‐waī Sheûng‐Taì mōng‐keì k‘uĭ: che‐chuê kòh‐mîn; wĭng m̄‐kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ.
12 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hing‐heí; kuí neĭ‐chi‐shaú: m̄‐mōng‐keì kw‘àn‐foó‐chi‐yān.
13 Okₒ‐yān yan‐hōh miŭ‐shî Sheûng‐Taì? k‘uĭ sam ĭ‐waī neĭ pit m̄‐chaakₒ‐fāt.
14 K‘eī‐shāt ĭ‐king kaàm‐ch‘aatₒ; k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng ts‘aān‐hoî lōng‐tūk, neĭ pit t‘aí‐kìn, shan‐shaú laī‐pò‐yìng k‘uĭ: k‘ūng‐foó‐yān pit‐í‐laaî neĭ; koo‐tūk‐yān neĭ pit‐foō‐chôh.
15 Uên neĭ tá‐chitₒ okₒ‐yān shaú‐peì: chui‐kaù tsuî‐yān okₒ‐sẑ, táng k‘uĭ mŏ waī‐laû.
16 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wĭng‐uĕn waī wōng: līt‐pong‐chi‐yān, pit tsoî ts‘ź‐ch‘uè shaû mīt.
17 Him‐pei‐chi‐yān shóh‐k‘aū, neĭ chak‐ĭ laī‐t‘eng: neĭ pit‐kin‐tîng k‘uĭ sam:
18 Neĭ pit waî koo‐tsź kûng shaû wat chi yān shan‐uen, lîng shaì‐yān m̄‐kóm tsoì húng‐haakₒ k‘uĭ.
