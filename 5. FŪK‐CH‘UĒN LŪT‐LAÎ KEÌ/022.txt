1 NEĬ yeūk kìn neĭ hing‐taî‐kè ngaū yeūng maī‐shat lô‐t‘ō, pat‐hóh chà tsokₒ m̄‐kìn, pit iù taaì‐faan‐huì hing‐taî‐ch‘uè.
2 Neĭ hing‐taî yeūk leī neĭ uĕn, waāk neĭ m̄‐shik k‘uĭ, tsaû iù laai‐tò tsẑ‐keí ka‐chung, t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ haí‐ch‘uè, táng‐tò neĭ hing‐taî laī wán‐ts‘ām, chì peí‐faan k‘uĭ.
3 Neĭ uê‐kìn k‘uĭ‐kè luī; waāk uê‐kìn k‘uĭ‐kè i‐fūk; k‘āp k‘uĭ faān shat‐laû‐kè māt‐kîn, to pit iù kóm‐yeûng hāng; pat‐hóh chà tsokₒ m̄‐kìn.
4 ¶ Yeūk kìn neĭ hing‐taî‐kè ngaū luī titₒ‐tsoî lô‐pin, pat‐hóh chà tsokₒ m̄‐kìn, pit iù pong‐chôh k‘uĭ foō‐heí.
5 ¶ Nuĭ‐yān m̄‐hó cheukₒ naām‐yān i‐fūk, naām‐yān m̄‐hó cheukₒ nuĭ‐yān i‐fūk: faān kóm‐yeûng hāng‐kè, haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐kè Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐tsang‐oò à.
6 ¶ Neĭ tsoî lô‐sheûng, yeūk uê‐kìn tseukₒ‐taù tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn, waāk tsoî shué‐sheûng, waāk tsoî teî‐sheûng, luĭ‐pîn yaŭ tseukₒ‐tsaí, waāk tseukₒ‐taân, tseukₒ‐ná pūk tsoî tseukₒ‐tsaí‐sheûng, waāk tsoî tseukₒ‐taân‐sheûng, neĭ pat‐hóh tseung ná kûng tsaí to chukₒ‐huì:
7 Ying‐tong fòng ná fei‐huì, tūk‐haî ning k‘uĭ tsaí, kóm‐tsaû hóh‐ĭ tak fuk, ka‐tsang yāt‐tsź.
8 ¶ Neĭ yeūk heí san uk, iù tsoî uk‐pooì sz̀‐waī on laān‐kon, húng‐p‘à yaŭ yān ts‘ūng uk‐sheûng titₒ‐lōk‐laī, chì‐lîng laū‐huet‐kè tsuî, kwai‐ue neĭ uk‐k‘eí.
9 Neĭ pat‐hóh tseung leŭng‐yeûng‐kè chúng, saatₒ‐tsoî neĭ p‘ō‐t‘aī‐uēn, húng‐p‘à neĭ saatₒ‐lōk‐kè chúng; shóh‐kitₒ‐kè kwóh‐tsź, k‘āp p‘ō‐t‘aī‐shuê shóh‐kitₒ‐kè kwóh‐tsź, to pat‐hóh yûng.
10 ¶ Neĭ pat‐hóh yûng ngaū‐luī t‘ūng‐maaī kaang‐t‘īn.
11 Pat‐hóh cheukₒ kaàn‐tsaāp‐kè i‐fūk, tsik‐haî tseung yeūng‐mō kûng saì‐mā t‘ūng‐chik‐kè.
12 ¶ Neĭ shóh‐cheukₒ tsoî‐shan‐kè shaam, iù tsoî shaam‐pin sz̀‐waī chíng suî.
13 ¶ Yeūk yaŭ yān ts‘uì ts‘ai, kûng k‘uĭ t‘ūng‐fōng chi‐haû, tsaû tsang‐oò k‘uĭ,
14 Sau‐yūk k‘uĭ lîng k‘uĭ yaŭ m̄‐hó‐kè shing‐mīng, tsik‐haî wâ Ngŏh ts‘uì ni‐kòh‐nuĭ‐yān, kûng k‘uĭ t‘ūng‐fōng, kìn k‘uĭ m̄‐ching‐kitₒ:
15 Nuĭ‐yān‐kè foô‐mŏ, tsaû iù tseung kòh‐nuĭ shaú‐ching‐kè p‘āng‐kuì, ning‐huì poón‐shēng‐kè moōn, tò cheúng‐lŏ‐ch‘uè:
16 Nuĭ‐yān‐kè foô‐ts‘an, iù tuì cheúng‐lŏ wâ, Ngŏh tseung ngŏh‐kè nuĭ, peí ni‐kòh‐yān waī‐ts‘ai, taân k‘uĭ tsang‐oò k‘uĭ;
17 Sau‐yūk k‘uĭ, wâ, Ngŏh kìn kòh‐nuĭ‐yān m̄‐ching‐kitₒ: taân ngŏh nuĭ shaú‐ching‐kè p‘āng‐kuì haí ni‐ch‘uè. Ue‐shî foô‐mŏ tsaû tseung pò paaí‐tsoî poón‐shēng cheúng‐lŏ mîn‐ts‘īn.
18 Poón‐shēng‐kè cheúng‐lŏ, iù chukₒ‐chuê kòh‐yān, laī chaakₒ‐fāt k‘uĭ;
19 Yaû fāt k‘uĭ ch‘ut ngān yat‐paakₒ shī‐haakₒ, peí‐kwòh nuĭ‐yān‐kè foô‐ts‘an nuĭ‐yān yīng‐īn tsô k‘uĭ ts‘ai: yat‐shang kòm‐noî, pat‐hóh ch‘ut k‘uĭ; yan‐waî k‘uĭ tseung okₒ mīng, ka tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt ching‐kitₒ t‘ūng‐nuĭ‐kè shan‐sheûng.
20 Yeūk kòh‐ti sẑ haî chan‐shāt, nuĭ‐yān mŏ shaú‐ching‐kè p‘āng‐kuì, tsaû iù chukₒ kòh‐nuĭ‐yān ch‘ut‐huì, taaì‐tò k‘uĭ foô‐mŏ uk‐k‘eí moōn‐haú, poón‐shēng‐kè yān, iù yûng shēk tseung k‘uĭ tèng‐sź:
21 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ ts‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐chung, tsoî foô‐ka hāng kaan‐yām‐kè sẑ: kóm‐yeûng, tsaû tsoî neĭ‐teî chi‐chung, tseung ts‘ź okₒ ch‘uē‐mīt.
22 ¶ Yān yeūk kûng yaŭ‐cheûng‐foo‐kè foŏ‐yān t‘ūng‐fōng, tsaû iù tseung kòh‐foŏ‐yān, k‘āp kòh‐kaan foo, yat‐ts‘aī chî‐sź, kóm‐yeûng: tsoî neĭ‐teî chi‐chung, tseung ts‘ź‐okₒ ch‘uē‐mīt.
23 ¶ Yeūk yaŭ t‘ūng‐nuĭ shaû cheûng‐foo p‘ìng‐tîng, yaŭ yān tsoî shēng‐luĭ uê‐cheūk k‘uĭ, kûng k‘uĭ hāng‐yām;
24 Neĭ‐teî tsaû iù chukₒ k‘uĭ leŭng‐yān, taaì‐ch‘ut shēng‐moōn, yûng shēk tèng‐sź k‘uĭ‐teî; yan‐waî kòh‐t‘ūng‐nuĭ, sui‐īn tsoî shēng‐luĭ, taân mŏ kiù‐haàm; yīk yan kòh‐naām‐yān tìm‐yūk lūn‐shè‐kè ts‘ai à: kóm‐yeûng, tsoî neĭ‐teî chi‐chung, tseung ts‘ź‐okₒ ch‘uē‐mīt.
25 ¶ Yeūk yaŭ yān tsoî t‘īn yĕ, uê‐kìn ts‘āng shaû p‘ìng‐tîng‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ, mĭn‐k‘eŭng kûng k‘uĭ hāng‐yām: tūk‐shî iù tseung kòh‐kûng‐t‘ūng‐nuĭ‐hāng‐yām‐kè naām‐yān chî‐sź:
26 Tsaû m̄‐shaí tîng kòh‐t‘ūng‐nuĭ‐kè tsuî; yan k‘uĭ mŏ ying‐sź‐kè tsuî à: ni‐ti sẑ haî hó‐ts‘z̆ yān heí‐shan huì‐shaatₒ‐sź lūn‐shè yat‐yeûng:
27 Yan‐waî kòh‐yān, tsoî t‘īn‐yĕ, uê‐kìn ts‘āng shaû p‘ìng‐tîng‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ, t‘ūng‐nuĭ sui‐īn kiù‐haàm, yīk mŏ yān kaù k‘uĭ.
28 Yeūk yaŭ yān uê‐kìn meî‐ts‘āng shaû‐p‘ìng‐tîng‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ, mĭn‐k‘eŭng k‘uĭ t‘ūng‐hāng‐yām peî‐yān‐t‘aí‐kìn;
29 Kòh‐hāng‐yām‐kè naām‐yān tsaû iù ch‘ut ngān nğ‐shāp shī‐haakₒ, peí‐kwòh t‘ūng‐nuĭ‐kè foô‐ts‘an; yan k‘uĭ ĭ‐king tìm‐yūk kòh‐t‘ūng‐nuĭ, iù ts‘uì k‘uĭ waī ts‘ai, yat‐shang kòm‐noî, pat‐hóh ch‘ut k‘uĭ.
30 Yān m̄‐hó ts‘uì foô‐ts‘an‐kè ts‘ai, m̄‐hó k‘itₒ‐hoi foô‐ts‘an‐kè p‘eĭ.
