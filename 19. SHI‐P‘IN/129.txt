1 UĒ‐KAM Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tong wâ, Ngŏh tsẑ‐yaù‐shī, luĭ‐ts‘z̀ peî‐yān kaaú‐iŭ ngŏh:
2 Ngŏh tsẑ‐yaù‐shī, luĭ‐ts‘z̀ peî‐yān‐kaaú‐iŭ‐ngŏh: taân‐haî m̄‐yēng‐tak ngŏh.
3 Tīk‐yān sheung ngŏh pooì‐tsekₒ: hó‐ts‘z̆ laī‐teî hān‐lô kīk‐ch‘eūng.
4 Waī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kung‐î: tseung okₒ‐yān‐chi‐shīng‐sokₒ kotₒ‐t‘uĕn.
5 Taân‐faān tsang‐oò Sun‐mān‐ché, shî‐pit shaû yūk paaî‐tsaú.
6 K‘uĭ‐teî hó‐ts‘z̆ uk‐sheûng‐chi‐ts‘ó, meî‐ts‘āng pāt‐leī, ĭ‐king foo‐kó:
7 Mŏ yān kotₒ k‘uĭ laī‐moŏn‐shaú; mŏ‐yān chaatₒ k‘uĭ laī‐moŏn‐waaī.
8 Kwòh‐lô‐chi‐yān, yīk‐m̄‐wâ, Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chuk‐fuk neĭ: ngŏh t‘okₒ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐mēng, t‘aì neĭ k‘aū‐fuk.
