1 À‐SAATₒ‐KÈ tsaí Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng, k‘uĭ tsẑ k‘eūng kung‐kik Ĭ‐shik‐līt.
2 Tsaû ch‘itₒ‐laāp kwan‐ping tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐pó‐cheùng‐kè shēng, yaû paaí‐ch‘itₒ shò‐yīng tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐teî, k‘āp tsoî k‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an À‐saatₒ shóh‐ts‘uí Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐kè kokₒ‐shēng.
3 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘ūng‐maaī Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ, yan‐waî k‘uĭ hāng tsó‐foô Taaî‐waî ch‘oh‐hāng‐kè tô, m̄‐k‘aū Pa‐līk;
4 Tsaû k‘aū k‘uĭ‐foô‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, tsun‐hāng k‘uĭ kaaì‐mîng, m̄‐i Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè hāng‐waī.
5 Koò‐ts‘ź Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kin‐koò kwokₒ tsoî k‘uĭ shaú; Yaū‐t‘aaì chùng‐yān sùng‐laĭ‐māt‐kwòh Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ; kóm‐tsaû Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ taaî‐yaŭ tsuen‐wīng fòh‐ts‘oī à.
6 K‘uĭ kuí‐sam tsun‐hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tô: ī‐ch‘é tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐ch‘uē huì yau‐t‘aān, kûng À‐sha‐laāt.
7 Tong k‘uĭ ts‘ŏh‐waî taî‐saam nīn, tsaû tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ kwan‐cheúng Pîn‐hoí‐yik, Oh‐pa‐taí, Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à, Nā‐taân‐īp, Maĭ‐koi‐ngă, huì‐kaaù‐fàn Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐shēng.
8 Yīk t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ‐teî, tá‐faatₒ Leî‐meî‐yān, tsik‐haî Shî‐mâ‐ngă, Neī‐taaî‐neī, Sai‐pa‐teî, À‐saatₒ‐haak, Shî‐maĭ‐la‐moōt, Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan, À‐toh‐neī‐ngă, Toh‐peì‐à, Tuēt‐à‐toh‐neī‐ngă, kaai‐haî Leî‐meî‐yān; yaû t‘ūng k‘uĭ‐teî tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă, Yeukₒ‐laān.
9 Ni‐ti chùng‐yān tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì kaaù‐fàn, tsaû taaì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lūt‐faatₒ‐kè shue, ts‘ūn‐hāng Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐shēng, tsaû kaaù‐fàn yān‐mān.
10 P‘à Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè húng‐kuî, lām‐tò Yaū‐t‘aaì sz̀‐waī kokₒ‐teî‐kè kwokₒ, chì‐m̄‐kóm kung‐tá Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ.
11 Yaŭ Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān sùng laĭ‐māt, uĕ‐k‘āp shuì‐kùng ngān kwòh Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ; À‐la‐paakₒ‐yān taaì kw‘ān‐ch‘uk, tsik‐haî mīn‐yeūng‐kung ts‘at‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ‐chekₒ, shaan‐yeūng‐kung ts‘at‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ‐chekₒ.
12 Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ shīng‐waī k‘eūng‐taaî tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì; kìn‐chuk kin‐koò wōng‐kung, k‘āp foó‐foò shēng.
13 K‘uĭ yaŭ hó‐toh kung‐ch‘īng, tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐shēng: yaû‐yaŭ chìn‐sẑ, tsik‐haî taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ, kaai‐tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng.
14 Chìn‐sẑ‐kè shò‐mūk, chiù‐i k‘uĭ foô‐ka‐kè shò līt‐mīng tsoî‐haû: shūk Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè yaŭ ts‘in‐foo‐cheúng; Aapₒ‐nā tsô shaú‐lĭng, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ yaŭ taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ saam‐shāp‐maân yān.
15 K‘eī‐ts‘z̀ Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ yaŭ taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ î‐shāp‐paatₒ‐maân yān.
16 Yaû k‘eī‐ts‘z̀ Shik‐leĭ‐kè tsaí À‐mă‐ts‘ē, k‘uĭ kom‐sam kwai‐heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ yaŭ taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ î‐shāp‐maân yān.
17 Shūk Pîn‐ngă‐măn yaŭ taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ Ĭ‐leî‐à‐taaî; t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ yaŭ cha‐kung‐tsìn k‘āp cha‐tóng‐p‘aaī‐kè î‐shāp‐maân yān.
18 K‘eī‐ts‘z̀ Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐paatₒ, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ yaŭ shāp‐paatₒ‐maân, haî uê‐peî kaau‐chìn‐kè yān.
19 Ts‘ź kaai‐haî shî‐haû ue wōng, lîng‐tsẑ yaŭ wōng shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐laāp hon‐shaú sz̀‐waī Yaū‐t‘aaì kokₒ‐shēng‐kè à.
