1 HÂ‐SHOKₒ wōng Yē‐pan, ĭ‐king t‘eng‐mān ts‘ź‐sẑ, tsaû tá‐faatₒ yān huì kìn Mă‐tûn wōng Yeukₒ‐paatₒ, kûng Shan‐lūn‐kè wōng, Aapₒ‐shaatₒ‐kè wōng,
2 K‘āp chuê‐tsoî pak‐pîn‐kè shaan‐teî, chuê‐tsoî Kei‐neī‐līt naām‐pîn‐kè, kûng p‘īng‐uēn, chuê‐tsoî Toh‐ĭ kíng‐kaaì sai‐pîn‐kè chùng‐wōng,
3 Yaû huì kìn tsoî tung‐pîn k‘āp sai‐pîn‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān, kûng À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān, Haak‐yān, Peí‐leĭ‐saí‐yān, chuê‐tsoî shaan‐teî‐kè Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐yān, chuê‐tsoî Māt‐sẑ‐pa‐teî Haak‐moōn‐shaan‐keukₒ‐kè Hei‐meî‐yān.
4 Kûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè kwan‐ping yat‐ts‘aī ch‘ut‐laī, yaŭ hó‐toh‐yān, hó‐ts‘z̆ hoí‐pin‐kè sha kòm‐toh, mă kûng mă‐ch‘e yīk hó‐toh.
5 Ni‐ti chùng‐wōng to tsuî‐maaī laī‐tò Maĭ‐lūn shuí‐pin, līn‐maaī on‐yīng, seúng kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tá‐cheùng.
6 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Neĭ m̄‐hó yan k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû waì‐kuî: mīng‐yāt taaî‐yeukₒ ni‐chân‐shī‐haû, ngŏh pit tseung k‘uĭ‐teî kaau‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, shaatₒ‐saaì k‘uĭ‐teî: neĭ pit iù chaám‐t‘uĕn k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè mă‐keukₒ‐kan, yûng fóh shiu k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè mă‐ch‘e.
7 Ue‐shî Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kûng chùng‐kwan‐ping huì, tsoî Maĭ‐lūn shuí‐pin; fat‐īn kung‐tá kóh‐ti‐ch‘aū‐tīk.
8 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung k‘uĭ‐teî kaau‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shaú, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kung‐tá, chui‐tò taaî Sai‐tûn, Māt‐leî‐fat‐mă‐yam, Māt‐sẑ‐pa; heùng tung‐kè shaan‐kuk shaatₒ‐saaì, yat‐kòh to mŏ laū‐ts‘uēn.
9 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè laī‐hāng; chaám‐t‘uĕn k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè mă‐keukₒ‐kan, yûng fóh shiu k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè mă‐ch‘e.
10 ¶ Kóh‐shī Yeukₒ‐shue‐à faan laī, chìm‐tuēt Hâ‐shokₒ, yûng to shaatₒ‐sź Hâ‐shokₒ wōng: yan‐waî Hâ‐shokₒ tsô kóh‐ch‘uè chùng‐kwokₒ‐kè t‘aū‐mūk.
11 Faān chuê‐tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè‐kè yān, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à to yûng to laī‐shaatₒ mīt‐saaì: k‘uĭ‐teî faān‐yaŭ‐heì‐kè to mŏ laū‐ts‘uēn: yaû yûng fóh‐shiu Hâ‐shokₒ‐shēng.
12 Ni‐ti chùng‐wōng‐kè shēng k‘āp wōng, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à to chìm‐tuēt‐hiu, tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pūk‐yān Moh‐sai shóh‐fan‐foò, yûng to tseung k‘uĭ‐teî shaatₒ‐mīt.
13 Tūk‐haî heí‐tsoî shaan‐kong‐sheûng‐kè shēng, ch‘uē Hâ‐shokₒ chi‐ngoî, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān m̄‐fān‐shiu; tūk‐haî Hâ‐shokₒ peî Yeukₒ‐shue‐à fān‐shiu.
14 Ts‘ź chùng‐shēng‐kè ts‘oī‐māt kûng ch‘uk‐shang, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān to kwai tsẑ‐keí; waī‐haî ti‐yān tsaû yûng to shaatₒ‐saaì, faān‐yaŭ‐heì‐kè yat‐kòh to mŏ‐laū‐ts‘uēn.
15 Ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò pūk‐yān Moh‐sai‐kè, Moh‐sai kóm‐yeûng fan‐foò Yeukₒ‐shue‐à; Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsun‐ts‘ūng laī tsô, faān Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè sẑ, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à mŏ yat‐yeûng m̄‐tsun‐ts‘ūng laī‐tsô.
16 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tuēt‐hiu kòh‐taatₒ ts‘uēn‐teî, tsik‐haî shaan‐teî, naām‐pîn ts‘uēn‐teî, Hóh‐shaan ts‘uēn‐teî, p‘īng‐uēn, kw‘òng‐yĕ, k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐shaan‐teî kûng p‘īng‐uēn;
17 Ts‘ūng sheŭng‐tò Sai‐ĭ seung‐kân‐kè Hâ‐lāk‐shaan, chì‐tò Leî‐pa‐nuên‐shaan‐kuk‐kè Pa‐līk‐ka‐tak, tsik‐haî tsoî Haak‐moōn‐shaan‐hâ‐kè: yaû tseung kóh‐ti wōng shaatₒ‐sź.
18 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kûng kokₒ‐wōng tá‐cheùng hó‐noî.
19 Ch‘uē‐hiu chuê‐tsoî Kei‐p‘ìn‐kè Hei‐meî‐yān chi‐ngoî, mŏ yat‐kòh shēng kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān wōh‐hó: k‘ui‐haî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tá‐cheùng‐tak‐tó.
20 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chíng‐ngaâng k‘uĭ‐kòh‐sam, shaí k‘uĭ‐teî heùng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tuì‐tīk tá‐cheùng, mīt‐saaì k‘uĭ‐teî, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐shaû līn‐măn, to peî‐tsiú‐mīt, chiù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai kè.
21 ¶ Kòh‐shī, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à laī tò, tseung À‐naāp‐yān tsik‐haî tsoî shaan‐teî, k‘āp tsoî Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn, Taí‐pit, À‐nā‐paakₒ, Yaū‐taaî ts‘uēn shaan, Ĭ‐shik‐līt ts‘uēn‐shaan‐kè: Yeukₒ‐shue‐à to tsûn‐teî tsiú‐mīt, yaû waí‐mīt k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shēng.
22 À‐naāp‐yān mŏ‐yat‐kòh laū‐ts‘uēn, tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè teî‐fong: tūk‐haî Ka‐saatₒ, Ka‐tāk, À‐shāt‐tāk, chûng‐yaŭ‐ti laū‐ts‘uēn‐kè.
23 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à kóm‐yeûng tuēt kóh‐ch‘uè ts‘uēn‐teî, haî‐chiù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faān‐shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè; Yeukₒ‐shue‐à k‘aaĭ‐laī peí‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsô ch‘aán‐īp, haî i kokₒ‐chi‐p‘aaì, kokₒ‐tsung‐tsūk. Ue‐shî kòh‐kwokₒ tsaû on‐tsîng mŏ‐tá‐cheùng lokₒ.
