1 YAŪ‐T‘AAÌ wōng Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ taî‐shāp‐paatₒ nīn, À‐hap‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐laān, tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leì‐à tang‐waì tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng shāp‐ì nīn.
2 K‘uĭ hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn waī‐okₒ‐kè; taân m̄‐ts‘z̆ foô‐mŏ kòm‐kwaan‐haî: yan‐waî ch‘uē‐huì k‘uĭ foô shóh‐tsô Pa‐līk‐kè tseûng.
3 Taân hōk‐faan Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè okₒ, tsik‐haî lîng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān faân‐tsuî‐kè.
4 ¶ Moh‐aapₒ wōng Maĭ‐sha haî mūk‐yeūng chué‐tsoí, mooĭ nīn tseung ko‐yeūng shāp‐maân, meî‐tsín‐mō‐kè kung‐mīn‐yeūng shāp‐maân, naāp‐kùng‐kwòh Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng.
5 À‐hap sź haû, Moh‐aapₒ wōng poôn‐yīk Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng.
6 Tong shī Yeukₒ‐laān wōng ch‘ut Saatₒ‐mă‐leì‐à, tím‐shó Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
7 Yaû tá‐faatₒ yān huì Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ‐ch‘uè wâ, Moh‐aapₒ wōng poôn‐yīk ngŏh: neĭ háng t‘ūng ngŏh huì kung‐tá Moh‐aapₒ m̄‐ni? K‘uĭ wâ, Ngŏh háng huì: neĭ‐ngŏh mŏ‐fan peí‐ts‘ź, ngŏh mān tsik neĭ mān, ngŏh mă tsik neĭ mă.
8 Wōng wâ, Ngŏh‐teî tong‐yaū pin‐t‘iū‐lô sheŭng‐huì ni? Taapₒ wâ, Yaū Ĭ‐tung kw‘òng‐yĕ kóh‐t‘iū‐lô lokₒ.
9 Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng, k‘āp Ĭ‐tung wōng: yat‐ts‘aī t‘ūng‐huì, wân‐hāng ts‘at yāt lô‐ch‘īng: kwan‐luĭ k‘āp ts‘uī‐taaì‐kè ch‘uk‐shang mŏ shuí yám.
10 Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng wâ, Oi‐tsoi! Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chiû‐tsaāp ni‐saam‐kòh wōng, seúng kaau‐kwòh Moh‐aapₒ‐yān shaú lokₒ!
11 Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ wâ, Ni‐ch‘uè heí mŏ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sin‐chi, táng ngŏh tsīk k‘uĭ mân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā me? Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng‐kè shān‐chung yaŭ yat‐kòh wâ, Ni‐ch‘uè yaŭ Sha‐faatₒ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐sha, tsik‐haî tó shuí Ĭ‐leî‐à saí shaú kóh‐kòh.
12 Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ wâ, K‘uĭ pit fûng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîng lokₒ. Ue‐shî Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng, t‘ūng Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ k‘āp Ĭ‐tung wōng lōk‐huì kìn k‘uĭ.
13 Ĭ‐leî‐sha tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng wâ, Ngŏh t‘ūng neĭ yaŭ‐mat‐kwaan‐shipₒ? huì mân neĭ foô‐kè sin‐chi, neĭ mŏ‐kè sin‐chi a. Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng tuì k‘uĭ wâ, M̄‐haî à: ngŏh chi‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ĭ‐king chiû‐tsaāp ngŏh‐teî saam‐kòh wōng laī, seúng kaau‐kwòh Moh‐aapₒ‐yān‐kè shaú.
14 Ĭ‐leî‐sha wâ, Ngŏh chí‐shang‐oōt maân‐kwan‐kè Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, k‘eĭ‐tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn faatₒ‐shaî wâ, Ngŏh yeūk m̄‐t‘aí Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐sha‐faatₒ‐kè ts‘īng‐mîn, pit m̄‐leĭ neĭ, m̄‐koò neĭ.
15 Taân uē‐kam iù taaì yat‐kòh ngōk‐sz laī ngŏh‐ch‘uè. Ngōk‐sz t‘aān‐k‘ām‐chi‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè shaú, kòng‐lām Ĭ‐leî‐sha, k‘uĭ tsaû wâ,
16 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Iù kwāt kau‐kooì moŏn‐saaì ts‘ź kuk‐chung.
17 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Neĭ‐teî sui m̄‐kìn‐fung, m̄‐kìn‐uĕ; taân shuí pit‐īn moŏn ts‘ź kuk, táng neĭ‐teî k‘āp shang‐ch‘uk to yaŭ‐tak‐yám.
18 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā suèn ni‐ti haî siú‐sẑ: k‘uĭ yīk tseung Moh‐aapₒ‐yān kaau‐kwòh neĭ‐teî shaú.
19 Kokₒ‐pó‐cheùng meĭ‐shēng, neĭ‐teì pit kung‐p‘òh, chaám‐fāt‐kokₒ‐meĭ‐shuê, sak‐chuê shóh‐yaŭ shuí‐ts‘uēn, yaû k‘aaĭ shēk‐t‘aū waí‐waaî kokₒ‐meĭ‐teî.
20 Mīng‐chiu‐tsó hìn tsaì‐māt‐chi‐shī, t‘aí‐la, yaŭ shuí ts‘ūng Ĭ‐tung‐kè lô laū‐tò, ch‘ung‐moŏn ts‘ź teî.
21 Moh‐aapₒ chùng‐yān, t‘eng‐mān saam wōng sheŭng‐laī, seúng‐kung‐tá k‘uĭ‐teî, tsaû tsuî‐tsaāp faān nāng p‘ei‐kaapₒ‐kè, tsẑ‐shiù‐chì‐cheúng, to k‘eĭ‐tsoî kíng‐pin.
22 Chiu‐tsó heí‐shan‐shī, yāt chiù‐ue shuí‐mîn, Moh‐aapₒ‐yān t‘aí‐kìn tuì‐mîn shuí hūng uē huetₒ:
23 Tsaû wâ, Ni‐ti haî huetₒ à: saam wōng pit‐tîng paaî‐mōng, chùng‐seung kung‐tá: Moh‐aapₒ‐yān à, huì ts‘eúng‐tuēt ts‘oī‐māt là.
24 K‘uĭ‐teî huì‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè yīng, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû heí‐laī kung‐tá Moh‐aapₒ‐yān, chì k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt ts‘īn‐pîn paaî tsaú. Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chui‐kón tá‐paaî‐Moh‐aapₒ‐yān, yāp‐tò k‘uĭ kíng‐noî.
25 Tsaû ch‘aakₒ‐laân k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè shēng, kokₒ‐yān wing shēk‐t‘aū t‘īn moŏn shóh‐yaŭ‐meĭ‐teî; yaû sak‐chuê kokₒ‐shuí‐ts‘uēn, chaám‐fāt kokₒ‐meĭ‐shuê: chì‐ue Kat‐hap‐la‐sitₒ, tūk shîng‐hâ shēng‐kè shēk; taân fei‐shēk‐kè ping, sz̀‐waī kung‐tá.
26 Moh‐aapₒ wōng t‘aí‐kìn chân‐shaì shâm‐taaî, pat‐nāng taí‐tīk, tsaû taaì‐chuê ts‘at‐paakₒ cha‐kìm‐kè ping, seúng ch‘ung‐ch‘ut chân‐ngoî, tò Ĭ‐tung wōng kóh‐ch‘uè, taân‐haî pat‐nāng.
27 Ue‐shî tseung ying kaì‐waî‐kè cheúng‐tsź, tsoî shēng‐sheûng hìn‐waī faān‐tsaì. Kóm‐tsaû t‘ùng‐hân Ĭ‐saik‐līt: saam wōng tsaû leī‐hoi Moh‐aapₒ wōng, kwai poón‐kwokₒ.
