1 TONG Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐sai‐à‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam ch‘í‐ch‘oh tang‐waî‐kòh‐shī, yaŭ ts‘ź‐īn‐uĕ, yaū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ch‘uēn‐kóng, wâ,
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Iù‐k‘eĭ‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐chi‐uên, tuì Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè chùng‐yap, tsik‐haî tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk kìng‐paaì‐kè kóng, yaû tseung ngŏh shóh‐fan‐foò‐neĭ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, kóng‐saaì kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐t‘eng; m̄‐hó kaám‐shiú yat‐kuì:
3 Waāk‐ché k‘uĭ‐teî háng t‘eng‐ts‘ūng, kokₒ‐yān yaû chuèn‐heùng leī‐hoi k‘uĭ okₒ‐lô m̄‐tîng, táng ngŏh hóh‐ĭ waî tsoi‐naân‐kè sẑ tsẑ‐fooì, tsik‐haî yan k‘uĭ hāng‐waī‐kè okₒ‐ts‘īng, yaŭ chué‐ì heùng k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè tsoi‐naân.
4 Neĭ iù‐heùng‐k‘uĭ kóng, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘óng‐yeūk neĭ m̄‐háng t‘eng‐ts‘ūng ngŏh, i‐chuê ngŏh shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐laāp tsoî‐neĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn‐kè lūt‐faatₒ laī‐hāng.
5 T‘eng‐ts‘ūng ngŏh chùng‐pūk sin‐chi‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsik‐haî ngŏh tá‐faatₒ‐huì neĭ‐ch‘uè‐kè: hó‐tsó heí‐shan tá‐faatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî, neĭ‐teî sheûng‐ch‘é m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng;
6 Uē‐kwóh m̄‐háng‐t‘eng, ngŏh tsaû lîng ni‐kaan‐uk hó‐ts‘z̆ Shì‐lōh, yaû ni‐kòh‐shēng heùng teî‐sheûng shīng‐waī chaù‐chòh‐kè.
7 Kòh‐ti tsaì‐sz, kûng sin‐chi, uĕ‐k‘āp chùng‐yān, to t‘eng‐mān Yē‐leî‐maĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk kóng ni‐ti‐shuetₒ‐wâ.
8 Ī‐ch‘é Yē‐leî‐maĭ tò‐kóng‐uēn‐saaì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò k‘uĭ tuì chùng‐tsź‐mān kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ kòh‐chân‐shī, chùng‐tsaì‐sz kûng sin‐chi, uĕ‐k‘āp taaî‐chùng tsź‐mān, lōk‐shaú chukₒ‐chuê k‘uĭ, wâ, Neĭ yat‐tîng iù‐sź‐lokₒ.
9 Neĭ yan‐hōh t‘okₒ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mēng kóng uê‐īn, wâ, Ni‐kaan‐tîn‐uk pit‐shīng‐waī Shî‐lōh kóm, kòh‐shēng to shīng‐waī fong‐faì mŏ‐yān kui‐chuê‐kè ni? Taaî‐chùng tsź‐mān to‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk, haí Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐ch‘uè tsuî‐maaī.
10 ¶ Ī‐ch‘é Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè kwan‐cheúng t‘eng‐mān ts‘ź‐sẑ kòh‐shī, tsaû yaū wōng‐kung sheŭng‐tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, haí yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk san‐moōn kòh‐ch‘uè ts‘ŏh‐laāp.
11 Ue‐shî kòh‐ti tsaì‐sz kûng sin‐chi, heùng‐kwan‐cheúng uĕ‐k‘āp taaî‐chùng tsź‐mān kóng, wâ, Ni‐kòh‐yān ying‐koi shaû‐sź; yan‐waî k‘uĭ kóng uê‐īn kung‐kik ts‘ź‐shēng, chiù‐i neĭ‐teî ts‘an‐ĭ shóh‐t‘eng‐kwòh‐kè lokₒ.
12 Ue‐shî Yē‐leî‐maĭ tuì‐chùng‐kwan‐cheúng uĕ‐k‘āp taaî‐chùng tsź‐mān kóng, wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tá‐faatₒ‐ngŏh heùng‐chuê ni‐kaan‐tîn‐uk, yaû heùng‐chuê ni‐kòh‐shēng, kóng‐saaì neĭ shóh‐t‘eng‐mān‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ.
13 Koò‐ts‘ź uē‐kam iù‐koí‐chìng neĭ‐kè lô‐t‘ō, kûng neĭ‐kè hāng‐waī, yaû shûn‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐teî Sheûng‐Taì‐kè sheng‐yam; kóm‐tsaû Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pit‐waî k‘uĭ heùng‐neĭ shóh‐kóng‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ tsẑ‐fooì.
14 Chì‐ue ngŏh, t‘aí‐a, ngŏh tsoî neĭ‐kè shaú: tsaû chiù‐i‐neĭ‐ngaăn‐ts‘īn, ĭ‐waī‐hó yaû ĭ‐waī‐cheūk kóm‐yeûng toî ngŏh.
15 Tūk‐haî iù‐chi‐tak‐chan, uē‐kwóh neĭ‐teî chíng‐sź ngŏh, tsaû lîng mō‐koo‐chi‐huetₒ kwai‐ue neĭ‐teî shan‐sheûng, yaû kwai‐ue ts‘ź‐shēng, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung kui‐mān‐chi‐sheûng: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chan‐chìng tá‐faatₒ‐ngŏh‐laī, haí neĭ‐teî‐kè ĭ‐chung, kóng‐saaì ni‐ti‐shuetₒ‐wâ à.
16 Ue‐shî kwan‐cheúng kûng taaî‐chùng‐tsź‐mān, tuì‐kòh‐ti tsaì‐sz k‘āp sin‐chi wâ; Ni‐kòh‐yān m̄‐haî‐ying‐tong‐shaû‐sź a: yan‐waî k‘uĭ k‘okₒ‐shāt haî t‘okₒ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ngŏh‐teî Sheûng‐Taì‐kè mēng tuì‐ngŏh‐teî‐kóng‐kè à.
17 Ue‐shî kíng‐noî lŏ‐taaî‐chi‐chung, keí‐kòh‐heí‐laī, tuì tsź‐mān‐kè ooî‐chùng kóng‐wâ,
18 Tong Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Hei‐sai‐ka kòh‐shī, Moh‐leĭ‐sha‐yān Maĭ‐ka kóng uê‐īn, yaû tuì Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsź‐mān wâ, Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Sun‐shaan tseung‐pit‐k‘aaĭ laī‐p‘ā laī‐kaang, hó‐ts‘z̆‐t‘īn kóm‐yeûng, Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng pit‐shīng‐waī laân‐ngă‐tui, tîn‐t‘ōng‐kè shaan, yīk hó‐ts‘z̆ shuê‐lām ko‐tsùn‐ch‘uè.
19 Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Hei‐sai‐ka kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè chùng‐yān, heí‐haî chíng‐sź k‘uĭ me? Heí‐m̄‐haî kìng‐waì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yaû hat‐k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yan‐tín, yaû Yē‐Wōh‐Wā waî shóh‐kóng‐ch‘ut kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsoi‐wôh tsẑ‐fooì me? Uē‐kwóh haî tsô ts‘ź‐sẑ, tsaû hāng taaî‐okₒ, luî‐k‘āp tsẑ‐keí‐kè līng‐wān che.
20 Kim‐ch‘é chûng‐yaŭ kòh‐yān, t‘okₒ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mēng kóng uê‐īn, tsik‐haî Kei‐līt‐yē‐lām‐yān Shî‐mâ‐ngă‐kè tsaí Oo‐leĭ‐à, k‘uĭ to heùng‐chuê ts‘ź‐shēng, yaû heùng‐chuê ts‘ź‐teî kóng uê‐īn, chiù‐faan‐Yē‐leî‐maĭ shóh‐kóng‐kè yat‐yeûng:
21 Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam wōng, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ taaî‐chùng‐yŭng‐sẑ, uĕ‐k‘āp kwan‐cheúng, t‘eng‐mān k‘uĭ‐ti‐shuetₒ‐wâ kòh‐shī, kòh‐wōng seúng chíng‐sź k‘uĭ: waī‐haî Oo‐leĭ‐à t‘eng‐mān kòh‐shī, tsaû fong‐heí‐laī, t‘ō‐tsaú‐yāp‐huì Oi‐k‘āp;
22 Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam wōng tá‐faatₒ‐yān‐huì Oi‐k‘āp, tsik‐haî À‐kaakₒ‐p‘òh‐kè tsaí Leî‐nā‐taan, t‘ūng‐maaī keí‐kòh‐yān huì Oi‐k‘āp.
23 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû laai Oo‐leĭ‐à ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp, yaû taaì‐k‘uĭ‐laī‐tò Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam wōng kòh‐ch‘uè; kóh‐kòh wōng tsaû k‘aaĭ‐kìm laī‐shaatₒ‐k‘uĭ, yaû tseung k‘uĭ‐kè shi wing‐hiu‐lōk‐î‐teî‐kè fān‐mô‐ch‘uè.
24 Waī‐haî Sha‐faan‐kè tsaí À‐hei‐kom‐chi‐shaú, tsaû pó‐oô Yē‐leî‐maĭ, táng k‘uĭ‐teî pat‐nāng kaau k‘uĭ lōk chùng‐yān‐chi‐shaú, laī‐chíng‐sź k‘uĭ lokₒ.
