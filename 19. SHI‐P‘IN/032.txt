1 YĀN yaŭ‐kwòh tak‐shè, yaŭ tsuî tak‐che‐k‘oì‐ché, chan‐haî yaŭ‐fuk.
2 Taân‐faān Yē‐Wōh‐Wā m̄‐chap‐tsuî k‘uĭ, k‘uĭ sam‐chung mŏ kaan‐chà‐ché, kóm‐yeûng‐chi‐yān, chan‐haî yaŭ‐fuk.
3 Ngŏh meî ch‘ut‐sheng‐chi‐shī, shīng‐yāt t‘aàn‐sik, kan‐kwat tsîm shui.
4 Neĭ ch‘ŭng shaú yāt‐yê ka‐chaakₒ ngŏh: ngŏh t‘aí‐chi‐tsz‐yûn, pìn‐waī hâ‐yāt‐chi‐kon‐hŏn.
5 Ngŏh chiu‐yîng keí‐tsuî, m̄‐che‐ím tsẑ‐keí‐chi‐fei. Ngŏh tsẑ‐keí wâ, Ngŏh tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ts‘īn yîng‐tsuî; neĭ tsaû shè ngŏh tsuî‐okₒ.
6 Yan‐ts‘ź faān‐kìng‐k‘īn‐chi‐yān, ch‘àn hóh‐ĭ uê‐cheūk‐neĭ‐chi‐shī, iù k‘eī‐k‘aū neĭ: sui yaŭ hūng‐shuí tsàm‐moŏn, to pit m̄‐k‘āp k‘uĭ shan.
7 Neĭ haî ngŏh ts‘ōng‐shan‐chi‐shóh; shaí ngŏh t‘uetₒ‐leī foó‐naân; neĭ pit lîng tak‐kaù‐chi‐koh, sz̀‐pîn waī‐chuê ngŏh.
8 Kìng‐k‘īn‐chi‐yān à, ngŏh iù kaaù neĭ, tseung tong‐hāng‐chi‐lô chí‐shî neĭ: ngŏh pit waî neĭ kaì‐suèn, ngŏh ngaăn pit t‘aí‐koò neĭ.
9 Neĭ m̄‐hó hōk lōh‐mă‐chi‐uē‐ch‘ún, shî‐pit iù yûng‐haām yûng‐keung laī‐lāk‐chuê k‘uĭ haú: m̄‐haî, tsaû m̄‐t‘eng shaí.
10 Okₒ‐yān shî‐pit hó‐toh yau‐shaū, waī‐haî í‐laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ché, tsaû sz̀‐pîn shaû‐yan.
11 Neĭ‐teî shîn‐yān, tong iù waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā laī‐foon‐heí faaì‐lōk: taân‐faān sam‐chìng‐chi‐yān, ying‐tong foon‐sheng foo‐kiù.
