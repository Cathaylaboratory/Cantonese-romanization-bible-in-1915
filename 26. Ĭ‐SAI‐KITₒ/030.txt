1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ‐KÈ īn‐uĕ, tsoì‐kòng‐lām‐ue ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐kóng‐uê‐īn, yaû wâ, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Iù‐faatₒ‐sheng haàm, Yaŭ tsoi‐wôh‐kè yāt à!
3 Yan‐waî yāt‐k‘eī‐laī‐kân, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐yāt laī‐kân, haî māt‐wān‐chi‐yāt; tsô‐î‐pong‐yān‐kè shī‐haû.
4 Pit‐yaŭ to‐kìm kòng‐lām‐ue Oi‐k‘āp, tsoî Oi‐k‘āp peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè yān pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè kòh‐shī, pit‐yaŭ t‘ùng‐foó tsoî Koó‐shāt‐chi‐chung, yān‐teî pit‐ts‘uí‐huì k‘uĭ kw‘ān‐chùng, k‘uĭ kei‐chí yīk‐pit‐peî‐ch‘aakₒ‐tai.
5 Koó‐shāt, Puk, Lô‐tak k‘āp tsaāp‐tsūk‐kè chùng‐mān, Koó‐pa k‘āp‐laāp‐māng‐yeukₒ‐chi‐teî‐kè yān, pit‐peî‐to‐kìm pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè.
6 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Shóh‐yaŭ‐foō‐chuê Oi‐k‘āp‐kè, to‐ooĭ‐k‘ing‐paaî; k‘uĭ‐k‘uēn‐pìng‐kè kiu‐ngô, to‐ooĭ‐kòng‐tai: ts‘ūng Tsuî‐ĭ‐neī‐kè shuè‐laū, tsoî k‘eī‐chung peî‐to‐kìm pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè, Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ.
7 K‘uĭ‐teî tsoî‐fong‐faì kwokₒ‐teî‐chi‐chung, shīng‐waī fong‐faì‐kè, k‘uĭ shēng‐yap yīk‐tsoî‐peî‐waí‐laân‐shēng‐yap‐chi‐chung.
8 Ngŏh tsoî Oi‐k‘āp t‘aù‐fóh, ī‐ch‘é shóh‐pong‐chôh‐k‘uĭ‐kè, peî‐tsiú‐mīt‐saaì kòh‐shī, k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
9 Tong‐kòh‐shī, yaū‐ngŏh‐mîn‐ts‘īn pit‐yaŭ sz̀‐ché shīng‐shuēn ch‘ut‐huì, lîng‐on‐īn‐kui‐chuê‐kè Koó‐shāt‐yān king‐fong, tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐ch‘uè, pit‐yaŭ t‘ùng‐foó, hó‐ts‘z̆ tong Oi‐k‘āp‐kòh‐yāt kóm: yan‐waî t‘aí‐a, ts‘ź‐sẑ laī‐kân lokₒ.
10 ¶ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh‐to‐ooĭ yûng Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ‐kè shaú, lîng Oi‐k‘āp‐kè kw‘ān‐chùng chí‐sik.
11 K‘uĭ t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ tsź‐mān, haî‐līt‐pong‐chi‐chung hóh‐p‘à‐kè, pit‐peî‐taaì‐laī tsiú‐mīt‐kòh teî: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐pāt‐ch‘ut pá‐kìm, kung‐kik Oi‐k‘āp, tseung peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, ch‘ung‐moŏn‐kíng‐noî.
12 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng‐kong‐hōh kon‐k‘okₒ, yaû maaî‐kòh‐teî kaau‐lōk okₒ‐yān‐kè shaú: ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐yûng ngoî‐yān‐kè shaú, lîng kòh‐teî uĕ‐k‘āp k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè, shīng‐waī fong‐faì: ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ts‘āng kóng‐kwòh lokₒ.
13 ¶ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐tsiú‐mīt ngaŭ‐tseûng, yaû‐lîng‐tiu‐hak‐kè tseûng‐tsoî Nōh‐fat‐ch‘uè chí‐sik; ī‐ch‘é shūk Oi‐k‘āp‐teî‐kè, m̄‐tsoì‐yaŭ kwan‐cheúng shang‐ch‘ut: ngŏh pit‐on‐yat‐kîn hóh‐p‘à‐kè sẑ, tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐teî‐ch‘uè,
14 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng Paatₒ‐lōh fong‐faì, yaû‐tsoî‐Sóh‐on t‘aù‐fóh, tsoî Nōh‐ch‘uè hāng‐shám‐p‘oòn.
15 Ngŏh ooĭ‐tseung‐ngŏh făn‐hân tó‐lōk Sùn‐chi‐sheûng, tsik‐haî Oi‐k‘āp kin‐koò‐chi‐shóh; yaû haí Nōh‐ch‘uè t‘uĕn‐tsuēt kw‘ān‐chùng.
16 Ngŏh ooĭ‐tsoî‐Oi‐k‘āp t‘aù‐cheūk‐fóh: Sùn pit‐shaû taaî‐t‘ùng‐foó, Nōh yīk‐peî‐waí‐ch‘aakₒ, Nōh‐fat shīng‐yāt pit‐yaŭ‐ch‘aū‐tīk.
17 À‐mān kûng Peí‐paakₒ‐shāt‐kè shiù‐nīn‐yān pit‐peî‐to‐kìm pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè: ī‐ch‘é ni‐leŭng‐kòh‐shēng, to‐peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐huì.
18 Tong ngŏh haí Taapₒ‐peí‐nīk waí‐ch‘aakₒ Oi‐k‘āp‐kè aakₒ: k‘uĭ k‘uēn‐pìng‐kè kiu‐ngô, tsoî k‘eī‐chung chí‐sik kòh‐shī, yāt‐t‘aū to‐pìn‐waī hak: chì‐ue ts‘ź‐shēng, pit‐yaŭ‐wān che‐k‘oì, k‘eī‐chung‐kè chùng‐nuĭ, pit‐peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐huì.
19 Ngŏh pit‐chiù‐kóm‐yeûng tsoî Oi‐k‘āp hāng‐shám‐p‘oòn: k‘uĭ‐teî ooĭ‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
20 ¶ Tong shāp‐yat‐nīn ching‐uēt ch‘oh‐ts‘at‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue‐ngŏh, wâ,
21 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, ngŏh ĭ‐king aaú‐chitₒ Oi‐k‘āp wōng‐kè shaú‐peì; yaû t‘aí‐a, m̄‐ts‘āng peî‐chaatₒ‐hó‐foo‐chuê i‐sheung‐kè yeūk, peí‐ch‘e‐luk‐ch‘uk‐chuê, táng hóh‐ĭ‐tak‐yaŭ‐līk laī‐cha‐chuê pá‐kìm.
22 Shóh‐ĭ Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; T‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik Oi‐k‘āp wōng Faatₒ‐lŏ, yaû ooĭ‐aaú‐chitₒ k‘uĭ shaú‐peì, līn kin‐k‘eūng‐kè, kûng ĭ‐king‐chitₒ‐t‘uĕn‐kè; yīk pit‐lîng pá‐kìm tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐shaú‐chung titₒ‐lōk.
23 Ngŏh pit‐fan‐saàn Oi‐k‘āp‐yān tsoî‐līt‐pong‐chi‐chung, yaû p‘aaì‐hoi‐k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî līt‐kwokₒ‐ch‘uè.
24 Ngŏh ooĭ‐kin‐koò Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè shaú, yaû chai‐pá‐kìm tsoî k‘uĭ‐shaú‐chung: waī‐haî ngŏh ooī‐aaú‐chitₒ Faatₒ‐lŏ‐kè shaú‐peì, k‘uĭ pit‐tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn hō‐foo, hó‐ts‘z̆ shaû‐sheung‐chì‐sź‐kè yān kóm‐yeûng‐hō‐foo.
25 Ngŏh ooĭ‐foō‐heí Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè shaú‐peì, Faatₒ‐lŏ‐kè shaú‐peì ooĭ‐shuī‐tai; ngŏh chai ngŏh pá‐kìm tsoî‐Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè shaú, yaû k‘aaĭ‐laī shan‐hoi tsoî Oi‐k‘āp‐teî‐chi‐sheûng, kòh‐shī, k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
26 Ngŏh pit‐fan‐saàn Oi‐k‘āp‐yān tsoî līt‐pong‐chi‐chung, yaû p‘aaì‐hoi k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî līt‐kwokₒ‐ch‘uè; k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
