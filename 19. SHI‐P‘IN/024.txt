1 TEÎ uĕ‐k‘āp teî‐sheûng‐chi‐maân‐māt; shaì‐kaaì uĕ‐k‘āp shaì‐sheûng‐chuê‐chi‐yān, to shūk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
2 K‘uĭ tseung teî‐chi‐kan‐kei, on‐tsoî hoí‐sheûng, laāp‐tsoî hōh‐chung.
3 Mat‐shuī sheŭng tak Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐ko‐shaan? mat‐shuī k‘eĭ‐tak k‘uĭ‐chi‐shìng‐shóh?
4 Waī‐haî shaú kon‐tsîng, sam ts‘ing‐kitₒ; tsúng pat heùng hui‐mŏng, pat‐shaî ká‐uên.
5 Kóm‐yeûng‐chi‐yān, pit tak. Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chuk‐fuk, shi‐kaù‐chi‐Chué, pit ch‘ing‐k‘uĭ‐waī‐î.
6 Ni‐táng haî k‘aū Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐yān, tsik Ngă‐kokₒ haû‐toî, laī k‘aū kìn‐neĭ‐mîn‐ché.
7 Neĭ kokₒ‐moōn iù faaì‐hoi; koó‐sik‐chi‐oô, neĭ tong kuí‐hoi; táng tsuen‐wīng‐chi‐Wōng yāp‐huì.
8 Ni‐kòh tsuen‐wīng‐chi‐wōng haî mat‐shuī? Tsik‐haî yaŭ‐līk yaŭ‐nāng‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, chìn‐mō‐pat‐shìng‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lokₒ.
9 Neĭ kokₒ‐moōn iù faaì‐hoi; koó‐sik‐chi‐oô, neĭ tong kuí‐hoi; táng tsuen‐wīng‐chi‐wōng yāp‐huì.
10 Ni‐kòh tsuen‐wīng‐chi‐wōng haî mat‐shuī? Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsik‐haî tsuen‐wīng‐chi‐wōng.
