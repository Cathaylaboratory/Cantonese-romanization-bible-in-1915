1 YĒ‐WŌH‐WĀ tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Faatₒ mā‐fung‐kè yān tak kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè shī‐haû, tîng‐laî haî kóm. Iù taaì k‘uĭ tò tsaì‐sz‐ch‘uè:
3 Tsaì‐sz iù ch‘ut yīng‐p‘oōn‐ngoî t‘aí‐kwòh; yeūk kìn k‘uĭ‐kè mā‐fung hó‐faan.
4 Tsaû iù fan‐foò yān waî kòh‐tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐tsîng kè, wán leŭng‐chekₒ kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè shang tseukₒ, yaû ning paakₒ‐heung‐mūk, k‘āp kòng‐shik‐sìn ngaū‐ts‘at‐ts‘ó:
5 Tsaì‐sz iù fan‐foò tseung yat‐chekₒ tseukₒ, tsoî ngă‐heì‐noî‐kè shang‐shuí‐sheûng t‘ong‐hiu.
6 Tsaì‐sz iù tseung kóh‐chekₒ shang tseukₒ, kûng paakₒ‐heung‐mūk, kòng‐shik‐sìn, ngaū‐ts‘at‐ts‘ó, t‘ūng‐maaī tsàm‐tsoî shang‐shuí‐sheûng shóh‐t‘ong‐kè tseukₒ huetₒ chi‐chung:
7 Yaû k‘aaĭ‐laī shá‐lōk kòh mā‐fung tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè yān ts‘at ts‘z̀, tsaû tîng k‘uĭ waī kitₒ‐tsîng, yaû tseung shang tseukₒ fòng tsoî t‘īn‐ch‘uè.
8 Tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐tsîng‐kè yān tong saí i‐fūk, t‘aì‐saaì mō‐faatₒ, yûng shuí saí‐yūk, tsaû suèn‐waī kitₒ‐tsîng: īn‐haû hóh‐ĭ yāp yīng‐p‘oōn, taân yīng‐iù chuê‐tsoî tsẑ‐keí‐kè cheùng‐mōk‐ngoî ts‘at yāt.
9 Tò taî‐ts‘at yāt, tong tsoì t‘aì‐saaì tsẑ‐keí‐kè t‘aū‐faatₒ, oō‐so, meī‐mō, ts‘uēn‐shan‐kè mō to t‘aì‐saaì: yaû yûng shuí saí i‐fūk k‘āp saí shan, kóm chì suèn‐waī kitₒ‐tsîng.
10 Tò taî‐paatₒ yāt, iù ning leŭng‐chekₒ mŏ‐hā‐ts‘z‐kè yeūng‐koó‐tsaí, yat‐chekₒ meî‐moŏn yat‐suì‐kè yeūng‐tsẑ‐tsaí, ĭ‐faatₒ shāp‐fân‐saam‐kè wôh‐yaū saì‐mîn, tsô sò‐tsaì, yaû ka yaū yat‐hokₒ.
11 Hāng‐kitₒ‐laĭ‐kè tsaì‐sz, iù tseung shaû‐kitₒ‐kè yān, kûng kokₒ‐yeûng laĭ‐māt, taaì‐tò ooî‐mōk moōn‐haú, tò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn:
12 Tsaì‐sz iù tseung yat‐chekₒ kung‐yeūng‐tsaí, hìn‐sheŭng tsô shūk‐hin‐tsaì, kûng yaū yat‐hokₒ, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn iū‐paaí, laī tsô iū‐tsaì:
13 Iù tsoî shìng‐teî t‘ong yeūng‐tsaí, tsik‐haî tsoî t‘ong shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì shang kûng faān‐tsaì shang‐kè teî‐fong: shūk‐hin‐tsaì haî kwai‐ue tsaì‐sz, t‘ūng shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì yat‐yeûng: haî chì‐shìng‐kè:
14 Tsaì‐sz iù ning‐ti shūk‐hin‐tsaì shang‐kè huetₒ, ch‘ā‐lōk tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐kè yān yaû‐ĭ‐chue‐sheûng, yaû ch‘ā k‘uĭ yaû‐shaú yaû‐keukₒ‐kè taaî‐chí:
15 Tsaì‐sz tseung yat‐hokₒ‐kè yaū, ning‐ti cham‐lōk tsẑ‐keí‐kè tsóh‐cheúng:
16 Yûng yaû‐shaú‐kè chí yăm tsóh‐cheúng‐kè yaū, shá‐lōk Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn ts‘at ts‘z̀:
17 Yaû tseung shaú‐chung shîng‐hâ kè yaū, ch‘ā‐lōk tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐kè yān yaû‐ĭ‐chue‐sheûng, yaû ch‘ā k‘uĭ yaû‐shaú yaû‐keukₒ‐kè taaî‐chí, tsik‐haî ch‘ā shūk‐hin‐tsaì huetₒ kòh‐ch‘uè:
18 Tsaì‐sz iù tseung shaú‐chung shîng‐hâ‐kè yaū, cham‐lōk tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐kè yān kòh‐t‘aū‐sheûng: yaû tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘ìn, waî k‘uĭ shūk‐tsuî.
19 Tsaì‐sz iù hìn shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì, waî kóh ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn pat‐kitₒ tsaû tak‐kitₒ‐kè yān shūk‐tsuî; īn‐haû t‘ong faān‐tsaì shang:
20 Tsaì‐sz iù tseung faān‐tsaì k‘āp sò‐tsaì hìn‐tsoî t‘aān‐sheûng: waî kòh‐yān shūk‐tsuî, kòh‐yān kóm‐tsaû tak‐kitₒ‐tsîng.
21 ¶ Kòh‐yān yeūk‐haî p‘ān‐k‘ūng, pat‐nāng paân kóm‐toh laĭ‐māt; tsaû tong ning yat‐chekₒ yeūng‐tsaí iū yat‐hă, tsô shūk‐hin‐tsaì, waî k‘uĭ shūk‐tsuî, yaû ning shāp‐fân‐yat‐kè wôh‐yaū saì‐mîn, k‘āp yaū yat hokₒ, tsô sò‐tsaì;
22 Chiù k‘uĭ‐kè līk‐leûng, waāk yûng leŭng‐chekₒ paan‐kau, leŭng‐chekₒ paāk‐kopₒ‐tsaí; yat‐chekₒ tsô shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì, yat‐chekₒ tsô faān‐tsaì.
23 Tak‐kitₒ‐haû taî‐paatₒ yāt, tong tseung kokₒ‐māt ning‐huì tsaì‐sz‐ch‘uè, tò ooî‐mōk moōn‐haú, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn.
24 Tsaì‐sz iù tseung shūk‐hin‐tsaì‐kè yeūng‐tsaí, k‘āp yaū yat hokₒ, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn iū‐paaí, laī tsô iū‐tsaì:
25 Tsaû t‘ong‐hiu shūk‐hin‐tsaì‐kè yeūng‐tsaí, tseung‐ti huetₒ ch‘ā‐tsoî tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐kè yān yaû‐ĭ‐chue‐sheûng, yaû ch‘ā k‘uĭ yaû‐shaú yaû‐keukₒ‐kè taaî‐chí:
26 Tsaì‐sz yaû iù tseung‐ti yaū, cham‐lōk tsẑ‐keí‐kè tsóh‐cheúng:
27 Yûng yaû‐shaú‐chí yăm tsóh‐cheúng‐kè yaū, shá‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn ts‘at ts‘z̀:
28 Yaû tseung shaú‐chung‐kè yaū, ch‘ā‐lōk tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐kè yān yaû‐ĭ‐chue‐sheûng, k‘āp yaû‐shaú yaû‐keukₒ‐kè taaî‐chí, tsik‐haî ch‘ā shūk‐hin‐tsaì huetₒ kòh‐ch‘uè:
29 Tsaì‐sz iù tseung shaú‐chung shîng‐hâ‐kè yaū, cham‐lōk tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐kè yān kòh‐t‘aū‐sheûng, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn waî k‘uĭ shūk‐tsuî.
30 Yaû tseung k‘uĭ līk‐leûng shóh‐nāng‐paân‐kè, waāk yat‐chekₒ paan‐kau, waāk yat‐chekₒ paāk‐kopₒ‐tsaí hìn‐sheŭng;
31 Tsik‐haî k‘uĭ shóh‐nāng‐paân‐kè, yat‐chekₒ tsô shūk‐tsuî‐tsaì, yat‐chekₒ tsô faān‐tsaì, k‘āp sò‐tsaì: tsaì‐sz tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn, iù waî tseung‐tak‐kitₒ‐kè yān shūk‐tsuî.
32 Faān‐yān faatₒ mā‐fung, waāk p‘ān‐k‘ūng pat‐nāng paân‐tak kitₒ‐chi‐laĭ‐māt‐kè, yaŭ ni‐ti tîng‐laî à.
33 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai À‐lūn wâ,
34 Neĭ‐teî yāp‐hiu ngŏh ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ tsô ch‘aán‐īp‐kè Ka‐naām‐teî, ngŏh yeūk lîng yat‐kaan uk faatₒ mooī, hó‐ts‘z̆ mā‐fung, tsik‐haî tsoî neĭ tsô ch‘aán‐īp‐kè teî‐fong;
35 Uk chué tong laī tuì tsaì‐sz wâ, Ngŏh t‘aí ngŏh‐kè uk, hó‐ts‘z̆ haî faatₒ mooī:
36 Tsaì‐sz meî yāp uk t‘aí‐kwòh chi‐ts‘īn, tong fan‐foò k‘uĭ poon hung kaan uk, mĭn‐chì uk‐noî‐kè heì‐mĭng, to ĭm pat‐kitₒ; īn‐haû tsaì‐sz hóh‐ĭ yāp uk t‘aí:
37 Tsaì‐sz iù t‘aí, yeūk kìn kòh‐ti mooī tsoî uk‐kè ts‘eūng‐sheûng, faatₒ‐ch‘ut hūng‐shik, waāk lūk‐shik, tsoî ts‘eūng‐sheûng tai‐haâm;
38 Tsaì‐sz iù yaū uk ch‘ut‐laī, tò moōn‐ngoî, tseung uk fung‐sóh ts‘at yāt:
39 Tò taî‐ts‘at yāt, tsaì‐sz iù tsoì huì t‘aí; yeūk kìn mooī tsoî ts‘eūng‐sheûng shang toh;
40 Tsaû tong fan‐foò tseung‐yaŭ‐mooī‐kè shēk‐t‘aū ch‘aakₒ‐lat, wing‐tsoî shēng‐ngoî oo‐waì‐kè teî‐fong:
41 Yaû tseung uk‐noî sz̀‐waī‐kè ts‘eūng kwaatₒ‐tsêng, shóh kwaatₒ lat‐kè ch‘ān, tó‐lōk shēng‐ngoî oo‐waì‐kè teî‐fong:
42 Yûng pīt‐ti shēk pó‐t‘īn ch‘aakₒ‐huì‐kè shēk; yûng pīt‐ti fooi măn‐kwòh kaan uk.
43 Yeūk ch‘aakₒ‐huì shēk, kwaatₒ ts‘eūng măn ts‘eūng chi‐haû, tsoì yaŭ mooī waân, tsoî‐uk noî faatₒ‐în;
44 Tsaì‐sz tong tsoì huì t‘aí, yeūk kìn mooī tsoî uk‐noî shang toh, kóm haî taaî‐mā‐fung tsoî uk‐noî: tsaû suèn waī pat‐kitₒ.
45 Iù tseung uk ch‘aakₒ‐laân, līn kòh‐ti shēk kûng mūk, k‘āp uk‐noî shóh‐yaŭ‐kè, to poon‐huì shēng‐ngoî oo‐waì‐kè teî‐fong.
46 Faān fung‐sóh kòh‐kaan‐uk‐kè yāt‐tsź, yāp‐huì‐kè yān, pit ĭm oo‐waì chì‐tò yê‐maăn.
47 Tsoî kòh‐kaan uk fàn kûng shīk‐kè; pit iù saí‐kwòh i‐fūk.
48 Yeūk măn‐fooi‐chi‐haû, tsaì‐sz yāp‐huì t‘aí‐kwòh, kìn mooī tsoî uk‐noî m̄ooĭ shang toh, tsaû iù tîng kaan‐uk waī kitₒ‐tsîng: yan kóh‐ti waân‐ch‘uè hó‐faan lokₒ.
49 K‘uĭ hāng kitₒ‐uk‐chi‐laĭ, tong ning leŭng‐chekₒ tseukₒ, k‘āp paakₒ‐heung‐mūk, kòng‐shik‐sìn, ngaū‐ts‘at‐ts‘ó:
50 Tseung yat‐chekₒ tseukₒ tsoî ngă‐heì‐noî‐kè shang‐shuí t‘ong‐hiu:
51 Yaû ning paakₒ‐heung‐mūk, ngaū‐ts‘at‐ts‘ó, kòng‐shik‐sìn, k‘āp shang tseukₒ, t‘ūng tsàm‐tsoî shang‐shuí‐sheûng shóh‐t‘ong‐kè tseukₒ huetₒ chi‐chung, ning‐heí, laī shá kaan‐uk ts‘at ts‘z̀:
52 Yaû yûng tseukₒ huetₒ, shang shuí, shang tseukₒ, paakₒ‐heung‐mūk, ngaū‐ts‘at‐ts‘ó, k‘āp kòng‐shik‐sìn, laī kitₒ‐tsîng kaan‐uk:
53 Tseung kòh‐chekₒ shang tseukₒ fòng‐tsoî shēng‐ngoî‐kè t‘īn‐kaan, kóm‐yeûng waî uk shūk‐tsuî: uk tsaû kitₒ‐tsîng lokₒ.
54 ¶ Ni‐ti haî kokₒ‐yeûng mā‐fung shang sín pêng‐chìng‐kè tîng‐laî,
55 Yaû haî i‐fūk kûng uk‐kè faatₒ mooī,
56 K‘āp faū‐chúng, foo‐ch‘ong, ch‘ut‐paan‐kè tîng‐laî;
57 Chí‐shî k‘uĭ‐teî pin‐yeûng haî pat‐kitₒ, pin‐yeûng haî kitₒ: ni‐ti to‐haî mā‐fung‐kè tîng‐laî à.
