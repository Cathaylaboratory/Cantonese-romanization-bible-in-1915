1 TONG Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐sai‐chi‐tsẑ Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam taî‐sz̀ nīn, yaŭ ts‘ź‐īn yaŭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ, wâ,
2 Iù k‘aaĭ shue‐kuén yat‐cheung, sé‐saaì ngŏh shóh‐kûng neĭ kóng, kung‐kik Ĭ‐shik‐līt, yaû kung‐kik Yaū‐t‘aaì, k‘āp kung‐kik chùng‐kwokₒ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsẑ ngŏh kûng‐neĭ‐kóng kòh‐yāt, tsik‐haî tsẑ Yeukₒ‐sai‐à‐kè yāt‐tsź, chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
3 Waāk‐ché Yaū‐t‘aaì‐ka háng t‘eng ngŏh heùng‐k‘uĭ shóh‐tîng‐tuēt‐kè tsoi‐wôh m̄‐tîng; táng‐tò k‘uĭ kokₒ‐yān chuèn‐heùng leī‐hoi okₒ‐lô; táng ngŏh hóh‐ĭ shè‐mĭn k‘uĭ‐kè hin‐yaū kûng tsuî‐okₒ.
4 Ue‐shî Yē‐leî‐maĭ chiû Yē‐leî‐à‐chi‐tsź Pa‐lūk: Pa‐lūk tsaû haí Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐chi‐haú, tsipₒ‐tó Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì k‘uĭ shóh‐kóng‐kè īn‐uĕ, sé‐saaì lōk‐shue‐kuén yat‐cheung.
5 Yē‐leî‐maĭ yaû‐fan‐foò Pa‐lūk wâ, Ngŏh peî kw‘àn‐chuê; tsaû pat‐nāng yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk:
6 Shóh‐ĭ neĭ iù‐huì, tseung neĭ tsoî kòh‐kuén‐shue haí ngŏh‐haú shóh‐sé‐lōk‐kè, tong kàm‐shīk kòh‐yāt, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, heùng tsź‐mān‐kè ĭ: tūk‐ch‘ut Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, yaû iù tūk‐kwòh yaū k‘uĭ shēng‐yap ch‘ut‐laī‐kè taaî‐chùng shūk Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè t‘eng‐t‘im.
7 Waāk‐ché k‘uĭ‐teî háng tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn, ch‘īng‐sheŭng pán‐k‘aū, yaû háng chuèn‐heùng, kokₒ‐yān leī‐hoi okₒ‐lô m̄‐tîng: yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐tîng‐tuēt kóng‐mīng kung‐kik ni‐ti‐tsź‐mān‐kè nau‐nô, k‘āp făn‐hân, haî hó‐taaî‐kè.
8 Neī‐leî‐à‐chi‐tsź Pa‐lūk, tsaû i‐chuê‐saaì sin‐chi Yē‐leî‐maĭ shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè laī‐tsô, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, haí kòh‐kuén‐shue, tūk‐ch‘ut Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ.
9 ¶ Fòng‐ch‘é tò Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐sai‐à‐chi‐tsź Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam taî‐nğ nīn kaú‐uēt kòh‐shī, Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng chùng‐kui‐mān, uĕ‐k‘āp ts‘ūng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè shēng‐yap laī‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè chùng‐yān, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn, shing‐mīng kàm‐shīk‐chi‐tsitₒ.
10 Ue‐shî Pa‐lūk tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk, haí Sha‐faan‐chi‐tsź shue‐leî K‘eī‐mă‐leĭ‐kè fōng, tsoî sheûng‐uên‐noî t‘ipₒ‐kân yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk san‐moōn kòh‐ch‘uè, yaū shue‐chung heùng taaî‐chùng tsź‐mān‐chi‐ĭ, tūk‐ch‘ut Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ.
11 Sha‐faan‐chi‐suen K‘eī‐mă‐leĭ‐chi‐tsź Maĭ‐koi‐ngă, yaū shue‐chung t‘eng‐mān‐saaì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòh‐shī,
12 Tsaû lōk‐huì wōng‐kung‐ch‘uè, yāp shue‐leî‐kè fōng: yaû t‘aí‐hă, taaî‐chùng kwan‐cheúng tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè ts‘ŏh, tsik‐haî shue‐leî Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă, kûng Shî‐mâ‐ngă‐chi‐tsź Teî‐loī‐ngă, k‘āp À‐kaakₒ‐p‘òh‐chi‐tsź Leî‐nā‐taan, Sha‐faan‐chi‐tsź K‘eī‐mă‐leĭ, Hap‐nā‐neī‐à‐chi‐tsź Sai‐taí‐ka, uĕ‐k‘āp taaî‐chùng kwan‐cheúng.
13 Ue‐shî Maĭ‐koi‐ngă tseung‐saaì k‘uĭ shóh‐t‘eng‐mān‐kè īn‐uĕ kóng‐mīng kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî, tsik‐haî tong Pa‐lūk heùng‐tsź‐mān‐kè ĭ tūk‐ch‘ut kòh‐kuén‐shue kòh‐shī shóh‐t‘eng‐mān‐kè.
14 Koò‐ts‘ź chùng‐kwan‐cheúng tá‐faatₒ Koó‐shî‐chi‐tsang‐suen, tsik Shî‐leî‐maĭ‐chi‐suen, tsik Neī‐taaî‐neī‐chi‐tsź, Yaū‐taí, huì‐tò Pa‐lūk‐ch‘uè, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Iù‐shaú‐cha‐chuê neĭ heùng tsź‐mān‐kè ĭ shóh‐tūk‐kwòh kòh‐kuén‐shue laī‐ni‐ch‘uè. Kóm‐tsaû Neī‐leî‐à‐chi‐tsź Pa‐lūk, shaú‐cha‐chuê kòh‐kuén‐shue, laī‐tò k‘uĭ‐teî‐ch‘uè.
15 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Chikₒ‐koón ts‘ŏh‐ch‘uè, tūk‐kwòh‐ngŏh‐teî t‘eng la. Kóm‐tsaû Pa‐lūk tūk‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî t‘eng.
16 Ue‐shî shīng‐tsaû kóm‐yeûng, k‘uĭ‐teî t‘eng‐mān‐saaì‐ti īn‐uĕ, tsaû fong‐heí‐laī, tiû‐chuèn‐mîn, peí‐ts‘ź seung‐tuì, yaû tuì Pa‐lūk wâ, Ngŏh‐teî pit‐iù‐k‘aaĭ‐saaì ni‐ti īn‐uĕ, kóng‐kwòh kòh wōng chi.
17 K‘uĭ‐teî yaû mân Pa‐lūk wâ, Chikₒ‐koón wâ ngŏh‐teî‐chi, Neĭ tím‐yeûng ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐haú sé‐saaì ni‐ti īn‐uĕ ni?
18 Ue‐shî Pa‐lūk taapₒ k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, K‘uĭ yûng haú mīng‐kóng ni‐ti chùng‐īn‐uĕ kwòh‐ngŏh, ngŏh yûng māk sé‐lōk kuén‐shue‐kè.
19 Ue‐shî kwan‐cheúng tuì Pa‐lūk wâ, Neĭ t‘ūng Yē‐leî‐maĭ, iù‐huì nīk‐maaī; tsúng‐m̄‐hó‐peî‐yān‐chi neĭ tsoî‐pin‐ch‘uè.
20 K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû yāp‐uên‐noî laī‐kìn kòh‐wōng, taân‐haî k‘uĭ‐teî tseung kòh‐kuén‐shue shau‐maaī tsoî shue‐leî Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă‐kè fōng, yaû tsûn‐tseung ni‐ti‐īn‐uĕ, kóng‐kwòh kòh‐wōng t‘eng.
21 Kóm‐tsaû kòh‐wōng shaí Yaū‐taí huì‐lóh kòh‐kuén‐shue: k‘uĭ tsaû yaū shue‐leî Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă‐kè fōng nim‐laī. Yaū‐taí tseung‐laī tūk‐kwòh kòh‐wōng t‘eng, yaû tūk‐kwòh k‘eĭ‐tsoî‐wōng‐chak‐pin chùng‐kwan‐cheúng t‘eng.
22 Kong‐ngaam kaú‐uēt kòh‐shī, kòh‐wōng ts‘ŏh‐tsoî tung‐t‘in‐kè uk: tsoî k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn, yaŭ fóh‐p‘oōn chong‐fóh‐shiu‐cheūk‐kè.
23 Kwóh‐īn kóm‐yeûng shīng‐tsaû, Yaū‐taí tūk‐kwòh saam‐sz̀‐p‘in chi‐shī, kòh‐wōng k‘aaĭ pá‐to‐tsaí laī‐kotₒ‐laân kòh‐kuén‐shue, wing‐lōk kòh‐p‘oōn‐chung‐kè fóh‐laī, táng‐tò kòh‐kuén‐shue peî‐p‘oōn‐chung‐kè fóh shiu‐mīt‐saaì.
24 K‘uĭ‐teî to‐m̄‐king‐fong, yaû m̄‐maakₒ‐laân k‘uĭ i‐sheūng, kòh‐wōng kûng‐maaī t‘eng‐mān‐saaì ni‐ti‐īn‐uĕ‐kè shān‐pūk to‐m̄‐fong.
25 Fòng‐ch‘é Leî‐nā‐taan, Teî‐loī‐ngă, k‘āp K‘eī‐mă‐leî, heùng‐kòh‐wōng toî‐k‘eī, k‘aū k‘uĭ m̄‐hó shiu kòh‐kuén‐shue: waī‐haî k‘uĭ m̄‐háng‐t‘eng k‘uĭ shóh‐k‘aū.
26 Kòh‐wōng tsaû fan‐foò wōng ka‐chi‐tsź Yē‐la‐mīt, kûng Aapₒ‐līt‐chi‐tsź Sai‐lāk‐à, k‘āp Aapₒ‐t‘itₒ‐chi‐tsź Shî‐leî‐maĭ, huì chukₒ‐shue‐leî Pa‐lūk, kûng sin‐chi Yē‐leî‐maĭ: waī‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shau‐ts‘ōng k‘uĭ‐teî.
27 ¶ Kòh‐wōng shiu‐mīt kòh‐kuén‐shue, kûng Pa‐lūk ts‘ūng Yē‐leî‐maĭ kòh‐haú shóh‐sé‐ch‘ut‐kè īn‐uĕ chi‐haû, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, kòng‐lām‐ue Yē‐leî‐maĭ, wâ,
28 Iù tsoì‐ning yat‐kuén‐shue, tsoî k‘eī‐chung sé‐saaì ts‘īn‐shī tsoî taî‐yat‐kuén‐shue‐kè īn‐uĕ, tsik‐haî Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam shóh‐shiu‐mīt‐kè.
29 Yaû lûn‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam, iù wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Neĭ ts‘āng shiu‐mīt ni‐kuén‐shue, wâ, Neĭ yan‐hōh tsoî k‘eī‐chung sé‐wâ, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng pit‐tîng ooĭ‐laī‐tsiú‐mīt ts‘ź‐teî, yaû lîng‐yān kûng ch‘uk‐shang tsoî k‘eī‐noî mŏ‐hiu ni?
30 Koò‐ts‘ź Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lûn‐k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam, kóm wâ; K‘uĭ pit‐mŏ‐laū‐lōk‐yān ts‘ŏh Taaî‐waî kòh‐waî‐sheûng: kòh‐shi‐hoī pit‐peî‐wing‐ch‘ut, yāt‐kaan king‐līk īt‐heì, yê‐kaan king‐līk suetₒ‐sheung.
31 Ngŏh pit‐yan k‘uĭ‐kè tsuî‐hin yīng‐fāt k‘uĭ, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè shān‐pūk; ngŏh to‐ooĭ‐k‘aaĭ‐saaì ngŏh shóh‐tîng‐tuēt, kóng‐mīng kung‐kik k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsoi‐wôh, kòng‐lōk k‘uĭ‐teî, uĕ‐k‘āp Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān, kûng shūk Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐chi‐sheûng; waī‐haî k‘uĭ tsúng‐m̄‐t‘eng‐ts‘ūng.
32 Ue‐shî Yē‐leî‐maĭ ning taî‐î kuén‐shue, peí‐kwòh shue‐leî Neī‐leî‐à‐chi‐tsź Pa‐lūk; k‘uĭ tsaû ts‘ūng Yē‐leî‐maĭ‐kè haú, sé‐lōk‐saaì Yaū‐t‘aaì wōng Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kam tsoî fóh‐chung shiu‐mīt kòh‐kuén‐shue‐kè īn‐uĕ: yaû lîng‐ngoî ka‐hó‐toh t‘ūng‐ì‐kè īn‐uĕ.
