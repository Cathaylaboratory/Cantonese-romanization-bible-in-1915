1 NGŎH pit yat‐sam tsaàn‐meĭ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; ī‐ch‘é ch‘uēn‐shūt neĭ shóh‐tsô‐chi‐k‘eī‐sẑ.
2 Chì‐sheûng‐chi‐Chué, ngŏh waî neĭ foon‐heí faaì‐lōk: ngŏh iù koh‐ch‘eùng neĭ mēng.
3 Ngŏh ch‘aū‐tīk t‘uì‐tsaú‐chi‐shī, shî‐pit tsoî neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn titₒ‐tó mīt‐mōng.
4 Neĭ ts‘ŏh‐tsoî ko‐waî, kung‐î shám‐p‘oòn; yă‐ts‘āng shan‐ngŏh‐chi‐uen, leĭ‐ngŏh‐chi‐òn.
5 Neĭ yă‐ts‘āng chaakₒ‐fāt līt‐kwokₒ, tsiú‐mīt okₒ‐yān, wĭng‐uĕn mootₒ‐lat k‘uĭ‐kè mēng.
6 Tīk‐yān tsûn‐teî paaî‐mōng: k‘uĭ‐chi‐shēng‐ch‘ī, neĭ keì mīt‐hiu; k‘uĭ‐chi‐mēng yīk siu‐mīt.
7 Waī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wĭng‐shaì ts‘ŏh‐waî: ch‘itₒ‐laāp ko‐waî laī‐hāng shám‐p‘oòn.
8 I kung‐î shám‐p‘oòn t‘in‐hâ, chiù chìng‐chīk ch‘ī‐leĭ maân‐kwokₒ.
9 Shaû‐wat‐chi‐yān, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pīt tsô k‘uĭ peî‐naân‐chi‐ch‘uè, tong waân‐naân‐chi‐yāt, pit tsô peî‐naân‐chi‐ch‘uè.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, shik neĭ mēng ché, shî‐pit í‐laaî neĭ: yan neĭ m̄‐leī‐hoi faān‐k‘aū‐neĭ‐ché.
11 Koh‐tsûng tsoî Sun‐shaan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: tseung k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng, suen‐ch‘uēn līt‐kwokₒ.
12 Yan‐waî faān laū‐yān‐huetₒ‐ché, k‘uĭ pit ch‘ā‐ch‘ut, pit keì‐chuê: yīk m̄‐mōng‐keì k‘ūng‐foó‐yān‐chi‐kiù‐haàm.
13 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū līn‐măn ngŏh; neĭ yă‐ts‘āng ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh ue sź‐moōn, uē‐kam k‘aū neĭ t‘aí‐hă ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, shóh‐ka ngŏh‐chi‐foó‐naân:
14 Táng ngŏh tsoî Sun‐shēng‐moōn‐ch‘uè laī‐tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ: ī‐ch‘é foon‐heí neĭ‐chi‐ch‘íng‐kaù.
15 Līt‐pong‐chi‐yān, haâm‐lōk tsẑ‐keí shóh‐kwāt‐chi‐haang: k‘uĭ keukₒ ch‘īn‐tsoî tsẑ‐keí shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐chi‐mŏng.
16 Yān kìn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐sheúng‐fāt, tsaû shik‐tak k‘uĭ ch‘ut: okₒ‐yān‐chi‐hāng‐waī, faán‐luî tsẑ‐keí.
17 Okₒ‐yān pit tôh‐lōk yam‐foó, mōng‐keì Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐līt‐pong, yīk t‘ūng yat‐yeûng.
18 P‘ān‐k‘ūng‐chi‐yān, Sheûng‐Taì mŏ wĭng‐uĕn mōng‐keì: kw‘àn‐foó‐chi‐yān, yīk pit mŏ wĭng‐uĕn shat‐mông.
19 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hing‐heí; m̄‐peí shaì‐yān tak‐shìng: uên maân‐kwokₒ tsoî neĭ‐chi‐ts‘īn shaû shám.
20 K‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā lîng līt‐pong húng‐kuî: táng k‘uĭ tsẑ‐keí chi‐tò pat‐kwòh haî faān‐yān.
