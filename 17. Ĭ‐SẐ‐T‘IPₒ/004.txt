1 MŪK‐TAÍ‐KOÍ chi‐tò faān tsô‐shīng‐kè sẑ, tsaû maakₒ‐līt i‐fūk, cheukₒ ts‘o‐mā‐i, mūng fooi ch‘ut‐huì shēng‐chung, taaî‐sheng foó‐huk;
2 Yaû chīk‐tò wōng‐kè moōn‐ts‘īn: yan‐waî cheukₒ ts‘o‐mā‐i‐kè, pat‐hóh yāp wōng‐kè moōn.
3 Wōng‐kè mîng tsik‐haî sheûng‐uê shóh‐tò kokₒ‐shaáng‐chung, Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān yaŭ foó‐yau kàm‐shīk, huk‐yap oi‐t‘ùng; ī‐ch‘é yaŭ hó‐toh fàn‐tsoî mā‐pò kûng fooi‐ch‘uè.
4 Fūk‐sẑ wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ‐kè kung‐nuĭ kûng t‘aaì‐kaàm, tseung ni‐ti‐sẑ laī‐kóng‐kwòh k‘uĭ chi. Wōng‐haû hó‐yau‐shaū; tsaû sùng i‐fūk kwòh‐Mūk‐taí‐koí cheukₒ, iù k‘uĭ t‘uetₒ‐hiu mā‐i: taân k‘uĭ m̄‐háng shaû.
5 Ue‐shî Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ kiù wōng shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐laāp fūk‐sẑ‐k‘uĭ‐kè yat‐kòh t‘aaì‐kaàm, mēng‐kiù Hap‐taāt laī, fan‐foò k‘uĭ huì Mūk‐taí‐koí‐ch‘uè, mân ni‐ti haî mat‐yĕ‐sẑ‐ts‘īng, haî mat‐yĕ‐uēn‐koò.
6 Hap‐taāt tsaû ch‘ut‐tò shēng‐chung‐kè footₒ‐teî, tsik‐haî wōng‐moōn‐chi‐ts‘īn, kìn Mūk‐taí‐koí.
7 Mūk‐taí‐koí tseung tsẑ‐keí faān‐shóh‐uê‐kìn‐kè sẑ, k‘āp Hap‐maân shóh‐ying‐shīng kuen‐shue wōng foò‐kè ngān‐shò, tak tsiú‐mīt Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, kóng‐saaì‐kwòh Hap‐taāt chi.
8 Yīk tseung tsoî Shue‐shaan shóh‐ch‘aau‐sé ch‘uēn‐ch‘ut‐laī, iù‐mīt Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐kè sheûng‐uê‐kó kaau‐kwòh Hap‐taāt, chì k‘uĭ peí Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ t‘aí, yaû t‘okₒ k‘uĭ huèn Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ huì kìn wōng, waî tsẑ‐keí poón‐tsūk‐kè yān haí k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn hán‐k‘aū.
9 Hap‐taāt tsaû faan‐huì, tseung Mūk‐taí‐koí‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, kóng‐kwòh Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ chi.
10 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ tsaû tuì Hap‐taāt kóng, shaí k‘uĭ huì kìn Mūk‐taí‐koí wâ;
11 Wōng shóh‐yaŭ shān‐pūk kûng kokₒ‐shaáng‐kè tsź‐mān, to chi‐tò yaŭ yat‐t‘iū tîng‐laî, yeūk‐m̄‐haî fûng wōng mîng, shîn‐yāp noî‐uên kìn‐wōng‐kè, mō‐lûn naām‐nuĭ, pit iù sź, ch‘uē‐fei wōng kuí‐heí kam‐kwai shaí k‘uĭ tak‐shang‐kè, tsaû m̄‐shaí‐sź: în‐tsoî ngŏh mŏ‐fûng wōng chiû, ĭ‐king saam‐shāp yāt lokₒ.
12 Yān tsaû tseung Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tuì Mūk‐taí‐koí kóng.
13 Mūk‐taí‐koí t‘okₒ k‘uĭ ooī‐fuk Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ wâ, Neĭ maĭ ĭ‐waī tsoî wōng‐kung‐noî, tsoî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān‐chung tūk‐haî neĭ hóh‐ĭ mĭn kà.
14 Ts‘ź‐shī neĭ yeūk tsúng‐m̄‐ch‘ut‐sheng, Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān pit ts‘ūng‐pīt‐ch‘uè‐tak‐ch‘íng‐kaù; tsaû neĭ kûng neĭ foô‐kè ka iù mīt‐tsuēt lokₒ: yaû tím‐chi neĭ keì‐tak haû‐waî, m̄‐haî tsô kam‐yāt‐kè kei‐ooî ni?
15 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ tsaû fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐teî faan‐huì tuì Mūk‐taí‐koí wâ.
16 Neĭ iù huì chiu‐tsaāp Shue‐shaan‐shēng shóh‐yaŭ‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, waî ngŏh kàm‐shīk saam yāt saam yê, tsung‐m̄‐yám m̄‐shīk: ngŏh kûng ngŏh‐kè shiù nuĭ yīk‐kóm‐yeûng kàm‐shīk; īn‐haû ngŏh faân‐laî huì kìn wōng: yeūk iù‐sź tsaû‐sź lokₒ.
17 Kóm‐tsaû Mūk‐taí‐koí huì, i‐chuê Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ faān shóh‐fan‐foò k‘uĭ‐kè laī tsô.
