1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT‐YĀN tsoî Ka‐naām shóh‐tak‐kè teî, tsik‐haî tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, kûng Nuên‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à, k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chi‐p‘aaì‐chung, poón‐ka‐kè‐kokₒ‐tsūk‐cheúng, shóh‐fan‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî tsô ch‘aán‐īp‐kè, līt mīng tsoî haû.
2 Kóh‐ti ch‘aán‐īp, tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ Moh‐sai shaú shóh‐fan‐foò, chap‐ch‘aū fan‐hoi, peí‐kwòh kaú‐kòh chi‐p‘aaì, k‘āp poòn chi‐p‘aaì.
3 Yan‐waî Moh‐sai tsoî Yeukₒ‐taân‐ngoî, ĭ‐king tseung ch‘aán‐īp, ts‘z̀‐kwòh leŭng‐kòh poòn‐chi‐p‘aaì: tūk‐haî tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî chi‐chung mŏ ts‘z̀‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān.
4 Yan‐waî Yeukₒ‐shat‐kè haû‐yuî, fan tsô leŭng‐kòh chi‐p‘aaì, tsik‐haî Mă‐nā‐sai, Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn: shóh‐ĭ m̄‐tseung ch‘aán‐īp ts‘z̀‐kwòh Leî‐meî‐yān, tūk‐haî peí k‘uĭ yaŭ shēng hóh‐ĭ kui‐chuê, k‘āp shēng‐ngoî‐kè kaau‐yĕ, hóh‐ĭ fòng ch‘uk‐shang, ts‘ōng ts‘oī‐māt.
5 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsun‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè, tseung teî fan‐p‘aaì chùng‐yān.
6 ¶ Kóh‐shī, Yaū‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen nuì‐tò Kat‐kaapₒ Yeukₒ‐shue‐à‐ch‘uè: Kei‐neī‐saí‐yān Yē‐foo‐neī‐kè tsaí Ka‐lāk tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî Ka‐t‘itₒ‐pa‐neī‐à, waî neĭ ngŏh tuì Sheûng‐Taì pūk Moh‐sai shóh‐kóng‐kè, neĭ to chi‐tò le.
7 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè pūk Moh‐sai, tá‐faatₒ ngŏh ts‘ūng Ka‐t‘itₒ‐pa‐neī‐à, huì kw‘ai‐t‘aàm Ka‐naām‐teî, kòh‐shī ngŏh sz̀‐shāp suì; ngŏh i ngŏh‐kè poón‐sam laī ooī‐fuk k‘uĭ.
8 Taân‐haî t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh sheŭng‐huì‐kè hing‐taî, shaí paakₒ‐sìng sòng‐sam: waī ngŏh tsûn‐sam tsun‐ts‘ūng ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
9 Tong kóh‐yāt, Moh‐sai faatₒ‐shaî wâ, Neĭ keì haî tsûn‐sam tsun‐ts‘ūng ngŏh Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaû neĭ keukₒ shóh‐taāp‐kè teî‐fong, pit wĭng‐uĕn kwai neĭ kûng neĭ tsź‐suen tsô ch‘aán‐īp.
10 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā chiù‐i shóh‐ying‐shīng, pó‐ts‘uēn ngŏh shang‐mîng ĭ‐king yaŭ sz̀‐shāp‐nğ nīn lokₒ, tsik‐haî ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung ni‐ti shuetₒ‐wâ, tuì Moh‐sai kóng, tong Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān hāng kw‘òng‐yĕ kóh‐shī: în‐shī ngŏh paatₒ‐shāp‐nğ suì lokₒ.
11 ¶ Kam‐yāt ngŏh‐kè līk yīng‐īn chòng‐kîn, hó‐ts‘z̆ Moh‐sai tá‐faatₒ ngŏh huì chi‐shī, waāk sheŭng‐chân, waāk ch‘ut‐yāp: uē‐kam ngŏh‐kè līk to haî kóm.
12 Koò‐ts‘ź neĭ iù tseung ni‐kòh shaan ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh, tsik‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tong yāt shóh‐kóng‐kè; kòh‐yāt neĭ t‘eng‐mān tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè yaŭ À‐naāp‐yān, ī‐ch‘é yaŭ ko‐taaî‐kin‐koò‐kè shēng; yeūk‐haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh, tsaû ngŏh‐kè līk pit‐nāng kón‐chūk k‘uĭ‐teî, chiù Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kóng‐kè.
13 Ue‐shî Yeukₒ‐shue‐à chuk‐fuk Yē‐foo‐neī‐kè tsaí Ka‐lāk, tseung Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ tsô ch‘aán‐īp.
14 Shóh‐ĭ Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn tsô‐hiu Kei‐neī‐saí‐yān Yē‐foo‐neī‐kè tsaí Ka‐lāk‐kè ch‘aán‐īp, chì‐tò kam‐yāt, yan‐waî k‘uĭ tsûn‐sam tsun‐ts‘ūng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à.
15 Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn mēng kiù Kei‐līt‐à‐pa; ni‐kòh À‐pa, p‘īng‐sò tsoî À‐naāp‐yān‐chung, haî chì‐tsuen‐taaî‐kè. Haû‐loī kóh‐ch‘uè teî‐fong on‐tsîng, mŏ tá‐cheùng lokₒ.
