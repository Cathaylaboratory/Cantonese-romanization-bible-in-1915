1 SHĒNG‐TS‘EŪNG keì tsô‐shīng, ngŏh yaû ĭ‐king on moōn‐shìn, laāp‐hiu hon‐shaú‐moōn‐kè, kûng au‐koh‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp Leî‐meî‐yān, ts‘ź‐sẑ shīng‐tsaû,
2 Ngŏh p‘aaì‐waí ngŏh‐kè hing‐taî Hap‐nā‐neī, kûng kung‐tsoí Hap‐nā‐neī‐à, koón‐leĭ Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng: yan‐waî Hap‐nā‐neī‐à haî chung‐sùn‐kè yān, ī‐ch‘é kìng‐waì Sheûng‐Taì, chùng‐yān to m̄‐k‘āp‐tak k‘uĭ.
3 Ngŏh tsaû tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Táng‐toî yāt‐t‘aū īt chi‐shī, īn‐haû hoi Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng shēng‐moōn, yīng‐īn k‘eĭ‐laāp hon‐shaú chi‐shī; k‘uĭ‐teî iù kwaan‐moōn, neĭ‐teî iù sheŭng‐shaan: yīk iù p‘aaì‐waí hon‐shaú Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐kè kui‐mān, kokₒ‐òn paan‐ts‘z̀ fōng‐shaú, kokₒ‐shaú kokₒ‐ka tuì‐mîn‐kòh‐ch‘uè.
4 K‘eī‐shēng kwóng‐taaî: taân shēng‐chung‐kè tsź‐mān hei‐shiú, fōng‐uk chûng meî‐ts‘āng heí.
5 Ngŏh‐kè Sheûng‐Taì kóm‐tûng ngŏh sam, chiu‐tsaāp tseukₒ‐waî koon‐cheúng kûng tsź‐mān, táng chiù‐i k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsūk‐p‘ó laī tím‐mēng. Ngŏh wán‐tó taî‐yat‐ts‘z̀ sheŭng‐laī‐kè tsūk‐p‘ó, tsoî tsūk‐p‘ó yaŭ sé‐lōk,
6 Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, sik‐yāt shóh‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè, tsik‐haî chuê‐tsoî shaáng‐noî peî‐lŏ‐leūk‐kè tsź‐mān, peî‐shik‐fòng faan‐laī Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng kûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐teî, kokₒ kwai poón‐shēng, tsik‐haî t‘ūng‐maaī Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ;
7 Yē‐shue‐à, Neī‐hei‐maĭ, À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à, La‐maĭ, Nā‐hap‐mă‐neī, Mūk‐taí‐koí, Pit‐shaan, Māt‐paatₒ, Pit‐waai, Leĭ‐wāng, Pa‐nā. Faan laī Ĭ‐shik‐līt tsź‐mān‐kè shò‐mūk, līt‐mēng tsoî‐haû;
8 Pa‐lūk‐kè tsź‐suen, î‐ts‘in‐yat‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐î yān.
9 Shî‐faatₒ‐t‘aī‐à‐kè tsź‐suen, saam‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐î yān.
10 À‐la‐kè tsź‐suen, lūk‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐î yān.
11 Pa‐hap‐moh‐aapₒ‐kè tsź‐suen, shūk Yē‐shue‐à kûng Yeukₒ‐aapₒ‐kè tsź‐suen, î‐ts‘in‐paatₒ‐paakₒ‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
12 Ĭ‐laān‐kè tsź‐suen, yat‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐sz̀ yān.
13 Saatₒ‐t‘ó‐kè tsź‐suen, paatₒ‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐nğ yān.
14 Saatₒ‐koí‐kè tsź‐suen, ts‘at‐paakₒ‐lūk‐shāp yān.
15 Pán‐noî‐kè tsź‐suen, lūk‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
16 Peí‐paaì‐kè tsź‐suen, lūk‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
17 Aapₒ‐kaapₒ‐kè tsź‐suen, î‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐î yān.
18 À‐toh‐neī‐kon‐kè tsź‐suen, lūk‐paakₒ‐lūk‐shāp‐ts‘at yān.
19 Pit‐waai‐kè tsź‐suen, î‐ts‘in‐lūk‐shāp‐ts‘at yān.
20 À‐ting‐kè tsź‐suen, lūk‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐nğ yān.
21 À‐tāk‐kè tsź‐suen, shūk Hei‐sai‐ka‐kè tsź‐suen, kaú‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
22 Hap‐shûn‐kè tsź‐suen, saam‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
23 Peí‐ts‘oì‐kè tsź‐suen, saam‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐sz̀ yān.
24 Hap‐la‐kè tsź‐suen, yat‐paakₒ‐shāp‐î yān.
25 Kei‐p‘ìn‐kè tsź‐suen, kaú‐shāp‐nğ yān.
26 Paakₒ‐leî‐hāng, k‘āp Neī‐t‘ōh‐faatₒ‐kè yān, kûng yat‐paakₒ‐paatₒ‐shāp yān.
27 À‐nā‐tāt‐yān, yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐paatₒ.
28 Paakₒ‐à‐sz‐mă‐fat‐yān sz̀‐shāp‐î.
29 Kei‐līt‐yē‐lām, kûng Kei‐fei‐la, k‘āp Peì‐lūk‐yān, kûng ts‘at‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐saam.
30 La‐mă, k‘āp Ka‐pa‐yān, kûng lūk‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐yat.
31 Māk‐mă‐yān, yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐î.
32 Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî k‘āp Oi‐yān, kûng yat‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐saam.
33 Taî‐î‐kòh Neī‐p‘òh‐yān, nğ‐shāp‐î.
34 Taî‐î‐kòh Ĭ‐laān‐kè tsź‐suen, yat‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐sz̀ yān.
35 Hap‐lām‐kè tsź‐suen, saam‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp yān.
36 Yē‐leî‐koh‐kè tsź‐suen, saam‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐nğ yān.
37 Lūt Hap‐tāk k‘āp Oh‐nōh‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng ts‘at‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp‐yat yān.
38 Sai‐nā‐kè tsź‐suen, saam‐ts‘in‐kaú‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp yān.
39 Tsaì‐sz yaŭ shūk Yē‐shue‐à ka Yē‐taí‐à‐kè tsź‐suen, kaú‐paakₒ‐ts‘at‐shāp‐saam yān.
40 Yam‐māk‐kè tsź‐suen, yat‐ts‘in‐nğ‐shāp‐î yān.
41 Pa‐shūt‐kè tsź‐suen, yat‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐ts‘at yān.
42 Hap‐lām‐kè tsź‐suen, yat‐ts‘in‐shāp‐ts‘at yān.
43 Leî‐meî‐yān yaŭ Hōh‐taaî‐wai shūk Yē‐shue‐à kûng Kaapₒ‐mīt‐kè tsź‐suen, ts‘at‐shāp‐sz̀ yān.
44 Au‐koh‐kè yaŭ À‐saatₒ‐kè tsź‐suen, yat‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
45 Hon‐shaú‐moōn‐kè yaŭ Sha‐lūng‐kè tsź‐suen, À‐tāk‐kè tsź‐suen, Taāt‐moōn‐kè tsź‐suen, À‐kuk‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐taí‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen, Shuetₒ‐p‘aaī‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng yat‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐paatₒ yān.
46 Tîn‐uk fūk‐yīk‐ché yaŭ Sai‐hap‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐so‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen, Taapₒ‐pūk‐kè tsź‐suen,
47 K‘eī‐lūk‐kè tsź‐suen, Sai‐à‐kè tsź‐suen, Pa‐tûn‐kè tsź‐suen,
48 Leî‐pa‐nā‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐ka‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen, Saatₒ‐maaĭ‐kè tsź‐suen,
49 Hap‐naān‐kè tsź‐suen, Kat‐tāk‐kè tsź‐suen, Ka‐hap‐kè tsź‐suen,
50 Leî‐à‐ngă‐kè tsź‐suen, Leĭ‐sùn‐kè tsź‐suen, Neī‐koh‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen,
51 Ka‐saàn‐kè tsź‐suen, Oo‐saatₒ‐kè tsź‐suen, Pa‐sai‐à‐kè tsź‐suen,
52 Peí‐ts‘oì‐kè tsź‐suen, Maĭ‐oô‐nīng‐kè tsź‐suen, Neī‐p‘ó‐sam‐kè tsź‐suen,
53 Paatₒ‐pat‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐kat‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐fat‐kè tsź‐suen.
54 Paatₒ‐lūk‐kè tsź‐suen, Maĭ‐hei‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐sha‐kè tsź‐suen,
55 Paatₒ‐kuk‐kè tsź‐suen, Sai‐sai‐la‐kè tsź‐suen, Taaî‐mă‐kè tsź‐suen,
56 Neī‐ts‘ē‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐taí‐pa‐kè tsź‐suen.
57 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn chùng‐pūk‐kè tsź‐suen: yaŭ Sóh‐t‘aaì‐kè tsź‐suen, Sóh‐peí‐līt‐kè tsź‐suen, Peí‐lô‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen,
58 Ngă‐la‐kè tsź‐suen, Taāt‐kwan‐kè tsź‐suen, Kat‐tāk‐kè tsź‐suen,
59 Shî‐faatₒ‐t‘aī‐à‐kè tsź‐suen, Hap‐tik‐kè tsź‐suen, shūk Sai‐pa‐yam‐kè P‘òh‐kei‐lāk‐kè tsź‐suen, À‐maĭ‐kè tsź‐suen.
60 Taân‐faān tîn‐uk fūk‐yīk‐ché, kûng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn chùng‐pūk‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng saam‐paakₒ‐kaú‐shāp‐î yān,
61 Ts‘ūng Tāk‐maĭ‐la, Tāk‐hap‐saatₒ, Kei‐lūk, Aapₒ‐taân, Yam‐māk: shóh‐sheŭng‐laī‐kè, pat‐nāng piú‐mīng k‘uĭ foô‐ka tsung‐tsūk, haî shūk Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān m̄‐haî.
62 Ts‘ź‐yān haî Teî‐loī‐à‐kè tsź‐suen, Toh‐peí‐kè tsź‐suen, Neī‐koh‐taaî‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng lūk‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐î yān.
63 Tsaì‐sz‐chung yaŭ Hap‐pa‐ngă‐kè tsź‐suen, Koh‐sẑ‐kè tsź‐suen, Pa‐sai‐loī‐kè tsź‐suen, k‘uĭ ts‘uì Kei‐līt‐yān Pa‐sai‐loī‐kè nuĭ tsô ts‘ai, koò‐ts‘ź k‘uĭ k‘aaĭ Pa‐sai‐loī‐kè mēng tsô mēng à.
64 Ts‘ź‐ka‐kè yān, haí tsūk‐p‘ó shóh‐tsoì‐chi‐chung ch‘ā‐kwòh, wán tsẑ‐keí‐kè uēn‐tsīk to m̄‐kìn: koò‐ts‘ź chùng‐yān ĭ‐waī k‘uĭ m̄‐kitₒ‐tsîng, m̄‐chún k‘uĭ‐teî tong tsaì‐sz‐kè chik‐yīk.
65 Fong‐paakₒ tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Pat‐hóh shīk chì‐shìng‐kè māt, chì‐tò yaŭ Oo‐līng kûng T‘ó‐mīng chi‐tsaì‐sz k‘eĭ‐laāp‐kè.
66 Ooî‐chùng kûng‐kaì yaŭ sz̀‐maân‐î‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ‐lūk‐shāp yān.
67 Ts‘ź‐ngoî chûng‐yaŭ k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè pūk‐p‘eĭ, ts‘at‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐ts‘at yān: k‘eī‐chung yaŭ au‐koh‐kè naām‐nuĭ, î‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp‐nğ yān.
68 K‘uĭ‐teî yaŭ mă, ts‘at‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐lūk chekₒ: lōh, î‐paakₒ‐sz̀‐shāp nğ chekₒ:
69 Lokₒ‐t‘ōh, sz̀‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp‐nğ chekₒ: luī, lūk‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ‐î‐shāp chekₒ.
70 Yaŭ‐ti tsó‐foô ka‐cheúng, waî kung‐tsokₒ kuen‐chôh. Fong‐paakₒ kuen kam yat‐ts‘in taāt‐leĭ‐hak, p‘oōn, nğ‐shāp‐kòh, tsaì‐sz laĭ‐fūk, nğ‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp kîn, kaau‐kwòh foò‐fōng.
71 Yaû‐yaŭ tsó‐foô ka‐cheúng, kuen kam î‐maân taāt‐leĭ‐hak, ngān î‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ meī‐nā, kaau‐kwòh kung‐tsokₒ‐kè foò‐fōng.
72 K‘eī‐uē tsź‐mān shóh‐kuen‐kè kam, î‐maân taāt‐leĭ‐hak, ngān î‐ts‘in neī‐nā, tsaì‐sz‐kè laĭ‐fūk, lūk‐shāp‐ts‘at kîn.
73 Kóm‐tsaû tsaì‐sz Leî‐meî‐yān shaú‐moōn‐kè, au‐koh‐kè, yaŭ‐ti tsź‐mān kûng tîn fūk‐yīk‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān, kaai chuê tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè poón‐shēng. Tò‐hiu taî‐ts‘at uēt, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ĭ‐king tsoî k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè poón‐shēng.
