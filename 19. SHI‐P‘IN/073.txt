1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT‐YĀN sam‐chung ts‘ing‐kitₒ‐ché, Sheûng‐Taì shāt‐tsoî shi‐yan toî k‘uĭ.
2 Taan‐haî ngŏh kei‐oō shat‐tsuk; kei‐oō titₒ‐tó.
3 Ngŏh kìn kiu‐ngô‐chi‐yān tak‐fuk‐lūk, hung‐okₒ‐chi‐yān tak‐p‘īng‐on, ngŏh hó‐pat‐p‘īng.
4 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ tò sź to mŏ foó‐naân: heì‐līk yīk chòng‐kîn.
5 Pīt‐yān‐chi‐foó‐ch‘óh k‘uĭ mŏ fân; pīt‐yān‐chi‐faān‐moôn k‘uĭ mō‐kon.
6 Koò‐ts‘ź k‘uĭ kiu‐ngô‐chi‐sam, hó‐ts‘z̆ kéng‐sheûng taaì‐tak kam‐huen; k‘uĭ‐chi‐k‘eūng‐waāng, hó‐ts‘z̆ shan‐sheûng cheukₒ hó‐i‐fūk.
7 K‘uĭ shan‐t‘aí feī‐yûn, sheung‐ngaăn tāt‐ch‘ut: k‘uĭ‐chi‐shóh‐tak, toh‐kwòh k‘uĭ sam‐chi‐shóh seúng.
8 Ni‐ti‐yān k‘aaĭ okₒ‐īn laī‐tsô siù‐wâ: kiu‐ngô laī‐kóng k‘eūng‐waāng‐chi‐īn.
9 K‘uĭ hoi‐haú waí‐p‘òng t‘in, iū‐shīt nô‐mâ teî.
10 Koò‐ts‘ź Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐mān, chuèn‐heùng k‘uĭ‐teî: sam‐chung foon‐uēt, uē kéng‐hotₒ‐chi‐yān, t‘aam‐yám toh shuí, tak shuí yám‐paaú.
11 Tsaû wâ, Sheûng‐Taì tím chi‐tò ni? Chì‐Sheûng‐Chi‐Chué, tím‐shik‐tak ni?
12 Ni‐ti okₒ‐yān, faán‐tak wĭng‐uĕn shûn‐leî; k‘uĭ ts‘oī‐māt yāt‐yāt ka‐tsang.
13 Waī‐haî ngŏh kitₒ‐tsîng kòh‐sam, yīk shî t‘ō‐lō, kitₒ‐tsîng tuì‐shaú, yīk shî mō yīk.
14 Yan ngŏh shīng‐yāt shaû‐foó, chiu‐chiu shaû‐chaakₒ.
15 Ngŏh yeūk‐haî seúng kóm‐yeûng kóng‐kwòh‐yān‐chi; pîn shî tuì neĭ‐chi‐tsź‐mān m̄‐chuê.
16 Ngŏh seúng mīng‐paāk ni‐kòh tô‐leĭ, kìn hó‐naān‐kaaí;
17 Táng‐tò ngŏh yāp Sheûng‐Taì shìng‐tîn‐chi‐shī; chì‐hiú‐tak k‘uĭ‐teî tò‐taí tím‐yeûng.
18 Neĭ chai k‘uĭ‐teî tsoî waāt‐teî: táng k‘uĭ haâm‐tsoî mīt‐mōng‐chi‐chung.
19 K‘íng‐haak‐kaan, tsaû paaî‐waaî! tò‐taí haî peî‐king‐fong shóh‐mīt‐mōng.
20 Sheûng‐Taì à, neĭ hing‐heí‐chi‐shī, hó‐ts‘z̆ faatₒ‐mûng fàn‐síng; t‘aí‐hing k‘uĭ faū‐wā.
21 Ngŏh sam‐chung paì‐aì, ch‘eūng‐uē‐to‐kotₒ.
22 Ngŏh uē‐ch‘ún mō‐chi: tsoî neĭ‐chi‐ts‘īn, hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘ām‐shaù yat‐yeûng.
23 Taân‐haî ngŏh sheūng‐shī t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ: neĭ yûng yaû‐shaú foō‐ch‘ī ngŏh.
24 Neĭ pit yûng kaaù‐fàn yăn‐tô ngŏh, īn‐haû tsipₒ ngŏh yāp wīng‐iû‐chi‐chung.
25 Ch‘uē‐neĭ‐chi‐ngoî, tsoî t‘in ngŏh mō‐shóh‐yeŭng‐mông? tsoî‐teî ngŏh mō‐shóh‐foon‐uēt.
26 Ngŏh‐chi‐shan‐t‘aí, uĕ‐k‘āp ngŏh sam, to ooĭ faì‐tsûn: waī‐haî Sheûng‐Taì tsô ngŏh p‘oōn‐shēk, yaû tsô ngŏh wĭng‐uĕn ch‘aán‐īp.
27 Uĕn‐leī‐neĭ‐ché, shî‐pit paaî‐mōng: tsùng‐sz‐yūk leī‐hoi‐neĭ‐ché, neĭ pit mīt k‘uĭ.
28 Waī‐haî ngŏh ĭ ts‘an‐kân Sheûng‐Taì waī‐fuk: ngŏh sheūng‐shī í‐laaî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tseung neĭ shóh‐yaŭ‐hāng‐waī laī‐suen‐ch‘uēn.
