1 TAAÎ‐WAÎ wōng shaû‐ko nīn‐lŏ; tsaû k‘aaĭ ts‘ám‐i che‐k‘oì, yīk m̄‐tak‐nuĕn.
2 Koò‐ts‘ź k‘uĭ shān‐pūk tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Pat‐uē waî ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng, wán yat‐kòh t‘ūng‐nuĭ: shaí k‘uĭ k‘eĭ‐tsoî wōng mîn‐ts‘īn kung‐yeŭng; ī‐ch‘é fàn‐tsoî neĭ waaī‐chung, lîng ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng tak‐nuĕn lokₒ.
3 Kóm‐tsaû tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt ts‘uēn‐kíng‐noî laī wán meĭ‐maaû‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ, tsaû wán‐tak yat‐kòh Shue‐nîm‐kè t‘ūng‐nuĭ mēng‐kiù À‐peì‐shaatₒ, yaû taaì‐k‘uĭ‐tò wōng‐ch‘uè.
4 Ni‐kòh‐t‘ūng‐nuĭ kīk‐chi‐meĭ‐maaû, tsaû laī kung‐yeŭng fūk‐sẑ wōng: waī‐haî wōng m̄‐ts‘an‐kân k‘uĭ à.
5 Tong‐shī Hap‐k‘āp‐kè tsaí À‐toh‐neī‐ngă tsẑ‐tsuen wâ, Ngŏh iù tsô wōng: tsaû waî tsẑ‐keí uê‐peî keí shîng‐ch‘e kûng k‘ē‐mă‐kè yān, k‘āp ts‘īn‐t‘aū pan‐tsaú‐kè nğ‐shāp‐yān.
6 K‘uĭ foô‐ts‘an heùng‐loī meî‐yaŭ lîng k‘uĭ yau‐sam wâ, Neĭ tsô‐mat kóm‐yeûng hāng ni? k‘uĭ mîn‐maaû kīk‐hó; shang tsoî Aapₒ‐sha‐lūng chi‐haû.
7 K‘uĭ kûng Sai‐lŏ‐ngă‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐aapₒ k‘āp tsaì‐sz À‐peì‐à‐t‘aapₒ sheung‐leūng: ts‘ź‐leŭng‐yān kan‐ts‘ūng À‐toh‐neī‐ngă pong‐chôh k‘uĭ.
8 Taân tsaì‐sz Saatₒ‐tuk, kûng Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî‐kè tsaí Peì‐nā‐ngă, sin‐chi Nā‐taan, Shî‐mooĭ, Leĭ‐ĭ, k‘āp shūk Taaî‐waî‐kè yŭng‐sẑ, yat‐k‘oì to m̄‐pong‐chôh À‐toh‐neī‐ngă.
9 À‐toh‐neī‐ngă haí Yán‐lōh‐kitₒ, kân Sóh‐hei‐līt p‘oōn‐shēk, t‘ong yeūng kûng ngaū k‘āp feī‐ch‘uk‐shang, ts‘éng k‘uĭ shóh‐yaŭ‐kè hing‐taî, tsik‐haî wōng‐kè chùng‐tsaí, k‘āp tsô wōng shān‐pūk‐kè Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān:
10 Taân m̄‐ts‘éng sin‐chi Nā‐taan, k‘āp Peì‐nā‐ngă, kûng chùng‐yŭng‐sẑ, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè hing‐taî Shóh‐lōh‐moōn.
11 Ue‐shî Nā‐taan tuì Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè mŏ‐ts‘an Pāt‐shî‐pa wâ, Hap‐k‘āp‐kè tsaí À‐toh‐neī‐ngă tsô wōng, neĭ m̄‐ts‘āng t‘eng‐mān me? Ngŏh chué Taaî‐waî yīk m̄‐chi‐tò.
12 Koò‐ts‘ź uên neĭ chún ngŏh waî neĭ ch‘itₒ‐faatₒ, chì neĭ hóh‐nāng pó‐ts‘uēn tsẑ‐keí k‘āp neĭ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐kè shang‐mîng.
13 Iù yāp‐huì kìn Taaî‐waî wōng, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng à, neĭ heí‐m̄‐haî tuì neĭ p‘eĭ faatₒ‐shaî wâ, Neĭ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, pit kaì ngŏh tsô wōng, ts‘ŏh ngŏh waî me? yeūk‐haî kóm, waî‐mat À‐toh‐neī‐ngă tsô wōng ni?
14 T‘aí‐a, neĭ haí kóh‐ch‘uè kûng wōng kóng chi‐shī, ngŏh tsaû yāp‐laī, chiù‐i neĭ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ kóng‐kwòh wōng chi.
15 Pāt‐shî‐pa yāp shat tò wōng‐ch‘uè: wōng nīn‐keí hó‐lŏ‐taaî; Shue‐nîm‐kè nuĭ À‐peì‐shaatₒ fūk‐sẑ wōng.
16 Pāt‐shî‐pa oò‐tai‐t‘aū kìng‐paaì wōng. Wōng wâ, Neĭ seúng k‘aū mat‐yĕ ni?
17 K‘uĭ tuì wōng wâ, Ngŏh chué à, neĭ yă‐ts‘āng heùng neĭ p‘eĭ‐nuĭ, chí‐chuê neĭ Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā faatₒ‐shaî, wâ, Neĭ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn pit ts‘ŏh ngŏh‐kè waî, kaì‐chuê ngŏh tsô wōng.
18 T‘aí‐a, în‐shī À‐toh‐neī‐ngă tsô wōng là; ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng, neĭ to m̄‐chi‐tò:
19 K‘uĭ ĭ‐king t‘ong hó‐toh ngaū yeūng k‘āp feī‐ch‘uk, yaû ts‘éng wōng‐kè chùng‐tsź, pìng tsaì‐sz, À‐peì‐à‐t‘aapₒ kûng kwan‐cheúng Yeukₒ‐aapₒ: waī‐haî mŏ ts‘éng neĭ pūk Shóh‐lōh‐moōn.
20 Ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng à, Ĭ‐shik‐līt chùng‐yān‐kè ngaăn mông‐chuê neĭ, seúng neĭ wâ, mat‐shuī kaì ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng, ts‘ŏh‐waî.
21 Yeūk m̄‐haî, tsaû ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng, kûng līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám chi‐shī, ngŏh kûng ngŏh tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, pit tòng‐waī tsuî‐yān à.
22 T‘aí‐a, Pāt‐shî‐pa kûng wōng kóng chi‐shī, sin‐chi Nā‐taan yāp‐laī.
23 Yaŭ‐yān tsaù‐chi wōng wâ, Sin‐chi Nā‐taan tò‐laī. Nā‐taan yāp‐tò wōng‐mîn‐ts‘īn, tsaû pūk‐tó teî‐mîn‐ch‘uè.
24 Nā‐taan tsaù‐wâ, Ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng, neĭ kwóh‐īn wâ‐kwòh À‐toh‐neī‐ngă tsoî‐haû pit kaì ngŏh tsô wōng, ts‘ŏh ngŏh waî me?
25 Yan k‘uĭ kam‐yāt ĭ‐king lōk‐huì, t‘ong hó‐toh ngaū‐yeūng kûng feī‐ch‘uk‐shang, yaû ts‘éng wōng‐kè chùng‐tsź, k‘āp kwan‐ping‐kè chùng‐tseùng, kûng tsaì‐sz À‐peì‐à‐t‘aapₒ; t‘aí‐a, k‘uĭ‐teî haí k‘uĭ mîn‐ts‘īn yám‐shīk, tsaû wâ, Uên À‐toh‐neī‐ngă wōng maân‐suì à.
26 Taân ngŏh tsik‐haî neĭ‐kè pūk kûng tsaì‐sz Saatₒ‐tuk, k‘āp Yē‐hōh yē‐taaî‐kè tsaí Peì‐nā‐ngă, t‘ūng neĭ pūk Shóh‐lōh‐moōn to mŏ ts‘éng à.
27 Ni‐ti‐sẑ haî ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng shóh‐tsô m̄‐haî ni, waī meî‐ts‘āng chí‐shî neĭ pūk, mat‐shuī kaì ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng, ts‘ŏh kòh‐waî ni?
28 Taaî‐waî wōng taapₒ wâ, Kiù Pāt‐shî‐pa tò ngŏh‐ch‘uè. K‘uĭ tsaû yāp‐tò wōng‐ch‘uè, k‘eĭ‐tó wōng‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn.
29 Wōng tsaû faatₒ‐shaî wâ, Kaù ngŏh līng‐wān ch‘ut shóh‐yaŭ‐kè waân‐naân, kóh‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shang‐oōt‐kè.
30 Ngŏh yă‐ts‘āng chí‐chuê Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heùng neĭ faatₒ‐shaî wâ, Neĭ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tsoî ngŏh chi‐haû, pit ts‘ŏh ngŏh kòh‐waî, kaì ngŏh tsô wōng; ngŏh kam‐yāt pit‐tîng chiù‐i laī hāng.
31 Pāt‐shî‐pa tsaû oò‐tai‐t‘aū pūk‐mîn tò‐teî‐ch‘uè kìng‐paaì wōng, wâ, Uên ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng Taaî‐waî wĭng‐shang à.
32 Taaî‐waî wōng tsaû wâ, Kiù tsaì‐sz Saatₒ‐tuk, kûng sin‐chi Nā‐taan, k‘āp Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî‐kè tsaí Peì‐nā‐ngă laī ngŏh‐ch‘uè. K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû laī‐tò wōng mîn‐ts‘īn.
33 Wōng tsaû tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Iù t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ‐teî, taaì‐lĭng neĭ chué‐kè chùng‐pūk, lîng ngŏh tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn k‘ē ngŏh tsẑ‐keí‐kè luī, taaì k‘uĭ lōk‐tò K‘eī‐fàn:
34 Haí kòh‐ch‘uè tsaì‐sz Saatₒ‐tuk, kûng sin‐chi Nā‐taan, iù kò ku‘ĭ tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè wōng: ī‐ch‘é neĭ‐teî ià ch‘ui‐kokₒ wâ, Uên Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng maân‐suì à.
35 Īa haû iù kan‐ts‘ui k‘uĭ sheŭng‐laī, k‘uĭ pit laī‐ts‘ŏh ngŏh waî; yan‐waî k‘uĭ pit kaì ngŏh tsô wōng: ngŏh ĭ‐king laāp k‘uĭ tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt k‘āp Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè kwan à.
36 Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî‐kè tsaí Peì‐nā‐ngă tuì wōng wâ, Shīng‐sam‐shóh‐uên la: uên ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yīk haî kóm‐yeûng wâ lokₒ.
37 Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘ūng‐maaī Shóh‐lōh‐moōn, hó‐ts‘z̆ yă‐ts‘āng t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng yat‐yeûng; ī‐ch‘é lîng k‘uĭ kwokₒ‐waî, kàng‐taaî‐kwòh ngŏh chué, ngŏh wōng Taaî‐waî‐kè waî à.
38 Kóm‐tsaû tsaì‐sz Saatₒ‐tuk, sin‐chi Nā‐taan, Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî‐kè tsaí Peì‐nā‐ngă, kûng shî‐waî k‘āp sẑ‐fûng‐kè yān, lōk‐huì ts‘éng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn k‘ē‐sheŭng Taaî‐waî wōng‐kè luī, taaì k‘uĭ tò K‘eī‐fàn.
39 Tsaì‐sz Saatₒ‐tuk tsaû ts‘ūng tîn‐mōk‐chung ning moŏn‐ko‐kè kokₒ, laī‐kò Shóh‐lōh‐moōn. K‘uĭ‐teî tsaû ch‘ui‐kokₒ; chùng‐tsź‐mān yat‐ts‘aī wâ, Uên wōng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn maân‐suì à.
40 Chùng‐tsź‐mān kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ sheŭng‐huì; ī‐ch‘é ch‘ui‐tīk taaî‐foon‐lōk, chì sheng‐yam līt teî à.
41 À‐toh‐neī‐ngă kûng k‘uĭ shóh‐ts‘éng‐kè chùng‐haakₒ, kong‐ngaam yám‐shīk‐uēn, tsaû t‘eng‐mān ni‐ti‐sẑ lokₒ. Yeukₒ‐aapₒ t‘eng‐mān‐ch‘ui‐kokₒ‐sheng‐chi‐shī tsaû wâ, Shēng‐chung waî‐mat‐sẑ yaŭ kòm ts‘ō‐tsaāp‐kè sheng ni?
42 K‘uĭ sheûng‐kóng chi‐shī, tsaì‐sz À‐peì‐à‐t‘aapₒ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan laī‐tò: À‐toh‐neī‐ngă tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Yāp‐laī la; yan neĭ haî chung‐î‐kè yān, pit‐haî pò hó‐yam kwòh‐ngŏh‐t‘eng à.
43 Yeukₒ‐nā‐taan taapₒ À‐toh‐neī‐ngă wâ, Chan‐haî ngŏh‐teî‐kè chué Taaî‐waî, ĭ‐king laāp Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tsô wōng lokₒ.
44 Wōng tá‐faatₒ tsaì‐sz Saatₒ‐tuk, sin‐chi Nā‐taan, Yē‐hōh‐yē‐taaî‐kè tsaí Peì‐nā‐ngă, k‘āp shî‐waî fûng‐sẑ‐kè yān, t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ huì, tsaû lîng k‘uĭ k‘ē wōng‐kè luī:
45 Yaû tsaì‐sz Saatₒ‐tuk kûng sin‐chi Nā‐taan, tsoî K‘eī‐fàn ĭ‐king kò k‘uĭ tsô wōng: chùng‐yān ts‘ūng K‘eī‐fàn sheŭng‐huì; ī‐ch‘é foon‐lōk, chì‐tò t‘ung shēng chàn‐tûng‐chi‐sheng‐yam. Ni‐ti haî neĭ t‘eng‐mān‐kè sheng‐yam à.
46 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn yīk tang‐kwokₒ‐kè waî.
47 Kim‐chi wōng‐kè chùng‐pūk, laī chuk‐fuk ngŏh chué Taaî‐waî wōng‐wâ, Uên Sheûng‐Taì lîng Shóh‐lōh‐moōn‐chi‐mēng, peí neĭ‐kè mēng kàng‐tsuen‐wīng, lîng k‘uĭ‐kè kwokₒ‐waî, peí neĭ‐kè kwokₒ‐waî kàng‐hing‐shîng. Wōng tsoî ch‘ōng‐sheûng oò‐tai‐t‘aū.
48 Wōng yaû kóm wâ, Chuk‐tsê Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, yan‐waî ĭ‐king ts‘z̀‐yat‐yān, kam‐yāt ts‘ŏh ngŏh‐kè waî, ī‐ch‘é lîng ngŏh ts‘an‐ngaăn t‘aí‐kìn à.
49 T‘ūng‐maaī À‐toh‐neī‐ngă‐kè chùng‐haakₒ king‐fong, tsaû heí‐laī kokₒ‐kwai tsẑ‐keí‐kè lô.
50 À‐toh‐neī‐ngă yan Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tsaû kuî‐p‘à‐heí‐laī, huì cha‐chuê t‘aān‐kokₒ.
51 Yaŭ‐yān tuì Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wâ, T‘aí‐a, À‐toh‐neī‐ngă kuî‐p‘à Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng: yan kìn k‘uĭ huì cha‐chuê t‘aān‐kokₒ, wâ, Uên Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng kam‐yāt waî ngŏh faatₒ‐shaî, ying‐shīng m̄‐k‘aaĭ to‐kìm shaatₒ k‘uĭ pūk‐yān à.
52 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wâ, Yeūk kìn k‘uĭ tsô chung‐î‐kè yān, k‘uĭ yat‐t‘iū t‘aū‐faatₒ pit m̄‐lōk‐tò teî‐ch‘uè: yeūk kìn k‘uĭ hāng‐okₒ, tsaû pit‐tîng iù sź.
53 Kóm‐tsaû Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng, tá‐faatₒ yān taaì k‘uĭ ts‘ūng t‘aān lōk‐laī. À‐toh‐neī‐ngă laī‐tò, haí Shóh‐lōh‐moōn wōng mîn‐ts‘īn oò‐tai‐t‘aū: Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tsaû tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Hóh‐ĭ faan‐huì neĭ ka la.
