1 KÓM‐TSAÛ wōng kûng Hap‐maân foô wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ kè ìn.
2 Wōng ts‘z̀‐yāt ts‘ŏh īn‐tsīk chi‐shī, yaû mân Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ wâ, Haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ à, neĭ yaŭ seúng pán‐mat‐yĕ ni? ngŏh pit ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ: neĭ yaŭ seúng k‘aū mat‐yĕ ni? chì‐tò kwokₒ‐yat‐poòn, ngŏh pit waî neĭ tsô‐shīng.
3 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ taapₒ wâ, Wōng à, ngŏh yeūk‐haí neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn tak‐yan‐ch‘úng, wōng yeūk‐ĭ‐waī‐cheūk, tsaû uên wăn‐chún ngŏh shóh‐pán, tseung ngŏh sìng‐mîng ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh, wăn‐chún ngŏh shóh‐k‘aū, tseung ngŏh‐kè tsung‐tsūk ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh:
4 Yan‐waî ngŏh kûng‐ngŏh‐kè tsung‐tsūk peî‐yān‐maaî, seúng tsiú‐mīt shaatₒ‐lūk lîng ngŏh‐teî sź‐mōng. Yeūk maaî ngŏh‐teî tsô nō, tsô p‘eĭ, ngŏh yīk m̄‐ch‘ut‐sheng, sui‐īn kóm‐yeûng, tīk‐yān yīk‐pat‐nāng pó wōng shóh‐shat.
5 Ue‐shî À‐hap‐ts‘uī‐lŏ wōng taapₒ wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ wâ, Kóm‐laāp‐sam tsô kóm‐yeûng haî‐mat‐shuī‐ni, kòh‐yān haí‐pin‐ch‘uè ni?
6 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ wâ, Ch‘aū‐yān, tīk‐yān, tsik‐haî ni‐kòh‐okₒ‐yān Hap‐maân lokₒ. Kóm‐tsaû Hap‐maân haí wōng‐kûng‐haû‐kè mîn‐ts‘īn king‐fong.
7 Wōng tsaû taaî‐nô heí‐laī, leī‐hoi īn‐tsīk, yāp‐uê‐uēn huì: Hap‐maân kìn wōng laāp‐ì seúng fāt k‘uĭ; tsaû heí‐shan heùng wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ k‘aū sìng‐mîng.
8 Wōng ts‘ūng uê‐uēn faan‐tò ts‘ŏh īn‐tsīk‐kè kung; Hap‐maân ĭ‐king pūk‐tó Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ shóh‐tsoî‐kè ch‘ōng‐sheûng. Wōng tsaû wâ. K‘uĭ yaû tsoî‐kung‐noî, haí ngŏh mîn‐ts‘īn seúng līng‐yūk wōng‐haû me? Ts‘ź‐īn yat‐ch‘ut wōng‐haú, chùng‐yān tsaû k‘ám‐chuê Hap‐maân‐kè mîn.
9 Yaŭ yat t‘aaì‐kaàm, Hap‐p‘òh‐nā tsaù wōng wâ, T‘aí‐a, Hap‐maân laāp mūk‐kà ko‐nğ‐cheûng, seúng tiù Mūk‐taí‐koí tsoî‐k‘eī‐sheûng, uē‐kam laāp‐tsoî Hap‐maân‐uk‐luĭ, Mūk‐taí‐koí sik‐yāt tseung chung‐īn pò‐chi wōng à. Wōng wâ, Iù tseung Hap‐maân tiù‐tsoî mūk‐kà‐sheûng.
10 Chùng‐yān tsaû tseung Hap‐maân tiù‐tsoî k‘uĭ waî Mūk‐taí‐koí shóh‐laāp‐kè mūk‐kà‐sheûng. Kóm‐tsaû wōng‐kè nô‐heì chí‐sik lokₒ.
