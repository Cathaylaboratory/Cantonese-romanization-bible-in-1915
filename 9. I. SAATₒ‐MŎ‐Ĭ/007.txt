1 KEI‐LĪT‐YĒ‐LĀM‐YĀN laī‐tò, tsaû tseung Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeukₒ‐kwaî t‘oī‐sheŭng‐huì, fòng tsoî shaan‐sheûng À‐peì‐nā‐t‘aatₒ‐kè‐uk, fan‐p‘aaì k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ hon‐shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeukₒ‐kwaî.
2 ¶ Yeukₒ‐kwaî haí Kei‐līt‐yē‐lām hó‐noî yāt‐tsź, yat‐heì î‐shāp nīn: Ĭ‐shik‐līt ts‘uēn‐ka to heùng‐mô Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
3 Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt ts‘uēn‐ka wâ, Neĭ‐teî yeūk haî tsûn‐sam kwai‐shûn Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, tsaû iù k‘aaĭ î‐pong‐yān‐kè shān, k‘āp À‐taaî‐lūk ts‘ūng neĭ‐teî chung‐kaan ch‘uē‐huì, kin‐sam heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, taan‐tūk fûng‐sẑ k‘uĭ: k‘uĭ tsaû pit ch‘íng‐kaù neĭ‐teî t‘uetₒ‐leī Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè shaú.
4 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû ch‘uē‐huì Pa‐līk kûng À‐taaî‐lūk, taan‐tūk fûng‐sẑ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
5 ¶ Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ wâ, Tsuî‐tsaāp Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tò Maĭ‐sz‐pa, táng ngŏh waî neĭ‐teî t‘ó‐kò Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
6 Kóm‐tsaû chùng‐yān tsoî Maĭ‐sz‐pa tsuî‐tsaāp, tá‐shuí tîn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn, haí kóh‐yāt kàm‐shīk, tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè wâ, Ngŏh‐teî yă‐ts‘āng tak‐tsuî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā. Ue‐shî Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ haí Maĭ‐sz‐pa shám‐p‘oòn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
7 Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān t‘eng‐mān Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haí Maĭ‐sz‐pa tsuî‐tsaāp, Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐kè mūk‐paakₒ, tsaû sheŭng‐huì kung‐tá Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān. Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān t‘eng‐mān, tsaû hó p‘à Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān.
8 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tuì Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ wâ, Uên neĭ pat‐hitₒ waî ngŏh‐teî hán‐ts‘itₒ k‘aū ngŏh‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh‐teî t‘uetₒ‐leī Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè shaú.
9 Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ tsaû k‘aaĭ yat‐chekₒ yám‐uĕ‐kè yeūng‐tsaí, tsûn‐hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsô faān‐tsaì: waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wăn‐chún k‘uĭ.
10 Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ kong‐ngaam hìn‐tsaì kóh‐chân‐shī, Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān sheŭng‐ts‘īn, seúng kûng Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kaau‐chìn: kòh‐yāt Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng taaî‐luī kung‐kik Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān; Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān tsaû paaî‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān mîn‐ts‘īn.
11 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān ts‘ūng Maĭ‐sz‐pa ch‘ut‐laī, chui‐kón Fei‐leî sẑ‐yān, kung‐tá k‘uĭ‐teî, chīk‐tò Paakₒ‐kaapₒ‐kè hâ‐pin.
12 Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ tsaû k‘aaĭ yat‐faaì shēk laāp‐tsoî Maĭ‐sz‐pa kûng Shîn‐kè chung‐kaan, koí kòh‐mēng‐kiù Ĭ‐pîn‐ĭ‐ch‘itₒ, wâ, Tò uē‐kam Yē‐Wōh‐Wā pong‐chôh ngŏh‐teî.
13 Kóm‐tsaû Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān peî chaì‐fūk, m̄‐kóm tsoì‐yāp Ĭ‐shik‐līt kíng‐noî: Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ tsô sẑ‐sz‐kè shī‐haû, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shaú tīk‐chuê Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān.
14 Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān shóh‐ts‘uí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐kè shēng‐yap, ts‘ūng Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn chì Ka‐tāk, to kwai‐faan Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān; shūk shēng‐yap‐kè sz̀ kíng, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān yīk heùng Fei‐leî‐sẑ‐yān‐kè shaú ts‘uí‐faan. Kòh‐shī Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān t‘ūng À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān wōh‐hó.
15 Saatₒ‐mŏ‐ĭ yat‐shang to tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè sẑ‐sz.
16 Mooĭ‐nīn ts‘ūn‐hāng tò Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî, Kat‐kaapₒ, Maĭ‐sz‐pa, tsoî ts‘ź kokₒ‐ch‘uè shám‐p‘oòn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān.
17 Tsaû faan‐huì La‐mă; yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐kè ka haí‐kóh‐ch‘uè; k‘uĭ haí‐kóh‐ch‘uè shám‐p‘oòn Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān; yaû haí‐kóh‐ch‘uè waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heí‐hiu yat‐tsôh t‘aān.
