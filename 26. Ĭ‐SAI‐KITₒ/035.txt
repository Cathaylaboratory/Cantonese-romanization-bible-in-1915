1 FÒNG‐CH‘É Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue‐ngŏh, wâ,
2 Yān‐chi‐tsź à, iù‐tîng neĭ‐mîn heùng Sai‐ĭ‐shaan kóng uê‐īn kung‐kik, yaû tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ,
3 Sheûng‐Chuē Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Sai‐ĭ‐shaan à, t‘aí‐a, ngŏh kung‐kik neĭ, ooĭ‐heùng‐neĭ shan‐hoi ngŏh‐shaú, lîng‐neĭ tsô‐fong‐faì tsô‐king‐k‘eī‐kè à.
4 Ngŏh ooĭ‐paaî‐waaî‐neĭ‐kè shēng‐yap, neĭ pit‐shīng‐waī fong‐faì‐kè, neĭ yīk‐pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
5 Yan neĭ yaŭ‐sheūng‐ts‘uēn‐kè ch‘aū‐hân, yaû tong k‘uĭ waân‐naân shī‐haû, yaû tong k‘uĭ moōt‐shī tsuî‐hin‐kè kitₒ‐kūk, tseung Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐tsź‐suen kaau‐kwòh to‐kìm‐kè nāng‐līk:
6 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ, Koò‐ts‘ź chiù‐ngŏh‐shang‐oōt, ngŏh pit‐heùng‐neĭ uê‐peî‐huetₒ: ī‐ch‘é huetₒ‐pit‐chui‐kón neĭ: yan neĭ m̄‐tsang‐oò‐huetₒ, shóh‐ĭ huetₒ ooĭ‐chui‐kón neĭ.
7 Kóm‐tsaû ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng Sai‐ĭ‐shaan tsô‐king‐k‘eī‐kè, yaû tsô fong‐faì‐kè, yīk haí‐ch‘uè t‘uĕn‐tsuēt faān‐king‐kwòh‐kè, waāk faan‐laī‐kè.
8 Ngŏh pit‐k‘aaĭ‐shūk‐k‘uĭ peî‐shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè ch‘ung‐moŏn k‘uĭ shaan: tsoî neĭ kong‐lĭng k‘āp shaan‐kuk, kûng neĭ kokₒ‐t‘iū‐shuí‐lô, kòh‐ti peî‐to‐kìm shaatₒ‐lūk‐kè, ooĭ‐pūk‐tó‐ch‘uè.
9 Ngŏh ooĭ‐lîng neĭ shīng‐waī sheūng‐shī‐kè fong‐faì, neĭ‐kè shēng‐yap, pit‐mŏ‐kui‐mān: neĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
10 Yan neĭ ts‘āng wâ, Ni‐leŭng‐kwokₒ k‘āp ni‐leŭng‐pong pit‐ooĭ shūk‐ngŏh, ī‐ch‘é ngŏh‐teî pit‐ooĭ‐shīng‐shaû‐kè; taân Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kíng‐īn tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè:
11 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Koò‐ts‘ź chiù‐ngŏh‐shang‐oōt, ngŏh pit‐i‐neĭ‐nau‐nô, yaû i‐neĭ‐tò‐keî, tsik‐haî yaū‐ue neĭ‐ch‘aū‐hân heùng k‘uĭ‐teî faatₒ‐ch‘ut‐kè laī‐hāng; ngŏh shám‐p‘oòn‐neĭ kòh‐shī, pit‐tîng tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐chung hín‐în tsẑ‐keí à.
12 Neĭ‐pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh tsik Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ts‘āng‐t‘eng‐saaì neĭ‐sitₒ‐tūk, tsik‐haî neĭ shóh‐kóng laī‐kung‐kik Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè shaan, wâ, K‘uĭ‐teî peî‐fong‐faì‐kè, yaû peí‐kwòh ngŏh‐teî t‘an‐mīt‐kè.
13 Neĭ‐teî heùng‐ngŏh, k‘aaĭ‐haú tsẑ‐ko tsẑ‐kw‘a, yaû ka‐toh neĭ‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ kung‐kik ngŏh: ngŏh ts‘āng‐t‘eng‐kwòh à.
14 Sheûng‐Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm wâ; Ts‘uēn‐teî faaì‐lōk‐kòh‐shī, ngŏh pit‐lîng‐neĭ fong‐faì.
15 Hó‐ts‘z̆ neĭ waî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐ka‐kè ch‘aán‐īp faaì‐lōk, yan neĭ shīng‐waī‐fong‐faì‐kè, ngŏh tsaû chiù‐kóm‐yeûng heùng‐neĭ‐tsô: Sai‐ĭ‐shaan à, k‘āp Ĭ‐tung‐ts‘uēn‐teî, t‘ung to‐ooĭ‐shīng‐waī‐fong‐faì‐kè: k‘uĭ‐teî pit‐chi‐tò ngŏh haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā.
