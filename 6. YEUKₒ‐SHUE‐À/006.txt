1 YĒ‐LEÎ‐KOH‐YĀN yan p‘à Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān: tsaû īm‐māt shaú‐shēng, mŏ yān ch‘ut‐yāp.
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ, Ngŏh ts‘āng tseung Yē‐leî‐koh kûng kóh‐ch‘uè‐kè wōng, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè taaî‐yŭng‐sẑ, to kaau‐kwòh neĭ shaú.
3 Neĭ‐kè yat‐ts‘aì ping‐ting, iù mooĭ yāt waī‐chuê shēng hāng yat‐ts‘z̀. Chiù‐kóm‐yeûng hāng lūk yāt.
4 Ts‘at‐kòh tsaì‐sz, iù tsoî yeukₒ‐kwaî ts‘īn‐pîn ning ts‘at‐kòh yeūng‐kung‐kokₒ‐kè hô‐t‘ūng: tò taî‐ts‘at yāt, iù waī‐chuê shēng hāng ts‘at ts‘z̀, tsaì‐sz iù ch‘ui heúng‐kokₒ.
5 K‘uĭ‐teî ch‘ui heúng‐kokₒ ch‘ut ch‘eūng‐sheng, neĭ‐teî t‘eng‐mān kóh‐ti‐sheng chi‐shī, chùng‐paakₒ‐sìng iù taaî‐sheng foo‐kiù; shēng‐ts‘eūng tsaû pit k‘ing‐titₒ, paakₒ‐sìng iù chīk‐tsaú sheŭng‐ts‘īn.
6 Nuên‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à chiû chùng‐tsaì‐sz laī, tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù t‘oī‐heí yeukₒ‐kwaî, ts‘at‐kòh tsaì‐sz, iù tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn ning ts‘at‐kòh yeūng‐kung‐kokₒ‐kè hô‐t‘ūng.
7 Yaû tuì paakₒ‐sìng wâ, Neĭ‐teî iù sheŭng‐ts‘īn waī‐chuê kòh‐shēng, faān cha‐ping‐heì‐kè yān, to iù tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeukₒ‐kwaî ts‘īn‐pîn hāng.
8 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tuì paakₒ‐sìng kóng uēn, ts‘at‐kòh tsaì‐sz ning ts‘at‐kòh yeūng‐kung‐kokₒ‐kè hô‐t‘ūng, tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng, tseung heúng‐kokₒ laī‐ch‘ui: Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeukₒ‐kwaî kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ‐teî.
9 Cha‐ping‐heì‐kè yān, tsoî ch‘ui heúng‐kokₒ‐kè tsaì‐sz ts‘īn‐pîn hāng, k‘eī‐uē chùng‐yān kan‐ts‘uī yeukₒ‐kwaî haû‐pîn, tsaì‐sz yat‐tsẑ hāng, yat‐tsẑ ch‘ui heúng‐kokₒ.
10 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à fan‐foò paakₒ‐sìng wâ, Neĭ‐teî m̄‐hó foo‐kiù, m̄‐hó lîng yān t‘eng‐mān neĭ‐kè sheng, neĭ haú tsúng m̄‐hó ch‘ut shuetₒ‐wâ, táng‐tò ngŏh fan‐foò neĭ‐teî foo‐kiù‐kè yāt‐tsź; kóh‐shī tsaû hóh‐ĭ foo‐kiù lokₒ.
11 Kóm‐tsaû Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeukₒ‐kwaî waī‐chuê shēng hāng yat‐ts‘z̀: chùng‐yān tsaû faan‐huì yīng‐p‘oōn, tsoî‐yīng‐noî hitₒ‐suk.
12 ¶ Mīng‐chiu Yeukₒ‐shue‐à hó‐tsó heí‐shan, tsaì‐sz yaû t‘oī‐heí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè yeukₒ‐kwaî.
13 Ts‘at‐kòh tsaì‐sz tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yeukₒ‐kwaî ts‘īn‐pîn, ning ts‘at‐kòh yeūng‐kung‐kokₒ‐kè hô‐t‘ūng, hāng‐kán laī‐ch‘ui: cha‐ping‐heì‐kè yān, tsoî k‘uĭ ts‘īn‐pîn hāng; k‘eī‐uē chùng‐yān kan‐ts‘uī Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yeukₒ‐kwaî haû‐pîn, tsaì‐sz yat‐tsẑ hāng, yat‐tsẑ ch‘ui heúng‐kokₒ.
14 Taî‐î yāt, chùng‐yān yaû waī‐chuê shēng hāng yat ts‘z̀, tsaû faan‐huì yīng‐p‘oōn: lūk yāt to haî kóm‐yeûng tsô.
15 Taî‐ts‘at yāt hó‐tsó t‘in chûng haak chi‐shī, chùng‐yān heí‐shan, chiù‐yeûng waī‐chuê shēng hāng: waī‐haî ni‐yāt tsaû waī‐chuê shēng hāng ts‘at ts‘z̀.
16 Tò taî‐ts‘at ts‘z̀, tsaì‐sz ch‘ui heúng‐kokₒ, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tsaû tuì paakₒ‐sìng wâ, Neĭ‐teî foo‐kiù la; yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung shēng ts‘z̀‐kwòh neĭ lokₒ.
17 Kòh‐shēng k‘āp k‘eī‐chung faān shóh‐yaŭ‐kè, to iù kwai‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: waī tong tsiú‐mīt‐kè māt, tūk‐haî keî‐foŏ La‐hōp, k‘āp faān tsoî k‘uĭ ka‐chung‐kè, hóh‐ĭ ts‘uēn‐oōt, yan‐waî k‘uĭ yán‐ts‘ōng ngŏh‐teî shóh‐tá‐faatₒ‐kè sz̀‐ché à.
18 Neĭ‐teî kìn tong‐waí‐mīt‐kè māt, pit iù kán‐shân, húng‐p‘à neĭ sz‐tsẑ ning kóh‐ti‐māt, tsaû shaí tsẑ‐keí pìn‐waī tong‐waí‐mīt‐kè; ī‐ch‘é līn‐luî Ĭ‐shik‐līt ts‘uēn yīng, hoî k‘uĭ‐teî yīk pìn‐waī tong‐waí‐mīt‐kè.
19 Taân shóh‐yaŭ kam‐ngān waāk t‘ūng‐t‘itₒ‐kè heì‐mĭng, to iù kwai‐ue Yē‐Wōh‐Wā: shau‐yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè foò‐fōng.
20 Kóm‐tsaû paakₒ‐sìng foo‐kiù, tsaì‐sz ch‘ui heúng‐kokₒ: paakₒ‐sìng t‘eng‐mān heúng‐kokₒ sheng chi‐shī, tsaû hó‐taaî‐sheng iu, shēng‐ts‘eūng tsûn‐teî k‘ing‐titₒ, paakₒ‐sìng yāp shēng, kokₒ‐yān heùng‐ts‘īn chīk hāng, ue‐shî tak‐hiu kòh‐shēng.
21 Yaû yûng to shaatₒ‐saaì faān tsoî shēng‐chung‐kè naām‐nuĭ lŏ‐shiù, ngaū‐yeūng kûng luī.
22 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tuì kw‘ai‐t‘aàm kóh‐ch‘uè‐kè leŭng‐yān wâ, Neĭ‐teî huì‐tò keî‐foŏ ka‐chung, tseung k‘uĭ k‘āp k‘uĭ faān‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè to taaì‐ch‘ut‐laī, haî chiù neĭ‐teî heùng k‘uĭ shóh‐faatₒ‐shaî‐kè.
23 Kóh‐leŭng‐kòh tsô kw‘ai‐t‘aàm‐kè shiù‐nīn‐yān tsaû huì, tseung La‐hōp kûng k‘uĭ foô‐mŏ hing‐taî, k‘āp k‘uĭ faān‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè; pìng‐yat‐ts‘aì ka‐kuèn, to‐taaì‐tò Ĭ‐shik‐līt yīng‐ngoî.
24 Chùng‐yān tsaû yûng fóh fān‐shiu kóh‐shēng, kûng k‘eī‐chung faān‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè: tūk‐haî shóh‐yaŭ kam‐ngān waāk t‘ūng‐t‘itₒ‐kè heì‐mĭng, tsaû shau‐yāp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐luĭ‐kè foò‐fōng.
25 Yeukₒ‐shue‐à tseung keî‐foŏ La‐hōp kûng k‘uĭ foô‐ka, k‘āp faān‐shóh‐yaŭ‐kè, to lîng ts‘uēn‐oōt; yan‐waî k‘uĭ yán‐ts‘ōng Yeukₒ‐shue‐à shóh‐tá‐faatₒ, kw‘ai‐t‘aàm Yē‐leî‐koh‐kè sz̀‐ché à; foŏ‐yān chuê‐tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐chung, chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
26 Kóh‐shī, Yeukₒ‐shue‐à heùng chùng‐yān faatₒ‐shaî wâ, Faān hing‐heí tsoì‐kìn ni‐kòh Yē‐leî‐koh shēng‐kè, pit tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn shaû chaù‐chòh: chuk shēng‐kei‐kè, pit sź cheúng‐tsź, on shēng‐moōn‐kè, pit sź yaù‐tsź.
27 Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘ūng‐maaī Yeukₒ‐shue‐à; k‘uĭ‐kè mēng sheng ch‘uēn‐wān kóh‐ch‘uè ts‘uēn‐teî.
