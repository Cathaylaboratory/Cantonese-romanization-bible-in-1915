1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT‐YĀN ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp teî chi‐haû, taî‐î nīn ching‐uēt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsoî Sai‐naaĭ kw‘òng‐yĕ tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
2 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān iù tsoî shóh‐tîng‐kè yāt‐k‘eī, shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ.
3 Tsik‐haî poón‐uēt shāp‐sz̀ yāt tò aai‐maăn: tong shaú shóh‐tîng‐kè yāt‐k‘eī, chiù kokₒ‐yeûng tîng‐laî, kokₒ‐yeûng laĭ‐mān laī shaú tsitₒ.
4 Ue‐shî Moh‐sai tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān wâ, Iù shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ.
5 Ching‐uēt shāp‐sz̀ yāt aai‐maăn kòh‐shī, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû tsoî Sai‐naaĭ kw‘òng‐yĕ shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ: to tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè, chiù kóm‐yeûng laī hāng.
6 Yaŭ keí‐kòh‐yān peî sz‐shi ĭm oo‐waì, pat‐hóh haí kóh‐yāt shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ: koò‐ts‘ź kóh‐yāt huì‐tò Moh‐sai À‐lūn mîn‐ts‘īn:
7 Mân wâ, Ngŏh‐teî peî sź‐shi ĭm oo‐waì: tsô‐mat tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān chi‐chung, tong shóh‐tîng‐kè yāt‐k‘eī, m̄‐chún ngŏh‐teî hìn laĭ‐māt kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ni?
8 Moh‐sai tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Neĭ‐teî tsaâm‐shī táng‐haû, ngŏh iù t‘eng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tím‐yeûng fan‐foò neĭ‐teî.
9 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
10 Neĭ iù tuì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān kóng wâ, Yeūk neĭ‐teî k‘āp neĭ‐teî haû‐yuî chi‐chung, yaŭ yān peî sź‐shi ĭm oo‐waì, waāk hāng‐kán uĕn‐lô, to iù shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ.
11 Tong î‐uēt shāp‐sz̀ yāt aai‐maăn kòh‐shī, pit iù shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ, tseung yeūng‐tsaí kûng mō‐kaaù‐péng k‘āp foó‐ts‘oì t‘ūng‐shīk.
12 Yat‐ti to m̄‐laū‐tò mīng‐yāt, yīk pat‐hóh tá‐chitₒ yeūng‐tsaí yat‐t‘iū kwat: iù chiù uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè tîng‐laî laī‐hāng.
13 Yān yeūk kitₒ‐tsîng, yīk m̄‐haî hāng‐kán uĕn‐lô, taân tsẑ‐keí m̄‐háng shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ, ts‘ź yān pit tsoî paakₒ‐sìng chi‐chung mīt‐tsuēt, yan k‘uĭ tò‐hiu shóh‐tîng‐kè yāt‐k‘eī: to m̄‐tseung laĭ‐māt hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā, ts‘ź yān tsaû pit tsẑ‐yâm k‘eī tsuî.
14 Yeūk tsoî neĭ‐ch‘uè keì‐kui‐kè luĭ‐haakₒ, uên tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn, shaú uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ; iù tsun‐i uē‐uēt‐tsitₒ‐kè tîng‐laî k‘āp laĭ‐mān laī hāng: luĭ‐haakₒ kûng poón‐t‘ó‐kè yān, to t‘ūng‐yat laî.
15 ¶ Tong kìn‐laāp waī‐mōk kóh‐yāt, yaŭ wān che‐k‘oì waī‐mōk, tsik‐haî yeukₒ‐kwaî‐sheûng‐kè: tsẑ maăn‐haak chì‐tò t‘in‐kwong, yaŭ hó‐ts‘z̆ fóh k‘oì‐chuê mōk‐sheûng.
16 Haû‐loī yat‐laū haî kóm: yāt‐kaan yaŭ wān che‐k‘oì, yê‐kaan yaŭ hó‐ts‘z̆ fóh.
17 Wān ts‘ūng mōk‐sheûng shing‐heí, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû heí‐ch‘īng: wān tsoî kòh‐ch‘uè t‘īng‐chí, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè on‐yīng.
18 Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò laī heí‐ch‘īng, yīk tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò laī on‐yīng: kòh‐wān che‐k‘oì mōk‐sheûng chi‐shī, k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû chuê‐tsoî yīng‐noî.
19 Yeūk wān che‐k‘oì mōk‐sheûng hó‐noî yāt‐tsź, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû kán‐shaú Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò m̄‐heí‐ch‘īng.
20 Waāk wān che‐k‘oì mōk‐sheûng, yaŭ‐keí‐yāt yīk haî kóm; k‘uĭ‐teî kui‐chuê k‘āp heí ch‘īng, to haî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè mîng‐lîng.
21 Waāk wān che‐k‘oì tsẑ maăn‐haak chì t‘in‐kwong, yīk‐haî kóm, chiu‐tsó wān shing, tsaû iù heí‐ch‘īng: m̄‐lûn yāt‐yê, wān shing to‐iù heí‐ch‘īng.
22 Wān che‐k‘oì mōk‐sheûng, waāk leŭng‐yāt, waāk yat‐uēt, waāk yat‐nīn kòm‐noî, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān tsaû chuê‐tsoî yīng‐noî, m̄‐heí‐ch‘īng: tūk‐haî wān shing‐heí kóh‐shī tsaû heí‐ch‘īng.
23 K‘uĭ‐teî tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò laī on‐yīng, yīk tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fan‐foò laī heí‐ch‘īng: shaú‐chuê shóh‐chí‐shî‐kè cheung‐ch‘īng, to tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ Moh‐sai shaú shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè.
