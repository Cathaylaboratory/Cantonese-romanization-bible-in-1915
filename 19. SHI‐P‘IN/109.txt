1 NGŎH shóh‐tsaàn‐meĭ‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì, k‘aū neĭ m̄‐hó m̄‐ch‘ut‐sheng;
2 Yan‐waî okₒ‐yān kûng chà‐ngaî‐yān, hoi‐haú laī‐waí‐p‘òng ngŏh: tuì ngŏh kóng taaî‐wâ.
3 K‘aaĭ uèn‐hân‐chi‐īn waī‐chuê ngŏh; mŏ‐uēn‐koò laī‐kung‐tá ngŏh.
4 Ngŏh oì k‘uĭ, k‘uĭ faán‐chuèn ch‘aū‐hân ngŏh: ngŏh waī‐yaŭ‐chuen‐sam k‘eī‐t‘ó che.
5 K‘uĭ‐teî k‘aaĭ okₒ laī‐pò‐shîn, k‘aaĭ uèn laī‐pò‐yan.
6 K‘aū neĭ shaí okₒ‐yān chaì‐fūk k‘uĭ: shaí ch‘aū‐tīk k‘eĭ‐tsoî k‘uì yaû‐pin.
7 Uên k‘uĭ shaû‐shám‐chi‐shī, tsaû tîng‐k‘uĭ‐waī‐okₒ‐yān: k‘uĭ‐chi‐k‘eī‐t‘ó, faán‐suèn‐waī‐tsuî‐okₒ.
8 Uên k‘uĭ mîng tuén; pīt‐yān tak k‘uĭ‐chi‐chik‐fân.
9 Uên k‘uĭ‐chi‐tsź koo‐foó, k‘uĭ‐chi‐ts‘ai waī kwá‐foŏ.
10 Uên k‘uĭ haû‐yuî, laū‐leī shat‐shóh: tsô‐hat‐ī, kui‐chuê laân‐uk, uĕn huì‐hat‐shīk.
11 Uên chaaì‐chué ch‘itₒ‐kaì laī‐lóh k‘uĭ shóh‐yaŭ: uĕn yān laī‐ts‘eúng k‘uĭ ka‐ts‘oī.
12 Uên mō‐yān līn‐măn k‘uĭ: mŏ‐yān shi‐yan‐tín kwòh‐k‘uĭ koo‐tsź.
13 Uên k‘uĭ‐chi‐tsź‐suen tsuēt‐saaì; k‘uĭ‐chi‐mēng mootₒ‐lat, m̄‐ch‘uēn‐kwòh taî‐î toî.
14 Uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā keì‐nîm k‘uĭ līt‐tsó‐chi‐okₒ; m̄‐mootₒ‐lat k‘uĭ mŏ‐ts‘an‐chi‐tsuî.
15 Uên k‘uĭ‐chi‐tsuî‐okₒ, sheūng‐tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐ts‘īn, tsoî shaì‐sheûng mīt‐tsuēt‐saaì k‘uĭ‐chi‐mēng.
16 Yan k‘uĭ m̄‐seúng shi‐līn‐măn kwòh‐yān, faán iù pik‐hoî kòh‐ti foó‐ch‘óh, p‘ān‐k‘ūng, sheung‐sam‐chi‐yān, laī shaatₒ‐sź k‘uĭ.
17 K‘uĭ keì‐hò chaù‐chòh‐yān, koò‐ts‘ź chaù‐chòh lām‐tò k‘uĭ shan: k‘uĭ keì‐m̄‐foon‐heí chuk‐fuk‐yān, koò‐ts‘ź fuk‐fân uĕn‐leī k‘uĭ.
18 K‘uĭ ĭ‐king tseung chaù‐chòh cheukₒ‐tsoî‐shan, hó‐ts‘z̆ i‐fūk kóm, koò‐ts‘ź chaù‐chòh hó‐ts‘z̆ shuí yāp k‘uĭ t‘ŏ, hó‐ts‘z̆ yaū yāp k‘uĭ kwat.
19 Uên ni‐ti chaù‐chòh, yaū‐uē i‐fūk, cheukₒ‐kán k‘uĭ shan, yaū‐uē t‘iū‐taaì, sheūng‐ch‘uk k‘uĭ iu.
20 Taân‐faān ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘aaĭ waí‐p‘òng shuetₒ‐wâ, kung‐kik‐ngŏh‐līng‐wān‐ché, uên Yē‐Wōh‐Wā to kóm‐yeûng pò‐yìng k‘uĭ.
21 Ngŏh Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, yan neĭ‐chi‐mēng, k‘aū hó‐hon‐toî ngŏh: neĭ chi ts‘z̄‐pei shâm‐hó, k‘aū neĭ ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh.
22 Ngŏh haî foó‐ch‘óh p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐chi‐yān, sam‐chung yau‐sheung.
23 Ngŏh mîng tseung tsûn, hó‐ts‘z̆ yāt‐yíng‐chi‐ts‘ē: yīk hó‐ts‘z̆ wōng‐ch‘ūng peî‐yān‐kón‐chūk.
24 Ngŏh yan kàm‐shīk, sat‐t‘aū uĕn‐yeūk; chau‐shan‐yūk shaù, mŏ‐ti‐feī‐yûn.
25 Ngŏh peî‐k‘uĭ‐waí‐p‘òng: k‘uĭ kìn ngŏh tsaû iū‐t‘aū.
26 Ngŏh‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì Yē‐Wōh‐Wā à, k‘aū neĭ pong‐chôh: i neĭ‐chi‐ts‘z̄‐pei laī‐ch‘íng‐kaù ngŏh:
27 Táng k‘uĭ‐teî chi‐tò ni‐ti haî neĭ shóh‐hāng; haî ch‘ut‐tsẑ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā neĭ‐chi‐shaú.
28 Yâm‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ chaù‐chòh ngŏh, waī uên neĭ chuk‐fuk ngŏh: k‘uĭ‐teî hing‐heí laī‐shaû‐yūk; waī‐haî neĭ pūk tsaû hó‐foon‐heí.
29 Ngŏh‐chi‐ch‘aū‐tīk, mūng‐sau shaû‐yūk, hó‐ts‘z̆ shan‐t‘aí cheukₒ‐chuê i‐sheūng.
30 Ngŏh yûng haú kīk‐līk‐chuk‐tsê Yē‐Wōh‐Wā; ī‐ch‘é tsoî‐chùng‐yān‐chi‐chung laī‐tsaàn‐tsûng.
31 Yan‐waî k‘uĭ shî‐pit k‘eĭ‐tsoî p‘ān‐foó‐chi‐yān yaû‐pin, ch‘íng‐kaù k‘uĭ, m̄‐peí okₒ‐yān tîng‐k‘uĭ‐waī‐yaŭ‐tsuî.
