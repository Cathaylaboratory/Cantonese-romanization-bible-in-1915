1 SAI‐TAÍ‐KA tsô wōng taî‐kaú nīn, shāp uēt ch‘oh‐shāp yāt, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng taaì‐lĭng chùng‐kwan, laī kung‐tá Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, tuì‐chuê on‐yīng; yaû tsoî sz̀‐waī, chuk‐luĭ kung‐shēng.
2 Shēng peî‐waī‐kw‘án, chīk‐tò Sai‐taí‐ka wōng taî‐shāp‐yat nīn.
3 Sz̀‐uēt ch‘oh‐kaú yāt, shēng‐chung‐kè paakₒ‐sìng to tsuēt‐leūng, t‘ung shēng kei‐fong.
4 Ue‐shî shēng‐p‘òh, yê‐kaan kokₒ‐chìn‐sẑ, to heùng t‘ipₒ‐kân wōng‐uēn leŭng‐shēng‐kei‐kè moōn t‘ō‐tsaú: (yan‐waî Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān sz̀‐waī kung‐shēng:) wōng tsaû heùng p‘īng‐uēn‐lô huì.
5 Ka‐lāk‐taí‐kè kwan‐ping chui‐kón wōng, tsoî Yē‐leî‐koh‐kè p‘īng‐uēn chui‐tó k‘uĭ: wōng‐kè chùng‐kwan, leī‐hoi k‘uĭ sz̀‐saàn.
6 Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān tsaû chukₒ‐chuê wōng, taaì‐tò Laāp‐la, tò Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐ts‘īn; tsaû tsoî kóh‐ch‘uè shám‐p‘oòn k‘uĭ.
7 Tseung Sai‐taí‐ka chùng‐tsź, tsoî k‘uĭ ngaăn‐ts‘īn shaatₒ‐hiu, yaû waatₒ‐hiu Sai‐taí‐ka‐kè leŭng‐ngaăn, yûng t‘ūng‐lîn fōk‐chuê k‘uĭ, laai‐huì Pa‐peí‐lūn.
8 ¶ Tong Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ taî‐shāp‐kaú nīn, nğ‐uēt, ch‘oh‐ts‘at yāt, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè shān shî‐waî‐cheúng Neī‐pò‐saatₒ‐la‐taam, laī‐tò Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng:
9 Yûng fóh fān‐shiu Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè tîn‐uk, k‘āp wōng‐kè kung, kûng Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng yat‐ts‘aì fōng‐uk, pìng shóh‐yaŭ taaî‐kaan‐kè chaāk, to yûng fóh fān‐shiu.
10 Kan‐ts‘uī shî‐waî‐cheúng‐kè Ka‐lāk‐taí chùng‐kwan, waí‐ch‘aakₒ Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng sz̀‐waī‐kè shēng.
11 Shēng‐chung shîng‐hâ‐kè uē‐mān, uĕ‐k‘āp t‘ō‐peî t‘aū‐hōng Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐kè yān, uĕ‐k‘āp kw‘ān‐chùng shóh‐shîng‐hâ‐kè yān, shî‐waî‐cheúng Neī‐pò‐saatₒ‐la‐taam to lŏ‐leūk‐huì.
12 Taân shî‐waî‐cheúng laū‐hâ mān‐chung chì‐p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐kè, laī sau p‘ō‐t‘ō‐uēn, k‘āp tsô‐kaang‐chùng‐yān.
13 Faān Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè t‘ūng‐ch‘uĕ, kûng p‘oōn‐tsôh, k‘āp Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐chung‐kè t‘ūng‐hoí, Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān tsûn‐teî waí‐līt, tseung t‘ūng‐liû taaì‐huì Pa‐peí‐lūn.
14 Yaû taaì‐huì woh, ch‘aán, chuk‐tsín, lō, k‘āp t‘ūng‐heì, tîn‐chung shaí‐yûng‐kè.
15 Shóh‐yaŭ p‘oōn, uē, kam‐heì, ngān‐heì, shî‐waî‐cheúng yīk taaì‐huì.
16 Shóh‐lōh‐moōn tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn shóh‐tsô‐kè leŭng‐t‘iū t‘ūng‐ch‘uĕ; yat‐kòh t‘ūng‐hoí, kûng p‘oōn‐tsôh, ni‐ti yat‐ts‘aì heì‐mĭng‐kè t‘ūng, pat‐nāng ch‘ing‐leūng.
17 Ni‐yat‐t‘iū ch‘uĕ, ko shāp‐paatₒ ch‘ekₒ, ch‘uĕ‐sheûng yaŭ t‘ūng‐téng: ko‐saam ch‘ekₒ; waī‐chuê téng‐kè laān‐kon, shēk‐laū, to haî t‘ūng‐kè: taî‐î‐t‘iū līn laān‐kon t‘ūng‐maaī yat‐yeûng.
18 Shî‐waî‐cheúng ts‘uí tsaì‐sz‐cheúng Sai‐loî‐à, kûng foò tsaì‐sz Sai‐faan‐ngă, k‘āp pá‐shaú tîn‐moōn‐kè saam‐kòh‐yān:
19 Yaû ts‘ūng shēng‐luĭ ts‘uí yat‐kòh koón‐leĭ chìn‐sẑ‐kè waân‐koon, yaû tsoî shēng‐luĭ chukₒ‐chuê kìn‐wōng‐mîn‐kè nğ‐kòh‐yān, k‘āp kaì‐shò kwokₒ‐mān, tsô kwan‐cheúng‐kè shîn‐sé‐ché yat‐yān, pìng‐tsoî shēng‐chung shóh‐chukₒ‐kè kwokₒ‐mān lūk‐shāp yān:
20 Shî‐waî‐cheúng Neī‐pò‐saatₒ‐la‐taam tseung ni‐ti‐yān taaì‐tò Laāp‐la Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng‐ch‘uè:
21 Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng tá k‘uĭ‐teî, ī‐ch‘é tsoî Hap‐moōt‐teî‐kè Laāp‐la laī‐shaatₒ k‘uĭ‐teî. Kóm‐tsaû Yaū‐taaî‐yān peî‐lŏ‐leūk, ch‘ut k‘uĭ poón‐teî.
22 Chì‐ue Yaū‐taaî‐kwokₒ shîng‐hâ‐kè uē‐mān, Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ shóh‐shîng‐hâ‐kè, wōng tsaû fung Sha‐faan‐kè suen, À‐hei‐kom‐kè tsaí K‘eī‐taaî‐leî, tsô k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsúng‐tuk.
23 ¶ Chùng‐kwan‐cheúng kûng kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè yān, t‘eng‐mān Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng fung K‘eī‐taaî‐leî tsô tsúng‐tuk, kóm‐tsaû Neī‐taaî‐neī‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐shāt‐mă‐leî, Ka‐leĭ‐à‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐hap‐naān, Neī‐t‘ōh‐faatₒ‐yān Taan‐oô‐mīt‐kè tsaí Sai‐loī‐à, Mă‐ka‐yān‐kè tsaí Yē‐sha‐neī, taaì‐chuê kan‐ts‘uī‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐yān, to laī‐tò Māt‐sẑ‐pa, K‘eī‐taaî‐leî‐ch‘uè.
24 K‘eī‐taaî‐leî heùng k‘uĭ‐teî k‘āp kan‐ts‘uī k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè yān faatₒ‐shaî, tuì k‘uĭ‐teî wâ, Maĭ yan Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè shān‐pūk kuî‐p‘à: hóh‐ĭ chuê‐tsoî ts‘ź‐teî, fūk‐sẑ Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng; kóm‐tsaû neĭ‐teî hóh‐heúng p‘īng‐on à.
25 Taân shuī‐pat‐chi taî‐ts‘at‐uēt yaŭ shūk‐wōng‐tsūk‐kè, Ĭ‐leî‐sha‐mă‐kè suen, Neī‐taaî‐neī‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐shāt‐mă‐leî, taaì‐chuê shāp‐kòh kan‐ts‘uĭ‐kè yān, laī tá K‘eī‐taaî‐leî, chì k‘uĭ sź, yaû shaatₒ tsoî Māt‐sẑ‐pa t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ‐kè Yaū‐taaî‐yān, k‘āp Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān.
26 Chùng‐mān m̄‐lûn taaî‐siú, k‘āp chùng‐kwan‐cheúng, yan‐waî kuî‐p‘à Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān, to heí‐laī huì Oi‐k‘āp.
27 ¶ Tong Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan peî‐lŏ‐leūk taî‐saam‐shāp‐ts‘at nīn, shāp‐î uēt î‐shāp‐ts‘at yāt Pa‐peí‐lūn wōng Ĭ‐meî‐maĭ‐lōh‐taāt, tang‐waî kóh‐nīn, tsaû shing‐ko Yaū‐taaî wōng Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan, fòng k‘uĭ ch‘ut‐kaam‐lō;
28 Ī‐ch‘é yûng hó‐shuetₒ‐wâ on‐waì k‘uĭ, ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ ts‘ŏh‐waî ko‐kwòh t‘ūng‐tsoî Pa‐peí‐lūn‐kè chùng‐wōng‐kè waî;
29 Shaí k‘uĭ oôn‐huì ts‘aū‐fūk: Yeukₒ‐ngă‐kan tsaû yat‐shang kûng wōng t‘ūng‐tsīk.
30 K‘uĭ‐kè leūng‐shīk haî wōng shóh‐ts‘z̀, yāt‐yāt yaŭ tîng‐laî, chīk‐tò k‘uĭ chung‐shan.
