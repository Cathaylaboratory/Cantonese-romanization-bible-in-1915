1 WAN‐YĪK chi‐haû, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai k‘āp tsaì‐sz À‐lūn‐kè tsaí Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ wâ,
2 Neĭ‐teî iù tseung Ĭ‐shik‐līt ooî‐chùng, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsung‐tsūk, faān î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng, hóh‐nāng lām‐chân tá‐cheùng‐kè, kaì‐kwòh ch‘ā‐shó.
3 Moh‐sai k‘āp tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî à‐saatₒ, tsoî Moh‐aapₒ kw‘òng‐yĕ, haî Yē‐leî‐koh tuì‐mîn, kân Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh kóh‐ch‘uè, suen‐kóng wâ,
4 Tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò Moh‐sai‐kè; tong iù tseung ch‘ut Oi‐k‘āp‐kè Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, faān î‐shāp‐suì ĭ‐sheûng‐kè to ch‘ā‐shó, kóh‐ti shò‐mūk līt mīng tsoî haû.
5 ¶ Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè cheúng‐tsź Laū‐pîn: Laū‐pîn‐kè tsź‐suen; yaū Hap‐nōk shang‐kè haî Hap‐nōk tsūk: yaū Pa‐lô ch‘ut‐kè, haî Pa‐lô tsūk:
6 Yaū Hei‐sz‐lūn shang‐kè, haî Hei‐sz‐lūn tsūk: yaū Ka‐maĭ ch‘ut‐kè, haî Ka‐maĭ tsūk.
7 Ni‐ti haî Laū‐pîn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk: peî‐shó‐kwòh‐kè, kûng‐kaì sz̀‐maân‐saam‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp yān.
8 Pa‐lô‐kè tsaí; Ĭ‐leî‐aapₒ.
9 Ĭ‐leî‐aapₒ‐kè tsaí; Neī‐mŏ‐leî, Taaî‐taan, À‐peì‐laān. Ni‐leŭng‐kòh, Taaî‐taan, À‐peì‐laān, tsoî ooî‐chung peí‐kaán‐suén‐kè, Koh‐la yat tóng pooì‐yīk Sheûng‐Taì chi‐shī, k‘uĭ yīk t‘ūng‐maaī kung‐kik Moh‐sai, À‐lūn:
10 Fóh shiu‐sź î‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp yān chi‐shī, teî hoi‐haú t‘an‐hiu k‘uĭ‐teî, k‘āp Koh‐la, yat‐tóng to t‘ūng sź‐mōng: kóm k‘uĭ‐teî tsô kíng‐kaaì‐kè keì‐hô.
11 Waī‐haî Koh‐la‐kè chùng‐tsź m̄‐sź.
12 ¶ Sai‐mîn tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsūk: yaū Neī‐mŏ‐leî shang‐kè, haî Neī‐mŏ‐leî tsūk; yaū Ngă‐mān shang‐kè, haî Ngă‐mān tsūk: yaū Ngă‐kan shang‐kè, haî Ngă‐kan tsūk:
13 Yaū Sai‐la shang‐kè, haî Sai‐la tsūk: yaū Sha‐lōh shang‐kè, haî Sha‐lōh tsūk.
14 Ni‐ti haî Sai‐mîn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè kokₒ‐tsūk, kaì‐kwòh kûng‐kaì î‐maân‐î‐ts‘in‐î‐paakₒ yān.
15 ¶ Ka‐tak tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsūk: yaū Sai‐fan shang‐kè, haî Sai‐fan tsūk: yaū Hap‐kei shang‐kè, haî Hap‐kei tsūk: yaū Shue‐neī shang‐kè, haî Shue‐neī tsūk:
16 Yaū Oh‐sẑ‐neī shang‐kè, haî Oh‐sẑ‐neī tsūk: yaū Ĭ‐leĭ shang‐kè, haî Ĭ‐leĭ tsūk:
17 Yaū À‐lūt shang‐kè, haî À‐lūt tsūk: yaū À‐leĭ‐leî shang‐kè, À‐leĭ‐leî tsūk.
18 Ni‐ti haî Ka‐tak chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk, faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì sz̀‐maân‐līng‐nğ‐paakₒ yān.
19 ¶ Yaū‐taaî‐kè tsaí, haî Ĭ kûng Oh‐naām: taân Ĭ kûng Oh‐naām to sź tsoî Ka‐naām‐teî.
20 Yaū‐taaî k‘eī‐uē‐kè tsź‐suen, chiù‐k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsūk; yaū Shî‐la shang‐kè, haî Shî‐la tsūk: yaū Faatₒ‐lāk‐sẑ shang‐kè, haî Faatₒ‐lāk‐sẑ tsūk: yaū Sai‐la shang‐kè, haî Sai‐la tsūk.
21 Faatₒ‐lāk‐sẑ‐kè tsaí‐suen; yaū Hei‐sz‐lūn shang‐kè, haî Hei‐sz‐lūn tsūk: yaū Hap‐mŏ‐lāk shang‐kè, haî Hap‐mŏ‐lāk tsūk.
22 Ni‐ti haî Yaū‐taaî chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk, faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì ts‘at‐maân‐lūk‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ yān.
23 ¶ Ĭ‐saatₒ‐ka tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsūk: yaū T‘ōh‐la shang‐kè, haî T‘ōh‐la tsūk: yaū Foo‐à shang‐kè, haî Foo‐à tsūk:
24 Yaū Ngă‐shūt shang‐kè, haî Ngă‐shūt tsūk: yaū Shan‐lūn shang‐kè, haî Shan‐lūn tsūk.
25 Ni‐ti haî Ĭ‐saatₒ‐ka chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk, faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì lūk‐maân‐sz̀‐ts‘in‐saam‐paakₒ yān.
26 ¶ Sai‐pò‐lūn tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsūk: yaū Sai‐līt shang‐kè, haî Sai‐līt tsūk: yaū Ĭ‐lūn shang‐kè, haî Ĭ‐lūn tsūk: yaū Ngă‐līt shang‐kè, haî Ngă‐līt tsūk.
27 Ni‐ti haî Sai‐pò‐lūn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk, faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì lūk‐maân‐līng‐nğ‐paakₒ yān.
28 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shat‐kè tsai, Mă‐nā‐sai, Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn, fan‐waī leŭng‐chi‐p‘aaì.
29 Mă‐nā‐sai tsź‐suen: yaū Mă‐kat shang‐kè, haî Mă‐kat tsūk: Mă‐kat shang Kei‐līt: yaū Kei‐līt shang‐kè, haî Kei‐līt tsūk.
30 Kei‐līt tsź‐suen: yaū Yē‐sz shang‐kè, haî Yē‐sz tsūk: yaū Hei‐lāk shang‐kè, haî Hei‐lāk tsūk:
31 Yaū À‐sẑ‐līt shang‐kè, haî À‐sẑ‐līt tsūk: yaū Shî‐kìm shang‐kè, haî Shî‐kìm tsūk:
32 Yaū Shî‐maĭ‐taaî shang‐kè, haî Shî‐maĭ‐taaî tsūk: yaū Hei‐fat shang‐kè, haî Hei‐fat tsūk.
33 Hei‐fat‐kè tsaí Sai‐lōh‐fei‐hap, mŏ tsaí, tūk yaŭ nuĭ: nuĭ mēng haî kiù Mă‐la, Nōh‐à, Hotₒ‐la, Māt‐ka, Tak‐saatₒ.
34 Ni‐ti haî Mă‐nā‐sai chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk, faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì nğ‐maân‐î‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ yān.
35 ¶ Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chi‐p‘aaì: yaū Shue‐t‘aī‐la shang‐kè, haî Shue‐t‘aī‐la tsūk: yaū Peì‐kitₒ shang‐kè, haî Peì‐kitₒ tsūk: yaū Taaî‐hòn shang‐kè, haî Taaî‐hòn tsūk.
36 Shue‐t‘aī‐la tsūk‐kè tsź‐suen: yaū Ĭ‐laān shang‐kè, haî Ĭ‐laān tsūk.
37 Ni‐ti haî Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk, faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì saam‐maân‐î‐ts‘in‐nğ‐paakₒ yān. Ni‐ti to haî Yeukₒ‐shat‐kè tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ chúng‐tsūk‐kè.
38 ¶ Pîn‐ngă‐măn tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chùng tsūk: yaū Peì‐la shang‐kè, haî Peì‐la tsūk: yaū À‐shāt‐pīt shang‐kè, haî À‐shāt‐pīt tsūk: yaū À‐hei‐laān shang‐kè, haî À‐hei‐laān tsūk:
39 Yaū Shue‐faán shang‐kè, haî Shue‐faán tsūk: yaū Oô‐faán shang‐kè, haî Oô‐faán tsūk.
40 Peì‐la‐kè tsaí, À‐lāk kûng Naaĭ‐maân: yaū À‐lāk shang‐kè, haî À‐lāk tsūk: yaū Naaĭ‐maân shang‐kè, haî Naaĭ‐maân tsūk.
41 Ni‐ti haî Pîn‐ngă‐măn chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk: faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì sz̀‐maân‐nğ‐ts‘in‐lūk‐paakₒ yān.
42 ¶ Taân‐kè tsź‐suen, chiù k‘iŭ‐kè tsung‐tsūk: yaū Shue‐hōm shang‐kè, haî Shue‐hōm tsūk. Ni‐ti haî Taân chi‐p‘aaì‐kè tsūk.
43 Shue‐hōm tsūk faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì lūk‐maân‐sz̀‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ yān.
44 ¶ À‐ch‘itₒ tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsūk: yaū Yam‐nā shang‐kè, haî Yam‐nā tsūk: yaū Yē‐suî shang‐kè, haî Yē‐suî‐tsūk: yaū Peì‐leĭ‐à shang‐kè, haî Peì‐leĭ‐à tsūk.
45 Peì‐leĭ‐à‐kè tsaí, haî Hei‐paakₒ kûng Mootₒ‐kitₒ: yaū Hei‐paakₒ shang‐kè, haî Hei‐paakₒ tsūk: yaū Mootₒ‐kitₒ shang‐kè, haî Mootₒ‐kitₒ tsūk.
46 À‐ch‘itₒ‐kè nuĭ mēng‐kiù Saatₒ‐la.
47 Ni‐ti haî À‐ch‘itₒ chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk; faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì nğ‐maân‐saam‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ yān.
48 ¶ Naāp‐taaî‐leî tsź‐suen, chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsūk: yaū Ngă‐sitₒ shang‐kè, haî Ngă‐sitₒ tsūk: yaū Koo‐neī shang‐kè, haî Koo‐neī tsūk:
49 Yaū Yē‐shik shang‐kè, haî Yē‐shik tsūk: yaū Shî‐kím shang‐kè, haî Shî‐kím tsūk.
50 Ni‐ti haî Naāp‐taaî‐leî chi‐p‘aaì‐kè chùng‐tsūk: faān peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì sz̀‐maân‐nğ‐ts‘in‐sz̀‐paakₒ yān.
51 Shóh‐yaŭ peî‐shó‐kè Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, tsúng‐kaì lūk‐shāp‐maân‐līng‐yat‐ts‘in‐ts‘at‐paakₒ‐saam‐shāp yān.
52 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Moh‐sai wâ,
53 Iù tseung Ka‐naām‐teî fan‐kwòh ni‐ti chùng tsūk tsô ch‘aán‐īp, chiù k‘uĭ‐teî mēng‐shò.
54 Yān toh tsaû peí toh tsô ch‘aán‐īp, yān shiú tsaû peí shiú tsô ch‘aán‐īp: chiù shóh‐kaì kokₒ‐tsūk‐kè shò‐mūk, laī ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî.
55 Taân fan‐teî pit iù chap‐ch‘aū: chiù līt‐tsó kokₒ‐chi‐p‘aaì‐kè mēng laī shīng‐kaì.
56 Fan ch‘aán‐īp waāk peí toh waāk peí shiú, to chiù shóh‐chap‐kè ch‘aū laī fan.
57 ¶ Leî‐meî‐yān chiù k‘uĭ‐kè chùng‐tsūk, faān peî shó‐kwòh‐kè līt‐mīng tsoî haû: yaū Kaakₒ‐shûn shang‐kè, haî Kaakₒ‐shûn tsūk: yaū Koh‐hāt shang‐kè, haî Koh‐hāt tsūk: yaū Maĭ‐la‐leĭ shang‐kè, haî Maĭ‐la‐leĭ tsūk.
58 Leî‐meî tsūk‐chung: yaŭ Laāp‐neī tsūk, Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn tsūk, Mă‐leî tsūk, Mŏ‐shî tsūk, Koh‐la tsūk. Koh‐hāt shang Òm‐laān.
59 Òm‐laān‐kè ts‘ai mēng‐kiù Yeukₒ‐kei‐pīt, haî Leî‐meî‐kè nuĭ, tsoî Oi‐k‘āp shang‐kè: k‘uĭ t‘ūng Òm‐laān shang À‐lūn, Moh‐sai, k‘āp k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè tsź‐mooî Maĭ‐leĭ‐òm.
60 À‐lūn shang Nā‐taapₒ, À‐peì‐oô, Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ, Ĭ‐taaî‐mă.
61 Nā‐taapₒ kûng À‐peì‐oô, k‘aaĭ m̄‐shìng‐kitₒ‐kè fóh hìn‐kwòh Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû sź‐hiu.
62 Leî‐meî‐yān‐kè naām‐ting, faān tsẑ yat uēt ĭ‐sheûng, ĭ‐king peî‐shó‐kè, kûng‐kaì î‐maân‐saam‐ts‘in, k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐haí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐chung t‘ūng‐maaī peì‐shó: yan‐waî tsoî Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān‐chung, mŏ peí ch‘aán‐īp kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî à.
63 ¶ Ni‐ti haî Moh‐sai kûng tsaì‐sz Ĭ‐leî‐à‐saatₒ shóh‐kaì‐kwòh‐kè, tsik‐haî tsoî Moh‐appₒ kw‘òng‐yĕ, Yeukₒ‐taân‐hōh‐pin tuì‐chuê Yē‐leî‐koh kóh‐ch‘uè, laī ch‘ā Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān shò.
64 Ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn Moh‐sai kûng‐tsaì‐sz À‐lūn tsoî Sai‐naaĭ kw‘òng‐yĕ, shóh‐kaì‐kè Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān, uē‐kam peî‐shó‐kè, mŏ yat‐kòh haî ts‘ūng‐ts‘īn peî‐shó‐kè tsoî‐noî.
65 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā yă‐ts‘āng kóng k‘uĭ‐teî pit sź tsoî kw‘òng‐yĕ. Koò‐ts‘ź ch‘uē‐hiu Yē‐foo‐neī‐kè tsaí Ka‐lāk, k‘āp Nuên‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à chi‐ngoî, chùng‐yān mŏ yat‐kòh laū‐ts‘uēn.
