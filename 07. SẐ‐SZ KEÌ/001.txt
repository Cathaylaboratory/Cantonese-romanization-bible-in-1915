1 YEUKₒ‐SHUE‐À sź‐hiu chi‐haû, Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān k‘aū Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Pin‐kòh ying‐koi waî ngŏh‐teî sin sheŭng‐huì kung‐tá Ka‐naām‐yān laī kûng k‘uĭ‐teî kaau‐chìn ni?
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Yaū‐taaî iù sheŭng‐huì, ngŏh tseung kóh‐taatₒ‐teî kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ shaú.
3 Yaū‐taaî tuì k‘uĭ hing‐taî Sai‐mîn wâ, Ts‘éng neĭ t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh huì ngŏh chap‐ch‘aū shóh‐tak‐kè teî, táng ngŏh‐teî kung‐tá Ka‐naām‐yān; haû‐loī ngŏh yīk t‘ūng‐maaī neĭ huì neĭ chap‐ch‘aū shóh‐tak‐kè teî. Kóm‐tsaû Sai‐mîn kûng Yaū‐taaî t‘ūng‐maaī huì.
4 Yaū‐taaî tsaû sheŭng‐huì; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung Ka‐naām‐yān, Peí‐leĭ‐saí‐yān, kaau‐kwòh k‘uĭ‐teî shaú: k‘uĭ‐teî haí Peì‐sitₒ shaatₒ‐hiu yat‐maân tīk‐yān.
5 Yaû haí Peì‐sitₒ uê‐cheūk À‐toh‐neī‐peì‐sitₒ: kûng k‘uĭ kaau‐chìn, shaatₒ‐paaî Ka‐naām‐yān, Peí‐leĭ‐saí‐yān.
6 Taân À‐toh‐neī‐peì‐sitₒ t‘ō‐tsaú; taaî‐chùng chui‐kón chukₒ‐tó k‘uĭ, chaám‐t‘uĕn k‘uĭ‐kè shaú‐chí‐kung, keukₒ‐chí‐kung.
7 À‐toh‐neī‐peì‐sitₒ wâ, Kaû‐shī yaŭ ts‘at‐shāp‐kòh wōng, k‘uĭ‐kè shaú‐chí‐kung, keukₒ‐chí‐kung, to peî‐chaám‐t‘uĕn, haí ngŏh t‘oī‐taí chap sap‐suì‐kè shīk‐māt: uē‐kam Sheûng‐Taì chiù‐i ngŏh shóh‐tsô‐kè, laī pò‐yìng ngŏh. Taaî‐chùng tsaû laai À‐toh‐neī‐peì‐sitₒ huì Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, k‘uĭ tsaû haí kòh‐ch‘uè sź.
8 ¶ Yaū‐taaî‐yān kung‐tá Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng ts‘uí‐hiu shēng, yûng to shaatₒ kòh‐ti‐yān, yaû fòng fóh shiu shēng.
9 Haû‐loī Yaū‐taaî‐yān lōk‐huì kung‐tá chuê‐shaan, chuê‐naām‐fong, chuê‐hung‐kuk‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān.
10 Yaū‐taaî yaû huì kung‐tá chuê‐Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān: shaatₒ‐hiu Shî‐shai, À‐hei‐maân, Taāt‐maaĭ, (Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn kaû‐shī kiù‐tsô Kei‐līt‐à‐pa.)
11 Yaû ts‘ūng kòh‐ch‘uè huì kung‐tá Peì‐pit‐kè kui‐mān, Peì‐pit kaû‐shī kiù‐tsô Kei‐līt‐sai‐fat:
12 Ka‐lāk wâ, Kung‐tá Kei‐līt‐sai‐fat, ts‘uí‐hiu shēng kòh‐yān, ngŏh pit tseung ngŏh nuĭ Aapₒ‐saatₒ peí k‘uĭ tsô ts‘ai.
13 Ka‐lāk‐kè saì‐ló, Kei‐naāp‐kè tsaí Oh‐tak‐īp ts‘uí‐hiu kòh‐shēng: Ka‐lāk tsaû tseung k‘uĭ nuĭ Aapₒ‐saatₒ laī peí k‘uĭ tsô ts‘ai.
14 Aapₒ‐saatₒ tseung kwòh‐moōn‐kè shī‐haû, huèn cheûng‐foo heùng foô‐ts‘an k‘aū yat‐faaì t‘īn: Aapₒ‐saatₒ ue‐shî lōk‐luī; Ka‐lāk mân‐k‘uĭ wâ, Neĭ seúng k‘aū mat‐yĕ ni?
15 Aapₒ‐saatₒ taapₒ wâ, Ngŏh seúng neĭ ts‘z̀ fuk kwòh‐ngŏh: yan‐waî neĭ ĭ‐king tseung naām‐pîn‐kè teî‐fong ts‘z̀‐kwòh ngŏh: k‘aū neĭ k‘aaĭ shuí‐ts‘uēn peí ngŏh t‘im. Ka‐lāk tsaû tseung sheûng‐ts‘uēn hâ‐ts‘uēn ts‘z̀‐kwòh k‘uĭ.
16 ¶ Moh‐sai‐kè à‐k‘aŭ haî Kei‐neī‐yān, k‘uĭ‐kè tsź‐suen, kûng Yaū‐taaî‐yān ts‘ūng tsó‐shuê shēng, huì‐tò À‐laāp naām‐pîn‐kè Yaū‐taaî kw‘òng‐yĕ; tsaû chuê‐tsoî paakₒ‐sìng chi‐chung.
17 Yaū‐taaî kûng k‘uĭ hing‐taî Sai‐mîn, huì kung‐tá chuê Sai‐faatₒ shēng‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān, tseung tsûn‐teî laī mīt‐saaì. Kòh‐shēng kiù‐tsô Hotₒ‐mă.
18 Yaū‐taaî yaû ts‘uí‐hiu Ka‐saatₒ līn‐maaī kíng‐teî, À‐shāt‐kei‐lūn līn‐maaī kíng‐teî, Ĭ‐kaakₒ‐lūn līn‐maaī kíng‐teî.
19 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘ūng‐maaī Yaū‐taaî; tsaû kón‐chūk chuê‐shaan‐kè tīk‐yān; tūk‐haî pat‐nāng kón‐chūk chuê‐shaan‐kuk‐kè yān, yan‐waî k‘uĭ‐teî yaŭ t‘itₒ‐ch‘e à.
20 Chùng‐yān tsun‐i Moh‐sai‐kè mîng, tseung Hei‐paakₒ‐lūn ts‘z̀‐kwòh Ka‐lāk: Ka‐lāk tsaû ts‘ūng kòh‐ch‘uè kón‐chūk À‐naāp‐kè saam‐kòh tsaí.
21 Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐yān, mŏ kón‐chūk haí Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng kui‐chuê‐kè Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐yān; tsaû Yē‐pò‐sẑ‐yān kûng Pîn‐ngă‐măn‐yān, t‘ūng‐chuê Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng chì‐tò kam‐yāt.
22 ¶ Yeukₒ‐shat‐kè tsūk, yīk sheŭng‐huì kung‐tá Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî: tsaû Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ‐teî.
23 Yeukₒ‐shat‐kè tsūk, tá‐faatₒ yān huì kw‘ai‐t‘aàm Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî. (Kòh‐shēng kaû‐shī kiù‐tsô Lô‐sz.)
24 Shaú‐mông‐kè yān, t‘aí‐kìn yat‐kòh‐yān ts‘ūng shēng ch‘ut‐laī, tsaû tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Ts‘éng neĭ tseung yāp‐shēng‐kè lô‐kìng, chí‐shî ngŏh‐teî, ngŏh‐teî pit‐tîng haû‐toî neĭ.
25 Kòh‐yān tsaû tseung yāp‐shēng‐kè lô‐kìng, chí‐shî k‘uĭ‐teî, k‘uĭ‐teî tsaû yûng to tá‐paaî kòh‐shēng; waī‐haî tseung chí‐lô‐kìng kòh‐yān k‘āp k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘uēn‐ka fòng‐huì.
26 Kòh‐yān huì Haak‐yān‐kè teî‐fong, chuk‐hiu yat‐kòh shēng, koí mēng kiù‐tsô Lô‐sz: tò uē‐kam to haî on ni‐kòh mēng.
27 ¶ Mă‐nā‐sai mŏ kón‐chūk chuê Paakₒ‐shîn, kûng shūk Paakₒ‐shîn ts‘uen‐heung‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, chuê Taaî‐naāp kûng shūk Taaî‐naāp ts‘uen‐heung‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, chuê Toh‐ĭ kûng‐shūk Toh‐ĭ ts‘uen‐heung‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, chuê Ĭ‐paakₒ‐līn kûng‐shūk Ĭ‐paakₒ‐līn ts‘uen‐heung‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, chuê Maĭ‐kat‐toh kûng‐shūk Maĭ‐kat‐toh ts‘uen‐heung‐kè paakₒ‐sìng: Ka‐naām‐yān tsaû tîng‐ì chuê‐tsoî kòh‐ti teî‐fong.
28 Tò‐hiu Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān k‘eūng‐shîng‐kè shī‐haû, tsaû shaí Ka‐naām‐yān fūk‐yīk, waī‐haî mŏ‐kón‐ts‘ing k‘uĭ huì.
29 ¶ Ĭ‐faatₒ‐līn‐yān mŏ kón‐chūk chuê Kei‐shik‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān; kóm‐tsaû Ka‐naām‐yān tsoî Kei‐shik t‘ūng‐chuê.
30 ¶ Sai‐pò‐lūn mŏ kón‐chūk chuê Kat‐lūn‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, kûng‐chuê Nā‐hap‐lūk‐kè paakₒ‐sìng; Ka‐naām‐yān tsaû t‘ūng‐maaī k‘uĭ chuê, yaû naāp‐kùng kwòh k‘uĭ à.
31 ¶ À‐ch‘itₒ mŏ kón‐chūk chuê À‐hoh‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, kûng Sai‐tûn‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, k‘āp À‐laāp, À‐kaakₒ‐sik, Haak‐pa, À‐fei‐kaakₒ, Leĭ‐hōp‐kè paakₒ‐sìng:
32 À‐ch‘itₒ‐yān yan‐waî mŏ kón‐chūk poón‐t‘ó‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān tsaû chuê‐tsoî Ka‐naām‐yān‐kè chung‐kaan.
33 ¶ Naāp‐taaî‐leî mŏ kón‐chūk chuê Paakₒ‐shî‐māk‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, kûng‐chuê Paakₒ‐à‐naāp kè paakₒ‐sìng; Naāp‐taaî‐leî‐yān, tsaû chuê‐tsoî poón‐t‘ó‐kè Ka‐naām‐yān chung‐kaan: taân‐haî chuê Paakₒ‐shî‐māk‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, chuê Paakₒ‐à‐naāp‐kè paakₒ‐sìng, to naāp‐kùng kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî.
34 ¶ À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān, k‘eūng‐pik Taân‐yān, yāp shaan kui‐chuê: m̄‐peí k‘uĭ lōk‐laī shaan‐kuk:
35 À‐moh‐leĭ yān, tîng‐ì chuê‐tsoî À‐ngă‐lūn kè Hei‐līt‐shaan, kûng Sha‐aapₒ‐pan: Yeukₒ‐shat tsūk shìng‐hiu, shaí À‐moh‐leĭ yān naāp‐kùng.
36 À‐moh‐leĭ‐yān‐kè teî‐kaaì, haî ts‘ūng À‐hak‐la‐pan, yaû ts‘ūng p‘oōn‐shēk ĭ‐sheûng.
