1 NGŎH sam hó‐ts‘z̆ shuí‐kwán: faatₒ‐ch‘ut shîn‐īn, waî wōng tsokₒ‐koh: ngŏh‐t‘iū‐leî haî haaú‐shaú sé‐tsẑ‐yān‐chi‐pat.
2 Neĭ peí‐kaaù shaì‐yān kàng‐meĭ: moŏn‐haú yan‐tín‐chi‐īn: koò Sheûng‐Taì chuk‐fuk neĭ, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn.
3 Yaŭ līk‐chi‐yān, neĭ ying‐tong tseung to p‘ooì‐tsoî iu‐kaan; hín‐ch‘ut neĭ‐chi‐wīng‐iû wai‐yŭng.
4 Neĭ waî chan‐leĭ him‐sùn kung‐î, shi‐chín wai‐yŭng, ts‘ŏh ch‘e ts‘īn‐huì, neĭ yaû‐shaú pit‐tîng tsô‐ch‘ut hóh‐p‘à‐chi‐sẑ‐laī.
5 Neĭ‐chi‐tsìn shâm‐leî, shê‐chùng ngŏh wōng tīk‐yān‐chi‐sam; līt‐kwokₒ fūk‐tsoî neĭ keukₒ‐hâ.
6 Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐waî haî wĭng‐uĕn: neĭ‐chi‐kwokₒ‐pìng haî chìng‐chīk.
7 Neĭ hò‐shîn oò‐okₒ: koò‐ts‘ź Sheûng‐Taì tsik‐haî neĭ Sheûng‐Taì, tseung‐yaū ch‘ā‐tsoî neĭ‐shan‐sheûng, lìng neĭ foon‐lōk, shìng‐kwòh neĭ‐chi‐t‘ūng‐poôn.
8 Neĭ i‐fūk yaŭ moōt‐yeūk, lō‐ooì, yūk‐kwaì‐chi‐heung, ts‘ūng tseûng‐ngā‐tîn ch‘ut‐laī‐chi‐ngōk‐yam, lîng neĭ faaì‐lōk.
9 Tsô neĭ‐chi‐fei‐p‘ān, k‘ei‐chung yaŭ līt‐kwokₒ‐chi‐kung‐chué: wōng‐haû taaì Oh‐fei kam shaú‐shik, k‘eĭ‐laāp neĭ yaû‐pîn.
10 Nuĭ‐tsź à, neĭ iù‐t‘eng iù‐t‘aí, iù‐chak‐ĭ laī‐t‘eng; m̄‐hó keì‐nîm neĭ‐chi‐paakₒ‐sìng, uĕ‐k‘āp foô‐ts‘an‐chi‐ka;
11 Wōng tsaû foon‐heí neĭ‐chi meĭ‐maaû: yan‐waî k‘uĭ haì neĭ‐chi‐Chué; neĭ ying‐tong kìng‐paaì k‘uĭ.
12 T‘ui‐lōh‐chi‐nuĭ‐tsź, pit sùng laĭ‐māt laī; paakₒ‐sìng‐chung‐chi‐foò‐kwaì‐ché, pit k‘aū neĭ‐chi‐yan‐tín.
13 Wōng‐haû chuê‐tsoî kung‐chung, tsuen‐wīng mō‐peí: k‘uĭ‐chi‐i‐sheūng, haî kam‐sìn shóh‐saù.
14 Cheukₒ‐heí koò‐saù i‐fūk, ch‘iū‐kìn kwan‐wōng: kòh‐ti p‘ooī‐poôn t‘ūng‐nuĭ, taaì‐chuê k‘uĭ laī kìn neĭ.
15 K‘uĭ‐teî shî‐pit foon‐heí faaì‐lōk: taaì‐yāp wōng‐kung.
16 Neĭ pit yaŭ tsź‐suen, tsipₒ‐tsūk neĭ‐chi‐līt‐tsó, neĭ iù laāp k‘uĭ waī wōng, ch‘ī‐leĭ ts‘uēn‐teî.
17 Ngŏh pit tseung neĭ‐chi‐mēng, ch‘uēn‐yeūng maân‐toî: koò‐ts‘ź līt‐kwokₒ tsaàn‐meĭ neĭ, chì‐tò wĭng‐uĕn.
