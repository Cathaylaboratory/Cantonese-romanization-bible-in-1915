1 TONG Taaî‐leî‐oo wōng taî‐î‐nīn, lūk‐uēt ch‘oh‐yat‐yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, t‘okₒ‐sin‐chi Hap‐kei, ch‘uēn‐tò Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè fong‐paakₒ, Ch‘itₒ‐t‘itₒ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ yaû ch‘uēn‐tò tsaì‐sz‐cheŭng Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐taapₒ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à wâ,
2 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî kóm‐kóng, Ni‐ti‐tsź‐mān wâ, Ngŏh‐teî faan‐laī kìn‐heí Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐chi‐shī, ĭ‐king laī‐tò me?
3 Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, t‘okₒ‐sin‐chi Hap‐kei, ch‘uēn‐tò wâ,
4 Ni‐kaan‐tîn‐uk, yīng‐īn haî fong‐faì‐kè, heí‐haî neĭ‐teî‐kui‐chuê t‘in‐fa‐paán uk‐kè shī‐haû me?
5 Shóh‐ĭ maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tsaû‐kóm wâ; Iù‐tîng‐sam ts‘aam‐seúng‐hă neĭ‐kè lô‐t‘ō.
6 Neĭ saatₒ‐lōk‐kè toh, shau‐yāp‐kè shiú; neĭ‐teî yaŭ‐shīk, waī‐haî m̄‐tak‐kaù; neĭ‐teî yaŭ‐yám, waī‐haî m̄‐tak‐paaú; neĭ‐teî cheukₒ‐i‐fūk, waī‐haî mŏ‐yat‐kòh‐tak‐nuĕn; faān‐haî chaân‐tó kung‐ngān, tsaû chai‐lōk yaŭ‐lung‐kè toî‐ch‘uè.
7 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ; Iù‐tîng‐sam ts‘aam‐seúng‐hă neĭ‐kè lô‐t‘ō.
8 Iù‐sheŭng‐huì‐shaan‐ch‘uè, ning‐mūk‐liû laī‐heí‐kòh‐kaan‐uk; ngŏh yīk‐haí‐kòh‐kaan‐uk lōk‐ì, yaû‐tak‐tó wīng‐kwong, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî‐kóm wâ lokₒ.
9 Neĭ mông‐tak‐toh, t‘aí‐hă, shīng‐waī‐hó‐shiú; neĭ ning‐faan huì‐kwai kòh‐shī, ngŏh tsaû‐ch‘ui‐huì lokₒ. Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Yan‐hōh‐koò ni? Haî yan ngŏh‐kè uk laū‐lōk fong‐faì‐kè, neĭ‐teî‐kokₒ‐yān kíng‐īn tsaú‐tò tsẑ‐keí‐kè uk.
10 Shóh‐ĭ waî‐neĭ‐teî‐kè uēn‐koò, t‘in‐to chí‐chuê m̄‐lōk‐lô‐shuí, teî‐yīk‐t‘īng‐chuê m̄‐shang t‘ó‐ch‘aán‐kè.
11 Ngŏh chiû‐hŏn‐tsoi kòng‐lām‐ue‐ts‘ź‐teî, kòng‐lām‐ue‐shaan, ue‐wōh, ue‐tsaú, ue‐yaū, ue‐teî‐t‘ó shóh‐shang‐ch‘aán‐kè, yaû ue‐yān‐ka, ue‐ch‘uk‐shang, ue‐yān‐shaú shóh‐tsô kokₒ‐yeûng‐kè kung‐foo à.
12 ¶ Ue‐shî Ch‘itₒ‐t‘itₒ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ, kûng tsaì‐sz‐cheúng Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐taapₒ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à, t‘ūng‐maaī tsź‐mān uē‐shîng‐kè yān, t‘eng‐ts‘ūng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘uĭ‐teî‐kè Sheûng‐Taì‐chi‐sheng‐yam, uĕ‐k‘āp sin‐chi Hap‐kei‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐t‘okₒ‐lōk k‘uĭ‐kè, ī‐ch‘é tsź‐mān tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn to‐kuî‐p‘à.
13 Ue‐shî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè sz̀‐ché Hap‐kei, tsun‐i Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐fan‐foò‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, heùng‐tsź‐mān kóng‐wâ, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā wâ, Ngŏh t‘ūng‐maaī‐neĭ à.
14 Ī‐ch‘é Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kóm‐tûng Yaū‐t‘aaì‐kè fong‐paakₒ, Ch‘itₒ‐t‘itₒ‐kè tsaí Shóh‐lōh‐pá‐paakₒ, kûng tsaì‐sz‐cheúng Yeukₒ‐saatₒ‐taapₒ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐shue‐à‐kè sam‐shān, uĕ‐k‘āp tsź‐mān shóh‐uē‐shîng‐yān‐kè sam‐shān;
15 K‘uĭ‐teî tong Taaî‐leî‐oo wōng taî‐î nīn, lūk‐uēt î‐shāp‐sz̀‐yāt, tsaû laī haí maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk hoi‐kung lokₒ.
