1 NGŎH yaû t‘aí‐kìn yat‐chekₒ shaù, ts‘ūng hoí sheŭng‐laī, yaŭ ts‘at‐kòh‐t‘aū, shāp‐chekₒ‐kokₒ, kokₒ taaì‐chuê shāp‐téng koon‐mĭn, t‘aū‐sheûng yaŭ sitₒ‐tūk‐kè mēng.
2 Ngŏh shóh‐kìn‐kè shaù, haî hó‐ts‘z̆ paaù, k‘uĭ‐keukₒ hó‐ts‘z̆ hūng‐yān‐keukₒ, k‘uĭ‐haú hó‐ts‘z̆ sz‐haú: ī‐ch‘é lūng k‘aaĭ tsẑ‐keí‐kè nāng, tsẑ‐keí‐kè waî, uĕ‐k‘āp taaî‐k‘uēn ts‘z̀‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ.
3 Ngŏh t‘aí‐kìn k‘uĭ yat‐kòh‐t‘aū, hó‐ts‘z̆ shaû‐sheung tseung‐kân‐sź; k‘uĭ tseung‐kân‐sź‐kè sheung‐haú tak‐hó‐faan: ts‘uēn‐teî‐kè yān, tsaû kìn‐ch‘ut‐k‘eī, laī‐kan‐ts‘ūng kóh‐shaù.
4 Ī‐ch‘é paaì kòh‐t‘iū‐lūng, yan k‘uĭ ts‘z̀‐k‘uēn‐kwòh‐shaù: yaû paaì kòh‐chekₒ shaù, wâ, Mat‐shuī ts‘z̆‐tak ni‐chekₒ‐shaù ni? mat‐shuī nāng kûng k‘uĭ kaau‐chìn ni?
5 Yaŭ king‐kw‘a sitₒ‐tūk‐kè haú ts‘z̀‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ; yaû‐yaŭ k‘uēn‐pìng ts‘z̀‐kwòh‐k‘uĭ, hóh‐ĭ hāng‐sẑ sz̀‐shāp‐î uēt.
6 K‘uĭ hoi‐haú sitₒ‐tūk Sheûng‐Taì, laī sitₒ‐tūk k‘uĭ‐kè mēng, kûng waī‐mōk, k‘āp chuê‐tsoî t‘in‐kè.
7 Yaû ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ hóh‐ĭ kûng shìng‐t‘ō kaau‐chìn, tsaû tá‐shìng: yaû ts‘z̀ k‘uēn‐pìng kwòh‐k‘uĭ, hóh‐ĭ koón‐leĭ kokₒ‐tsūk, kokₒ‐yam, kokₒ‐kwokₒ.
8 Taân‐faān haí teî kui‐chuê, k‘uĭ mēng m̄‐sé‐lōk tsẑ‐ch‘òng‐shaì ĭ‐loī peî‐shaatₒ‐kè Ko‐Yeūng‐chi‐shang‐mîng‐ch‘aakₒ‐kè, to pit‐paaì kòh‐shaù.
9 Faān yaŭ‐ĭ‐kè, tsaû iù t‘eng lokₒ.
10 Taân‐faān lŏ‐leūk‐yān‐kè, yīk pit‐peî‐yān‐lŏ‐leūk‐k‘uĭ: k‘aaĭ‐kìm‐shaatₒ‐yān‐kè, yīk pit‐peî‐yān‐k‘aaĭ‐kìm‐shaatₒ‐k‘uĭ. Shìng‐t‘ō‐kè yán‐noî kûng sùn‐tak, haî tsoî‐ts‘ź lokₒ.
11 ¶ Ngŏh yaû t‘aí‐kìn pīt‐î‐chekₒ shaù, ts‘ūng teî‐sheŭng‐laī; yaŭ leŭng‐chekₒ‐kokₒ hó‐ts‘z̆ yeūng‐tsaí, kóng‐shuetₒ‐wâ hó‐ts‘z̆ lūng kóm.
12 Ni‐chekₒ shaù yīk hāng‐saaì taî‐yat chekₒ‐shaù‐kè k‘uēn, tsoî‐ue k‘eī‐ts‘īn, lîng teî kûng chuê‐tsoî‐teî‐sheûng‐kè yān, to paaì taî‐yat‐chekₒ shaù, tsik‐haî shaû‐tseung‐kân‐sź‐kè sheung‐haú tak‐hó‐faan‐kè.
13 Yaû hāng taaî‐î‐tsik, haí yān mîn‐ts‘īn, lîng fóh ts‘ūng t‘in lōk teî.
14 Yaû k‘aaĭ haí shaù‐mîn‐ts‘īn shóh‐tak‐k‘uēn‐pìng laī‐hāng‐kè î‐tsik, tsaû maī‐waāk chuê‐tsoî teî‐sheûng‐kè; ī‐ch‘é tuì chuê‐teî‐sheûng‐chi‐yān wâ, ying‐koi waî peî‐kìm‐shóh‐chaám‐sheung chûng‐shang‐kè shaù, laī‐chíng kòh‐tseûng.
15 K‘uĭ tak‐k‘uēn ts‘z̀‐shang‐mîng‐kwòh shaù‐tseûng, lîng shaù‐tseûng ooĭ‐kóng shuetₒ‐wâ, ī‐ch‘é lîng taân‐faān m̄‐paaì shaù‐tseûng‐kè yān peî‐shaatₒ‐sź.
16 Yaû lîng chùng‐yān, pat‐lûn taaî, siú, p‘ān, foò, chué, pūk, to‐haí k‘uĭ yaû‐shaú, waāk haí k‘uĭ ngaāk‐t‘aū, shaû yàn‐keì:
17 Táng yān‐teî m̄‐shaû yàn‐keì, tsik‐haî kòh‐shaù‐kè mēng, waāk k‘uĭ mēng‐kè shò‐mūk, tsaû m̄‐tak maaĭ‐maaî à.
18 Tsoî ts‘ź yaŭ chì‐waî. Faān yaŭ ts‘ung‐mīng‐kè, tong kaì‐suèn‐kwòh shaù‐kè shò‐mūk: yan‐waî haî yān‐kè shò‐mūk à; k‘uĭ‐kè shò‐mūk, tsaû‐haî lūk‐paakₒ‐lūk‐shāp‐līng‐lūk.
