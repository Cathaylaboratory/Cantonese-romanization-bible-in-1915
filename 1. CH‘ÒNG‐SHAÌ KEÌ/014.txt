1 TONG‐SHĪ, Òm‐la‐fei tsô Shî‐nā wōng, À‐leūk tsô Ĭ‐la‐saatₒ wōng, Kei‐taaî‐lŏ‐mă tsô Ĭ‐laān wōng, T‘aī‐taāt tsô līt‐kwokₒ wōng;
2 T‘ūng‐maaī huì kung‐tá Shóh‐toh‐mă wōng Peì‐la, Ngōh‐moh‐la wōng Peì‐sha, Aapₒ‐mă wōng Shî‐naāp, Sai‐pin wōng Shî‐maĭ‐pīt, uĕ‐k‘āp Peì‐la wōng, Peì‐la tsik‐haî Sóh‐ĭ.
3 Nğ‐kòh wōng tsuî‐ooî tsoî Sai‐t‘īng kuk, tsik‐haî Īm hoí.
4 Ni nğ‐kòh wōng, fūk‐sẑ Kei‐taaî‐lŏ‐mă shāp‐î nīn, tò shāp‐saam nīn, tsaû pooì‐poôn lokₒ.
5 Shāp‐sz̀ nīn, Kei‐taaî‐lŏ‐mă, kûng t‘ūng māng kè wōng laī ts‘aī, haí À‐t‘aī‐lūt, Ka‐nīng, kung‐tá Leĭ‐fāt‐yam kè yān, haí Hap‐māk, kung‐tá So‐sai kè yān, haí Sha‐meī, Kei‐līt‐t‘īng, kung‐tá Ĭ‐maĭ kè yān,
6 Tsoî Sai‐ĭ shaan, kung‐tá Hōh‐leĭ yān, yat tò kân kw‘ōng‐yĕ kè Ĭ‐pa‐laān.
7 Sz̀‐kòh wōng faan laī tò On‐māt‐paatₒ, tsik‐haî Ka‐t‘itₒ, kung‐tá À‐mă‐lāk shóh yaŭ kè teî‐fong, uĕ‐k‘āp chuê tsoî Hap‐saí‐sùen‐taaî‐mă kè À‐moh‐leĭ yān.
8 Ue‐shî Shóh‐toh‐mă wōng, Ngōh‐moh‐la wōng, Aapₒ‐mă wōng, Sai‐pin wōng, Peì‐la wōng, to paaí ts‘aī tuî‐nğ tsoî Sai‐t‘īng kuk;
9 Huì kung‐tá Ĭ‐laān wōng Kei‐taaî‐lŏ‐mă, līt‐pong wōng T‘aī‐taāt, Shî‐nā wōng Òm‐la‐fei, Ĭ‐la‐saatₒ wōng À‐leūk; kóm haî sz̀‐kòh wōng kûng nğ‐kòh wōng tá.
10 Sai‐t‘īng kuk yaŭ hó‐toh shēk‐yaū haang; Shóh‐toh‐mă wōng, k‘āp Ngōh‐moh‐la wōng paaî tsaú, titₒ‐lōk haang‐chung; k‘eī‐uē tsaú yāp shaan.
11 Sz̀‐kòh wōng tseung Shóh‐toh‐mă Ngōh‐moh‐la, shóh yaŭ māt‐kîn, uĕ‐k‘āp leūng‐ts‘ó, to ts‘eúng saaì tsaú huì.
12 À‐paakₒ‐laān kè chāt Lōh‐tak, chuê tsoî Shóh‐toh‐mă, tsẑ‐keí līn‐maaī māt‐kîn, to peî tīk‐yān ts‘eúng‐hiu tsaú huì.
13 Yaŭ yat‐kòh t‘ō‐tsaú ch‘ut laī kè yān, laī kóng‐kwòh Hei‐paakₒ‐loī yān À‐paakₒ‐laān chi; À‐paakₒ‐laān chuê kân À‐moh‐leĭ yān Maân‐leĭ kè tseûng‐shuê kòh‐ch‘uè, Maân‐leĭ kûng hing‐taî Ĭ‐shāt‐kukₒ, À‐naaĭ: to t‘ūng À‐paakₒ‐laān kitₒ‐māng.
14 À‐paakₒ‐laān mān‐tak kòh chāt peî yān lŏ‐leūk, tsaû taaì‐lĭng ka‐chung shóh yeŭng, lîn‐tsaāp kè pūk saam‐paakₒ‐yat‐shāp‐paatₒ yān, chui tò Taân kè teî‐fong.
15 Laû‐yê taaì‐chuê ti pūk fan lô huì kung‐tá, chui tò Taaî‐mă‐shik pak‐pin kè Hōh‐pa shēng.
16 Shóh yaŭ peî ts‘eúng kè māt‐kîn, līn foŏ‐nuĭ yān‐mān, to lóh‐faan saaì laī.
17 À‐paakₒ‐laān tá‐paaî‐hiu Kei‐taaî‐lŏ‐mă, kim hōp‐maaī kè wōng, k‘uĭ faan‐kwai chi shī, Shóh‐toh‐mă wōng ch‘ut tò Sha‐meī kuk, tsik‐haî wōng kuk, yīng‐tsipₒ k‘uĭ.
18 Saatₒ‐laăng wōng Māk‐kei‐saí‐tak, tsô chì ko Sheûng‐Taì kè tsaì‐sz: ning péng kûng tsaú ch‘ut‐laī.
19 Chuk‐fuk À‐paakₒ‐laān wâ, Uên t‘in teî chi Chué, chì ko chi Sheûng‐Taì, ts‘z̀ fuk kwòh À‐paakₒ‐laān:
20 Chì ko chi Sheûng‐Taì, keì tseung tīk‐yān kaau‐kwòh neĭ shaú, haî ying‐tong tsaàn‐meĭ kè. À‐paakₒ‐laān k‘aaĭ shóh tak kè māt‐kîn shāp fân ning yat, sùng‐kwòh Māk‐kei‐saí‐tak.
21 Shóh‐toh‐mă wōng tuì À‐paakₒ‐laān wâ, Yān tsaû peí faan ngŏh, māt‐kîn neĭ ning huì la.
22 À‐paakₒ‐laān tuì Shóh‐toh‐mă wōng wâ, Ngŏh chí‐chuê chì ko Sheûng‐Taì, t‘in teî chi Chué Yē‐Wōh‐Wā laī shaî,
23 Yat t‘iū sz, yat t‘iū haaī‐taaì, ngŏh to m̄ iù neĭ kè, mĭn‐chì neĭ wâ ngŏh lîng À‐paakₒ‐laān foò‐haû:
24 Waī‐haî pūk‐yān shóh shīk kè, uĕ‐k‘āp t‘ūng‐maaī ngŏh hāng chi yān À‐naaĭ, Ĭ‐shāt‐kuk, Maân‐leĭ; fân shóh ying tak kè, tsaû yaū‐tak k‘uĭ ning huì.
