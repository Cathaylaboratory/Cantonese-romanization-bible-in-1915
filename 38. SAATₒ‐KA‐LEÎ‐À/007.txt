1 TONG Taaî‐leî‐oo wōng taî‐sz̀ nīn, kaú‐uēt, tsik‐haî Kat‐lāk‐uēt ch‘oh‐sz̀ yāt, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ kòng‐lām‐ue Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à;
2 Tong‐shī shūk Paakₒ‐tāk‐leî‐chi‐yān, ĭ‐king tá‐faatₒ Shî‐leĭ‐sz, Leĭ‐kìm‐maĭ‐lāk, k‘āp kan‐ts‘ūng k‘uĭ‐kè yān, laī‐heùng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā hán‐k‘aū yan‐tín,
3 Yaû seúng tuì‐shūk maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐t‘ōng‐kè tsaì‐sz, uĕ‐k‘āp sin‐chi kóng wâ, Ngŏh tsoî nğ‐uēt tsẑ‐keí k‘ui‐pīt‐hoi‐kè, ying‐tong huk‐yap, chiù‐i ngŏh kòm‐toh‐nīn shóh‐tsô‐kè, haî‐m̄‐haî ni?
4 Ue‐shî maân‐kwan‐chi Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ, kòng‐lām‐ue‐ngŏh wâ,
5 Iù‐tuì kíng‐noî‐kè chùng‐tsź‐mān, uĕ‐k‘āp tsô‐tsaì‐sz‐kè kóng wâ, Neĭ ts‘at‐shāp‐nīn kòm‐noî, tsoî‐nğ‐uēt yaû‐tsoî‐ts‘at‐uēt, kàm‐shīk taam‐yau kòh‐shī, heí‐haî‐heùng‐ngŏh kàm‐shīk, tsik‐haî kwai‐ue ngŏh kóm‐tsô me?
6 Ī‐ch‘é neĭ waāk shīk waāk yám, heí‐m̄‐haî waî tsẑ‐keí shīk, waî tsẑ‐keí yám me?
7 Tong Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng yaŭ‐yān kui‐chuê, yaû‐tak‐hing‐wông, ī‐ch‘é sz̀‐waī shóh‐shūk‐kè shēng‐yap kûng‐naām‐fong, uĕ‐k‘āp p‘īng‐teî yaŭ‐kui‐mān kòh‐shī, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā t‘okₒ ts‘īn‐shī sin‐chi, kóng‐ch‘ut‐kè shuetₒ‐wâ, neĭ‐teî heí‐m̄‐ying‐koi t‘eng‐kè me?
8 ¶ Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐kè īn‐uĕ yaû kòng‐lām‐ue Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à wâ,
9 Maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā ĭ‐king kóng kóm wâ, Iù chiù‐i‐shīng‐shāt shám‐p‘oòn, yaû‐iù‐kokₒ‐yān tseung king‐sut ts‘z̄‐pei, shi‐kwòh k‘uĭ hing‐taî à:
10 Yaû m̄‐hó hak‐pōk kwá‐foŏ, waāk koo‐tsź‐kè, waāk luĭ‐haakₒ‐kè, waāk p‘ān‐k‘ūng‐kè; neĭ‐teî mŏ‐yat‐kòh sam‐chung seúng‐ch‘ut sheung‐hoî‐kè sẑ, laī‐kung‐kik hing‐taî à.
11 Waī‐haî neĭ‐teî m̄‐háng t‘eng‐ts‘ūng, yaû chuèn‐heùng pokₒ‐t‘aū, tsẑ‐sak‐k‘eī‐ĭ, táng‐tò‐pat‐nāng‐t‘eng‐kè.
12 Ī‐ch‘é chíng‐ngaâng‐k‘uĭ‐sam, p‘à‐t‘eng‐lūt‐faatₒ, kûng maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā k‘aaĭ‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐shān, t‘okₒ ts‘īn‐shī‐kè sin‐chi shóh‐ch‘uēn‐kè īn‐uĕ; koò‐yaŭ taaî‐nau‐nô, tsẑ‐maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā kòng‐kè lokₒ.
13 Ī‐ch‘é shīng‐tsaû kóm, hó‐ts‘z̆ k‘uĭ kiù‐sheng, k‘uĭ‐teî m̄‐háng‐t‘eng; chiù‐i‐kóm‐yeûng k‘uĭ‐teî‐kiù‐sheng‐kòh‐shī, ngŏh‐pit‐m̄‐háng‐t‘eng, maân‐kwan‐chi‐Yē‐Wōh‐Wā haî‐kóm wâ lokₒ:
14 Taân‐haî ngŏh iù‐k‘aaĭ suên‐fung tá‐saàn‐k‘uĭ‐teî, tsoî k‘uĭ m̄‐shik‐kwòh‐kè līt‐pong chi‐chung. Kóm‐tsaû tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐teî‐chi‐haû, ts‘ź‐kíng pìn‐waī fong‐faì‐kè, táng‐tò mŏ‐yān king‐kwòh, yaû mŏ‐yān faan‐ooī‐kè: yan‐waî k‘uĭ tseung‐hóh lōk‐chi‐teî, shīng‐waī fong‐faì‐kè lokₒ.
