1 SHEÛNG‐TAÌ à, ngŏh‐teî tsaàn‐tsûng neĭ, tsaàn‐tsûng neĭ: yan‐waî kìn neĭ‐chi‐î‐tsik, tsaû chi‐tò neĭ‐chi‐mēng, leī‐ngŏh‐mŏ‐uĕn.
2 Yāt‐k‘eī yat‐tò, ngŏh pit i kung‐î laī‐shám‐p‘oòn.
3 Teî‐t‘ó paaî‐mōng, yān‐mān leī‐saàn: waī‐haî ngŏh foō‐heí ch‘uĕ‐shēk.
4 Ngŏh tuì king‐kw‘a‐chi‐yān wâ, M̄‐hó king‐kw‘a: tuì hāng‐okₒ‐chi‐yān wâ, M̄‐hó ch‘īng‐kok.
5 M̄‐hó ch‘īng‐ko‐kòh‐kokₒ: m̄‐hó ngaâng‐kéng laī‐kóng.
6 Yān tak‐faatₒ‐taāt, m̄‐haî ts‘ūng tung‐pîn, ts‘ūng sai‐pîn‐laī, yīk m̄‐haî ts‘ūng naām‐shaan.
7 Naaĭ‐haî Sheûng‐Taì tsô yīng‐koon: shing ni‐kòh, kòng kóh‐kòh, tsaû‐haî k‘uĭ.
8 Yan‐waî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā‐chi‐shaú, cha‐chuê chekₒ‐pooi, tsaú‐meî kīk‐laāt; t‘iū‐wôh shâm‐haû; Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tseung tsaú cham‐ch‘ut: teî‐sheûng okₒ‐yān, pit‐tîng līn tsaú‐cha to yám‐saaì.
9 Waī‐haî ngŏh pit wĭng‐uĕn ch‘uēn‐yeūng; ch‘eùng‐shi koh‐tsûng Ngă‐kokₒ‐chi‐Sheûng‐Taì.
10 Sheûng‐Taì wâ, Okₒ‐yān‐chi‐kokₒ, ngŏh pit chitₒ‐t‘uĕn; shîn‐yān‐chi‐kokₒ, ngŏh pit kuí‐heí.
