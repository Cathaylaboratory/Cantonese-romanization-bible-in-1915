1 TAÎ‐SAAM yāt haî kóm, Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ cheukₒ‐heí ch‘iū‐fūk, k‘eĭ‐tsoî wōng‐kung‐kè noî‐uên tuì kung‐ch‘uè: wōng tsoî wōng‐kung‐noî, ts‘ŏh k‘uĭ wōng‐waì tuì‐chuê kung‐moōn.
2 Wōng kìn Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ k‘eĭ‐tsoî uên‐noî: tsaû heùng‐k‘uĭ shi‐yan, heùng‐k‘uĭ shan‐ch‘ut shaú‐shóh‐cha‐kè kam‐kwai. Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ tsaû hāng‐ts‘īn moh‐kam‐kwai‐t‘aū.
3 Wōng tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ à, neĭ seúng mat‐yĕ? neĭ k‘aū mat‐yĕ ni? chì‐tò kwokₒ‐kè yat‐poòn, ngŏh pit peí‐kwòh neĭ.
4 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ wâ, Wōng yeūk ĭ‐waī shîn, tsaû ts‘éng wōng kûng Hap‐maân kam‐yāt foô ngŏh waî k‘uĭ shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐kè ìn.
5 Wōng tsaû wâ, Shaí Hap‐maân kap‐ts‘uk tsun hāng, táng hóh‐ĭ shīng‐tsaû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ shóh‐kóng‐kè. Kóm‐tsaû wōng kûng Hap‐maân foô Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ shóh‐ch‘itₒ‐kè ìn.
6 Ts‘ŏh īn‐tsīk chi‐shī, wōng tsaû mân Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ wâ, Neĭ seúng pán mat‐yĕ? ngŏh pit ts‘z̀ neĭ: neĭ k‘aū mat‐yĕ, chì‐tò kwokₒ‐kè yat‐poòn, ngŏh pit waî neĭ tsô‐shīng.
7 Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ taapₒ wâ, Ngŏh shóh‐pán‐k‘aū haî kóm;
8 Yeūk haí wōng mîn‐ts‘īn tak‐yan‐ch‘úng, wōng yeūk ĭ wăn‐chún ngŏh shóh‐pán, hāng ngŏh shóh‐k‘aū, waī hó, tsaû ts‘éng wōng kûng Hap‐maân foô ngŏh waî k‘uĭ tseung‐ch‘itₒ‐kè ìn, ngŏh mīng‐yāt pit i‐ts‘ūng wōng shóh‐kóng laī‐hāng.
9 Kòh‐yāt Hap‐maân sam‐chung faaì‐lōk, hó‐foon‐heí ch‘ut‐huì: taân Hap‐maân kìn Mūk‐taí‐koí tsoî wōng‐moōn m̄‐heí‐laī, līn‐shan to m̄‐yuk‐hă, tsaû heùng Mūk‐taí‐koí ch‘ung‐moŏn nau‐nô.
10 Sui‐īn haî kóm, Hap‐maân tsaû mĭn‐k‘eŭng yán‐noî: faan‐huì‐kwai tá‐faatₒ‐yān, ts‘éng k‘uĭ‐kè‐p‘āng‐yaŭ, kûng k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘ai Sai‐lāk laī.
11 Hap‐maân tseung k‘uĭ foò‐haû‐kè wīng‐iû, tsaí‐nuĭ chùng‐toh, kûng wōng shing‐k‘uĭ‐ko ch‘ut moŏn‐ch‘iū shān‐pūk kwan‐cheúng‐chi‐sheûng, kóng‐saaì kwòh‐k‘uĭ‐teî t‘eng.
12 Hap‐maân kim‐chi wâ, Wōng‐haû Ĭ‐sẑ‐t‘ipₒ ch‘itₒ ìn, ch‘uē‐hiu‐ngŏh‐chi‐ngoî, m̄‐chún‐pīt‐yān kûng wōng foô‐ìn; mīng‐yāt wōng‐haû yaû ts‘éng ngŏh t‘ūng‐maaī wōng foô‐ìn.
13 Taân‐haî ngŏh chûng kìn Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān, Mūk‐taí‐koí tsoî wōng‐moōn ts‘ŏh‐ch‘uè, tsaû ni‐ti to mŏ‐yik‐ue ngŏh.
14 Ue‐shî k‘uĭ‐kè ts‘ai Sai‐lāk, kûng yat‐ts‘aì‐kè p‘āng‐yaŭ, tuì k‘uĭ wâ, Pat‐uē ch‘itₒ yat‐kòh nğ‐cheûng‐ko‐kè mūk‐kà, mīng‐chiu k‘aū wōng tseung Mūk‐taí‐koí tiù‐tsoî‐sheûng‐t‘aū: īn‐haû neĭ faaì‐lōk t‘ūng‐maaī wōng foô‐ìn. Hap‐maân ĭ ts‘ź‐sẑ waī hó; tsaû lîng yān tsô‐heí mūk‐kà.
