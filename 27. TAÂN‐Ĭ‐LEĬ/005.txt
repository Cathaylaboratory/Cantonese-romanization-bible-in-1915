1 PAAKₒ‐SHA‐SAATₒ wōng paaí‐ch‘itₒ hó‐taaî‐kè īn‐tsīk, ts‘z̀‐k‘uĭ‐ti yaŭ‐tseukₒ‐kè yat‐ts‘in‐yān yám‐ìn, haí kòh‐yat‐ts‘in yān‐chi‐ts‘īn laī‐yám‐tsaú.
2 Paakₒ‐sha‐saatₒ haí‐ch‘óh‐yám‐tsaú‐kòh‐shī, fan‐foò k‘aaĭ‐k‘uĭ‐foô‐ts‘an Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ, tsoî‐Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tîn‐chung ts‘eúng‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè kam‐ngān heì‐mĭng; ning‐laī táng wōng kûng k‘uĭ‐ti yaŭ‐tseukₒ‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ kokₒ‐ts‘ai, kokₒ‐ts‘ipₒ, to‐k‘aaĭ‐laī chong‐tsaú‐yám.
3 Chùng‐yān tsaû‐k‘aaĭ‐tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng Sheûng‐Taì tîn‐chung, ts‘eúng‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè kokₒ‐yeûng‐kam‐ngān‐heì‐mĭng ning‐laī; wōng tsaû kûng k‘uĭ‐ti yaŭ tseukₒ‐kè, uĕ‐k‘āp k‘uĭ ts‘ai, k‘uĭ ts‘ipₒ, to k‘aaĭ‐laī chong‐tsaú‐yám.
4 Taaî‐chùng yám‐tsaú, tsaû tsaàn‐meĭ k‘uĭ‐kè kam, ngān, t‘ūng, t‘itₒ, mūk, shēk, shóh‐tsô‐kokₒ‐shān.
5 Tsik‐hak‐kòh‐chân, yaŭ yat‐chekₒ‐yān‐kè shaú‐chí hín‐în‐ch‘ut‐laī, yaû sé‐ti‐tsẑ haí wōng‐kung paāk‐fooi‐kè ts‘eūng‐ch‘uè, chuk‐t‘oī heùng‐chuê kòh‐taatₒ, wōng tsaû‐kìn sé‐tsẑ‐kè shaú‐cheúng.
6 Wōng mîn tsaû‐pìn‐shik, sam‐kè nîm faān‐iŭ, chì‐tò iu‐tsitₒ‐hó‐ts‘z̆ lat‐kóm, sat‐t‘aū chàn‐tò seung‐chông.
7 Wōng tsaû taaî‐sheng‐kiù k‘uĭ‐ti naām‐mō‐kè Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān, uĕ‐k‘āp sing‐sẑ yāp‐saaì‐huì. Wōng tsaû kóng‐kwòh Pa‐peí‐lūn yaŭ‐chì‐waî‐kè yān t‘eng, tuì‐k‘uĭ‐wâ, Mō‐lûn pin‐kòh yaŭ‐nāng tūk‐ni‐ti, yaû‐tseung‐ti‐kaaí‐faatₒ laī‐chí‐shî ngŏh, tsaû‐pit‐peí tsź‐p‘ō k‘uĭ‐cheukₒ, ts‘z̀‐kam‐sokₒ‐laī‐kwà‐k‘uĭ‐kéng, yaû shaí k‘uĭ tsô‐kwokₒ‐chung saam‐táng‐kè tsoí‐seùng.
8 Kóm‐tsaû wōng shóh‐yaŭ‐chì‐waî‐kè yān yāp‐saaì‐huì: to m̄‐ooĭ‐tūk kòh‐ti‐tsẑ, yīk‐m̄‐ooĭ‐k‘aaĭ‐kòh‐ti‐kaaí‐faatₒ laī‐chí‐shî wōng.
9 Koò‐ts‘ź Paakₒ‐sha‐saatₒ wōng kìn‐hó‐faān‐moôn, yaû pìn‐hiu mîn‐shik, k‘uĭ‐ti‐yaŭ‐tseukₒ‐kè, kòh‐kòh‐to‐ch‘à‐î.
10 Wōng‐haû yan wōng‐kûng‐k‘uĭ‐yaŭ‐tseukₒ‐kè shóh‐kóng‐ti‐sẑ, tsaû‐yāp‐tò‐paaí‐ìn‐kè tîn: wōng‐haû tuì‐k‘uĭ wâ, Wōng à, uên neĭ wĭng‐shang: neĭ‐kòh‐sam m̄‐shaí‐faān‐moôn, neĭ‐kòh‐mîn m̄‐shaí‐pìn‐shik:
11 Neĭ kwokₒ‐chung yaŭ‐yat‐kòh‐yān, peí‐Shìng‐Shān‐kè Shān shóh‐kóm‐tûng; haí neĭ‐foô tsoî‐shang‐nīn‐kaan, k‘uĭ‐sam‐ts‘ōng‐chuê‐kè līng‐leî, ts‘ung‐mīng, k‘āp‐chì‐waî, hó‐ts‘z̆ Sheûng‐Taì‐kè chì‐waî; ni‐kòh‐yān, neĭ‐foô Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ wōng, tsaû haî‐wōng neĭ‐kè foô, laāp‐k‘uĭ tsô‐kòh‐ti‐mīng‐chitₒ‐kè, naām‐mō‐kè, Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān, k‘āp sing‐sẑ‐kè lĭng‐tsaû;
12 Ni‐kòh‐yān, mēng‐kiù Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ, wōng yaû on‐k‘uĭ‐mēng tsô Paakₒ‐taí‐sha‐saatₒ, k‘uĭ yaŭ shîn‐sìng, yaû‐yaŭ chì‐shik, mīng‐chitₒ, ooĭ‐kaaí‐mûng, mīng‐paāk‐yán‐uĕ, kaaí‐tak‐ī‐waāk‐kè ì‐sz̀: uē‐kam iù‐kiù‐Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ‐laī, k‘uĭ‐pit‐tîng kaaí‐mīng‐kè lokₒ.
13 Kóm‐tsaû taaì Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ tò‐wōng‐mîn‐ts‘īn. Wōng tuì Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ wâ, Neĭ‐haî Yaū‐t‘aaì‐yān haû‐yuî, haí foo‐ts‘aū‐chi‐chung, ngŏh‐kè foô wōng, haí Yaū‐t‘aaì kwokₒ shóh‐taaì‐ch‘ut‐laī‐kè Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ me?
14 Ngŏh ĭ‐king t‘eng‐mān neĭ peí‐chùng‐shān‐kè shān shóh‐kóm‐tûng, neĭ‐sam ts‘ōng‐chuê līng‐leî ts‘ung‐mīng, kûng hó‐taaî‐kè chì‐waî.
15 Uē‐kam kòh‐ti chì‐waî, naām‐mō‐kè, ĭ‐king taaì‐laī‐ngŏh‐ch‘uè‐mîn‐ts‘īn, táng‐k‘uĭ‐hóh‐ĭ tūk‐ni‐ti‐tsẑ, k‘aaĭ‐kòh‐ti kaaí‐laī‐chí‐shî‐ngŏh; taân‐k‘uĭ‐m̄‐ooĭ kaaí‐mīng ni‐ti‐chiû‐t‘aū:
16 Ngŏh ĭ‐king mān‐tak‐neĭ hó‐ooĭ‐kaaí‐kóm‐yeûng‐kè, kûng kaaí‐tak‐hoi‐ī‐waāk‐kè ì‐sz̀: uē‐kwóh neĭ ooĭ‐tūk‐ni‐ti‐tsẑ, k‘aaĭ‐kòh‐kaaí‐laī‐chí‐shî ngŏh, tsaû pit‐peí‐tsź‐p‘ō neĭ‐cheukₒ, ts‘z̀‐kam‐sokₒ laī‐kwà‐neĭ‐kéng‐ch‘uè, yaû shaí‐neĭ tsô‐kwokₒ‐chung saam‐táng‐kè tsoí‐seùng.
17 Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ tsaû haí‐wōng‐ts‘īn taapₒ‐wâ, Neĭ shóh‐ts‘z̀‐kè hóh‐kwai‐faan‐tsẑ‐keí, neĭ‐shóh‐sheúng kè hóh‐kwai‐ue‐pīt‐yān; taân‐ngŏh‐uên‐tūk‐ni‐ti‐tsẑ kwòh‐wōng‐t‘eng ī‐ch‘é‐k‘aaĭ‐kòh‐ti‐kaaí laī‐chí‐shî wōng.
18 Wōng à, chì‐sheûng‐kè Sheûng‐Taì, ts‘z̀‐neĭ‐foô Neī‐pò‐kaapₒ‐neī‐saatₒ tak‐kwokₒ‐k‘uēn wīng‐kwong, wai‐īm‐tsuen‐kwaì:
19 Yan Sheûng‐Taì shóh‐ts‘z̀ k‘uĭ‐kè wai‐k‘uēn, tsau kokₒ‐mān, kokₒ‐kwokₒ kokₒ‐t‘ó‐yam‐kè, to haí‐k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn tá‐chàn‐king‐fong: k‘uĭ yâm‐ì shang‐shaatₒ, chik‐ch‘ut;
20 Taân k‘uĭ‐kòh‐sam‐kuì‐ngô; k‘uĭ kòh‐chì‐kiu‐king; ī‐ch‘é waān káng, tsaû peî‐kaakₒ‐huì‐wōng‐waî: ī‐ch‘é wīng‐kwong to‐tuēt‐hiu:
21 K‘uĭ‐yīk peî‐kón‐chūk, leī‐hoi yān‐ch‘uè; k‘uĭ‐sam pìn‐waī shaù‐sam, yaû kûng‐luī t‘ūng‐chuê; yaû pit‐shīk‐ts‘ó hó‐ts‘z̆‐ngaū kóm, peí t‘in‐lô laī‐shap; táng‐k‘uĭ‐chi‐tò‐chì‐sheûng kòh‐waî Sheûng‐Taì, chué‐leĭ‐yān‐kè kwokₒ; ī‐ché i‐chuê‐tsẑ‐keí‐kè ì‐sz̀, ch‘itₒ‐yān‐laī‐tá‐leĭ.
22 Paakₒ‐sha‐saatₒ à, neĭ‐tsô‐k‘uĭ‐tsaí, chi‐tò ni‐ti‐kokₒ‐yeûng‐sẑ, neĭ‐kòh‐sam chûng‐m̄‐him‐pei;
23 Waī‐haî tsẑ‐ko kung‐kik‐t‘in‐kè Chué; taaî‐chùng yaû‐ning k‘uĭ‐shat‐kè heì‐mĭng, laī‐neĭ mîn‐ts‘īn, neĭ‐kûng‐neĭ‐ti‐yaŭ‐tseukₒ‐kè, kûng neĭ‐ti‐kokₒ‐ts‘ai, kokₒ‐ts‘ipₒ, to‐ning‐laī‐chong‐tsaú; ī‐ch‘é neĭ tsaàn‐meĭ kam, ngān, t‘ūng, t‘itₒ, mūk, shēk, shóh‐tsô‐kè kokₒ‐shān, tsaû‐haî t‘aí‐m̄‐kìn, t‘eng‐m̄‐mān, chi‐m̄‐tò‐kè: taân‐haî yaŭ‐neĭ foo‐k‘ap tsoî‐k‘uĭ‐shaú; ī‐ch‘é leĭ‐neĭ kokₒ‐lô‐kè Shān, neĭ meî‐ts‘āng tsuen‐wīng k‘uĭ:
24 Kòh‐shī yaū‐k‘uĭ‐mîn‐ts‘īn; shaí‐chekₒ‐shaú‐cheúng laī‐sé‐ch‘ut ti‐tsẑ.
25 Shóh‐sé‐kè‐tsẑ haî kóm, Maĭ‐ni, Maĭ‐ni, Taí‐kitₒ Oo‐peí‐lāk‐sam.
26 Kòh‐ti‐kaaí haî kóm: Maĭ‐ni kè haî Sheûng‐Taì ĭ‐king suèn‐neĭ‐kè kwokₒ laī chaaú‐ts‘ing à.
27 Taí‐kitₒ‐kè; Haî neĭ chai‐lōk t‘in‐p‘īng m̄‐kaù‐ch‘ŭng à.
28 Peí‐lāk‐kè haî neĭ‐kè kwokₒ ĭ‐king fan‐hoi, laī‐kaau‐kwòh Maĭ‐t‘aaì‐yān Pa‐sai‐yān à.
29 Kóm‐tsaû Paakₒ‐sha‐saatₒ fan‐foò chùng‐yān, tsaû k‘aaĭ tsź‐p‘ō peí Taân‐ĭ‐leĭ cheukₒ, yaû k‘aaĭ kam‐sokₒ kwà‐k‘uĭ‐kéng‐ch‘uè; ī‐ch‘é ch‘uēn‐chí‐fung‐k‘uĭ tsô‐kwokₒ‐chung saam‐táng‐kè tsoí‐seùng.
30 Tsaû kòh‐maăn Ka‐lāk‐taí‐yān‐kè wōng Paakₒ‐sha‐saatₒ, peí‐yān shaatₒ‐hiu kòh‐shī,
31 Maĭ‐t‘aaì‐yān Taaî‐leî‐oo, nīn‐lūk‐shāp‐î‐suì, ts‘uí‐hiu k‘uĭ‐kòh‐kwokₒ.
