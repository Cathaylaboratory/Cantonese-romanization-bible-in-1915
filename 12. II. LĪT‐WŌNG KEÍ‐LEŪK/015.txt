1 Ĭ‐SHIK‐LĪT wōng Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm taî‐î‐shāp‐ts‘at nīn, Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐mă‐tsê‐kè tsaí À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à tang‐waî.
2 K‘uĭ mŏ haî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng‐yān, mēng‐kiù Yē‐leî‐hóh. K‘uĭ nīn‐keí shāp‐lūk suì, tang‐waî tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng, tsô wōng nğ‐shāp‐î nīn.
3 K‘uĭ hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn waī‐shîn‐kè, hōk‐faan k‘uĭ foô À‐mă‐tsê shóh‐hāng;
4 Taân‐haî mŏ ch‘uē‐huì yau‐t‘aān: chùng‐yān yīng‐īn haí yau‐t‘aān‐sheûng hìn‐tsaì fān‐heung.
5 Yē‐Wōh‐Wā fāt wōng, lîng k‘uĭ fung‐tsāt, chì‐tò sź yāt, tsaû pīt‐kung tūk‐kui. Wōng tsź Yeukₒ‐taam chî‐leĭ ka‐sẑ, yaû chî‐leĭ kwokₒ‐mān.
6 À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
7 À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám; tsòng‐tsoî Taaî‐waî‐shēng, haí k‘uĭ līt‐tsó‐kè fān‐teî: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐taam, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
8 ¶ Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à taî‐saam‐shāp‐paatₒ nīn, Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè tsaí Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à, haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng lūk‐kòh uēt.
9 K‘uĭ hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn‐waī‐okₒ‐kè: hōk‐faan k‘uĭ līt‐tsó, pat leī Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè tsuî, shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haâm‐lōk‐tsuî.
10 Ngă‐p‘at‐kè tsaí Sha‐lūng maū‐faán k‘uĭ, tsoî chùng‐paakₒ‐sìng mîn‐ts‘īn, kik k‘uĭ yaû shaatₒ k‘uĭ, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
11 Saatₒ‐ka‐leî‐à k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, haî tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue.
12 Ni‐ti haî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tuì Yē‐oô shóh‐kóng‐kè, wâ, Neĭ‐kè tsź‐suen, tseung‐loī pit kaì Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐kè kwokₒ‐waî, chì‐tò sz̀ toî. Kwóh‐īn chiù kóm shīng‐tsaû lokₒ.
13 ¶ Yaū‐taaî wōng Oo‐sai‐à taî‐saam‐shāp‐kaú nīn, Ngă‐p‘at‐kè tsaí Sha‐lūng tang‐waî; k‘uĭ haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à tsô wōng yat‐kòh uēt.
14 Ka‐taí‐kè tsaí Maĭ‐nā‐în, ts‘ūng Tak‐saatₒ sheŭng‐tò Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à, haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à kung‐kik Ngă‐p‘at‐kè tsaí Sha‐lūng, shaatₒ k‘uĭ, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
15 Sha‐lūng k‘eī‐uē‐kè sz̀, k‘āp k‘uĭ tím‐yeûng maū‐faán, haî tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue.
16 Ue‐shî Maĭ‐nā‐în kung‐tá Tik‐saatₒ, k‘āp faān‐tsoî‐shēng‐chung‐kè yān, tsẑ Tak‐saatₒ heí, waí‐waaî sz̀‐kíng: yan‐waî mŏ kûng k‘uĭ hoi shēng‐moōn, koò‐ts‘ź kung‐tá shēng, yaû t‘ong‐hoi k‘eī‐chung shóh‐yaŭ‐kè yân‐foŏ.
17 ¶ Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à taî‐saam‐shāp‐kaú nīn, Ka‐taí‐kè tsaí Maĭ‐nā‐în, tang‐waî tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng, haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à tsô wōng shāp nīn.
18 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng‐okₒ: pat‐leī Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè tsuî, shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haâm‐lōk‐tsuî.
19 Kóh‐shī À‐shūt wōng Puk laī‐kung kwokₒ: Maĭ‐nā‐în tseung ngān yat‐paakₒ‐nğ‐shāp‐maân sùng‐kwòh k‘uĭ, ts‘éng Puk pong‐chôh, waî k‘uĭ kin‐laāp kwokₒ.
20 Maĭ‐nā‐în k‘eŭng‐sokₒ Ĭ‐shik‐līt kokₒ‐taaî‐ts‘oī‐chué kuen‐shue, lîng k‘uĭ‐teî kokₒ‐ch‘ut ngān nğ‐shāp shī‐haakₒ, tsaû k‘aaĭ‐laī peí‐kwòh À‐shūt wōng. Kóm‐tsaû À‐shūt wōng faan‐huì m̄‐haí kwokₒ‐chung t‘īng‐laū.
21 Maĭ‐nā‐în k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
22 Maĭ‐nā‐în kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám; k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka‐hāt kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
23 ¶ Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à taî‐nğ‐shāp nīn, Maĭ‐nā‐în‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka‐hāt tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng î nīn.
24 K‘uĭ hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn‐waī‐okₒ‐kè: pat‐leī Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè tsuî, shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haâm‐lōk‐tsuî.
25 Peí‐ka‐hāt‐kè tseung‐kwan, Leĭ‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka maū‐faán, k‘uĭ tsoî Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à haí wōng‐kung‐ch‘uè kung‐kik k‘uĭ, À‐lokₒ, À‐leĭ, pìng Kei‐līt‐yān nğ‐shāp, t‘ūng‐maaī‐maū‐faán: Peí‐ka shaatₒ k‘uĭ, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
26 Peí‐ka‐hāt k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, to tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue.
27 ¶ Yaū‐taaî wōng À‐saatₒ‐leĭ‐à taî‐nğ‐shāp‐î nīn, Leĭ‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka, haí Saatₒ‐mă‐leî‐à tang‐waî tsô Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng, tsoî‐waî î‐shāp nīn.
28 K‘uĭ tsoî Yē‐Wōh‐Wā mîn‐ts‘īn hāng okₒ: pat‐leī Neī‐paatₒ‐kè tsaí Yē‐lōh‐p‘òh‐òm‐kè tsuî, shaí Ĭ‐shik‐līt‐yān haâm‐lōk‐tsuî.
29 Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Peí‐ka nīn‐kaan, À‐shūt wōng Tik‐laāp‐peí‐leî‐sz laī tuēt‐hiu Ĭ‐wān, À‐paakₒ‐paakₒ‐mă‐ka, À‐nōh, Kei‐tāk, Hâ‐shokₒ, Kei‐līt, Ka‐leî‐leî, Naāp‐taaî‐leî ts‘uēn‐teî, tsûn‐teî lŏ‐leūk kóh‐ti‐yān huì À‐shūt.
30 Oo‐sai‐à‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐taam taî‐î‐shāp nīn, Ĭ‐la‐kè tsaí Hōh‐sai, maū‐faán Leĭ‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka kik k‘uĭ, yaû shaatₒ k‘uĭ, kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
31 Peí‐ka k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, to tsoì‐lōk Ĭ‐shik‐līt līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue.
32 ¶ Ĭ‐shik‐līt wōng Leĭ‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka taî‐î nīn, Yaū‐taaî wōng Oo‐sai‐à‐kè tsaí Yeukₒ‐taam tang‐waî.
33 K‘uĭ tang‐waî‐chi‐shī, nīn‐keí î‐shāp‐nğ suì, tsoî Yē‐lô‐saatₒ‐laăng tsô wōng shāp‐lūk nīn. K‘uĭ‐kè mŏ haî Saatₒ‐tuk‐kè nuĭ, mēng‐kiù Yē‐lŏ‐sha.
34 K‘uĭ hāng Yē‐Wōh‐Wā shóh‐kìn‐waī‐shîn‐kè: hōk‐faan k‘uĭ foô Oo‐sai‐à faān shóh‐tsô‐kè.
35 Taân‐haî mŏ ch‘uē‐huì yau‐t‘aān: chùng‐yān yīng‐īn haí yau‐t‘aān hìn‐tsaì fān‐heung. Yeukₒ‐taam kìn‐tsô Yē‐Wōh‐Wā tîn‐uk‐kè shūng‐moōn.
36 Yeukₒ‐taam k‘eī‐uē‐kè sẑ, faān k‘uĭ shóh‐hāng‐kè, heí‐m̄‐haî tsoì‐lōk Yaū‐taaî līt‐wōng‐keí‐leūk‐kè shue me?
37 Tong kóh‐yāt‐tsź, Yē‐Wōh‐Wā heí‐shaú shaí À‐laān wōng Leĭ‐sùn, k‘āp Leĭ‐mă‐leî‐kè tsaí Peí‐ka huì kung‐tá Yaū‐taaî.
38 Yeukₒ‐taam kûng k‘uĭ līt‐tsó t‘ūng‐ts‘ám, tsòng‐tsoî k‘uĭ tsó‐foô Taaî‐waî‐kè shēng, haí k‘uĭ līt‐tsó‐kè fān‐teî: k‘uĭ‐kè tsaí À‐hap‐sẑ kaì k‘uĭ tsô wōng.
